PROCEDURE  : Use art from an art pack
COMMAND    : /use-an-art-pack
SAY        : "use the art packs", "find art for", "is there a texture for", "use something
             from potential-assets"
RUNS       : local (most pack images are not in git, only on the owner's machine)
TAKES      : something that needs art (an item sprite, a block texture, a pattern, an icon)
LEAVES     : the chosen files copied into the module that uses them, transformed as
             needed, with the paper trail written
==============================================================================

WHEN TO USE
-----------
Whenever the mod needs art. Everything in intake/potential-assets/ is there to be used:
copy it, crop it, recolour it, or transform it, in any pack, without asking (owner's
standing permission, 2026-10-01). What is still required is the paper trail, because
the mod's own licence lets forks redistribute it onward.
Third-party art from anywhere ELSE is a licence check first (CLAUDE.md, License).

BEFORE YOU START
----------------
Read intake/potential-assets/README.txt, and the chosen pack's SOURCE-AND-LICENSE.txt
for its credit line. The packs differ:
- one pack is CC0 and committed to git;
- the others keep their images out of git (a .gitignore rule per pack), and the owner
  places them on disk by hand.
A pack whose images are missing is not a bug. Its MANIFEST.txt and
SOURCE-AND-LICENSE.txt say how to fetch it again.

STEPS
-----
1. Find candidates. Browse the packs for the need, and read sizes and file types:
   - some packs' file extensions lie, so check the real format;
   - web tiles are not at the mod's pixel scale.
2. Copy ONLY the specific files you need, into the module that uses them, under this
   mod's own names. Transform them there as needed (scale to the mod's pixel grid,
   recolour, crop, combine).
3. Write the paper trail in the same change:
   a. the pack's USAGE.txt: each file, where it went, and whether it was modified. An
      empty USAGE.txt means nothing from that pack is in the game;
   b. THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md, the first time anything from a pack is used, with the
      credit line from its notes. Some packs need extra wording: one forbids reuse of
      its icons and needs the MIT / CC BY-SA grants carved back for those files, and another
      must say where a tile came from and that its author is unknown. README.txt has
      the exact requirements per pack. THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md ships inside the jar and
      stays plain text.
4. Run the art pipeline for the kind of art:
   - item sprites -> bake-item-recolours.mjs, then check-duplicate-item-art.mjs, which
     fails on near-duplicates;
   - block art -> bake-block-art.mjs, then bake-block-previews.mjs and bake-item-art.mjs.
     Those refuse unchanged vanilla art on the wiki.
   See the regenerate table in procedures/rebake.txt (rebake).
5. Look at it in the game, or in the wiki preview, before calling it done
   (hand-over-a-test, if it needs the owner's eyes).
6. Pictures: show the owner the art in place (the PNG, and in game or on the page where
   it is used), by photograph-horses.txt, "PICTURES FROM OTHER PROCEDURES".

DONE WHEN
---------
The art is in place, USAGE.txt and THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md name it, the art checks are
green, and it is pushed.

NEVER
-----
- Never commit a whole pack, or any image from a pack whose images are kept out of git,
  except the specific files used. Never `git add -f` around a pack's ignore rule.
- Never publish a pack whole: not in a release zip, a wiki download or an editor asset
  bundle.
- Never re-add the deleted fifth pack (README.txt says why).
- Never skip USAGE.txt or the notice: the permission to use is not a waiver of credit.
