PROCEDURE  : Hand a test over to the owner
COMMAND    : /hand-over-a-test
SAY        : "what should I test", "give me something to test", "set up a test",
             "update the test kit", "re-aim the kit"
RUNS       : local (it edits the debug kit and launches the game)
TAKES      : something built that only the owner's eyes or hands can check
LEAVES     : a re-aimed test kit, a clean horse dimension, and a numbered list in chat
==============================================================================

WHEN TO USE
-----------
Whenever a session asks the owner to check something in the game by hand. She is the
only person who can play, so her time in the game is the scarcest thing in the project.
A kit full of finished tests spends it on work already done, and a vague ask ("go look
at the yard") spends it guessing what was meant.
If a pen can answer the question without her, build a pen instead (run-tests).

BEFORE YOU START
----------------
Read the newest entries in wiki/verification.html, and the Verification tabs they link.

STEPS
-----
1. Audit the test kit: DebugTestWorldHandler.BATCHES and giveBatch, in
   neoforge-26.1.2/.../server/.
   - Is every batch still relevant? Delete a batch whose checks are confirmed, and move
     the rest up.
   - Re-aim any slot whose item is already confirmed.
   - Batch 1 is always the next thing to test, and the newest tests go there.

2. Kit conventions:
   - One hotbar per batch, at most nine slots. Batch 1 is given on login. `/testkit n`
     swaps the hotbar for batch n, and `/testkit` lists the batches. Never fill the whole
     inventory: a 36-slot kit was "SUPER overwhelming".
   - Use preset eggs (PresetHorseSpawnEggItem.of plus a CUSTOM_NAME) for the exact
     horses each test needs, plus its consumables and blocks, and give one chat-legend
     line per slot.
   - Taming needs a stick (right-click tames on the spot). Breeding needs golden
     carrots. Any batch that asks her to tame or breed carries them, and both sexes
     wherever it breeds.
   - Files the kit cannot make (drop-in breeds or genes, breed-spawning.toml) are
     prepared in the dev run's run/phc/.

3. Audit the horse dimension. Is every extra structure or pen there still for a test that
   is open? Remove the ones whose tests are finished.

4. Rebuild and launch as launch-game describes. Never compile while she has the client
   open.

5. End the hand-over message with a numbered list in chat: every step in order, and
   what a pass looks like for each. Visual checks go last.
6. Pictures, where a check has a look: a picture of what a pass should look like, taken
   by photograph-horses.txt, "PICTURES FROM OTHER PROCEDURES" BEFORE the hand-over launch (it needs the client), so she
   knows what she is looking for.

DONE WHEN
---------
The kit's batch 1 is the next test, the dimension holds nothing finished, the game is
up, and the numbered list is in chat.

NEVER
-----
- Never ask her to test something a pen or a unit test could answer.
- Never hand over a kit that still holds confirmed tests.
- Never say "go look at the yard" without the numbered steps.
