PROCEDURE  : Add a gene
COMMAND    : /add-a-gene
SAY        : "add a gene", "make a gene", "new gene", "a gene that ...", "add an allele"
RUNS       : local (it bakes, tests and rebuilds the designer's wasm)
TAKES      : the owner's idea for a gene, or a scoped feature issue that describes one
LEAVES     : one registered gene with its tests, goldens, page, bakes and wasm, pushed
==============================================================================

WHEN TO USE
-----------
Use this for any new locus, or a new allele on an existing one. There are three kinds,
and they are built differently:
  COAT     - paints the coat and does nothing else (a marking, a colour, a pattern).
             Built as a data-driven JSON gene unless the vocabulary cannot express it.
  ABILITY  - does something in the game (flight, a drop, an aura, a temper, a stat, a
             tolerance, a death effect). Paints nothing, or paints only as a side effect.
  ADD-ON   - grows a mesh on the horse (the unicorn horn, antlers, ram horns).
A gene that is two kinds at once (a horn that glows and grants an aura) is built as
each kind in turn: the part first, then the ability.
The long reference is wiki/making-a-gene.html. Its tabs are named in the steps below.
If a scoped feature issue (or, transitionally, an intake treatment file) already
describes the gene, use process-intake: it builds a
unit, then calls back into this procedure for the gene work.
A new BREED to carry the gene is a separate design: see design-a-breed.

BEFORE YOU START
----------------
1. `git pull --ff-only origin main`.
2. Read wiki/text/philosophy.txt (the gene must earn its place: utility, not
   decoration, except in breeding), and the gene pages of the closest existing genes.
3. Know the save rule (CLAUDE.md hard rule 10). Allele order is saved. Never reorder or
   rename an existing allele. A new allele is added without moving the existing ones.
   On a Java gene that means APPENDED, after the wild type if the wild type was declared
   last. A pair is stored lower order first, so a one-copy horse then reads n/Tsk and its
   form's numbers are on the SECOND copy: any rule that reads "the copy that carries the
   allele" must look the slot up, never assume slot 0 (TusksGene.copyFor), and its test
   must cover an allele on each side of the wild type.

STEPS
-----
1. QUESTION EVERYTHING UP FRONT.
   Before reading code in depth and before writing anything, find every decision the
   request leaves open, and ask them all at once. Use the question tool: 2-4 concrete
   options each, your recommendation first and marked. Ask in consecutive rounds,
   without starting work in between, until nothing below is unanswered. Then build
   without stopping to ask again, except for a blocker nobody could have foreseen.
   If the request or treatment already answers a question, do not ask it; say which
   answers you took from where. If the owner does not know, take your recommendation
   and record it under "Calls made without the owner".

   Ask about every kind:
   a. The concept in one sentence. Which kind is it (coat / ability / add-on / mixed)?
   b. Natural (it exists in real horses) or magical? It is never both. Natural means
      a source, and a Science tab that cites it.
   c. Alleles: how many, their names and tokens, and which one is wild type.
      Dominance: dominant, recessive, incomplete, or matched-pair.
   d. What each genotype looks like or does: one outcome per pair that differs.
   e. Rarity in the wild, and which existing breeds carry it, at what share. A new breed
      to show it off goes to design-a-breed.
   f. Sex: is it sex-linked or sex-limited (like the stallion-only antler allele)?
   g. Foals: does it show from birth?
   h. Per-horse variation: what differs between two horses with the same genotype
      (size, tint, count, strength)? Those become epigenome values on the allele copy.
   i. Health: any health or fertility effect? (This goes on Gameplay as a sentence.)
   j. Interactions: what other genes change it, or hide it? Does it stack with similar
      genes?
   k. Breeding items: should a carrot or splice exist for it?
   l. Saves: can every existing world keep loading? (Almost always, with a migration.
      A gene that cannot is a compat - second-number - release, and that is only for
      a substantial feature.) A finished gene otherwise rides the next patch release,
      which the owner calls (release, step 1).
   Ask too, for a COAT gene:
   m. Where on the body, and on which bases (black, bay, chestnut, grey, dilutes)?
   n. Shape and edge: spots, lines, patches, gradients; hard or soft edges; size and
      density; is there an SVG or reference image?
   o. Colours: absolute colours, or relative to the base coat? Does mane, tail, muzzle
      or hoof change?
   p. Draw order against other markings: over or under white, dapples, roan?
   Ask too, for an ABILITY gene:
   q. The trigger: always, when ridden, at night, in rain, on death, on interaction,
      near a mob?
   r. The effect and its size: numbers, radius, duration, cooldown, maximum targets.
   s. Who it affects: the horse, the rider, the owner, other horses, mobs? What happens
      in multiplayer, and on someone else's horse?
   t. How the player perceives it: particles, sound, a message, an info-screen line?
   u. Config: may a server turn it off or tune it, and what are the defaults?
   v. Does it change horse AI or behaviour priorities?
   w. Modded content: should it reach other mods' mobs, items or blocks (as Chaos does)?
   x. Balance: what stops it being the best gene to have, and what does it cost?
   Ask too, for an ADD-ON gene:
   y. Where it grows (an existing anchor: forehead or crown; or a new one), and does
      it come in pairs?
   z. Shapes or styles, and how many variants; the size range; symmetric or lopsided?
   aa. Colour: its own tint, the coat's colour, a tip colour, glow, a material (crystal,
       bone, growth)? Are any of these separate modifier genes?
   bb. Does it hide under, or clash with, a saddle, rider, armour or other gear?
   cc. Do mares and foals grow it, and how does it scale with the horse's size gene?
   dd. Does it realistically have bone in it? Then it needs a BONE VERSION (owner,
       2026-10-02): the alleles or bands that draw it as bare bone on a skeleton horse -
       bone-coloured, unlit, nothing soft (wings: the bones alone, no membrane). Ask what
       the bone version looks like, and whether the skeleton breeds' pools carry it (every
       horn-like part is in their horn count group). A treatment's "no breed is changed"
       does not answer this: UndeadBreedsTest.theHornKindsInTheGroupAreEveryHornLikePart
       holds the standing rule that every horn-like part joins that pool, so ask (the
       tusks session found the clash only when that test went red, 2026-10-05).
       A part with NO bone still gets the pool question: the crystal growths joined it as
       ordinary crystals, colour unpinned (owner, 2026-10-05). Then hornsFollowTheCountGroup
       needs the kind's look allowed, as it allows the crystals' translucency.
       A gene with SEVERAL forms asks one more thing: does the skeleton get one form, or
       any of them? A count group's locus can hold a weighted choice of pairs
       (wiki/breed-format.html#count-groups; the tusks slot rolls one of three, owner
       2026-10-08), and the choice shares the locus's one slot.

2. Read the code. Hand open-ended sweeps to an Explore agent (look-it-up). Grep every
   class and file named below before relying on it. Path shorthands:
     C = common/src/main/java/com/example/horsegenetics/common
     N = neoforge-26.1.2/src/main/java/com/example/horsegenetics/neoforge
     R = common/src/main/resources/horsegenetics
     T = common/src/test/java/com/example/horsegenetics/common

3. Steps for every kind:
   a. Priority and family.
      - Natural genes sit in 0-99 (80-99 for genes that paint nothing). Magical genes
        sit at 100 and up.
      - Order is by (priority, key); the order genes are registered in is ignored.
      - C/genetics/GeneFamily.java derives the family. Magical genes go by priority band
        (MAGICAL_BANDS) unless listed in MAGICAL_OVERRIDES. Natural genes go by
        NATURAL_OVERRIDES, then NATURAL_HEALTH if the gene paints nothing, then
        NATURAL_BANDS.
      - A gene that paints nothing NEEDS an override line, or it files wrongly in the
        sidebar and both editors.
   b. Tokens.
      - Java tokens: alphanumeric, ideally 1-5 characters, unique within the gene.
      - JSON tokens: anything except "/" and "-".
      - No token may contain "|" (ResearchTopicTest).
      - The key is horsegenetics.<gene>; the wild type is "n". Grep every token for a
        clash with existing alleles.
   c. Founders: put the expressing combinations in the founder table. No invisible
      carriers in the wild, except a deliberate CARRIERS_ONLY design, said in notes.
      Disorders are carrier-only. `python intake/tools/founders.py --check` reports
      the tier-derived table.
   d. Breeds: edit R/breeds/*.json. Then `:common:bakeBreedFiles` and
      `:common:bakeGeneCensus`. Tests: BreedFilesTest, the breed's own *BreedTest,
      BreedFounderTest, BreedClimateRuleTest.
   e. Editors (hard rule 5): both the horse designer (web/.../HorseEditor.java,
      DesignerApi.java) and N/client/CustomHorseSpawnScreen.java read the registry, and
      shared rules live in C/genetics/EditorRules.java. Edit them only for a deliberate
      divergence, and write that divergence in the comment on BOTH files.
   f. Java genes need a description in C/genetics/GeneDescriptions.java
      (GeneDescriptionCoverageTest). JSON genes carry blurb, notes and expression
      descriptions. Genes have no lang keys, unless the gene adds an item or block.

4. COAT gene.
   Path A - data-driven JSON. This is the default.
   1. Create R/genes/<slug>.json. Use making-a-gene's tabs:
      - "The file" for the format: format 3, key, name, phase, priority, alleles (the
        LAST is the population baseline), expressions, founders, knobs, notes;
      - "Shapes" for the shape;
      - "Masks & ops" for the paint.
      The gene creator (wiki/gene-creator/) drafts two alleles and one outcome. Edit
      anything bigger by hand. The creator drops "needs".
   2. Add the filename to R/genes/index.json BY HAND. It is the only thing that ships a
      bundled gene: a jar has no folder to walk. Never regenerate it from the folder
      (hard rule 6), because an absence there is a killswitch.
   3. Tests, by name:
      - *GeneFilesTest*, which runs the real parser. If the SVG point ceiling fails,
        run `node intake/tools/fit-svg.mjs <gene.json> --write`.
      - *DeadLayerTest. A layer that paints nothing fails it: fix the gene, then delete
        its line from dead-layers.txt.
   4. Look at it.
      - `:common:bakeGeneIcons`: no icon means the gene paints nothing.
      - `node intake/tools/coverage.mjs <gene.json>`: which layer reached which body
        part.
      - `:common:bakeCoatSamples`: side views in build/coat-samples/.
      Render before claiming it looks right: a measurement has agreed with a broken coat
      before.
   5. The page is GENERATED from the JSON by bakeGeneWikiPages. Edit the JSON, not the
      page. Only the Verified block and extra tabs are carried over (GeneWikiCarryTest).
   6. Only if you add a mask or an op: making-a-gene#contracts lists the files, and
      every one must change or the two engines drift silently:
      - SpecSchema;
      - SpecPainter;
      - the creator's schema.js, spec-engine.js and spec-model.js;
      - noise.js, if needed;
      - the tab text;
      - a use in R/example-genes/prismatic.json.
      Then run `:common:bakeSpecFixtures`, `check-parity.mjs`, `:common:bakeCreatorAssets`
      and `:web:bakeDesignerAssets`. Break spec-engine.js on purpose once: parity must
      go red.
   Path B - a Java gene, only when the JSON vocabulary cannot express it.
   1. Create C/genetics/genes/<Name>Gene.java.
      - Extend AbstractNaturalGene (restrict) or AbstractMagicalGene (tint).
      - Declare it with TwoAlleleGene.gene(...)..., or implement Gene directly for
        three or more alleles.
      - Never write through the coat: use mutableCopy(), and dilute/whiten rather than
        bare scaling.
      - Per-horse numbers come from epiSchema() and ctx.epigeneticsFor(KEY), marked
        .varies().
      - No Random, UUID, time or mutable statics, and cover every pair.
   2. Register it in C/genetics/Genes.java: a public static final field, plus the
      BUILTINS list.
   3. Write T/genetics/genes/<Name>GeneTest.java. GeneCoatHookTest is the pattern.
   4. The page is HAND-WRITTEN, with tabs gameplay, coding and science, plus
      verification. bakeGeneWikiPages still registers it.
   5. A gene whose look is a whole sheet of art (the undeath loci) implements
      CoatSheetContribution and names its sheets in sheetResources(). The game and
      every tool load them from that by themselves; the horse designer does NOT:
      add the PNGs to web/build.gradle.kts's include list and the keys to java.js's
      SHEETS, or the browser silently bakes the plain coat. Sheets converted from
      vanilla art go through common/sheet-sources/manifest.txt (rebake row z).

5. ABILITY gene.
   1. Pick the shape, in this order of preference:
      - JSON with an "effects" block on an expression, if an existing verb does it.
        The verbs are the AbilityType registrations: traversal, attribute, emitter,
        mob_effect, yield, glow, healing, spread, charges, breath, on_death, item_drop,
        mob_aura, night_watch, day_watch, combat, sound, produce, teleport, summon,
        temper, bond, ward. flying.json is the worked example. An existing verb needs no
        NeoForge change.
      - Java base classes:
        - AbstractAbilityGene - one allele, always magical;
        - AbstractMatchedPairGene - many alleles, acts only on a matched pair
          (LycanGene, EggLayerGene, SpawnerGene);
        - AbstractClimateGene;
        - AbstractMagicStatGene;
        - or Gene plus AbilityContribution / EpigeneticAbilityContribution (one or the
          other, never both);
        - stats and health go through TraitContribution, HealthContribution and
          RecessiveDisorderGene (making-a-gene #noncoat).
   2. Every expression must be wildType, so the coat never re-bakes. The baseline
      allele is declared last and contributes zero.
   3. Add a MAGICAL_OVERRIDES entry (MAGIC_BEHAVIOUR, MAGIC_YIELD, MAGIC_EMISSION or
      MAGIC_BODY), or it files as a coat gene.
   4. Translation into the game is server-authoritative. Handlers:
      - N/server/GeneAbilityHandler (the tick loop);
      - GeneYieldHandler;
      - GeneDeathHandler;
      - NightBehaviourHandler;
      - GeneReactionHandler;
      - GeneWardHandler.
      All of them collect through C/genetics/spec/HorseAbilities.activeFor. A bespoke
      mechanic gets its own handler (LycanthropyHandler, ChaosRoster).
   5. A new verb (making-a-gene#contract-verb) touches all of these:
      - a record on GeneAbility;
      - register(...) in AbilityType;
      - a case in the handler;
      - a section on making-a-gene's Effects tab;
      - a client RenderLayer, if it draws.
      AbilityType is on the spec/ row of rebake's table (row d), so run bakeSpecFixtures,
      then check-parity.mjs.
   6. Config: server keys in N/ServerConfig.java, each with a safe accessor and a
      default. A bad value must never crash a world load. Document each key on the
      gene's page, give it a HOME in wiki/tools/bake-config-keys.mjs, and run that bake
      (add-a-config-option step 9).
   7. Saved state: a new attachment goes in N/data/ModAttachments.java. Under rule 10,
      any change to an existing one ships with its migration and a test that loads the
      old shape.
   8. AI: if goals are added or reprioritised, run
      `node wiki/tools/bake-behaviour-hierarchy.mjs`.
   9. Tests:
      - a unit test in T/genetics/genes/ (LycanGeneTest and ChaosMobLociTest are
        patterns). Mutate one assertion's subject on purpose, to prove the test bites;
      - a gametest in N/gametest/ModGameTests.java for anything that only the game can
        prove;
      - a yard pen (DebugTestYard) that logs its own PASS or FAIL, for anything that
        needs time.
   10. Traps:
       - dose counts only the first-declared allele, unless abilityDose is used;
       - matched-pair seeds rolled independently never agree, so use
         Gene.foundersShareSeeds();
       - a radius effect needs max_targets;
       - effects must be perceivable;
       - common/ imports nothing from Minecraft;
       - the dev run has no modded mobs or items.

6. ADD-ON gene (a grown part). model-parts' Coding tab "adding a part" is the recipe;
   the antlers session (wiki/session-log/d2026-10-01-antlers.txt) is the worked
   example.
   1. C/parts/PartKind.java: add values, one per side. Never mirror with a negative
      scale: it turns culled boxes inside out. Add cases in styles(), sizeBuckets(),
      showsOnFoal(), glowRegions() and any helper predicates.
      PartGeneratorTest.everyKindIsCompletelyDeclared checks them. A part that lies
      along the body also answers saddleZoned() (a saddle or rider hides the elements
      under the saddle) and scalesPerElement() (a row: each element scales about its
      own root, so every box must be in a group); wiki/model-parts.html#body-parts.
      SaddleZoneTest.everySpineIsOneNumberedChainFrontToBack is written for the spine
      row's MAX_SPINES groups: a saddle-zoned kind with a different count (the crystals'
      six clusters) is excluded there and holds its numbering in its own gene test.
      An element of several boxes that must scale and hide as one (a crystal cluster) is
      ONE tree: root it on an upright box and hang the rest off it at t=0, so the
      one-tree-per-group rule and the saddle zone hold unchanged; model-parts.html#crystals.
      Two rows in one mesh (the plates' shoulder and hip) share their group numbers, so
      a group may have more than one root: PartModel acts on every box whose parent is
      outside its group. Tell PartGeneratorTest's tree count, and say so in the gene's
      test; model-parts.html#plates.
      A part with a see-through region answers translucentRegions() and sets
      AttachedPart.opacity; one with a soft region (a membrane) leaves it off in its
      bone version through AbstractPartColourGene.boneOnly; model-parts.html#translucent-regions.
   2. A new place to grow: C/parts/PartAnchor.java, plus a case in
      N/client/AttachedPartLayer.anchor(...), with offsets for adults AND foals. Walk
      from the model root (it has a scale and an offset). Every mesh grows up its own -y;
      a part that must face another way (the narwhal horn, forward from SNOUT) is turned
      by a fixed rotation AFTER the anchor's translate, before the per-horse tilt -
      model-parts.html#snout. Read the vanilla model for which bone the place moves with:
      the muzzle (upper_mouth) shares head's frame, and no jaw animates in 26.1.2.
      A part that HANGS (a sabre fang) needs no layer turn at all: put a half-turn about x
      on the mesh's own root box. A half-turn keeps the part on the anchor's y axis, so the
      layer's stretch and tilt still act along it; a quarter-turn does not, which is why the
      narwhal's is in the layer. model-parts.html#jaw-lip.
   3. The generator: C/parts/<X>Generator.java, beside HornGenerator, AntlerGenerator
      and RamHornGenerator. It emits PartNodes parent-before-child. Add a size class
      (like AntlerSize), and cases in PartGenerators.build and in PartShape's
      nominalLength and stretchTo. Bake the topology at its maximum, and show counts
      out of that maximum.
   4. The sheet: add a region constant in C/parts/PartSheet.java AND the same constant,
      plus a paint() call, in neoforge-26.1.2/tools/bake-part-sheet.mjs. The two
      constants are repeated by value and drift silently. Run
      `node neoforge-26.1.2/tools/bake-part-sheet.mjs` and commit
      .../textures/entity/horse/part_sheet.png.
   5. The granting gene is Java and magical (a MAGIC_PARTS priority slot). It returns
      AttachedPart(s), with epigenome values on the copy: size, a seed, counts, reach,
      girth, asymmetry, tint. Modifier genes (form, colour, glow, crystal, growth, tip)
      are separate Java genes. A form allele's order equals its generator's habit index.
   5b. A part's own colour locus (one per part, owner 2026-10-01) is a short subclass of
      C/genetics/genes/AbstractPartColourGene: key, name, priority, Words. DragonHornColourGene
      is the example. A soft part (beard, ruff, feathering) samples PartSheet.HAIR.
   6. C/genetics/GrownParts.java: add the call in of(...), plus a dress<X> method. ALSO
      add the granting locus to the everyPart baseline in shapes(Gene). Otherwise its
      modifier genes look invisible to EditorRules.changesLooks, and the editors never
      randomise them.
   7. Add every new locus to GeneFamily.MAGICAL_OVERRIDES as MAGIC_PARTS. Then the
      Genes fields, BUILTINS and GeneDescriptions.
   8. The renderer, N/client/PartModel.java. The deferred-submit trap: every per-horse
      change goes through setupAnim with that submit's Slice, setting EVERY field on
      EVERY box every time. Otherwise the second horse overwrites the first one's shared
      mesh. Never add children to the body or head in createHdBodyMesh: it shifts the
      HD UVs.
   9. Tests:
      - PartGeneratorTest (parent order, determinism, kind completeness, the sheet
        grid). Watch theWholeSetOfMeshesIsSmall: the part cache has no eviction, and
        the box total grows with styles times buckets;
      - a gene test like AntlersGeneTest, covering the sex gate, the foal gate, the
        epigenome reaching the part, and editor visibility. Mutate the sex gate once;
      - breed tests for any breed that pins it.
   10. The browser editor cannot draw a mesh; the game's spawn screen can. Keep that
       divergence written in BOTH editor files' comments.
   10b. The bone version (question dd). Build it as alleles or band reach on the part's own
       loci - a Bon colour allele (AbstractPartColourGene has one), a shade end that only a
       breed reaches (RamHornsGene's shade below 0), an "off" allele for the soft parts (a
       wing's membrane) - never as a special case in the renderer. Add it to the skeleton
       breeds' files (great_valley_skeleton_horse.json, blackened_skeleton_horse.json) when
       the owner says they carry it, extend UndeadBreedsTest's bone checks, and add the row
       to wiki/model-parts.html#bone-versions.
   11. Update wiki/model-parts.html's Coding tab: its kind and region counts go stale
       with each part.
   12. Placement is computed from model boxes, so it is unverified until it is seen.
       The Verification tab asks for:
       - every animation;
       - a foal;
       - two horses with different counts in one frame;
       - the biggest size under a ceiling;
       - with a saddle and rider.
       Hand it over with hand-over-a-test.

7. Goldens: every new gene moves them.
   Run `--tests '*CoatBakeGoldenTest'` and `--tests '*CoatPipelineGoldenTest'`. On a
   mismatch, each writes its actual to common/build/. Check WHICH rows moved, then copy
   them to common/src/test/resources/. A gene that paints nothing should only ADD bake
   rows at the baseline hash, and change no pipeline hash. Any other movement is a
   regression until explained. Every new gene rewrites EVERY pipeline-golden line, because
   each line's key is the whole genotype code: compare only the last (hash) column, e.g.
   awk '{print $NF}' on both files with CRs stripped, before copying the actual.

8. The gene page.
   - Three tabs inside article.doc: <section class="tab-panel"
     data-tab="gameplay|coding|science">. Gameplay is for a player, and carries health
     as a sentence. Design rationale goes on Science. Natural genes must have a Science
     tab (check-gene-tabs.mjs).
   - Plus a Verification tab (data-tab="verification") that opens with a
     note verify-summary box, saying what to look at in game.
   - A painting gene gets the preview: <div class="gene-preview"
     data-gene="horsegenetics.x"></div> and the gene-preview.js script.
   - Never hand-add the page to wiki/pages.js or index.html (hard rule 7).

9. The bake list, in this order. The timeline dates a gene by the first commit that
   names its key, so commit the code before baking the timeline.
     :common:bakeGeneBundle   :common:bakeGeneIcons   :common:bakeGeneWikiPages
     :common:bakeMarkingFacts :common:bakeGeneCensus
     node wiki/tools/bake-gene-timeline.mjs
     :common:bakeUnverifiedGenes  (after the timeline: it sorts by it)
     :web:bakeDesignerAssets  then  node wiki/tools/check-designer-boots.mjs
     node wiki/tools/bake-verification-index.mjs  (and bake-roadmap-index.mjs)
     node wiki/tools/build-search-index.mjs, check-links.mjs, bake-agent-text.mjs
   Commit everything those wrote (rebake's gene row, g, lists them). A wasm rebuild
   after a data-only bake may be byte-noise: compare the two, and revert the wasm if
   nothing reachable changed.

9b. Pictures: show the owner the gene, by photograph-horses.txt, "PICTURES FROM OTHER
    PROCEDURES". A coat gene: bakeCoatSamples side views. An add-on: a photo shoot,
    every form, two-tone and bone, with the framing its anchor needs. An ability: a
    shot of whatever it shows (particles, a glow), or "no pictures".

10. Finish with `./gradlew :neoforge-26.1.2:build`, then end-session. The gene rides the
    next release; release step 1 decides whether one is worth offering.

DONE WHEN
---------
- The gene is registered and baked, its tests and both goldens are green by name, and
  its page carries Gameplay, Coding, Science and Verification tabs.
- The designer boots.
- The owner has seen pictures of it (step 9b), unless nothing about it can be seen.
- The build is green and everything is pushed.
- The report says what was asked and answered, the calls made without the owner, what
  a full :common:test run would still check, and where to look first in game.

NEVER
-----
- Never start building with questions still open that the owner could have answered at
  the start.
- Never reorder or rename an existing allele (saves; hard rule 10).
- Never regenerate R/genes/index.json from the folder (hard rule 6).
- Never port a gene to JavaScript (hard rule 3).
- Never let a gene that paints nothing fall into a coat family. Give it an override.
- Never claim it looks right without rendering it.
- Never run the full :common:test suite.
