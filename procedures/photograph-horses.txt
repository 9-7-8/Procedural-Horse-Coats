PROCEDURE  : Photograph horses in game
COMMAND    : /photograph-horses
SAY        : "show me pictures", "photograph the horses", "screenshot the parts",
             "show me examples in game"
RUNS       : local only (it launches the dev client)
TAKES      : a built tree; no client running, or one the owner says may close
LEAVES     : run/screenshots/photo-*.png, one per shot, looked at, and shown to the owner
==============================================================================

WHEN TO USE
-----------
When the owner wants to see horses with given genes in the real game, without spawning
them herself: a new grown part, a colour, a stacking case. It photographs a fixed list
of genotypes on a stage in the sky. It does not test behaviour, animation or anything
that needs time; for those, build a yard pen (run-tests) or hand her a test
(hand-over-a-test).

BEFORE YOU START
----------------
1. Read launch-game. Its rules about her world, the log and closing a client all apply.
2. The tool: N/server/DebugPhotoShoot.java (the stage and the SHOTS list) and
   N/client/DebugPhotoShootClient.java (camera timing and the screenshot), where
   N = neoforge-26.1.2/src/main/java/com/example/horsegenetics/neoforge. It runs only
   when the client starts with -PphotoShoot, in singleplayer.

STEPS
-----
1. Write the shots. Edit DebugPhotoShoot.SHOTS: a file name, a sex, a genetic code
   (full keys, horsegenetics.<gene>=<a>/<b>, joined with "-"), and a dragon horn form or
   -1. Genes left out are wild type. Epigenetic numbers (length, girth, shade) are rolled
   per horse and differ every run; to pin one, extend record() to re-roll until it is met,
   as the dragon horn form already does.
   A code of breed:<id> rolls a founder of that breed instead (every pin and band
   applies): "|+key=a/b" stamps a pair onto the roll, "|key=a/b" re-rolls until it is
   there. Stamp anything rare - re-rolling for a 1-in-640 founder outran the camera and
   saved an empty stage. Framing.WIDE shows the whole horse, Framing.FOAL a whole foal. The camera frames head and shoulders; a long
   unicorn horn still leaves the top of the frame. WIDE looks nearly down the horse's
   length from the front, so a part along the back (spines, a sail) comes out end-on: give
   stage() a side camera for one (the back sail session, 2026-10-05, put it 2-4 blocks due
   north or south of the horse). A part standing OUT from the flank (the plates' spikes)
   points at a side camera and comes out end-on too: add a camera behind the horse (east of
   it, a block or two up), which shows both flanks at once (the plates session, 2026-10-05).
   A still frame against the sky or the white stage cannot
   show whether a translucent part is see-through; put something with detail behind it.
   Grep every allele token before using it.
2. Compile: ./gradlew :neoforge-26.1.2:compileJava, logged to a file, exit code read.
3. Copy run/logs/latest.log and debug.log to the scratchpad first: they hold the owner's
   last play session (CLAUDE.md: read it rather than asking), and the launch overwrites
   them. Put them back after step 7. Then launch by launch-game step 5, with the task
   :neoforge-26.1.2:runClientTestWorld and the extra argument -PphotoShoot. Use
   -WindowStyle Normal: a minimized window renders nothing worth saving.
4. Watch run/logs/latest.log for "[photo] staged", "[photo] saved", "[photo] done",
   /ERROR] and Exception. The shoot starts 400 ticks after login and takes about four
   seconds a shot.
5. Look at every picture with Read before showing it. Say what each one actually shows,
   including what is too small or hidden to judge. A picture is not a pass.
6. Show them. If a file cannot be attached in this session, publish one private gallery
   artifact with the shots embedded as data URIs (artifact-design first), and give the
   link. The owner often reads it on a phone:
   - Show the whole frame. Do not crop or zoom in CSS; move the camera instead (stage()).
   - Give every grid that holds a picture grid-template-columns: minmax(0, 1fr), or the
     picture's natural 854px width forces the column wider than the screen.
   - After any scripted edit to the page's CSS, count its { and } before publishing. An
     unclosed @media block silently drops every rule after it.
   - To check phone width, wrap a copy in the publish skeleton's viewport meta and
     screenshot it in headless Chrome. On Windows it lays out at no less than 500px
     whatever --window-size says, so print innerWidth and scrollWidth into the page:
     equal means no sideways overflow.
7. Close the client by launch-game steps 2 and 4: read the log, then
   tools/stop-dev-java.ps1 (-WhatIf first).
8. Revert any SHOTS edit that only served this run, unless the owner wants it kept.

DONE WHEN
---------
Every shot is saved and described, the owner has the pictures, and no client this
procedure launched is still running.

NEVER
-----
- Never close a client the owner is playing in to take pictures.
- Never call a part verified because it looks right in a screenshot: placement in motion,
  foals and other gear are Verification-tab checks of their own.
- Never commit the screenshots: run/ is not tracked.
