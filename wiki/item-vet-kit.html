Item / breeding

Vet’s kit

Geld a stallion so he never breeds, or examine a horse: where a mare is in her heat, and whether she is carrying twins.

How to use it

Do thisOnWhat happens
Sneak + right-clickA stallion or colt you own He becomes a gelding, for good.
Right-clickA mare Where she is in her heat and when it ends, or how long her pregnancy has to go and whether it is twins. It also says if a stallion has already covered her this heat, and whether she is nursing.
Right-clickA stallion How many covers he has made today, and whether he is past the three that are at full odds — or that he is a gelding.

Every use costs one of its 64 uses. Make one from shears, leather and an iron ingot, or buy one from the equestrian scientist.

What gelding does

  • He never covers a mare in heat, and a seed jar cannot be filled from him. A jar filled before still works. He will give a foal if you stand there and feed him and a mare golden carrots: that path takes no notice of anything but age and sex (owner, 2026-09-25). Gelding still does what it is for - a cut stallion left in a paddock sires nothing, ever - it just is not proof against a player holding two carrots.
  • He stops acting like a stallion. He doesn’t spar, guard mares, lead a bachelor band or walk to a mare in heat, and keeps company the way mares do.
  • He bonds with you a quarter faster.
  • His info screen says Gelding, and his nameplate symbol is grey.
  • It stays with him if he dies and is summoned back, or is sold on a transfer paper.

It does not change his genes or his health. About half the males a cowboy sells are already geldings, and cost a little less. More on breeding on fertility & gestation.

Where it lives

  • ModItems.VET_KIT, a plain Item with durability(64). The model borrows vanilla’s bundle texture as a placeholder.
  • server/VetKitHandler handles PlayerInteractEvent.EntityInteract and cancels on both sides. Sneaking gelds: male, not already gelded, tamed, and HorseOwnership.isOwner, then HorseRecords.apply(horse, record.withGelded(true)), which syncs the record to tracking clients. A plain use prints ReproHandler.vetReport.
  • The flag is HorseRecord.gelded (codec field gelded), and HorseRecord.entire() is the stallion test. HorseRecord.sexLabel is the display word.
  • Recipe data/horsegenetics/recipe/vet_kit.json; trade villager_trade/equestrian_scientist/level_1/sell_vet_kit.json — 4 emeralds at Novice, three uses.

Gelding reaches the record (shown 2026-09-30)

Shown by the clockwork run in the debug test yard, as the last step of the SEED JAR clockwork pen in row AQ west (seed jars): the yard's fake-player hands sneak-use the kit on the jar stud, read his record's gelded flag, and hold out an empty jar. The first run with it logged CLOCKWORK PASS: “is now a gelding”, the record's gelded flag true, and the empty jar refused with “is a gelding - there is nothing to collect.” So the flag reaches HorseRecord, not just the chat line, and the jar handler reads it.

A gelding never covers (shown 2026-10-01)

A mare in heat penned with a gelding is never covered. The GELDING clockwork pen in row AU gelded a tamed stallion the real way (the yard's hands sneak-using the kit) and kept two mares in heat beside him for an hour, no carrots, with an entire stallion and two mares next door as the control. It logged CLOCKWORK PASS at 00:53 on 2026-10-02: “0 pregnancies and 0 foals in the gelding pen; 720 looks of 720 found a mare who could still be covered this heat within 3.0 blocks of him”, against 48 pregnancies in the control pen. So the cover path is asked and says no; it is not that the pair never met.

The icon, since 2026-09-29

The kit used to be vanilla’s bundle, hue-shifted red — a brown leather pouch tinted, which read as a red bag rather than anything medical. It is now a licensed icon: a stoppered flask with a paper tag, from Raven Fantasy HD by Caio Carlos of Clockwork Raven Studios. Its recipe has been removed from bake-item-recolours.mjs, because that tool writes straight over textures/item/ and a recipe left behind would have repainted the new icon on the next re-bake.

This file is not covered by this mod’s licence. The pack permits use in a free mod on the condition that players are told they may not take the assets on, so the MIT and CC BY-SA grants are carved back for this sprite specifically. The wording is in THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md, which ships inside the jar.
