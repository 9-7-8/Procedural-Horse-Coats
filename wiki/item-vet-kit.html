Item / breeding

Vet’s kit

Geld a stallion so he never breeds, or examine a horse: where a mare is in her heat, and whether she is carrying twins.

How to use it

Do thisOnWhat happens
Sneak + right-clickA stallion or colt you own He becomes a gelding, for good.
Right-clickA mare Where she is in her heat and when it ends, or how long her pregnancy has to go and whether it is twins. It also says if a stallion has already covered her this heat, and whether she is nursing.
Right-clickA stallion How many of his three covers for today he has used, or that he is a gelding.

Every use costs one of its 64 uses. Make one from shears, leather and an iron ingot, or buy one from the equestrian scientist.

What gelding does

  • He never breeds: not with a mare in heat, not with golden carrots, and a seed jar cannot be filled from him. A jar filled before still works.
  • He stops acting like a stallion. He doesn’t spar, guard mares, lead a bachelor band or walk to a mare in heat, and keeps company the way mares do.
  • He bonds with you a quarter faster.
  • His info screen says Gelding, and his nameplate symbol is grey.
  • It stays with him if he dies and is summoned back, or is sold on a transfer paper.

It does not change his genes or his health. About half the males a cowboy sells are already geldings, and cost a little less. More on breeding on fertility & gestation.

Where it lives

  • ModItems.VET_KIT, a plain Item with durability(64). The model borrows vanilla’s bundle texture as a placeholder.
  • server/VetKitHandler handles PlayerInteractEvent.EntityInteract and cancels on both sides. Sneaking gelds: male, not already gelded, tamed, and HorseOwnership.isOwner, then HorseRecords.apply(horse, record.withGelded(true)), which syncs the record to tracking clients. A plain use prints ReproHandler.vetReport.
  • The flag is HorseRecord.gelded (codec field gelded), and HorseRecord.entire() is the stallion test. HorseRecord.sexLabel is the display word.
  • Recipe data/horsegenetics/recipe/vet_kit.json; trade villager_trade/equestrian_scientist/level_1/sell_vet_kit.json — 4 emeralds at Novice, three uses.

Known gaps
