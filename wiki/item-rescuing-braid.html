Rescuing braid

A one-use piece of mane or tail gear that turns a killing blow into a trip home instead of just a reprieve: the moment it would break, the horse (and its rider, if mounted) blinks back to a bound stall and comes up already fleeing. None of it is built. This page exists so the plan has its own home rather than a paragraph in the intake to-do list.

Nothing on this page exists in the game

Everything below is a plan. When a piece of it ships, it comes off this Roadmap tab and is written up on a Gameplay and a Coding tab here instead — the page grows tabs as the thing grows, the way every other gear page on the wiki does.

What is still planned here

A mane-or-tail item that binds to a horse’s stall (or a holding pen), rendering as a dyed streak in the hair, and that consumes itself the instant the horse’s last-stand immunity would trigger — teleporting the horse and any rider to the bound stall and dropping the horse straight into its escape behaviour, rather than leaving it standing where it was hit.

What it is

A rescuing braid is worn in the mane or tail slot of the nineteen-slot gear roster, and does one thing: it is a second, stronger save layered on top of the last-stand immunity every horse already gets for free. Where last stand only buys six seconds standing in place, a worn braid spends itself to move the horse (and rider) somewhere safer at the same instant — its own bound stall.

  • It needs a stall first. The braid has to be bound to one of the horse’s own stall signs, or to its owner’s holding pen — the same two destinations a ticket already sends a horse to. A horse with neither cannot wear one, the same way it cannot be sent home by ticket.
  • It triggers with last stand, not instead of it. The moment a blow would take the horse to zero and last-stand immunity would normally hold it at half a heart in place, a worn braid instead breaks, and the horse (and its rider, if one is aboard) is teleported to the bound stall and enters the same fleeing behaviour low-health escape already gives a horse that survives a killing blow.
  • One use. The braid is destroyed the instant it fires. A horse saved this way needs a fresh braid before it can be saved this way again — exactly the same shape as last stand needing to heal back to full before it will save the horse a second time.
  • Dyed wool decides its colour. It renders as a streak worked into the mane or tail, tinted to whatever wool went into it, purely cosmetic on top of a mechanical item — the same split tack dyeing already draws between what an item does and how it looks.
  • Craftable from wool, horse hair and an ender pearl. The wool supplies the colour, the horse hair ties it to the rest of the item roster’s one shared material, and the ender pearl is the same “this reaches somewhere else” ingredient the bound ticket already uses for leaving a stall behind.
  • Dyeable after crafting, at the Tack Dyeing Bench, the same as a saddle’s seat or a bridle’s leather — so a braid crafted from the wrong-coloured wool is not a wasted ender pearl.

Open questions

  • Rider handling. A rider aboard at the moment of the save has to travel with the horse rather than being left behind or unhorsed mid-teleport — the same problem bolting already solves by keeping the rider mounted through the whole event, so the braid should reuse that path rather than invent a second one.
  • Order against last stand. Whether the braid replaces the six-second immunity window entirely or rides on top of it (immune, teleported, then fleeing) is a design call to make before the trigger hook is written, not after.
  • Binding UX. Whether binding a braid to a stall is a right-click on the stall sign (mirroring how a blank stall sign itself binds), or a right-click on the horse while standing in its stall, needs settling against the existing stall-sign interaction pattern.
  • Which slot wins if both are worn. A horse has both a mane and a tail slot; whether a braid in each is two independent saves or the second is refused while one is already armed needs a rule before both slots can accept the item.

Anticipated files

Nothing here is settled, but the shape follows the existing stall/ticket/last-stand machinery rather than inventing a parallel one:

  • An item class beside TicketItem.java, holding a bound stall reference the same way a ticket resolves one at use time rather than at craft time.
  • A hook into whatever fires common/care/LastStand’s immunity window, so the braid can intercept the same event rather than polling health every tick.
  • A tag entry in the mane and tail slots’ item tags (horsegenetics:gear/mane, horsegenetics:gear/tail), per how every gear slot decides what it accepts.
  • A recipe under data/horsegenetics/recipe/: wool (any colour, by tag) + horse hair + ender pearl, picked up automatically by bake-recipe-reference.mjs.
