Gameplay / items

Items, recipes & the hair economy

Every item the mod registers, how you get it, and what it does. One Horse Genetics creative tab holds all of them (ModItems.TAB_ITEMS), and nearly all are built on one material — horse hair, which you get by shearing a horse. This page is the source of truth for the item roster and the recipes; the systems the items plug into are documented on their own pages and linked from each row.

House rules for every recipe here
  • Shapeless, with exactly one exception: the blank transfer paper is a ring of eight paper around a horse hair, and the ring is the idea. Everywhere else the shapes are not the puzzle.
  • At least one modded ingredient in every recipe, and every output is a modded item, so nothing can collide with a vanilla or third-party recipe.
  • The material chain is reversible — a bundle goes back to four hairs, a rope back to three bundles — so nothing is a trap.

The material chain

Horse hair is the floor of the whole economy. Every other recipe on this page takes hair, a hair product, or something crafted from one, which is what makes shearing worth doing more than once.

Shearing

Right-click an adult horse with shears (server/HorseShearHandler): 1–3 horse_hair drop, the shears take a point of durability, and the horse gains +5 bond through HorseCareHandler.awardBondFor — so shearing is the one interaction that is both an economy input and a relationship input (horse care).

It is gated to once per 24 000-tick Minecraft day, which is the mod-wide rule for every time-gated action. The gate is a stored game-time stamp under the key "shear" on data/HorseCooldownsAttachment — a plain Map<String, Long> on the horse, shared with the yield verb’s per-gene cooldowns ("yield:<geneKey>"). Because it is stored on the horse rather than in a static map, it survives a reload and cannot leak between worlds.

A sheared horse does not look sheared

The mechanic is complete; the visual is not built. It is settled as a low-fidelity render-layer overlay — it only has to read as vaguely different, so no coat variant and no extra bake — driven off the same "shear" cooldown stamp. See the roadmap.

Breeding carrots

Five carrot items. Four are crafted from a golden carrot, horse hair and one signature reagent; the fifth is parameterised by a research paper. What each one does to a foal is on the carrot family — this table is the recipes only.

Research papers

research_paper carries a research_gene data component naming one gene. Right-click it to add that gene to your gene database and unlock its carrot line; the tooltip shows the gene’s name, rarity tier and blurb.

  • Chest loot — injected into ~8 vanilla chest tables by a global loot modifier, with the gene weighted by its rarity tier (data/loot/AddResearchPaperModifier).
  • Written by hand — in the Horse Browser (H), pick a gene you have already discovered and put a single book in the crafting grid. One book → one paper for that gene. There is no JSON recipe for this; it is menu/HorseBrowserRecipes, because the result depends on which gene is selected in the screen.
  • Traded from another player — which is the point of the second route: knowledge is shareable rather than hoardable.

Whistles

Right-click anywhere: every tamed horse you own within range, in the same dimension, not currently ridden, teleports to a grid of spots beside you. A leashed horse is unleashed and comes; a horse already within 3 blocks, or the one you are riding, is left alone. A chime plays and a chat line counts what answered. ~3 second use cooldown.

Stall signs

A two-step item. Right-click a horse with a blank stall_sign and it becomes a bound_stall_sign carrying that horse’s UUID and name in a bound_horse data component. Right-click the outside face of a wall with the bound sign and three things happen at once (item/StallSignItem):

  1. A real oak_wall_sign is placed, with the horse’s name written on it.
  2. server/StallDetector flood-fills the air behind that wall — this layer ±1, air only, capped at 512 blocks. An enclosed area becomes that horse’s stall.
  3. The stall is stored in data/StallData, a server-global SavedData of StallRecords, one per bound horse, and its outline is flashed with HAPPY_VILLAGER particles.

Breaking the sign block releases the stall. In a dev build, F7 re-flashes the outline of every stall near you and prints a summary (server/StallDebug).

Definition and storage only

Nothing consumes a stall yet. There is no teleport-to-stall, no auto-return, no “assigned pen” behaviour — the tickets are the intended consumer and they are inert. Cleanup is thin too: only breaking the exact sign block releases a stall, so rebuilding the wall elsewhere or removing the horse leaves a stale record.

Tickets — registered, inert

Four tiers craft correctly and do nothing. The stated intent is “teleport a bound horse to its stall”, which is now possible (StallRecord.center() exists) and simply unbuilt. All four share one texture.

Transfer papers

How a horse changes hands. Full mechanics on Villagers & transfer papers; this is the item roster.

ItemRecipeWhat it carries
Blank transfer paper Shaped: 8 × paper around 1 × horse hair paper_bearer — the crafter, stamped on at the bench
Signed transfer paper Right-click a horse you own with a blank; or buy one from the cowboy horse_deed — the horse's id, name, breed, breeder, and its genetic and epigenome codes, which are what let the item draw as the horse (below)
  • One blank per craft. Eight paper and a hair is a deliberate act, not a stack you carry around.
  • A signed paper is drawn as the horse it names, not as a paper: the mod's HD horse mesh in that horse's own generated coat, shrunk into the slot (client/TransferDeedRenderer). It is the same picture in the cowboy's merchant window, in your inventory, in your hand and on the ground. The point is matching a paper to an animal: a stall of six papers is six identical pictures with different names in the tooltip, and walking out to collect one meant reading a name and then reading names off horses. A dun in the slot and a dun in the paddock is one glance. It works off the deed's own snapshot of the genome, so a paper for a horse that is unloaded, sold on twice or dead still draws the animal it was written for.
  • A signed paper never stacks. Two of them are never the same item, and a stack could not show you which horse is which. Its name is the horse's name, so a cowboy's merchant window reads as a list of horses rather than a list of identical papers.
  • The blank is bound; the signed one is not. Only the bound player can sign, and only against a horse they currently own. After that it is a bearer instrument and anyone may redeem it.

Horse Trader's Post

The horseman's workstation, and the only block on this page. 3 hair cloth + 2 braided rope + 2 fence (shapeless; any #minecraft:fences). Placing one near an unemployed villager gets you a horseman; one villager per post.

It is a plain block with no behaviour of its own — everything happens because it backs the horsegenetics:horse_traders_post POI. Its texture is vanilla's barrel, as a placeholder.

The custom horse spawn egg

Creative only. A reskin of the vanilla horse spawn egg — same icon, and it shows in the vanilla Spawn Eggs tab as well as this mod’s. It has no recipe and it does not spawn anything directly: right-clicking it opens client/CustomHorseSpawnScreen, a full genome editor with a gene list down the left, a live orbitable 3D horse in the middle, and controls on the right.

  • Every registered gene is a row, alphabetically by display name. A gene starts off the horse; clicking the row adds it, homozygous for the first allele that does something, and only then does it grow allele buttons. Genes with more than three alleles get a dropdown instead of a cycle button.
  • Reroll epi. re-rolls the epigenome and the preview redraws, which is the only surface anywhere that makes per-allele epigenetics visible — bay leg heights, grey dapples, splash edges.
  • Breed picks one of the 49 breeds and rolls a real founder of it into the editor; Randomize rolls inside the selected breed’s pools, or unconstrained with no breed.
  • Copy code / Paste code round-trip the genotype code through the clipboard, which turns a bug report into something reproducible.
  • The epigenome travels with the genotype to the server, so the horse that spawns is the horse you were looking at. The server re-checks creative mode and that you are holding the egg.

It is the fastest way to test any unplayed system: adding a health gene defaults it to the affected genotype, so “add PLOD1, pick Foal, spawn” is a one-click lethal foal.

The creative tab

item/ModCreativeTabs registers one Horse Genetics tab holding every item in ModItems.TAB_ITEMS. The custom spawn egg additionally appears in vanilla’s Spawn Eggs tab. In a dev build, the Spawn Test Horse World button’s starting inventory contains one of every item.

Known gaps

  • Art. The four tickets share one texture, the three whistles share one, both stall signs borrow minecraft:item/oak_sign, and braided_rope / hair_cloth have recipes but no real textures.
  • The tickets do nothing, and the stall system they would consume has no other consumer either.
  • The sheared look is unbuilt (above).
  • Art. The blank transfer paper reuses the research-paper texture and the Horse Trader's Post wears vanilla's barrel. The signed paper no longer needs art at all — it draws as the horse — but its sprite is still what the game throws as particles when one breaks.
  • There is no double-wide gate block, which the design document lists as a braided-rope product.
  • The known_gene_splice_carrot is one generic item parameterised by a component, so every gene’s carrot looks the same in the inventory.
