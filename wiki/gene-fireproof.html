Magical gene / no coat effect

Fireproof

A horse that does not burn, and neither do you while you are on it. It swims through lava rather than walking on top of it — submerged, slow, and entirely unbothered.

Half seen: immunity yes, the rest no

Horse and rider surviving fire was owner-confirmed in the 2026-09-10 sessions. What has not been watched is what could not be judged from the saddle: that the lava speed is inherited rather than flat, that a carrier still burns, and that LavaTravelMixin did not quietly speed up every other entity in the game. Those are on the Verification tab.

Lava is a route, not a wall

The horse sinks into lava and swims through it the way an ordinary horse swims through water: slowly, at the surface, with its rider safe. That is the whole reason to breed one. A lava lake stops being a detour.

Health

Strictly an improvement. It costs nothing and removes an entire category of death. It is homozygous recessive and absent from wild horses as a homozygote, so you will be breeding for it rather than finding it.

Immunity is a damage cancel, and it stores nothing

GeneAbilityHandler.onTraversalDamage cancels any LivingIncomingDamageEvent whose source carries the minecraft:is_fire damage-type tag - so lava, fire blocks, burning and other mods’ heat sources alike - when it lands on a horse expressing the gene, or on the player who is its first passenger at the moment of the hit. Nothing is written onto the player, so there is nothing to take off on dismount and no invulnerability to duplicate: step off and the next hit is an ordinary one.

The first version did nothing, and the reason is worth keeping. It called clearFire() on the tick, which puts out the burning but not the damage: lava and fire blocks hurt directly, and vanilla’s own immunity is a flag on the entity type, which a gene cannot set per horse. The owner rode one into lava on 2026-09-10 and neither of them was protected.

Swimming, not walking

Walking on lava is the cheaper build — the walk_on_water branch already does the buoyancy trick and pointing it at lava is a few lines. It was rejected because a horse trotting across a lava lake reads as a bug in someone else’s mod, whereas a horse wading through it reads as a horse that belongs there. The extra cost is a fire-damage cancel for the rider and a movement speed in lava.

How it floats. The first build only slowed the sink, from the server, and in play the horse simply sank: a horse under a rider is simulated by the rider’s client, and the server’s velocity changes never reach it. Vanilla lava movement pulls down by a quarter of the horse’s GRAVITY attribute each tick, and attributes do sync to the client, so lava_swim holds a transient gravity modifier of -0.16 (net -0.08) while the lava is above the horse’s fluid-jump threshold. That settles into a slow rise, and dropping it at the surface makes the horse bob there - the float vanilla gives a ridden horse in water and not in lava. The speed is no longer vanilla lava’s fixed crawl: travelInLava reads no attribute, so mixin/LavaTravelMixin replaces its hardcoded 0.02 acceleration with the horse’s own lava-speed attribute, set from each copy’s lavaSpeed between FireproofGene.MIN_LAVA_SPEED (five times vanilla, near a walk) and MAX_LAVA_SPEED (twenty times). Every other entity keeps vanilla’s value. Standing on lava like a strider is still not done: that is canStandOnFluid, an entity method with no event.

One shared change, three genes

The target: self | rider | both parameter on traversal is wanted by this gene, by bird boned and by ocean-born. Build it once, with the removal path included, and all three are safe.

Verified

The rider is immune too, dismount included (owner-confirmed in the 2026-09-10 play sessions).

A ridden horse crosses lava fast (owner, in the yard's lava channel, 2026-09-13): “lava is fast now!” (session log). This was the first thing LavaTravelMixin, the project's first mixin, delivered; gap 179 and the channel pen closed with it. Not yet seen: breeding a faster swimmer from two parents, which is what the epigenetic range is for.

The controls hold, and an unridden horse floats (test yard, pen FIREPROOF, the run of 2026-10-02 03:52 on a56afcbb, five clockwork PASS lines). A carrier burns: over 100 ticks in fire an Frp/n horse went 22 → 12 while an Frp/Frp beside it lost nothing (03:52:51; horse only, no rider). Only fire is cancelled: a generic hit (standing in for the sword) and a fall of 4.0 each took exactly 4.000 off an Frp/Frp horse and off an n/n control (03:52:49). The mixin is per horse: driven with the same input down sealed lava lanes, a plain horse (lava_movement 0.0200) and a pig (no attribute) each crawled 1.0 block in 200 ticks while the Frp/Frp horse (0.1244) swam 10.8 (03:52:57). Unridden, it floats: on a 4-deep pool its feet stayed between −0.54 and +0.13 of the surface over 30 samples, and with its AI goals stripped the buoyancy alone held it between −0.52 and −0.30 (03:53:23).

Why it is not two loci

Fire immunity and lava swimming could have been separate genes, the way swimming and breathing are separate on the water side. They are one here because lava immunity without lava swimming is a horse that stands in fire unharmed and can do nothing with it — there is no interesting horse in between, so there is no reason to make a breeder find two.

What needs verifying here

Fireproof and its controls are confirmed, so what is left is that the lava speed is inherited and visibly varies, that a carrier’s rider still burns, and that no wild horse is born fireproof.

Still open (NOT played)

The immunity, the crossing speed and the yard’s controls are confirmed (§Verified). What is below is the rider half of one control, the founder table, and the epigenetic range, which is the only reason to breed two of these together and has never been read.

Nothing here needs looking at to judge — do these first

  • A carrier’s rider burns. The carrier horse itself burns (§Verified); the yard had no rider. Stand a Frp/n horse in fire with you aboard. Pass: you take fire damage on the usual schedule — the rider’s immunity must be keyed on the expressing horse too.
  • The speed is inherited and drifts. Breed two expressing horses whose lava_movement readouts differ. Pass: the foal’s attribute sits inside FireproofGene.MIN_LAVA_SPEED–MAX_LAVA_SPEED, is not vanilla’s value, and is not identical to either parent. This is the one thing the §lava-speed record explicitly leaves open.
  • No wild horse is fireproof. The founder table is carriers-only. Pass: you meet carriers and never a wild horse that swims lava — the first one is bred. Note the contradiction if it fails the other way: FireproofGene’s own comment on the speed floor talks about “a wild fireproof horse caught in the world”, which the carriers-only table says cannot exist.

In the game, with your hands

  • A slow one and a fast one look different. Two expressing horses at either end of the range, across the same lava. Pass: the difference is large enough to notice without timing it, which is what makes the epigenetic range worth breeding for rather than a number on a page.
