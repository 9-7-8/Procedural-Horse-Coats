Every field — the belly patch, the body roaning, the leg-edge jitter — is a warped fractal from PatchNoise. The first version sampled raw low-frequency BodyNoise.value, whose integer lattice showed through as axis-aligned squares.

Each leg’s white edge is jittered per-texel so the stockings end in a ragged line rather than the clean ring splash’s whitenLowerLeg leaves — the fix that CoatRegions still owes splash is done here in the gene.
