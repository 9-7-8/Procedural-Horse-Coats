Horse behaviour hierarchy

Every AI goal a horse runs, vanilla’s and this mod’s, stacked by priority — so you can see which behaviours can interrupt which, where two of them are fighting over the same thing, and where a new one of yours belongs.

Generated

Everything between the markers below is written by wiki/tools/bake-behaviour-hierarchy.mjs, straight from the source: the mod’s addGoal calls with their priority constants resolved, each goal’s flags from its own setFlags, and vanilla’s horse goals from the patched sources jar. Re-run it after adding, removing or re-prioritising a goal; never edit the list by hand.

How to read it

  • Lower numbers win. Every tick a horse’s goal selector offers each goal the chance to start, in priority order.
  • Flags are what goals compete for. MOVE, LOOK and JUMP can each be held by one running goal at a time. A goal that wants a flag held by a higher-numbered goal interrupts it; one that wants a flag held by an equal or lower number waits.
  • Equal priority never interrupts. Two goals at one number that claim the same flag take turns by whichever started first — and when both want to start on the same tick, the one registered first. The page marks every such pair. Most are fine (they never want to run at once); a pair that does is a bug waiting to be reported as “the horse ignored X”.
  • A goal with no flags blocks nothing and is blocked by nothing.
  • Most of this mod’s goals are on every horse and decide inside canUse whether they apply — a horse that is not sun-sensitive carries the shade goal and it never starts. That keeps a gene’s behaviour working for a wild horse whose record is written a tick after it joins.

Adding a behaviour of your own

  1. Pick the priority by what it must interrupt. Read down the list for the goals that should not run while yours does and sit above them — and below anything that must win over you: the inspect hold (0), panic and sun shade (1), fighting (3). Wandering is 6 and above; a behaviour a horse does “when nothing else is going on” belongs there.
  2. Claim only the flags you use. A goal that only turns the head needs LOOK; claiming MOVE too will quietly stop the horse following its band or its owner while yours runs.
  3. Add it once, on join. Listen for EntityJoinLevelEvent on the server, check the horse does not already have your goal (a reloaded chunk fires the event again), and add it: 
    if (event.getEntity() instanceof Horse horse && !event.getLevel().isClientSide()) {
    for (WrappedGoal w : horse.goalSelector.getAvailableGoals()) {
        if (w.getGoal() instanceof MyGoal) return;
    }
    horse.goalSelector.addGoal(5, new MyGoal(horse));
}
  4. Keep canUse cheap. It runs every tick for every horse, and the horse dimension has hundreds. Throttle entity and block scans with a cooldown, and cache anything read off the genome — see SunShadeGoal.
  5. Stand down for the inspect hold and for riders. Check horse.isVehicle() and horse.isLeashed(); the hold at priority 0 takes care of itself.
  6. Re-run the bake if your goal is in this repository, and look for your name in a conflict line.
