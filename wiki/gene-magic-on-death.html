Magical gene / no coat effect

Magic on death

What happens to the world where the horse died — a lava source or a water source. It says nothing at all about items; that is magic item drop, and the two are kept apart on purpose.

That last row is not a shrug. The two variants are answers to one question — what happens here — and a horse carrying one of each is asking for two contradictory things. Rather than pick a winner, the locus says the pair is silent.

There was a third one, and it exploded

Xpl/Xpl made the horse go off like a creeper when it died. It was retired on 2026-09-24, on the owner’s call, after the locus had been run on a heavily modded server: the half of this gene worth having is the water source, and an explosion whose carrier looks like every other horse is a stable somebody loses to a pairing they did not know they had made. A horse or a splice carrot that still carries Xpl is covered under Coding — in short, the locus comes back at n/n and the carrot does nothing.

No feral horse floods its own field

This is the one locus in the mod whose founder table is carriers only. Wild horses carry the alleles — a few percent each — and no wild horse is ever born with two of anything, so a feral animal never leaves a lava spring where it fell.

Every other magical gene here does the opposite, and deliberately: a locus whose carrier is invisible puts its expressing combinations in the wild, so that what you catch is what you watched it do. This one is a hazard rather than a prize, and a horse that fills its own stall with lava has to be somebody’s fault.

Health

None while alive. A Lav/Lav horse is an ordinary horse right up until it stops being one — and then there is a lava source where it was standing, which is a problem for whatever is standing next to it, the floor, and anything it drops.

The volatile allele, and what a horse still carrying it does now

Xpl was deleted on 2026-09-24 (owner, from the modded server: “remove the explode allele entirely — a doubled pair should resolve to wild type instead”). It had been verified working nine days earlier: an Xpl/Xpl horse that was also Dia/Dia logged [watch] EXPLOSION | 224 blocks, 1 entities, blew out part of its pen and dropped 5 diamonds and no leather, with a witness horse 6.2 blocks away unhurt. It was removed because it worked, not because it did not.

Gone with it: the explode choice on the on_death verb — in AbilityType, in the creator’s schema.js, and in the handler’s switch — so no data-driven gene can ask for one either, and GeneDeathHandler.EXPLOSION_RADIUS. The yard’s DEATH BOOM pen is now DEATH DIAMONDS, which keeps the Dia half nothing else watches and drops the blast, the witness and the rebuild-the-pen-afterwards step.

There is no migration, and none is needed. Three things could be holding an Xpl:

  • A saved horse. Its genotype code goes through Genotype.readableStored, which drops a segment naming an allele this build does not have and logs [genes] a saved horse carried '…', which this version cannot read - that locus is back at its default. The horse loads as n/n here and is otherwise untouched — the same route Cleave’s retired Clv took in 0.5.000.
  • A splice carrot. The carrot stores known:horsegenetics.magic_on_death:Xpl:Xpl as text and resolves it at breeding time through alleleOrNull, never fromToken, precisely so a retired allele is inert rather than fatal. The carrot is spent and nothing happens, which KnownGeneSpliceTest pins.
  • A gene database entry. A collected horsegenetics.magic_on_death|Xpl id simply stops matching anything, and the locus’s “alleles collected” total drops by one. Nothing cleans the dead id out of that set; it is a string in NBT that no longer names anything.

Why the ground and the drops are two verbs

Because they are two questions with two answers, and folding them into one would make “explodes and drops diamonds” impossible to express as the two independent loci it is. As two verbs on two genes it is a cross; as one table it would have been a row somebody had to remember to write.

They are nonetheless handled in one translator class, because they share exactly one thing — the moment — and nothing else, including which event they can run on. LivingDropsEvent can rewrite the drop list and cannot usefully place a block; LivingDeathEvent is the other way round.

The fluids only fill a free block

A lava or water source is placed only into air, or into something a fluid would wash away anyway. The alternative reading — replace whatever is there — turns a novelty locus into a griefing tool that nobody can see coming, since the carrier looks like any other horse.

neoforge/server/GeneDeathHandler.java
var existing = level.getBlockState(at);
if (existing.isAir() || existing.canBeReplaced()
        || existing.getFluidState().getType() != Fluids.EMPTY) {
    level.setBlockAndUpdate(at, state);
}

This used to switch itself off in the horse dimension, and that was the reason it appeared to do nothing. The guard was defensible when written — a horse dimension full of lava springs eats itself — but the yard now has a pen whose entire purpose is killing horses to see what they leave behind, floored in stone for exactly that. It was removed on 2026-09-13 after the report “drops a sword on death did not drop anything”, from a pen built that morning which could not have worked for one second. It was the seventh protection in that dimension to turn out to be the reason a gene did nothing.

The exception that proves the founder rule

The standing rule for a magical locus with an invisible carrier is that the wild population sits on the combinations that show. The argument is a good one: once a carrier is invisible, a founder population full of carriers is a locus run in the dark — you cannot see what a horse has, so you cannot choose what to pair it with, and every result is an accident.

That argument assumes the outcome is something you would want to find. Here it is not. A wild population of horses that flood or light the ground they die on would mean a player who has never heard of this gene loses something to it, and the discovery would be an accident in the bad direction. So this locus runs the health loci’s rule instead — carriers only, never affected — and the interesting horse is one you assembled from two ordinary-looking parents.

The useful one

Water is on the same locus as lava, which makes the whole thing a gamble worth taking: two recessives sharing a locus means a breeder chasing an infinite water source is also breeding, in the same herd, the thing that burns the barn down.

Why the third one went

The explosion was the strongest version of that gamble and it was cut anyway (2026-09-24), which is worth being honest about: it was not cut for being broken, or unbalanced, or hard to breed. It was cut because of where the mod is actually played. On a server with other people’s builds in blast range, a hazard whose carrier is invisible stops being a gamble the breeder took and becomes one their neighbour took without being asked. Lava is the same shape of hazard held to one block, and one block is the difference.

The general rule it leaves behind: a magical locus may cost the horse anything, and may cost the breeder whatever they walked into, but an outcome that reaches somebody who never touched the gene has to be small enough to stand next to.

What needs verifying here

Both of magic on death’s deaths are confirmed on a built floor, which leaves the mixed pair — a Lav/Wat horse must do nothing at all — the founder rule that no feral horse can ever leave a lava spring, and, since 2026-09-24, that a saved horse carrying the retired Xpl loads at n/n instead of taking the world down.

Still open (NOT played)

The yard’s row AD kills one horse of each expressing kind and logs what it left, so the remaining questions are the row that is supposed to do nothing, the founder rule, and the one thing the retirement introduced.

  • No feral horse carries two copies. This is the one locus whose founder table is carriers only. Pass: across a large wild sample every on-death allele you find sits opposite an n — a single wild Lav/Lav horse is a failure of the founder table, not bad luck, and the first evidence of it would be a burnt pasture.
