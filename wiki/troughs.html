Troughs and salt licks

From Animania, and asked for specifically as a trough built out of the existing modular-wood framework, in any square or rectangular shape.

No block of this mod feeds a horse today. Feeding is grazing and hand-feeding — HungerFoodGoal walks a horse about ten blocks to the best thing it can reach, with a hay bale as the largest meal — so a trough is the first place a horse eats from, and that is the feature rather than the block.

  • The wood half is genuinely reusable and the paint half is not. compat/MaterialScan and ModdedMaterials.woods() already serve double gates, jumps and carts from one roster, so “a trough in any wood, vanilla or modded” is an existing pattern with three precedents. But there is no shared paint system — jumps, tack and generated armour each tint their own way, and two of them duplicate the same dye-reading lines with a source comment saying “if a third place needs dyes, extract it then”. A trough plus the cart and jump bench would be the third and fourth places. That extraction is now due.
  • Multi-block in any rectangle is the genuinely new part. Nothing in the mod assembles a structure from several placed blocks; the nearest thing is StallDetector, which recognises an enclosure the player built rather than forming one. Worth reading it first — the answer to “what counts as a valid shape, and what happens when someone breaks one block of it” is already worked out there.
  • Salt licks were asked for alongside. They have no mechanism here at all — a block a horse walks to and uses over time, which is closer to care than to feeding. Keep it carrot-not-stick: a salt lick that grants something is in keeping; a horse that suffers without one is the husbandry-chore version and is exactly what the owner's note warns against when it says to hold Animania's ideas to this mod's philosophy.
  • Any mod's trough-alike should work, the way compat/HayBales made every mod's hay bale grazeable with a tag rather than a list. Same trick, same reason.
