Tickets

Four tiers of ticket that craft correctly and, at the moment, do nothing at all.

These do not work yet

All four tickets can be crafted and none of them has any effect. They are meant to teleport a horse to its stall, and that is simply unbuilt. Craft them if you like collecting things; there is nothing to use them on.
