Dun

Real-horse TBX3. Two effects at once: a mild, hue-keeping body dilution, and primitive markings — a full-length dorsal stripe and faint horizontal leg barring — that read dark because everything around them got lighter, not because pigment was added.

How the markings survive the dilution

Phase 1 is downward-only — a gene can never add pigment back. So dun computes, per texel a marking strength and lerps its dilution off where a marking is: a dorsal-stripe texel skips the dilution entirely and keeps the base colour, a leg-bar texel keeps extra black only.

keepRed is not a constant. A black horse still carries red = 1 (its blackness is all eumelanin), so keeping any of that red lands grullo in the gradient’s warm browns instead of on its neutral column. So keepRed is interpolated by the texel’s black content: a chestnut keeps almost all its red and comes out a pale red dun, a black one loses nearly all of it and comes out a mouse-grey grullo, a bay body sits between. A leg bar keeps black rather than re-introducing red, or a bar on a black leg reads as a warm patch, not a dark band.

common/genetics/genes/DunGene.java
float keepRed   = lerp(KEEP_RED_CHESTNUT, KEEP_RED_BLACK, b);   // b = this texel's black
float keepBlack = KEEP_BLACK;

float dorsal = (float) CoatRegions.dorsalStripe(skin, part, point, DORSAL_HALF_WIDTH);
keepRed   = lerp(keepRed,   1f, dorsal);   // dorsal: skip the dilution, keep the base
keepBlack = lerp(keepBlack, 1f, dorsal);

if (isLeg(part)) {
    float bar = (float) CoatRegions.legBar(skin, part, point, BAR_SPACING, BAR_DUTY, BAR_REACH);
    keepBlack = Math.max(keepBlack, lerp(KEEP_BLACK, BAR_KEEP_BLACK, bar));   // black only
}
f.setRed(px, py, r * keepRed);
f.setBlack(px, py, b * keepBlack);

Simplification

The real locus has three alleles — D (full dun), d1 (non-dun but still marked), d2 (no dun, no markings) — whose relationship (D > d1 = d2 for dilution, but only d1 carries markings) the old per-gene dominance label could not express. The combination table can: three alleles, six combinations, an outcome per row. So this is still the two-allele form, but the third allele is now a gene rewrite rather than a framework change — a roadmap item. Leg barring is also a hand-rolled Y-phase rather than a reuse of BodyStripes (which runs on X).
