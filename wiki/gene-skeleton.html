Magical gene / recessive / one of the two undeath loci

Skeleton

Two copies and the horse is its own bones. Everything that is not skeleton is cut away, the bone shows through the horse’s coat, and whatever colour the horse carries is the colour of its bones. It never drowns, and it counts as undead. One copy is a carrier that looks and acts like any other horse. No wild horse has it: every one comes from a vanilla skeleton horse that converted into a Great Valley Skeleton Horse, or from breeding them.

Crossing two of them

What two copies do

  • The look. Vanilla’s skeleton horse, laid over the horse’s own coat: its holes cut the horse away, its bone colour shows through, and its shading is put back on top. A white horse makes clean white bones; a dun, yellowed old ones; a spotted horse, spotted bones.
  • Black eyes, whatever its other genes would have given it.
  • It never drowns — the horse, not its rider.
  • It is undead to anything that asks: cleansing light, a holy ward, an aura that names the undead.

Everything else a skeleton horse is known for — eating bones, turning on its rider after dark until it is given one, dropping bones when it dies, the soul fire at its hooves, its speed — belongs to the breed, not to this gene. A skeleton foal of two ordinary parents is bones with an ordinary horse’s appetite and temper.

One copy

Nothing shows and nothing changes. A carrier is not undead for any purpose. Two carriers bred together have a one in four chance of a skeleton foal.

Is it good for the horse?

It has no health effect of its own. The horse cannot drown, which is all it gains.

The sheet pass

The gene implements CoatSheetContribution with pass SHAPE, which CoatTextureComposer.bake runs as step 6, after the overlay phase. For each texel, with S the converted skeleton sheet (horse_skeleton.png / _baby, keyed skeleton_adult / skeleton_baby) and C the finished coat: S’s alpha replaces C’s (binary, so the model’s own ENTITY_CUTOUT_NO_CULL discards the holes); the colour is lerp(S, C, coat_opacity × C.a); and it is multiplied by lerp(1, L, shade_strength), L being S’s luminance over its brightest. The eye texels keep C. It runs after the overlay because the bleached strain is locked white by extreme white dominant, and the lock refuses every overlay write; the cut-outs are not a marking. A host that has not loaded the sheet (a test’s synthetic set) skips the pass and bakes the plain coat.

Black eyes

AbstractUndeathGene is an EyeRequestContribution: an expressing horse requests Blk at both irises and both sclerae and the wild sector at both eyes. The undeath loci sort after every white locus, so the request wins over a white’s blue eye or blue wedge (a skeleton with a hidden splash carrier would otherwise have one).

Undead, for the rest of the mod

common/genetics/Undeath.kindOf(Genotype) is the one answer; the game asks it through server/UndeadHorses (cached per genetic code), and MobGroups’s undead group adds it to the entity-type tag. mixin/HorseUndeadVoiceMixin gives an expressing horse vanilla’s skeleton horse ambient, hurt and death sounds.

Invented

There is no skeleton horse in nature. The gene is the owner’s answer to how much of the mod an undead horse gets: all of it, by making undeath a locus on an ordinary horse rather than an entity type beside it. It is recessive so that it can surface, rarely, from two ordinary-looking parents — the thing the mod is for — and it is only the bones: the diet, temper and stats are other loci the breed pins (the breed sets the bundle).

What needs verifying here

No skeleton horse of this mod has been seen in a running game: the cut-outs, underlay and shading are rendered in the side-view preview and unit-tested, not watched on a moving horse.

The look on a moving horse

Spawn a Great Valley Skeleton Horse egg of each strain and watch it walk, graze and rear: the holes should show the far side of the horse through the ribs, as vanilla’s do, with no white flash on the first frame. Tune coat_opacity and shade_strength by eye with a breed band if the bone reads too dark or too much like coat.

A foal

A skeleton foal should wear the baby skeleton sheet, cut the same way.

It never drowns

Ride one into deep water and stay under: the horse’s air should never run out; the rider’s should.
