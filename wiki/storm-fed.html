Planned / not built

Storm fed

A locus that fills a reservoir inside the horse and spends none of it. Standing in a storm, in rain, in snow, in water or in lava tops the horse up; being struck by lightning fills it in one burst. Then nothing happens, on purpose — it is written to be drunk from by a second gene. It was asked for, and researched before it was deferred, and the research is mostly about the awkward order that half a gene forces. None of it is built.

Storm fed — a gene that stores power and spends none

A locus that builds an internal reservoir while some condition holds, and does nothing whatever with it. Known as stormfed only because storms were the first allele found: the shipped set would be Storm, Rain, Snow, Lightning (a burst on being struck, and no lightning damage), Water and Lava. All alleles homozygous recessive. The rates of accrual and decay live in the epigenetics of each copy, so two horses with the same allele fill at different speeds.

The point is that it is half a gene on purpose. It is designed to be consumed: other genes read the reservoir and spend it, which is the mod's first deliberately polygenic pair. That makes the ordering awkward and worth naming now — a gene that only stores is untestable by looking at a horse, and a gene that only spends has nothing to spend until this exists. Build them together or build the spender first against a stubbed reservoir.

What it needs that does not exist: per-horse mutable state that is not a genotype and not an epigenetic constant — a number that changes every tick. The epigenome is the wrong home (it is inherited and drifts, not a running total) and so is the genotype. This is an attachment, and the first gene to want one.

It is not the only plan waiting on that attachment. Momentum sprint wants a per-tick number on the horse for the same reason, so whichever of the two is built first should put the attachment somewhere the other can use rather than growing a second one beside it.
