Ram horns

One copy and the horse grows a pair of ram’s horns — on a mare or a stallion with Rh, on a stallion only with Rhm, the way most horned sheep breeds have horned rams and polled ewes. How big they are, how far they curl, how thick and what shade are written on the allele. The third grown part, after the unicorn horn and antlers, and built on the antler pattern.

Crossing two of them

Two ways to have horns

  • Rh — horns on every horse that carries it, mare or stallion.
  • Rhm — horns on a stallion only. A mare with it is polled and passes it on, and her genotype reads exactly like a stallion’s.

Both are dominant, and Rh wins over Rhm. Foals grow no horns; they come in when the horse grows up, and stay.

Finding them

Rare in the wild — a few horses in a thousand carry either allele, mostly the stallion-only one. Two breeds carry them on purpose: the Mirrorgazer, horned on every horse, and the Not A Sheep, horned like a real flock.

What varies

  • Size — five classes, bud to massive; small to standard in the wild, and a line bred for size climbs to massive and stops.
  • Curl — how far round a curled horn goes, from a little over half a turn to more than a full one; on a corkscrew or horizontal-twist horn, how tightly it twists; on a scimitar, how far back it sweeps.
  • Girth and shade — thicker or thinner, pale horn to black.

Two more genes finish them: ram horn form picks the shape — curled, scimitar, corkscrew, four-horned, horizontal twist or scurs — and ram horn tip fades the points into a colour of their own. A horse can have a unicorn horn, antlers and ram horns all at once.

Is it good for the horse?

It does nothing at all, for good or ill: no stat, no attack, no hitbox. It is a look.

The antler pattern, again

Asked for as “a ram horn that follows the same pattern” (owner, 2026-10-01), and the choices were put to the owner: inheritance like antlers, all four shapes, and a tip colour gene with hoofprints that match it. So it is the antlers page with different geometry: the sex gate in grows(genotype) rather than the expression, a foal gate in PartKind.showsOnFoal, two kinds (RAM_HORN_RIGHT/LEFT) at the same CROWN anchors, one mesh per side, and everything composed in GrownParts.of. No new layer, render-state field or config key.

The epigenome, by cost

  • size: the class is the mesh's size bucket (RamHornSize, five classes; a bigger horn is more segments so a full curl stays smooth); the remainder is a uniform stretch.
  • curl: one of RamHornGenerator.CURLS buckets, in the mesh key with the shape — how far round a curl turns, how far back a scimitar sweeps, or how much a corkscrew or a horizontal twist rolls per segment.
  • girth and shade: a draw-time scale and colour. Shade is pale horn to black; with a coloured tip it is the base colour. Below 0 it runs on from pale horn to bare bone at −1 (the antler’s PALE_BONE) — the part’s bone version. No wild horse rolls below 0; the skeleton breeds band it to −1–−0.95. Added 2026-10-02, with the clamp widened from 0–1, so no saved horse’s horn changed.

Two-tone colour now runs along each branch

A two-tone part is drawn a box at a time in the colour AttachedPart.tintAt gives that far along. That used to be the box's index over the box count, which is right for a chain and wrong for a four-horned ram, whose curled horn hangs off the upright one’s root and would have been coloured as if it continued it. PartModel.along(i) is now the box’s depth over its depth plus the longest run still below it — identical for a chain, and root-to-tip for each horn of a branched part.

Analogous, not measured

Invented, like the antler loci. Real sheep horn inheritance is famously shaped by one region, RXFP2, with polled, horned and scurred types, and in many breeds by sex: rams horned, ewes polled or with scurs. Rh and Rhm are that difference as two alleles, the way Ant and Antm are for deer. The mod does not claim the real locus works this way, and does not borrow its name.

What needs verifying here

No ram horn has been seen in a running game: whether a curl clears the face and ear, and how each shape and size reads, are unchecked.

A curl that clears the head

The curl goes back, down past the ear and forward; its outward lean is meant to keep it off the cheek. Look at every curl bucket at standard and massive size, adult only, walking, grazing and rearing. A massive curl may cut through the neck or the bridle.

The sex and foal gates

An Rhm/n mare should be polled, the same stallion horned; an Rh/n mare horned; a foal of any genotype bare until it grows up.
