Fertility & gestation

A design page for a system that does not exist yet. Mares would come into heat and carry a pregnancy, and stallions would vary in how fertile they are. Everything here is a proposal or an open question. Nothing on this page is built, and the decision about how far it may change vanilla breeding is still being made.

The constraint: vanilla breeding stays

The owner’s standing rule is that this mod adds to vanilla behaviour and does not remove it. Fertility is where that rule bites hardest. In their words, when the research came in: they were “worried about that violating my ‘we don’t remove vanilla behavior’ too much”.

What vanilla does today, and what this mod already puts on top of it:

  • Vanilla: feed two tamed adults golden carrots or golden apples, and a foal appears at once. Both parents then wait about five minutes before breeding again, and the foal grows up in about twenty (Minecraft Wiki).
  • This mod: the foal’s genotype and epigenome are drawn from its parents at that same instant (breeding), and it joins its dam’s wild band if she has one (band life). The seed jar breeds a mare from a stored stallion through the same instant path. Its gestation step is the open item on the roadmap (assisted reproduction).

A pregnancy means the foal does not appear at the moment of breeding. Applied to golden-carrot breeding, that replaces vanilla behaviour outright. Everything below is sorted by how much of vanilla each option touches.

Three ways to add it

As proposed to the owner. None is chosen.

  1. Fertility as genes only — vanilla untouched. Fertility loci change the odds and timing inside vanilla’s own breeding, and nothing else. Such a locus could give a pairing some chance of no foal, lengthen or shorten the wait before breeding again, and rarely produce twins. A horse carrying the wild type breeds exactly as vanilla does, so the rule holds for every horse that has not been bred for it. This is the research’s “overlay model”, with genes deciding the overlay.
  2. Gestation only through this mod’s own breeding. The seed jar (and any later assisted-reproduction tier) makes a mare pregnant, and the foal comes some Minecraft days later. Golden carrots stay instant. Vanilla breeding keeps its behaviour, and the new system lives entirely on paths vanilla never had. The roadmap’s gestation item already scoped this.
  3. Gestation everywhere, as a server setting that is off by default. The research’s “full reproduction model”: golden-carrot breeding makes a mating record, and a foal arrives at the due date. It is the only option that replaces vanilla, and only on a server that opts in.

Recommendation, not yet agreed: 1 and 2 now, 3 later if at all. Option 1 makes fertility heritable, which is what this mod is about. Option 2 gives pregnancy a home that breaks no rule. Option 3 needs everything 2 builds anyway, so nothing is lost by leaving it for last.

What the research says

The abbreviated research is on breeding’s science tab and is not repeated here. The parts that shape the design:

  • The sexes run on different clocks. Mares cycle, about 21 days, with a heat of 2–8 days and most fertile at its end, and mostly only in the long-day season. Stallions have no cycle, just a seasonal quality modifier.
  • Nursing does not stop conception. Foal heat comes about a week after birth, so a mare can be pregnant and nursing at once. Lactation costs her condition. It does not lock her.
  • Straight time compression is unplayable. Squeeze 340 days into one Minecraft day and heat lasts 18 seconds. The fix the research lands on: scale every stage from one gestation setting, then give each independent stage a floor of one Minecraft day.

A draft model, for whichever option is chosen

Research suggestions carried over so next session does not start from the intake file. No number here is decided.

Mare states

  • One state at a time, in priority order: PREGNANT > POSTPARTUM > ESTRUS > DIESTRUS > ANESTRUS.
  • LACTATING is a flag beside the state, not a state. It can be set together with pregnancy or heat.
  • Peak fertility is a window inside estrus (its last part), not a stage after it. A cycle is estrus plus diestrus, so the two are not floored separately. Getting this wrong double-counts days.
  • Every deadline is stored as an absolute tick (dueTick, estrusEndTick). A counter that pauses when the horse’s chunk unloads would never catch up.
  • Stallions have no state. They get a fertility quality (a gene, age, condition) and perhaps a limit on covers per day.

Timing

  • One master setting, gestation_days. Every other stage is gestation_days × reference_days / 340, floored at one Minecraft day. At a one-day gestation that gives a two-day cycle (one day in heat, one not).
  • The clock. The research suggests a hybrid: pregnancy follows world time, so sleeping speeds it up, and heat follows active ticks, so a player cannot sleep through every window. This is undecided, and it has to agree with how the mod’s other day-based rules already count days.
  • Seasons are optional and invisible: Minecraft has none, so a “breeding season” would be its own repeating timer that the player only sees as “in season” or “out of season”.

Conception odds (the research’s starting point)

MareChance per well-timed breeding
Peak of heat, healthy adult70–85%
Early or late in heat35–60%
Foal heat, normal recovery45–70%
Nursing, in poor condition20–45%
Out of heat, or pregnant0%

Repeated covers in one heat can add up, but should be capped around 90–95%, so that feeding one pair twenty items is not a guarantee. Under option 1, a fertility gene would move these numbers, not replace them.

What it would plug into

  • The breeding handler (HorseBreedingHandler), where the foal’s genotype is drawn and its social attachment and band are set. A pregnancy moves that to birth, so the sire’s genome has to be stored on the mare at conception. The seed jar already does this for its sire.
  • Band life already knows a foal’s dam and birth tick, and the dam-and-foal goal. A foal born from a pregnancy should arrive into that the same way.
  • The roadmap’s gestation edge cases (assisted reproduction): the mare is killed while carrying, the chunk unloads, the world reloads mid-term, and a carrying mare is fed golden carrots.
  • Where the code goes. The state machine and every number are pure logic, so they belong in common/ with unit tests, and the day arithmetic stays Java 8-safe. The NeoForge side is one attachment and a check when the horse ticks or loads.
  • What a player sees: a line on the horse’s information screen (in heat, pregnant with days left, nursing), probably next to the Social section.

Open questions for the owner

  1. Does a fertility locus that sometimes gives no foal already count as removing vanilla behaviour, or is a gene that changes the odds fine?
  2. Which of the three options, or which combination?
  3. For a pregnancy through the mod’s own breeding: how long should it last, and should a pregnant mare act differently (slower, cannot be ridden)?
  4. Should wild bands breed on their own through this system, or stay out of it?
  5. Heat at all? Option 2 could have pregnancy without any cycle: a seed jar works whenever it is used.
  6. Does sleeping advance a pregnancy?
  7. Twins: a rare outcome of a fertility gene, or left out because they are a medical risk in real horses?
