Magical gene / recessive / turns the antlers to crystal, paints nothing

Crystal antlers

Two copies and a horse's antlers are crystal: the beams and tines see-through in a gem colour, the points solid. Which gem is inherited. On a horse without antlers this gene does nothing at all.

Crossing two of them

Finding one

Recessive and rare: about one wild horse in twenty has two copies, and almost none of them has antlers. A crystal rack is something you breed — find a two-copy horse, and cross it into an antlered line.

What it looks like

The rack keeps its shape and takes a gem's colour instead of bone's: clear quartz, amethyst, rose quartz, citrine, emerald, sapphire or ruby. The beams and tines are see-through — you can see the head behind them — and the points are solid, which gives each tine an edge. The gem is written on the allele and almost never changes between parent and foal, so a line of amethyst antlers stays amethyst.

Is it good for the horse?

It does nothing to the horse at all, for good or ill. Crystal antlers are no harder, sharper or more fragile than bone ones; they are only a look.

How it reaches the rack

GrownParts.of retints the antler with the gem colour and sets its translucent flag. The layer then draws it as two submits: the BONE region through entityTranslucent at AttachedPart.CRYSTAL_ALPHA, and everything else solid — the points (BONE_TIP) through the ordinary cutout pass. That is the split the treatment asked for (Attached model parts 12.5): the blended, sorted pass is the one that costs, so it draws the least it can. The "which translucent render type is a layer offered" question it left open has an answer — RenderTypes.entityTranslucent, the same one a fading ridden horse's horn already uses.

There is no real locus here

A magical gene with no real counterpart. It is a modifier of the antlers rather than a part of its own (decision D6): it can only change a rack the antler locus has already grown. Crystal growths on the body, if they come, are a separate gene with a separate part.

What needs verifying here

A crystal rack has not been seen in game, so the translucent shafts' sorting against the horse and each other is unchecked.

See-through shafts, solid points

A Cry/Cry antlered horse in each gem: the head should show through the beams, the points should be solid, and nothing should vanish or flicker as the camera circles. Translucent boxes inside one mesh sort per submit, not per box, so a tine behind the beam may draw in the wrong order — look for it. Then a crystal rack on a ridden, fading horse, and next to water and stained glass.
