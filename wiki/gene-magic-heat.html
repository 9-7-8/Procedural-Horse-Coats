Magical gene / no coat effect

Magic heat tolerance

Better or worse in a hot biome. A tolerant allele, a sensitive allele and a neutral wild type; how much each copy is worth, and whether it goes to speed, jump or both, is written on the copy. Its twin is magic cold tolerance.

Built, and not yet seen in game

The gene and its breed pools are tested. What it does to a horse in a desert has not been watched yet; see To be verified.

What it does

CombinationIn a hot biome
two tolerant copiesmarkedly faster, or jumps markedly higher, or some of each
one tolerant copysomewhat better
one tolerant, one sensitiveclose to normal — the two pull against each other
one sensitive copysomewhat worse
two sensitive copiesmarkedly slower, or jumps markedly lower, or some of each
anything with no variant copyno difference, anywhere

Hot means every desert, badlands and savanna, the jungles, the mangrove swamp and the Nether. Anywhere else the horse is exactly its normal self.

Who carries it

Desert and hot-country breeds tend to be tolerant: the Arabian, Akhal-Teke and Marwari nearly all are, and the Andalusian, Lusitano, Caspian, Karabakh, Banker Horse, both Paso Finos and the Mustang often are. Heavy feathered drafts and cold-country horses tend to be sensitive: the Friesian, Clydesdale, Shire, Belgian and Icelandic often are. The full list is on the breeds page. A Feral Mixed horse carries one copy now and then.

Health

None. A sensitive horse is slower in the heat, never hurt by it.

How it reaches the game

AbstractClimateGene.abilitiesFor splits each copy's signed delta by its own speed_share and sums the halves across both copies, then emits at most two attribute modifiers, movement_speed and jump_strength, both multiply_total and both gated on hot_biome. The multiplier never falls below MIN_FACTOR, so a horse is slowed and never stopped. delta is a bounded normal draw about MEAN_DELTA, floored at MIN_DELTA so a tolerant copy cannot point the wrong way. Wild founders carry one copy at WILD_CARRIER_PERCENT, half each way.

hot_biome is a world flag (AbilityType.WORLD_FLAGS), sampled on an interval by GeneAbilityHandler.sampleWorld. It holds when the biome's base temperature is at least HOT_BIOME_TEMPERATURE (1.0, where vanilla's savanna starts), or the biome is in #minecraft:is_jungle, or it is the mangrove swamp. The temperature test takes in modded hot biomes for free; the jungle tag takes in modded jungles that declare it. The API use was type-checked against the running client, not run.

What it stands for

Real horses do differ in how they cope with heat, and the differences follow where a breed was made. Desert breeds such as the Arabian and the Akhal-Teke are lean, thin-skinned and fine-coated, which sheds heat. Heavy draft breeds carry a lot of muscle for their surface area, often with thick manes and feathering, and are known to struggle in hot, humid weather; anhidrosis, a failure to sweat, turns up in several of them. Cold-country ponies grow dense double coats that work against them in the heat.

None of that is one gene. Heat tolerance in real horses is body shape, coat, sweat response and fitness together. The mod makes it a single magical locus so that a player can breed for it, and gives each breed pool a share that follows its history (the breeds page has the reasons). The magnitude and the split between speed and jump are game choices, not measured quantities.

What needs verifying here

Riding Htol/Htol and Hsen/Hsen across a plains-to-desert border is the whole of it: the change must start within a few seconds of crossing and reverse on the way back, with an Arabian herd in a desert mostly tolerant.
