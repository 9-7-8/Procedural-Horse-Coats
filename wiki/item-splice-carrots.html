Item group / breeding

The splice carrots

Carrots that add a gene to a foal. One targets a gene you have researched; the others roll one at random, either from the whole pool or from one themed slice of it.

Known Gene Splice — the targeted one

Pick the gene yourself. Craft it with a research paper for the gene you want, and feeding it to a parent gives the foal that gene.

Recipe: golden carrot + a research paper + horse hair — and nothing else, exactly three filled slots. The same three at every rarity: what a rare gene costs is finding the paper, not cutting carrots from it.

This one is not filtered

The random splices refuse to hand a foal something that would kill it or cost it hearts. The Known splice does not: you named the gene and you can read what it does, so splicing a horse into a lethal genotype on purpose is allowed. Check the gene’s own page first.

Unknown Gene Splice — the random one

Recipe: golden carrot + horse hair + gunpowder. It picks one gene at random and gives the foal a random version of it. It will never pick something that kills the foal or costs it hearts — see the breeding carrots.

The five themed splices

Each is the plain Unknown Gene Splice refined with one more reagent, and each narrows the random draw to one kind of gene. Same surprise, smaller pool.

A splice that lands marks the foal

A foal that got a gene from a splice carrot carries that fact for life, and so do its descendants — a spliced line is visibly a spliced line. The detail is on the carrot family page.

The Known Gene Splice is a custom recipe type

Not a JSON shapeless recipe: server/recipe/KnownGeneSpliceRecipe. It requires golden carrot + a research paper + horse hair, and nothing else — exactly three filled slots. The gene is read off the paper at craft time, so a drop-in gene file gets its carrot the moment it registers. A fourth slot took the gene’s rarity ingot until that charge was retired — see the rarity tier is no longer a price.

This recipe lands in four places

KnownGeneSpliceRecipe (what it requires), SpliceRecipeDisplay (the canonical slot layout), RarityItems (the tier → item table, deliberately off common/), and wiki/gene-carrot/gene-carrot.js, which redraws both for the wiki. The wiki cannot read the last two — they are Minecraft-side — so a change there is silent on the pages until it is made here too.

One item, many genes

known_gene_splice_carrot is one generic item parameterised by a data component, so every gene’s carrot looks the same in the inventory. That is a known gap.

The blacklist, and why it is derived

SpliceSafety works the random pool out from what the genes actually do rather than from a typed list of keys: every combination the carrot could roll is resolved through HorseTraits on an otherwise wild-type horse, and the locus is dropped if any of them produces a condition worse than INFORMATIONAL or leaves the horse short of baseline health. A new health gene is excluded the moment it is registered, by having done its job. Full argument on the carrot family page.

Verified

A splice carrot breeds (the yard's carrot pens, rows L to N, by 2026-09-13).

The substituted locus is really there, it is in the carrot's theme, and no foal came out with a disorder (2026-09-15). Nothing records which locus a splice picked, so the yard's rows AJ-AK find it the way SpliceOutcome.spliceReached does: an allele on the foal that neither parent carries. Each pen arms a mare with one carrot's effect, lets natural covers breed her, and re-arms her after every foal. In the 09:26 run's first fifteen foals, thirteen showed a splice and every one was in its theme:

  • dilution: mushroom Mu, tiger_eye TE2;
  • white: roan Rn, kit W35, manchado ma;
  • marking: pangare Pa2, plus one eye-sector hit;
  • performance: pdk4 A, twice;
  • magical: iridescent_rosette Irc;
  • the unthemed carrot: squiggle Sq and light Lteye, plus one sclera hit, all in the safe pool.
No foal had a condition above informational. The other two showed no new allele; a splice can hand down a substitute copy a parent already had. The two eye-locus hits may be Eyes.force rewriting eye loci, not the carrot; the pen skips eye loci now. A full game day later (the 09:40 run, eye loci skipped): 31 foals, 14 with a splice, every one in theme:
  • dilution: flaxen Fl1, mushroom Mu, matp prl, tiger_eye TE1;
  • performance: ryr2 J, ckm T, lcorl L, pdk4 A;
  • magical: moonwing Mnw, qhada Qha, concentric_eyespots Cek;
  • unthemed: geode P, hued_pangare Hpa, sky_shadow Sky.
None had a disorder. That day's white and marking foals (3 and 6) happened to show none: a splice is invisible whenever the substitute pair holds only alleles the parents already carry. Still open: the performance carrot's baseline check, that it never rolls HMGA2 and only the tall/fast LCORL copy; and the particle colour.

The spread of loci over many foals, and no disorder in any of them (2026-09-15, the 16:11 run, six hours unattended). The earlier runs settled the theme rule on fifteen and then thirty-one foals; this one ran all six pens for six hours and is the sample the two of them wanted. Not one FAIL line in the whole run. Foals that landed a new allele, every one of them in its carrot's theme: dilution 77, performance 69, magical 63, the unthemed carrot 63, white 62, marking 16. Every remaining foal logged no splice seen (a splice can hand down a copy a parent already had), which is the documented invisible case rather than a miss — and marking's low share is that same effect, since a marking substitute is usually a copy the dam already had. No foal in any pen carried a condition above INFORMATIONAL, which is SpliceSafety's derived blacklist holding across several hundred foals instead of a few dozen. The unthemed pen alone ran 106 foals with no death, no dwarf and no horse short of baseline health.

What needs verifying here

Lineage labelling is the risk: a spliced foal should read “Spliced (Breed)”, keep it through a backcross, and be priced by the cowboy without counting toward his purebred string — after which the five themed recipes still have to craft into named carrots rather than raw lang keys.

Deterministic parent-to-foal splice policy

Confirm the policy before changing the recipe: a splice carrot may guarantee that the fed parent’s selected allele reaches the foal, while uncertainty remains only in whether the other parent makes that allele visible. This is deliberately not a flat 25% roll, and the existing theme and safety rules must remain intact if the policy changes.

Still open from before 2026-09-13 (NOT played)

  • A spliced line reads "Spliced (Breed)". Feed a gene splice carrot, breed, and read the foal's info panel; breed that foal back to a purebred of the same breed; offer it to the cowboy. Pass: the label appears when the allele lands, the backcross stays Spliced, and the cowboy prices it as its base breed without counting it in his purebred string (gap 85).
  • The five themed splice recipes work at a crafting table: Unknown Gene Splice carrot plus light grey dye, bone meal, an ink sac, a rabbit's foot or an amethyst shard (the browser's Recipes tab only shows them). Pass: each gives its carrot with a name and texture (no magenta cube, no raw lang key).
  • Themed splices through shared code. Combine a themed splice with another carrot at a crafting table: it merges and still rolls in theme. Feed one to a foal: "a foal cannot be bred", effect not consumed. Pass also: the plain Unknown Gene Splice carrot behaves exactly as before.
