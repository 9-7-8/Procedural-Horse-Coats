Magic day watch

What the horse does about you while the sun is up — the daylight twin of magic night watch. The same five-rung ladder from staring through walls to standing directly behind you, and the same silence on its feet. After dark it is an ordinary horse.

Crossing two of them

The five

CombinationWhile it is light

Any of the five is silent on its feet while it is light. If the horse also carries magic day temper and that has something to hunt or flee, the temper wins and the watching waits.

Rare in the wild

Where every wild horse carries a night watcher, most carry nothing here: each variant is carried silently by about one and a half wild horses in a hundred, and none shows it.

Why daylight does not make it friendlier

The night watcher borrows the oldest fear there is: something moving where you cannot see it in the dark. In daylight you can see it, and that is the point of this twin — a horse that stands behind you at noon, in a bright empty field, silently, is unsettling for a different reason: there is nowhere for it to have been hiding.
