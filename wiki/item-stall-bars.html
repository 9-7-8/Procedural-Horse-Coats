Stall bars — a third per-material block family

A connecting, waterloggable horizontal-bars block, built the way jumps and the double gates already are: authored for vanilla's woods by a tools/bake-*.mjs script, generated for other mods' materials at run time by a compat/Generated* class, and the two halves kept in step by hand because nothing checks them.

  • It has to earn its place under the utility rule — nothing this mod adds may be purely aesthetic except breeding — and a block that pens a horse no better than a fence does not. Both of its utilities fall out of the same physical fact, which is a good sign they are the right ones. Bars are see-through, so a stalled horse still counts its herd and stays settled where a solid wall would isolate it. And bars are reachable, so you can groom, feed and lead a horse through them without opening the gate.
  • The herd half is the interesting one. It is a second consumer for a herd computation that has none, and it makes stable layout matter — the first time a block this mod adds cares where it sits relative to another.
  • It makes the jump bake cache worth doing. JumpModel.Unbaked.bake already loops every material once per blockstate variant. A third family on the same pattern is the point at which that stops being wasteful and starts being the cost — a cache keyed on (model id, rotation), as the standards row on the jumps page already argues on its own grounds.

From Simply Equine, whose BlockHorizontalBars is the shape — connecting, waterloggable, and with no gameplay attached to it whatever.
