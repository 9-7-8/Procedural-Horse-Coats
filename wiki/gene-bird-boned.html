Magical gene / no coat effect

Bird boned

Hollow-boned and light with it. Neither the horse nor its rider takes falling damage, from any height. The cheapest possible description of a gene that changes how you use the terrain.

Half seen: the expressing horse, not the carrier

A Brd/Brd horse and its rider surviving a fall was owner-confirmed in the 2026-09-10 sessions. What has not been watched is the other half of the claim — that a carrier falls exactly as any horse does, and that no wild horse is ever bird boned. Those are on the Verification tab.

What it changes

Mountains stop having a wrong side. A ravine becomes a shortcut. It pairs obviously with magic jump, and the pairing is the point — jump is dangerous alone, and this makes the danger go away.

Health

Pure benefit, with one caveat worth knowing: it removes fall damage, not falling. A horse that falls into a ravine is unhurt and still in a ravine.

Immunity is a damage cancel, and it stores nothing

GeneAbilityHandler.onTraversalDamage cancels any LivingIncomingDamageEvent carrying the minecraft:is_fall damage-type tag when it lands on a horse expressing the gene, or on the player who is its first passenger at the moment of the hit. Nothing is written onto the player, so stepping off ends it and there is no immunity to carry away. The tick still resets the horse’s fall distance, which keeps the landing sound quiet.

Why not the per-tick reset on its own. Fall damage is applied inside the move, before the post-tick handler runs, so one tick at full speed still hurt. And a rider’s fall damage is the horse’s, passed down by AbstractHorse.propagateFallToPassengers, so resetting the rider’s own fall distance never protected them at all. Found 2026-09-10 while fixing fireproof, which had the same flaw and was caught in play.

Prefer the attribute to the reset, if either will do

safe_fall_distance is already in the attribute table and reconciles itself off. Where an effect can be expressed as an attribute rather than a per-tick reset, the attribute path gets removal for free — which matters more than usual here, because the grant reaches a player.

Nearly nothing to build

Of the three genes wanting rider-targeted traversal, this is the one with no other moving parts. If the shared target parameter is built against this gene first, the dangerous half of the change gets tested in the simplest possible setting before fireproof and ocean-born depend on it.

Verified

The rider is immune too, dismount included (owner-confirmed in the 2026-09-10 play sessions). A rider’s fall damage is the horse’s, handed down by propagateFallToPassengers, so the gene never covered riders before that fix.

A gene whose value is entirely in another locus

On its own this is a safety net you rarely notice. Bred alongside magic jump, it turns a stat that mostly kills horses into one worth pushing. The mod has several of these — baiting beside health and fighting is the other clear one — and they are what make a stable a strategy rather than a collection.

What needs verifying here

Bird boned’s rider immunity is confirmed, but nobody has checked the other half of the claim — that a carrier still falls to its death exactly as before, and that no wild horse is ever bird-boned.

Still open (NOT played)

The rider is covered already (§Verified), so what is left is the control side: a damage cancel that fires too widely looks identical to one that fires correctly until you drop something that was supposed to get hurt.

Nothing here needs looking at to judge — do these first

  • A carrier is not immune. Spawn a Brd/n horse from the custom horse spawn egg and drop it from twenty blocks, with you aboard. Pass: both of you take the ordinary fall damage. The cancel keys on the expressing horse, and “every horse in the world stopped taking fall damage” is the invisible failure that matters here — nothing announces it.
  • No wild horse is bird-boned. The founder table is carriers-only, so the allele is in the population and the homozygote is not. Pass: over a lot of wild horses you find carriers and never one that shrugs off a fall; the first immune horse in a world is one somebody bred.
  • Only fall damage is cancelled. Hurt an expressing horse another way — a sword, a cactus, drowning. Pass: it takes all of it normally. The handler filters on the minecraft:is_fall damage-type tag and a filter that is wrong here makes an invulnerable horse, which reads as a good gene rather than a bug.
  • It removes the damage, not the falling. Ride one off a cliff into a ravine. Pass: you land unhurt at the bottom and are still at the bottom — nothing catches, slows or floats the horse.

In the game, with your hands

  • It pairs with magic jump the way the page claims. Breed an expressing horse that also carries a high magic jump. Pass: you can jump it repeatedly from its own maximum height without ever hurting it, which is the whole argument for the gene existing.
