The chase gene

A locus deciding how long a horse you have provoked keeps coming after you — a few seconds, until it loses sight of you, across a fixed distance, never at all, or forever. It was asked for, and researched before it was deferred; the research found most of the machinery already in the mod, because horses can already fight and a wild one already gives up when it loses its target. What is missing is the locus that turns the giving-up rule into a heritable number. None of it is built.

What is still planned here

Six alleles — timed, distance, sight, never, infinite and wild-type — generalising WildHorseForgetTargetGoal into a Java gene whose chase duration is inherited and drifts, with vanilla’s panic goal to out-argue for Flag.MOVE first.

A gene for how long an aggravated horse gives chase

Alleles n, timed, distance, sight, never, infinite. n is whatever the behaviour is now, and every natural breed is n unless stated — which costs nothing, because BreedFounder already forces every magical gene to wild for any breed that does not name it. A breed is exactly its breed sheet.

  • The substrate exists. HorseAggroHandler already gives every horse an attack-damage attribute and a melee goal, and WildHorseForgetTargetGoal already clears a wild horse's target after a fixed interval without line of sight. That goal is very nearly sight and timed already; this gene generalises it.
  • It needs Java, not just JSON. A duration in ticks or a distance in blocks that runs in families means per-allele epigenetics, and a JSON gene's knobs are for painting, not effects. Subclass AbstractAbilityGene, override epiSchema(), read it in abilitiesWhenExpressed(EpiValues) — IntimidatingGene is the template, and it is a behaviour gene whose radius is inherited and drifts.
  • Watch the goal-priority trap. The comment in HorseAggroHandler records that vanilla's panic goal sits at priority one and the melee goal at three, both holding Flag.MOVE, so the first hit a fighting horse took handed movement to panic and the fight starved. A chase that must outlast being hurt lands in exactly that fight.
