1 · Base coat

Extension and agouti are the pigment every other coat gene works on, so they come first. Pick the shape of the breed’s colour; refine the pools afterwards on the right.

Founders

Real BreedFounder.roll draws off the file as it stands — the pool, the wild-type forcing, the magic draw, the stat targets and the bands. These are the horses a herd of this breed would be made of.

2 · Gene pools

A gene the breed does not name is rolled wild if it paints, and left at its global founder rate if it does not. Naming one replaces that with the weighted pairs below.

3 · Epigenetic bands

Not which allele but how much — how dark the shade renders, how far the splash covers. Every founder is given a number inside the band on both copies; after that it inherits and drifts like any other.
