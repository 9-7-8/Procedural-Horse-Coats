Magical gene / recessive / one of the two undeath loci

Zombie

Two copies and the horse is dead flesh: the rotted green-grey hide of a zombie horse, whatever colour its other genes would have made it, though magical markings still show over it. It counts as undead. One copy is a carrier that looks and acts like any other horse. No wild horse has it: every one comes from a vanilla zombie horse that converted into a Graveborn Warmblood, or from breeding them.

Crossing two of them

What two copies do

  • The look. Vanilla’s zombie horse hide is the horse’s base coat. Its natural colour — bay, chestnut, a pattern — never shows, but a magical marking paints over the hide as over any coat: the Long-buried strain is a zombie with grave dust on it.
  • Black eyes.
  • It is undead to anything that asks: cleansing light, a holy ward, an aura that names the undead.

The raw-meat diet, the after-dark temper, the rotten-flesh calm, the flesh drops and the slow, tough body belong to the breed. A zombie foal of two ordinary parents is a rotted hide on an ordinary horse.

One copy

Nothing shows and nothing changes. A carrier is not undead for any purpose.

Is it good for the horse?

It has no health effect at all.

The seed pass

CoatSheetContribution with pass SEED: right after phase 2 the converted zombie sheet (horse_zombie.png / _baby) replaces the colour field the natural genes resolved, so the magical phase paints over it. The white template is divided back out of the seed, because the composite multiplies the field onto the template and the template has shading of its own — so an unmarked zombie comes out as vanilla’s zombie horse, not a shade darker. At the very end the sheet’s own alpha cuts its handful of holes (85 texels on vanilla’s 64px sheet). Black eyes, the undead check and the voice are shared with the skeleton: see skeleton.

Invented

Zombie horses are not real. The locus is the skeleton’s twin, and its look is a base coat rather than a cut-out because vanilla’s zombie horse is a whole horse recoloured, with only a few pixels knocked out — and its green-grey is no pigment a horse has, so it cannot come out of the natural genes.

What needs verifying here

No zombie horse of this mod has been seen in a running game: the seeded hide and the Long-buried dust are rendered in the side-view preview, not watched in the world.

The hide under daylight and in the dark

Spawn a Graveborn Warmblood egg of each strain: Fresh should be indistinguishable from vanilla’s zombie horse at a glance; Long-buried should carry dark grave dust up the legs, along the belly and over the muzzle.
