Body plate colour
(Magical gene / codominant / colours the body plates, paints nothing)
What colour a horse’s shoulder and hip plates are. The same colours and rules as horn colour (white, the rainbow, pink, black, grey or chaos), on a locus of its own. Two different copies give every plate an edge in the second colour. On a horse without plates it does nothing.

Crossing two of them The colours, and two-tone plates Exactly the horn colour table . Two copies of one colour make every plate that colour, both sides of the horse alike. Two different ones make each plate one colour and its edge the other: the lip along its bottom, and the rib or the spike on a ridged or spiked plate. The plate takes whichever colour comes earlier in the list, so Red/Blu is red plates edged in blue, either way round. Every copy carries a little shade of its own, so no two red-plated horses are quite the same red. Bare bone Two Bon copies are the plates’ bone version: bare ivory bone, the way a skeleton’s plates would be. No wild horse carries Bon ; the skeleton horses do, so a skeleton that grows plates grows them in bone. Plates are all bone, so unlike a back sail nothing is left off. Is it good for the horse? It does nothing to the horse at all. It is only the colour of the plates. Its gene carrot
