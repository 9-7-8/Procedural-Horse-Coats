Diet — what the horse will eat
(Natural gene / paints nothing)
A wild type that eats what any horse eats, and a set of narrow diets — raw meat, fish, wheat, cake, potions, lava, one metal, one gem, and blood — each needing two identical copies to show. A narrow diet refuses almost everything, and what it does accept feeds it far better: a single gold bar takes a metal-eater to full health however hurt it was.

Crossing two of them The trade A narrow diet is a genuine cost. Almost nothing on the ground will feed the horse, and the mod’s gated healing is untouched by it, so a lava-eater in a paddock still needs its lava carried to it. What it buys is efficiency , and the numbers grade by breadth — the only axis that makes the bargain legible. Two Dbld copies and the horse eats nothing a hand can offer , and does not heal beside hay and water either. It heals only by biting . When it is hurt, it hunts. It walks up to something living, bites it for half a heart, and heals three hearts. Then it leaves that animal alone for a whole day, so a cow bitten today is fine and gets bitten again tomorrow. Any living mob will do — a cow, a sheep, a villager, a creeper — nearest first. A player only if there is nothing else. If no other mob it can bite is within reach, it will bite a player, its owner included. Creative players are left alone. Tamed cats and dogs last of all — below players. Never another horse , and never anything undead. Not in the sun , if it is also sun-sensitive : shelter comes first, dinner after dark. Keep one beside a pen of livestock and it looks after itself without emptying the pen; keep one alone and it starves slowly.
