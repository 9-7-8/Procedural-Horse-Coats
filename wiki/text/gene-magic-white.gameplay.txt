Magic white
(Magical gene / phase 3)
Two copies paint the whole horse white , over whatever coat it really has. One copy shows nothing. The white is painted low in the magical order, so any marking the horse also carries still draws on top of it.

Crossing two of them What it does White from nose to tail. A black horse, a cremello and a leopard all come out the same white. The real coat is still there. It is only painted over, so a white horse bred to a bay can throw a bay foal that never looked white at all. Markings still show. If the horse also carries a magical marking — stripes, a sigil, a pattern — it is drawn on top of the white. Nothing else. No change to the eyes, the body or the appetite. The white half of the Dhampir is this gene; its red eyes, its sunburn and its strength are other loci. About three wild horses in a hundred carry one silent copy.
