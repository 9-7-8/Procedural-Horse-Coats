Fireproof
(Magical gene / no coat effect)
A horse that does not burn, and neither do you while you are on it. It swims through lava rather than walking on top of it — submerged, slow, and entirely unbothered.

Half seen: immunity yes, the rest no Horse and rider surviving fire was owner-confirmed in the 2026-09-10 sessions . What has not been watched is what could not be judged from the saddle: that the lava speed is inherited rather than flat, that a carrier still burns, and that LavaTravelMixin did not quietly speed up every other entity in the game. Those are on the Verification tab . What it does Combination Outcome Frp/Frp horse and rider immune to fire; swims in lava anything else nothing — it burns like any horse Lava is a route, not a wall The horse sinks into lava and swims through it the way an ordinary horse swims through water: slowly, at the surface, with its rider safe. That is the whole reason to breed one. A lava lake stops being a detour. Health Strictly an improvement. It costs nothing and removes an entire category of death. It is homozygous recessive and absent from wild horses as a homozygote, so you will be breeding for it rather than finding it.
