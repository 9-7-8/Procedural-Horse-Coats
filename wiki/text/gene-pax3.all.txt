PAX3 (splash white)
(Natural gene / phase 1)
The second splash white locus, carrying SW2 and SW4 . Its twin is MITF , and it exists to make one point that a single splash gene could not: a horse can be splash twice over. SW2 is the rare , breed-clustered splash allele — Quarter Horses and American Paints above all — not the common one. The common one is SW1 , next door.
