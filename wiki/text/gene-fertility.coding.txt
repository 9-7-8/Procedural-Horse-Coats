Fertility — how readily a horse breeds
(Natural gene / paints nothing)
Three alleles. n is ordinary. Two copies of sf make every breeding half as likely to take. tw makes a pregnancy more likely to be twins. Every copy also carries a small number that nudges a mare’s odds. What a pregnancy is, and when a mare can have one, is on fertility & gestation .

The class common/genetics/genes/FertilityGene , registered as Genes.FERTILITY at priority 3, between sex and diet. It paints nothing, and GeneFamily files it under Other natural genes by override. Without that, the “invisible is health” rule would list it with the disorders. alleleFactor(pair) — SUBFERTILE_FACTOR for sf/sf , else 1. Conception.vanillaFoalChance is the product of both parents’. twinChance(pair) — by pair.count(tw) . fertility(genome) — the expressed copy’s fertility value ( Epigenome.expressed ). mareFactor(genome) — the allele factor times that value; what Conception.attempt multiplies the stage’s odds by. The sire contributes only his allele factor and his covers today. epiSchema() is one Gaussian, mean 1.0 and sigma 0.05, floored at 0.7. The design span is mean 6 sigma, so a founder sits in 0.7–1.3. Founders come from a table of carriers only ( n/n , sf/n , tw/n , tw/tw ). It is not feralOnly , so a breed file may name it. Tests: FertilityGeneTest , and the conception odds in ReproRulesTest . The founder table ends on tw/tw , and the genotype test knows it (2026-09-15). GenotypeTest.randomDrawsOneFloatPerGeneInGeneOrder expects a maximal roll to land on each gene's last bucket, and read that as the baseline; fertility's last bucket is the twin homozygote. The test now exempts fertility the way it exempts the two night loci, and passes.
