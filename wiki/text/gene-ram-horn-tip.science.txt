Ram horn tip
(Magical gene / recessive / colours the horn tips, paints nothing)
Two copies and a horse’s ram horns keep their own shade at the base and fade into a colour of their own toward the points — any colour, inherited with the allele. Hoofprints from the molten hooves MltH allele come out in the same colour. On a polled horse it does nothing.

There is no real locus here A magical gene with no counterpart. Its own locus, rather than a number on the horn allele, so that it can be bred in or out on its own — the antler modifiers’ reasoning.
