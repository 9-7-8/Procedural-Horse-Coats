Unicorn horn
(Magical gene / recessive / grows geometry, paints nothing)
A horse with two copies grows a single tapering horn on its forehead — and it is the first thing in this mod that is a piece of the horse rather than a picture on it . Nothing about the coat changes. How long the horn is, how thick, how tightly it spirals and how far it leans are all written on the allele copy and inherited with it, so a horn you like breeds true and a line selected for length keeps getting longer. This gene grows every horn white : its colour, its glow and the dust it sheds are three more genes .

There is no real locus here Obviously. This is one of the frankly magical genes and it makes no claim on horse genetics at all. What is worth saying is where the shape of the gene came from, because it was not arbitrary. Why recessive, and why carriers A dominant horn would be a horn you find rather than a horn you breed. One copy showing means the first unicorn you meet is the whole story, and any line descended from it is visibly unicorn from the first cross — there is nothing to work toward. Recessive puts the interesting object one step behind a thing you can notice and act on: I have two carriers. It is the same reason cutie mark is recessive, and it is the shape almost every showpiece locus in this mod takes. The frequency is then a balance between two failures. Too rare and the gene is content nobody ever sees, which the mod already has too much of; too common and a unicorn is not a unicorn. One in four hundred wild horses, with one in twenty carrying, puts an unbred horn somewhere between “a genuine find” and “something that has happened to you”, and makes the carrier route clearly the sensible one. Why the horn varies at all It would have been less work to ship one horn. The reason not to is the premise of the whole mod ( philosophy ): a coat is not chosen from a catalogue, and a horn drawn from a list of five would be the first thing here that was. A gene that grants the same object to every horse carrying it is a switch, and a switch has nothing to breed toward — you either have it or you do not, and the second unicorn is identical to the first. The epigenome is what makes the difference, and it needed no new machinery: a gene declares named numbers on each allele copy, founders roll them, and every breeding nudges them. So “a line bred for length” is a real activity with a real result, and two unicorns are no more alike than two bay horses are. Why the horn facets, and why that is the right trade A horn is a stack of small square prisms, not a smooth cone, so it has visible facets. A genuinely smooth horn would need custom vertex geometry rather than boxes — possible (the hay portal already does it) at the price of owning the vertex code. It was not taken, for two reasons. The first is that blocky is the game's own look and a faceted, carved-looking horn is not a compromise anybody has to apologise for. The second is more interesting: everything about the boxes is plain arithmetic in common/ , which is what lets a unit test walk every mesh the mod can build with no game running, and what would let the browser designer preview a horn's silhouette from the same code rather than a second implementation of it in JavaScript. The one piece of geometry that is a trap Vanilla composes a model part's rotation as rotationZYX , so a segment is turned about x first and then about the parent's y. On a segment with no bend, those are the same axis as the horn's own, and the y rotation is a clean roll — a square section rolled a little per segment reads as a twisted horn, because the corners trace a helix down the length. On a segment that is also bent, the same rotation sweeps the bend sideways instead, and fifteen segments of that is a corkscrew whose tip points somewhere behind the horse's ear. So the four styles split cleanly: the smooth one is the only one that curves, and the three twisted ones are dead straight. That is not a concession — a straight tapered spiral is the storybook horn and a narwhal tusk both, and the gentle forward curve is what an untwisted horn needs to stop reading as a spike. PartGeneratorTest asserts that no box ever carries both, because it is the sort of invariant a later tidy-up removes without anything going red.
