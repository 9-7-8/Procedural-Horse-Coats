Whistles
(Item / tool)
Three area whistles call every tamed horse you own nearby, each reaching further than the last. The fourth, the ender whistle , is bound to one horse for good and brings it to you from anywhere — even another dimension.

The area whistles item/WhistleItem , one class and a radius per tier. Bond gating and pathing instead of teleporting are still open on the roadmap tab . The ender whistle item/EnderWhistleItem — use calls, inventoryTick checks for dust once a second. Stacks to one. appendHoverText names the bound horse, reading it off the component rather than the entity: the horse may be unloaded, in another dimension, or dead, and the client cannot reach it in any of those cases. Same shape as the bound stall sign , which is where the wording came from. server/EnderWhistleCalls — binding (an EntityInteract handler using HorseOwnership.bindRefusal , the same refusal stall signs use), calling, and the dust. The binding is the existing BOUND_HORSE component. data/HorseWhereabouts — a server-global SavedData of each tamed horse’s last dimension and block, and whether it died. Written by server/HorseWhereaboutsHandler on join, on leave, every ten seconds (only when it moved more than 16 blocks or changed dimension), and on death. The ancestry database keeps a dead horse’s record for the pedigree, so it cannot answer “is it alive” — this can. How a call finds a horse Any level with the entity loaded: move it now. Otherwise the last sighting’s chunk gets a TicketType.PORTAL ticket (radius 2, which expires by itself) and the call waits on the server tick for up to five seconds for the entity section to load. Same dimension: snapTo . Another dimension: Entity.teleport(TeleportTransition) , which re-creates the entity in the target level. Ownership is checked at call time , never at bind time, which is what makes the whistle follow a sale. It moves an entity found by UUID and never creates one, so it cannot duplicate a horse. A horse deleted rather than killed — the horse dimension clearing its pens — is gone from the ancestry database, and that crumbles the whistle too. Known gaps All four whistles share one texture. Nothing gates the area whistles or binding on bond. The far-chunk wait and the cross-dimension teleport are written against 26.1.2 sources and not seen working — see the verification tab . A horse moved while its chunk was unloaded by something other than itself (a piston, another mod) is looked for where it was last seen, and not found.
