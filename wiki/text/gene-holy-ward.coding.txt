Holy ward
(Magical gene / no coat effect)
Hostile mobs do not appear near this horse. Not pushed away — they never spawn in the first place, out to a radius written on the allele copy, at least eight blocks and never more than sixteen.

Gene key horsegenetics.holy_ward Alleles Hly warding, n Inheritance homozygous recessive Reaches the game as a spawn-suppression aura Epigenetic radius, minimum 8, capped at 16 Coat effect none, ever Cancel natural spawns only, or it silently breaks mob farms Check the spawn reason. Cancelling spawner-block, spawn-egg, breeding, structure or command spawns breaks the player’s mob farm, other mods’ spawners, and any quest or event that places a mob — and it breaks them invisibly , because nothing errors. Only natural spawning is this gene’s business. The handler runs on every spawn attempt in the world This is the only effect in the mod that hooks a global, high-frequency event rather than the horse tick. Iterating horses per spawn attempt is a genuine tick cost on a busy server. Cache the warding positions per level per tick and early-out on an AABB test before doing anything else. The cap is a balance decision, not a placeholder Sixteen blocks was chosen so the ward covers a camp and not a base. Raising it later is a gameplay change that quietly retires every other defensive gene. It interacts with spawner, deliberately Because spawner is required to go through the normal spawn path, a warding horse standing next to a spawning one suppresses it. That is the correct behaviour and it falls out for free — provided spawner does not shortcut the event. Why this is not a mob_aura mode Every other radius effect in the format runs on the horse’s own tick and looks outward. This one runs on somebody else’s event and looks inward, and folding it into mob_aura would put a global event handler behind a verb whose whole contract is “a beat, a radius, a cap”. Keeping it separate keeps that contract honest. Verified The ward works (2026-09-13, ten minutes unattended, the player thirty blocks off). The warded chamber saw 39 spawns attempted and 0 arrive; the unwarded control saw 53 and 53. The two hooks straddle the cancellation: FinalizeSpawnEvent fires before the ward can cancel, so it counts attempts, and EntityJoinLevelEvent fires only if nothing cancelled, so it counts arrivals. The gap between the columns is the gene. A measuring trap: the first overnight attempt (2026-09-12 to 13) logged no monster spawned line in three hours, because BaseSpawner.serverTick only runs within requiredPlayerRange , 16 blocks, of a player. The control line (“no monster spawned lines anywhere means stop, because a silent spawner and a perfect ward look identical”) is what kept it from reading as a pass. The biome that spawns naturally replaced the spawner. Spawn eggs still work beside a warding horse (owner-confirmed in the 2026-09-10 play sessions). The ward cancels natural spawns only, by the allow-list of spawn reasons in GeneWardHandler.isNatural . Still open: the ward stopping once the horse dies. A spawner farm beside a warding horse keeps producing (2026-09-15, the 16:11 run). This was the open half of the note above. A spawner horse's summons go through EntityType.spawn as NATURAL on purpose, so claim and spawn-blocking mods still see them as ordinary spawns - which meant the ward refused them whenever one landed inside its radius. GeneAbilityHandler.summoning() now lets a summon through, and the yard's SPAWNER WARD pen tests it: eight meals beside a Hly/Hly horse made 16 made, 2 inside the ward's eight blocks, PASS, with no refusal line logged ( spawner ).
