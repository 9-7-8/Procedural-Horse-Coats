Rescuing braid
(Gameplay / non-genetic)
A one-use piece of mane or tail gear that turns a killing blow into a trip home instead of just a reprieve. The instant the horse would die the braid breaks, and the horse — with you on it, if you were riding — is standing in its own stall , bolting. It is the first thing in the game that goes in the mane or the tail.

Three subscribers on one event RescuingBraidHandler is the third thing in the mod hanging off LivingDamageEvent.Pre to keep a horse alive, and the priorities are the composition rather than tidiness: HorseLastStandHandler at NORMAL — refuses the blow, holds the horse at LastStand.healthLeft , draws the particles. RescuingBraidHandler at LOW — sees a horse on that floor and moves it. EmergencyStasisHandler at LOWEST — dropped from LOW to make room . Its own comment used to explain LOW as “after the last stand”; it now also means “after the braid”, and the reason is the same both times. The braid goes before the chamber because it is the more specific instrument (owner’s call): worn on one horse on purpose, where a chamber covers every horse its carrier owns. Nothing is wasted by that ordering, which is what makes it safe — a horse the braid has moved is out of the fight and takes no further blows, so the chamber never gets asked and is never spent. Why it tests a state, not a killing blow This is the trap in the whole feature and it would have shipped green. By the time the braid is asked, the last stand above it has usually already set the damage to zero — so damage >= health , the obvious condition, answers no on precisely the horses a braid exists for. Every test passes, the item crafts, the layer draws, and the braid never fires. So RescuingBraid.fires asks where the horse is : the blow is fatal, or the horse is already sitting on the last stand’s floor. That is the same conclusion StasisRescue reached from the other end of the same event, for the same reason, and RescuingBraidTest pins both halves — including one assertion that is really about two features at once: a horse on that floor must still be below Escape.DEFAULT_THRESHOLD , or the promise that it arrives bolting is not kept by anything. The item carries nothing RescuingBraidItem has no data component, no durability and no state beyond vanilla’s dyed_color , because the destination is resolved when it breaks, not when it is made (owner’s call, against the original plan, which wanted a binding step and a bound-stall reference like BoundHorse ). What that buys: no binding UI to design, no component to sync, no braid pointing at a stall that has been pulled down, and a spare in a chest that works for whichever horse you put it on. What it costs is that the item cannot describe its own destination before it fires, which is why the no-destination case is a chat line rather than a refusal at the point of wearing. resolve asks StallData.forHorse then StallData.penOf , and TicketHandler.landingSpot is what says yes — it re-reads the sign, re-runs the stall detection live and finds a spot the horse fits in, so a stall somebody has filled with hay bales refuses here rather than suffocating an animal. That is StallRecall ’s rule verbatim: the Send home button makes exactly this choice, and a braid making a different one would be a second answer to one question. The move, and the rider TicketHandler.arrive is called rather than copied, so the braid gets the portal particles, the enderman sound and HorseLeads.untieFor for free — and a future change to how a horse travels reaches this without anyone remembering it exists. The rider is the part that is genuinely new. Within one level, teleportTo carries an entity’s passengers — the property GeneReactionHandler ’s blink already relies on. Across levels it does not : the entity is re-created in the target and its passengers are put down. So reseat looks the horse up again by id in the world it landed in, sends the rider after it, and remounts. Every other teleport in the mod dodges this by refusing a ridden horse outright ( TicketHandler , StallRecall , EnderWhistleCalls ); a braid cannot, since being ridden is the situation it is bought for. Nothing forces the escape behaviour, and nothing should. HorseEscapeGoal reads the health bar and whether anything has hurt the horse lately on its own canUse tick, and both are still true on the far side of the teleport — Escape.THREAT_LINGERS_TICKS outlasts the trip by a wide margin. So the horse comes up bolting through the goal’s own door, which is the one that has been played, rather than through a forceStart written for this. The streak is a mask, and the mask is baked BraidLayer is the structural twin of EmissiveCoatLayer : one submitModel of the whole horse through a texture that is transparent everywhere it must not paint. What differs is that the texture is a mask — flat white where the mane or the tail is — and the colour is passed as the submit’s own tint, so one pair of files covers every colour a player can mix rather than sixteen baked sheets. The masks are baked from HorseSkinGeometry , not painted. The two patches are at texOffs(56, 36) and texOffs(42, 36) , doubled onto the sheet. Hand-painting two rectangles at those coordinates would work, and would silently paint a horse’s neck the day somebody moves a cube. HairMaskTool asks the mesh instead and hard-fails on a part that covers no texel, because a mask nothing draws through is a braid nobody can see and it would ship green. Re-run :common:bakeHairMasks when the mesh moves. Gear is a synced attachment, so extractRenderState reads the worn stacks off the horse with no packet of this layer’s own, and the two colours land on GeneticHorseRenderState as plain ints — zero for nothing worn, which is the answer for almost every horse on screen almost all the time. Why vanilla’s dye and not the mod’s tint The mod already has a tack colour component, and the braid deliberately does not use it. tack_tint is SaddleTint(seat, bridle, metal) — three zones, because a saddle has a seat, a bridle and its fittings. A braid is one plait of one colour, and forcing it through a three-zone record would mean a zone-names case, a tooltip that says the same word three times, and a bench screen with two slots that do nothing. So it is in #minecraft:dyeable and carries vanilla’s dyed_color , which buys the whole colour story for one tag file: dyeing in a crafting grid, mixing several dyes, washing it out in a cauldron, the tooltip line and the tinted inventory icon are all vanilla’s code doing what it already does. The item definition’s default tint and GeneticHorseRenderer.BRAID_UNDYED are a pair and must stay equal — a braid that looked one colour in the hand and another on the horse would read as a bug.
