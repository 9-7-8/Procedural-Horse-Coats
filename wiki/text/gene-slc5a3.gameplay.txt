SLC5A3 (foal immunodeficiency syndrome)
(Natural gene / health)
A recessive lethal that gives you nothing to see. The foal is born looking perfectly healthy, runs with its dam for weeks, and then fails as the antibodies it borrowed from her run out and its own never arrive.

Crossing two of them The one disorder that is common Every other recessive lethal in this mod is a rarity you meet by accident. This one is not — not inside the breed it belongs to. Roughly two Fell Ponies in five carry a copy , so breeding two Fells together without checking is not unlucky, it is the ordinary case. The Dales Pony carries the same mutation. That makes it the clearest argument in the mod for reading a horse’s papers before pairing it. With a gene database entry for both parents the cross is a decision; without one it is a coin you flip twice. No founder is ever affected The founder table lists only the clear horse and the carrier. A wild-caught horse is an adult that survived, so it can carry this and never have it, and the homozygote’s weight is simply absent from the table. The only way to produce an affected foal is to breed two carriers. Where you will actually meet it An unaffiliated wild horse carries it at well under one in a hundred. A Fell Pony carries it at thirty-eight in a hundred. That gap is deliberate and it is the whole point of per-breed pools: a disorder can be vanishing globally and commonplace inside one population, and a mod that only had one global number could not say so.
