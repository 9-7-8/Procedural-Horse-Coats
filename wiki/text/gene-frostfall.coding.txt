Frostfall
(Magical gene / mane and tail)
Mane, forelock and tail losing their colour from partway down, ending white - and reaching it at different depths strand by strand, so the front is feathered rather than level.

It is listed in the index.json beside it and registered by Genes ' class initialiser at startup. The roots keep the hair's own colour, the far ends are white or near it, and the middle passes through a neutral grey. The transition takes up something between a third and two-thirds of the length, so there is no line to point at; and because bundles of strands reach white at different depths, the front is interdigitated rather than a clean horizontal.
