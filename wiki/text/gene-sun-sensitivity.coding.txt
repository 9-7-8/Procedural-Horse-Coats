Sun sensitivity
(Magical gene / behaviour)
Two copies and daylight burns the horse. Caught under open sky by day it runs for the nearest deep cover or water — jumping a fence if it can — and stays there until dark. One copy shows nothing at all.

The split onto this locus is not play-tested — see the Verification tab . The handler and its goal SunSensitivityHandler.onTick — every 40 ticks, staggered by entity id. It checks sunlight first (a few block reads) and only then parses the genome, so the parse only happens for a horse actually standing in the open by day. Damage is onFire at half a heart, not vanilla ignition. SunShadeGoal at priority 1, on every horse . It asks the genome once, lazily, and does nothing for a horse that is not sensitive. It used to be added only to a horse already known to be a dhampir at the moment it joined the level — but a wild founder's record is written a tick after it joins, so a wild horse never got the goal until its chunk reloaded. Panic yields to it. HorsePanicGoal holds its ground when the horse is on fire and sun-sensitive, because vanilla's panic sits at the same priority, holds the same movement flag, and was added first — it starved the shade goal and the horse ran in circles until it died. Blood diet reads it too. A sun-sensitive blood-drinker does not go hunting while it is in the sun. Not an effects verb , for the reason the dhampir gene gave: a verb with exactly one user is a hand-written handler wearing a disguise. It survived the split, alone and in the full strain (2026-09-15). The yard's SUN ONLY pen holds a Sun/Sun horse with nothing else of the dhampir, a hay bale, and a roofed corner.
