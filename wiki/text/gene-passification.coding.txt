Passification
(Magical gene / no coat effect)
The way in to a horse that would otherwise kill you. It adds no aggression of its own — it says what you can offer the animal to make it stop, and while the bargain holds it will not target you for any reason.

Gene key horsegenetics.passification Alleles Prm , Tmp , FPr , APr , FTm , ATm , n Inheritance incomplete dominant — one copy expresses, and a compound heterozygote expresses both Epigenetics offering (a category index), amount , duration , cooldown Reaches the game as no effect verb — see below Coat effect none, ever The veto has to be asked at every chokepoint, not unpicked afterwards “Will not select the player as a target for any reason ” only means something if each reason asks. Passification.suppresses is consulted in four places: the temper verb, HorseAggroHandler.aggro (hit-it-and-its-herd), HerdGoals (a dam defending her foal) and GeneReactionHandler (guardian's retaliation). A gene added later that targets a player and does not ask here is a bug in that gene. The item is an index, so the table may only grow at the end The epigenome stores numbers, so “the item this horse wants” can only ever be a position in a list the gene owns ( PassificationGene.OFFERINGS ). Appending is free. Re-ordering it silently changes what every existing horse in every save is asking for , and nothing will tell you. Why it is not an effect verb It would have been a verb with exactly one user, which is the same test lycanthropy and sun sensitivity failed. So the gene owns the genetics and the numbers and exposes one pure function, routesOf ; the NeoForge module owns the feeding, the clock and the veto. It is also not a standing effect but a veto on somebody else's, which no verb in the set can express. “Extremely high priority” is not the priority number The locus is required to override every other aggression effect. A gene's priority() could not do that: for a gene that paints nothing the number is only a slot in the genotype code's layout. The override is enforced by where the check lives — at the moment a target is acquired, after whatever decided to acquire it. The number here is merely high enough that a reader sorting the registry finds it after the loci it talks over.
