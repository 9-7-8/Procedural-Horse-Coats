Crystal growth colour
(Magical gene / codominant / colours the crystal growths, paints nothing)
What colour a horse’s crystal growths are. The same colours and rules as horn colour (white, the rainbow, pink, black, grey or chaos), on a locus of its own. Two different copies make the crystals one colour and their points the other. On a horse without crystals it does nothing.

Crossing two of them The colours, and two-tone crystals Exactly the horn colour table . Two copies of one colour make every crystal that colour, see-through, with solid points of the same colour. Two different ones make the see-through crystal one colour and its solid point the other. The crystal takes whichever colour comes earlier in the list, so Red/Blu is red glass with blue points, either way round. Every copy carries a little shade of its own, so no two ruby-crystalled horses are quite the same red. Bone Two Bon copies are simply bone-coloured crystal, still see-through. A crystal has no bone in it, so unlike the other body parts there is no bare-bone version. No wild horse carries Bon . The skeleton horses grow crystals in ordinary colours, with this locus not pinned (owner, 2026-10-05). Is it good for the horse? It does nothing to the horse at all.
