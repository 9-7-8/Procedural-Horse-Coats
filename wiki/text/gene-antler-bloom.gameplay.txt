Blooming antlers
(Magical gene / recessive / grows leaves on the antlers, paints nothing)
Two copies and leaves, moss or blossom grow on a horse's antlers — a clump on every tine and a few along each beam. Which of the three is inherited. On a horse without antlers this gene does nothing at all.

Crossing two of them Finding one Recessive: about one wild horse in twelve has two copies, and most of those have no antlers to bloom. The Rimehart carries it, and every Rimehart that blooms grows moss. What it looks like Small clumps on the tines and the beams, in leaf green, moss olive or blossom pink. A tine the horse has not fully grown carries its clump at the same partial size. The growth is written on the allele and almost never changes between parent and foal. Is it good for the horse? It does nothing to the horse at all, for good or ill. Nothing can be harvested from the leaves. Its gene carrot
