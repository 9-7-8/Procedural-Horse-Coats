How horses actually reproduce The two sexes run on completely different clocks. Mares cycle seasonally and have a short fertile window; stallions are fertile all year. And a mare can conceive again while still nursing, often within about a week of foaling. Mares: a 21-day cycle, in season Mares are long-day seasonal breeders: they begin cycling as spring days lengthen, cycle through summer, and mostly stop in winter, so that an eleven-month pregnancy lands a foal in the next spring. Those signs mean receptive , not ovulating — breeders confirm timing with ultrasound. Stallions: no cycle A mature, sound stallion can breed year-round. He is still somewhat seasonal — semen output and libido peak in the long days and dip in winter — but it is a quality modifier, not an on/off state. Fertility also depends on semen quality and on how often he is used. Pregnancy, foal heat and nursing Gestation averages about 340 days , commonly 320 to 370. Almost always one foal; twins are high-risk. Foal heat — the first estrus after birth — starts about 5 to 12 days after foaling, with ovulation around day 7 to 10. Nursing does not prevent conception. A healthy mare can foal in spring, conceive on foal heat, and spend most of the next year both pregnant and nursing. That is how annual foaling happens. Breeders sometimes skip foal heat after a hard birth, a retained placenta, infection, poor body condition or an older mare — because of the uterus’s recovery and her condition, not because of the milk. The problem with putting this in a game Vanilla Minecraft produces a foal the instant two horses are fed. A realistic model separates courtship, mating, conception, pregnancy and birth — which is also what would let a foal’s genes be drawn at birth from a stored sire. The hard part is time: Straight compression breaks the short stages. Squeeze a 340-day pregnancy into one Minecraft day (20 minutes) and the whole 21-day cycle becomes 74 seconds, with heat lasting 18 seconds — accurate and unplayable. The usual fix is a floor. Scale every stage from one gestation setting, then clamp each independent stage (heat, diestrus, recovery, foal heat, lactation) to a minimum such as one Minecraft day. The proportions come back as the gestation setting grows. Nested windows are not stages. Peak fertility is the end of heat, not a day after it, and a cycle is heat plus diestrus — clamping those separately double-counts. Lactation is a condition, not a lock. The biologically honest version lets a nursing mare conceive and charges her body condition for it instead. Clock choice matters. Counting world ticks means sleep does not skip a pregnancy; counting world days means it does. A hybrid (pregnancy follows world time, heat follows active ticks) stops a player sleeping through every breeding window. Most of this is built now Heat, natural covering, conception odds that vary by phase, pregnancy, twins, foal heat and nursing are all implemented — common/repro and server/ReproHandler — and are described on fertility & gestation . Golden-carrot breeding still exists alongside them and is still an instant foal, unless a parent is carrying a breeding carrot, which turns it into a pregnancy instead. What is not built is the list immediately above this note: a body condition model, a real lactation lock, and the clock-choice question.
