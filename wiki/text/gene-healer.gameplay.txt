Healer
(Magical gene / phase 3)
A horse that mends the people standing next to it, and carries a red stripe down the middle of its mane that says so. Recessive, so about one wild horse in a hundred and twenty is one — and the sixth of the population carrying a single copy looks like every other horse.

What it does What to look for: a red stripe down the middle of the mane . That marking is the tell, and it is the only way to spot one by eye. What it does: the horse heals players standing next to it. Breeding it: recessive, so a horse needs two copies to do anything. About one wild horse in a hundred and twenty is a healer, but roughly a sixth of all horses carry a single hidden copy — so two ordinary-looking parents throwing a healer is common, and is the usual way people get one.
