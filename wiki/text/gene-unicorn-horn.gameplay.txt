Unicorn horn
(Magical gene / recessive / grows geometry, paints nothing)
A horse with two copies grows a single tapering horn on its forehead — and it is the first thing in this mod that is a piece of the horse rather than a picture on it . Nothing about the coat changes. How long the horn is, how thick, how tightly it spirals and how far it leans are all written on the allele copy and inherited with it, so a horn you like breeds true and a line selected for length keeps getting longer. This gene grows every horn white : its colour, its glow and the dust it sheds are three more genes .

Crossing two of them Finding one Recessive and rare: about one wild horse in four hundred is a unicorn, and roughly one in twenty carries a copy without showing it. So the realistic route is not to wait for one but to notice you have carriers — cross two and about one foal in four grows a horn. A known gene splice carrot for the locus makes each step of that deterministic rather than a coin flip. Two unicorns are not alike The gene grants a horn. Everything about which horn is epigenetic, and the horn is redrawn from those numbers rather than chosen from a list: What Range How it inherits Length A nub barely out of the skull, through short , standard (about twice the height of the head) and long , to a narwhal tusk longer than the whole skull Continuous. Drifts a hair at every breeding, so a line creeps up or down the scale over generations rather than jumping Thickness A needle to a stout cone Continuous, as above Twist Four shapes: smooth (the only one that curves forward), loose , spiral , tight A category. Almost never changes; when it does it changes outright, so a spiral-horned line stays spiral-horned Lean Mostly forward over the face; a few horses carry one raked back over the poll instead Continuous Four genes make a horn This gene decides whether a horse has a horn and what shape it is. Three more finish it, and each does nothing at all to a horse without a horn — so a hornless horse can carry a whole unicorn's worth of colour, glow and dust and pass it on without a sign: Gene What it does to the horn Horn colour White, the rainbow, pink, black, grey or chaos. Two different copies make a two-tone horn: one colour with a tip of the other Horn glow Recessive: two copies and the horn glows in the dark, in its own colour Horn dust Recessive: two copies and the horn sheds a fine falling dust in its own colours A foal born with the gene wears its own small horn from the day it is born; it grows into the full one. Is it good for the horse? It does nothing at all, for good or ill. A horn moves no stat, grants no ability, changes no hitbox, and does not affect what the horse can be ridden through or how it is fed. It is worth exactly what you think it looks worth. If it costs you frames A horn is roughly as much geometry again as the horse carrying it, so a large herd of unicorns is the case worth a setting. Three live in this client’s own config file: parts.enabled — draw grown parts at all. parts.glow — draw the extra full-bright pass a luminous horn needs. Off, it still draws, lit like the rest of the horse. This is the first thing to try if a shader pack makes horns look wrong. parts.detailDistance — how close a horse must be for its horn to be drawn. Turning it off does not take the horn away Every one of those settings gates a drawing and nothing else. A horse with a horn still has it: it breeds it, passes it to its foals, and shows it in the gene list and the browser, and it is back on screen the moment the setting is.
