Aggression
(Magical gene / no coat effect)
What a horse attacks, and when. One locus, one grid: a time of day crossed with what it goes for . Being in the saddle is not protection — a horse that hunts riders throws you off first.

Gene key horsegenetics.aggression Alleles three times × five targets, plus n — see AggressionGene.TIMES and TARGETS Inheritance homozygous recessive, and recessive to the other variants Reaches the game as a temper effect with a when carrying the clock Coat effect none, ever The time of day is a condition, not a verb There were once two temperament verbs for this, night_temper and day_temper , on the argument that a gate written onto every allele by hand would be wrong the first time somebody forgot it. That argument is about a gene file . This locus generates its alleles from a table in Java, so the gate is written once and cannot drift between them — and the general temper verb already carried a when . Both time verbs were deleted. It holds a radius. It must never chase A horse that pursues is a horse that dies forty blocks from its owner, and the owner blames the gene — correctly. Attack what comes within reach and return. Full pursuit and owner-leashed pursuit were both specified and both rejected in design; if a later session wants either, it is reopening a settled call. “Everything” includes other horses, and “herds” does not passive deliberately excludes every equine in MobGroups , because a horse is an Animal and a foal that flees or fights “herds” would otherwise turn on its own band — which is exactly what gap 241 was. That exclusion used to live in the night handler's private matcher and was nearly lost when fleeing moved onto the shared verb. A gene that really means other horses says horses . Half-built: a ridden horse will not pursue The buck works — GeneAbilityHandler.buckIfItIsTheRider ejects the passenger the moment the horse targets them. “Ignores its rider and goes for the target” does not. A ridden horse's movement is driven by its passenger rather than by its navigation, so the melee goal cannot walk it anywhere while somebody is aboard: it will turn and swing at whatever comes to it, and no more. Making it genuinely ignore the reins needs rider control suppressed while it has a target, in the shape of mixin.HorseRearFightMixin . See gap 268 . Some of it can be caught Unlike the night locus it replaced — whose founder table was entirely heterozygous, so no wild horse ever expressed one — this one puts expressing combinations in the wild, and the nastier the target the rarer they are. A horse that hunts monsters is something to find; a horse that hunts everything is something somebody bred. That is not only flavour: passification exists to rescue a wild horse that is aggressive to players, and there has to be one. (Owner's call.) Verified All eight forms of the locus this replaced were confirmed (2026-09-13, as magic_night_temper ). They were never broken, only unwatchable, for three reasons that each looked like “the gene does nothing”: the horse dimension had no night (a dimension_type needs timelines as well as a default_clock ); the biome was the_void , so the monster-targeted forms had nothing to be about; and the fleeing forms were in 4 × 7 stalls, which confirmed the hunting forms at once and hid the flee forms completely.
