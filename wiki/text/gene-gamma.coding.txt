(Magical gene / colour modifiers)

Gamma Two to four diffuse fields of colour a foot or two across, on the barrel and the back of the flank, gradient at every margin and hard-edged at none of them. The hue is not one colour: it sweeps along the horse, so a field near the shoulder and a field near the hip are different colours and the change between them is continuous. Each field carries an outer bloom a few inches past its own edge at a third of its core saturation, falling to nothing. That bloom is what makes it read as light rather than as paint. the outer bloom — SPOTS , painted with RAMP the core of each field — SPOTS , painted with RAMP , and it glows Wild type No gamma.
