Magic pull
(Magical gene / no coat effect)
How much the horse can shift. The fifth magical body-stat locus, beside size , speed , health and jump — and the one whose number is a score out of ten rather than a multiplier . A horse carrying nothing here pulls at 5 ; a Shire pulls at 10 and a Falabella at 1. Both copies count and they add, one allele positive and one negative.

What reads it Three things, all added 2026-09-18. Carts — the score sets a ceiling on how fast this horse can drag a given load, and the horse’s own speed decides how close to that ceiling it gets ( carts §2 ). A second rider — a score of 6 or better and the horse carries two. Machinery — the score and speed blend into the horsegenetics:draft_power attribute that animal-labour mods can read ( compatibility ). The conversion is common/cart/CartDraft and lives there rather than here on purpose: what a score is worth in cart loads is a decision for whatever hitches the horse to something, not a fact about the horse’s genetics.
