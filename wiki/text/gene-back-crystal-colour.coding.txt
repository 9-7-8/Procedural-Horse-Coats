Crystal growth colour
(Magical gene / codominant / colours the crystal growths, paints nothing)
What colour a horse’s crystal growths are. The same colours and rules as horn colour (white, the rainbow, pink, black, grey or chaos), on a locus of its own. Two different copies make the crystals one colour and their points the other. On a horse without crystals it does nothing.

Everything else is AbstractPartColourGene ’s. Its Words carry a split sentence (“the crystals are one, the base, and their points the other”). partGrows answers from the crystal locus, so the short form leaves the colour off a horse without crystals. The split costs no new machinery. A see-through part is drawn in two passes. The blended pass draws its kind’s see-through regions in the base tint, which for the crystals is the CRYSTAL shafts. The solid pass draws everything else in the tip tint, which is the BONE_TIP points. This is the same path the sail takes, with the membrane as the base and the spines as the tip. GrownParts.of never calls boneOnly for the crystals: there is nothing soft to leave off.
