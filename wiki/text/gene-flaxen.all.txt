Flaxen — the chestnut’s pale mane
(Natural gene / phase 1)
A graded lightening of the mane, tail and forelock on a chestnut horse, from a few gold strands to a near-white fall of hair over a red body. It touches nothing else, and it is completely invisible on any horse that makes black hair — which carries it and hands it on regardless.
