Markings — the star and the sock
(Natural gene / phase 1)
The ordinary white a horse can have without carrying any pattern gene at all: a star, a stripe, a snip, a coronet, a sock, a stocking. Most marked horses in life are marked by this and not by splash or sabino, and until now the mod had no way to say so.

Crossing two of them More copies, more white An additive dosage locus, the same shape as sooty and shade : two working alleles that stack, so what matters is how many copies and how strong, not which parent they came from. combination dosage what shows m/m 0 clean — no white anywhere M1/m 1 a star, or a coronet or two above the hooves M1/M1 , M2/m 2 a star and often a snip, socks on a leg or two M2/M1 3 a stripe or a narrow blaze, and socks M2/M2 4 a broad blaze and high stockings Two horses of the same dosage still differ, because each carries its own stored offset and its own face and leg rolls. That is what stops a herd of M1/m horses all wearing the same star. Not every leg is marked Each leg rolls separately and has to clear a threshold before it whitens anything, which is why a three-socked horse with one clean leg is common and four identical socks are not. The rolls are stored, not rolled fresh , so a horse marks the same way every time it is drawn — and its foals inherit the tendency. Pattern genes stack on top of this Markings runs at priority 65, before every white pattern locus ( tobiano is 72, and KIT , MITF , PAX3 and EDNRB run after it).
