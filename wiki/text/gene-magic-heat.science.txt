Magic heat tolerance
(Magical gene / no coat effect)
Better or worse in a hot biome. A tolerant allele, a sensitive allele and a neutral wild type; how much each copy is worth, and whether it goes to speed, jump or both, is written on the copy. Its twin is magic cold tolerance .

What it stands for Real horses do differ in how they cope with heat, and the differences follow where a breed was made. Desert breeds such as the Arabian and the Akhal-Teke are lean, thin-skinned and fine-coated, which sheds heat. Heavy draft breeds carry a lot of muscle for their surface area, often with thick manes and feathering, and are known to struggle in hot, humid weather; anhidrosis, a failure to sweat, turns up in several of them. Cold-country ponies grow dense double coats that work against them in the heat. None of that is one gene. Heat tolerance in real horses is body shape, coat, sweat response and fitness together. The mod makes it a single magical locus so that a player can breed for it, and gives each breed pool a share that follows its history ( the breeds page has the reasons). The magnitude and the split between speed and jump are game choices, not measured quantities.
