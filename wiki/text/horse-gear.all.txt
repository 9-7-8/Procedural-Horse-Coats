Horse gear: the nineteen slots
(Gameplay / non-genetic)
Everything a horse wears, in one place. A Gear tab on the horse information screen holds nineteen slots laid out on a picture of the horse itself — head, mane and tail, body, chest, a slot per leg and a slot per hoof, and two for saddlebags. Two of them are real vanilla equipment slots and the other seventeen are a data attachment of our own, which is the constraint the whole system is shaped by. The slots exist; almost nothing to put in them does yet. The roster, the storage and the screen shipped first on purpose — every piece that follows is one row in a tag. What is still planned is on this page’s own Roadmap tab, which is the single home for gear plans.
