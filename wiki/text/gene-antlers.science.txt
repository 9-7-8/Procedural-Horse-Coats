Antlers
(Magical gene / dominant, sex-limited allele / grows geometry, paints nothing)
One copy and the horse grows a rack of antlers from the top of its skull — on a mare or a stallion with Ant , on a stallion only with Antm . No two racks are alike: how big, how many tines, how far they reach, how thick and what shade of bone are all written on the allele and inherited with it. The second gene in the mod, after the unicorn horn , that grows a piece of the horse rather than colouring it.

Analogous, not measured There is no antler locus in horses, and these are invented names for invented genes (decision D8). The design borrows from deer, and says so rather than claiming a mechanism. Real antlers are bone, grown from pedicles on the skull and cast and regrown every year under the control of testosterone — which is why in almost every deer only the males carry them. Reindeer and caribou are the exception, with antlers on both sexes. Ant and Antm are that difference as two alleles: the reindeer arrangement and the red-deer one. The mod does not cast antlers (decision D4): a rack here is permanent, with no velvet and no year clock. And it does not borrow a real horn gene's name. The sheep horn locus, RXFP2 , is the famous horned-versus-polled gene in sheep, but whether anything like it governs antlers in deer is not established, so the page makes no such claim. Why dominant, when the horn is recessive A unicorn is meant to be a find, and recessive makes it one. Antlers are meant to be a line : dominant means one antlered parent is enough, so a breeder who has one can keep it. The sex-limited allele puts the find back in — a mare carrying Antm looks like nothing, and is the horse that makes antlered colts. Why symmetric by default Real racks are rarely perfectly symmetric, and reindeer are often strikingly lopsided. But a lopsided rack on every wild horse reads as a rendering bug before it reads as biology. So the owner's call (D3) is that wild racks mirror exactly, and lopsidedness is a thing a breed brings — the Rimehart is that breed.
