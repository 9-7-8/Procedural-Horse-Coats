Part built / one part ships, the rest planned Attached model parts Meshes hung on the horse model, driven by a gene or by a breed — a unicorn horn, a row of dorsal spines, a pair of wings, feathered hooves. They need no animation of their own, because they are parented to a part of the horse that is already animated. It is the mod’s first visual that is not a texture , which is why this is a page rather than a line on a list. The foundation is built and two parts use it : the unicorn horn and antlers , which an antlered breed, the Rimehart , carries on every horse. Spines, crystals, hoof feathering and wings are the same machinery plus a generator each, and are still on the Roadmap tab . Built, and not yet seen in a running game The layer, the mesh cache, the horn and the antlers are unit-tested and compile. A horn has been seen on screen, following the head (2026-09-30); no antler has , and its placement is computed from the horse model’s boxes rather than tuned against a live animal. The open checks are on the horn’s and the antlers’ Verification tabs, and this page’s .
