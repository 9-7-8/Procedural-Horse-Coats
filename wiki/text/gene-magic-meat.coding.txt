Averaged, not summed Every other epigenetic locus here that reads both copies adds them. This one averages, and the difference is a claim about what the number means. A per-copy percentage on the body-stat genes is a dose : two copies of an allele that each push the horse are genuinely twice the push. The quantity of meat on an animal is not a dose, it is a property of the animal — a homozygote is one horse, not two half-horses.
