The four posts
(Blocks / villagers)
The four equestrians ’ workstations. Put one down near an unemployed villager and you get that trade, and only that trade — which is why there are four of them and not one.

What they are for All four are shapeless, and all four start the same: 2 horse hair + 2 fences of any kind. The last three slots say which post it is. Leatherworker’s Post — + 3 leather Scientist’s Post — + 2 glass bottles + 1 paper Supplier’s Post — + 2 more horse hair + 1 carrot Metalsmith’s Post — + 3 iron ingots Place one near an unemployed villager and they take up that trade. One villager per post , so a second scientist needs a second Scientist’s Post. That is the whole of how an equestrian comes to exist — no house, no bed and no special structure needed. The blocks do nothing else; they are the job sites and that is the point of them. Which post you put down decides which shop you get , and there is no way to change a villager’s mind afterwards short of breaking the post while they are still Novice, the way vanilla works for every other profession. The leatherworker sells tack, the scientist carrots and breed eggs, the metalsmith armour, and the supplier everything else — and the supplier is the only one who buys horse hair, which makes him the one most players will want first. The trade tables are on the villagers page. You will meet all four without crafting any, if you find a cowboy’s homestead : the four posts stand in a row along the house’s frontage with the Cowboy Hitch on the other side of the doorstep and the bench at the end, with a villager waiting at each one. Break one and you get it back. Worth saying because for a while the hitch did not: it shipped with no loot table at all, so breaking one destroyed it. All five drop themselves now.
