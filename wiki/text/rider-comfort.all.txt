Rider comfort
(Gameplay / non-genetic)
Five small things that make riding less irritating, borrowed from other mods that already do them. None touches genetics, and each is small enough to ship alone. One of the five has shipped — a lead now comes back to you rather than being left on the ground when this mod teleports the horse wearing it. The other four are still plans, and the last of them — fading the horse out as you look down — touches the coat itself and is the only one with a real unknown in it.
