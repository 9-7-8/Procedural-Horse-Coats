Rider comfort
(Gameplay / non-genetic)
Five small things that make riding less irritating, borrowed from other mods that already do them. None touches genetics, and each is small enough to ship alone. All five have shipped. A lead comes back to you whether this mod teleported the horse out from under it or the leash simply snapped; you can mine from the saddle at the speed you would standing there; ALT + E opens your own pack from the saddle; a piece of tack goes on with a right-click instead of a trip through the Gear tab; and the horse you are riding fades out of your way as you look down at the ground.

All five have shipped. A lead comes back to you whether this mod teleported the horse out from under it or the leash simply snapped; you can mine from the saddle at the speed you would standing there; ALT + E opens your own pack from the saddle; a piece of tack goes on with a right-click instead of a trip through the Gear tab; and the horse you are riding fades out of your way as you look down at the ground. All of it is built Harvested from the quality-of-life cluster of the survey backlog and moved here whole from the main roadmap , as five pieces to be built one per session. The last of them went in on 2026-09-29, so this page has no Roadmap tab any more and has left that index by itself — what is left is the Gameplay and Coding tabs for what exists, and a Verification tab for the parts nobody has played yet. Every piece but one shipped with a config switch , per the config sweep . The exception is the inventory keybind , which did so deliberately: an unbound key is already the switch. Config — the switch each of these wants The coat pipeline Living beside other mods The returned lead was the first of the five and took an afternoon; mining from the saddle was the second and took less; the inventory keybind was the third and was two classes and a lang key; the tack click was the fourth and was smaller than planned, because two thirds of it turned out to be vanilla's already; the lead's other half was the fifth and much the largest, two mixins for something that reads like one line. The fade was the last and the only one whose result nobody has looked at — the code can prove a horse is drawn on a blending pipeline and cannot prove it looks like a horse, which is why its Verification entry is longer than its Coding one.
