Rider comfort
(Gameplay / non-genetic)
Five small things that make riding less irritating, borrowed from other mods that already do them. None touches genetics, and each is small enough to ship alone. Four of the five have shipped — a lead now comes back to you rather than being left on the ground when this mod teleports the horse wearing it, you can mine from the saddle at the speed you would standing there, ALT + E opens your own pack from the saddle, and a piece of tack goes on with a right-click instead of a trip through the Gear tab. What is left is the other half of the lead, and — the only one with a real unknown in it — fading the horse out as you look down, which touches the coat itself.
