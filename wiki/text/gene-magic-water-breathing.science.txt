Magic water breathing
(Magical gene / no coat effect)
How long the horse lasts under water before it starts to drown. The graded counterpart of the underwater_breathing traversal flag, which is absolute — and independent of magic swim speed , because crossing a river quickly and surviving the bottom of a lake are different problems.

The pair it forms with swim speed Two loci, independent draws, and between them they describe every useful kind of water horse: the ferry (fast, ordinary lungs), the diver (ordinary speed, long breath), and the one that is both, which nobody catches. They were deliberately not built as one gene with two knobs. A single locus would mean the two facts were inherited together for ever, and the interesting horses are exactly the ones where they came apart — a beautiful swimmer that cannot stay under, or a horse that can walk a lake bed at a crawl. What breeding does to it The percentage on each copy drifts slightly at every breeding, so a line selected for long breath keeps improving on the horses it started from rather than converging on them. That is the same mechanism every epigenetic locus here uses, and it is why the wild range is a starting point rather than a ceiling.
