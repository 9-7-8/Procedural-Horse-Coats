Undead horses
(Breeds & spawning / built, not yet seen in game)
Undeath is two genes, not two entity types. A skeleton horse is a horse with two copies of the skeleton allele, and a zombie horse one with two copies of zombie , so the genome, the coat pipeline, breeding, records, bond and the body stats all reach them because they reach every horse. Vanilla’s own zombie and skeleton horses — in old saves, from skeleton traps, from /summon and eggs — turn into this mod’s horses the first time they are ticked: a Graveborn Warmblood or a Great Valley Skeleton Horse — or, in the Nether, a Blackened Skeleton Horse , which also runs wild there.

What happens to your vanilla undead horses Nothing happens to them until a server operator turns conversion on. It ships off, because it cannot be undone. An operator first runs /horseundead test : one vanilla skeleton horse and then one zombie horse are spawned beside them (tamed to them, named and saddled), converted by the real converter, and checked. Once that passes, /horseundead enable turns it on for the whole server ( below ). From then on, each vanilla undead horse becomes one of this mod’s horses the first time its chunk loads, with nothing more to do. It keeps its name, owner, tame state, saddle, armour, lead, age and the share of its health it had left , and gets a pedigree and a coat of its own. Its speed, jump and hearts are re-rolled from its new genes , so a zombie horse comes out slower and much tougher than it was, a skeleton horse quicker and frailer. A horse you already owned arrives calm toward you and stays that way; an untamed one is as hostile as the breed is (below) until it is offered its item. Its bond starts at zero. A horse with you on it waits until you get off. A skeleton trap is still a trap. It springs as vanilla’s does; the horse it leaves behind converts after. Other mods’ undead horses are left alone. It can be switched off again with /horseundead disable or undead.convert in the server config ( below ). Off, vanilla undead horses stay vanilla; ones already converted stay what they became. The two breeds Neither is sold, stabled or found in a wild herd: they come only from conversion and from their spawn eggs (the Nether’s Blackened is the wild one). Both are hostile to their rider after dark until passified, have black eyes , do not burn in daylight, and are drawn at the size of vanilla’s undead horses. Great Valley Skeleton Horse Graveborn Warmblood Body quick (speed 8), a good jumper, fragile slow (speed 2), a poor jumper, very hard to kill (health 10), strong Eats bones — one heals it completely raw meat Passified by one bone, for good two to four rotten flesh , for one to three minutes — tame it inside the calm Drops one to three bones instead of leather one to three rotten flesh instead of leather Also never drowns; soul fire at its hooves; sometimes a horn of bone (below) — Strains Bleached (clean white bone), Weathered (yellowed, crazed and dusty) Fresh, Long-buried (grave dust) The Blackened Skeleton Horse The Nether’s skeleton horse, and the commonest horse there — rare, but more common than the Netherhorse, the Nightmare or the Mirrorgazer. Everything the Great Valley horse is, charred black (its horns stay bone), fireproof , and wild: herds of it on the Nether’s floors, in the dark. A vanilla skeleton horse that converts in the Nether becomes one; anywhere else it becomes a Great Valley. Horns of bone Every horn-like part a horse can grow is in the skeleton’s pool — a unicorn horn, antlers, ram’s horns, dragon horns and, since 2026-10-05, a row of dorsal spines , a back sail of bare rays with no membrane, shoulder and hip plates and tusks (a narwhal horn, boar tusks or sabre fangs, one of the three; owner, 2026-10-08) — all in bone, none glowing or shedding. The one exception is crystal growths : a crystal has no bone, so a skeleton grows ordinary see-through crystals in any colour (owner, 2026-10-05). About one skeleton in ten wears one kind, one in twenty two kinds, one in eighty three, and about one in 640 four. None wears more than four, and the rest have none. Every part with bone in it has a bone version like this, so a part that ships later can join them. Breeding them They breed as any horse does. Two skeletons give skeleton foals; a skeleton and an ordinary horse give ordinary-looking foals that carry it, and two carriers can surprise you. The diet, temper and drops ride on their own genes, so a bred-down skeleton can have an ordinary appetite and a gentle temper and still be bones. The switch undead.convert (server config, default false since 0.5.042; the owner reversed treatment D2 on 2026-10-03, so a server opts in only after watching it work). /horseundead (operators, level 2) says whether it is on and whether a test has passed since the server started; test converts one skeleton and one zombie horse beside the operator; enable , refused until a test has passed, sets the key and saves it; disable clears it. Each test subject carries the persistent flag horsegenetics:convert_anyway , which lets the converter take that one horse on its own entity tick with the key off; the command never converts from its own call. Removing the mod, or turning this off, leaves any converted horse as a plain minecraft:horse , as every other horse of this mod is left.
