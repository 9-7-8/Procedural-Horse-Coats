Undead horses
(Breeds & spawning / built, not yet seen in game)
Undeath is two genes, not two entity types. A skeleton horse is a horse with two copies of the skeleton allele, and a zombie horse one with two copies of zombie , so the genome, the coat pipeline, breeding, records, bond and the body stats all reach them because they reach every horse. Vanilla’s own zombie and skeleton horses — in old saves, from skeleton traps, from /summon and eggs — turn into this mod’s horses the first time they are ticked: a Graveborn Warmblood or a Great Valley Skeleton Horse .

What happens to your vanilla undead horses Each one becomes one of this mod’s horses the first time its chunk loads, with nothing to do. It keeps its name, owner, tame state, saddle, armour, lead, age and the share of its health it had left , and gets a pedigree and a coat of its own. Its speed, jump and hearts are re-rolled from its new genes , so a zombie horse comes out slower and much tougher than it was, a skeleton horse quicker and frailer. A horse you already owned arrives calm toward you and stays that way; an untamed one is as hostile as the breed is (below) until it is offered its item. Its bond starts at zero. A horse with you on it waits until you get off. A skeleton trap is still a trap. It springs as vanilla’s does; the horse it leaves behind converts after. Other mods’ undead horses are left alone. A server can switch it off: undead.convert in the server config ( below ). Off, vanilla undead horses stay vanilla; ones already converted stay what they became. The two breeds Neither is sold, stabled or found in a wild herd: they come only from conversion and from their spawn eggs. Both are hostile to their rider after dark until passified, have black eyes , do not burn in daylight, and are drawn at the size of vanilla’s undead horses. Great Valley Skeleton Horse Graveborn Warmblood Body quick (speed 8), a good jumper, fragile slow (speed 2), a poor jumper, very hard to kill (health 10), strong Eats bones — one heals it completely raw meat Passified by one bone, for good two to four rotten flesh , for one to three minutes — tame it inside the calm Drops one to three bones instead of leather one to three rotten flesh instead of leather Also never drowns; soul fire at its hooves; sometimes a horn of bone (below) — Strains Bleached (clean white bone), Weathered (yellowed, crazed and dusty) Fresh, Long-buried (grave dust) Horns of bone Every horn-like part a horse can grow is in the skeleton’s pool — a unicorn horn, antlers, ram’s horns and dragon horns — all in bone, none glowing or shedding. About one skeleton in ten wears one kind, one in twenty two kinds, one in eighty three, and about one in 640 all four. The rest have none. Breeding them They breed as any horse does. Two skeletons give skeleton foals; a skeleton and an ordinary horse give ordinary-looking foals that carry it, and two carriers can surprise you. The diet, temper and drops ride on their own genes, so a bred-down skeleton can have an ordinary appetite and a gentle temper and still be bones. The switch undead.convert (server config, default true ). Removing the mod, or turning this off, leaves any converted horse as a plain minecraft:horse , as every other horse of this mod is left.
