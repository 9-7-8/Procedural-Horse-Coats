LYCAN — the werewolf locus
(Magical recessive / no coat effect)
A horse with two matching copies stops being a horse at nightfall . Not a horse wearing a wolf: a real wolf, which breeds with wolves, is tamed with bones, and is a horse again at dawn with the same name and the same pedigree. Every peaceful land animal in the game is a shape on this locus, and every one of them is recessive to the wild type and to each other.

Crossing two of them Two copies of the same allele, or nothing The rule the whole locus is built on Wlf/n is a horse. Wlf/Cat is also a horse — two shapes, and it settles on neither. Only a matched pair shifts. That is what makes a locus this wide worth breeding rather than a slot machine. If any one of its many alleles showed, a shifter would be common and which animal you got would be luck. Requiring the same allele twice makes it a search with a target: two wild shifters caught in different biomes are probably different animals, and their foals are horses. The disappointment is the game. Combination Outcome What happens at dusk n/n wild nothing Wlf/n lycan-carrier nothing — and never a founder Wlf/Cat lycan-mismatched nothing; both shapes are carried and both are passed on Wlf/Wlf lycan-wlf it becomes a wolf The mismatched horse is its own named outcome rather than a fold into the wild type, because it is a genuinely different thing to be holding: it is one parent of two different lines at once. Nothing that swims, nothing that flies, nothing hostile The rule, since 2026-09-24 A shape is only on this locus if it is a peaceful animal that walks on land . The fish, the squids, the axolotl, the bat, the bee, the parrot, the allay and the happy ghast were all shapes once, and are not any more. The other two per-mob loci — leader of the pack and the spawner — are happy to name a squid or a bee, because there the animal turns up beside the horse. This one replaces the horse, and a body is not a costume. A were-cod left a horse drowning in air on a hillside; a were-bat was over the treeline two seconds after dusk with somebody's mare inside it. Each of the three is the same complaint in the end: the owner cannot get their horse back. It is enforced twice. The allele set is the roster's ground-dwelling half, so the alleles do not exist to be bred; and the shift itself asks the animal the game hands it — its spawn category, its navigation, whether it is marked hostile — and refuses a body that swims, flies or hunts. The second check is the one that will still hold when the roster is built from the live registry and starts naming mobs from other people's mods. Identity survives the night The horse goes into an attachment on the animal, as its complete Entity.saveWithoutId tag — which on NeoForge carries the mod’s data attachments as well. So the record, the name, the pedigree, the bond , the cooldowns and the cowboy’s brand all cross the night untouched, and so does the horse’s UUID . That last one is the load-bearing part: a shifted horse is the same horse , not a lookalike, so the ancestry database , the breeding roster and the stalls are all undisturbed by the whole business. Storing an entity inside another entity Unusual, and worth being explicit about. The alternative was to keep the horse alive somewhere off-map and teleport it back, which needs a holding dimension, a reaper for the ones whose animal died, and a rule for what happens when the chunk unloads. A tag that travels with the animal has none of those problems: unload the chunk and both go to disk together, and kill the animal and the horse dies with it — which is exactly what should happen, and is why the attachment is deliberately not copyOnDeath . What a shifted animal does that an ordinary one does not Behaviour Detail It trails a cloud A faint puff of coloured dust round the body whenever it moves on the ground — “surrounded by”, not “trailing from”. The colour is epigenetic , and it is the only way to tell a were-cow from a cow. It holds a grudge Hit it and it follows and strikes back — the wolf’s temper, given to every form, so a were-chicken really will chase you across a field. It forgets at sunrise or after three seconds without line of sight, the same memory a wild horse herd uses. It cannot be ridden The point of the gene is the night you cannot ride home. The grudge is one goal rather than the usual melee-plus-target pair, because the forms have no attack : a chicken has no ATTACK_DAMAGE attribute at all, so the vanilla melee goal has nothing to read. Adding the attribute to every mob type the locus can name would hand every sheep, rabbit and wandering trader in the world an attack stat it never had, for the sake of the one in ten thousand that is secretly a horse. Dealing a flat three points by hand touches nothing that is not already ours. The shapes Every vanilla mob that is both non-hostile and walks on land , minus the horse family, because a horse that turns into a horse at night is not a gene. A mechanical rule, not a taste That is why the list contains a strider and a wandering trader without anybody having to defend either, and it is why the two golems, the villager, the copper golem and the mannequin are out — they are MobCategory.MISC . The frog and the turtle stay: both walk, and neither drowns in air. The tadpole does, and does not. The mob ids are strings , because common/ may not import Minecraft, and the translator resolves them against the live registry. An id this game version has never heard of simply never shifts, and says so once in the log. So the chance of meeting a shifter moves with the length of the table above, while the chance of meeting a were-wolf in particular does not — which is the right way round, since the one animal you are hunting for should not get rarer because the game added a mob. Every shifter you meet in the world is one you can watch shift; every carrier in the world is one somebody bred. Dusk, dawn, and dimensions with no night The check is Level.isDarkOutside() rather than !isBrightOutside() , and those are not the same thing: both are false in a dimension with a fixed time of day. Asking it this way makes the Nether and the End permanent day for this gene, which is the right answer — a place with no nightfall should not hold a horse in wolf shape forever. The horse dimension used to be on that list, but it has real nights now, so its shifters change like anyone's. Dawn sets a horse down on solid ground. The horse comes back where its animal is, unless there is nothing under the animal: no solid block or water within three blocks below. Then it comes back where the animal last stood on the ground, or, if that is not known, where it changed at dusk. This was written for the flying forms — until 2026-09-15 a were-bat could wake up in mid-air over the void and fall out of the world ( gap 243 ) — and it stays now that they are gone, because a walking animal still finds ledges, boats and a floor somebody mined out overnight. An animal that dies in the night still takes its horse with it, wherever it is. Its gene carrot The known carrot hands over one copy, never two — a shifter is the reward for breeding two carriers of the same shape, and a carrot that produced one outright would skip the entire locus.
