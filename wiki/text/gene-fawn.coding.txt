Fawn
(Magical gene / spots and rings)
A soft-edged darkened topline with a single flowing line of small round spots running along it, neck to rump, and a pale underside to the tail. A third allele carries the same marking worked in greys instead of white - dark where the pale form is light, and the same number of steps.

The file common/src/main/resources/horsegenetics/genes/fawn.json — a data-driven gene , not a Java class. It is listed in the index.json beside it and registered by Genes ' class initialiser at startup. The re-toning is a mapping rather than a repaint: every achromatic target moves into the dark end of the scale keeping its order and its spacing, and every SATURATED one is left alone, because that colour is the gene rather than its lightness. Fawn A darkened topline in the manner of sooty - a soft band down the crest, back and croup with no edge to it at either end - and, sitting on that dark band and nowhere else, one consecutive run of small round spots somewhere between the neck and the rump. Never two clusters with a gap between them, never a spiral or a pattern, just a flowing line of them, and all of them small. They keep off the face, the belly and the legs. Most fawn horses also carry a stylised eyeliner, a pangare-like lightening underneath, and a pale underside to the tail. the darkened topline the run sits on — AXIS , painted with TOWARD the flowing line of spots — SPOTS × STROKES × AXIS , painted with TOWARD eyeliner — SPOTS × AXIS , painted with TOWARD the underside of the tail — AXIS , painted with TOWARD Black Fawn The same marking as the white form with the light taken out of it: the same 2 steps, moved to the dark end of the scale. the darkened topline the run sits on — AXIS , painted with TOWARD the flowing line of spots — SPOTS × STROKES × AXIS , painted with TOWARD eyeliner — SPOTS × AXIS , painted with TOWARD the underside of the tail — AXIS , painted with TOWARD Coloured Fawn The same run of spots in a colour the line carries rather than in white. Fully recessive: it takes two copies, and a single copy beside the white allele shows white.
