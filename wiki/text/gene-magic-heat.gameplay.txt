Magic heat tolerance
(Magical gene / no coat effect)
Better or worse in a hot biome. A tolerant allele, a sensitive allele and a neutral wild type; how much each copy is worth, and whether it goes to speed, jump or both, is written on the copy. Its twin is magic cold tolerance .

Built, and not yet seen in game The gene and its breed pools are tested. What it does to a horse in a desert has not been watched yet; see the Verification tab . What it does Combination In a hot biome two tolerant copies markedly faster, or jumps markedly higher, or some of each one tolerant copy somewhat better one tolerant, one sensitive close to normal — the two pull against each other one sensitive copy somewhat worse two sensitive copies markedly slower, or jumps markedly lower, or some of each anything with no variant copy no difference, anywhere Hot means every desert, badlands and savanna, the jungles, the mangrove swamp and the Nether. Anywhere else the horse is exactly its normal self. Who carries it Desert and hot-country breeds tend to be tolerant: the Arabian, Akhal-Teke and Marwari nearly all are, and the Andalusian, Lusitano, Caspian, Karabakh, Banker Horse, both Paso Finos and the Mustang often are. Heavy feathered drafts and cold-country horses tend to be sensitive: the Friesian, Clydesdale, Shire, Belgian and Icelandic often are. The full list is on the breeds page . A Feral Mixed horse carries one copy now and then. Health None. A sensitive horse is slower in the heat, never hurt by it.
