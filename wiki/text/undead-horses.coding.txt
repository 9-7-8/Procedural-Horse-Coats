Undead horses
(Breeds & spawning / built, not yet seen in game)
Undeath is two genes, not two entity types. A skeleton horse is a horse with two copies of the skeleton allele, and a zombie horse one with two copies of zombie , so the genome, the coat pipeline, breeding, records, bond and the body stats all reach them because they reach every horse. Vanilla’s own zombie and skeleton horses — in old saves, from skeleton traps, from /summon and eggs — turn into this mod’s horses the first time they are ticked: a Graveborn Warmblood or a Great Valley Skeleton Horse .

The breed sets the bundle (settled 2026-10-01) The question was whether one undead locus may bundle several effects, against “a concept is a breed, not a gene” . It does not need to: each undeath gene is only the visible undeath (the look, the black eyes, “undead” to the mob groups, and the skeleton’s breathing). Diet, temper, passification, drops, particles and stats are separate, existing loci the two breeds pin. The only new genes are the two undeath loci; the rest is new alleles on old ones: Dbone on diet , Bne / Rot on magic item drop, Blk on both iris loci, Bon on horn colour and dragon horn colour, and offerings 14 ( minecraft:bone ) and 15 ( minecraft:rotten_flesh ) on passification . Every one was appended, so no saved horse’s allele moved, and none has a wild share. The converter — server/UndeadHorseConverter On EntityTickEvent.Post , under HorseFoundingTickHandler ’s budget (one budget for every founding), never on the join event: writing Attributes.SCALE from anywhere else has crashed the chunk system before. A small table maps an exact EntityType to a pool name — SKELETON_HORSE to skeleton , ZOMBIE_HORSE to zombie — and is the extension point for another mod’s undead horse; subclasses are not converted. Waits while a player rides it, while it is an armed skeleton trap ( SkeletonHorse.isTrap() , public in 26.1.2), while it is dead or dying. Skips for good a horse whose persistent data carries horsegenetics:keep_vanilla . Saves the undead horse whole ( saveWithoutId ) and loads the tag into a new minecraft:horse created with EntitySpawnReason.CONVERSION . The two share AbstractHorse ’s save format, so the UUID, position, age, custom name, tame flag and owner, saddle and armour, lead, tags, persistence and other mods’ persistent data all come across with no copying here. Founds it from a breed whose sheet says "undead_of" that pool, picked uniformly with SeededRng(uuid, "undead-conversion") — the same seed for the sex, genome and name, so a replay makes the same horse. A custom name becomes the record’s barn name over a rolled registered name. The record’s owner mirrors the entity’s. The body is re-resolved and the health fraction restored. A tamed horse has every passification route settled FOREVER for its owner (D25). Swaps : lets the old lead go without its item (the tag holds the same lead; dropping it too would duplicate it), ejects passengers, discard() s the old entity (no death, drops or notices), adds the new one, remounts the non-player passengers and syncs coat and care to trackers. Discard comes first because two live entities cannot share a UUID. Fails safe. If the level refuses the new horse, a vanilla copy is rebuilt from the same tag and put back. Any exception leaves the original untouched; after five tries it is left vanilla until the level reloads. The failure mode is the horse still being there, and still vanilla. Gametests: undead_horses_convert (a tamed, named, saddled, half-health zombie and a plain skeleton: one entity per UUID, a real record, the gene, the breed, owner, name, saddle, health fraction and passification all kept), undead_trap_waits , undead_keep_vanilla . The art — vanilla’s own sheets, converted Vanilla’s zombie and skeleton horses are the horse mesh with another texture — one layer definition in LayerDefinitions , stored at 1/1.1 of the horse’s scale, which is why both breeds are size [0.86, 0.95] . Their sheets are copied into common/sheet-sources/minecraft/ and converted to this mod’s layout by coat/skin/HorseSheetConverter ( ./gradlew :common:convertHorseSheets , from common/sheet-sources/manifest.txt ). It scales by an integer factor (nearest neighbour up, box average down), re-lays the adult’s shared leg and ear patches into the HD mesh’s own patches as read from HorseSkinGeometry.patches , and in vanilla mode applies vanilla’s cube mirror to the left legs (every face flipped and the two side strips swapped — read from 26.1.2’s ModelPart$Cube ). In straight mode it reproduces the shipped white template to the pixel, which HorseSheetConverterTest holds it to. The notice is in THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md . The undeath genes reach the art through CoatSheetContribution : each names its sheets ( skeleton_adult …), every host loads them into the LutSet ’s sheets beside the LUTs (the game’s texture factory, LutSet.fromRegistry for every tool, and DesignerApi.setSheet from java.js ), and the composer runs the zombie’s seed and the skeleton’s shape pass. See the two gene pages. Two breed-file fields "undead_of": "skeleton" | "zombie" joins a breed to a pool. Joining a pool is editing that breed’s file; the converter needs no change. "count_groups" : a set of loci where the sheet weighs how many of them express and the founder then picks which — how the skeleton’s horns are drawn (weights [5350, 640, 320, 80, 10] ). See the breed format . Also built with it MobGroups : the undead group adds a horse that Undeath.kindOf says is undead (two copies, never a carrier). mixin/HorseUndeadVoiceMixin : vanilla’s undead horse sounds on an undead horse — a mixin because the sound verb plays beside a horse’s voice and cannot replace it. Passification.onOffer runs at HIGH and lets go of an item once that player’s calm is permanent, so the skeleton’s bone is its offering until paid and its food after. The bone diet, the two drops ( bones , rotten_flesh on the item_drop verb), and the undead breeds’ black-eye request. Owner’s calls (2026-10-02) Every horn-like part in the skeleton’s pool, all bone, no glow or emission; a Bon allele for the two horn colour loci. Odds P(k) = P(k−1) / 2 k−1 from ten percent, through a new count group rather than independent loci or strains. “Cannot drown” is the absolute underwater_breathing flag on the skeleton gene, not the graded water-breathing locus. Weathered is a dunalino — red dun with one cream — which took the pink out of the old bone in the preview (double cream washed out; champagne went grey). The converter is Java in common/ with a Gradle task, not a script.
