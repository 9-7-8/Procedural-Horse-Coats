What a server owner or a single player may turn down, turn off or retune without touching the code. Some config already exists — this page is its plan, not its reference A config surface is in the game today: a small, scattered set of hand-written options across ServerConfig and ClientConfig , plus two genuinely thorough generated blocks in BreedSpawningConfig and CartsConfig . What follows describes that surface and says what should become of it; it is not a list of the options themselves, and the option list is not documented anywhere in the wiki yet . Read the config classes for what is actually there.
