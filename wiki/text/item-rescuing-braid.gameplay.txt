Rescuing braid
(Gameplay / non-genetic)
A one-use piece of mane or tail gear that turns a killing blow into a trip home instead of just a reprieve. The instant the horse would die the braid breaks, and the horse — with you on it, if you were riding — is standing in its own stall , bolting. It is the first thing in the game that goes in the mane or the tail.

What it is Craft a braid from any wool, a horse hair and an ender pearl , work it into a horse’s mane or tail on the Gear tab , and forget about it. It does nothing at all until the day something is about to kill that horse, and then it spends itself: the blow is refused, the braid is gone, and the horse is at home. It is the second thing in the mod that saves a horse without being used, and the two are deliberately different instruments. An emergency stasis chamber is yours — carried, covering every horse you own, and it answers by taking the animal out of the world into a bottle you then have to find and open. A braid is the horse’s — worn, covering only the animal wearing it, and it answers by putting it somewhere you will walk past tomorrow, still an animal, still saddled, standing where it lives. Where it sends a horse To that horse’s own stall. Failing that — no stall, or the stall has been built over, filled up or pulled down since — to your holding pen . Those are the same two destinations, picked in the same order, that the browser’s Send home button already uses, and it lands the horse by the same rules a ticket does: it will not put an animal down inside a wall, and it reaches across worlds. A braid is not bound to anywhere. It works out where home is at the moment it breaks, which means one in a chest works for whichever horse you put it on, a braid can never be pointing at a stall you took down last week, and moving a horse to a new stall needs nothing done to the braid. The price is that the item cannot tell you in advance where it will send a horse — and that a horse with no stall and no holding pen is wearing one for nothing. That last case is not silent: you get one line saying so, and the braid is not spent . It is still in the hair the day you hang a sign up. What actually happens, in order Three separate things try to keep a horse alive now, and they run in a fixed order so they add up rather than fight: Last stand goes first. A horse’s free once-per-recovery save refuses the blow, leaves it on half a heart and gives it its few seconds of immunity, with the totem particles. Then the braid. It sees a horse standing on that sliver and takes it home — so the horse arrives inside the immunity window, which is the whole point of doing it in this order. A horse teleported out of a fight and then killed by the first thing it lands beside has been moved rather than saved. Then the emergency chamber , if you are carrying one, as the net behind both. It never sees a horse a braid has already sent home, so nothing is wasted by the ordering — the bottle you did not spend is still in your pocket for the next horse. A braid still works when the last stand has nothing left to give — a horse that has not healed back up since the last time it was saved — which is the case it matters most in. You come with it If you are riding when the braid breaks, you arrive too. That is the one thing this feature must not get wrong: being separated from your horse by the item that was supposed to save you both is worse than the blow. Within one world you simply travel with it; across worlds the horse is re-created on the far side, so you are sent after it and put back on. And it comes up bolting . A horse that has just been held on half a heart is well under the health it runs for its life at, and whatever hurt it counts as a fresh threat for a few more seconds, so the ordinary escape behaviour takes over the moment it lands — reins thrown, at a gallop, around its own stall. It settles as soon as the fright wears off. Two slots, two lives A horse has a mane slot and a tail slot, and a braid in each is two independent saves . The mane one goes first; the tail one is still there for the next time. That is the same answer the emergency chamber gives to wanting a second save — carry two — and it is what stops the second hair slot being a slot that exists and cannot be used for the thing it is for. The colour A braid is drawn as a plait worked into the mane or the tail, in whatever colour it has been dyed, and it is dyed exactly like leather armour : put it in a crafting grid with any dye, or several, and wash it out in a cauldron if you change your mind. An undyed one is the colour of horse hair. The colour does nothing. It is the one place on a piece of tack this mod allows pure decoration, because the item’s job is already paid for — see utility, not decoration , and the same split the Tack Dyeing Bench draws between what a saddle does and what colour it is. What it refuses A foal wears nothing , braids included — every gear slot is closed until a horse grows up. Only horses. A donkey or a mule gets the last stand like everything else, but stalls are a horse’s, so a braid has nowhere to send one. A wild horse is nobody’s , so there is no holding pen to fall back to and no stall to look up.
