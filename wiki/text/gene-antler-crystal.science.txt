Crystal antlers
(Magical gene / recessive / turns the antlers to crystal, paints nothing)
Two copies and a horse's antlers are crystal: the beams and tines see-through in a gem colour, the points solid. Which gem is inherited. On a horse without antlers this gene does nothing at all.

There is no real locus here A magical gene with no real counterpart. It is a modifier of the antlers rather than a part of its own (decision D6): it can only change a rack the antler locus has already grown. Crystal growths on the body, if they come, are a separate gene with a separate part.
