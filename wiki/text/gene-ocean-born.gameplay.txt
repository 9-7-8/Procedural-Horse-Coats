Ocean-born
(Magical gene / no coat effect)
Neither the horse nor its rider can drown. That is all it does, and it is deliberately all it does — the four other water loci are staying separate, and this is the one piece none of them covered.

Built, and not yet seen in game The gene, its inheritance and its founder table exist and are covered by the test suite; the translator that turns it into behaviour was written against the 26.1.2 sources and is flagged unverified in its own comments. Nothing on this page has been watched happening to a horse. What to look at, and what is most likely to be wrong, is on the Verification tab . What it does Combination Outcome Ocn/Ocn horse and rider never drown anything else nothing It is one of five, not a replacement for them The water side of the mod is deliberately spread across separate loci, and breeding a genuinely amphibious horse means collecting all of them: Waterborn — walks on the surface, and can be milked for water. Magic swim speed — how fast it crosses. Magic water breathing — how long it lasts under, as a dial. Ocean-born — whether drowning happens at all, for both of you. Health Removes drowning entirely for horse and rider. No cost.
