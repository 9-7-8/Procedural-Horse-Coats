Magic day watch
(Magical gene / no coat effect)
What the horse does about you while the sun is up — the daylight twin of magic night watch . The same five-rung ladder from staring through walls to standing directly behind you, and the same silence on its feet. After dark it is an ordinary horse.

Why daylight does not make it friendlier The night watcher borrows the oldest fear there is: something moving where you cannot see it in the dark. In daylight you can see it, and that is the point of this twin — a horse that stands behind you at noon, in a bright empty field, silently, is unsettling for a different reason: there is nowhere for it to have been hiding.
