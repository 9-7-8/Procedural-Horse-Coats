Brow ridge
(Magical gene / dominant / grows geometry, paints nothing)
One copy and the horse grows a low wide ridge of bone across its brow, above and in front of the eyes. Plain, notched into three bumps or set with three short spines, and how tall and wide it is and how tall its bumps stand, is written on the allele. Its colour is a gene of its own, brow ridge colour .

The mesh: a bar laid on its side A box hangs along its own -y and may be thinner across x than it is deep, never wider ( PartGeneratorTest holds that). So a bar wider than it is tall is a box turned a quarter round z : rooted at the right end of the brow, five units long, running across to the left. Its square section is the ridge’s height and its depth front to back. It sinks a fifth of a unit into the skull so a stretched ridge shows no gap. At draw time the part’s stretch is along the anchor’s up, which is the ridge’s height, and its girth is across it, which is the bar’s own length. That is why width on the copy is the part’s girth, and why a wider ridge is also a little deeper. What stands on the bar is turned a quarter back to stand up: a plain ridge’s step lies along the top, a notched one’s three bumps and a spined one’s three two-box spines stand at a fifth, a half and four fifths of the way across. The epigenome, by cost height : the class is the mesh’s size bucket (three), the remainder a stretch along the anchor’s up. width : free, the part’s girth. form and bump : baked, BrowRidgeGenerator.style(form, bump) . A plain ridge has no bumps and is one style whatever its bucket, a trim to fit the box budget . What the first photo shoot changed The first build’s bumps stood as tall as the horse’s ears at wild sizes, which is a crest and not a low ridge. The ladder came down from 0.9–2.2 units to 0.7–1.7 and the bump shares from 0.45–1.2 of the bar’s height to 0.3–0.75 (2026-10-09). The bone version Bon/Bon on the colour locus . A ridge is all bone, so nothing is left off. Both skeleton breeds carry brow_ridge in their horn count group as a slot of its own, with the colour pinned to bone (owner, 2026-10-09).
