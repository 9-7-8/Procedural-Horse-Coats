Ear fin colour
(Magical gene / codominant / colours the ear fins, paints nothing)
What colour a horse’s ear fins are. The same colours and rules as horn colour (white, the rainbow, pink, black, grey or chaos), on a locus of its own. Two different copies give every ray a point or a rod in the second colour. On a horse without ear fins it does nothing.

Crossing two of them The colours, and two-tone fins Exactly the horn colour table . Two copies of one colour make both fins that colour. Two different ones make each ray one colour and its point the other; on a frill the second colour is the rod down the middle of each ray. The ray takes whichever colour comes earlier in the list, so Red/Blu is red fins tipped in blue, either way round. Every copy carries a little shade of its own, so no two red-finned horses are quite the same red. Bone is only a colour here Ear fins have no bone version: a fin is cartilage, like an ear, and no skeleton horse grows one. The Bon allele is still on this gene, because every part’s colour gene shares one list, but no breed carries it here. Bred in by hand it gives warm ivory fins and nothing else. Is it good for the horse? It does nothing to the horse at all. It is only a colour. Its gene carrot
