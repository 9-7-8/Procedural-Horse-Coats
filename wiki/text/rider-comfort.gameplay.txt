Rider comfort
(Gameplay / non-genetic)
Five small things that make riding less irritating, borrowed from other mods that already do them. None touches genetics, and each is small enough to ship alone. One of the five has shipped — a lead now comes back to you rather than being left on the ground when this mod teleports the horse wearing it. The other four are still plans, and the last of them — fading the horse out as you look down — touches the coat itself and is the only one with a real unknown in it.

Your lead comes back A horse cannot stay leashed to a fence while it is being teleported across the world, so anything that summons one has to untie it first. It used to leave the lead lying on the ground where the horse had been standing — which, when you had just called the horse to you from sixty blocks away, meant walking back for it. With an ender whistle or an interdimensional ticket it was worse than that: the lead was left in the dimension the horse started in, and you were unlikely to ever see it again. Now the lead goes into your pack , or drops at your own feet if your pack is full. Nothing else changes: the horse still arrives, and it still arrives untied. It applies wherever this mod moves a horse on purpose — all three area whistles , the ender whistle, and both kinds of ticket . It does not apply when a horse walks into a portal on its own: the person holding the lead is standing right there in that case, so the lead lands at their feet already and vanilla’s own behaviour is the legible one. Server owners who would rather have vanilla exactly can turn it off with behaviour.leads_return , which puts the lead back on the ground where the horse was.
