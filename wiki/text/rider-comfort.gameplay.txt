Rider comfort
(Gameplay / non-genetic)
Five small things that make riding less irritating, borrowed from other mods that already do them. None touches genetics, and each is small enough to ship alone. Three of the five have shipped — a lead now comes back to you rather than being left on the ground when this mod teleports the horse wearing it, you can mine from the saddle at the speed you would standing there, and ALT + E opens your own pack from the saddle. The other two are still plans, and the last of them — fading the horse out as you look down — touches the coat itself and is the only one with a real unknown in it.

Your lead comes back A horse cannot stay leashed to a fence while it is being teleported across the world, so anything that summons one has to untie it first. It used to leave the lead lying on the ground where the horse had been standing — which, when you had just called the horse to you from sixty blocks away, meant walking back for it. With an ender whistle or an interdimensional ticket it was worse than that: the lead was left in the dimension the horse started in, and you were unlikely to ever see it again. Now the lead goes into your pack , or drops at your own feet if your pack is full. Nothing else changes: the horse still arrives, and it still arrives untied. It applies wherever this mod moves a horse on purpose — all three area whistles , the ender whistle, and both kinds of ticket . It does not apply when a horse walks into a portal on its own: the person holding the lead is standing right there in that case, so the lead lands at their feet already and vanilla’s own behaviour is the legible one. Server owners who would rather have vanilla exactly can turn it off with behaviour.leads_return , which puts the lead back on the ground where the horse was. Mining from the saddle Minecraft mines at a fifth speed whenever you are not standing on the ground. That rule is there to stop you tunnelling as you fall, and it is a good rule — but a player sitting on a horse is not standing on anything either, so it caught riders too. One sapling across the path, one dirt block on a ledge, and you had to get off, break it, and get back on . Now you mine from the saddle at the speed you would mine standing in the same spot. Nothing else about mining changes: the same tools, the same blocks, the same enchantments. The horse has to have its feet on the ground. Mid-jump, or on a horse that flies, the fifth-speed penalty still applies — you are genuinely in the air then, and mining out of the air is a different thing from mining from the saddle. This is the one piece on this page that changes a vanilla rule rather than adding something beside it, so the switch matters more here than elsewhere: behaviour.mounted_mining_penalty_removed is on by default, and turning it off restores vanilla exactly. It is a server setting, so on a server it is the server’s answer that counts, not each player’s. Your own inventory, from the saddle This mod takes E away while you are riding: instead of vanilla’s horse screen you get this mod’s own horse screen , which is a far better trade than it sounds — the screen it replaces held a saddle slot and a barding slot and nothing else. But it left a rider with no way at all to open their own bag without getting off the horse first. ALT + E now opens your own inventory. E on its own still opens the horse’s screen, exactly as before. It works on foot too , not only in the saddle. A key that only functions while mounted would be one more thing to remember, and when it did nothing you would have no way to tell whether the key was unbound or the game had decided you were not riding. There is no config option for this one , which makes it the exception on this page. Unbinding the key in Options Controls already switches it off, and it is listed there under Inventory , directly beneath vanilla’s own “Open/Close Inventory” — which is where you would be looking anyway if you were wondering what happened to E . Rebind either of them, or swap the two over, and the mod follows. On a donkey or a mule nothing changed to begin with: this mod keeps no record of one, so E opens vanilla’s screen with its chest grid, as it always did. The new key still works there, and gives you the same pack it gives you anywhere else.
