Cheek spike colour
(Magical gene / codominant / colours the cheek spikes, paints nothing)
What colour a horse’s cheek spikes are. The same colours and rules as horn colour (white, the rainbow, pink, black, grey or chaos), on a locus of its own. Two different copies make every spike two-tone from root to point. On a horse without cheek spikes it does nothing.

Crossing two of them The colours, and two-tone spikes Exactly the horn colour table . Two copies of one colour make every spike that colour, both cheeks alike. Two different ones make each spike one colour at its root and the other at its point . The root takes whichever colour comes earlier in the list, so Red/Yel is red spikes tipped in yellow, either way round. Every copy carries a little shade of its own. Bare bone Two Bon copies are the spikes’ bone version: bare ivory bone. No wild horse carries Bon ; the skeleton horses do, so a skeleton that grows cheek spikes grows them in bone. A spike is all bone, so nothing is left off. Is it good for the horse? It does nothing to the horse at all. It is only a colour. Its gene carrot
