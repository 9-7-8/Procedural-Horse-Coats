Ram horn form
(Magical gene / dominance series / shapes the ram horns, paints nothing)
The shape of a horned horse’s ram horns : curled, scimitar, corkscrew, four-horned, horizontal twist or scurs. One shape on both sides. On a polled horse it does nothing.

Analogous, not measured The shapes are real sheep and goat horn types — the ram’s spiral, the straight twisted horn of the Hungarian Racka, the two pairs of a Jacob, the backswept sabre of a domestic goat or an ibex ( Capra , whose horns are narrow from side to side and deep from front to back), the level corkscrew horns of the extinct Egyptian Ovis longipes palaeoaegyptiacus that the god Khnum wears, and scurs — but in real animals they are breed characters and several loci, not one allele series. Polycerate (multi-horned) sheep do carry a dominant trait; the rest of the series is a gameplay rule.
