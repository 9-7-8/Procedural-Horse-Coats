Ram horn form
(Magical gene / dominance series / shapes the ram horns, paints nothing)
The shape of a horned horse’s ram horns : curled, corkscrew, four-horned or scurs. One shape on both sides. On a polled horse it does nothing.

Analogous, not measured The four shapes are real sheep and goat horn types — the ram’s spiral, the straight twisted horn of the Hungarian Racka, the two pairs of a Jacob, and scurs — but in real animals they are breed characters and several loci, not one allele series. Polycerate (multi-horned) sheep do carry a dominant trait; the rest of the series is a gameplay rule.
