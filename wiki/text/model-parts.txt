Attached model parts
(Planned / not built)
Blockbench meshes hung on the horse model, driven by a gene or by a breed — a unicorn horn, a row of dorsal spines, a pair of wings, feathered hooves. They need no animation of their own, because they would be parented to a part of the horse that is already animated. It would be the mod’s first visual that is not a texture , which is why this is a page rather than a line on a list. None of it is built , and it is the prerequisite for the reindeer-breed content in the breed book, which is content for machinery that does not exist yet.
