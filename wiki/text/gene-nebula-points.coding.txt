Nebula Points
(Magical gene / fields and regions)
Black points - head, muzzle, ears, mane and tail - with a fan of light running up the crest and raking across the barrel. The dominant form glows in the dark; the recessive one is the same pattern in plain pigment.

Nebula Points The points go to black first and everything else is drawn over them, so the light has something to be light against. The fan up the neck is a band on a saw wave, which is what makes it lie along the crest rather than wrap round the throat; the tail is a ramp from near-black at the dock to pale at the tip. All of it is emissive, so a nebula horse is visible at night. The tail is a RAMP in part space, so it runs dark at the dock to pale at the tip whatever length the tail is.
