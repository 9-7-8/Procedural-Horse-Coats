Magic day watch
(Magical gene / no coat effect)
What the horse does about you while the sun is up — the daylight twin of magic night watch . The same five-rung ladder from staring through walls to standing directly behind you, and the same silence on its feet. After dark it is an ordinary horse.

Crossing two of them The five Combination While it is light Wst/Wst stops, faces you and stares — through walls, from anywhere. Wnr/Wnr closes to about ten blocks, then does the same. Wsi/Wsi watches only while it can see you; a closed door ends it. Wun/Wun stands where you are not looking, and watches from there. Wbh/Wbh stands close behind you — until you turn round. anything else an ordinary horse Any of the five is silent on its feet while it is light. If the horse also carries aggression and that has something to hunt or flee, the temper wins and the watching waits. Rare in the wild Where every wild horse carries a night watcher, most carry nothing here: each variant is carried silently by about one and a half wild horses in a hundred, and none shows it.
