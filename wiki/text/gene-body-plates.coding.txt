Body plates
(Magical gene / dominant / grows geometry, paints nothing)
One copy and the horse grows overlapping plates of bone over its shoulders and its hips, stepped down the flank like the lames of a suit of armour, the same on both sides. Whether they are smooth, ridged or spiked, and how big, how many, how tightly lapped and how flared they are, is written on the allele. Their colour is a gene of its own, body plate colour .

The mesh: slabs that hang and lap A slab is a flat box ( PartNode.width , thin across the horse) turned half round z , so it hangs from its top edge, and leaned out at the bottom by the flare. Slab j starts j × drop below the top, still flush with the body, so the one above stands proud of it. drop is a share of the slab’s height set by the overlap bucket, and is stepped closer for a big slab that would otherwise reach past ROOM (nine units down the flank). Every slab carries a lip along its bottom edge, and a ridged slab adds a rib along its middle; both are bars turned a quarter round x to run along the body. A spiked slab adds a spike of two boxes standing straight out. All of them hang straight off the slab, which is what makes them the tip colour ( see the colour page ). A slab sinks a tenth of a unit into the body so its top shows no gap. Slab j of the shoulder and slab j of the hip are both group j , numbered top down (owner, 2026-10-05), so the count shows the top k of each cluster and grows the next one in on both. BodyPlatesGeneTest holds that numbering, the mirroring and the colour shape. The epigenome, by cost size : the class is the mesh’s size bucket (three; where the slabs step down the flank), the remainder a uniform per-element grow, so stretch and girth are equal. form , overlap and spikiness : baked, PlateGenerator.style(form, overlap, spikiness) , three of each. count : shown out of the most baked, per cluster, the antler’s tine rule. There is no separate girth : the treatment lists size, count, overlap and spikiness, and every number on the copy has to reach the part.
