The lasso
(Planned / not built)
A rope that catches a wild horse and holds it while you gentle it , so that getting a horse is something you do with a tool instead of something you repeat until it works. It was asked for, and researched before it was deferred; the research kept the catching and threw away the multi-day wait the idea arrived with. None of it is built.
