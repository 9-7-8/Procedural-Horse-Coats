Ear fin colour
(Magical gene / codominant / colours the ear fins, paints nothing)
What colour a horse’s ear fins are. The same colours and rules as horn colour (white, the rainbow, pink, black, grey or chaos), on a locus of its own. Two different copies give every ray a point or a rod in the second colour. On a horse without ear fins it does nothing.

Everything else is AbstractPartColourGene ’s. Its Words carry a split sentence (“each ray is one, the base, and its point or rod the other”). GrownParts.of puts one pair of tints on both sides. The split is the mesh’s shape and no extra code: a ray is a root, so it sits at 0 on PartModel.along , and its point or rod hangs off it with nothing below, so it sits at 1. Why every ray is a root is on the granting gene’s page. boneOnly is not read: there is nothing to leave off.
