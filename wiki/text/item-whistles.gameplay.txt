Whistles
(Item / tool)
Three area whistles call every tamed horse you own nearby, each reaching further than the last. The fourth, the ender whistle , is bound to one horse for good and brings it to you from anywhere — even another dimension.

What happens when you blow an area whistle Every tamed horse you own within range, in the same dimension and not currently being ridden, teleports to a spot beside you. A leashed horse is unleashed and comes. A chime plays and a chat line tells you how many answered. Horses already within 3 blocks, and the one you are riding, are left where they are. There is a roughly 3 second cooldown between uses. The four whistles Item Recipe (shapeless) Reach Basic whistle horse hair + iron nugget + stick every horse of yours within 16 blocks Golden whistle basic whistle + gold ingot within 32 blocks Echo whistle golden whistle + echo shard within 64 blocks Ender whistle echo whistle + eye of ender + ender pearl one horse, from anywhere, any dimension The ender whistle Bind it. Right-click one of your tamed horses with an unbound ender whistle. It hums, and it is bound to that horse for good — it can never be unbound or moved to another horse. Craft one per horse you want to call. Blow it anywhere. The horse appears in front of you in a puff of portal particles, however far away it was, whatever dimension it was in. If it was a long way off and nobody was near it, it takes a moment: the whistle says it “echoes a long way off”, and the horse arrives within a few seconds. Use it as often as you like , with a five-second cooldown. Only the horse’s owner can call it. If you sell the horse with transfer papers , the whistle goes with it in spirit: blow it after that and you are told the horse no longer answers to you. Hand the whistle to the new owner and it works for them. A horse with somebody else riding it stays put. If the horse dies, the whistle turns to dust — the next time you are holding it, with a message saying so. One left in a chest crumbles the moment you pick it up. If the horse was never seen where it was last recorded — it wandered off in a chunk nobody had loaded — nothing answers, and the whistle says so. All four look the same The whistles share one texture, so the four tiers are only told apart by their names. A bound ender whistle at least says Bound to: <horse> on its tooltip, and an unbound one says how to bind it. Keep the area whistles apart in your hotbar, or rename them at an anvil.
