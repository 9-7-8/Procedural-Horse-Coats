Whistles
(Item / tool)
Three area whistles call every tamed horse you own nearby, each reaching further than the last. The fourth, the ender whistle , is bound to one horse for good and brings it to you from anywhere — even another dimension. A horse close enough to simply come walks in rather than appearing, the ender whistle asks for a bond before it will bind, and one key blows the lot .

What happens when you blow an area whistle Every tamed horse you own within range, in the same dimension and not currently being ridden, teleports to a spot beside you. A leashed horse is untied and comes, and the lead goes into your pack rather than falling where the horse was standing. A chime plays and a chat line tells you how many answered. Horses already within 3 blocks, and the one you are riding, are left where they are. There is a roughly 3 second cooldown between uses. Each whistle says what it reaches. Hold one and the tooltip gives its radius in blocks, and reminds you that a ridden horse stays put and a leashed one is untied — the two outcomes that otherwise look like the whistle not working. The ender whistle’s tooltip names its horse instead. Near horses walk; far ones appear A whistle used to teleport everything it reached, at any distance — so blowing one at a horse standing ten blocks away in an open field made it vanish and pop back at your elbow, which looks like a bug even though it was the feature working. Now a horse that can simply walk to you does. It sets off at a brisk trot and arrives on its own feet. Anything further away, or that cannot get to you, appears beside you exactly as before. The route is what counts, not how far off it looks. A horse fifteen blocks away on the wrong side of a fence is a long walk round, so it is teleported. Open the gate four blocks along and the same horse walks. One blow can do both at once: near ones set off, far ones appear, and the chat line counts them all together. If it gets stuck, it is teleported anyway. A horse that stops making headway, or takes too long, is brought the old way. A whistle never ends with your horse standing behind a fence. A horse in trouble is left alone. One that is on fire, freezing or being hurt drops out of the call and is not teleported — it has something more urgent to do than come when called, and yanking it to your feet is not help. The ender whistle still always teleports. Its whole point is “from anywhere”, and no horse can walk across a dimension. One key blows every whistle you carry Press ' and every whistle in your pack sounds at once — no need to find the right one in a hotbar of four identical icons. Rebindable in Controls, and selecting a whistle reminds you which key it is on. It is not literally every whistle, because that would be silly: a bigger area whistle already covers a smaller one, so only the largest you are carrying is blown and the others are left unused, cooldown and all. Every ender whistle is blown, since each is bound to a different horse — except two bound to the same horse, which call it once between them. A horse an area whistle has already brought is not called a second time by its own ender whistle. You get one line of chat for the whole press, and one chime per whistle actually blown. Whistles still cooling down sit the press out; the key is not a way round a cooldown. Binding an ender whistle needs the horse's trust An ender whistle will only bind to a horse that already knows you — a horse at the bond tier where it starts coming toward you unasked . Below that it refuses, and says the horse does not trust you enough yet. The reasoning is that a whistle should be a reward for a bond you have already built, rather than something you craft in advance and use to skip building one. Ownership is still required as well: taming a horse is not the same as being trusted by it. It is not retroactive. Nothing re-checks bond after binding, so a whistle bound before this existed stays bound, and a horse whose bond later decays keeps answering its own whistle. The gate is on earning the whistle, not on keeping it. The four whistles Item Recipe (shapeless) Reach Basic whistle horse hair + iron nugget + stick every horse of yours within 16 blocks Golden whistle basic whistle + gold ingot within 32 blocks Echo whistle golden whistle + echo shard within 64 blocks Ender whistle echo whistle + eye of ender + ender pearl one horse, from anywhere, any dimension The ender whistle Bind it. Right-click one of your tamed horses with an unbound ender whistle. It hums, and it is bound to that horse for good — it can never be unbound or moved to another horse. Craft one per horse you want to call. Blow it anywhere. The horse appears in front of you in a puff of portal particles, however far away it was, whatever dimension it was in. If it was a long way off and nobody was near it, it takes a moment: the whistle says it “echoes a long way off”, and the horse arrives within a few seconds. Use it as often as you like , with a five-second cooldown. Only the horse’s owner can call it. If you sell the horse with transfer papers , the whistle goes with it in spirit: blow it after that and you are told the horse no longer answers to you. Hand the whistle to the new owner and it works for them. A horse with somebody else riding it stays put. If the horse dies, the whistle turns to dust — the next time you are holding it, with a message saying so. One left in a chest crumbles the moment you pick it up. If the horse was never seen where it was last recorded — it wandered off in a chunk nobody had loaded — nothing answers, and the whistle says so. All four look the same The whistles share one texture, so the four tiers are only told apart by their names — and by their tooltips, which is what the tooltips are for. An area whistle states its radius; a bound ender whistle says Bound to: <horse> and an unbound one says how to bind it. In a hotbar, where no tooltip shows, they are still four identical icons: keep them apart, or rename them at an anvil.
