Whistles
(Item / tool)
Three area whistles call every tamed horse you own nearby, each reaching further than the last. The fourth, the ender whistle , is bound to one horse for good and brings it to you from anywhere — even another dimension. A horse close enough to simply come walks in rather than appearing, the ender whistle asks for a bond before it will bind, and one key blows the lot , and a bound ender whistle glimmers so you can pick it out of a hotbar. The fifth, the command whistle , does not call at all: hold it on a horse and a wheel of standing orders opens.

What happens when you blow an area whistle Every tamed horse you own within range, in the same dimension and not currently being ridden, teleports to a spot beside you. A leashed horse is untied and comes, and the lead goes into your pack rather than falling where the horse was standing. A chime plays and a chat line tells you how many answered. Horses already within 3 blocks, and the one you are riding, are left where they are. There is a roughly 3 second cooldown between uses. Each whistle says what it reaches. Hold one and the tooltip gives its radius in blocks, and reminds you that a ridden horse stays put and a leashed one is untied — the two outcomes that otherwise look like the whistle not working. The ender whistle’s tooltip names its horse instead. Near horses walk; far ones appear A whistle used to teleport everything it reached, at any distance — so blowing one at a horse standing ten blocks away in an open field made it vanish and pop back at your elbow, which looks like a bug even though it was the feature working. Now a horse that can simply walk to you does. It sets off at a brisk trot and arrives on its own feet. Anything further away, or that cannot get to you, appears beside you exactly as before. The route is what counts, not how far off it looks. A horse fifteen blocks away on the wrong side of a fence is a long walk round, so it is teleported. Open the gate four blocks along and the same horse walks. One blow can do both at once: near ones set off, far ones appear, and the chat line counts them all together. If it gets stuck, it is teleported anyway. A horse that stops making headway, or takes too long, is brought the old way. A whistle never ends with your horse standing behind a fence. A horse in trouble is left alone. One that is on fire, freezing or being hurt drops out of the call and is not teleported — it has something more urgent to do than come when called, and yanking it to your feet is not help. The ender whistle still always teleports. Its whole point is “from anywhere”, and no horse can walk across a dimension. One key blows every whistle you carry Press ' and every whistle in your pack sounds at once — no need to find the right one in a hotbar of four identical icons. Rebindable in Controls, and selecting a whistle reminds you which key it is on. It is not literally every whistle, because that would be silly: a bigger area whistle already covers a smaller one, so only the largest you are carrying is blown and the others are left unused, cooldown and all. Every ender whistle is blown, since each is bound to a different horse — except two bound to the same horse, which call it once between them. A horse an area whistle has already brought is not called a second time by its own ender whistle. You get one line of chat for the whole press, and one chime per whistle actually blown. Whistles still cooling down sit the press out; the key is not a way round a cooldown. Binding an ender whistle needs the horse's trust An ender whistle will only bind to a horse that already knows you — a horse at the bond tier where it starts coming toward you unasked . Below that it refuses, and says the horse does not trust you enough yet. The reasoning is that a whistle should be a reward for a bond you have already built, rather than something you craft in advance and use to skip building one. Ownership is still required as well: taming a horse is not the same as being trusted by it. It is not retroactive. Nothing re-checks bond after binding, so a whistle bound before this existed stays bound, and a horse whose bond later decays keeps answering its own whistle. The gate is on earning the whistle, not on keeping it. It pauses while the horse is ridden or on a lead, and while the player it follows is in another world or offline. An order never starves a horse. A hungry horse still goes to eat, and a frightened one still bolts. It is cleared by calling the horse with any whistle (calling a horse means you want it), by Send home, by putting it in a stasis chamber, by selling or giving it away, and when it changes dimension. Refused by a horse on a lead, one pulling a cart, or one not bonded enough, and the two combat orders by a horse not bred to fight; the wheel greys an order the aimed horse cannot take and says why. Getting one: crafted (above), or from an equestrian supplier at level 3 for 5 emeralds. Giving your first order ticks the “Give a horse an order” checklist task. Hunt monsters and Defend me: horses bred to fight Only a horse whose genes make it a fighter takes the two combat orders. It has to show one of these, not just carry it: Aggression , a matched pair that goes for monsters ( Adh , Anh or Aah ) — or for everything ( Ada , Ana , Aaa ). An “everything” horse still goes for what its genes say, you included: the order adds monsters, it calms nothing. Guardian , two copies. Magic fighter , hitting harder than an ordinary horse: a Fighter or a Champion. A Weak or Harmless horse is not one. Creepers are never picked. Nor are players, anybody’s pet, or a tamed horse — a tamed zombie horse included. A hurt horse breaks off. Below 30% of its health it drops the fight and goes back to its spot, or to you. It picks no new fight until it has healed past that. It is a leash, not a chase. A monster that runs more than a few blocks past the radius, or drags the horse that far, is let go. Riding pauses it , like every order: a defender does not leave you mid-ride. Server owners can change the reach and the break-off: orders.hunt_radius (16, from 8 to 32), orders.defend_radius (8, from 4 to 16) and orders.break_off_health (0.3; 0 means fight to the death), in phc/server.toml . More orders are planned: Graze nearby, Guard here and Go home. The ender whistle Bind it. Right-click one of your tamed horses with an unbound ender whistle. It hums, and it is bound to that horse for good — it can never be unbound or moved to another horse. Craft one per horse you want to call. Blow it anywhere. The horse appears in front of you in a puff of portal particles, however far away it was, whatever dimension it was in. If it was a long way off and nobody was near it, it takes a moment: the whistle says it “echoes a long way off”, and the horse arrives within a few seconds. Use it as often as you like , with a five-second cooldown. Only the horse’s owner can call it. If you sell the horse with transfer papers , the whistle goes with it in spirit: blow it after that and you are told the horse no longer answers to you. Hand the whistle to the new owner and it works for them. A horse with somebody else riding it stays put. If the horse dies, the whistle turns to dust — the next time you are holding it, with a message saying so. One left in a chest crumbles the moment you pick it up. If the horse was never seen where it was last recorded — it wandered off in a chunk nobody had loaded — nothing answers, and the whistle says so. A bound whistle glimmers An ender whistle that has been bound to a horse shimmers like an enchanted item . An unbound one does not. That is the whole of it — you can see at a glance, in a hotbar or a chest, which of your whistles are spoken for. It costs nothing and needs no new art, which is why it won over the second texture that was planned: the shimmer is worked out from whether the whistle is bound, so whistles you bound before this update glimmer too , with nothing to migrate and nothing to go and find. The three area tiers still look the same Basic, golden and echo share one texture and one icon, so they are told apart only by their names and their tooltips — each states its radius, which is what the tooltip is for. In a hotbar, where nothing draws a tooltip, they are three identical icons: keep them in order, or rename them at an anvil. Only the ender whistle is now distinguishable on sight, and only once it is bound. The blow-everything key takes most of the sting out of this, since picking the right icon is no longer how you use them.
