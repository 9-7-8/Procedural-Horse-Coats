Panda
(Magical gene / ground and strong white)
A strong white band round the belly and barrel, carrying on over the neck, head and tail, with the shoulder, the butt and all four legs keeping the coat - and the ears, eye patches and muzzle of a giant panda. A third allele carries the same marking worked in greys instead of white - dark where the pale form is light, and the same number of steps.

Panda White takes a band right round the middle of the horse - the belly and barrel, wrapping the whole circumference - and carries on over the neck, the head and the tail. The coat survives at the two ends of that band and nowhere else along the body: the shoulder in front of it, the butt and croup behind it, and all four legs the whole way down. The head is white apart from the ears, the muzzle and the patches round the eyes, which keep the coat and are what make it a panda; muzzle and ear markings are optional and the eye patches are a little stylised. Freckles of coat run along the top of the white band - small, or larger and fawn-like.
