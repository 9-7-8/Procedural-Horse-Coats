Sun sensitivity
(Magical gene / behaviour)
Two copies and daylight burns the horse. Caught under open sky by day it runs for the nearest deep cover or water — jumping a fence if it can — and stays there until dark. One copy shows nothing at all.

Crossing two of them What happens to the horse It burns. Half a heart every two seconds while it is outdoors, by day, with nothing overhead. Smoke comes off it. Rain, water and a roof all save it. A horse standing in a pond is safe; so is one under trees thick enough to hide the sky, or in a barn. It runs for shelter and prefers somewhere deep — at least two blocks in from the edge of the roof — so it does not end up half in the sun. It will jump a fence on the way if its jump is good enough. It stays put until night. Once sheltered it stands still rather than wandering back out. Ridden or on a lead, it is your problem. It will not bolt out from under a rider or pull a lead; it will just burn. It is not fire in the ordinary sense, so it does not panic and run in circles the way a horse that walked into lava does — it goes to its shelter.
