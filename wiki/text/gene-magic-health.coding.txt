Magic health
(Magical gene / no coat effect)
The magical version of the hearts the natural loci trade around — MSTN spends them for speed, HMGA2 gives them for being small. One of the five magical body-stat genes — size , speed , health , jump and pull — and like the other three multipliers, most wild horses carry a copy , worth about a tenth either way. Two copies of the same kind add , and no wild horse is ever born with two. (Pull is the odd one out: an additive score rather than a multiplier, and with no vampiric allele.)

Checklist on the Verification tab . One of a family of five This gene is a thin subclass of AbstractMagicStatGene , which is the magic body size pattern generalised to the three additive stats. Everything on this page is true of magic speed , magic health and magic jump alike; only the stat it moves and the allele names change. Magic body size is the odd one out — it multiplies scale , which carries its own two-stage natural clamp, so it kept its own class. Magic pull is the fifth axis, and it is not this class either. The locus has the same shape — codominant, one allele up, one down, a per-copy number inherited with the copy, both copies added — but its sum is added to a 1–10 score rather than multiplied into a game unit, and it has no Vmp allele (the Dhampir gets its pull from a per-strain score on the breed sheet instead). So the multiplier arithmetic, the vampiric rows and the × tables below are this gene’s and its two AbstractMagicStatGene siblings’, not pull’s. Because most wild horses carry a copy of all five, a wild-caught horse has a quiet, independent wobble on every axis at once, and a horse that is remarkable on any one of them is a breeding result rather than a catch. Vmp adds vampiricPerCopy() into the same sum as the other copy rather than an epigenetic percentage, so it is identical on every horse — it is the old dhampir gene’s triple health, split in half across the two copies. A codominant locus is the case where every combination genuinely differs, so collapsing any two rows would be a lie about the gene. The contribution is signed — Hardy positive, Frail negative — so the whole table falls out of one line and Hardy/Frail cancels because a positive and a negative are added, not because anything checks for it. It multiplies, it does not add The natural health loci add a weight per copy. This gene takes the sum of its two copies’ percentages and multiplies whatever those loci settled on, through TraitBuilder.multiplyHealthUnclamped , applied after every addition and bounded only by HorseTraits.MAGICAL_MIN_FACTOR / MAGICAL_MAX_FACTOR (ten times either way). common/genetics/genes/AbstractMagicStatGene.java @Override public void contribute(AllelePair pair, Genotype genotype, GeneEpigenetics epigenetics, TraitBuilder out) { double sum = signedDelta(pair.first(), epigenetics.copy(0)) + signedDelta(pair.second(), epigenetics.copy(1)); if (sum != 0.0) { applyMagic(out, 1.0 + sum); // multiplyHealthUnclamped(1.0 + sum) } } Multiplying is what makes it compose the way you would want: a magically hardy pony is still frailer than a magically hardy draught horse, because the natural loci settle the horse’s own health first and the magic scales whatever that turned out to be. The MIN_HEALTH floor (half a heart) is still applied last, so a doubled Frail horse stacked with disorders cannot resolve to zero hearts — killing a horse is the damage path’s job. It needs both copies’ epigenetics Every non-codominant epigenetic gene asks for “the copy that expresses”, because one locus produces one result. A codominant one cannot: asking for the expressed copy would count one allele twice and the other not at all. So this gene takes an GeneEpigenetics and asks it for copy(0) and copy(1) — the same accessor magic body size and mane colour need, for the same reason. Each copy’s percentage is a bounded normal draw (mean 10% , 7% ) off that copy’s stored epigenetic seed, floored at 1% so a Hardy allele can never come out subtracting. The seed is rolled once for a founder and inherited verbatim with the allele, so a foal that inherits the copy inherits the exact percentage — which is what makes breeding two good copies together a real project. Asked about a genotype with no epigenome to read (a wiki table, a punnett display), the gene reports the midpoint : exactly ×1.10 for one copy, ×1.20 for two. Not a health disorder It is not a HealthContribution . That marker is what the server config’s “off” position switches off, and it is meant for disorders — the dwarfisms, the lethal foals. Turning those off must not also quietly strip a horse of its magical vigour. A Frail/Frail horse here is a horse with fewer hearts, not a sick one; it never reports a Condition and is never suppressed. Deliberately health only It moves no other number. Not speed, not jump, not size, not pull . The natural loci already own the trade-offs between the stats; this gene is a clean multiplier on one axis so that the five can be bred independently.
