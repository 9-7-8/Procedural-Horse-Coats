Saddles
(Horse gear / the saddle slot)
On a horse the vanilla saddle is the bridle , and it steers. The saddle is this mod's own item, worn in the Saddle slot beside it: it makes the ride. While a player is aboard, a saddle adds a share of the horse's own speed and jump, up to 10% at the top tier . It comes in every material a chestplate is made of (leather, copper, chainmail, iron, gold, diamond and netherite), and in every material another mod makes a chestplate of. Horse armour now follows the same rule, which adds chainmail horse armour .

What a saddle is A horse used to need one item to be ridden, and that item did everything. Now there are two. The bridle (what vanilla calls a saddle) goes on the head and lets a rider steer. The saddle goes on the back and carries the rider better. A horse can wear either, both or neither: Bridle and saddle: you steer, and the saddle adds its bonus. Bridle only: you steer, at the horse's own speed and jump. Saddle only: below bond tier 3 you are a passenger ; the horse walks where it likes, and the saddle's bonus is still on while you sit there. Donkeys, mules and llamas keep vanilla's saddle; this one fits horses only. The tiers Every saddle is leather first. A metal saddle is a leather saddle with that metal's fittings, so you cannot make a saddle without leather. Its fittings are the colour of the matching chestplate. The bonus is a share of the horse's own numbers, so a saddle is worth the same proportion on a slow horse as on a fast one. With the default settings: Saddle While ridden Made from Leatherworker Leather +2.5% speed and jump 3 leather, a horse hair Novice, 6 emeralds Copper +4% a leather saddle, 2 copper ingots Apprentice, 9 Chainmail +5% a leather saddle, 2 iron chains — Iron +6% a leather saddle, 2 iron ingots Journeyman, 14 Golden +7% a leather saddle, 2 gold ingots Expert, 18 Diamond +8.5% a leather saddle, 2 diamonds Master, 26 Netherite +10% a diamond saddle, a netherite ingot — Another mod's material gets a saddle of its own if that mod also makes a chestplate of it: a gem saddle is worth what diamond is, an ingot saddle what iron is. It is made from a leather saddle and two of the ingot or gem, and is not sold. Getting one Craft it, or buy the leather, copper, iron, golden or diamond one from the equestrian leatherworker. Horses in a generated stable , and horses you buy from a dealer with transfer papers, come already wearing a bridle and a leather saddle. When the tack bench is built, it will make every saddle instead. Putting it on Right-click a tamed adult horse with the saddle, or put it in the Saddle slot of the horse's Gear tab. Shears take it off, as they do vanilla's. The horse's own screen shows its speed and jump without the saddle, because the bonus belongs to the ride, not to the horse. Horse armour in the same materials Horse armour and saddles come in the same materials: every material a chestplate is made of. Vanilla already makes horse armour in six of the seven; this mod adds chainmail horse armour (6 iron chains and a horse hair), which protects as much as chainmail does, between copper and iron. Another mod's metal gets a horse armour only if that mod also makes a chestplate of it, and that armour protects in proportion to the chestplate: a mod's strong chestplate makes a strong horse armour. If a metal used to have a horse armour and no longer qualifies, an old copy in your world loads as iron horse armour.
