Undead horses
(Planned / not built)
Two genes, and a spawning overhaul. This used to be an open question and now it is a design. The question was how much of the mod an undead horse gets; the answer the owner has given is all of it — a skeleton horse is any breed that happens to carry a skeleton gene, and a zombie horse is any breed carrying a zombie gene. Undeath stops being an entity type this mod stands beside and becomes two loci, which is the answer that costs the least new machinery and the most spawning work. None of it is built.
