Ear fins
(Magical gene / recessive / grows geometry, paints nothing)
Two copies and the horse grows a fin at the outer base of each ear, fanned out from the head and swept back. A single blade, a fan of three or a frill of five rays, and how big it is, how wide it opens and how many rays show, is written on the allele. Their colour is a gene of its own, ear fin colour .

Crossing two of them Two copies, either sex, foals too Recessive: only Efn/Efn grows fins. A horse with one copy shows nothing and passes it on, so two plain carriers can have a finned foal. It is not sex-limited. Ear fins are the soft head part, so a foal wears them from birth , at its own small size, the way a unicorn foal wears its horn. Where they sit On the side of the head at the outer base of each ear, leaning out and sweeping back over the neck. They sit on the skull, beside the ear and not on it. The two sides always match. From the side or from above you see the whole fan; from dead ahead or dead behind you see its edge. Finding one Rare in the wild, the same as a unicorn: about one horse in four hundred has fins and about one in ten carries a hidden copy. Skeleton horses never grow them. A fin is cartilage, like an ear, and a skeleton has no ears to speak of. What varies Form : a blade (one broad slab), a fan (three slabs, longer toward the back) or a frill (five narrower slabs in an arc, each with a ray down its middle). Size : two classes, small (about as long as the ear) and grand (up to as long as the head is deep). Small in the wild; a line bred for size reaches grand and stops. Spread : how wide a fan or a frill opens. Rays : how many a fan or a frill shows, the longest first. A wild fan is nearly always whole. A line can be bred down to one ray, or back up, and the next ray grows in gradually. Everything stacks: a horse can wear ear fins beside dragon horns, antlers and a unicorn horn. At large sizes they can touch each other. Is it good for the horse? It does nothing at all, for good or ill: no stat, no better hearing, no hitbox. It is a look. Its gene carrot
