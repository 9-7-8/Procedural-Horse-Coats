The four posts
(Blocks / villagers)
The four equestrians ’ workstations. Put one down near an unemployed villager and you get that trade, and only that trade — which is why there are four of them and not one.

They are POIs and nothing else Plain blocks with no behaviour of their own — everything happens because each one backs a POI of its own name ( horsegenetics:leatherworkers_post and the other three), registered in village/ModPoiTypes with maxTickets 1 the way every vanilla job site is. The professions that claim them are in village/ModVillagerProfessions ; the blocks themselves are four registerSimpleBlock calls in block/ModBlocks . A POI is only half the wiring. It has to be in the minecraft:acquirable_job_site tag before an unemployed villager will take the job, and that half is a tag file under data/minecraft/tags/point_of_interest_type/ . Tags merge across datapacks, so adding to vanilla’s is additive and safe where overriding a worldgen file would not be. The Cowboy Hitch must never appear in one : it is deliberately not a POI, and a tag naming a missing entry fails to load as a whole , taking vanilla’s own village POIs down with it — which it did, for four days ( the record ). Why four blocks and not one A POI hands out exactly one profession. Vanilla decides the job from the job site claimed, not the other way round, so a single shared post could only ever hand out a single job — and the version that tried to alternate could not undo the job-site ticket it had already granted. Four jobs therefore need four blocks, and the hitch is a fifth for the separate reason that a cowboy is an entity rather than a profession. They are identical in every respect but their name and their recipe, down to a shared cube_bottom_top model and one three-face texture set. Making each look like the trade it belongs to is open work. The texture files are named after a block that never existed All five posts draw horse_traders_post_{top,side,bottom}.png . There has never been a block called a Horse Trader’s Post — the old block was horsemans_table , the “Horseman’s Table”, and the wiki called it a Horse Trader’s Post in prose for a while without either of them ever matching the art. That was odd when the name was one block out of date; it is doubly odd now that the block those files were drawn for is retired and four differently-named ones share them. Left alone deliberately rather than missed: renaming a texture means touching every model that references it and re-checking the atlas, for no change a player can see, and the right moment to do it is when the four stop sharing one set. Written down here so the next person to open those files knows the name is a fossil and not a clue. Loot tables One per post, all of them the ordinary survives_explosion single-item drop, at data/horsegenetics/loot_table/blocks/ . The four were written at the same time the hitch finally got one — the hitch had shipped with none, which in this version means the block drops nothing rather than defaulting to itself. A block registered without noLootTable() and without a file is silently unrecoverable, and nothing in the build says so.
