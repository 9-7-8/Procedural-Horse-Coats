Horn dust
(Magical gene / recessive / sheds from the horn, paints nothing)
Two copies and a unicorn's horn sheds a fine dust that falls past its face, all the time, in the horn's own colours — a two-tone horn's dust fades from one of its colours to the other as it falls. On a horse without a horn this gene does nothing at all.

Crossing two of them Finding one Recessive: one copy shows nothing. In the wild a horse carries two copies or none — about one in twelve has two — so roughly one wild unicorn in twelve sheds dust. As with horn glow , most horses with two copies have no horn, so an ordinary horse can be the parent that gives your unicorn its dust. What it looks like A steady, fine fall of coloured motes from along the horn, standing or moving, drifting down about a block and a half to two blocks before they fade. The colour is always the horn's: whatever horn colour makes it, shade and chaos included. Is it good for the horse? It does nothing to the horse at all, for good or ill.
