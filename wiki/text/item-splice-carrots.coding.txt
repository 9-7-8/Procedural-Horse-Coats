The splice carrots
(Item group / breeding)
Carrots that add a gene to a foal. One targets a gene you have researched; the others roll one at random, either from the whole pool or from one themed slice of it.

The Known Gene Splice is a custom recipe type Not a JSON shapeless recipe: server/recipe/KnownGeneSpliceRecipe . It requires golden carrot + a research paper + horse hair, and nothing else — exactly three filled slots. The gene is read off the paper at craft time, so a drop-in gene file gets its carrot the moment it registers. A fourth slot took the gene’s rarity ingot until that charge was retired — see the rarity tier is no longer a price . This recipe lands in four places KnownGeneSpliceRecipe (what it requires), SpliceRecipeDisplay (the canonical slot layout), RarityItems (the tier item table, deliberately off common/ ), and wiki/gene-carrot/gene-carrot.js , which redraws both for the wiki. The wiki cannot read the last two — they are Minecraft-side — so a change there is silent on the pages until it is made here too. One item, many genes known_gene_splice_carrot is one generic item parameterised by a data component, so every gene’s carrot looks the same in the inventory. That is a known gap. The blacklist, and why it is derived SpliceSafety works the random pool out from what the genes actually do rather than from a typed list of keys: every combination the carrot could roll is resolved through HorseTraits on an otherwise wild-type horse, and the locus is dropped if any of them produces a condition worse than INFORMATIONAL or leaves the horse short of baseline health. A new health gene is excluded the moment it is registered, by having done its job. Full argument on the carrot family page . Verified A splice carrot breeds (the yard's carrot pens, rows L to N, by 2026-09-13). The substituted locus is really there, it is in the carrot's theme, and no foal came out with a disorder (2026-09-15). Nothing records which locus a splice picked, so the yard's rows AJ-AK find it the way SpliceOutcome.spliceReached does: an allele on the foal that neither parent carries.
