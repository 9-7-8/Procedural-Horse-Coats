Ram horns
(Magical gene / dominant, sex-limited allele / grows geometry, paints nothing)
One copy and the horse grows a pair of ram’s horns — on a mare or a stallion with Rh , on a stallion only with Rhm , the way most horned sheep breeds have horned rams and polled ewes. How big they are, how far they curl, how thick and what shade are written on the allele. The third grown part, after the unicorn horn and antlers , and built on the antler pattern.
