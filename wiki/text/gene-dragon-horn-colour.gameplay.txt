Dragon horn colour
(Magical gene / codominant / colours the dragon horns, paints nothing)
What colour a horse’s dragon horns are. The same colours and the same rules as horn colour — white, the rainbow, pink, black, grey or chaos, and two different copies make a two-tone pair — on a locus of its own, so a unicorn with dragon horns can wear a white horn and black dragon horns. On a horse without dragon horns it does nothing.

Crossing two of them The colours, and two-tone pairs Exactly the horn colour table : white in most copies, a few percent each of red, orange, yellow, green, blue, indigo, violet, pink, black and grey, and chaos rarest. Two copies of one colour give a pair of that colour; two different ones give horns of the earlier colour in that list with tips of the other, fading between. Both horns of a pair are coloured alike. Every copy carries a little shade of its own, so no two red pairs are quite the same red. Its own gene, not the horn’s Horn colour colours only a unicorn horn, and this colours only dragon horns.
