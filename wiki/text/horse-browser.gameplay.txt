The horse browser
(Gameplay / partly built)
The horse browser is the mod’s own index of everything you own and everything it knows — an H -key screen carrying a list of your horses, a searchable gene database, the recipes, the alleles, the breeds, a breeding preview, a table of every horse standing in the horse realm and a log of what has been happening to your stable . Part of it ships. Most of the built half is written up on Breeding & pedigree , under client sync, the information screen and the family tree ; the Log tab is written up here, and the rest of the plan is on the Roadmap tab.

The Log tab — what has been happening to your horses Press H and pick Log . It is a plain list of things that have happened to horses you own, newest at the top : every cover that went through and every one that did not, every foal, every death, every horse you tamed, bought, were handed or lost, every horse an emergency chamber caught, and every horse a rescuing braid sent home. Each row carries the day and the time it happened, what kind of thing it was, which horse it was about, and the result in a sentence. The two newest kinds — Rescues and Homings , each with a filter chip of its own — are here for the tab's own reason, harder than the rest. A chamber and a braid both fire by themselves , with nobody watching, possibly a thousand blocks away; the chat line is the only thing that ever says so and it scrolls past while you are busy with whatever was killing the horse. They are two kinds rather than one because they leave you in different places: a rescued horse is in a bottle you now have to find and open, and a homed one is standing in its stall. It is written down whether or not you were there to read it This is the point of the tab. The mod already says most of this in chat — a mare was not covered, a horse was killed — but chat reaches whoever is standing there, once, and scrolls away. The log is written on the server as things happen , including while the menu is shut, while you are in another dimension, while the chunk is unloaded, and while you are logged out entirely. Come back in the morning and the night is in the list. Turning the chat notices off in the config does not stop the log; that setting is about chat. The filter chips Along the top, right of the count: All , then one chip per kind of event, each with how many of that kind you have. Click one to see only those; click it again by picking another. Every kind gets a chip even when the count is zero, because a chip that only appears once you already have one teaches nobody what the log can tell them. Filtering never changes the order — newest is still first. Covers. Both halves: “was covered, and is in foal” and every refusal. A refused cover keeps its reason and its numbers, so “12 other horses within 16 blocks of her, and the limit is 8” reads in the log exactly as it read in chat. Those are the same refusals the chat line can end in, because they are literally the same sentence — see the Coding tab. Births. One row per foal, naming its dam and sire. Both breeding paths are here: a foal fed into being with a golden carrot, and one born overnight from a pregnancy. Deaths. What killed it, by name where something did and by cause where nothing did. Recorded for an owner who was nowhere near, which is the case the hurt and death chat lines deliberately do not cover. Taming. A wild horse you got a rope on. A foal is not a taming, even though a foal born to a tamed mare is tamed on the spot — it gets a birth row. Purchases, sales and transfers. The three ways a horse changes hands on a transfer paper . Buying a dealer’s stock is a purchase; taking on somebody else’s horse is a transfer; and when your horse is the one redeemed, a row appears in your log too — otherwise losing a horse is the one thing that happens to your stable that you are never told about, since the chat line goes to whoever spent the paper. How much it keeps The last two hundred events, per player. Past that the oldest fall off the end and the tab says so at the bottom. That is a real loss and it is the right one: a log is what has been happening , and nobody reads to the bottom of one that never ends. The pedigree is the permanent record and is not bounded. A refusal that is still true two seconds later is not written again — a crowded paddock is re-checked constantly and would otherwise fill the whole log by lunchtime. The same refusal for the same mare is one row for five minutes, which is the same quiet period the chat line uses. A different reason is always news, and two foals five minutes apart are always two rows. Refresh There is a Refresh button, top right, the same one My horses and Breeding preview carry. You should not often need it: the server pushes your log to you about once a second whenever something is added to it, so a tab left open fills in as the day goes on. Send home — free, from anywhere Every row of My horses has a Send home button. Press it and that horse goes to its own stall, or to your holding pen if it has no stall. It works on a horse in another world, on one in a chunk nobody has loaded, and on one sitting in a stasis chamber . It costs nothing unless the server has set a price. The stall is checked at the moment you press it. If the horse no longer fits — the sign is down, the stall is walled up or full — you are told why and nothing happens. There is a short wait between sends, five seconds by default, so a mashed button sends one horse. Only a trip that actually happened starts it. A server may put a price on it : one item and a count per trip, named in behaviour.send_home_payment_item and behaviour.send_home_payment_count . The button then reads, for example, Send home (2 x Emerald) , greys out when you cannot pay, and takes the items only when the horse arrives. A price replaces tickets for the button; it never charges both. Tickets and whistles still move a horse with no menu, and the tooltip says so. The command whistle ’s Go home order is the same trip for a horse beside you: the same stall or pen, the same price, but no wait between sends. The Realm tab's Bring home button is different and still spends a holding pen ticket: it tames a wild horse, which has no stall of its own yet.
