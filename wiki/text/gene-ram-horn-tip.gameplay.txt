Ram horn tip
(Magical gene / recessive / colours the horn tips, paints nothing)
Two copies and a horse’s ram horns keep their own shade at the base and fade into a colour of their own toward the points — any colour, inherited with the allele. Hoofprints from the molten hooves MltH allele come out in the same colour. On a polled horse it does nothing.

Crossing two of them Finding one Recessive: one copy shows nothing, and in the wild a horse has two copies or none — one in twenty has two, and almost none of those has horns. The Mirrorgazer carries it on every horse, over black horns. Matching hoofprints A horse with two copies of molten hooves ’ MltH leaves glowing prints in its horn-tip colour — or, without coloured tips, in its horns’ own shade — so its prints always match the ends of its horns.
