Horse shoes
(Horse gear / hoof equipment)
A horse shoe is one item to a hoof : four separate stacks in four separate gear slots, each in its own metal, drawn under the foot it is on. Five ship (copper, iron, golden, diamond and netherite) and every metal another mod brings gets one of its own. They are bought from the equestrian metalsmith. Each worn shoe adds a little speed and jump from its own metal, and a horse with at least one shoe on stands on powder snow. A shoe can take one of two enchantments: Grip, for ice, or Roadster, for paths and roads.

Design reasoning Shoes are a good fit for the mod because they are a piece of utility gear that is real and visible but still anchored to the horse rather than to a chest or a ride mode. They also create a clean dividing line between purely cosmetic tack and useful wearable items: a shoe is not decoration, it changes how the horse interacts with the world. That matches the mod's utility-over-decoration rule: what a shoe does (its metal's speed and jump, powder snow, grip on ice, speed on a road) is what makes it worth buying, and how it looks comes second. The design keeps each shoe as a separate stack on a separate hoof, because a horse can throw one shoe and wear unevenly, and a single "shod" flag could never say that. It is also why the bonus is a flat amount per shoe and not a percentage: four shoes are four additions, and a horse with three is exactly three quarters shod. Why a U under the foot The owner's shape (2026-10-11): a shoe is seen on the underside of the foot with only the barest bit on the hoof wall, runs round the front and sides and not the back, and is a U underneath too, so the frog shows. A band round the whole hoof wall would read as a boot.
