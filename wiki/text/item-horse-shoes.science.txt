Horse shoes
(Horse gear / hoof equipment)
A horse shoe is one item to a hoof : four separate stacks in four separate gear slots, each in its own metal, drawn under the foot it is on. Five ship (copper, iron, golden, diamond and netherite) and every metal another mod brings gets one of its own. They are bought from the equestrian metalsmith. A shoe does not change how the horse moves yet: the stat bonus, powder snow and the enchantments are on the Roadmap tab.

Design reasoning Shoes are a good fit for the mod because they are a piece of utility gear that is real and visible but still anchored to the horse rather than to a chest or a ride mode. They also create a clean dividing line between purely cosmetic tack and useful wearable items: a shoe is not decoration, it changes how the horse interacts with the world. That matches the mod's utility-over-decoration rule, and it is why the shoes built so far, which only show, are half a feature: the effects on the Roadmap tab are what make a shoe worth buying. The design keeps each shoe as a separate stack on a separate hoof, because a horse can throw one shoe and wear unevenly, and a single "shod" flag could never say that. It is also why the bonus is planned as a flat amount per shoe and not a percentage: four shoes are four additions, and a horse with three is exactly three quarters shod. Why a U under the foot The owner's shape (2026-10-11): a shoe is seen on the underside of the foot with only the barest bit on the hoof wall, runs round the front and sides and not the back, and is a U underneath too, so the frog shows. A band round the whole hoof wall would read as a boot.
