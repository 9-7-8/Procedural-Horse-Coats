Horse shoes
(Horse gear / hoof equipment)
Horse shoes are the first true hoof item in the accessories system: one item per hoof, each with its own material, enchantment and durability, yet all of them living under the same attachment-backed gear model the rest of the horse gear uses. They are not a special case on the horse; they are four independent stacks sharing one shared rule set. Any metal can be used, and the tier of that metal is what decides how much the shoe increases the horse's movement stats.

Design reasoning Shoes are a good fit for the mod because they are a piece of utility gear that is real and visible but still anchored to the horse rather than to a chest or a ride mode. They also create a clean dividing line between purely cosmetic tack and useful wearable items: a shoe is not decoration, it changes how the horse interacts with the world. That matches the mod's utility-over-decoration rule and keeps the shoe system from collapsing into a gimmick whose only value is a texture change. The real concurrency risk is not the art, it is the state model. The design should keep each shoe as a separate stack with a separate enchantment profile and a separate render root, because the horse body is too rich to treat footwear as a single global flag. That is the core rule to keep when this page is expanded later.
