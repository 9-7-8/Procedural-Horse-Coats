Vet’s kit
(Item / breeding)
Geld a stallion so he never breeds, or examine a horse: where a mare is in her heat, and whether she is carrying twins.

Where it lives ModItems.VET_KIT , a plain Item with durability(64) . The model borrows vanilla’s bundle texture as a placeholder. server/VetKitHandler handles PlayerInteractEvent.EntityInteract and cancels on both sides. Sneaking gelds: male, not already gelded, tamed, and HorseOwnership.isOwner , then HorseRecords.apply(horse, record.withGelded(true)) , which syncs the record to tracking clients. A plain use prints ReproHandler.vetReport . The flag is HorseRecord.gelded (codec field gelded ), and HorseRecord.entire() is the stallion test. HorseRecord.sexLabel is the display word. Recipe data/horsegenetics/recipe/vet_kit.json ; trade villager_trade/equestrian_scientist/level_1/sell_vet_kit.json — 4 emeralds at Novice, three uses. Gelding reaches the record (shown 2026-09-30) Shown by the clockwork run in the debug test yard, as the last step of the SEED JAR clockwork pen in row AQ west ( seed jars ): the yard's fake-player hands sneak-use the kit on the jar stud, read his record's gelded flag, and hold out an empty jar. The first run with it logged CLOCKWORK PASS : “is now a gelding”, the record's gelded flag true, and the empty jar refused with “is a gelding - there is nothing to collect.” So the flag reaches HorseRecord , not just the chat line, and the jar handler reads it. Known gaps A few stallion checks still read sex alone; see gap 229 . The texture is a placeholder. The icon, since 2026-09-29 The kit used to be vanilla’s bundle, hue-shifted red — a brown leather pouch tinted, which read as a red bag rather than anything medical. It is now a licensed icon: a stoppered flask with a paper tag, from Raven Fantasy HD by Caio Carlos of Clockwork Raven Studios. Its recipe has been removed from bake-item-recolours.mjs , because that tool writes straight over textures/item/ and a recipe left behind would have repainted the new icon on the next re-bake.
