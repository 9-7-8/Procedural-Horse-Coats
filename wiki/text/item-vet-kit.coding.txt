Vet’s kit
(Item / breeding)
Geld a stallion so he never breeds, or examine a horse: where a mare is in her heat, and whether she is carrying twins.

Where it lives ModItems.VET_KIT , a plain Item with durability(64) . The model borrows vanilla’s bundle texture as a placeholder. server/VetKitHandler handles PlayerInteractEvent.EntityInteract and cancels on both sides. Sneaking gelds: male, not already gelded, tamed, and HorseOwnership.isOwner , then HorseRecords.apply(horse, record.withGelded(true)) , which syncs the record to tracking clients. A plain use prints ReproHandler.vetReport . The flag is HorseRecord.gelded (codec field gelded ), and HorseRecord.entire() is the stallion test. HorseRecord.sexLabel is the display word. Recipe data/horsegenetics/recipe/vet_kit.json ; trade villager_trade/equestrian_scientist/level_1/sell_vet_kit.json — 4 emeralds at Novice, three uses. Gelding reaches the record (shown 2026-09-30) Shown by the clockwork run in the debug test yard, as the last step of the SEED JAR clockwork pen in row AQ west ( seed jars ): the yard's fake-player hands sneak-use the kit on the jar stud, read his record's gelded flag, and hold out an empty jar. The first run with it logged CLOCKWORK PASS : “is now a gelding”, the record's gelded flag true, and the empty jar refused with “is a gelding - there is nothing to collect.” So the flag reaches HorseRecord , not just the chat line, and the jar handler reads it. A gelding never covers (shown 2026-10-01) A mare in heat penned with a gelding is never covered. The GELDING clockwork pen in row AU gelded a tamed stallion the real way (the yard's hands sneak-using the kit) and kept two mares in heat beside him for an hour, no carrots, with an entire stallion and two mares next door as the control. It logged CLOCKWORK PASS at 00:53 on 2026-10-02: “0 pregnancies and 0 foals in the gelding pen; 720 looks of 720 found a mare who could still be covered this heat within 3.0 blocks of him”, against 48 pregnancies in the control pen. So the cover path is asked and says no; it is not that the pair never met. Known gaps A few stallion checks still read sex alone; see gap 229 . The texture is a placeholder. The icon, since 2026-09-29 The kit used to be vanilla’s bundle, hue-shifted red — a brown leather pouch tinted, which read as a red bag rather than anything medical. It is now a licensed icon: a stoppered flask with a paper tag, from Raven Fantasy HD by Caio Carlos of Clockwork Raven Studios. Its recipe has been removed from bake-item-recolours.mjs , because that tool writes straight over textures/item/ and a recipe left behind would have repainted the new icon on the next re-bake.
