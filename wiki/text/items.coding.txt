Items & recipes
(Gameplay / items)
Every item the mod adds, and where to read about it. They live in three creative tabs — Horse Genetics , then Horse Breeds for the one spawn egg per breed, then Horse Carts — and nearly all of them are built on one material: horse hair , which you get by shearing a horse.

The creative tab item/ModCreativeTabs registers one Horse Genetics tab holding every item in ModItems.TAB_ITEMS , plus one filled breed spawn egg per breed that has one. The blank breed egg is not listed anywhere: its whole content is the breed component, and one without a breed is a puzzle rather than an item. The custom spawn egg and the filled breed eggs additionally appear in vanilla’s Spawn Eggs tab. In a dev build, the Spawn Test Horse World button’s starting inventory contains one of every item. Known gaps, across the roster Art. The four tickets share one texture, the four whistles share one, both stall signs borrow minecraft:item/oak_sign . The blank transfer paper reuses the research-paper texture. A stall is only ever read by a ticket. There is no auto-return and no “assigned pen” behaviour, and only breaking the exact sign block releases one. The sheared look is unbuilt — see horse hair . Nothing generated for another mod’s materials has ever been drawn — not a modded double gate , not a modded horse armour, not either one’s icon — and no generated recipe has ever successfully parsed. Gaps 271–275 . The known_gene_splice_carrot is one generic item parameterised by a component, so every gene’s carrot looks the same in the inventory. Adding an item Recipes live in neoforge-26.1.2/src/main/resources/data/horsegenetics/recipe/ ; the item objects in neoforge-26.1.2/…/item/ModItems.java . When you add an item or change a recipe, give it a page here in the same change and register that page in wiki/pages.js — the roster above is a table of links, so an unlisted item is an item nobody can find.
