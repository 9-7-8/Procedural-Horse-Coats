Items & recipes
(Gameplay / items)
Every item the mod adds, and where to read about it. They live in three creative tabs — Horse Genetics , then Horse Breeds for the one spawn egg per breed, then Horse Carts — and nearly all of them are built on one material: horse hair , which you get by shearing a horse.

The creative tab item/ModCreativeTabs registers one Horse Genetics tab holding every item in ModItems.TAB_ITEMS , plus one filled breed spawn egg per breed that has one. The blank breed egg is not listed anywhere: its whole content is the breed component, and one without a breed is a puzzle rather than an item. The custom spawn egg and the filled breed eggs additionally appear in vanilla’s Spawn Eggs tab. In a dev build, the Spawn Test Horse World button’s starting inventory contains one of every item. Chest loot — every chest, any mod Four global loot modifiers put this mod’s things in chests: data/loot/AddResearchPaperModifier , AddGoldenCarrotSeedsModifier , AddBreedSpawnEggModifier and AddHorseTackModifier . Three of the four reach every chest table in every namespace ; the breed spawn egg still names its ten, because a foundation horse turning up in an arbitrary modded chest is a different question from a saddle doing so. Why a regex condition and not NeoForge’s neoforge:loot_table_id tests one exact id , so “put this in chest loot” was written as an any_of of however many ids somebody thought to list — eight to ten vanilla chests apiece. On a heavily modded server that is close to nothing: most of the chests a player opens belong to another mod’s structures, and none of those were ever in the list. That is why the owner had never once found a golden carrot seed. data/loot/LootTableMatchesCondition ( horsegenetics:loot_table_matches ) matches the queried table id against a regular expression instead, and [^:]+:chests/.+ says the whole thing in one line. It matches the path rather than the namespace, because vanilla and nearly every structure mod file their chest tables under chests/ ; it deliberately does not match block, entity, fishing or archaeology tables, since a horse armour falling out of a sheep is not what was asked for. A malformed pattern is a DataResult error rather than a thrown exception, so a datapack that mistypes one loses that modifier instead of the whole pack. 26.1.2 has no LootItemConditionType class. The registry holds the MapCodec itself, exactly as the loot-function registry does — see data/loot/ModLootConditions , and the API notes . AddHorseTackModifier draws one piece from a weighted pool: leather horse armour 10, saddle 8, iron 6, gold 4, diamond 1. Every modded armour shares a single share of 5 and is drawn uniformly inside it. That is not tidiness — compat/ModdedArmour registers one armour per metal any installed mod adds, and a 319-jar pack brings dozens, so a weight each would make vanilla’s four the rare ones and turn the pool into a survey of the player’s modlist. With no such mods installed the bucket is not in the draw at all. The saddle and the leather armour get a tack_tint , not vanilla’s dyed_color — same reason as the bench and SetTackTintFunction : dyed_color holds one value per stack, reaches only the blanket, and does nothing at all to a saddle. No two items look alike Every item in this pack is distinguishable from every other item in it, and from every item in Minecraft. That was not true until 2026-09-29: fifteen sprites were either a vanilla texture used unchanged or one drawing shared by up to five items, so a hotbar of tickets was five identical cards and the vet’s kit was a bundle. This matters more than tidiness. An item drawn as a vanilla one is an item a player does not notice they have picked up, and five items drawn the same are five tooltips they have to hover to sort out. The turnout ticket is the sharpest case: it is the one ticket that cannot be undone , and it used to be pixel-identical to the four that can be. The per-wood families — a wagon in each vanilla wood, and the same for every cart, gate and jump — look alike because acacia and jungle planks look alike. That is the contract: the sprite says what the thing is made of, and recolouring one would be a lie about the material. check-duplicate-item-art.mjs is what keeps this true, and it fails the build rather than warning. It asks four questions: does any model draw a bare vanilla texture, do two models share one, are two of our sprites the same picture, and — the one that matters most — are two of them too alike to tell apart . That last test exists because the first version of this pass shipped a recolour that changed every pixel, passed a hash comparison, and was invisible to the eye. A checker that certifies the thing it was meant to catch is worse than no checker. The colours themselves are a table in bake-item-recolours.mjs , one line per sprite with its source and its numbers, so re-tuning one is an edit and a re-run. It derives from pristine copies under tools/item-art/ rather than from the live textures, which is what makes running it twice safe. Known gaps, across the roster Art. Every item in the roster is now at least distinguishable — see no two items look alike — but a good many of them are a recolour of somebody else’s drawing rather than a drawing of their own. The whistles are one whistle in four colours, the tickets one ticket in five, and the vet’s kit is a bundle. That is a real improvement on two items you could not tell apart and it is not the same thing as art. A stall is only ever read by a ticket. There is no auto-return and no “assigned pen” behaviour, and only breaking the exact sign block releases one. The sheared look is unbuilt — see horse hair . Nothing generated for another mod’s materials has ever been drawn — not a modded double gate , not a modded horse armour, not either one’s icon — and no generated recipe has ever successfully parsed. Gaps 271–275 . The known_gene_splice_carrot is one generic item parameterised by a component, so every gene’s carrot looks the same in the inventory. Adding an item Recipes live in neoforge-26.1.2/src/main/resources/data/horsegenetics/recipe/ ; the item objects in neoforge-26.1.2/…/item/ModItems.java . When you add an item or change a recipe, give it a page here in the same change and register that page in wiki/pages.js — the roster above is a table of links, so an unlisted item is an item nobody can find.
