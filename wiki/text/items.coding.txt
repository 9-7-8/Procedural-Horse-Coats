Items & recipes
(Gameplay / items)
Every item the mod adds, and where to read about it. They live in three creative tabs — Horse Genetics , then Horse Breeds for the one spawn egg per breed, then Horse Carts — and nearly all of them are built on one material: horse hair , which you get by shearing a horse.

The creative tab item/ModCreativeTabs registers one Horse Genetics tab holding every item in ModItems.TAB_ITEMS , plus one filled breed spawn egg per breed that has one. The blank breed egg is not listed anywhere: its whole content is the breed component, and one without a breed is a puzzle rather than an item. The custom spawn egg and the filled breed eggs additionally appear in vanilla’s Spawn Eggs tab. In a dev build, the Spawn Test Horse World button’s starting inventory contains one of every item. Chest loot — every chest, any mod Four global loot modifiers put this mod’s things in chests: data/loot/AddResearchPaperModifier , AddGoldenCarrotSeedsModifier , AddBreedSpawnEggModifier and AddHorseTackModifier . Three of the four reach every chest table in every namespace ; the breed spawn egg still names its ten, because a foundation horse turning up in an arbitrary modded chest is a different question from a saddle doing so. Why a regex condition and not NeoForge’s neoforge:loot_table_id tests one exact id , so “put this in chest loot” was written as an any_of of however many ids somebody thought to list — eight to ten vanilla chests apiece. On a heavily modded server that is close to nothing: most of the chests a player opens belong to another mod’s structures, and none of those were ever in the list. That is why the owner had never once found a golden carrot seed. data/loot/LootTableMatchesCondition ( horsegenetics:loot_table_matches ) matches the queried table id against a regular expression instead, and [^:]+:chests/.+ says the whole thing in one line. It matches the path rather than the namespace, because vanilla and nearly every structure mod file their chest tables under chests/ ; it deliberately does not match block, entity, fishing or archaeology tables, since a horse armour falling out of a sheep is not what was asked for. A malformed pattern is a DataResult error rather than a thrown exception, so a datapack that mistypes one loses that modifier instead of the whole pack. 26.1.2 has no LootItemConditionType class. The registry holds the MapCodec itself, exactly as the loot-function registry does — see data/loot/ModLootConditions , and the API notes . AddHorseTackModifier draws one piece from a weighted pool: leather horse armour 10, saddle 8, iron 6, gold 4, diamond 1. Every modded armour shares a single share of 5 and is drawn uniformly inside it. That is not tidiness — compat/ModdedArmour registers one armour per metal any installed mod adds, and a 319-jar pack brings dozens, so a weight each would make vanilla’s four the rare ones and turn the pool into a survey of the player’s modlist. With no such mods installed the bucket is not in the draw at all. The saddle and the leather armour get a tack_tint , not vanilla’s dyed_color — same reason as the bench and SetTackTintFunction : dyed_color holds one value per stack, reaches only the blanket, and does nothing at all to a saddle. Known gaps, across the roster Art. The four tickets share one texture, the four whistles share one, both stall signs borrow minecraft:item/oak_sign . The blank transfer paper reuses the research-paper texture. A stall is only ever read by a ticket. There is no auto-return and no “assigned pen” behaviour, and only breaking the exact sign block releases one. The sheared look is unbuilt — see horse hair . Nothing generated for another mod’s materials has ever been drawn — not a modded double gate , not a modded horse armour, not either one’s icon — and no generated recipe has ever successfully parsed. Gaps 271–275 . The known_gene_splice_carrot is one generic item parameterised by a component, so every gene’s carrot looks the same in the inventory. Adding an item Recipes live in neoforge-26.1.2/src/main/resources/data/horsegenetics/recipe/ ; the item objects in neoforge-26.1.2/…/item/ModItems.java . When you add an item or change a recipe, give it a page here in the same change and register that page in wiki/pages.js — the roster above is a table of links, so an unlisted item is an item nobody can find.
