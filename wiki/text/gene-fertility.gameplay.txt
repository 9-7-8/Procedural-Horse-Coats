Fertility — how readily a horse breeds
(Natural gene / paints nothing)
Three alleles. n is ordinary. Two copies of sf make every natural cover half as likely to take. tw makes a pregnancy more likely to be twins. Every copy also carries a small number that nudges a mare’s odds. What a pregnancy is, and when a mare can have one, is on fertility & gestation .

Crossing two of them What each combination does Horse Shown as What it means n/n , sf/n Wild type Ordinary. One sf copy is silent. sf/sf Subfertile Every natural cover is half as likely to take. Golden carrots are not affected - that path always gives a foal - so this slows a herd left to itself and never a pairing you arranged by hand. tw/n , tw/sf Twin-prone A pregnancy is twins about one time in ten. tw/tw Strongly twin-prone A pregnancy is twins about one time in four. An ordinary mare’s pregnancy is twins about one time in fifty. Twins only happen in a pregnancy , never from plain golden carrots. It is the mare’s alleles that count for twins. For subfertility, either parent counts, and two subfertile parents make it a quarter. It has no effect on health. Where it turns up Wild horses sometimes carry sf or tw , and a few are tw/tw , but no wild horse is subfertile. That is something you breed.
