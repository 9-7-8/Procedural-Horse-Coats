The horse realm
(Gameplay / world)
A flat, sunlit, bounded field where nothing can hurt a horse. Step through a hay-bale portal, let your surplus go with a freedom stick , and it stays there — healed, wild, keeping its name and its whole pedigree — until somebody else tames it.

What it is for Breeding horses makes more horses than anyone wants. The honest ways to deal with the rest are all bad: kill them, wall them up in a pen you stop visiting, or turn them loose in the Overworld where a creeper, a drop or a hostile night finishes them. The realm is the fourth answer. It is a commons : everybody’s horses, in one field, safe from everything including each other, and anybody may take one home. It is not the horse dimension you may already know. That one is a private test corridor full of pens, it is still there, and it is reached by F6 now rather than by a portal. The hay portal goes here instead. Getting there, and getting back Exactly as before: build a vertical rectangle of hay bales, right-click a frame block with a golden carrot , and stand in it for ten seconds. Horses go through on their own after three seconds, so the roped-horse shortcut still works — right-click the portal while leading a string of them and they all go. Your own portal always comes out in the same place. There are a hundred portals in the field, on a grid, and the one you arrive at is decided by where in the Overworld you built yours. That is the whole reason the field is usable: the horses you released last week are where you left them, and two players who built their portals in different places do not land on top of each other. Any of the hundred takes you home again, to the exact portal you came in by. If somebody mined it while you were away, it is put back. Your own tamed horses standing within about fifty blocks of the exit come with you; everything you released stays. The field Thing What it is Size A thousand chunks square — sixteen thousand blocks on a side. Big, and finite : you can walk it, search it, and lose nothing in it. The ground One flat layer of dirt on bedrock. No hills, no caves, no trees, nothing to fall into or get stuck behind. Water A small pool every eighty blocks. Not decoration — a horse only heals near water , so a dry field would be one where a hurt horse never recovered. Exits A hay portal every sixteen hundred blocks, a hundred of them, so you are never more than about a thousand blocks from a way out. The edge An invisible wall. Nothing walks, gallops or is led out of the field. The sky Permanent day. It never gets dark, it never rains. Everything else There isn’t any. No mob but a horse can be in the realm — not spawned, not summoned, not led in. You cannot build or break there, and neither can anybody else. The field is shared and it stays the way it is. Nothing happens to a horse here Every horse in the realm — released, or yours and just visiting — cannot be hurt, cannot die and cannot despawn . Not by you, not by another player, not by fall, fire, drowning, starvation or a lethal foal genotype. All of it comes straight back the moment the horse leaves. Letting one go Craft a freedom stick from a feather, a stick and one horse hair , and right-click your own horse with it while you are both in the realm. It is healed to full, its tack comes back to you, and it is wild. It is still the same horse. Its name, its genes, its breed, its parents and its whole family tree are untouched. Whoever tames it next — the ordinary way, by riding it until it stops throwing them, or by feeding it — gets all of that, and becomes its owner. You cannot release a horse that is not yours, and the stick does nothing outside the realm. It lasts 64 releases, and a refusal costs nothing. They breed, slowly Released horses form bands and breed like wild horses, and their foals are born wild and protected too — so a field that people keep adding to fills up on its own. It runs at a quarter speed by default. Heat, the wait between covers and the length of a pregnancy are all four times what they are at home. A server owner can change that with realm.breeding_rate_percent , anywhere from 0 to 100; zero stops it entirely, pregnancies included. Nothing at all happens while nobody is there. A mare left pregnant in an empty realm is exactly as pregnant when somebody next walks in, however long that took. The field does not run in the background, and a server pays nothing for one nobody is visiting.
