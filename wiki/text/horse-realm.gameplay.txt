The horse realm
(Gameplay / world)
A flat, sunlit, bounded field where nothing can hurt a horse. Step through a hay-bale portal, let your surplus go with a freedom stick , and it stays there — healed, wild, keeping its name and its whole pedigree — until somebody else tames it.

What it is for Breeding horses makes more horses than anyone wants. The honest ways to deal with the rest are all bad: kill them, wall them up in a pen you stop visiting, or turn them loose in the Overworld where a creeper, a drop or a hostile night finishes them. The realm is the fourth answer. It is a commons : everybody’s horses, in one field, safe from everything including each other, and anybody may take one home. It is not the horse dimension you may already know. That one is a private test corridor full of pens, it is still there, and it is reached by F6 now rather than by a portal. The hay portal goes here instead. Getting there, and getting back Build a vertical rectangle of cobblestone , right-click a frame block with a golden carrot , and stand in it for ten seconds. Horses go through on their own after three seconds, so the roped-horse shortcut still works — right-click the portal while leading a string of them and they all go. It used to be hay, and a horse ate one A hay bale is the largest single meal in the mod and a horse eats it out of the world , not out of your hand — so a portal framed in hay is a portal the animals standing next to it will eventually eat, collapsing it and stranding whoever was on the far side. That is two features working correctly and meeting, which is why the answer is a frame nothing wants to eat rather than a rule about grazing. The golden carrot is unchanged : it was never the part a horse could take off the wall. Every portal comes out in the same place — the exit in the middle of the field. It used to be one of the hundred, picked from where in the Overworld you built yours, so that two players never landed on top of each other. That turned out to be the wrong thing to want: in a field sixteen thousand blocks across, landing apart does not keep players out of each other's way, it keeps their horses apart, in pockets that never meet. A shared field whose whole point is the horses living in it wants them together. Any of the hundred takes you home again, to the exact portal you came in by. If somebody mined it while you were away, it is put back. Your own tamed horses standing within about fifty blocks of the exit come with you; everything you released stays. The field Thing What it is Size A thousand chunks square — sixteen thousand blocks on a side. Big, and finite : you can walk it, search it, and lose nothing in it. The ground One flat layer of grass on bedrock, at y 64 — the height the Overworld's own sea level sits at, so the sky is where your eye expects it. No hills, no caves, no trees, nothing to fall into or get stuck behind. Water A small pool every eighty blocks. Not decoration — a horse only heals near water , so a dry field would be one where a hurt horse never recovered. Exits A portal every sixteen hundred blocks, a hundred of them, so you are never more than about a thousand blocks from a way out. You always arrive at the middle one; the rest are there for walking home from. The edge An invisible wall. Nothing walks, gallops or is led out of the field. The sky Permanent day. It never gets dark, it never rains. Everything else There isn’t any. No mob but a horse can be in the realm — not spawned, not summoned, not led in. You cannot build or break there, and neither can anybody else. The field is shared and it stays the way it is. Nothing happens to a horse here Every horse in the realm — released, or yours and just visiting — cannot be hurt, cannot die and cannot despawn . Not by you, not by another player, not by fall, fire, drowning, starvation or a lethal foal genotype. All of it comes straight back the moment the horse leaves. Letting one go Leave it there and walk out. A horse of yours still standing in the realm a few seconds after you have gone is wild: healed to full, unowned, free to join a band. Nothing to craft and nothing to remember — if you took it home it is yours, and if you left it behind you left it behind. Horses standing near you when you step into the exit come home with you (about fifty blocks), so this only ever catches the ones you genuinely left. And it is not a trap: there are a few seconds in which nothing has happened yet, so stepping back through by mistake costs you nothing. It is not only the portal Logging out in the realm, dying there, or being teleported out all count as leaving — and so does a horse of yours sitting in country nobody has loaded for a week, which goes wild the moment somebody walks back into it. The rule is not “when you use the exit” ; it is a tamed horse in the realm whose owner is not also in the realm , which is true in all of those cases without any of them being handled. The freedom stick is still there for doing it now , deliberately, while you are standing next to the animal — feather, stick and one horse hair , right-clicked on your own horse in the realm. It is the same release, only immediate, and it hands you the saddle back rather than dropping it in the grass. It is still the same horse. Its name, its genes, its breed, its parents and its whole family tree are untouched. Whoever tames it next — the ordinary way, by riding it until it stops throwing them, or by feeding it — gets all of that, and becomes its owner. You cannot release a horse that is not yours, and the stick does nothing outside the realm. It lasts 64 releases, and a refusal costs nothing. They breed, slowly Released horses form bands and breed like wild horses, and their foals are born wild and protected too — so a field that people keep adding to fills up on its own. It runs at a quarter speed by default. Heat, the wait between covers and the length of a pregnancy are all four times what they are at home. A server owner can change that with realm.breeding_rate_percent , anywhere from 0 to 100; zero stops it entirely, pregnancies included. Nothing at all happens while nobody is there. A mare left pregnant in an empty realm is exactly as pregnant when somebody next walks in, however long that took. The field does not run in the background, and a server pays nothing for one nobody is visiting.
