Zombie
(Magical gene / recessive / one of the two undeath loci)
Two copies and the horse is dead flesh: the rotted green-grey hide of a zombie horse, whatever colour its other genes would have made it, though magical markings still show over it. It counts as undead . One copy is a carrier that looks and acts like any other horse. No wild horse has it: every one comes from a vanilla zombie horse that converted into a Graveborn Warmblood , or from breeding them.

Invented Zombie horses are not real. The locus is the skeleton’s twin, and its look is a base coat rather than a cut-out because vanilla’s zombie horse is a whole horse recoloured, with only a few pixels knocked out — and its green-grey is no pigment a horse has, so it cannot come out of the natural genes.
