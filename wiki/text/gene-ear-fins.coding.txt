Ear fins
(Magical gene / recessive / grows geometry, paints nothing)
Two copies and the horse grows a fin at the outer base of each ear, fanned out from the head and swept back. A single blade, a fan of three or a frill of five rays, and how big it is, how wide it opens and how many rays show, is written on the allele. Their colour is a gene of its own, ear fin colour .

It is two kinds (one per side), EarFinGenerator and EarFinSize , and one line in GrownParts.of dressed by its colour locus . The granting locus is in GrownParts.shapes ’s every-part baseline. The ears do not animate, so the fins are on the head The treatment left this to be read from the sources. In 26.1.2 left_ear and right_ear are PartPose.ZERO children of head , and AbstractEquineModel.setupAnim moves head_parts , the body, the legs and the tail and nothing else. So the ears never move on their own, and either bone would hold a fin still. The anchor walks to head , as every other head anchor does. Because a foal draws this part, FOAL_EAR is a real offset read from BabyHorseModel , not a copy of the adult’s. The mesh: every ray rooted on the anchor A ray is a flat box ( PartNode.width , thin across the fan) turned about x by its place in the fan and then leaned out about z by LEAN . A box turns about x first, so all the rays lie in one plane: the one that holds the head’s front-to-back axis, tipped outward. A blade’s or a fan’s ray carries a narrower point; a frill’s carries a rod down its middle that stands a little proud and past its end. Each ray is its own tree, and the kind scales per element ( PartKind.scalesPerElement ). The first build hung the rays off a shared knuckle. A photo shoot showed what that did to a two-tone fin: a slab one box down a chain sits halfway along PartModel.along , so it was drawn a muddy half-blend of its two colours. A root sits at 0 and is the base colour whole. Every root is at the anchor, so the per-element grow is the same scale a whole-part one would give. A fan’s or a frill’s rays are groups, numbered longest first, so the count shows the first k and grows the next one in. A blade is one group that always shows. EarFinsGeneTest holds the numbering, the mirroring and the colour shape. The epigenome, by cost size : the class is the mesh’s size bucket (two), the remainder a uniform grow, so stretch and girth are equal. form and spread : baked, EarFinGenerator.style(form, spread) . A blade has nothing to open and is one style whatever its spread. rays : shown out of the form’s most (three for a fan, five for a frill), the antler’s tine rule. A blade ignores it. Two size classes and one blade style are a trim to fit the mesh cache’s box budget . There is no membrane between the rays; the treatment leaves it for later, and the sail’s translucent region is the way to add one.
