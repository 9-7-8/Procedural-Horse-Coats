Hydrophobic
(Magical gene / no coat effect)
It will not be ridden into water. Take one in past its knees and it throws you and makes for the nearest shore — which is what every horse in vanilla Minecraft does, and which this mod otherwise quietly fixed for you.

A defect in the magical family The natural side of the mod has disorders; the magical side has almost nothing a breeder actively wants to remove. This is one, and it is a useful shape to have: a locus whose entire breeding project is getting rid of it.
