Seed jars
(Item / breeding)
A jar that stores a stallion’s genetics so you can breed a mare with him later — from anywhere, and without him being there.

How to use one Craft an empty seed jar : glass bottle + horse hair. Right-click an adult stallion you own, in breeding mode (fed a golden carrot). The jar fills and is stamped with his genetics, plus any breeding carrots he was carrying. Right-click an adult mare you own, in heat — the stallion in the jar does not have to be yours. If it takes she is pregnant , and the foal comes when the pregnancy ends. The jar is used up either way. A mare who is not in heat refuses the jar, and you keep it. Her information screen says how long until she is. The jar’s tooltip names the stallion it holds and says if it carries carrot effects, so a shelf of jars reads as a list of sires. The foal is bred exactly as it would be if the two horses had met, so everything on breeding & pedigree applies. What it costs Filling the jar uses up the stallion’s breeding mode, and counts against the three covers he makes in a day before his odds drop. It never stops him: past three he goes on filling jars and covering mares, at half the chance. A jar that does not take is still used up.
