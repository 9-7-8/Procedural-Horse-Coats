Body plate colour
(Magical gene / codominant / colours the body plates, paints nothing)
What colour a horse’s shoulder and hip plates are. The same colours and rules as horn colour (white, the rainbow, pink, black, grey or chaos), on a locus of its own. Two different copies give every plate an edge in the second colour. On a horse without plates it does nothing.

Everything else is AbstractPartColourGene ’s. Its Words carry a split sentence (“each plate is one, the base, and its edge, rib or spike the other”), because the shared root-to-tip wording does not describe a plate. GrownParts.of puts one pair of tints on both sides. The split costs no new machinery. A two-tone part is drawn one box at a time, each in tintAt(PartModel.along(i)) , and along is a box’s depth over the depth of its branch. A slab is a root with children, so it sits at 0 and takes the base colour. The lip, rib and both spike boxes all hang straight off the slab with nothing below them, so they sit at 1 and take the tip colour. That is why a spike’s point hangs off the slab rather than off the spike’s base: chained, the base would sit at 0.5 and be drawn half one colour and half the other. BodyPlatesGeneTest.theSlabIsTheBaseAndItsEdgeTheTip holds the shape, and a mutant that chained the spike turned it red (owner, 2026-10-05: edge and feature in the tip colour).
