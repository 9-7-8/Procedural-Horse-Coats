Ocular
(Magical gene / spots and rings)
Eyes down the horse's neck - open ones, with a white, an iris in the line's own colour and a pupil. They are drawn off a single lattice, so every one of them is a real eye and not a smear that happens to be round.

Many eyes Two copies, and the neck is crowded with them - most of the lattice carries an eye. Each is built from one cell of a single scatter, drawn four times at shrinking radius: a darker socket, the white, the iris and the pupil, concentric because they are the same cell rather than because anything lined them up.
