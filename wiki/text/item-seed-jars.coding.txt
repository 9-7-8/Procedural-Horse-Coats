Seed jars
(Item / breeding)
A jar that stores a stallion’s genetics so you can breed a mare with him later — from anywhere, and without him being there.

What the jar stores The jar stores a GenomeSample — a genotype and an epigenome frozen to code strings — in a stored_genome data component, and the allele draw happens at conception , exactly as for every other pregnancy. StallionSeedJarHandler checks ReproHandler.receptive and calls ReproHandler.breed with the mare’s live genome, the jar’s stored one, and the jar’s carrot_effects as the sire’s. There is no second breeding code path. Filling calls ReproHandler.recordCover and disarm on the stallion. Full flow on fertility & gestation . Known gaps The synthetic sire record still uses the stored UUID as its pedigree edge, and its generation is 0; see the roadmap tab . Verified Collection and the tooltip work (owner-confirmed 2026-09-02). The first test that day found the held jar never changed in creative, because the result went to another slot; the jar now transforms in the player’s hand, creative included. The genome string the tooltip showed omitted the data-driven genes, a GeneCodeDisplay display bug rather than bad stored data, fixed 2026-09-03 and not re-checked. The breeding-mode gate and impregnation are below . The jar's whole run, end to end (shown 2026-09-30 by the clockwork run in the debug test yard). The SEED JAR clockwork pen, row AQ west, holds a stud and two mares tamed to the yard's fake-player hands (the jar checks ownership by UUID) and logs each step as CLOCKWORK PASS or FAIL . The first run passed every step: the unfed stud refused (“must be in breeding mode - feed it first”) and kept the empty jar; a golden carrot put him in love, the jar filled and he left breeding mode; the mare out of heat refused (“not in heat - about 1 min to go”) and the jar was kept; the mare in heat took it and was pregnant; and her foal's record named the stud as its father. On the second run the jar did not take (“It didn't take”, the fertility dice), which the pen logs as INCONCLUSIVE rather than a failure, and the sire check with it.
