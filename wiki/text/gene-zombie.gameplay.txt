Zombie
(Magical gene / recessive / one of the two undeath loci)
Two copies and the horse is dead flesh: the rotted green-grey hide of a zombie horse, whatever colour its other genes would have made it, though magical markings still show over it. It counts as undead . One copy is a carrier that looks and acts like any other horse. No wild horse has it: every one comes from a vanilla zombie horse that converted into a Graveborn Warmblood , or from breeding them.

Crossing two of them What two copies do The look. Vanilla’s zombie horse hide is the horse’s base coat. Its natural colour — bay, chestnut, a pattern — never shows, but a magical marking paints over the hide as over any coat: the Long-buried strain is a zombie with grave dust on it. Black eyes. It is undead to anything that asks: cleansing light, a holy ward, an aura that names the undead. The raw-meat diet, the after-dark temper, the rotten-flesh calm, the flesh drops and the slow, tough body belong to the breed . A zombie foal of two ordinary parents is a rotted hide on an ordinary horse. One copy Nothing shows and nothing changes. A carrier is not undead for any purpose. Is it good for the horse? It has no health effect at all.
