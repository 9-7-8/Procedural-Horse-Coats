Brow ridge colour
(Magical gene / codominant / colours the brow ridge, paints nothing)
What colour a horse’s brow ridge is. The same colours and rules as horn colour (white, the rainbow, pink, black, grey or chaos), on a locus of its own. Two different copies make the bar one colour and what stands on it the other. On a horse without a brow ridge it does nothing.

Crossing two of them The colours, and a two-tone ridge Exactly the horn colour table . Two copies of one colour make the whole ridge that colour. Two different ones make the bar one colour and what stands on it the other: a plain ridge’s step, a notched one’s bumps, or the points of a spined one’s spines. The bar takes whichever colour comes earlier in the list, so Wht/Blu is a white bar with blue bumps, either way round. Every copy carries a little shade of its own. Bare bone Two Bon copies are the ridge’s bone version: bare ivory bone. No wild horse carries Bon ; the skeleton horses do, so a skeleton that grows a brow ridge grows it in bone. A ridge is all bone, so nothing is left off. Is it good for the horse? It does nothing to the horse at all. It is only a colour. Its gene carrot
