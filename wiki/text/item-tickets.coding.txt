Tickets
(Item / stabling)
A ticket sends a horse home. Right-click one of your horses with one and it goes to its stall ; the ticket is spent. The tier decides how far apart the two are allowed to be.

Where it lives item/TicketItem is the item and its Tier — the tier and the tooltip words, nothing else. The interaction is server/TicketHandler , on EntityInteract , following the same shape as every other horse interaction in the mod: cancelled on both sides so the client does not predict a mount, and everything done server-side (see API notes ). The destination comes from StallData.forHorse , but the stall is measured again, live, from the sign rather than read back from the record. Two reasons, and the first one cost a horse: the stored span is a bounding box, and a box around any room that is not a plain cuboid contains the walls inside it. The second is that people rebuild stalls, so re-running the detector means a stall that has been widened or re-fenced since the sign went up is the stall the horse arrives in. The chunk is pulled in first, because a horse teleported into unloaded terrain is the failure that looks exactly like a horse that was deleted. If there is nowhere to stand, the ticket refuses and is not spent — and "somewhere to stand" means somewhere this horse's whole box fits. It used to settle for dead centre when nothing fitted, which is a horse placed inside a block; now it refuses, and every ticket writes a [trace] ticket line giving the room it found and the spot it chose. The version before this scanned the bounding volume and, finding nothing, fell through to the sign’s own block — flush against a wall, with wall material at head height, and the one position in the routine never checked for anything. The horse suffocated. A refusal the player can read beats a horse in a wall. The holding pen is the same machinery pointed at a player instead of a horse: item/HoldingPenSignItem writes a PenRecord (owner, dimension, sign) into StallData , keyed by the player so a second sign replaces the first, and item/HoldingPenTicketItem is handled in TicketHandler.sendToPen - the same live measure, the same dead-centre StallDetector.landingSpot , the same arrive . Breaking the sign goes through StallSignHandler.onSignBroken , which now releases a pen as well as a stall. The ticket’s texture is the ticket’s art hue-rotated, as a placeholder. item/TurnoutTicketItem is handled in TicketHandler.turnOut , and is deliberately two existing pieces bolted together rather than a third implementation of either: arrive for the travel (the same particles, sound and lead handling as every other ticket) and server/HorseRelease.makeWild for the release, which is the single definition of what wild means and the only thing that remembers the temper reset and the wild-spawn mark. The order is load-bearing - move first, release second - so the tack is handed to a player still standing in the world the horse left, rather than dropped on the realm floor where they cannot reach it. The release goes to the horse arrive returns , not the one it was handed: across worlds vanilla builds a new entity from the old one's saved data, and until issue #29 (2026-10-04) the release went to the removed one - the horse in the realm stayed tamed and saddled while the saddle was also handed back. The gametest turnout_releases_the_horse_that_arrived turns a saddled horse out into the Nether (the harness has no realm) and holds one saddle, in the pack, and a wild horse on the far side; it goes red with the old reference. If the move fails, nothing is released or spent. It is also the only release that clears the horse’s StallRecord : the freedom stick works only inside the realm and HorseRealmFeral only fires on a horse already there, so neither can ever meet a horse that still has a stall. It has no destination to measure - the realm has one entrance at HorseRealm.arrivalSpot() on ground the dimension guarantees - so it is the one ticket with nothing that can fail late. It shares the ticket texture too. Known gaps Never played. Shipped in 0.3.7 and not once used in-game — see the Verification tab . The cross-dimension move ( Entity.teleport , which vanilla's teleportTo wraps) is the one the hay portal uses; the gametest turnout_releases_the_horse_that_arrived now runs it Overworld to Nether, but not in a played game. All four share one texture. Verified A stall ticket lands a horse properly (owner, the third play session of 2026-09-10), as a basis still to be pushed on. The horse browser's Send home button no longer spends tickets (2026-10-02, the owner's call). From 2026-09-25 it spent the cheapest ticket in the pack whose tier reached the horse's world. It is now free from anywhere, unless the server sets an item price, which replaces tickets for the button. The checks written for the old spend (cheapest first, the tier refusing, the pen ticket preferred) were retired unplayed with it. Tickets themselves are unchanged: they are still the way to send a horse home by hand, with no menu. See the browser page . Spending a ticket brings a horse home (owner-confirmed in play, 2026-09-10, after the stall rewrite).
