Breeding & pedigree
(Start here)
The single source of truth for the breeding, horse-record, pedigree and stat-inheritance system. Layer 1 ( common/ , pure Java) owns the rules; layer 2 ( neoforge-26.1.2/ ) only translates — it never decides how codes combine, what an ancestor is, or the band a foal’s stats come from.

Breeding, as a player sees it Feed a mare and a stallion the usual golden food and they make a foal, the vanilla way. What is different is what the foal is : every gene is inherited, one copy from each parent, so its coat, its eyes, its speed, health, jump and size all come from its parents rather than from a dice roll. Two horses of the same sex will not breed. Stats are genetic. A fast pair tends to make fast foals, and the horse information screen colours each number green or red against the parents’. Carriers are real. A plain-looking horse can hand on something it does not show; the Genes tab lists every locus a horse is carrying anything at. Breed labels combine. Two of the same breed breed true, two breeds make a named cross, and anything messier is Mixed. A pairing can fail. Some pairs share a hidden lethal: the mare miscarries and loses half a heart, with a line saying what it looked like but never which gene. Other foals are born with a disorder and die within seconds. Magic turns up by itself, very occasionally. About one foal in a thousand is born carrying a magical gene neither parent had — a mutation, out of nowhere — and one in a million takes two copies. Nothing you feed or pair makes it likelier, and it only ever happens to a foal somebody bred: a horse the world spawned is exactly its breed. Twins can be identical. About one twin pregnancy in ten is one egg that split, so the two foals are a single draw carried twice and are alike down to the last streak. See pregnancy . Names follow the family. A pair’s first foal takes the dam’s first name and the sire’s last; later foals drift toward fresh names. Everything is remembered. The family tree shows great-grandparents with their coats, and the Offspring tab reads the pedigree downward. A foal of your tamed mare is yours , tamed to you and starting at a quarter of her bond.
