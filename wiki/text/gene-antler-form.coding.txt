Antler form
(Magical gene / dominance series / shapes the antlers, paints nothing)
How an antlered horse's rack grows: forked like a red deer's, swept back with a brow tine like a reindeer's, broad and palmate like a moose's, crowned with a cup of tines, or a plain spike. One habit, drawn on both sides. On a horse without antlers it does nothing at all.

Gene key horsegenetics.antler_form Class AntlerFormGene Priority 231 (magical band); only a code-order slot Family Grown parts Alleles Frk Brw Pal Crn n — dominance Pal > Crn > Brw > Frk > n Founders Hardy–Weinberg over Frk 0.50, Brw 0.12, Pal 0.08, Crn 0.08, n 0.22 Coat effect none The allele order is the generator's habit order Frk, Brw, Pal, Crn, n is the order the alleles are declared and stored in, and AntlerGenerator 's habit indices — so habitOf(pair) is the dominant allele's order() , and it becomes the first factor of the antler PartShape 's style. Dominance is not the declaration order; it is a separate list. Appending a habit is safe; reordering would rewrite every horse's rack, and removing one makes Genotype.readableStored drop the whole segment. How it reaches the rack GrownParts.of passes habitOf to AntlersGene.racksFor , which builds both sides in it. It carries no epigenetic numbers: how many tines and how big are the antler allele's, and this locus only picks the rule that places them.
