Dorsal spine colour
(Magical gene / codominant / colours the dorsal spines, paints nothing)
What colour a horse’s dorsal spines are. The same colours and rules as horn colour (white, the rainbow, pink, black, grey or chaos, and two different copies make a two-tone part), on a locus of its own. On a horse without spines it does nothing.

A two-tone row costs a submit per box rather than one for the whole row, because the layer draws two-tone parts a segment at a time ( AttachedPartLayer ). That is the existing horn path; a row is many short spines, so it pays more boxes for it. Two different colour alleles on an already rare horse is the uncommon case.
