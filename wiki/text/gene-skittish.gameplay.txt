It does not stop when you get on Aggression pauses nothing for a rider and neither does this, but the consequence is different and worse to own: a mount that bolts when a creeper comes round the corner is a horse you cannot take anywhere. That is the version of this gene a rider actually meets, and it is deliberately not gated away. The one that is genuinely hard to keep Fnp — flees riders after dark — is the difficult one. By day it is a perfectly ordinary horse that can be caught, tamed and ridden. After dark it will not let you near it. Nothing is wrong with the animal; you simply own a horse that is only yours for half of each day.
