(Magical gene / no coat effect)
What a horse runs from, and when. The mirror of aggression , allele for allele — and being ridden makes no difference to it at all.

Built, and not yet seen in game The locus was split out of the two temper genes on 2026-09-17. The record below was earned by the allele it replaces, on the same flee machinery. What to look at is on the Verification tab . Two different shy alleles show neither. It does not stop when you get on Aggression pauses nothing for a rider and neither does this, but the consequence is different and worse to own: a mount that bolts when a creeper comes round the corner is a horse you cannot take anywhere. That is the version of this gene a rider actually meets, and it is deliberately not gated away. The one that is genuinely hard to keep Fnp — flees riders after dark — is the difficult one. By day it is a perfectly ordinary horse that can be caught, tamed and ridden. After dark it will not let you near it. Nothing is wrong with the animal; you simply own a horse that is only yours for half of each day. If it runs from you, you may not be able to keep it either A wild horse that flees every player it sees cannot be approached, so it cannot be fed, so it cannot be tamed. That is the same dead end aggression reaches, walked from the other side: the hunting horse will not let you near it because it is coming for you, and this one will not let you near it because it is going the other way, and neither can be caught. The way in is the same. Passification is a separate locus that says what you can offer the animal, and since 2026-09-25 its veto covers fleeing as well as fighting — pay a shy horse what it asks and you stop being a reason to run, while everyone and everything else still is. A horse with an Fap or Faa pair and no passification allele is, in practice, a horse nobody will ever touch. That combination has only just become possible to meet. The Nightmare , added on 2026-09-25, is the first breed ever to pin this locus — Faa/Faa , flees everything, always — and it carries Prm/Prm beside it, so it is also the first horse in the mod that is frightened of you and buyable at the same time. Until then every shy horse was a wild roll, and nobody had bred one that had a way in; which is exactly why the flee half of the veto was missing for eight days and nothing noticed. Getting close enough to hand it the offering in the first place is the tempt goal 's job: an item that is a live passification route counts as a favourite whatever the horse eats, which means the wider notice radius and a gallop rather than a drift. The arithmetic is meant to beat the flee — the lure is noticed further off than a shy horse's flee radius and re-issues its path every tick, where a flee only re-picks on its beat — and it has never been watched doing so. Health Running is the safest thing on either temperament locus, and a shy horse generally outlives a brave one. The cost is where it ends up: a fleeing horse paths twelve blocks away from whatever frightened it and does not consider what is in that direction.
