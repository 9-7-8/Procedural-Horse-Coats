Rex
(Magical gene / spots and rings)
Open circles in a bright colour, at least three of them, anywhere on the horse - drawn over whatever else it is wearing, with the hooves in the same colour.

The file common/src/main/resources/horsegenetics/genes/rex.json — a data-driven gene , not a Java class. It is listed in the index.json beside it and registered by Genes ' class initialiser at startup. The prose is the part a reader checks a bake against; the list underneath is what the engine actually did. Rex Circles - outlines, not filled disks - in the same colour the horse's speckling runs to. Three at minimum and no upper limit; they can land anywhere and they sit over any paint marking the horse already has rather than under it. They do not disturb the speckling itself, and the hooves take the same colour.
