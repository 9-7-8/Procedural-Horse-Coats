Crystal growths
(Magical gene / dominant / grows geometry, paints nothing)
One copy and the horse grows clusters of crystals out of its back along the spine, from the withers to the croup: see-through shafts with solid points, fanning out from the midline. Where every crystal stands is the horse’s own and is inherited whole. How big they are, how many clusters there are and how far they fan are written on the allele. Their colour is a gene of its own, crystal growth colour .

Invented No animal grows crystals. The look borrows from geodes and quartz druses, clusters of hexagonal points breaking out of rock. It is drawn as eight-sided prisms because two crossed boxes is the cheapest prism the renderer has. This is fantasy and makes no claim about any real gene. It is dominant (the treatment’s call), so like the dorsal spines and the plates it shows on every carrier, and the sail is the body part you have to breed for.
