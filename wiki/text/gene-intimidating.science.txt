Intimidating
(Magical gene / no coat effect)
Nothing wants to be near it. Passive animals and hostile mobs alike keep outside a radius written on the allele copy — at least eight blocks, often more. Standing next to one is the quietest place in the world.

Keeping the cost It would have been easy to make this repel only hostiles and become a straightforwardly good gene. The passive half is kept because a magical locus with a real, concrete, everyday cost is rarer in this mod than one without, and “you cannot pasture this horse with your animals” is a cost a player meets on the first day rather than in a spreadsheet.
