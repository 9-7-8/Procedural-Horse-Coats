Transfer papers
(Item / trading)
How a horse changes hands. Sign a paper against a horse you own and whoever holds the paper can claim that horse — and the paper is drawn as the horse itself, so you can see which one it is.

How they work Craft a blank transfer paper : 8 paper in a ring around 1 horse hair. This is the one shaped recipe in the mod. Right-click a horse you own with the blank. It becomes a signed paper for that horse. You can also buy signed papers from the cowboy . Whoever holds the signed paper can redeem it for the horse. A signed paper is drawn as the horse Not as a piece of paper — as the actual animal, in its own coat, shrunk into the item slot. It is the same picture in your inventory, in your hand, on the ground and in a trader’s window. A stall of six papers is six different horses at a glance rather than six identical papers you have to read the names off. Things worth knowing One blank per craft. Eight paper and a hair is a deliberate act, not a stack you carry around. Signed papers never stack , because two of them are never the same horse. A paper’s name is the horse’s name. Only you can sign your blank , and only against a horse you currently own. Once signed, anyone holding it may redeem it. The picture works off a snapshot taken when the paper was signed, so it still draws the right horse even if that horse is far away, sold on twice, or dead. The command twin — /horsegive /horsegive <player> hands over the horse you are riding , or the one you are looking at within eight blocks, to somebody who is online. It is the same transfer a signed paper makes — ownership moves and nothing else does — without the crafting and without the walk. It does not replace the paper, and is not meant to. The paper is an object : a claim the bearer has not collected yet, which can be sold, handed on, bought from the cowboy or lost in a creeper hole, and which is redeemed by going and finding the animal it names. None of that is a command, and the paper is the more interesting thing for it. What the command is for is the case the paper is clumsy at — two players stood in the same paddock, one of whom wants the other to have the mare they are both looking at, and neither of whom wants to craft eight paper and a horse hair first. Anybody may run it, on a horse they own. Giving away your own property needs no permission, so it is a player’s command and not an operator’s. A gamemaster may give away any horse at all. That is the half that is a moderation tool: somebody has to be able to settle “he tamed my mare while I was offline” , and the riding rule deliberately has no operator exception for an op to simply ride it away with instead. The log says whenever the command was used this way. No naming a horse by name. It was considered and left out: a stable has four mares called Pepper sooner or later, and the failure mode of guessing is giving away the wrong animal — which, unlike almost everything else in this mod, the giver cannot undo on their own. Whoever is sitting on it gets off , at the moment it changes hands. commands.horse_give turns it off entirely — not refused, not registered, not tab-completed. A server that wants the paper to be the only way a horse changes hands gets exactly that.
