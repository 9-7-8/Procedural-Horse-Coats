Tobiano
(Natural gene / phase 1)
Large, smooth-edged white patches that cross the topline — the shape that separates tobiano from EDNRB frame overo . White is placed where a low-frequency BodyNoise field crosses a threshold, with a bias added toward the spine so the patches favour crossing the back. The legs come out white; the head stays coloured.
