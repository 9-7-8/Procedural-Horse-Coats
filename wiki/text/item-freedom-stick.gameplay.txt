Freedom stick
(Item / husbandry)
Give a horse back. Used on your own horse in the horse realm , it heals it, unclaims it, and leaves it standing there with its name, its genes and its whole pedigree intact for whoever tames it next.

Making one Shapeless: a feather , a stick , and one horse hair . It has 64 uses, and only a successful release costs one — every refusal is free and says why. Using it Lead the horse through a hay-bale portal into the horse realm, then right-click it with the stick. It refuses in three cases, all of them on purpose: If Then You are not in the horse realm Nothing. A horse released in the Overworld is a horse abandoned, not a horse given away — it would be somebody’s problem by morning and dead by the next one. The horse is not yours Nothing. The realm is shared; if anyone could unclaim anyone’s horse it would be the most dangerous place in the game to leave one. The horse is already wild Nothing, and it says so. What happens to the horse It is healed to full. It is the last thing you will ever do for it, and a hurt horse in a field may never breed and may take a long time to find water. Its tack comes back to you — saddle and body armour, into your inventory, or at your feet if it is full. It is wild. Not yours, not anybody’s. It has to be tamed the ordinary Minecraft way: ride it until it stops throwing you, or feed it. It is still itself. Same name, same genotype, same epigenome, same breed, same parents, same generation, same place in every family tree. Whoever tames it inherits all of that and becomes its owner. It joins a band with other released horses nearby, and breeds with them — slowly. See the realm .
