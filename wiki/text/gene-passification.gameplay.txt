Passification
(Magical gene / no coat effect)
The way in to a horse that would otherwise kill you. It adds no aggression of its own — it says what you can offer the animal to make it stop, and while the bargain holds it will not target you for any reason.

Built, and not yet seen in game New on 2026-09-17, and nothing here has been watched happening to a horse. The feeding interaction and the veto were written against the 26.1.2 sources and are flagged unverified in their own comments. What to look at is on the Verification tab . What it is for Aggression can produce a wild horse that attacks players on sight, and a horse like that is not a difficult horse, it is an enemy : you cannot get near it, so you cannot feed it, so you cannot tame it. Wherever that allele lands it is a dead end. This locus is the answer to it. The six ways in Allele What feeding it buys Prm the horse never targets you again Tmp it leaves you alone for a while, then wants asking again FPr permanent, but only while it is a foal APr permanent, but only once it is grown FTm temporary, foal only ATm temporary, adult only n no way in at all The two foal-only alleles are the cruel ones: miss the window while the animal is small and the adult is beyond reach for good. One copy is enough, and two different ones stack Unlike almost everything else in the behaviour family this is not a recessive. A single copy expresses, and a horse carrying two different passification alleles offers both routes independently — feed it either thing, either way. A mixed horse is therefore worth more than either of its parents, which is the point: there should be many ways to passify a horse, not a precedence ladder that wastes half of them. What it wants is written on the horse Which item, and how many of it — and for the temporary routes, how long the calm lasts and how long before you can buy it again — are all epigenetic . They are rolled once for a wild horse, written on the allele copy, inherited with it, and drift slightly each generation. Two Prm/n horses are not the same horse: one may want four apples and the other a single golden carrot, and a line can be bred toward an offering you can actually afford. What it does not do Passification is about targeting , and nothing else. A horse with a damaging aura goes on damaging what stands near it; a player who walks into one has not been betrayed by this gene. It is also per player : a horse you have calmed is still perfectly willing to kill your friend.
