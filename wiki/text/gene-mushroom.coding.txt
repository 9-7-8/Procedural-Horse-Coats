Mushroom
(Natural gene / phase 1)
The mirror of silver dapple : it dilutes pheomelanin only , and only when homozygous . On a chestnut the red body walks to a flat sepia-khaki — the “mushroom” colour. A black or bay horse carries it with almost nothing to show.

Cutting red only a little (v1 kept 0.44) barely moved a chestnut off its corner of the gradient and read as “no effect”; cutting it to ~0.12 and adding the black lands the texel near the gradient’s neutral column at a mid value — the dull grey-sepia that mushroom is.
