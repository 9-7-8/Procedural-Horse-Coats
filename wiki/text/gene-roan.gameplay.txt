Roan
(Natural gene / phase 1)
Classic or true roan: an even salt-and-pepper of white hairs through the whole trunk, over a base colour it does not change. Black plus white hairs is a blue roan , chestnut plus white a strawberry , bay plus white a bay roan . The leopard complex's varnish roan shares the word and almost nothing else.

What it does What to look for: white hairs mixed evenly right through the body, over a base colour that is otherwise unchanged. The head and lower legs stay solid. Black underneath gives a blue roan , chestnut a strawberry roan , bay a bay roan . It does not lighten the base colour, it mixes white into it. Do not confuse it with the leopard complex 's varnish roan, which shares the word and behaves nothing like it. Crossing two of them Corn spots Small islands of solid base colour left inside the roan field, as though the white hairs never reached them. They are made by suppressing the roaning rather than by painting anything over it, which is why a corn spot is the horse’s own colour at full strength with a definite edge — not a patch where the roaning merely thins out. A minority trait. The propensity is rolled on the Rn copy the same way the forehead white is, so most roans have none at all , and a roan that has them passes the tendency to any foal inheriting that copy — in the same places, because the lattice seed rides along with it. A line can be bred toward a spotty roan. They appear only where roan was going to put white, so the dark head, the solid mane and tail and the dark lower legs are unaffected.
