RAPGEF5 (EFIH)
(Natural gene / health)
Equine familial isolated hypoparathyroidism. An affected foal develops no parathyroid glands at all and cannot regulate its own calcium.

What it does What it is: a fatal disorder. An affected foal cannot regulate its own calcium and does not survive. Breeding around it: a foal is only affected if it gets a bad copy from both parents, so two carriers are the dangerous pairing. A carrier is perfectly healthy and looks like any other horse.
