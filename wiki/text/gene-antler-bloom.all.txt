Blooming antlers
(Magical gene / recessive / grows leaves on the antlers, paints nothing)
Two copies and leaves, moss or blossom grow on a horse's antlers — a clump on every tine and a few along each beam. Which of the three is inherited. On a horse without antlers this gene does nothing at all.
