Dragon horn colour
(Magical gene / codominant / colours the dragon horns, paints nothing)
What colour a horse’s dragon horns are. The same colours and the same rules as horn colour — white, the rainbow, pink, black, grey or chaos, and two different copies make a two-tone pair — on a locus of its own, so a unicorn with dragon horns can wear a white horn and black dragon horns. On a horse without dragon horns it does nothing.

Invented Dragon horns are not real, so neither is their colour. It is a separate locus from the unicorn horn’s because the owner chose one colour gene per part: a shared colour gene would force every part on a horse to match, and a breeder who wants a white horn over black dragon horns would have nothing to select.
