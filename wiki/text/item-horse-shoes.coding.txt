Horse shoes
(Horse gear / hoof equipment)
A horse shoe is one item to a hoof : four separate stacks in four separate gear slots, each in its own metal, drawn under the foot it is on. Five ship (copper, iron, golden, diamond and netherite) and every metal another mod brings gets one of its own. They are bought from the equestrian metalsmith. Each worn shoe adds a little speed and jump from its own metal, and a horse with at least one shoe on stands on powder snow. A shoe can take one of two enchantments: Grip, for ice, or Roadster, for paths and roads.

How it is built A shoe is an ordinary stack in an ordinary gear slot. Nothing about the slots, the attachment, the sync or the Gear tab is shoe-specific ( Horse gear: the slots ); what this feature added is the items, their place in four tags, and the step that turns four worn stacks into four drawn parts. Built 2026-10-12 (#164). The items item/HorseShoeItem : one class, an item per metal. The metal is the item and not a component, so the colour is a constant of the item ( tint() ). It stacks to 1. common/gear/HorseShoes.Metal lists the shipped metals, their ids and colours. ModItems.HORSE_SHOES registers one per entry. The ids use vanilla's spelling ( golden_horse_shoe ). compat/ModdedShoes registers one per material out of ModdedMaterials.armourMetals() , the list generated horse armour uses, so several mods' steels are one shoe. HorseShoes.ships(material) skips a mod's own copper, iron, gold, diamond or netherite. A shoe whose mod was removed loads as an iron shoe (an alias, by droppedArmours() ). compat/GeneratedShoes writes each modded shoe's item definition, name, its entry in the four tags and its trade. It writes no texture and no model. The slots and the tags The four hoof slots are HorseTackSlot.SHOE_FRONT_LEFT , _FRONT_RIGHT , _HIND_LEFT and _HIND_RIGHT , and each accepts its item tag, horsegenetics:gear/shoe_* . All four tags list every shoe. The generated pack's copies say replace: false and list only the modded shoes, because tags merge across packs. One icon, tinted Every shoe, shipped or generated, uses the one greyscale sprite textures/item/horse_shoe.png (drawn by tools/bake-drawn-items.mjs ; a placeholder for the owner to repaint) through the one model item/horse_shoe . Its item definition names the horsegenetics:metal_tint tint source, and client/MetalTintSource answers with HorseShoeItem.tint() . Drawing them A worn shoe is not an item model. It is the box-built PartKind.SHOE ( the worn side of the attached parts ), one mesh for four feet and every metal. GeneticHorseRenderer reads the four slots once per frame and calls HorseShoes.worn(frontLeft, frontRight, hindLeft, hindRight) with each shoe's tint, 0 for a bare hoof. That returns one WornPart per shod hoof on that hoof's PartAnchor , and AttachedPartLayer draws them. An unshod horse allocates nothing. The icon and the worn shoe take their colour from the same method, so they cannot disagree. The trades The five shipped trades are files under villager_trade/equestrian_metalsmith/level_N/ , listed in that level's trade_set , whose amount went from 3 to 4 so that a shoe never pushes an armour or a buying trade out of a level. A trade set is a registry and does not merge across packs, so the generated pack rewrites a level's file whole: compat/MetalsmithTrades collects what generated armour and generated shoes each add and writes each level once. A modded shoe is sold only where one of its metal's items is registered, the rule its armour follows (#14). Adding shoes changed what the generated pack holds, so the scan's fingerprint rules number went up ( MaterialScan.fingerprint , rules|4 ): a pack generated by an older jar is rebuilt on the first launch. What a worn shoe does Built 2026-10-12 (#247). The rule is pure and lives in common/gear/HorseShoes : bonus(rates, frontLeft, frontRight, hindLeft, hindRight) takes each hoof's Metal (or null for a bare one) and returns a Bonus , the plain sum of what each metal adds. shod(rates, shoes) is the powder snow rule: any one shoe, and it does not depend on a metal's numbers, so a metal set to zero still carries it. Since #248 the full form is bonus(rates, onRoad, Shoe, Shoe, Shoe, Shoe) ( the enchantments ); the form that takes metals is four plain shoes off a road. The rung. HorseShoeItem.tier() is the Metal the bonus is read from: itself for a shipped shoe, and HorseShoes.tierOf(gem) for a modded one (a gem where diamond is, an ingot where iron is). That is the answer ModdedArmour.protectionFor gives and it is pure for the same reason: nothing about another mod's item can be read when ours is registered. The modifiers. server/HorseShoeEffects.refresh reads the four hoof slots and holds the sum as one transient ADD_VALUE modifier on each attribute, horsegenetics:shoes/speed on MOVEMENT_SPEED and shoes/jump on JUMP_STRENGTH . A modifier is replaced only when its number differs and removed when the sum is zero, so an unshod horse carries none. When it runs. Not every tick. Shoes change only when a hoof slot is written, so HorseTackSlot.set calls it for a slot in Zone.HOOVES , and EntityJoinLevelEvent calls it once for a shod horse, because a transient modifier is never in the save. This is HorsePackHandler.refresh 's shape and not GeneAbilityHandler 's per-tick reconcile. The price: a changed shoes.* number reaches a horse when it next loads or is next shod. Saves. Nothing new is saved. The shoes are the stacks already in the gear attachment and the modifiers are rebuilt from them. The pack's slowdown is ADD_MULTIPLIED_TOTAL , so it scales the shod speed: a loaded horse loses the same share of its shoes' speed as of its own. Powder snow Vanilla asks one static question, PowderSnowBlock.canEntityWalkOnPowderSnow(Entity) , from the block's collision shape and from LivingEntity 's climb out of it. It answers yes for the powder_snow_walkable_mobs entity-type tag or for the item in the FEET slot. Neither fits: the tag would float every horse, shod or not, and a horse has no feet slot. There is no method on the entity to override (26.1.2 has no Entity.canStandOnPowderSnow() , which the treatment expected), so mixin/PowderSnowShoesMixin injects at RETURN of that static method and says yes for an AbstractHorse that HorseShoeEffects.shod , only when vanilla said no. It runs on both sides, because a ridden horse is moved by its rider's client; the gear attachment it reads is synced. The enchantments Built 2026-10-12 ( #248 ), the mod's first. Grip ( horsegenetics:grip ) and Roadster ( horsegenetics:roadster ) are data, data/horsegenetics/enchantment/*.json , with no effects block at all: a horse's shoes are four gear slots of this mod's, which no vanilla equipment slot reaches, so nothing vanilla would ever run an effect component. The files only make the enchantment exist, say what it goes on and what it costs. What it does is read in Java. What they go on. supported_items is the item tag horsegenetics:enchantable/horse_shoe , whose one entry is #horsegenetics:gear/shoe_front_left . Every shoe, shipped or generated for another mod's metal, is already in that tag, so the generated pack needed no change and its fingerprint did not move. Every shoe is registered enchantable ( ShoeEnchantments.ENCHANTABILITY , iron armour's), which is what an enchanting table asks for. Where they come from. Both are in minecraft:non_treasure ( data/minecraft/tags/enchantment/non_treasure.json , replace: false ), the one tag vanilla's table, librarian and loot tags all include. The conflict. Both name the enchantment tag horsegenetics:exclusive_set/horse_shoe as their exclusive_set , so a table or an anvil puts one of the two on a shoe and never both. That is the whole conflict: it is per shoe, and the four hooves may mix them. A stack given both by a command gets both; nothing in Java second-guesses it. The read. item/ShoeEnchantments.shoeOf(stack) turns a worn stack into a HorseShoes.Shoe(metal, grip, road) , the two levels read off that stack's own ENCHANTMENTS component. It is the only place a shoe's enchantments are read, on both sides. A level above three counts as three. One total. HorseShoes.bonus(rates, onRoad, ...) sums each shoe's metal, each shoe's roadster when onRoad , and each shoe's grip, into one Bonus(speed, jump, grip) . Roadster's speed is part of the same shoes/speed modifier as the metal's; there is no second modifier. Grip is a share from 0 to 1 and stops at 1. The road tick. The metal's bonus needs no tick, but Roadster depends on the block under the horse, which no event reports. HorseShoeEffects.refresh asks HorseShoes.watchesRoad , and only a horse some roadster would speed up is put in a weak map with what its ground last was. EntityTickEvent.Post looks at the block under those horses every ROAD_CHECK_TICKS (4), and calls the same refresh when the answer changed. With the map empty the handler is one isEmpty() per entity. Air underfoot (a jump, a ledge) is no answer, so the horse keeps what it had. A road is the block tag horsegenetics:shoe_roads , which a pack can add to. Grip. How far a mob slides is decided in one line of LivingEntity.travelInAir : getBlockState(posBelow).getFriction(level, posBelow, this) , stored in the local blockFriction . There is no event, and NeoForge's getFriction hook is for a mod's own blocks. mixin/ShoeGripMixin is a @ModifyVariable on that store (the method's first float local): for a block slicker than ordinary ground (0.6) under an AbstractHorse it returns HorseShoes.friction(blockFriction, grip) , the block's friction brought that share of the way down to 0.6. Everything else returns at the first comparison. It is a ModifyVariable and not a Redirect so another mod changing the same number composes with it. Both sides, as powder snow is. The worn shoe has no glint. The stack glints wherever an item is drawn (vanilla's foil); the box-built shoe on the hoof is drawn by AttachedPartLayer , which has no glint pass. That is on the Roadmap tab. The settings Server-side, in phc/server.toml , read together through ServerConfig.shoeRates() , which is the only thing that builds a HorseShoes.Rates . shoes.enabled (default on): the family's one switch. Off, shoes are still bought, worn and drawn, add nothing, and do not hold a horse up on powder snow. shoes.<metal>_speed and shoes.<metal>_jump , for copper, iron, gold, diamond and netherite: what one shoe of that metal adds, in the attribute's own units. The defaults are the DEFAULT_* constants in HorseShoes . Each is capped per shoe ( MAX_SPEED , MAX_JUMP ) and there is no cap across shoes (owner, 2026-10-09). The iron pair is also what another mod's ingot metals give, and the diamond pair its gems. shoes.grip_per_level (default 0.08, range 0 to 0.34): the share of the slide one level of Grip on one shoe takes away. It is read live, on both sides, so a change is felt at once. 0 turns Grip off. shoes.road_speed_per_level (default 0.002, range 0 to 0.015): the movement speed one level of Roadster on one shoe adds on a road. A change reaches a horse when it next loads, is next shod, or next steps on or off a road. 0 turns Roadster off, and then no horse's ground is watched. The top of each range is a constant in HorseShoes ( MAX_GRIP_PER_LEVEL , MAX_ROAD_SPEED_PER_LEVEL ), as the metals' caps are. shoes.enabled turns both enchantments off with everything else. The tooltip reads the same Rates , so it states the server's numbers and not the defaults; with the family off it says shoes change nothing on this server. What is tested HorseShoesTest (common), the bonus: four metals each add their own amount and taking any one off loses exactly its share; a bare horse gets nothing and each better metal gives more, inside the cap; a server's numbers are capped per shoe and not across shoes, an unreadable one falls back to the default, and the switch turns off both the bonus and powder snow; one shoe is enough for powder snow even when its metal adds nothing; a modded gem takes diamond's rung and an ingot iron's. Replacing the sum with an assignment turns two red. Gametest shoes_add_their_bonus : on a real horse, four metals put on one at a time each move both attributes by their own amount; one taken off takes its share; a refresh from bare modifiers puts them back (what a loading horse gets); all four off leaves the horse exactly what it was with no modifier on it. Removing the call in HorseTackSlot.set turns it red. Gametest a_shod_horse_stands_on_powder_snow : an unshod horse is refused by canEntityWalkOnPowderSnow and handed an empty collision shape; with one copper shoe it is accepted and handed a solid one; with the shoe off, refused again. Making the mixin answer no turns it red. HorseShoesTest (common), the enchantments (#248): grip and roadster on different shoes fold into one total with the metals, roadster only on a road and speed only; the same shoes on other hooves are the same total and two roadsters add; only a roadster horse has its ground watched; a level past three counts as three; grip takes its share of ice's slide, never past ordinary ground, and leaves ordinary ground exactly alone; a server's number is capped per level and the total stops at 1; the switch turns both off. Adding roadster off a road turns one red. Gametest shoe_enchantments_fold_into_the_total : both enchantments are registered, refuse to share a shoe, are offered at the enchanting table, go on every shipped shoe and not on boots, a saddle, horse armour or a sword, and every shoe is enchantable. Four shoes with four different loads read back hoof by hoof, off the horse and again through the gear attachment's own save codec. One Grip II shoe makes ice less slick by exactly its share. On stone the speed modifier is the metals' alone; with a dirt path put under the horse and no call from the test it becomes metals plus roadsters, and on stone again it goes back. Disabling the tick's refresh turns it red (0.014 where 0.022 was wanted). Gametest grip_shoes_slide_less_on_ice : two horses on one sheet of packed ice get the same shove; the unshod one must slide at least a block and a half and the one in four Grip III shoes at most half as far. Making the mixin return the block's own friction turns it red (both slid 2.79 blocks). HorseShoesTest (common): an unshod horse draws nothing; one shoe is drawn on its own hoof; four metals stay apart, in hoof order; a black metal is still a shoe; shipped ids are unique and a mod's copy of a shipped metal gets no second shoe. Reversing the hoof order turns it red. Gametest shoes_fit_their_hooves : every shipped shoe is accepted by all four hoof slots and fits no other slot; four metals on four feet read back as put on; taking one off hands it back and leaves the other three; a foal refuses. Removing one shoe from one tag turns it red.
