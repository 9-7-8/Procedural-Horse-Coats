Crystal growths
(Magical gene / dominant / grows geometry, paints nothing)
One copy and the horse grows clusters of crystals out of its back along the spine, from the withers to the croup: see-through shafts with solid points, fanning out from the midline. Where every crystal stands is the horse’s own and is inherited whole. How big they are, how many clusters there are and how far they fan are written on the allele. Their colour is a gene of its own, crystal growth colour .

It reuses the spine row’s anchor, count and saddle zone , and the sail’s see-through regions . The client is unchanged: a new kind with translucentRegions() naming the new CRYSTAL sheet region is all the renderer needs. It is one kind, CrystalGenerator and CrystalSize , and one line in GrownParts.of dressed by its colour locus . The granting locus is in GrownParts.shapes ’s every-part baseline. The mesh: one cluster, one tree Each cluster’s central crystal is the root , standing upright at the cluster’s place along the row. Every other crystal of the cluster hangs off that root at t = 0 , set out to the side and along the row, and leaned away from the midline by rz (further out leans further). So a cluster is one group with one root. The count hides it and the fractional grow shrinks it as a unit, the per-element scale grows it about its own centre, and PartGeneratorTest ’s one-tree-per-element rule holds unchanged. Because the root is upright, its children’s offsets are in the body’s own frame, and a crystal set to the side neither sinks nor floats. A crystal is three boxes. The first is a shaft. The second is the same shaft rolled /4 about its own axis, and the two crossed squares read as an octagonal prism. The third is a short, narrower cap at the top, rolled /8 . The shafts sample CRYSTAL , the cap BONE_TIP . A roll is only ever on a box that does not bend. The crystal antlers have no prism geometry of their own (they are the antler mesh drawn see-through), so the prism is new here, despite what the treatment assumed. The two end clusters sit exactly at the withers and the croup, and the inner four are jittered along the row by their arrangement. So under a saddle the first and last always show and the middle ones hide; the fifth cluster can fall either side of the saddle’s back edge. BackCrystalsGeneTest holds the numbering, the prism shape, the fan and the saddle. The epigenome, by cost seed : one of CrystalGenerator.VARIANTS arrangements, the antler rack’s rule ( EpiValue.seed , never nudged by drift). Each arrangement is one SeededRng walk with a fixed number of draws per cluster and per crystal, so the spread and the size class change only angles and lengths, never where anything stands. spread : baked, CrystalGenerator.style(variant, spread) , three buckets; it scales every outer crystal’s lean. size : the class is the mesh’s size bucket (three; the crystals’ lengths and thicknesses), the remainder a uniform per-element grow, so stretch and girth are equal. count : shown out of the most baked, the antler’s tine rule. See-through shafts, solid points The part’s opacity is AttachedPart.CRYSTAL_ALPHA , the crystal antler’s. It is not on the copy, because the treatment’s four numbers do not include it, and every number on the copy has to reach the part. The blended pass draws only the shafts in the base colour, and the solid pass draws the caps in the tip colour. No glow: glowRegions() answers SOLID as for every part nothing lights, which is where a glow gene would go.
