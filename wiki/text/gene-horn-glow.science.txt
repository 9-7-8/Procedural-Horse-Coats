Horn glow
(Magical gene / recessive / lights the horn, paints nothing)
Two copies and a unicorn's horn glows in the dark — in whatever colour horn colour gives it, both colours of a two-tone horn included. On a horse without a horn this gene does nothing at all.

There is no real locus here A magical gene with no real counterpart. It is its own locus rather than an allele of horn colour because glowing is independent of colour — the owner's words were “glow regardless of color” — and a separate locus is the only way any colour can also glow. Recessive, and with no wild carriers, by the same rule as every magical trail: the allele exists in the wild in the form that can show.
