Cheek spike colour
(Magical gene / codominant / colours the cheek spikes, paints nothing)
What colour a horse’s cheek spikes are. The same colours and rules as horn colour (white, the rainbow, pink, black, grey or chaos), on a locus of its own. Two different copies make every spike two-tone from root to point. On a horse without cheek spikes it does nothing.
