The chase gene
(Planned / not built)
A locus deciding how long a horse you have provoked keeps coming after you — a few seconds, until it loses sight of you, across a fixed distance, never at all, or forever. It was asked for, and researched before it was deferred; the research found most of the machinery already in the mod, because horses can already fight and a wild one already gives up when it loses its target. What is missing is the locus that turns the giving-up rule into a heritable number. None of it is built.
