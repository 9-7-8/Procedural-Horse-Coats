Aggression
(Magical gene / no coat effect)
What a horse attacks, and when. One locus, one grid: a time of day crossed with what it goes for . Being in the saddle is not protection — a horse that hunts riders throws you off first.

Built, and not yet seen in game The locus replaced three older ones on 2026-09-17 and nothing on this page has been watched happening since. Two of the records below were earned by the genes it absorbed and are kept because the machinery they tested is the same machinery. What to look at is on the Verification tab . The grid Every allele is a when and a who . The prefix is the time — Ad by day, An after dark, Aa always — and the last letter is what it goes for. c herds h monsters p riders e horses a everything by day Adc Adh Adp Ade Ada after dark Anc Anh Anp Ane Ana always Aac Aah Aap Aae Aaa Two copies of the same allele, or nothing Every allele here is recessive, and recessive to the others as well as to the wild type. Adh/n is a calm horse. Adh/Anh is also a calm horse — “hunts monsters by day” and “hunts monsters after dark” are two instructions and the animal settles on neither. That is what makes a locus this wide a search rather than a lottery: you must find the same allele twice. Two aggressive horses caught in different places will usually throw perfectly pleasant foals, and the disappointment is the game. What happens if you are on it Nothing about being ridden calms an aggressive horse. Which of two things happens depends on what it wants: if it is aggressive toward riders (or everything), it bucks you out of the saddle and comes for you; if it is aggressive toward anything else, it ignores you and deals with the target. The horse that absorbed this locus used to stop dead the moment anybody mounted it. That was a deliberate design and it is deliberately gone: a horse that hunts people is not a horse you can simply sit on. Health It picks fights, so it takes damage. This locus only makes the horse willing ; how hard it hits is magic fighter , and how much it can survive is magic health . An aggressive horse with neither bravely inconveniences a zombie and then dies of it. If it hunts riders, you may not be able to keep it A wild horse that attacks players on sight cannot be approached, so it cannot be fed, so it cannot be tamed. The way in is passification — a separate locus that says what you can offer the animal to make it stop. A horse with an Aap pair and no passification allele is, in practice, a monster that looks like a horse.
