Dun
(Natural gene / phase 1)
Real-horse TBX3 , and the mod’s second three-allele locus. Two effects that come apart: a body dilution that thins black pigment and leaves red almost alone, and primitive markings — a dorsal stripe into the tail, plus leg bars, a shoulder bar and a face mask on the horses that draw them — that read dark because everything around them got lighter. The third allele is the one that carries the markings without the dilution.
