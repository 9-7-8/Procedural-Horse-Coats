Horse stasis: chambers and the bank
(Gameplay / partly built)
A cheap way to hold horses without them costing server tick time. A stasis chamber is an item a horse goes into; a Horse Stasis Bank is a block that will hold many chambers and give them a browsable UI. The whole point is the cheapest tier, so a server with hundreds of horses can put most of them somewhere that costs nothing, instead of every horse being a real, ticking entity forever. The four chambers are built and work. The bank, and everything that depends on a bank, is not.

The four stasis-chamber tiers Craft a Basic Horse Stasis Chamber from a glass bottle, a wheat, a horse hair and a container of water — the water container is handed straight back, so it costs you a bucket of water and not a bucket. Right-click one of your own horses with an empty chamber and the horse goes in. Right-click the ground with a full one and it comes back out, in one piece: the same horse, with its bond, its name, its tack, its pregnancy, its cooldowns and its place in every pedigree exactly as they were. While it is in there the horse is not an entity at all. It does not tick, does not get hungry, does not grow up, cannot be hurt and costs the server nothing. That is the feature: two hundred horses you are not currently using can sit in a chest instead of standing in a field being simulated. The chamber is not spent Letting a horse out hands you the same chamber back, empty and ready for another one (owner's call, 2026-09-24). One chamber holds one horse at a time — that is what “single use” meant. You never have to re-craft a Spacer chamber to get your diamond's worth twice. Item Recipe Unlocks (needs the Bank, which is not built) Basic Horse Stasis Chamber Glass bottle + wheat + horse hair + a container of water, in any arrangement. Any modded water container matches, by tag. The container is not consumed. Storage only — no searchable information. Intermediate Horse Stasis Chamber Any chamber + an eye of ender. All of the horse's information becomes searchable. Advanced Horse Stasis Chamber Intermediate + a gold ingot. The bank passively collects the horse's milk/drops into its 9-slot buffer. Spacer Horse Stasis Chamber Advanced + a diamond. The horse can be bred while still inside the bank — the other parent must also be in a Spacer chamber — and the foal pops out into the world. Storage is free at every tier and always will be. What the upper tiers buy is what the bank may do with a horse, so until the bank exists an empty Basic chamber and an empty Spacer chamber do exactly the same job, and the tooltip says so rather than implying otherwise. Upgrading an occupied chamber carries the horse across — you do not have to let it out first. What it refuses, and why it says so A chamber is a crafted item used on purpose, so nothing here fails silently. It will not take a horse that is not yours or not tamed (the same HorseOwnership rule the stall signs, seed jar and transfer papers all use, with no creative exception), it will not take a horse with a rider on its back, and a chamber that already holds a horse says so rather than swallowing a second. Releasing refuses in one more case: if the horse this chamber names is already out in the world , which only happens if the full chamber was duplicated in creative. Two entities sharing one id would corrupt the pedigree, so the copy refuses and says why.
