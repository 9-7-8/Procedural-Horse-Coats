Magic jump
(Magical gene / no coat effect)
The magical version of the natural jump locus, RYR2 . One of the five magical body-stat genes — size , speed , health , jump and pull — and like the other three multipliers, most wild horses carry a copy , worth about a tenth either way. Two copies of the same kind add , and no wild horse is ever born with two. (Pull is the odd one out: an additive score rather than a multiplier, and with no vampiric allele.)

Checklist on the Verification tab . One of a family of five This gene is a thin subclass of AbstractMagicStatGene , which is the magic body size pattern generalised to the three additive stats. Everything on this page is true of magic speed , magic health and magic jump alike; only the stat it moves and the allele names change. Magic body size is the odd one out — it multiplies scale , which carries its own two-stage natural clamp, so it kept its own class. Magic pull is the fifth axis, and it is not this class either. The locus has the same shape — codominant, one allele up, one down, a per-copy number inherited with the copy, both copies added — but its sum is added to a 1–10 score rather than multiplied into a game unit, and it has no Vmp allele (the Dhampir gets its pull from a per-strain score on the breed sheet instead). So the multiplier arithmetic, the vampiric allele and the × tables below are this gene’s and its two AbstractMagicStatGene siblings’, not pull’s. Because most wild horses carry a copy of all five, a wild-caught horse has a quiet, independent wobble on every axis at once, and a horse that is remarkable on any one of them is a breeding result rather than a catch. The combination table Combination Outcome Jump strength Typical n/n wild unchanged ×1.00 Springy/n more one copy’s percentage ×~1.10 Springy/Springy double-more both percentages, added ×~1.20 Leaden/n less one copy’s percentage off ×~0.90 Leaden/Leaden double-less both percentages off ×~0.80 Springy/Leaden balanced the two nearly cancel ×~1.00 Ten combinations, ten outcomes once the vampiric allele is counted: Vmp/n ×1.50, Vmp/Springy and Vmp/Leaden that plus or minus the other copy’s percentage, and Vmp/Vmp ×2.00 exactly — the old dhampir’s jump, split across two copies. A codominant locus is the case where every combination genuinely differs, so collapsing any two rows would be a lie about the gene. The contribution is signed — Springy positive, Leaden negative — so the whole table falls out of one line and Springy/Leaden cancels because a positive and a negative are added, not because anything checks for it. It multiplies, it does not add The natural jump loci add a weight per copy. This gene takes the sum of its two copies’ percentages and multiplies whatever those loci settled on, through TraitBuilder.multiplyJumpUnclamped , applied after every addition and bounded only by HorseTraits.MAGICAL_MIN_FACTOR / MAGICAL_MAX_FACTOR (ten times either way). common/genetics/genes/AbstractMagicStatGene.java @Override public void contribute(AllelePair pair, Genotype genotype, GeneEpigenetics epigenetics, TraitBuilder out) { double sum = signedDelta(pair.first(), epigenetics.copy(0)) + signedDelta(pair.second(), epigenetics.copy(1)); if (sum != 0.0) { applyMagic(out, 1.0 + sum); // multiplyJumpUnclamped(1.0 + sum) } } Multiplying is what makes it compose the way you would want: a magically springy pony is still lower-jumping than a magically springy draught horse, because the natural loci settle the horse’s own jump first and the magic scales whatever that turned out to be. It needs both copies’ epigenetics Every non-codominant epigenetic gene asks for “the copy that expresses”, because one locus produces one result. A codominant one cannot: asking for the expressed copy would count one allele twice and the other not at all. So this gene takes an GeneEpigenetics and asks it for copy(0) and copy(1) — the same accessor magic body size and mane colour need, for the same reason. Each copy’s percentage is a bounded normal draw (mean 10% , 7% ) off that copy’s stored epigenetic seed, floored at 1% so a Springy allele can never come out subtracting. The seed is rolled once for a founder and inherited verbatim with the allele, so a foal that inherits the copy inherits the exact percentage — which is what makes breeding two good copies together a real project. Asked about a genotype with no epigenome to read (a wiki table, a punnett display), the gene reports the midpoint : exactly ×1.10 for one copy, ×1.20 for two. Deliberately jump only It moves no other number. Not speed, not health, not size, not pull . The natural loci already own the trade-offs between the stats; this gene is a clean multiplier on one axis so that the five can be bred independently. Verified The multiplier lands on the attribute (2026-09-14, the yard’s MAGIC JUMP pen, read all day): jump strength 0.57 and 0.63 against 0.5.
