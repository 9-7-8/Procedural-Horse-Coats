Manchado — white with islands in it
(Natural gene / phase 1)
The rare Argentine pattern: broad, clean white over the topline with smooth rounded islands of the base colour left inside it , a mostly white tail, and a head, belly and lower legs that stay dark. Close to the inverse of a leopard — a leopard puts dark spots on a white horse; this leaves dark islands in a white field.
