Vortex A broad teardrop reaching from the throatlatch back to about the girth, its outer boundary hard against the coat and made of the pointed ends of its own outermost bands rather than of any outline. Inside it, twelve to twenty-five bands run nose-to-tail on sinusoidal paths, widening at the crests and pinching at the inflections, merging and splitting and dying off between their neighbours. The four colours cycle in order and never repeat side by side, with the cyan and cream toward the core and the magenta and black at the margin.
