Ear fins
(Magical gene / recessive / grows geometry, paints nothing)
Two copies and the horse grows a fin at the outer base of each ear, fanned out from the head and swept back. A single blade, a fan of three or a frill of five rays, and how big it is, how wide it opens and how many rays show, is written on the allele. Their colour is a gene of its own, ear fin colour .

Invented No horse has fins. The look borrows from the fin-like ears drawn on sea horses, kelpies and merfolk in illustration, from the rayed fins of fish, and from the frills of lizards and the external gills of an axolotl. This is fantasy and makes no claim about any real gene. It is recessive and rare (the treatment’s call), so like the dragon horns it is a part you have to breed for.
