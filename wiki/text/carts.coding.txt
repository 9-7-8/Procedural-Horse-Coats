Carts: wagons, plows and what a horse is worth in harness
(Gameplay / non-genetic)
Six horse-drawn vehicles — a covered wagon you can drive from the box seat, a plow , a seed drill , a reaper , a supply cart and an animal cart — in every wood your modpack has , vanilla's twelve and anyone else's. How fast a horse pulls one is the first thing that ever read pulling ability , and it reads the horse's speed at the same time, on purpose — and now what is in the cart as well. Derived from AstikorCarts / NiftyCarts / UsefulCarts, MIT — full lineage below . Not play-tested ; checklist in verification .

18. Where the code is The split follows the mod's usual rule — as much as possible in common/ , and the NeoForge module translates. common/cart/CartDraft.java — the model . Pure: two doubles and a load in, a retention fraction out, plus loaded , which turns an empty load and a fill fraction into the load a vehicle actually asks for. No Minecraft, no RNG, no entity. This is the file to read. common/cart/CartKind.java — the six vehicles, their empty loads and their cargoShare , and MACHINE_LOAD for a treadmill. neoforge/server/HorseDraft.java — the bridge. Finds a live animal's pull and speed, keeps the haulage tally, and is the public surface other mods integrate against . Its retention(entity, kind) is the empty vehicle, since a CartKind has nothing to ask how full it is. neoforge/carts/ — the ported cart system itself. HorseCarts registers it. neoforge/carts/CartWood.java — what replaced vanilla's WoodType as the key. neoforge/compat/GeneratedCarts.java and tools/bake-carts.mjs — models, item definitions, recipes and lang, for modded woods and vanilla's respectively. 19. The one hook Upstream applied a flat pull_speed from the config in AbstractDrawnEntity.setPulling — one number, identical for every animal alive. That is now applyDraught , which reads the horse's genotype instead. The config option is gone rather than defaulted: an override would make the genetics decorative. The modifier is transient, multiplicative ( ADD_MULTIPLIED_TOTAL ), and computed from the attribute's base value — not its current value, which already has the modifier in it and would compound on every re-attach. An animal that is not one of this mod's horses gets HorseTraits.BASE_PULL . A donkey is not feeble for want of a genome. 20. Why the load is polled and not pushed applyDraught used to run exactly once per hitch, because the load was the vehicle's own weight and could not change. Cargo weight broke that: the number now moves while the horse stands still, from three unrelated places — an inventory changing, a chest going onto a wagon, an animal climbing into a cart. AbstractDrawnEntity.refreshDraught polls instead of hooking all three. A missed hook would be a cart that is silently the wrong weight, which is precisely the class of bug a play session cannot see; one poll cannot be forgotten by the next vehicle somebody adds. It is cheap because it only runs on a hitched cart, only once a second, and only touches the attribute when the number has actually moved. currentLoad() also walks the drawn chain, so a train of carts charges the horse for every vehicle in it rather than for the first. Depth-capped, because a looped hitch would otherwise recurse until the server fell over. What counts as "full" is per-vehicle and lives in the entity, not in common/ : fillLevel() is goods-per-slot for the inventory carts, seats for the animal cart, chests-then-goods for the wagon, and flat zero for everything else. None of those questions can be asked without a running game, which is exactly the line the module split draws. 21. Why the carts use RegisterEvent Everything else in this mod registers through DeferredRegister , and DoubleGates and ModdedArmour both go to real trouble to create their per-material entries during class load , because a DeferredRegister will not take an entry once it has attached to the bus. The carts have the same problem and skip the trouble: RegisterEvent fires after mod construction, so ModdedMaterials.scan() has already run and CartWood.all() is simply available. It is also the smaller diff against the MIT source we now maintain. The cost is that the carts do not appear in ModItems / ModEntities ; the class javadoc on HorseCarts says so. 22. The contract that lives twice tools/bake-carts.mjs covers vanilla's twelve at author time; compat/GeneratedCarts.java covers modded woods at run time. Exactly the arrangement the double gates use, and carrying exactly the same warning: change one, change the other. A drift shows as a modded cart that cannot be crafted while every vanilla one can, and logs nothing. Re-run the bake after touching any cart recipe, model or lang key: node neoforge-26.1.2/tools/bake-carts.mjs node neoforge-26.1.2/tools/check-recipes.mjs 23. No cart texture is shipped Upstream's cleverest idea, and the reason a cart can exist in a wood nobody drew art for. mixin/CartModelManagerMixin stitches every cart's entity texture at resource-reload time out of that wood's _planks , _log and stripped__log sprites as they sit on the block atlas. A resource pack that retextures oak planks retextures oak carts for free, and no Mojang art is redistributed. For a modded wood only the plank sprite is known — ModdedMaterials read it out of that mod's files. The log sprites are guessed from vanilla's naming and then checked against the atlas ; a mod that names its logs unusually gets planks in those places instead of a purple cart. Unverified : the "is this sprite real" check assumes the atlas answers an unknown id with the missing-texture sprite rather than throwing. Vanilla's long-standing behaviour, not confirmed on 26.1.2 with a mod installed. 24. Mixins, and the horse ones in particular Six cart mixins joined the mod's own, in horsegenetics.mixins.json — which now has a "client" block for the first time, for the four that touch client-only classes. CartHorseMixin (AbstractHorse) — overrides travel and maxUpStep and injects at getControllingPassenger , all for the box seat. Its travel override calls super.travel on both branches, so HorseFlightMixin 's inject on LivingEntity.travel still fires and flight is unaffected. Unverified — the interaction is argued, not observed. HorseTwoRidersMixin (AbstractHorse) — ours, not upstream's. canAddPassenger and getPassengerAttachmentPoint . The pillion offset is a number picked to look right, not measured. The steering itself is a PostilionEntity : a tiny invisible rider the cart puts on the horse, which the driver's seat then moves by packet. Three vehicles spawn one — the animal cart, the reaper and, as of this change, the wagon, which had the seat and the tooltip but never the rider. 25. The test common/src/test/java/.../cart/CartDraftTest.java . Three of the cases are the design: a balanced horse out-hauls both specialists ; pull sets a ceiling raw speed cannot pass , checked at speeds up to a million; neither stat is a dump stat — raising either one, on every vehicle, always hauls faster. The cargo rule brought three more: that an empty vehicle is exactly its bare load and a field implement never gains any, that the cargo term stays linear (half full is half the penalty, which is the only property that makes it predictable without reading the source), and that a loaded cart both slows a horse and keeps its ceiling — loading a cart must lower the asymptote, not escape it. ./gradlew :common:test --tests '*CartDraftTest' It earned its keep on the first run. The model originally had a flat floor on retention — "never below a quarter speed", so a hopeless horse still visibly moved — and the ceiling test failed, because past the speed where that floor engages haul becomes speed × 0.25 and climbs without limit. A fast enough weak horse out-pulled its own capacity, which is the exact failure the model exists to prevent. The floor was replaced by clamping the score at HorseTraits.MIN_PULL before the curve. 26.
