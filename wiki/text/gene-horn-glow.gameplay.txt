Horn glow
(Magical gene / recessive / lights the horn, paints nothing)
Two copies and a unicorn's horn glows in the dark — in whatever colour horn colour gives it, both colours of a two-tone horn included. On a horse without a horn this gene does nothing at all.

Crossing two of them Finding one Recessive: one copy shows nothing. In the wild a horse carries either two copies or none — about one in eight has two — so roughly one wild unicorn in eight has a glowing horn. Most horses with two copies have no horn to glow, which makes an ordinary-looking horse a good mate for a unicorn you want a glowing foal from. What it looks like The horn is drawn a second time at full brightness, so it stands out at night in its own colour: a red horn glows red, a white one white, and a black horn is a dark shape you can still see in the dark. If a shader pack makes it look wrong, parts.glow in the client config turns the glow off and keeps the horn. Is it good for the horse? It does nothing to the horse at all, for good or ill.
