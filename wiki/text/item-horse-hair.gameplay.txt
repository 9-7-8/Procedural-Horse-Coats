Horse hair
(Item / material)
Horse hair is the base material for almost everything the mod adds, and the only one — there is nothing you refine it into first. You get it by shearing a horse, and nearly every other item on the wiki takes it directly.

How you get it Right-click an adult horse with shears. You get 1–3 horse hair, the shears lose a point of durability, and the horse likes you a little more ( +5 bond — see horse care ). You can shear each horse once per Minecraft day . Foals cannot be sheared. Keeping a few horses around is the practical way to have hair coming in. A sheared horse still looks the same The shearing works, but the horse’s appearance does not change yet, so there is no way to tell by looking which of your horses you have already done today. There is no chain — hair goes in raw Hair used to be the bottom of a ladder: four hair made a hair bundle , three bundles a braided rope , four bundles a hair cloth , and the things you actually wanted took the rope or the cloth rather than the hair. All three are gone. Whatever a recipe wants hair for, it takes horse hair. What that changed, in hair The ladder was quietly expensive, because the cost compounded. A rope was twelve hairs and a cloth sixteen, so: Item Was Now Double fence gate 2 gates + 12 hair 2 gates, no hair at all Equestrian post 32 hair + fences + goods 2 hair + fences + goods Supplier’s post 56 hair + fences + carrot 4 hair + fences + carrot Cowboy hitch 68 hair + fences 5 hair + fences Hair is sheared one to three at a time, once per horse per day, so sixty-eight hair for one hitching post was several real days of brushing. Owner’s call. How recipes work in this mod Nearly every recipe is shapeless — the shape is not the puzzle. The one exception is the blank transfer paper . Every recipe uses at least one modded item and makes a modded item, so nothing here can clash with a vanilla recipe. The one exception is the double fence gates , which are two vanilla gates and nothing else. Nothing you craft is a trap, because there is nothing to convert and so no conversion to regret. What needs hair Breeding carrots and splice carrots — one hair each. Whistles , tickets , seed jars and transfer papers . All five village posts — the cowboy’s hitch and the four posts the equestrians work at — each a couple of hair and a couple of fences, plus whatever that equestrian works with.
