Magic heat tolerance
(Magical gene / no coat effect)
Better or worse in a hot biome. A tolerant allele, a sensitive allele and a neutral wild type; how much each copy is worth, and whether it goes to speed, jump or both, is written on the copy. Its twin is magic cold tolerance .

Gene key horsegenetics.magic_heat Alleles Htol Hsen n Inheritance codominant; both copies add, Hsen counting negative Epigenetic, per copy delta (the amount) and speed_share (1 all speed, 0 all jump) Condition the hot_biome world flag Class MagicHeatGene , on AbstractClimateGene How it reaches the game AbstractClimateGene.abilitiesFor splits each copy's signed delta by its own speed_share and sums the halves across both copies, then emits at most two attribute modifiers, movement_speed and jump_strength , both multiply_total and both gated on hot_biome . The multiplier never falls below MIN_FACTOR , so a horse is slowed and never stopped. delta is a bounded normal draw about MEAN_DELTA , floored at MIN_DELTA so a tolerant copy cannot point the wrong way. Wild founders carry one copy at WILD_CARRIER_PERCENT , half each way. hot_biome is a world flag ( AbilityType.WORLD_FLAGS ), sampled on an interval by GeneAbilityHandler.sampleWorld . It holds when the biome's base temperature is at least HOT_BIOME_TEMPERATURE (1.0, where vanilla's savanna starts), or the biome is in #minecraft:is_jungle , or it is the mangrove swamp. The temperature test takes in modded hot biomes for free; the jungle tag takes in modded jungles that declare it. The API use was type-checked against the running client, not run.
