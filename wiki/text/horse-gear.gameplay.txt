Horse gear: the nineteen slots
(Gameplay / non-genetic)
Everything a horse wears, in one place. A Gear tab on the horse information screen holds nineteen slots laid out on a picture of the horse itself — head, mane and tail, body, chest, a slot per leg and a slot per hoof, and two for saddlebags. Two of them are real vanilla equipment slots and the other seventeen are a data attachment of our own, which is the constraint the whole system is shaped by. The slots exist; almost nothing to put in them does yet. The roster, the storage and the screen shipped first on purpose — every piece that follows is one row in a tag. What is still planned is on this page’s own Roadmap tab, which is the single home for gear plans.

Where it is Open a horse’s inventory as you always have, and press the small i button beside it. That is this mod’s own horse information screen — the one with Overview, Genes, Health and the rest — and Gear is the second tab along. The H key is a different screen: that is the horse browser , your roster and reference, and it has no slots in it. Overview used to carry the two tack slots in a row under the stats. It now says how many pieces the horse is wearing and sends you here, because a row nineteen slots wide would have pushed the rest of Overview off the page. That line is the first thing on Overview, above the sex and the stats: what a horse has on is the one fact on that page that changes between two looks at it. The nineteen slots They are arranged on a small three-quarter view of the horse, in the place the gear actually goes: the near fore boot is under the near fore leg, the tail slot is behind the tail. You find a slot by looking at the horse rather than by reading a list. Zone Slots What goes there Head Headstall, Mask A bridle or a halter — one or the other, never both — and a fly mask or something with more in mind than flies. Mane and tail Mane, Tail Whatever is worked into the hair. Body Pad, Saddle, Barding, Blanket The pad under the saddle, the saddle, armour, and a rug or sheet for a horse that is not being ridden. Chest Chest A martingale or a collar, across the chest. Legs Boot × 4 One per leg. Hooves Hoof equipment × 4 Four independent hoof slots. See the horse-shoe page for the first planned occupant. Storage Bag × 2 Saddlebags, near side and off side. A bridle and a halter share one slot on purpose They are genuinely different pieces — a haltered horse can be led and lunged, a bridled one can be steered, and neither substitutes for the other — but a horse cannot wear both at once , so they are two items competing for one slot rather than two slots. This reverses an earlier plan that gave them a slot each; the distinction survives intact, it just lives in the items. What having a halter lets you do is ground work , still on the main roadmap because it is a riding mechanic rather than a piece of gear. Four legs and four hooves, not a set of four A horse can wear unevenly, and a roster that could only say “hoof equipment: yes” could never say which hoof is occupied. Eight leg and hoof slots is the price of being able to. Putting something on One click is a swap with your hand. Click a slot to put on what you are holding, or to take off what is there. There is no dragging: this is a plain screen with no container behind it, so there is no carried stack to drag with. Nothing is ever deleted. What comes off goes to your inventory, or on the floor at your feet if there is no room. Your horse, and within eight blocks. The screen opens on any horse — a stranger’s, a cowboy’s string — so you can see what one is wearing and no more. A slot you cannot use is drawn dim rather than hidden, because a gap where a slot should be reads as a bug and a greyed slot reads as the rule it is. A foal wears nothing. Every slot is closed until it grows up. Tack sized for an adult is the one thing a growing horse should not be carrying. Seventeen of the nineteen are empty by design Only the saddle and the barding have anything in the world that fits them today. The other seventeen accept nothing — not because they are broken, but because the gear that fills them has not been made. The tab says so on screen, so it reads as unfinished rather than faulty. Each piece joins its slot as it is made, and nothing on this page needs changing when one does. The Roadmap tab is what is coming. Gear does something, or it is not made Cosmetic-only equipment is the thing this mod does not add. Breeding is the one place pure aesthetics are allowed; everything else must provide utility, so tack, barding and hoof equipment carry real effects. That includes the mane and tail slots: whatever eventually goes in them has to do something, not merely look like a braid. The rule is philosophy § utility, not decoration , and it reversed an earlier “tack gets no stats” call — see design decisions .
