Markings — the star and the sock
(Natural gene / phase 1)
The ordinary white a horse can have without carrying any pattern gene at all: a star, a stripe, a snip, a coronet, a sock, a stocking. Most marked horses in life are marked by this and not by splash or sabino, and until now the mod had no way to say so.

Gene key horsegenetics.markings Priority 65 — before every white pattern locus Alleles M2 M1 m Outcomes wild, minimal-markings , markings , bold-markings , broad-markings Coat effect whitens the face marking and the lower legs Default allele m Founder draws 0 — every decision is stored epigenetics It consumes no randomness at paint time Unlike sooty , which spends a nextFloat() on its expression roll, every choice this gene makes is already in the epigenome : the face seed and its star, stripe and snip rolls come from the shared WhitePattern.faceSchema() , and each leg has its own EpiValue.perLeg entry. The horse therefore marks identically every time it is drawn, and a foal inherits the tendency rather than re-rolling from scratch. The face vocabulary is shared, deliberately The blaze is drawn by WhitePattern.faceMarking — the same call EDNRB , KIT , MITF and PAX3 use. A markings blaze and a sabino blaze are therefore the same code with a different jag , and cannot drift apart. This locus passes a small jag, because ordinary markings have the clean edge of a splash rather than the torn, roaned margin of a sabino. One locus is not what “polygenic” means Real ordinary markings are many small-effect variants plus developmental noise. This is a single additive locus standing in for that family, in the same way sooty and shade stand in for a chromosome-22 signal whose causal mutation nobody has identified. What the locus buys over an allele-free per-horse roll is that a breeder can select for it: the dosage is inherited Mendelianly, shows in the genotype code, and prints on a paper.
