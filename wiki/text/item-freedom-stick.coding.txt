Freedom stick
(Item / husbandry)
Give a horse back. Used on your own horse in the horse realm , it heals it, unclaims it, and leaves it standing there with its name, its genes and its whole pedigree intact for whoever tames it next.

Where it lives server/FreedomStickHandler , a PlayerInteractEvent.EntityInteract subscriber rather than an Item.interactLivingEntity override — the house rule, because vanilla turns any item used on a tamed horse into a mount, so the event has to be cancelled on both sides with the work done server-only. The item itself is one line in ModItems : a plain durable Item , like the vet’s kit . Un-taming is three calls, and the third is the one that matters setTamed(false) , setOwner(null) , setTemper(0) . The temper reset is what makes “tame it again” mean anything. Vanilla’s RunAroundLikeCrazyGoal rolls nextInt(maxTemper) < temper , and a horse that was tame is sitting at maximum — so without it the first player to sit on the horse tames it on the first roll and the release is a formality nobody would notice was broken. The saddle has to come off for a structural reason rather than a generous one: AbstractHorse.canUseSlot(SADDLE) asks isTamed() , so a saddle left on a horse that has gone wild is a saddle quietly destroyed. Re-taming needed no code Nothing on HorseRecord or in HorseAncestryData is derived from ownership, so releasing touches none of it and there is nothing to put back. HorseOwnerTrackingHandler reconciles the record’s owner mirror every forty ticks and accepts null — its javadoc already listed “a horse going wild again” as a case it handles, long before one existed. When somebody tames it, tameWithName sets the owner and the same tracker mirrors it back. tamedBy is deliberately not cleared. It records who first got a rope on this animal, it is written once, and a sale has never rewritten it either ( transfer papers ). The cost is that the re-tamer is not credited in attribution() ; the alternative was losing a fact about the horse’s history to make a nicer line of text, which is the wrong trade on a page whose entire subject is that the history survives. The wild-spawn mark A release sets BreedSpawnHandler.WILD_SPAWN_KEY on the horse so the herd systems will look at it. That is not sufficient on its own — HorseFoundingTickHandler strips the mark from any horse that loads carrying a record, which a released horse always does. The band actually comes from server/HorseRealmHerds ; see the realm’s note on it .
