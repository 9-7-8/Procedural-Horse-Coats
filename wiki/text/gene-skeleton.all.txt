Skeleton
(Magical gene / recessive / one of the two undeath loci)
Two copies and the horse is its own bones. Everything that is not skeleton is cut away, the bone shows through the horse’s coat, and whatever colour the horse carries is the colour of its bones. It never drowns, and it counts as undead . One copy is a carrier that looks and acts like any other horse. No wild horse has it: every one comes from a vanilla skeleton horse that converted into a Great Valley Skeleton Horse , or from breeding them.
