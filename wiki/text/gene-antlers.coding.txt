Antlers
(Magical gene / dominant, sex-limited allele / grows geometry, paints nothing)
One copy and the horse grows a rack of antlers from the top of its skull — on a mare or a stallion with Ant , on a stallion only with Antm . No two racks are alike: how big, how many tines, how far they reach, how thick and what shade of bone are all written on the allele and inherited with it. The second gene in the mod, after the unicorn horn , that grows a piece of the horse rather than colouring it.

It can, with nothing new: EpiValue.uniform(asymmetry, 0, 0).clampedTo(0, 1) . A zero-wide design range means EpiRoll rolls exactly 0 for every founder and EpiDrift 's step, a fraction of the design span, is exactly 0 — so drift never moves it. A breed band is stamped through the hard clamp ( BreedFounder.stampBands ), which is 0 to 1, so a breed can still write any value. AntlersGeneTest holds both halves. The one tool that divided by the design span — the breed designer 's slider — now runs such a value's slider over the clamp instead. How a rack reaches the screen GrownParts.of — the one door the client uses — asks racksFor(genotype, epigenome, form) with the habit antler form picked, gets two AttachedPart s, right then left, and dresses each with the three modifier loci. Everything else is the horn's machinery, unchanged in shape: One mesh per side. Two PartKind s, ANTLER_RIGHT and ANTLER_LEFT , rooted at the CROWN_RIGHT / CROWN_LEFT anchors just inside vanilla's ears. A mirrored copy by negative scale would turn boxes inside out under a culled render type, and a symmetric rack is just the same variant asked of both kinds. Baked at the maximum, shown by count. AntlerGenerator builds every tine the size class can hold, numbered base to tip in PartNode.group . PartModel.setupAnim hides the groups past shown and scales the fractional one, per submit — the deferred-submit rule the horn's two-tone slices already obey. ModelPart.xScale/yScale/zScale exist in 26.1.2 (read in the sources), which the treatment had left unverified. A region mask per pass. The solid bone, a crystal antler's see-through shafts, the bloom clumps and the glowing points are each one submit of the same mesh drawing only its own PartSheet regions ( BONE , BONE_TIP , BLOOM ). No foal. PartKind.showsOnFoal() is false for both antler kinds, and the layer skips them on a baby before anchoring. What the owner decided before it was built Put to the owner on the intake treatment and answered there, 2026-09-30; built as decided. D1 — both alleles. Ant on both sexes and Antm on stallions only. D2 — procedural. Racks come from AntlerGenerator , not hand-modelled meshes. D3 — symmetric by default. Asymmetry is an epigenetic number that founders roll as zero and drift never moves; only a breed can set it. Antler form is a plain dominance series, never codominant, and there are no per-parent-copy sides. D4 — permanent. No casting, no velvet, no year clock, no antler-cycle locus. D5 , a shed-antler item, is moot under it. D6 — crystal is a modifier. Crystal antlers change a rack; crystal growths on the body stay a separate part. D7 — rare and hand-seeded , like flying: the founder pairs are written out by hand. This is a Java gene, so the tools/founders.py skip list the treatment mentioned — for gene files — does not apply. D8 — invented names ; the science tab says the biology is analogous, not measured. D9 — size is one number on the allele, breedable and bandable, not a separate size locus. The three cosmetic modifiers kept: glow , crystal and bloom . A charge attack is out of scope ; nothing here assumes one. What it costs, and the cache bound An antler is at most AntlerGenerator.MAX_NODES boxes, asserted over every shape. Five habits × sixteen racks × five sizes × two sides is the whole set of antler meshes the client can ever bake, lazily, and PartGeneratorTest now bounds the cache by boxes over PartGenerators.allShapes() rather than by mesh count, since the antlers took that count past the horn-era limit of 256. A pen of antlered horses has not been measured. Settled 2026-10-01: magical, not natural The owner kept the antler loci magical , in the grown-parts family, as they shipped. The treatment had proposed natural, but no horse grows antlers, and a natural gene that paints nothing would have been filed under the disorders. The Greenwood's antlered mares ( Ant , both sexes, rather than the stallion-only Antm ) were confirmed the same day.
