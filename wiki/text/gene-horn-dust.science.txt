Horn dust
(Magical gene / recessive / sheds from the horn, paints nothing)
Two copies and a unicorn's horn sheds a fine dust that falls past its face, all the time, in the horn's own colours — a two-tone horn's dust fades from one of its colours to the other as it falls. On a horse without a horn this gene does nothing at all.

There is no real locus here A magical gene with no real counterpart. It asks the horn for its colour instead of having alleles of its own for it because the owner's brief was dust “that matches the horn's color”: a second colour system would be a second thing to keep in step, and would fail silently the first time the two disagreed.
