Potion milk
(Magical gene / no coat effect)
A mare who can be milked into a glass bottle rather than a bucket, and hands back a potion. One allele per effect. It does not touch the ordinary milk locus — a horse can have both, and which you get depends on what you are holding.

Gene key horsegenetics.potion_milk Alleles one per mob effect, plus n Inheritance incomplete dominance — one copy weak, two copies strong Reaches the game as a yield effect consuming minecraft:glass_bottle Yield kind potion — so milk volume governs it Coat effect none, ever Same-kind yields merge — built, and bottled A compound heterozygote carries two yield abilities, both triggered by a glass bottle, and a translator that fired only the first would hand back a single-effect potion while this page promises two. The merge is built : GeneYieldHandler collects every yield of one kind , de-duplicates by effect id, logs and skips an unrecognised one, and builds a single PotionContents . It is the one outcome here that can be half delivered without looking wrong, and a two-effect bottle has now been seen . Everything else already exists yield already takes consumes , so minecraft:glass_bottle is expressible today. minDose already gates the stronger half on two copies — its javadoc names incomplete dominance as the reason it exists. And declaring kind: potion hands governance to milk volume for free. The brew list is a divisor as well as a list The founder table is built as double each = WILD_CARRIER_PERCENT / brews.size() , with that same share given to every brew, so the length of the list is the wild rate of everything already in it . Appending is safe in the way passification 's offering table is not — nothing here is an index the epigenome stores, so no existing horse is rewritten — but it is not free. The two brews added on 2026-09-25 diluted every brew that was already there, without a line changing anywhere near them. Quote WILD_CARRIER_PERCENT and brews.size() if you need the number; do not write it down. Nothing here knows a curse from a blessing Wkn and Drk go through the same bottle(...) builder as every buff: the same WEAK_DURATION and STRONG_DURATION , the same minDose ladder, the same merge. So a matched Wkn/Wkn pair is a stronger, longer Weakness, and “the clearest reason to double a brew line” in the strong expression's own text is now written on two alleles where doubling is the opposite of a reward. That is the honest behaviour and it is deliberate — the gene has no opinion about which effects are nice — but it is the sort of sentence that reads as a bug when somebody meets it in play. Unknown effect ids must be skipped, not produced Effect ids are resolved against the live registry, following lycan 's rule that an id this game version has never heard of simply does nothing. An unrecognised id must make the yield not fire at all — producing a potion with no contents is worse than producing nothing, because it consumes the bottle. Why the compound gives both The alternative was lycan 's rule — a mismatched pair yields nothing — and it was considered and rejected here. Lycan needs mismatch to bite because a shifting horse is a whole animal and picking one of two shapes is incoherent. A potion is not a shape; two effects in one bottle is a thing that already exists in the game, so the honest reading of two half-doses is two half-doses. Two milk loci, deliberately This gene does not extend or override milk . They are different loci with different alleles and different containers, and a horse may express both. The container is the disambiguator, which is why the trigger carries the item rather than the gene carrying a mode. Verified The bottle: one potion per mare, both effects in a compound, one strong effect in a matched pair, the curses included, and every refusal says why (2026-09-30, the clockwork run in the debug test yard). DAIRY BY CLOCK, row AP west, holds a glass bottle out to six horses from a fake player that keeps every chat line, and each case logged CLOCKWORK PASS on every run from the second on: The merge. The Spd/Str mare filled one bottle holding minecraft:speed and minecraft:strength — one potion, both effects, at the weak grade. The de-duplication. The Spd/Spd mare filled one bottle holding a single minecraft:speed 2 — one entry at the stronger grade, not two stacked. The reading names the grade, not the duration. The curses. The Nightmare’s Wkn/Drk mare filled one bottle holding minecraft:weakness and minecraft:darkness , each at the weak grade — so the merge carries a curse like any buff, and minecraft:darkness resolves against the registry rather than being logged and skipped. The three refusals each say why, and keep the bottle. The hurt mare (health 11 of 22): “She's hurt, and won't give anything until she's healed.” The stallion: “The stallion rears and kicks you. Only a mare gives potion milk.” The foal: “A foal has no potion milk to give.” On the first run the hurt mare had healed from the pen’s water cauldron before her bottle came and gave a potion; with the pen dry ( DebugTestYard.dryPen ) every later run passed.
