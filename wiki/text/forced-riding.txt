Forced riding — the kelpie
(Planned / not built)
A wild horse that lets you mount, and then will not let you off. It carries the rider to deep water, and the rider’s only say in it is whatever warning they were given before they climbed on. It was asked for, and researched before it was deferred; the research went almost entirely into how to hold a rider, because the obvious way — cancelling the dismount key — is the way that does not port. None of it is built.
