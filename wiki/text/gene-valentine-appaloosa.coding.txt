The prose is the part a reader checks a bake against; the list underneath is what the engine actually did. Valentine Appaloosa The appaloosa arrangements, drawn with hearts instead of round spots. A white blanket lies over the hindquarter and hearts are cut out of it as holes, so the coat shows through each one; outside the blanket the same hearts are laid on the coat in white instead. Every heart stands upright with its point down. How much blanket there is varies a great deal - a scatter of hearts over the hip on one horse, a white sheet full of holes on the next.
