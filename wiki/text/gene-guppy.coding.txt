Guppy Colour runs the full length of the crest, divided across it into discrete upright bands - three to seven of them, irregular in width, and hard-edged where they meet, with no blending anywhere. Some bands are the hair's own colour or white instead. The forelock follows the same rule at a smaller count. The tail is different: there the colour lies in long streaks running with the fall of the hair rather than across it, over the upper part of it, interleaving where two hues meet into a mottled flame rather than a clean stripe. Both use the same set of hues. upright bands across the mane — STROKES , painted with PALETTE lengthwise streaks down the upper tail — STROKES × AXIS , painted with PALETTE Guppy carrier One copy shows nothing whatever.
