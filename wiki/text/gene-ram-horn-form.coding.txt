Ram horn form
(Magical gene / dominance series / shapes the ram horns, paints nothing)
The shape of a horned horse’s ram horns : curled, scimitar, corkscrew, four-horned, horizontal twist or scurs. One shape on both sides. On a polled horse it does nothing.

Gene key horsegenetics.ram_horn_form Class RamHornFormGene Priority 237 (magical band); only a code-order slot Family Grown parts Alleles Crl Crk Fhn Scr Hzt Sci — dominance Fhn > Crl > Sci > Crk > Hzt > Scr ; Crl is the default Founders Hardy–Weinberg over Crl 0.60, Crk 0.12, Scr 0.10, Fhn 0.08, Sci 0.06, Hzt 0.04 Coat effect none Allele order is generator order Crl, Crk, Fhn, Scr, Hzt, Sci is the declaration order, the stored order, and RamHornGenerator ’s shape indices, so shapeOf(pair) is the dominant allele’s order() and the first factor of the mesh style. Dominance is its own list. Append, never reorder: Hzt and then Sci came later and went on the end, so no saved horse changed shape, and RamHornsGeneTest pins the order. The horizontal twist is a flat corkscrew laid level Hzt (2026-10-09, #125) is RamHornGenerator.HORIZONTAL : the corkscrew’s chain — one unbranched run of boxes that roll and never bend — with its root leaned about 83° off upright, a little back, and every box flat ( PartNode.width at 0.6 of its girth), so the roll reads as a turning ribbon. The curl bucket picks the roll per segment (8° to 26°, shallower than a corkscrew’s), and the horn is 0.8 of its class length where a corkscrew is 0.9. A horn is at most twelve boxes against MAX_NODES . It took 0.04 of the founder table from Crl . Neither ram-horned breed’s pool names it, so RamHornBreedsTest checks the four shapes the pools do name. It cost the box budget one raise. The scimitar is a curl that stops early Sci (2026-10-09, #130) is RamHornGenerator.SCIMITAR : the curl’s chain — one unbranched run of boxes that bend backward and never roll — with a fraction of its bend. The root leans about 29° back and 16° out; the curl bucket is the whole sweep, shared out over the segments (35°, 55°, 78° or 100°, where a curl turns 200° to 390°); the outward lean per segment is a fifth of a curl’s, so the two blades stay nearly parallel; and every box is narrower across than it is deep ( PartNode.width at 0.7 of its girth), a goat’s horn in section. It is 1.05 of its class length, the longest reach of the six. A horn is at most twelve boxes against MAX_NODES , which settles the treatment’s question whether a blade fits. It has no ridges of its own beyond the horn region’s texture. It took 0.06 of the founder table from Crl . Its dominance slot, under curled and over corkscrew, is the treatment’s recommendation, taken with Hzt left where it was. No breed pool names it yet; the goat breeds that pin it are later pieces (#131 on). It fits inside the box budget ’s raise. One root for four horns A part mesh is one tree with one root ( PartGeneratorTest ), so the four-horned shape grows its curled horn from the base of the upright one ( t = 0 ), not as a second root. The dominance order — four-horned on top, scurs at the bottom — was this build’s call: real polycerate sheep carry a dominant trait, and scurs as the recessive makes a scurred line something you breed for.
