Ram horn form
(Magical gene / dominance series / shapes the ram horns, paints nothing)
The shape of a horned horse’s ram horns : curled, corkscrew, four-horned or scurs. One shape on both sides. On a polled horse it does nothing.

Gene key horsegenetics.ram_horn_form Class RamHornFormGene Priority 237 (magical band); only a code-order slot Family Grown parts Alleles Crl Crk Fhn Scr — dominance Fhn > Crl > Crk > Scr ; Crl is the default Founders Hardy–Weinberg over Crl 0.70, Crk 0.12, Scr 0.10, Fhn 0.08 Coat effect none Allele order is generator order Crl, Crk, Fhn, Scr is the declaration order, the stored order, and RamHornGenerator ’s shape indices, so shapeOf(pair) is the dominant allele’s order() and the first factor of the mesh style. Dominance is its own list. Append, never reorder. One root for four horns A part mesh is one tree with one root ( PartGeneratorTest ), so the four-horned shape grows its curled horn from the base of the upright one ( t = 0 ), not as a second root. The dominance order — four-horned on top, scurs at the bottom — was this build’s call: real polycerate sheep carry a dominant trait, and scurs as the recessive makes a scurred line something you breed for.
