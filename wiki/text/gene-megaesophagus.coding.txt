Megaesophagus
(Natural gene / health)
A slack gullet that will not push milk down. The foal chokes on what it swallows and inhales the rest.

Gene key horsegenetics.megaesophagus Priority 101 Alleles meg N Outcomes wild, megaesophagus-carrier , megaesophagus Coat effect none — every outcome is a wild type Default allele N Wild population 1.7% of founders carry one copy; none carries two Founder draws 1 × nextFloat() Catalogue entries 1 — it never changes what a horse looks like It paints nothing, and that is what makes it free Every combination of this gene is an expression marked wildType . That reads oddly for a gene that can kill a foal, and it is exactly right: wildType means changes nothing about the coat . Because none of the non-coat genes paints, Gene.affectsCoat() is false for all of them, they are left out of a horse’s texture key, and the whole locus collapses into one catalogue entry. What the gene actually does travels on the trait system instead.
