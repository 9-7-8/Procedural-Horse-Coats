RYR1 (malignant hyperthermia)
(Natural gene / health)
The only disorder in this mod that costs an affected horse nothing. One copy is enough to have it, and a horse that has it is a completely ordinary horse until something triggers an episode — and nothing in this game can.

Crossing two of them A disorder you can see, and mostly ignore Dominant , so there is no silent carrier: every horse with a copy shows it on its info panel and on its papers. Unlike HYPP and PSSM1 — the other two dominants — one copy takes no hearts, no speed and no jump. The horse is sound. That is not the mod being generous. Malignant hyperthermia is episodic : the fault only shows under general anaesthesia or extreme stress, and between episodes the animal is genuinely normal. There is no anaesthetic here and no stress model to hang a trigger on, so giving it a permanent heart penalty would invent a symptom the real condition does not have. Two copies is different. A horse with no normal channel left is genuinely compromised, and that combination does cost hearts, speed and jump. As with every dominant here, no wild horse is ever born with two — the worst outcome is always something a player bred. Why a wild horse can have this and a wild horse can never have PLOD1 A recessive disorder can be absent from its founder table entirely, because a wild-caught horse is an adult that survived. A dominant has nowhere to hide: if founders could never be affected, the allele could never enter the world and the locus would be dead code. So founders really can be born with one copy.
