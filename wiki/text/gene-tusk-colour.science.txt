Tusk colour
(Magical gene / codominant / colours the tusks, paints nothing)
What colour everything the tusks gene grows is: the narwhal horn, the boar tusks and the sabre fangs. The same colours and rules as horn colour (white, the rainbow, pink, black, grey or chaos), on a locus of its own. White is the wild type, and it reads as ivory. On a horse without tusks it does nothing.

Invented Real tusks are ivory: dentine, cream to yellowish, darkening with age. White here stands for that. The rainbow, the chaos and the two-tone are fantasy. It is a separate locus from every other part’s because the owner chose one colour gene per part, and one locus for all the tusks gene’s forms because the treatment did.
