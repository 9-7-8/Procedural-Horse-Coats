Dragon horns
(Magical gene / recessive / grows geometry, paints nothing)
Two copies and the horse grows a pair of horns behind its ears, swept back over the neck. Whether they curve, stand straight or curl, and how long, thick, swept and splayed they are, is written on the allele. Their colour is a gene of its own, dragon horn colour . The first of the head parts, and the fourth grown part after the unicorn horn , antlers and ram horns .

Gene key horsegenetics.dragon_horns Class DragonHornsGene Priority 239 (magical band), with its colour locus at 240; only a code-order slot Family Grown parts Alleles Drg n , recessive Founders Hardy–Weinberg at Drg 0.05 per allele — the unicorn’s figure Epigenome length (0–0.7, clamp 0–1), girth (0.80–1.25), form (one of three), sweep (0–1), splay (0–1) Parts DRAGON_HORN_RIGHT / LEFT at the NAPE_RIGHT / LEFT anchors, sheet HORN and HORN_TIP Coat effect none — every outcome is a wild type The ram horn pattern, with the unicorn’s inheritance Slice A of the head parts treatment: one part end to end, to settle the pattern the other five head parts reuse. Two kinds, one mesh per side (a mirrored copy by negative scale turns culled boxes inside out); a new pair of anchors, NAPE_RIGHT and NAPE_LEFT , with one offset per horse model in AttachedPartLayer.anchor ; DragonHornGenerator and DragonHornSize beside the ram horn’s; and one line in GrownParts.of , which dresses the pair with its colour locus . No new layer, render-state field or config key. The granting locus is in GrownParts.shapes ’s every-part baseline, so the editors see the colour locus as changing the look. The epigenome, by cost length : the class is the mesh’s size bucket (five; a longer horn is more segments), the remainder a uniform stretch, the ram horn’s rule. form , sweep and splay : all baked into the mesh — DragonHornGenerator.style(form, sweep, splay) , three forms by four sweep buckets by three splay buckets. Splay is baked rather than a transform because the layer’s only free rotation is the lean about x ; an outward angle on a pair needs a z rotation that differs by side, and adding one was not worth a new field for a number that drifts slowly. girth : a draw-time scale across the horn. Thirty-six styles by five classes by two sides is 360 meshes at most, each at most DragonHornGenerator.MAX_NODES boxes, inside PartGeneratorTest ’s box budget; baked lazily, so a world with no dragon horns bakes none. The nape anchor Head-local, on the adult: x 2.6 (just outside the ear, which spans x 0.55..2.55 ), y -9.5 , z 4.5 (half a unit inside the back of the skull). That keeps the root two units behind the antlers’ CROWN root and well behind the horn’s FOREHEAD . The foal’s offset exists because the switch must answer for every anchor, and is never drawn.
