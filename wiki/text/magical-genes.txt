Magical genes still to build
(Planned / not built)
The invented loci that have been asked for and not yet written. Three things sit here: more of the marking and “stamp” genes that already work, the flavour loci a breed designer could name in a JSON file to give a breed a character rather than a colour, and one gene that would read what the horse is wearing. None of it is built. The sections were moved here whole from the main roadmap , so that the list has a home of its own and can be added to without asking anybody’s permission.
