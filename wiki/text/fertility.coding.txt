Fertility & gestation
(Start here / breeding)
Mares come into heat, and breeding through this mod’s own tools makes a pregnancy . The foal arrives when it ends, not the moment the pair is bred. Plain golden carrots still give a foal at once, exactly as in vanilla. Fertility is inherited: the fertility gene decides how readily a horse breeds and how often a mare carries twins.

Takes dayTicks as a parameter so the testing tools can shrink everything at once. common/repro/Reproduction The attachment value: cycle phase, optional Pregnancy , last foaling tick, the foals being nursed, when they were last all away, and a stallion’s covers today. Holds no current state. common/repro/ReproRules stateAt(record, now, timing) , the odds, twins, early loss, the late slowdown, weaning. Golden-carrot breeding does not come through here at all and fertility does nothing to it — there was a vanillaFoalChance that halved its odds per sf/sf parent, and it is deleted. common/repro/NaturalCover decide(mare, record, now, timing, stallions, othersNearby) : every rule of a natural cover, as a verdict ( COVER , NOT_A_BREEDING_MARE , NOT_NOW , NO_STALLION , CROWDED ) plus which stallion. NaturalBreedingHandler only gathers the facts. common/repro/CoverNotice The owner-facing line for a cover that did not happen, and the quiet period that stops it repeating. A Reason per sentence: the cap, hurt, ridden, leashed, cowboy stock, and the three ways a cover itself ends. dueAgain always lets a changed reason through. common/repro/ReproText Every sentence: durations, refusals, the info screen's Breeding line, the vet's kit report. Tested, above all, so that twins show only through the kit. common/genetics/Conceivable A genotype a gene rules out ( canOccur ) is treated as an embryonic lethal on both breeding paths, with a synthetic cause. neoforge/data/ReproCodecs The save format of horsegenetics:horse_repro . Not copyOnDeath , not synced. neoforge/server/ReproHandler breed(...) , the entry point that folds armed carrots and calls Conception ; the 40-tick tick (early loss, birth, slowdown, weaning); the action-bar lines; breedingLine ; the clock’s debugStep . State is derived, never stepped stateAt returns the first that applies: PREGNANT (a pregnancy is stored), POSTPARTUM then FOAL_HEAT (measured from foaledTick ), then ESTRUS or DIESTRUS from floorMod(now + phase × cycle, cycle) . The phase is a fraction taken from the entity UUID by Reproduction.fresh , so a mare nobody has bred needs no attachment at all. ReproHandler.of deliberately does not attach a default. Nursing is a list beside the state, not a state. The hooks HorseBreedingHandler.onBabySpawn : same-sex pairings cancel, and nothing else does. A pregnant dam, a gelded sire, an armed parent and a subfertile pair all used to, and all four are gone: the foal goes straight through applyBredFoal , with any armed carrot’s GameteBias folded into the draw and spent on it. The one remaining cancel is the draw coming out an embryonic lethal. The patched Animal.spawnChildFromBreeding resets love and sets both 6000-tick cooldowns on a cancel — which no longer strands anyone, since BreedingCooldownHandler zeroes the cooldown on the next feed. StallionSeedJarHandler : filling counts a cover and moves the stallion’s armed carrots into the jar. Using it requires ReproHandler.receptive instead of vanilla love. NaturalBreedingHandler : every 40 ticks an adult mare asks ReproRules.mayTryNaturally (in heat, and her lastNaturalTry is before this heat began). She finds the best entire stallion within NATURAL_REACH — the nearest of those with covers still in hand, or the nearest of the tired if none has ( NaturalCover.better ) — checks the NaturalCover.Crowd she is in, stamps the try, and calls breed with her owner as bredBy . Before that, the same stallion must stay in reach for NaturalCover.COURTSHIP_TICKS (a transient Courtship per mare), exactly as vanilla's BreedGoal waits out loveTime >= 60 inside distanceToSqr < 9 . Like vanilla, there is no line of sight check; unlike vanilla, since 2026-09-25 a stallion she cannot walk to is dropped from the candidates ( canMeet , below ). Cowboy-branded horses are skipped. It is an entity tick, so only loaded chunks ever breed. tell turns the verdict into a CoverNotice.Reason for the mare’s owner, behind notices.owned_horse_breeding ; the ActionTrace lines beside it are unchanged and throttle on their own schedule, because they are the debug record and not owner-facing. The crowding cap is the world's, not a constant (2026-09-24). NaturalCover.Crowd is a pair — how many horses are around her, and how many this world allows — built in the one place that counts them and read by both decide and CoverNotice.line , so the sentence the owner reads always quotes the number that actually refused her. common/ cannot see a config, so the cap arrives as data: ServerConfig.nearbyHorseCap() fills it from fertility.nearby_horse_cap , and ReproRules.DEFAULT_NATURAL_CAP is only the default ( MAX_NATURAL_CAP bounds the option, since the same number sits inside a per-scan entity search). Nothing downstream may read the constant back: NaturalCoverTest.aWorldMayRaiseOrLowerTheCap refuses the same crowd under a cap of eight and covers under a high one. Gelding is HorseRecord.gelded , and HorseRecord.entire() is what every stallion check asks: the herd goals, rivalry, the jar and onBabySpawn . VetKitHandler gelds and examines ( ReproHandler.vetReport ). See gap 229 for what still reads sex alone. BreedingCooldownHandler is what makes "a golden carrot always works" true at the feed: it zeroes the vanilla breeding cooldown and puts an untamed adult in love itself, which vanilla’s handleEating will not do. It used to refuse love items to a pregnant mare and report the cooldown remaining; both are gone with the rules they described. The tamed-and-full-health half of the same rule is mixin/HorseAlwaysParentMixin . Birth is HorseBreedingHandler.bornFromPregnancy , which shares populateFoal with the instant foal. The breeder is looked up by name, and only gets checklist ticks and gene discovery if they are online. HerdGoals.HeatAttraction at priority 6. The info-screen line rides HorseSocialSyncPayload.breeding . Testing it With debug.tools on, ServerConfig.reproTiming() uses DEBUG_REPRO_DAY_TICKS for the day. A clock on an adult mare is ReproHandler.debugStep : out of heat to peak heat, pregnant to due now, postpartum to foal heat.
