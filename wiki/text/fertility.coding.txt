Fertility & gestation
(Start here / breeding)
Mares come into heat, and breeding through this mod’s own tools makes a pregnancy . The foal arrives when it ends, not the moment the pair is bred. Plain golden carrots still give a foal at once, exactly as in vanilla. Fertility is inherited: the fertility gene decides how readily a horse breeds and how often a mare carries twins.

Not copyOnDeath , not synced. neoforge/server/ReproHandler breed(...) , the entry point that folds armed carrots and calls Conception ; the 40-tick tick (early loss, birth, slowdown, weaning); the action-bar lines; breedingLine ; the clock’s debugStep . State is derived, never stepped stateAt returns the first that applies: PREGNANT (a pregnancy is stored), POSTPARTUM then FOAL_HEAT (measured from foaledTick ), then ESTRUS or DIESTRUS from floorMod(now + phase × cycle, cycle) . The phase is a fraction taken from the entity UUID by Reproduction.fresh , so a mare nobody has bred needs no attachment at all. ReproHandler.of deliberately does not attach a default. Nursing is a list beside the state, not a state. The hooks HorseBreedingHandler.onBabySpawn : a pregnant dam cancels the event. An armed parent sends the pair to ReproHandler.breed and cancels. The patched Animal.spawnChildFromBreeding resets love and sets both 6000-tick cooldowns on a cancel. Otherwise the instant foal goes through applyBredFoal , after one roll against vanillaFoalChance made only when it is below 1, so a wild-type pair draws exactly what it used to. StallionSeedJarHandler : filling counts a cover and moves the stallion’s armed carrots into the jar. Using it requires ReproHandler.receptive instead of vanilla love. NaturalBreedingHandler : every 40 ticks an adult mare asks ReproRules.mayTryNaturally (in heat, and her lastNaturalTry is before this heat began). She finds the nearest entire stallion within NATURAL_REACH who is under the cover limit, checks NATURAL_CAP , stamps the try, and calls breed with her owner as bredBy . Before that, the same stallion must stay in reach for NaturalCover.COURTSHIP_TICKS (a transient Courtship per mare), exactly as vanilla's BreedGoal waits out loveTime >= 60 inside distanceToSqr < 9 . Like vanilla, there is no line of sight check. Cowboy-branded horses are skipped. It is an entity tick, so only loaded chunks ever breed. tell turns the verdict into a CoverNotice.Reason for the mare’s owner, behind notices.owned_horse_breeding ; the ActionTrace lines beside it are unchanged and throttle on their own schedule, because they are the debug record and not owner-facing. Settled debug.tools shortens the reproductive day (the owner, 2026-09-13). With the testing tools on, ServerConfig.reproTiming() uses a one-minute day, so a heat and a pregnancy each last a minute. That is deliberate, including on a real server where the owner tests release builds with the switch on, where every mare runs about twenty times fast. It is the only thing behind that switch that changes how the game plays for everyone. A natural cover needs a healthy horse, not an untouched one (2026-09-16, closing gap 258). The rule was full health exactly — getHealth() >= getMaxHealth() — and that turned a single point of damage into permanent infertility, because healing is gated on standing near water ( HorseCareHandler.nearWater ): a horse in a dry pen never got the point back. A miscarriage costs the dam half a heart, so a mare who lost a pregnancy to a lethal pairing was never covered again . She went on cycling and reading In heat , stallions ignored her, and nothing was logged, because that verdict ( NOT_A_BREEDING_MARE ) was the one the handler passed over in silence. A wolf bite or a lost fight did the same to any horse. It is now a fraction, ReproRules.COVER_HEALTH , tested by ReproRulesTest at exactly the health a miscarriage leaves her; and the refusal now logs once a minute per mare, naming her health.
