Opalized
(Magical gene / colour modifiers)
The same substrate as Voided taken the other way - a marking comes out as iridescent cells of three to seven hues, pearlescent at the rim and strongest in the middle. Which marking depends on the allele: one opalizes every white region, the other every black one.

Crossing two of them Finding one About 1 in 1,786 wild horses is born showing this. That is the whole of the supply: catch one, or make one with the carrot below. Breeds set their own pools and a breed built around this gene will carry it far more often than the wild population does.
