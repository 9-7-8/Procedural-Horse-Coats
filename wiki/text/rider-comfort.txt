Rider comfort
(Planned / not built)
Five small things that make riding less irritating, borrowed from other mods that already do them: four are additions nobody would argue with, and one — fading the horse out as you look down — touches the coat itself and is the only one with a real unknown in it. None touches genetics, and each is small enough to ship alone. None of it is built. This page exists so that the plan has a home of its own rather than a section on a shared list, and so that when the first piece of it ships there is already a page to document it on.
