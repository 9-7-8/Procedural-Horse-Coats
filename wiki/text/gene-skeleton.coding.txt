Skeleton
(Magical gene / recessive / one of the two undeath loci)
Two copies and the horse is its own bones. Everything that is not skeleton is cut away, the bone shows through the horse’s coat, and whatever colour the horse carries is the colour of its bones. It never drowns, and it counts as undead . One copy is a carrier that looks and acts like any other horse. No wild horse has it: every one comes from a vanilla skeleton horse that converted into a Great Valley Skeleton Horse , or from breeding them.

Gene key horsegenetics.skeleton Class SkeletonGene extends AbstractUndeathGene , also AbilityContribution Priority 191 (magical coat band), beside zombie at 192 Alleles Skel , n (wild type, last) Outcomes skeleton (a marker: paints in no phase), a carrier and the wild type Founders n/n always — never in the wild, never spliced, no gene carrot Epigenome coat_opacity 0.55–0.65, shade_strength 0.80–0.90, averaged over both copies Effects traversal underwater_breathing , target self The sheet pass The gene implements CoatSheetContribution with pass SHAPE , which CoatTextureComposer.bake runs as step 6, after the overlay phase. For each texel, with S the converted skeleton sheet ( horse_skeleton.png / _baby , keyed skeleton_adult / skeleton_baby ) and C the finished coat: S’s alpha replaces C’s (binary, so the model’s own ENTITY_CUTOUT_NO_CULL discards the holes); the colour is lerp(S, C, coat_opacity × C.a) ; and it is multiplied by lerp(1, L, shade_strength) , L being S’s luminance over its brightest. The eye texels keep C. It runs after the overlay because the bleached strain is locked white by extreme white dominant , and the lock refuses every overlay write; the cut-outs are not a marking. A host that has not loaded the sheet (a test’s synthetic set) skips the pass and bakes the plain coat. Black eyes AbstractUndeathGene is an EyeRequestContribution : an expressing horse requests Blk at both irises and both sclerae and the wild sector at both eyes. The undeath loci sort after every white locus, so the request wins over a white’s blue eye or blue wedge (a skeleton with a hidden splash carrier would otherwise have one). Undead, for the rest of the mod common/genetics/Undeath.kindOf(Genotype) is the one answer; the game asks it through server/UndeadHorses (cached per genetic code), and MobGroups ’s undead group adds it to the entity-type tag. mixin/HorseUndeadVoiceMixin gives an expressing horse vanilla’s skeleton horse ambient, hurt and death sounds.
