Smoke Scroll
(Magical gene / ground and strong white)
Curling scrolls of smoke rise off the spine and pour down both flanks and all four legs, in black, grey and white.

The file common/src/main/resources/horsegenetics/genes/smoke_scroll.json — a data-driven gene , not a Java class. It is listed in the index.json beside it and registered by Genes ' class initialiser at startup. Key horsegenetics.smoke_scroll Phase magical — it adds signed colour in phase 3, after the pigment has resolved Priority 236 — where it sits in the one (priority, key) paint order Alleles Ssw · Ssk · n Varies per horse 3 values drawn once off the expressing allele copy, so a foal that inherits the copy inherits the look Built from AXIS, CENTERLINE, FRACTAL, SVG, TOWARD Why it is built this way Source: a traced cloud scroll (image_3_.svg, four paths). Only the black #010101 path is used, as the SVG mask on every scroll layer. The two grey paths in the trace are ragged dry-brush fragments, most of them smaller than a texel at this scale, so the grey and white smoke is drawn instead as that same outline stroked at two widths (MAX onto the fill, which dilates it) and torn with an FBM FRACTAL. That tearing is the smoke; the traced greys would have landed as single-pixel noise. The core went through fit-svg (5111 -> 700 points, tolerance 0.69 user units of a 1199-wide drawing). Its 'viewBox flipped' line reads 0% only because it samples the viewBox without the rotate transform - the tolerance is the real measure. Two copies of one drawing, both in the side plane so both flanks match. The fore copy is rotated 90 degrees so the billows sit on the barrel and the long trailing wisp runs down the front leg; the hind copy is its mirror (translate + scale(-1 1) ahead of the rotate), so the marking is symmetric fore-and-aft as well as left-right. Where a scroll crosses v 0.62 the side-plane extrusion carries it straight across the back. Those crossings are only at a few places, so a CENTERLINE dorsal line (x AXIS Y high on BODY, which keeps it off the belly) carries the smoke along the whole spine. That is three more layers rather than extra MAX terms, because a MAX term cannot be a product of two masks. Layer order matters: outer smoke, grey fringe, ink, each after the first with over: true so the tones stack instead of summing from the original coat. The grey fringe's FRACTAL is inverted with a high threshold so it is mostly solid with occasional breaks; the outer smoke's is not inverted and low-threshold so it is broken and wispy. billow is the dial: the width of the outer smoke round the scrolls, 1.4 to 2.6 body units. The grey fringe stays a fixed 0.9 so it never outgrows the smoke around it. No coloured form, deliberately: the brief was monochrome black, grey and white. The white form is the photographic negative of the ink form (white scrolls, silver fringe, charcoal wisps) rather than a same-order re-tone, because smoke with a pale heart and a dark edge reads as the same marking lit from inside - and it is the form that shows on black and dark bay horses, where the ink core disappears. The wisps down the legs are thin in the source; the stroke dilation is what keeps them to two or three texels wide rather than one.
