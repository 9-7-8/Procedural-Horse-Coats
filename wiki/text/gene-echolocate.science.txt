Echolocate
(Magical gene / no coat effect)
Every so often the horse makes a sound no horse should make, and for a moment you can see everything alive around you — through the walls, through the dark, out to a radius written on the allele copy.

A gene that changes what the player can see Almost every locus in the mod changes the horse. This one changes the rider’s information, which is a category the mod has barely touched and probably should — a horse bred for what it tells you is a different kind of animal from one bred for what it can do.
