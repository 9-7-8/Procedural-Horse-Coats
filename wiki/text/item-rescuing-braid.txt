Rescuing braid
(Planned / not built)
A one-use piece of mane or tail gear that turns a killing blow into a trip home instead of just a reprieve: the moment it would break, the horse (and its rider, if mounted) blinks back to a bound stall and comes up already fleeing. None of it is built. This page exists so the plan has its own home rather than a paragraph in the intake to-do list.
