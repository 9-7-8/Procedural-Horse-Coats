Saddles
(Horse gear / the saddle slot)
On a horse the vanilla saddle is the bridle , and it steers. The saddle is this mod's own item, worn in the Saddle slot beside it: it makes the ride. While a player is aboard, a saddle adds a share of the horse's own speed and jump, up to 10% at the top tier . It comes in every material a chestplate is made of (leather, copper, chainmail, iron, gold, diamond and netherite), and in every material another mod makes a chestplate of. Horse armour now follows the same rule, which adds chainmail horse armour .

Design reasoning The bonus is small and capped, so breeding stays what makes a fast horse (owner, 2026-10-09: 10% at the top tier, set in config). It is a share rather than a flat amount so a good saddle never matters more to a poor horse than to a good one. It applies only while a player rides, because the treatment says the saddle's stats belong to the ride and not to the horse, and the horse's screen leaves it out for the same reason. The materials are the chestplate's (owner, 2026-11-07) because a saddle is armour-adjacent leatherwork, and armour follows the same rule so the two never disagree about which metals exist. Gold sits above iron as it does for the shoes: the gold-fitted saddle is the show saddle.
