Flametouched
(Magical gene / fields and regions)
Fire on the horse - stylised flames licking up the legs and face with one copy, or a smooth ember gradient over the topline, face, hair and hooves with two. Never both on one horse.

The file common/src/main/resources/horsegenetics/genes/flametouched.json — a data-driven gene , not a Java class. It is listed in the index.json beside it and registered by Genes ' class initialiser at startup. It is the only form allowed to touch the topline. Up to half the horse.
