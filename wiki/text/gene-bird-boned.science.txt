Bird boned
(Magical gene / no coat effect)
Hollow-boned and light with it. Neither the horse nor its rider takes falling damage, from any height. The cheapest possible description of a gene that changes how you use the terrain.

A gene whose value is entirely in another locus On its own this is a safety net you rarely notice. Bred alongside magic jump , it turns a stat that mostly kills horses into one worth pushing. The mod has several of these — baiting beside health and fighting is the other clear one — and they are what make a stable a strategy rather than a collection.
