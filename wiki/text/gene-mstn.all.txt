MSTN (myostatin)
(Natural gene / performance)
The speed gene, and the first gene in this mod with no coat effect at all . Codominant: each C copy buys movement speed and pays for it in hearts, so the heterozygote is genuinely the midpoint of the two homozygotes rather than a copy of one of them.
