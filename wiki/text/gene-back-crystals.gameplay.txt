Crystal growths
(Magical gene / dominant / grows geometry, paints nothing)
One copy and the horse grows clusters of crystals out of its back along the spine, from the withers to the croup: see-through shafts with solid points, fanning out from the midline. Where every crystal stands is the horse’s own and is inherited whole. How big they are, how many clusters there are and how far they fan are written on the allele. Their colour is a gene of its own, crystal growth colour .

Crossing two of them One copy, either sex Dominant: Crg/n and Crg/Crg both grow crystals, so a crystal-grown horse bred to a plain one gives a crystal-grown foal at least half the time. It is not sex-limited. Foals grow none; the crystals come in when the horse grows up, and stay. Where they grow Up to six clusters in a row along the top of the back, from the withers to the croup, each of three to seven crystals: a tall one in the middle and shorter ones around it, leaning away from the spine. A saddle or a rider hides the clusters under the saddle and leaves the ones at the withers and the croup. Finding one Rare in the wild: about three horses in a thousand grow crystals, and because one copy is enough, every horse that carries the gene shows it. The skeleton horses can grow them too, as one of their horn-like parts. A crystal has no bone in it, so on a skeleton they are ordinary crystals in any colour. What varies Arrangement : where each crystal of each cluster stands, how many crystals a cluster has and how long each is against its cluster’s tallest. There are eight arrangements. A foal inherits its parent’s whole, and it only rarely changes in a line. Size : three classes, small , large and great . Small to large in the wild; a line bred for size reaches great and stops. Count : how many clusters show, from the withers back, two to six in the wild. A line can be bred down to a single cluster at the withers. Spread : how far the outer crystals of a cluster lean away from the spine, from nearly upright to a wide fan. Everything stacks: a horse can wear crystals, a sail, a row of dorsal spines, plates and every head part at once. They do not glow. Nothing makes them glow yet. Is it good for the horse? It does nothing at all, for good or ill: no stat, no ability, no hitbox. It is a look. Its gene carrot
