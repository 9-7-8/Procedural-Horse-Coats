Stall bars
(Planned / not built)
A waterloggable bars block that joins up along a stall front, in any wood the game has — the third block family in the mod to be authored for vanilla's woods and generated for everyone else's. None of it is built. This page exists so that the plan has a home of its own rather than a section on a shared list, and so that when the first piece of it ships there is already a page to document it on.
