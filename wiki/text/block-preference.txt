Block preference
(Planned / not built)
A horse that wants to be somewhere in particular — standing in water, standing in lava, submerged, or up a tree — and will walk itself there given the chance, as hard as its allele says and up to hurting itself when it cannot. It was asked for, and researched before it was deferred; what the research settled is that this is an AI goal and not one of the mod’s per-tick effects. None of it is built.
