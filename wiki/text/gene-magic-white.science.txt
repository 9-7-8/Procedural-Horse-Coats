Magic white
(Magical gene / phase 3)
Two copies paint the whole horse white , over whatever coat it really has. One copy shows nothing. The white is painted low in the magical order, so any marking the horse also carries still draws on top of it.

Is there a real recessive white? Not in horses. Every known white in the horse is dominant or incompletely dominant — the KIT dominant-white series, sabino, and the splashed whites — and the classic “two copies make it white” story belongs to lethal white overo, where two copies kill the foal. Recessive whites do exist in other animals: the white Leghorn chicken and some white cats and rabbits carry theirs as a recessive that switches pigment off. So this gene is honestly magical. It borrows the inheritance pattern of those other species, and it paints rather than removes pigment — which is why it works over any coat, and why the horse underneath is still there for its foals.
