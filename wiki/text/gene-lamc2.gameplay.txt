A recessive lethal in the draft breeds. The layer that anchors skin to the body never forms, so the two shear apart wherever the foal takes weight — and the hoof capsules come away with it.

Crossing two of them One of two JEB loci, and they are deliberately two LAMA3 is the other. They produce the same clinical picture by breaking the same structure in two different places, and in this mod they are separate genes rather than two alleles of one . That matters at the stud: a horse can carry one and be genuinely clear of the other, so a draft horse’s test result says nothing at all about a Saddlebred , and pairing a LAMC2 carrier with a LAMA3 carrier is completely safe. Folding them into one locus with two alleles would have made those two mutually exclusive — the ACAN shape, where any two broken copies leave no working one. That is right for ACAN and wrong here, because these are two genes. No founder is ever affected The founder table lists only the clear horse and the carrier. A wild-caught horse is an adult that survived, so it can carry this and never have it, and the homozygote’s weight is simply absent from the table.
