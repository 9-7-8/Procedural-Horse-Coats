Brow ridge
(Magical gene / dominant / grows geometry, paints nothing)
One copy and the horse grows a low wide ridge of bone across its brow, above and in front of the eyes. Plain, notched into three bumps or set with three short spines, and how tall and wide it is and how tall its bumps stand, is written on the allele. Its colour is a gene of its own, brow ridge colour .

Invented No horse has a bony brow ridge that stands off the skull. The look borrows from the brow bosses and horn cores of ceratopsians and pachycephalosaurs, the heavy brow of a gorilla, and the notched frontal crests of some chameleons. This is fantasy and makes no claim about any real gene. It is dominant (the treatment’s call), so it shows on every carrier.
