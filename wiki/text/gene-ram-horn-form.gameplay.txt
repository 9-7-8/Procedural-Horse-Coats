Ram horn form
(Magical gene / dominance series / shapes the ram horns, paints nothing)
The shape of a horned horse’s ram horns : curled, corkscrew, four-horned or scurs. One shape on both sides. On a polled horse it does nothing.

Crossing two of them The four shapes Allele Shape Looks like Fhn Four-horned A short upright pair and a curled pair sweeping down — a Jacob sheep Crl Curled Back, down past the ear and forward again — the ordinary ram’s horn Crk Corkscrew Long, straight and twisted, rising up and out — a Racka or a markhor Scr Scurs Loose stubs, the half-horned state Each hides the ones below it: four-horned shows over anything, curl over corkscrew and scurs. So scurs need two copies, and two curled horses can throw a scurred foal. Is it good for the horse? It does nothing to the horse at all, for good or ill — it only shapes horns it might have.
