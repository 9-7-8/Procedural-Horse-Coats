Ram horn form
(Magical gene / dominance series / shapes the ram horns, paints nothing)
The shape of a horned horse’s ram horns : curled, corkscrew, four-horned, horizontal twist or scurs. One shape on both sides. On a polled horse it does nothing.

Crossing two of them The five shapes Allele Shape Looks like Fhn Four-horned A short upright pair and a curled pair sweeping down — a Jacob sheep Crl Curled Back, down past the ear and forward again — the ordinary ram’s horn Crk Corkscrew Long, straight and twisted, rising up and out — a Racka or a markhor Hzt Horizontal twist Flat, wide horns straight out to the sides in a shallow twist — the old Egyptian ram, and Khnum Scr Scurs Loose stubs, the half-horned state Each hides the ones below it: four-horned shows over anything, curl over corkscrew, corkscrew over the horizontal twist, and everything over scurs. So scurs need two copies, and two curled horses can throw a scurred foal. The horizontal twist is the rarest shape in the wild and shows only beside another copy of itself or a scurs allele. How far it twists is the horn’s curl number, the one that says how far round a curled horn goes. Is it good for the horse?
