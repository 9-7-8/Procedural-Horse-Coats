Horn glow
(Magical gene / recessive / lights the horn, paints nothing)
Two copies and a unicorn's horn glows in the dark — in whatever colour horn colour gives it, both colours of a two-tone horn included. On a horse without a horn this gene does nothing at all.

Gene key horsegenetics.horn_glow Class HornGlowGene Priority 228 (magical band); only a code-order slot Family Grown parts , beside the unicorn horn Alleles Glw n Founders Glw/Glw 12.5%, n/n the rest — no wild carriers Coat effect none How it reaches the horn GrownParts.of asks HornGlowGene.glows(pair) and sets the part's emissive flag through AttachedPart.dressed . AttachedPartLayer then submits each slice a second time with entityTranslucentEmissive at full brightness and the same tint, so the glow is always the horn's own colour. It carries no epigenetic numbers. It replaced a one-in-eight glow category on the unicorn locus (2026-09-30), and the founder rate is set to that same one in eight, so the share of glowing wild unicorns did not change — what changed is that it can be bred for. A light horse's horn glows too (shipped 2026-10-02) The owner's call (2026-10-01): the horn follows the light locus, the way the cutie mark does. GrownParts.dressHorn sets the horn emissive when HornGlowGene.glows or LightGene.lightsHorse says so. lightsHorse is the one rule both share — any variant copy at the light locus, eyes-only Lteye included — and LightGene.modifyCutieMark now asks it too, so the mark and the horn cannot disagree. Only the glow moves; the horn's colours are still horn colour's. The light locus grows no horn of its own.
