Back sail colour
(Magical gene / codominant / colours the back sail, paints nothing)
What colour a horse’s back sail is: its membrane and its spines. The same colours and rules as horn colour (white, the rainbow, pink, black, grey or chaos), on a locus of its own. Two different copies split the sail rather than fading it. On a horse without a sail it does nothing.

Everything else is AbstractPartColourGene ’s. Its Words carry a split sentence, because the shared two-tone wording (“its tip the other”) is wrong for a part whose two colours are two regions rather than two ends. A two-tone sail costs nothing extra: the sail is already drawn in two passes, the membrane blended and the spines solid, and each pass takes one of the two colours ( translucent regions ). The bone version is boneOnly(pair) , read by GrownParts.of , which sets the sail’s opacity to 0.
