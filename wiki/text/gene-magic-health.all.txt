Magic health
(Magical gene / no coat effect)
The magical version of the hearts the natural loci trade around — MSTN spends them for speed, HMGA2 gives them for being small. One of the five magical body-stat genes — size , speed , health , jump and pull — and like the other three multipliers, most wild horses carry a copy , worth about a tenth either way. Two copies of the same kind add , and no wild horse is ever born with two. (Pull is the odd one out: an additive score rather than a multiplier, and with no vampiric allele.)
