Brindle (MBTPS2)
(Natural gene / X-linked)
Irregular white streaks , soft-edged and broken, running down from the topline and not matching from one side of the horse to the other . It is the mod's only sex-linked gene , and that is the point of it: it takes two copies to show, and a stallion has one X and so one copy — so every brindle horse is a mare , and every stallion who has it at all is a carrier. The trait runs visibly down one side of a pedigree in a way no autosomal gene can.

What it does What to look for: soft, broken white streaks running down from the back, and not matching on the two sides of the horse . That mismatch is the giveaway — no other pattern here does it. It is inherited differently from everything else. It rides on the X chromosome, and it takes two copies to show. A mare has two X chromosomes and can have both; a stallion has one, so he can never get there — only mares are ever brindle , and any stallion carrying it hides it completely.
