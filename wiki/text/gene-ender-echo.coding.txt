Ender echo
(Magical gene / no coat effect)
Shoot at it and it is not there any more. The horse blinks about eight blocks away from an incoming projectile, carrying whoever is riding it — which is either the best thing about it or the worst, depending on what is eight blocks away.

A server-side teleport must reach the riding client as a position it accepts, or the rider rubber-bands straight back and the gene appears to do nothing intermittently. A teleport verb that trusts its destination will eventually suffocate a horse inside a mountain with its owner on top. Failing to find a legal landing spot must mean no teleport this time . The eight-block cap is load-bearing Uncapped, this is free combat mobility and the best movement item in the mod attached to an animal. The range is a balance decision, not a placeholder. The trigger is shared on_hurt is a new entry in a closed set of four triggers. Guardian needs the closely-related on_owner_hurt , so the two should land together and the trigger machinery gets paid for twice. Why a trigger and not a condition Teleporting away from projectiles is an event , and the trigger set exists precisely so that event-driven effects do not have to be written as per-tick conditions that poll for something that already happened. The set is closed at four on purpose; adding to it is a real change and wants a second user, which is why this gene and guardian are scheduled together.
