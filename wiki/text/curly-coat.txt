Curly coat
(Planned / not built)
Two dominant variants curl the coat, mane and tail. KRT25 curls and thins the hair (hypotrichosis); SP6 curls it without thinning; KRT25 is epistatic to SP6 for the hair loss, so the plush full-coated curly is the SP6 -only horse. None of it is built — the coat pipeline has no axis for hair at all, which is why this page is a plan rather than a gene. It came off the roster audit on natural genes .
