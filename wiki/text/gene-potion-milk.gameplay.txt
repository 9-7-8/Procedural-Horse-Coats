Potion milk
(Magical gene / no coat effect)
A mare who can be milked into a glass bottle rather than a bucket, and hands back a potion. One allele per effect. It does not touch the ordinary milk locus — a horse can have both, and which you get depends on what you are holding.

Built, and not yet seen in game The gene, its inheritance and its founder table exist and are covered by the test suite; the translator that turns it into behaviour was written against the 26.1.2 sources and is flagged unverified in its own comments. Nothing on this page has been watched happening to a horse. What to look at, and what is most likely to be wrong, is on the Verification tab . What it does Right-click a tamed, grown mare with an empty glass bottle . A horse expressing this locus hands back a potion of whatever its alleles say. A bucket still gets you milk, if it has the milk gene; a bottle on a horse without this gene does nothing. Hold a bottle out to a stallion carrying it and he rears and kicks you, and tells you only a mare gives potion milk. A foal tells you it has nothing to give yet. Combination Outcome two copies of one effect that potion, at the stronger grade one copy of one effect that potion, weak two different effects both effects, both weak, in one bottle anything with an n the single effect, weak Two of the brews are not a gift Every allele at this locus used to hand back something a player wants — swiftness, strength, regeneration, and so on down the list. On 2026-09-25 Wkn (Weakness) and Drk (Darkness) joined it, and they are the first effects here that nobody would drink on purpose . They were added for the Nightmare , whose whole idea is a mare whose milk comes back cursed. Nothing about the horse, and nothing about the bottle, says which sort you are holding: a cursed potion looks exactly like a good one until you drink it. The Nightmare is also the first breed ever to pin this locus . Everything else that expresses potion milk does so because a wild carrier passed it on, and the pair is whatever breeding happened to produce; the Nightmare is Wkn/Drk on every founder. That is the compound heterozygote from the table above, so one bottle from a nightmare mare comes back carrying both Weakness and Darkness at the weak grade — the first compound anybody can brew where getting both is the punishment rather than the prize. An ordinary wild mare can hand you one The founder table spreads one fixed carrier share evenly across however many brews the gene lists, so adding two did not make wild potion mares any commoner — it made each particular brew rarer, and it put two effects nobody wants into the pool a wild mare draws from. A wild mare found in a meadow, with no connection to the Nether or to the Nightmare, can now milk out weak Weakness or weak Darkness. It is not a defect and it is not being fixed; it is written here because the horse gives no sign of it and the only way to find out is to drink. How often Governed by the same milk volume locus that governs everything else the horse can be milked for, without either gene knowing about the other. Health None to the horse. Like the plain milk gene, a mare has to be at her own full health: a hurt one refuses, and says she won’t give anything until she has healed (owner’s call, 2026-09-10 — before that, potion milk ignored health entirely).
