Antler glow
(Magical gene / recessive / lights the antlers' points, paints nothing)
Two copies and the points of a horse's antlers glow in the dark — every tine's tip and the end of each beam, in the antler's own colour. On a horse without antlers this gene does nothing at all.

There is no real locus here A magical gene with no real counterpart, and the horn glow locus's twin. Its own locus rather than an antler allele so that any rack, of any habit, size or colour, can also glow. Recessive, with no wild carriers, by the rule every magical trail follows: the allele is in the wild in the form that can show.
