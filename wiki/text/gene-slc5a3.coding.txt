SLC5A3 (foal immunodeficiency syndrome)
(Natural gene / health)
A recessive lethal that gives you nothing to see. The foal is born looking perfectly healthy, runs with its dam for weeks, and then fails as the antibodies it borrowed from her run out and its own never arrive.

That reads oddly for a gene that kills a foal, and it is exactly right: wildType means changes nothing about the coat . Because none of the non-coat genes paints, Gene.affectsCoat() is false for all of them and they are left out of a horse’s texture key. What the gene actually does travels on the trait system instead. The combination table combination outcome effect N/N wild type nothing fis/N slc5a3-carrier nothing — a completely normal horse fis/fis foal-immunodeficiency-syndrome −12 health, −0.04 speed, −0.15 jump — the foal is born and then dies Where the mod and the pathology disagree The real disorder kills over weeks , not seconds: an affected foal nurses, looks well, and declines as maternal antibody wanes. The mod has no age model and no immune model, so it uses the ordinary LETHAL_AT_BIRTH path and the foal dies shortly after being born. That is a real simplification and it loses the cruellest part of the condition — that the foal appears healthy long enough to be named and hoped for. Flagged here rather than hidden.
