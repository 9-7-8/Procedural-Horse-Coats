Flying
(Magical gene / magical coat genes)
A horse that leaves the ground, two different ways. Gliding is the dominant one and the one you will actually meet: ride it off a ledge and it soars on momentum like an elytra, trading height for speed and speed for height. True flight - going wherever it is pointed, with no regard for physics at all - is recessive and takes two copies, so it hides in horses that glide or in horses that do nothing. The gene paints nothing: a flying horse looks exactly like any other horse until it leaves the ground.
