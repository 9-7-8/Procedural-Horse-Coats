Brow ridge colour
(Magical gene / codominant / colours the brow ridge, paints nothing)
What colour a horse’s brow ridge is. The same colours and rules as horn colour (white, the rainbow, pink, black, grey or chaos), on a locus of its own. Two different copies make the bar one colour and what stands on it the other. On a horse without a brow ridge it does nothing.

Everything else is AbstractPartColourGene ’s. Its Words carry a split sentence (“the bar is one, the base, and its step, bumps or spine points the other”). The split is the mesh’s shape: the bar is the root, at 0 on PartModel.along ; a step or a bump hangs straight off it with nothing below, at 1. A spine is two boxes, so its lower box sits halfway and is drawn as a blend of the two colours, with the point the tip colour. BrowRidgeGeneTest holds that every last box is on the tip region.
