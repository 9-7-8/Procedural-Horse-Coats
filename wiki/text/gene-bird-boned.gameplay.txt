Bird boned
(Magical gene / no coat effect)
Hollow-boned and light with it. Neither the horse nor its rider takes falling damage, from any height. The cheapest possible description of a gene that changes how you use the terrain.

Half seen: the expressing horse, not the carrier A Brd/Brd horse and its rider surviving a fall was owner-confirmed in the 2026-09-10 sessions . What has not been watched is the other half of the claim — that a carrier falls exactly as any horse does, and that no wild horse is ever bird boned. Those are on the Verification tab . What it does Combination Outcome Brd/Brd no fall damage, horse or rider, at any height anything else nothing What it changes Mountains stop having a wrong side. A ravine becomes a shortcut. It pairs obviously with magic jump , and the pairing is the point — jump is dangerous alone, and this makes the danger go away. Health Pure benefit, with one caveat worth knowing: it removes fall damage, not falling. A horse that falls into a ravine is unhurt and still in a ravine.
