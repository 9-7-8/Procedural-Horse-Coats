Speckling
(Magical gene / speckle and dust)
Fine coloured speckles scattered over the coat and hooves to match - one colour with a single copy, two with a pair. Half a dozen other markings are described in terms of it.

The file common/src/main/resources/horsegenetics/genes/speckling.json — a data-driven gene , not a Java class. It is listed in the index.json beside it and registered by Genes ' class initialiser at startup.
