Magic swim speed
(Magical gene / no coat effect)
How fast the horse moves in water , and nothing else. It is unrelated to magic speed — not related-but-separate, unrelated — so a horse can be the slowest thing in the paddock and still cross a river faster than a boat.

What ships: the Java-only ability GeneAbility.Swim(factor) and mixin/HorseSwimMixin , which multiplies the push strength vanilla's LivingEntity.travelInWater hands to moveRelative . A horse that is not pushing is not moved. Vanilla runs that method on whichever side moves the horse, so a ridden horse is scaled on its rider's client, where the factor comes from ClientSwimHandler and the client record cache. Evidence, the 10:19 run: the SWIM SPEED pen's three horses swim eight five-second laps of walled channels, steered by their move control. Stne/Stne (factor 0.62) covered 1.83 blocks and plain covered 2.90, a ratio of 0.63 - proportional. Otr/Otr (factor 1.56) covered 21.87 blocks, about 7.5 times plain. The Otter overshoot is most likely because a horse steered by its AI pushes so weakly that a plain one barely gets going (0.37 blocks in five seconds), while the Otter's extra push gets it swimming. That is an inference, not measured. How it feels under a rider, whose push is far larger, is a tester's check ( for testers ). Not a body stat, and deliberately so The four magical body-stat genes push their sum into TraitBuilder , and end up as one of the four numbers on Traits . This gene does not, and neither does water breathing . Traits is a pure function of the genotype — four numbers and a list of conditions, re-derived wherever they are needed, stored nowhere, and meant to survive a version port unchanged. Swimming speed is not among those four and should not become the fifth: it means nothing without a running game, which is precisely the line GeneAbility exists on. Adding a field to Traits for every magical knob would drag Minecraft-shaped concepts into the one record that is supposed to be portable. So the genetics are shared and the sink is not. What it has is water_movement_efficiency — the share of its land speed a mob keeps in water, which is Depth Strider’s attribute and sounds like exactly what “how fast does this horse swim” means. It is not , on a horse: its base is 0, so multiplying it multiplies nothing, which is why this gene no longer uses it ( Verified ). The effect format used to offer swim_speed in its attribute list. It was never translated to anything, because there was nothing to translate it to; the list now names the attributes that exist, and the translator’s table is the same set. That table is closed on purpose, so a gene naming something this build has never heard of gets one log line rather than a silent no-op. Taking the modifier off again This is how the attribute path works generally — it is no longer this gene’s path, but it is the machinery every attribute gene beside it uses, and the removal half is the part that is easy to get wrong. The modifier is transient and its id is derived from the gene and the attribute, so re-applying it every tick is idempotent. The removal half is the interesting one: when an ability’s condition goes false it is skipped before it reaches the apply path, so nothing would ever take the modifier off. GeneAbilityHandler.clearAttributes therefore runs unconditionally after the ability loop and strips every modifier in the mod’s namespace that the horse is not currently asking for.
