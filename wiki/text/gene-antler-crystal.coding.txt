Crystal antlers
(Magical gene / recessive / turns the antlers to crystal, paints nothing)
Two copies and a horse's antlers are crystal: the beams and tines see-through in a gem colour, the points solid. Which gem is inherited. On a horse without antlers this gene does nothing at all.

Gene key horsegenetics.antler_crystal Class AntlerCrystalGene Priority 233 (magical band); only a code-order slot Family Grown parts Alleles Cry n Founders Cry/Cry 5%, n/n the rest — no wild carriers Epigenome gem — a category over AntlerCrystalGene.GEMS ; append only Coat effect none How it reaches the rack GrownParts.of retints the antler with the gem colour and sets its translucent flag. The layer then draws it as two submits: the BONE region through entityTranslucent at AttachedPart.CRYSTAL_ALPHA , and everything else solid — the points ( BONE_TIP ) through the ordinary cutout pass. That is the split the treatment asked for (Attached model parts 12.5): the blended, sorted pass is the one that costs, so it draws the least it can. The "which translucent render type is a layer offered" question it left open has an answer — RenderTypes.entityTranslucent , the same one a fading ridden horse's horn already uses.
