Skittishness
(Magical gene / no coat effect)
What a horse runs from, and when. The mirror of aggression , allele for allele — and being ridden makes no difference to it at all.

Gene key horsegenetics.skittish Alleles the same grid as aggression , plus n Inheritance homozygous recessive, and recessive to the other variants Reaches the game as a temper effect with "mood": "flee" Coat effect none, ever Fleeing was once implemented somewhere else entirely Until 2026-09-17 the temper verb returned early on any mood that was not aggressive , and fleeing was implemented in NightBehaviourHandler against the night verb instead. When this locus moved onto the shared verb the flee path had to move with it, into GeneAbilityHandler.flee . Had it not, this gene would have loaded, registered, baked, appeared in the census and done nothing whatsoever — which is the failure mode this codebase keeps paying for. If you add a mood, follow it all the way into the translator. “Herds” must not include the horse's own band passive excludes every equine in MobGroups . It is the whole of gap 241 below, and the exclusion moved from the night handler's private matcher into the shared group resolver when this locus was built — where every caller gets it rather than one. There is no vanilla avoid-goal to borrow Fleeing is a navigation call rather than a goal: the horse paths to a point FLEE_DISTANCE directly away from the nearest match and drops any target it had. Log whether the path was accepted. From outside, a flee that never fired and a flee that fired into a fence four blocks away look exactly alike, and Navigation.moveTo 's return is the only thing that tells them apart.
