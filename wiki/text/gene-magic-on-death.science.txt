Magic on death
(Magical gene / no coat effect)
What happens to the world where the horse died — a lava source or a water source. It says nothing at all about items; that is magic item drop , and the two are kept apart on purpose.

The exception that proves the founder rule The standing rule for a magical locus with an invisible carrier is that the wild population sits on the combinations that show . The argument is a good one: once a carrier is invisible, a founder population full of carriers is a locus run in the dark — you cannot see what a horse has, so you cannot choose what to pair it with, and every result is an accident. That argument assumes the outcome is something you would want to find. Here it is not. A wild population of horses that flood or light the ground they die on would mean a player who has never heard of this gene loses something to it, and the discovery would be an accident in the bad direction. So this locus runs the health loci’s rule instead — carriers only, never affected — and the interesting horse is one you assembled from two ordinary-looking parents. The useful one Water is on the same locus as lava, which makes the whole thing a gamble worth taking: two recessives sharing a locus means a breeder chasing an infinite water source is also breeding, in the same herd, the thing that burns the barn down. Why the third one went The explosion was the strongest version of that gamble and it was cut anyway (2026-09-24), which is worth being honest about: it was not cut for being broken, or unbalanced, or hard to breed. It was cut because of where the mod is actually played . On a server with other people’s builds in blast range, a hazard whose carrier is invisible stops being a gamble the breeder took and becomes one their neighbour took without being asked. Lava is the same shape of hazard held to one block, and one block is the difference. The general rule it leaves behind: a magical locus may cost the horse anything, and may cost the breeder whatever they walked into, but an outcome that reaches somebody who never touched the gene has to be small enough to stand next to.
