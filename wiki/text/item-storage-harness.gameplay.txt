Storage harness
(Gameplay / non-genetic)
The strap over a horse's rump and the metal frame on each flank that a chest hangs in. A horse wears one before it can carry a chest at all , and it takes a little of the load off. It comes with copper, iron, golden or netherite fittings — the better the metal, the more it helps — and its leather takes dye. Built on 2026-10-08 and not yet played; its enchantments are not built, and which ones it gets is still to be decided.

What it is A piece of gear with a slot of its own, Harness , beside the two Pack slots on the Gear tab. Hold one and right-click your horse and it goes on; or put it in its slot on the Gear tab or in the Dress window. An adult horse of your own, like every other piece. The harness comes first, and leaves last No harness, no chest. The Pack slots refuse everything on a horse that is not wearing one, and a right-click holding a chest says so rather than putting you in the saddle. It does not come off while a chest hangs from it. Take both chests off, then the harness. A server can drop the rule with packs.harness_required ; a harness is then only its weight bonus. The four metals What a horse carries slows it down , by how many items there are against how strong it is. A harness makes the load count for a little less. Harness Fittings The load weighs, by default Copper 2 copper ingots 2.5% less Iron 2 iron ingots 5% less Golden 2 gold ingots 7.5% less Netherite 1 netherite ingot 10% less Every one is crafted the same way otherwise: two leather and a horse hair with the metal, in any arrangement. There is no upgrading one into the next. Small on purpose How much a horse can carry is its pulling ability, and pulling ability is something you breed for. A tenth off, at best, is a harness that helps a good horse and cannot stand in for one. A server that would rather gear mattered more than bloodlines raises one number, packs.harness_best_reduction on the config page , and all four tiers move with it: the netherite harness takes that much off, and the other three are even steps below. The item's tooltip shows the figure your server uses. Dyeing it The leather dyes like leather armour — in the crafting grid with any dye, or several mixed. The fittings keep their metal's colour. On the horse the strap is the colour you dyed it. What it looks like on the horse A strap over the rump, a second running back along the spine to the tail, and on each flank a frame with a shelf under it. A chest sits in its frame; with no chest the frame is empty.
