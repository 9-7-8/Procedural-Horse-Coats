Magic heat tolerance
(Magical gene / no coat effect)
Better or worse in a hot biome. A tolerant allele, a sensitive allele and a neutral wild type; how much each copy is worth, and whether it goes to speed, jump or both, is written on the copy. Its twin is magic cold tolerance .
