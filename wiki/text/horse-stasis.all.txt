Horse stasis: chambers and the bank
(Gameplay / partly built)
A cheap way to hold horses without them costing server tick time. A stasis chamber is an item a horse goes into; a Horse Stasis Bank is a block that holds many chambers and lets you read the horses inside them. The whole point is the cheapest tier, so a server with hundreds of horses can put most of them somewhere that costs nothing, instead of every horse being a real, ticking entity forever. All four chambers are built and the bank does everything they buy — it keeps them, reads them on its Browse tab, mends them from its feed and water, collects what they drop, and breeds a pair you turn out at stud. What is still planned is the self-triggering Emergency chamber.
