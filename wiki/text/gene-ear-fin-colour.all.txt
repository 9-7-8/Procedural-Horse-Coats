Ear fin colour
(Magical gene / codominant / colours the ear fins, paints nothing)
What colour a horse’s ear fins are. The same colours and rules as horn colour (white, the rainbow, pink, black, grey or chaos), on a locus of its own. Two different copies give every ray a point or a rod in the second colour. On a horse without ear fins it does nothing.
