Diet — what the horse will eat
(Natural gene / paints nothing)
A wild type that eats what any horse eats, and a set of narrow diets — raw meat, fish, wheat, cake, potions, lava, one metal, one gem, and blood — each needing two identical copies to show. A narrow diet refuses almost everything, and what it does accept feeds it far better: a single gold bar takes a metal-eater to full health however hurt it was.
