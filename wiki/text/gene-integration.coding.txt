Integration It does not choose its own places; it grows out of the ones the horse already has. Anywhere the coat is genuinely dark - a bay's black points, the crest and the base of the mane, a dorsal stripe, the margin of any other dark marking - becomes a source, and the field propagates outward from it. Right at a source the elements merge into continuous patches with scalloped margins; a hand's span out they are separate circles and lobes; a foot or two out they thin to nothing, and beyond that there is none. A horse with no dark on it anywhere shows almost none of this, and the same horse with black points is covered from the knees up. merged patches right at the source — PIGMENT × PATCHES , painted with TOWARD elements thinning outward from it — SPOTS × PIGMENT , painted with TOWARD Coloured Integration The same marking in a colour the line carries rather than in white.
