Project / for volunteer testers For Testers Thank you for helping. This page is a list of things that still need someone to try in a normal Minecraft world. Each test is a short list of steps, one action per step. Do the steps in order. At the end, each test says what it looks like when it works and when something is wrong. If something is wrong, press the Report a problem with this test button under that test. You do not need to do all of them Pick any test. The ones near the top matter most, because they can break a save or crash the game without anyone noticing. The ones at the bottom are about how things look. Even one test done carefully is a real help. Before you start What you need Minecraft with NeoForge 26.1.2, and the Horse Genetics mod jar in your mods folder. A new, throwaway world for most tests. Some tests kill horses or break things on purpose. Cheats turned on , so you can switch to creative mode and use commands like /time set night . For a few tests, a second player. Those tests say so. Things the tests assume you know The horse browser: press H . It lists your horses, the gene database, recipes and a "Getting started" page. A horse's information screen: sneak and right-click any horse with an empty hand — wild or tamed, yours or not. On a tamed horse that is the gesture that used to open vanilla's inventory; the saddle and the barding moved onto this screen's Gear tab, and vanilla's is still there on E while you are riding, with the small i button on it to come back. Getting on a horse: right-click it, exactly as in vanilla. (There is also a Ride button at the top right of the information screen's Overview tab, for when you have just read about a horse and want it.) Saddling and barding: also on Overview — two slots under Tack . Hold the saddle and click the slot to put it on; click it with an empty hand to take it off again. Taming: ride a wild horse until hearts appear, the normal Minecraft way — press Ride , get thrown off, repeat. Some horses also come to you if you crouch while holding food. The vet's kit tells you about a horse. Use it on a horse without crouching: it says whether a mare is in heat, whether she is pregnant (and with twins), or how many times a stallion has bred today. Crouch-using it on your own stallion turns him into a gelding, so be careful. Heat and pregnancy: a mare is in heat for one Minecraft day (20 real minutes), then out of heat for one day. A pregnancy lasts one Minecraft day. Plain golden carrots still make an instant foal, like normal Minecraft. Making a horse with a particular gene: in creative mode, open the Horse Genetics creative tab and take the Custom Horse Spawn Egg . Right-click the ground to open its editor. Type the gene's name into the search box, add the gene, and set both of its buttons to the variant the test names. Then press Spawn . A test that says "a Holy ward horse" means a horse made this way. How to report a problem Every test has a Report a problem with this test button. What it does depends on whether you have set up the wiki's AI helper ( how ): With the AI helper: a chat opens and helps you write the report, asking only for what is missing. You check it and send it. Without it: GitHub opens a new issue with the test's name and a short form already filled in. You need a free GitHub account to press Submit. Either way, it helps a lot to say which step went wrong, what you saw, the mod version, and whether you were in single player or on a server. If the game crashed, attach the newest file from the crash-reports folder. Report a problem that is not on this page 1. Crashes and save safety (most important) Joining a world near horses does not kick you You need: a new world. A server and a second player if you can. Create a new world. Travel until you can see wild horses. Stay near them for one minute. Switch to creative mode. Take a Custom Horse Spawn Egg from the Horse Genetics tab. Open it and press Make egg . Keep the egg it gives you in your hotbar. Press H and open My horses . If you are on a server, ask a second player to join while horses are in view. It works if: nobody is disconnected. Something is wrong if: anyone is kicked with a message like "Failed to encode packet". Report a problem with this test The mod works in a plain install You need: a fresh NeoForge 26.1.2 profile with only this mod. Launch the game. Create a world with cheats on. Tame a wild horse. Open its information screen. In creative, take any filled breed spawn egg from the Horse Genetics tab. Use it on the ground. Type /locate structure horsegenetics:horse_stable in chat. Press H . Open the Gene database tab. Stand near a wild herd and watch it for one minute. Open a Custom Horse Spawn Egg, press Make egg , and hold the egg it gives you. Fill a seed jar from your own stallion (see the seed jar test ) and hold it. It works if: the world loads, the tamed horse has a generated name, wild horses have markings, the egg makes a horse of its breed, the command finds a stable, the gene list is filled, and holding the eggs and the jar near horses never disconnects you. Something is wrong if: the game crashes, you are disconnected, the horse has no name, the breed egg makes a "Feral Mixed" horse, or the gene list is empty. Report a problem with this test The F8 horse highlight does not crash the game You need: a wild herd nearby. Press F8 . Press F8 again. Press F8 once more. Stay near the herd for 4 minutes. Look at the name of the herd's leader. It works if: the second press says "Horse highlight OFF" and the outlines go away; after 4 minutes it says "Horse highlight timed out"; the leader's name turns back to white; the game keeps running. Something is wrong if: the game crashes, or chat says "Horse highlight hit an error and switched itself off". Report that message even though nothing crashed. It works if: nothing crashes. Something is wrong if: the game or server crashes. Attach the newest file from crash-reports . Looking after horses Healing needs water, and a horse that is not starving You need: a tamed horse, water, wheat, and a fenced spot on stone or sand with nothing a horse eats (no grass, hay or flowers). Hurt the horse a little with a small fall. Stand it next to water on grass. Watch its health for 20 seconds. Lead it away from the water. Watch its health for 20 seconds. Feed it wheat by hand. It works if: beside water it heals quickly, with hearts; away from water it stops healing; wheat still heals it; after two days with nothing to eat it no longer heals at all, even beside water; and it never loses health from being hungry. Something is wrong if: it heals far from water, never heals, keeps healing after two days without food, or takes damage from hunger.
