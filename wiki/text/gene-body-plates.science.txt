Body plates
(Magical gene / dominant / grows geometry, paints nothing)
One copy and the horse grows overlapping plates of bone over its shoulders and its hips, stepped down the flank like the lames of a suit of armour, the same on both sides. Whether they are smooth, ridged or spiked, and how big, how many, how tightly lapped and how flared they are, is written on the allele. Their colour is a gene of its own, body plate colour .

Invented No horse grows armour. The look borrows from the animals that do: the bony osteoderms in the skin of crocodiles and armadillos, the overlapping scutes of a pangolin, and the plated hide of an ankylosaur, arranged here like the lames of a pauldron over the shoulder and a tasset over the hip. This is fantasy and makes no claim about any real gene. It is dominant (the treatment’s call), so like the dorsal spines it shows on every carrier, and the sail is the body part you have to breed for.
