Horse hair
(Item / material)
Horse hair is the base material for almost everything the mod adds, and the only one — there is nothing you refine it into first. You get it by shearing a horse, and nearly every other item on the wiki takes it directly.

Shearing Right-click an adult horse with shears ( server/HorseShearHandler ): 1–3 horse_hair drop, the shears take a point of durability, and the horse gains +5 bond through HorseCareHandler.awardBondFor — so shearing is the one interaction that is both an economy input and a relationship input ( horse care ). It is gated to once per 24 000-tick Minecraft day , which is the mod-wide rule for every time-gated action. The gate is a stored game-time stamp under the key "shear" on data/HorseCooldownsAttachment — a plain Map<String, Long> on the horse, shared with the yield verb’s per-gene cooldowns ( "yield:<geneKey>" ). Because it is stored on the horse rather than in a static map, it survives a reload and cannot leak between worlds. The sheared look is unbuilt The mechanic is complete; the visual is not built. It is settled as a low-fidelity render-layer overlay — it only has to read as vaguely different, so no coat variant and no extra bake — driven off the same "shear" cooldown stamp, and the sync and the layer it needs are written out in gap 33 . The bundle, the rope and the cloth were removed Three items stood above hair: horse_hair_bundle (4 hair), braided_rope (3 bundles) and hair_cloth (4 bundles), each with a reverse recipe. Everything substantial took a rope or a cloth rather than hair, so the real prices compounded out of sight — a double fence gate at twelve hair, a cowboy hitch at sixty-eight. All three items, their six chain recipes, their lang keys, models and item definitions are deleted, along with horse_hair_bundle.png (the rope and the cloth never had art of their own; both borrowed it). Substitution was flat, one for one — one rope or one cloth became one hair — which is what makes the new prices so much lower. A shapeless recipe holds nine items, so preserving the old cost was not on the table even in principle. Three progress tasks went with them: BUNDLE_HAIR , CRAFT_ROPE and CRAFT_HAIR_CLOTH were about crafting items that no longer exist, and a task nothing can complete is exactly what check-progress-tasks.mjs exists to catch. KnownGeneSpliceRecipe also stopped accepting cloth as an alternative to hair, and the husbandry tutorial step lost three of its five icons. Six villager trades dealt in the removed items and were folded into the hair ladder rather than deleted. Both professions already improved their buy rate by level, and in raw-hair terms the intermediate trades had all been worse than the supplier’s level-1 hair trade — 32 to 48 hair per emerald against 16. The supplier now buys hair at 16 / 14 / 12 / 10 / 8 per emerald across its five levels, the leatherworker at 14 (level 2) and 10 (level 4), and the supplier’s level-1 sale of a bundle is now four hair for an emerald.
