Horn colour
(Magical gene / codominant / colours the horn, paints nothing)
What colour a unicorn's horn is — white, any colour of the rainbow, pink, black, grey, or chaos , a colour of its own on every copy. Nothing is dominant, so a horse with two different copies has a two-tone horn : one colour, with a tip of the other. On a horse without a horn this gene does nothing at all.

There is no real locus here Unicorns are not real, so neither is this. What is worth saying is why the colours are codominant rather than on a dominance ladder, which was the owner's choice between three options. A ladder hides the second colour a horse carries, and a blend turns most pairs of rainbow colours into brown. Codominance with a two-tone horn keeps both alleles on show, so the horn is also the genotype, and it makes a breeding goal of every pair: a violet horn tipped gold is something you plan for across generations. The small per-copy shade drift is the mod's premise applied to a colour gene: the alleles are the categories, and the epigenome keeps any two horses in a category from being identical.
