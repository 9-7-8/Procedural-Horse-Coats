Virtual stables
(Planned / not built)
A way to put a horse you own away — into an object you carry, or into a stall somewhere you can call it back from — so that moving a herd is not one lead rope at a time. None of it is built. This page exists so that the plan has a home of its own rather than a section on a shared list, and so that when the first piece of it ships there is already a page to document it on. The other half of the same question — what a horse carries for you — is horse chests, on the gear page .
