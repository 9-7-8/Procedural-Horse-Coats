Storage harness
(Gameplay / non-genetic)
The strap over a horse's rump and the metal frame on each flank that a chest hangs in. A horse wears one before it can carry a chest at all , and it takes a little of the load off. It comes with copper, iron, golden or netherite fittings — the better the metal, the more it helps — and its leather takes dye. Built on 2026-10-08 and not yet played; its enchantments are not built, and which ones it gets is still to be decided.

Where it is What Where The tiers and the sum common/pack/HarnessTier and PackLoad.lightened , pinned by PackLoadTest ( theHarnessLightensTheLoad , aServerMaySetTheHarness ) The item item/StorageHarnessItem , one class and an item per tier in ModItems The slot and both rules entity/HorseTackSlot.HARNESS , harnessed , accepts , mayTakeOff The refusal by hand server/TackEquipHandler The load server/HorsePackHandler.harnessReduction , read in refresh and by the Gear tab Drawing it client/HarnessLayer One number, divided evenly A tier's reduction is share() * best , where share() is its place in HarnessTier 's order over the number of tiers, and best is packs.harness_best_reduction . So the server has one dial and adding a tier re-divides it; nothing saved refers to a tier by number. The load the curve is asked about is PackLoad.lightened(weighed, reduction) — fractional on purpose, because a tenth off sixty-four items is not a whole number of items and rounding it would make a harness worth nothing on a small load. It is applied before the curve, so it moves a horse's maximum by the same share. MAX_HARNESS_BEST stops a config from making a load weigh nothing. An item per tier, not a component The house rule from bake-recipe-reference.mjs : a recipe cannot tell “with a component” from “without”, so anything a recipe must distinguish is its own item. The ids use vanilla's own spelling for the metals ( golden_storage_harness ) and are permanent. The tooltip is written from ServerConfig.packHarnessBestReduction() rather than from a lang string with a figure in it. Both rules live on the slot HorseTackSlot.accepts refuses a storage slot on a horse that is not harnessed , which every path asks: the Dress window's mayPlace , the picker's packet, and the right-click. mayTakeOff refuses the harness while either pack slot is full. TackEquipHandler adds only the sentence — a refusal that is claimed and said, the same way a foal's is, because a rule this mod invented that fails silently reads as a bug. isStorage() is the two pack slots by name, not the whole Storage zone the harness also sits in. The art Drawn in tools/bake-drawn-items.mjs as a two-layer item: storage_harness_leather , near white so that minecraft:dye tints it true and shared by all four, under a storage_harness_fittings_<metal> sprite each. All four are in minecraft:dyeable . The layer HarnessLayer is the first piece of gear here drawn as geometry rather than as a mask over the horse's own mesh, because a frame stands off the flank and a mask cannot. Two model parts built once from boxes in the body bone's units, baked straight from a LayerDefinition , submitted on the body bone through one flat white texture with the colour as the tint. The frames are placed from PackBox , the same numbers the chest is drawn and clicked at, so a chest sits in its frame by construction.
