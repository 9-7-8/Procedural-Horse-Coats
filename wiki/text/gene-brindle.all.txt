Brindle (MBTPS2)
(Natural gene / X-linked)
Irregular white streaks , soft-edged and broken, running down from the topline and not matching from one side of the horse to the other . It is the mod's only sex-linked gene , and that is the point of it: it takes two copies to show, and a stallion has one X and so one copy — so every brindle horse is a mare , and every stallion who has it at all is a carrier. The trait runs visibly down one side of a pedigree in a way no autosomal gene can.
