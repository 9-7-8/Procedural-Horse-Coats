Vet’s kit
(Item / breeding)
Geld a stallion so he never breeds, or examine a horse: where a mare is in her heat, and whether she is carrying twins.

How to use it Do this On What happens Sneak + right-click A stallion or colt you own He becomes a gelding , for good. Right-click A mare Where she is in her heat and when it ends, or how long her pregnancy has to go and whether it is twins. It also says if a stallion has already covered her this heat, and whether she is nursing. Right-click A stallion How many covers he has made today, and whether he is past the three that are at full odds — or that he is a gelding. Every use costs one of its 64 uses. Make one from shears, leather and an iron ingot , or buy one from the equestrian scientist . What gelding does He never covers a mare in heat, and a seed jar cannot be filled from him. A jar filled before still works. He will give a foal if you stand there and feed him and a mare golden carrots : that path takes no notice of anything but age and sex (owner, 2026-09-25). Gelding still does what it is for - a cut stallion left in a paddock sires nothing, ever - it just is not proof against a player holding two carrots. He stops acting like a stallion. He doesn’t spar, guard mares, lead a bachelor band or walk to a mare in heat, and keeps company the way mares do. He bonds with you a quarter faster. His info screen says Gelding , and his nameplate symbol is grey. It stays with him if he dies and is summoned back, or is sold on a transfer paper. It does not change his genes or his health. About half the males a cowboy sells are already geldings, and cost a little less. More on breeding on fertility & gestation .
