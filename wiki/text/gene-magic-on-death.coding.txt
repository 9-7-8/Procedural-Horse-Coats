Why the ground and the drops are two verbs Because they are two questions with two answers, and folding them into one would make “explodes and drops diamonds” impossible to express as the two independent loci it is. As two verbs on two genes it is a cross; as one table it would have been a row somebody had to remember to write. They are nonetheless handled in one translator class, because they share exactly one thing — the moment — and nothing else, including which event they can run on. LivingDropsEvent can rewrite the drop list and cannot usefully place a block; LivingDeathEvent is the other way round.
