Horn dust
(Magical gene / recessive / sheds from the horn, paints nothing)
Two copies and a unicorn's horn sheds a fine dust that falls past its face, all the time, in the horn's own colours — a two-tone horn's dust fades from one of its colours to the other as it falls. On a horse without a horn this gene does nothing at all.

Gene key horsegenetics.horn_dust Class HornDustGene — an EpigeneticAbilityContribution Priority 229 (magical band); only a code-order slot Family Grown parts , beside the unicorn horn Alleles Dst n Founders Dst/Dst 8%, n/n the rest — no wild carriers Coat effect none An emitter that asks the horn abilitiesFor returns one GeneAbility.Emitter , and only when the horse is Dst/Dst and GrownParts.of returns a horn. Its two colours are that horn's baseTint and tipTint , so the dust cannot disagree with the horn that is drawn. That needs the whole horse's epigenome, not this locus's (it has no numbers of its own), which is what GeneEpigenetics.epigenome() was added for; it is null when the question is asked of a genotype rather than a horse, and GrownParts then answers with midpoints. The particle falls Vanilla's coloured dust hangs where it appears: it randomises its own direction on spawn and multiplies its speed by a tenth, so no velocity sent with it survives. horsegenetics:horn_dust ( ModParticles.HORN_DUST , HornDustOptions ) is a type of the mod's own whose client particle, HornDustParticle , is vanilla's DustColorTransitionParticle with gravity set and a 30–50 tick life. The sprites are vanilla's generic_* , referenced, not copied. The horn anchor GeneAbilityHandler.anchorPoint gained a "horn" case: the head point, lifted by a random share of a horn's height. Like every anchor there it is estimated from the live bounding box — the server does not know the head's animated pose — so a grazing unicorn's dust starts where its horn would be if it looked up. It is not in AbilityType.EMITTER_ANCHORS , the list gene files may name: adding it there would make it part of the data format and the creator's parity surface, and no file gene has a horn.
