Saddles
(Horse gear / the saddle slot)
On a horse the vanilla saddle is the bridle , and it steers. The saddle is this mod's own item, worn in the Saddle slot beside it: it makes the ride. While a player is aboard, a saddle adds a share of the horse's own speed and jump, up to 10% at the top tier . It comes in every material a chestplate is made of (leather, copper, chainmail, iron, gold, diamond and netherite), and in every material another mod makes a chestplate of. Horse armour now follows the same rule, which adds chainmail horse armour .

How it is built The rule: common/gear/Saddles Saddles.Tier is the seven shipped materials, each with its item id, its fittings colour (what vanilla's chestplate icon of that material averages to, measured from the 26.1.2 jar with MaterialScan.averageColour ; leather under vanilla's default dye) and its share of the top tier's bonus (leather 0.25, copper 0.40, chainmail 0.50, iron 0.60, gold 0.70, diamond 0.85, netherite 1.00). Saddles.Rates holds the server's switch and the top tier's speed and jump, capped at MAX_TOP_BONUS . Saddles.bonus(rates, tier, ridden) is the only place the bonus is worked out, and it is NONE unless a player rides. tierOf(gem) puts another mod's material on the diamond or the iron rung, as the shoes and the armour do. Unit test: SaddlesTest . The items: item/HorseSaddleItem One item per material, registered from Saddles.Tier in ModItems.HORSE_SADDLES and from compat/ModdedSaddles for other mods. HorseSaddleItem.properties gives each one an Equippable for EquipmentSlot.SADDLE built like vanilla's Equippable.saddle() , except that its allowed entities are minecraft:horse alone and its asset is horsegenetics:saddle . Because it is a real equippable for the vanilla slot, isSaddled() , equip on right-click, shearing and the Gear tab's HorseTackSlot.SADDLE all work with no new path. Reins already treats anything real in that slot as "a saddle" (#74). Drawing it The equipment asset assets/horsegenetics/equipment/saddle.json has two horse_saddle layers: vanilla's seat leather ( minecraft:saddle ) and the hardware that sits on the seat ( horsegenetics:saddle_fittings , the stirrups and their buckles). The bridle and the bit are not in it; they are the bridle's, drawn from the Headstall. saddle_fittings.png is the complement of bridle_metal.png within vanilla's hardware: 16 of its 36 texels, baked by tools/tack/bake-saddle-textures.ps1 . client/TackClientExtensions tints layer 1 with the item's own colour and layer 0 with a bench-dyed seat colour if there is one. The icon is item/saddle (leather, untinted) under item/saddle_fittings (greys, tinted by client/MetalTintSource ), drawn in tools/bake-drawn-items.mjs as a placeholder. What it does: server/SaddleEffects Two transient ADD_MULTIPLIED_BASE modifiers, horsegenetics:saddle/speed and saddle/jump , held at Saddles.bonus 's answer. Nothing ticks: it runs on LivingEquipmentChangeEvent for the saddle slot, on EntityMountEvent (lowest priority, counting the player in or out by hand because the event fires before the passenger list changes), and on join. SaddleEffects.withoutSaddle takes the share back out, and HorseInfoScreen shows that number. Horses that come tacked up HorseTackSlot.dressWithBasicTack puts a bridle in an empty Headstall and a leather saddle in an empty saddle slot, on adult horses only. StablePopulator.spawnHorse calls it, and so does TransferPaperHandler when the paper's horse had no previous owner (a dealer's stock), as decided while scoping #72. The chestplate rule: compat/MaterialScan MaterialScan also collects every material some jar ships a <material>_chestplate item for ( assets/<ns>/items/ or the older models/item/ ), into Result.chestplates . It also averages each chestplate's own icon, and chestplateMetals is armourMetals cut to those materials and recoloured to their chestplates (owner, 2026-11-07; a chestplate with no readable art keeps the ingot's colour). The shoes keep the ingots' colours. ModdedArmour , GeneratedArmour and ModdedSaddles read it, and ModdedShoes still reads armourMetals , so every metal keeps its shoe. An armour whose material has no chestplate goes into droppedArmours , which aliases it to iron horse armour (and its would-be saddle to the iron saddle). The fingerprint's rules number went to 5 and lists the chestplates, so packs generated before this are rebuilt. Chainmail horse armour is ModItems.CHAINMAIL_HORSE_ARMOR : chainmail's ArmorMaterial with its own asset, the shipped plate tinted chain grey, because vanilla's chainmail asset has no horse layer. A modded armour's value: compat/ModdedArmourValue (#296) A modded horse armour registers with ModdedArmour.protectionFor 's guess (iron's body value for an ingot, diamond's for a gem), because no other item's armour is readable until components are bound at the first datapack load ( why ). Once worn or shown, ItemAttributeModifierEvent , fired whenever a stack's modifiers are read, replaces the body slot's minecraft:armor.body modifier with the value of that mod's own <material>_chestplate (any namespace but ours, the first by id, as MaterialScan takes the colour): the chest-slot armour its stack adds, run through common/gear/ArmourCurve . Each armour is worked out once, logged as compat: <id> follows <chestplate> (chest N) - M on a horse , and forgotten on a datapack reload ( TagsUpdatedEvent ). A chestplate that cannot be found or reads as no armour leaves the guess, and a read that throws is not kept. A horse already wearing the armour keeps its old value until the armour is taken off and put back. The metalsmith's tier is still picked from the guess. The curve: vanilla's plain rungs only (build-time call, 2026-11-07) Vanilla's chestplate-to-horse pairs are not a function: gold is chest 5 and horse 7, netherite chest 8 and horse 19 (diamond is 8 and 11), and our chainmail is 5 and 4. So the curve uses only leather, copper, iron and diamond (3 3, 4 4, 6 5, 8 11 in 26.1.2, read off ArmorMaterials at run time): a straight line between rungs, in proportion below leather, diamond's slope past it, and netherite's horse value as the cap. Gold and netherite are one material's deliberate exception each, not a trend for a modded material to follow. A modded chest 7 is 8 on a horse, 9 is 14, 10 is 17. Made without the owner; hers to amend. Unit test: ArmourCurveTest . The settings saddles.enabled (server, default on): off, saddles are worn and drawn and add nothing. saddles.top_speed , saddles.top_jump (server, default 0.1, range 0 to 0.25): what the netherite saddle adds. Every other tier is its fixed share of these. A changed number reaches a horse the next time a player gets on. What is tested SaddlesTest : the ceiling, nothing while unridden, the ladder climbs, the config caps, and the ids. MaterialScanTest : only a material with a chestplate gets armour and a saddle, the shoes keep every metal, the older model file counts and our own does not, and a new chestplate moves the fingerprint. ArmourCurveTest (#296): each rung maps to itself, straight lines between, diamond's slope to the netherite cap, proportional below leather, never decreasing, and refuses no reading or a non-function. Gametest a_chestplate_reads_as_its_horse_armour (#296): vanilla leather, copper, iron and diamond chestplates, read through ModdedArmourValue , give back their own horse armour's value. The dev game has no modded metal, so the event's swap itself is not driven. Gametest a_saddle_adds_only_while_ridden : fits a horse and not a donkey, chainmail armour fits the body slot, no bonus unridden, the top share while ridden, the screen's number is the horse's own, gone after dismount, and dressWithBasicTack .
