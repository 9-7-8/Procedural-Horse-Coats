Passification
(Magical gene / no coat effect)
The way in to a horse that would otherwise kill you. It adds no aggression of its own — it says what you can offer the animal to make it stop, and while the bargain holds it will not target you for any reason.

A bargain, not a switch The interesting thing about this locus is that it makes a dangerous horse into a negotiation rather than a wall. The animal is not tamed, not broken, and not fixed; it has been paid, and for the temporary routes it will want paying again. That is a different relationship from the one every other horse in the mod offers, and it only exists because aggression is allowed to be genuinely unmanageable. Why the calm is per player A horse that one person could defuse for everybody would make an aggressive line a single afternoon's problem on any shared world. Keeping the bargain between the animal and the person who fed it means an aggressive bloodline stays dangerous to everyone who has not done the work — which is what makes breeding one a decision rather than a novelty.
