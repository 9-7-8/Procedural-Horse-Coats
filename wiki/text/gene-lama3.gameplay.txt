LAMA3 (junctional epidermolysis bullosa, Saddlebred)
(Natural gene / health)
The same disorder as LAMC2 , in a different gene and a different breed — and the best-measured disorder frequency in this mod.

Crossing two of them The one number here that was not chosen Almost every carrier rate in this mod’s health layer is a judgement call — picked so a wild horse is healthy and an inbred line is not. This one is not picked. A random 2007 foal-crop sample found 9 carriers in 175 foals , a mutant allele frequency of 0.026, and that is the figure the American Saddlebred ships with. The word doing the work is random . Most disorder frequencies in horses come from animals tested because somebody suspected something, which inflates the number in ways nobody can measure afterwards. A random foal crop has no such bias, which makes this a better piece of evidence than most of what the rest of the health layer stands on. No founder is ever affected The founder table lists only the clear horse and the carrier. A wild-caught horse is an adult that survived, so it can carry this and never have it, and the homozygote’s weight is simply absent from the table. The only way to produce an affected foal is to breed two carriers. Not interchangeable with LAMC2 A Saddlebred carrying this is clear of the draft variant , and vice versa.
