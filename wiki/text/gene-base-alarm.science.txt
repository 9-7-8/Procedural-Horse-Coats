Base alarm
(Magical gene / no coat effect)
It whinnies when something hostile comes near. Not near your base — near the horse , which is why where you stable one is the whole of how you use it.

Dominant on purpose Most of the behaviour family is recessive and rare. An alarm is only useful if you happen to have one, so this is one of the few that should turn up without being hunted — a common, mildly useful gene that makes an ordinary stable slightly safer.
