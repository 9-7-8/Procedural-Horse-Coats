Back sail colour
(Magical gene / codominant / colours the back sail, paints nothing)
What colour a horse’s back sail is: its membrane and its spines. The same colours and rules as horn colour (white, the rainbow, pink, black, grey or chaos), on a locus of its own. Two different copies split the sail rather than fading it. On a horse without a sail it does nothing.
