EDNRB (frame overo)
(Natural gene / phase 1)
Frame overo, and the model’s first genuine lethal . Bold jagged-edged white on the flanks that never crosses the topline — and, when two carriers meet, an all-white foal that in life does not survive its first days. This is what used to be horsegenetics.frame , renamed to its locus alongside KIT , MITF and PAX3 .
