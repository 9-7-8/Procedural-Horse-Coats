Tusks
(Magical gene / dominant / grows geometry, paints nothing)
One gene for the growths of the mouth and muzzle, with a form for each allele: a narwhal horn , one long spiral tusk straight out of the front of the muzzle; boar tusks , a pair curling up from the corners of the lower jaw; and sabre fangs , a pair of long blades hanging from the upper lip past the chin. A horse with two different forms grows both. Each form’s length, thickness, curve and shape are written on its own allele. Their colour is a gene of its own, tusk colour .

Invented No horse grows tusks. The narwhal’s tusk is a real thing: a single spiralled canine tooth that grows forward through the upper lip, almost always on the left and almost always in males. This horn takes the look and not the biology. It is centred on the muzzle, not offset to one side (owner, tusks treatment), and mares grow it as often as stallions. It is fantasy and makes no claim about any real gene. The forms combine (a horse with two different forms grows both) because the parts already stack, so there is nothing to suppress.
