Music enjoyer
(Magical gene / no coat effect)
Play a record near this horse and it will stand there giving off hearts and slowly deciding it likes you. One of the few ways to build bond that does not involve feeding, riding or waiting.

Bond as something a gene can touch Bond has so far been entirely about what the player does. This is the first locus where the horse has an opinion about how it would like to be won over, and it opens a category the mod has not used: genes that change the husbandry rather than the animal.
