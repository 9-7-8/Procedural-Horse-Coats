The great majority of the marking is a haze you can read the coat straight through, spread over the trunk and trailing onto the head and the legs as a few straggling tangents rather than as a field. Somewhere on the barrel or the shoulder is the body of the cloud - a small dense knot, a hand's span or two across, where the colour actually arrives - and inside that the saturation curdles into stronger and weaker cells so the coat still reads through it in places. There is no boundary to find anywhere on any of it.
