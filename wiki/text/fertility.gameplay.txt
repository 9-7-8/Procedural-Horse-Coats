Fertility & gestation
(Start here / breeding)
Mares come into heat, and breeding through this mod’s own tools makes a pregnancy . The foal arrives when it ends, not the moment the pair is bred. Plain golden carrots still give a foal at once, exactly as in vanilla. Fertility is inherited: the fertility gene decides how readily a horse breeds and how often a mare carries twins.

Two kinds of breeding You do this You get Feed two horses golden carrots (neither has eaten a breeding carrot) A foal at once , as in vanilla. Heat does not matter. Feed golden carrots to a pair where either horse has eaten a breeding carrot A pregnancy , if the mare is in heat and it takes. Use a seed jar on your mare A pregnancy , if she is in heat and it takes. Leave a stallion with mares He covers each mare once per heat, and she may become pregnant . See below . Heat A mare is in heat for about one Minecraft day , then out of it for about one day, and so on. No two mares share the same timing. Her information screen shows it on the Breeding line in the Body section: In heat , In heat – best time now , or Not in heat – about 12 min to go . A mare in heat and a nearby stallion also walk toward each other. The second half of a heat is the best time. Out of heat, a mare refuses a seed jar and the jar is kept. Stallions have no heat. Will it take? When Chance Second half of a heat 80% First half of a heat 50% Foal heat, the first heat after she foals 60% Not in heat, pregnant, or just foaled no chance Her own fertility moves these a little either way, and a subfertile horse halves them. A stallion's day is never over. His first three covers or jar fills in a Minecraft day are at full odds; after that his odds are halved for the rest of the day, but he goes on covering. Nothing is ever more certain than 95%. The action bar says straight away whether it took. A mare with two stallions in reach takes the one who still has covers in hand, even if he is standing further off; a stallion past his three only covers when there is no fresher one about ( why ). Pregnancy It lasts one Minecraft day (twenty minutes) by default. A server can make it longer. Her Breeding line says Pregnant – about 14 min to go . In the last third she is 15% slower , and the line adds heavy and slow . A pregnant mare refuses golden carrots . She foals where she stands. The foal belongs to whoever owns her then , and is credited as bred by whoever arranged the breeding. If she is somewhere unloaded when she is due, she foals as soon as you come back. If she dies, the pregnancy is lost. Twins are rare, and more common with the twinning allele. Usually both are born alive; sometimes only one, and occasionally neither. About one twin pregnancy in ten is identical. One egg that split, so the two are a single foal drawn once: the same sex, the same colour, and the same markings down to the last streak — genuinely indistinguishable, because they share their epigenetics as well as their genes. The other nine pairs in ten are fraternal, two ordinary draws that are no more alike than any brother and sister. Two things follow from an identical pair being one genome rather than two: they can never be a colt and a filly, and if the draw was a lethal one the pregnancy loses both or neither. Two parents that both carry a lethal like MET sometimes make a pregnancy that is lost early . She comes back into heat afterwards. Pregnancy does not affect her health. After the birth For about a day she cannot be bred. Then comes foal heat , a heat at slightly lower odds, and then her normal cycle again. She is nursing (shown on the Breeding line) until her foal grows up, or until it has been more than 32 blocks from her for a whole day. Nursing does not stop her being bred again. Foals from plain golden carrots do none of this. Their dam is not nursing and her cycle carries on. A stallion left with mares Every adult horse, tamed or wild, breeds on its own. When a mare is in heat and a stallion stays within three blocks of her for a few seconds, as any vanilla animal must before breeding, he covers her once that heat (a burst of hearts), at the odds above. If it does not take, nothing more happens until her next heat. The foal is credited to her owner. A fence between them does not stop it, just as it does not stop two vanilla cows. It does not happen if: either horse is badly hurt — below 90% of its health; a scratch does not stop it ( why ) — ridden, or on a lead; he is a gelding ; fifty or more other horses , foals included, are within 16 blocks of her — a server setting ( the settings ); the horses are a cowboy's, whose string never breeds; nobody is near enough for that area to be loaded. She tells you why When one of your own mares is not covered for a reason you can do something about, you get a line in chat: the paddock is over its crowding limit (with both the count and the limit this server plays with), she is hurt, she is being ridden, she is on a lead, or she is a cowboy’s branded stock. When a cover does happen you are told whether it took. Only you are told, only while you are in the same world as her, and the same reason is not repeated for five minutes — though a different reason always speaks, so an owner who has just emptied a paddock hears what stopped it this time instead of silence. The line is also written down. Every one of these outcomes goes into the browser’s Log tab as well, as the same sentence — so a cover that happened while you were logged out, or in another world, is still there to read. That half is not gated on the chat setting. A mare who is simply between heats , or who has no stallion in reach , says nothing at all. That is nearly every mare nearly all of the time, and a line each would bury the ones that matter. Turn the lot off with notices.owned_horse_breeding . Geldings and the vet’s kit A vet’s kit , used while sneaking on your own stallion or colt, gelds him for good. A gelding never breeds, never fills a seed jar, and sires nothing from golden carrots. He does not spar, guard mares or go looking for a mare in heat, and he bonds with you a quarter faster. His information screen says Gelding and his nameplate symbol is grey. About half a cowboy’s males are geldings, and they cost a little less. Used without sneaking, the kit examines a horse: where a mare is in her heat and when it ends, whether a pregnant mare is carrying twins, and how many covers a stallion has made today. The settings fertility.nearby_horse_cap in the server config, from 1 to 1,000, and 50 by default . This is how many other horses may stand within 16 blocks of a mare and still let a stallion cover her; foals and wild horses count, and only horses she shares a pen with. It was a hard-coded eight, which is a hobby paddock rather than a stable — on a busy server that quietly stopped nearly every cover, and the refusal is the one the owner does hear about, so it read as the mod breaking. Server-side on purpose: whether a foal exists has to be one answer for everyone on the server. fertility.gestation_days , from 1 to 340. Every other stage grows with it in proportion, but is never shorter than one day, so at 340 a mare keeps a realistic three-week cycle. The clock is game time: sleeping and /time set do not move anything. With the server’s testing tools on, each of those “days” is one real minute.
