The cart and jump bench
(Planned / not built)
A bench you stand at to have a cart or a jump built to order — a wood and a colour chosen for each piece, the wheels of one timber and the bed of another — rather than crafting a whole cart from a grid recipe that already knows which wood it is. None of it is built. This page exists so that the plan has a home of its own rather than a section on a shared list, and so that when the first piece of it ships there is already a page to document it on.
