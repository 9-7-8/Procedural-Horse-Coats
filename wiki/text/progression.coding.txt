The checklist & the collection
(Gameplay / progression)
Two answers to “what do I do now?” — a checklist that walks you through everything the mod can do, and a collection of every allele in it with the ones you have never met still under fog.

Tasks are an enum in common/ common/progress/ProgressTask is the vocabulary: an id, a title, a hint and a group, with no Minecraft in it, so it is testable without a game and it survives the version port. The hooks that complete a task are necessarily Minecraft-side and live next to whatever event already existed — taming in the tame handler, foals in the breeding handler, whistles in the whistle item, crafting in ProgressHooks . ProgressTask.Group is the chapters of the book , not a filing system of its own, and it is only used for grouping the summary section. Which sub-chapter a task belongs to is held the other way round, in TutorialPage.Step.tasks() — the page owns the mapping because the page is what draws it, and that is what ClientProgress.allDone reads to decide whether a contents row is painted gold. A task in the enum but in no Step would show only in the summary and could never finish a sub-chapter, so the two lists are kept in step by hand. Every task is wired, and a check says so A checklist with an item nobody can tick is worse than a shorter checklist: it reads as a bug and the player cannot tell which one. Because the enum is in one module and the hooks are scattered across another by design, nothing but a sweep can prove they are all reachable — node neoforge-26.1.2/tools/check-progress-tasks.mjs is that sweep, and it fails by name. HorseProgress.complete(player, task) is the whole API a handler uses. It swallows a client-side player, a null and a server-less world, because it is called from a dozen files and the twelfth call site will be careless. HorseProgressData is the SavedData behind it, keyed by player UUID beside the gene database, and completing is idempotent — the first time is the only time anything happens. One trigger, and a hundred baked advancements What happens on that first time is ProgressTaskTrigger , this mod’s single CriterionTrigger ( horsegenetics:progress_task , registered in ModTriggers ). A trigger per task would be a hundred classes saying the same thing; instead the condition names the task, and three shapes of it cover the whole tree: Condition Matches Used by {"task": "tame_mare"} that one task every leaf {"chapter": "wild"} anything in that chapter the eight chapter nodes {} any task at all the tab’s root The JSON under data/horsegenetics/advancement/ is generated by node neoforge-26.1.2/tools/bake-advancements.mjs , which reads the enum and rewrites the whole directory — so a deleted task loses its advancement too. Titles and descriptions are literal text components rather than translation keys, because the English already lives in the enum and a lang key would be a second copy of it kept in step by hand. The one thing the bake cannot derive is the icon : those are a hand-written map in the script, validated against the mod’s own item definitions and against vanilla’s item list read out of the patched jar — an icon naming an item that does not exist makes the advancement fail to load silently, with nothing in the log. Add a task, re-run the bake check-progress-tasks.mjs now fails on a task with no advancement and on an advancement no task backs, alongside its original sweep for a task nothing can complete. A new checklist item is therefore three things: the enum line, the hook, and an icon in the bake — and the check names whichever you forgot. HorseProgressData.awardEverythingDone , called from ProgressHooks.onLogin , fires the trigger for everything a player has already ticked. It is what lets advancements arrive after the fact on a save that was played without them; awarding one a player holds does nothing, so it is safe every login rather than once. The collection is a second set, on purpose GeneDatabaseData holds two things per player now: the book of discovered genes, and a flat set of geneKey|token for alleles seen. They are separate because sharing one map would have made one behaviour quietly become the other — an entry in the book is what knows() tests, so recording baseline alleles there would have unlocked every gene in the mod off one ordinary horse. Both ride the same GeneDatabaseSyncPayload , which grew a third field, and both are mirrored client-side in ClientGeneDatabase . collect() is called from GeneDiscoveryHandler.discoverFrom , which is already the one place that sees a genotype the player has earned.
