Saddles
(Horse gear / the saddle slot)
On a horse the vanilla saddle is the bridle , and it steers. The saddle is this mod's own item, worn in the Saddle slot beside it: it makes the ride. While a player is aboard, a saddle adds a share of the horse's own speed and jump, up to 10% at the top tier . It comes in every material a chestplate is made of (leather, copper, chainmail, iron, gold, diamond and netherite), and in every material another mod makes a chestplate of. Horse armour now follows the same rule, which adds chainmail horse armour .
