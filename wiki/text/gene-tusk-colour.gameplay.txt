Tusk colour
(Magical gene / codominant / colours the tusks, paints nothing)
What colour everything the tusks gene grows is: today, the narwhal horn. The same colours and rules as horn colour (white, the rainbow, pink, black, grey or chaos), on a locus of its own. White is the wild type, and it reads as ivory. On a horse without tusks it does nothing.

Crossing two of them The colours, and two-tone tusks Exactly the horn colour table . Two copies of one colour make the whole tusk that colour. Two different ones make it two-tone from root to tip: the colour that comes earlier in the list at the root, fading to the other at the point, so Red/Blu is red at the face and blue at the tip, either way round. Every copy carries a little shade of its own. One colour gene covers every form. When the boar tusks and sabre fangs arrive, a horse with fangs and a narwhal horn will wear both in the same colour. Bare bone Two Bon copies are the bone version: a tusk is a tooth, so on a skeleton it is bare bone. No wild horse carries Bon ; the skeleton horses do, so a skeleton that grows a narwhal horn grows it in bone. Is it good for the horse? It does nothing to the horse at all.
