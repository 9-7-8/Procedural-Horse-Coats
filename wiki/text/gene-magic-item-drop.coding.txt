Magic item drop
(Magical gene / no coat effect)
What the horse leaves behind — diamonds, a spawn egg that puts this horse back, or an enchanted sword. Every variant takes two copies and none of them shows on the living animal, which is the point: it is worth breeding a line for and impossible to spot in a field.

Replace, except meat Every value on the item_drop verb replaces the vanilla drop except meat , which is added beside it. The asymmetry belongs to the genes rather than to the verb: a horse that leaves diamonds instead of its leather is a different animal, and a meaty one is the same animal with more on it.
