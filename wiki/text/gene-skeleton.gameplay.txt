Skeleton
(Magical gene / recessive / one of the two undeath loci)
Two copies and the horse is its own bones. Everything that is not skeleton is cut away, the bone shows through the horse’s coat, and whatever colour the horse carries is the colour of its bones. It never drowns, and it counts as undead . One copy is a carrier that looks and acts like any other horse. No wild horse has it: every one comes from a vanilla skeleton horse that converted into a Great Valley Skeleton Horse , or from breeding them.

Crossing two of them What two copies do The look. Vanilla’s skeleton horse, laid over the horse’s own coat: its holes cut the horse away, its bone colour shows through, and its shading is put back on top. A white horse makes clean white bones; a dun, yellowed old ones; a spotted horse, spotted bones. Black eyes , whatever its other genes would have given it. It never drowns — the horse, not its rider. It is undead to anything that asks: cleansing light, a holy ward, an aura that names the undead. Everything else a skeleton horse is known for — eating bones, turning on its rider after dark until it is given one, dropping bones when it dies, the soul fire at its hooves, its speed — belongs to the breed , not to this gene. A skeleton foal of two ordinary parents is bones with an ordinary horse’s appetite and temper. One copy Nothing shows and nothing changes. A carrier is not undead for any purpose. Two carriers bred together have a one in four chance of a skeleton foal. Is it good for the horse? It has no health effect of its own. The horse cannot drown, which is all it gains.
