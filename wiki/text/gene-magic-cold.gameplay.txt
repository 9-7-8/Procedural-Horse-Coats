Magic cold tolerance
(Magical gene / no coat effect)
Better or worse in a cold biome. A tolerant allele, a sensitive allele and a neutral wild type; how much each copy is worth, and whether it goes to speed, jump or both, is written on the copy. Its twin is magic heat tolerance .

Built, and not yet seen in game The gene and its breed pools are tested. What it does to a horse in the snow has not been watched yet; see the Verification tab . What it does Combination In a cold biome two tolerant copies markedly faster, or jumps markedly higher, or some of each one tolerant copy somewhat better one tolerant, one sensitive close to normal — the two pull against each other one sensitive copy somewhat worse two sensitive copies markedly slower, or jumps markedly lower, or some of each anything with no variant copy no difference, anywhere Cold means anywhere snow can fall where the horse stands: every snowy biome, and the high ground of a temperate one once it is above the snow line. It does not have to be snowing. Who carries it Northern and mountain breeds tend to be tolerant: the Icelandic, Fjord, Shetland, Exmoor, Canadian Horse and Przewalski's horse nearly all are, and the Dales, Dartmoor, Haflinger, Trakehner and Mustang often are. The Thoroughbred is the one breed that leans sensitive. The full list is on the breeds page . A Feral Mixed horse carries one copy now and then. Health None. A sensitive horse is slower in the cold, never hurt by it.
