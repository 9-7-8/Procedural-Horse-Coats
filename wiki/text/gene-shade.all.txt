(Natural gene / paints nothing itself)
The regulatory locus beside agouti. It draws no pixel of its own and every horse carries it, but on a bay its dosage — plus whether extension is E / E and whether agouti is A / a — decides whether that bay is a blood bay , an ordinary bay , a liver bay or a seal brown .
