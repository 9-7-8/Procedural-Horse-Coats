Horse shoes
(Horse gear / hoof equipment)
A horse shoe is one item to a hoof : four separate stacks in four separate gear slots, each in its own metal, drawn under the foot it is on. Five ship (copper, iron, golden, diamond and netherite) and every metal another mod brings gets one of its own. They are bought from the equestrian metalsmith. A shoe does not change how the horse moves yet: the stat bonus, powder snow and the enchantments are on the Roadmap tab.

What they are A horse shoe is a real piece of gear worn on a hoof. Each hoof holds its own shoe, so a horse can be shod on one foot, or in four different metals, and nothing treats the four as a set. One shoe per hoof. Near fore, off fore, near hind, off hind: the four Shoe slots at the bottom of the Gear tab's paper doll. Each shoe is independent. One can be taken off or swapped without touching the other three. Any shoe fits any hoof. There is no left or right shoe. Foals are not shod. A foal wears no gear at all. Getting them Shoes are bought from the equestrian metalsmith , one metal at each of his five levels, cheapest first. There is no crafting recipe: tack is to be made at the tack bench, which is not built yet (owner, 2026-10-12), and the bench will add the making. All of them are in the mod's creative tab. Shoe Metalsmith level Price, each Copper Horse Shoe 1 (novice) 2 emeralds Iron Horse Shoe 2 3 emeralds Golden Horse Shoe 3 5 emeralds Diamond Horse Shoe 4 7 emeralds Netherite Horse Shoe 5 (master) 10 emeralds A metal or gem another mod adds (tin, steel, ruby) gets a shoe named after it, in that metal's own colour, sold at the level its horse armour is sold at. A mod's own copy of a metal above gets no second shoe. Putting them on Open the horse's menu, go to the Gear tab, and put a shoe in a hoof slot. Or right-click your own tamed horse with a shoe in hand: it goes on the first bare hoof, near fore first. Click a shod slot to take the shoe back. What they look like A shoe is a U of metal under the foot, open at the heel, so the frog shows inside it. From the side only a thin edge shows at the ground; the whole shoe shows under a lifted foot, on a rearing or galloping horse. It is the colour of its metal, and the icon in your inventory is the same colour. What they do Nothing yet beyond being worn and seen. A flat speed and jump bonus from the metal, walking on powder snow, and two enchantments (grip, and speed on paths) are planned and not built. See the Roadmap tab. Why they are different from boots Boots are a leg item and sit in the four Boot slots above the shoes. A boot is about the leg as a whole and a shoe is about the ground under the hoof, so they are separate slots and a horse may wear both.
