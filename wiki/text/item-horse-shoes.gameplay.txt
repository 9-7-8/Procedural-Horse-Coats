Horse shoes
(Horse gear / hoof equipment)
A horse shoe is one item to a hoof : four separate stacks in four separate gear slots, each in its own metal, drawn under the foot it is on. Five ship (copper, iron, golden, diamond and netherite) and every metal another mod brings gets one of its own. They are bought from the equestrian metalsmith. Each worn shoe adds a little speed and jump from its own metal, and a horse with at least one shoe on stands on powder snow. A shoe can take one of two enchantments: Grip, for ice, or Roadster, for paths and roads.

What they are A horse shoe is a real piece of gear worn on a hoof. Each hoof holds its own shoe, so a horse can be shod on one foot, or in four different metals, and nothing treats the four as a set. One shoe per hoof. Near fore, off fore, near hind, off hind: the four Shoe slots at the bottom of the Gear tab's paper doll. Each shoe is independent. One can be taken off or swapped without touching the other three. Any shoe fits any hoof. There is no left or right shoe. Foals are not shod. A foal wears no gear at all. Getting them Shoes are bought from the equestrian metalsmith , one metal at each of his five levels, cheapest first. There is no crafting recipe: tack is to be made at the tack bench, which is not built yet (owner, 2026-10-12), and the bench will add the making. All of them are in the mod's creative tab. Shoe Metalsmith level Price, each Copper Horse Shoe 1 (novice) 2 emeralds Iron Horse Shoe 2 3 emeralds Golden Horse Shoe 3 5 emeralds Diamond Horse Shoe 4 7 emeralds Netherite Horse Shoe 5 (master) 10 emeralds A metal or gem another mod adds (tin, steel, ruby) gets a shoe named after it, in that metal's own colour, sold at the level its horse armour is sold at. A mod's own copy of a metal above gets no second shoe. Putting them on Open the horse's menu, go to the Gear tab, and put a shoe in a hoof slot. Or right-click your own tamed horse with a shoe in hand: it goes on the first bare hoof, near fore first. Click a shod slot to take the shoe back. What they look like A shoe is a U of metal under the foot, open at the heel, so the frog shows inside it. From the side only a thin edge shows at the ground; the whole shoe shows under a lifted foot, on a rearing or galloping horse. It is the colour of its metal, and the icon in your inventory is the same colour. What they do Each shoe adds a little speed and a little jump, from its own metal. The four hooves are added up, so three shoes are three quarters of four, and four different metals are four different amounts. There is no bonus for a matching set. A shoe taken off takes its share with it. Metal Speed, one shoe Speed, four Jump, four (about) Copper +0.09 m/s +0.35 m/s +0.11 m Iron +0.13 m/s +0.52 m/s +0.17 m Golden +0.17 m/s +0.69 m/s +0.23 m Diamond +0.22 m/s +0.86 m/s +0.28 m Netherite +0.26 m/s +1.04 m/s +0.34 m Those are the defaults, and they are small on purpose: an ordinary horse runs at about 5 to 14 m/s, so the best four shoes are worth roughly a tenth of an average horse and how fast a horse is stays a matter of breeding. Jump height is not a straight line in the number behind it, so the jump column is for an average jumper; a strong jumper gets a little more from the same shoe. A shoe's tooltip states what one of it adds on the server you are on, and the horse's own screen shows the total in its Speed and Jump lines. One shoe is enough for powder snow. A horse with a shoe on any hoof, of any metal, stands on top of powder snow the way a player in leather boots does. An unshod horse sinks. Another mod's metal gives what iron gives if it is an ingot and what diamond gives if it is a gem, the same rung its horse armour stands on. A server can change every one of those numbers, or switch shoes off altogether ( the settings ). Enchanting them A horse shoe takes one of two enchantments, each with three levels. They are the only enchantments that go on a shoe, and they go on nothing else. Enchantment What it does One shoe, per level Four shoes at III Grip The horse slides less on ice, packed ice, blue ice and anything else slippery. 8% of the slide gone 96%: ice is very nearly ordinary ground Roadster The horse is faster while it is on a path or a road. +0.09 m/s +1.04 m/s Each shoe carries its own. The four hooves are added up, like the metals: two shoes of Grip II are the same as one of Grip III and one of Grip I. One shoe, one enchantment. Grip and Roadster will not go on the same shoe. A horse can still wear both, on different feet: two Grip shoes and two Roadster shoes is a horse for a road that crosses a frozen river. Grip never makes ice better than ground. It only takes the slide away, so a horse on grass is exactly as it was. What counts as a road for Roadster: dirt paths (the paths a village is built on, and any grass you have run a shovel over), cobblestone and mossy cobblestone, stone bricks of every kind, bricks and smooth sandstone, and the slabs of those. The speed arrives within a stride of stepping on and leaves within a stride of stepping off; a jump does not lose it. Getting one. Put a shoe in an enchanting table, or put a Grip or Roadster book on a shoe at an anvil. The books turn up wherever ordinary enchanted books do: the table, librarians and loot. An enchanted shoe glints in your inventory and in the Gear tab, and its tooltip says what its enchantment is worth on the server you are on. The shoe drawn under the hoof does not glint. Why they are different from boots Boots are a leg item and sit in the four Boot slots above the shoes. A boot is about the leg as a whole and a shoe is about the ground under the hoof, so they are separate slots and a horse may wear both.
