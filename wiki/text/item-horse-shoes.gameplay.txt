Horse shoes
(Horse gear / hoof equipment)
Horse shoes are the first true hoof item in the accessories system: one item per hoof, each with its own material, enchantment and durability, yet all of them living under the same attachment-backed gear model the rest of the horse gear uses. They are not a special case on the horse; they are four independent stacks sharing one shared rule set. Any metal can be used, and the tier of that metal is what decides how much the shoe increases the horse's movement stats.

What they are A horse shoe is a real piece of gear worn on a hoof rather than a cosmetic shape on the body. This means each hoof can hold its own piece and each piece can hold its own enchantment state, material profile and damage. The point is not that the horse wears four shoes as a set; the point is that a horse can wear one healthy front shoe and one worn hind shoe without the system treating them as one single state. One shoe per hoof. Front left, front right, hind left, hind right. Each shoe is independent. One can break, be enchanted or be removed without changing the others. They are real equipment. They are in the gear attachment, not in a separate horse-only container, and they are not a flag on the horse. All shoes walk on powered snow. Even a plain shoe grants the horse the ability to move across powered snow without sinking or losing traction. Metal tiers and stat bonuses Horse shoes are made from a metal material, and the metal's tier determines the baseline stat boost. Higher-tier metals add more flat bonus to speed and jump than lower-tier ones, while the actual value is derived from the material configuration rather than from a hardcoded switch on a single item. The point is that a common metal makes a modest improvement, while a rare or superior metal gives a larger, recognizably better hoof profile. The stat bonus is intentionally a flat bonus , not a percentage multiplier. That keeps the system readable and easy to balance: a tier 1 metal gives a small bump to speed and jump, a tier 2 or 3 metal gives a larger bump, and the horse's existing movement stats remain easy to compare from one shoe to the next. Because the bonus is flat, the design is also easier to tune around breeding, load and terrain. Tiered material bonus. Material tier determines the flat increase to jump and speed. Any metal qualifies. The system should accept any material with a valid metal tier, not only a fixed shortlist of named metals. Enchantments add special abilities. Some enchantments grant effects such as traction, impact reduction, or terrain-specific movement, while the core tier bonus remains the steady rule. Why they are different from boots Boots are a leg item and therefore sit in a broader leg slot model. Shoes are a hoof item and they need a different render target, a different effect scope, and a different durability profile. The important difference is that a boot is about the leg as a whole and a shoe is about the ground interaction at the hoof. The horse-side math therefore belongs on the hoof slot rather than the leg slot, even though they share the same equipment system.
