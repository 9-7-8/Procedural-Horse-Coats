Diet — what the horse will eat
(Natural gene / paints nothing)
A wild type that eats what any horse eats, and a set of narrow diets — raw meat, fish, wheat, cake, potions, lava, one metal, one gem, and blood — each needing two identical copies to show. A narrow diet refuses almost everything, and what it does accept feeds it far better: a single gold bar takes a metal-eater to full health however hurt it was.

The feeding half — refusing an apple, healing off a bar of gold, leaving a bucket behind — has never been run in a game. See the Verification tab . Which metal, which gem An ingot-eater does not eat metal in general — it wants one metal, and a gem-eater one gem. Diet.variants() says how many there are and the choice is one stored number — variant , on the expressing copy — so it is fixed for the horse’s life and a foal that inherits the copy inherits the craving. Two ingot-eating siblings can want different bars. It is kept as a position in [0, 1) rather than an index because how many variants there are depends on which diet the alleles gave the horse. common/ names no item, so the count is the contract between the two sides: DietFoods must list exactly variants() items for each, and DietFoods.verify() runs at startup and throws if it does not. That check is there because the failure is silent — an out-of-range index would give you a horse that never accepts anything and no error anywhere. Feral only, and never a carrier No named breed has any of it. DietGene returns true from Gene.feralOnly() , which is new with this gene: BreedFounder forces such a locus to its wild type on every breed. (Every magical gene a breed does not name is forced wild too now; feralOnly predates that and says the same thing louder.) A lava-eating Shire is not a breed trait. A breed that genuinely wanted one could still name the locus in its own pool — that check runs first. A founder is never a carrier — except of blood. The founder table lists n/n and the homozygotes and nothing between, so a feral horse that rolls into this gene has the diet outright. Dbld is the mirror: never outright in the wild (a horse that could only heal by biting would not have lived to be caught), a few silent carriers at DietGene.BLOOD_CARRIER_PERCENT . The random splice carrot cannot hand one to a foal. Its spliceTable() is the mirror image — n/n and the carriers , with blood left out entirely. An unasked-for horse that only eats gold is a trap; a carrier is a gift. The named Known Gene Splice carrot is heterozygous too, so a player who wants the diet still has to breed for it. Early on purpose, so it can be overridden A horse has one diet, so several genes may have a claim on it and exactly one answer comes out. HorseDiet.resolve walks Genes.codeOrder() , asks every DietContribution on the way, and keeps the last claim — which is the opposite of the eye channel’s ranked contest, and is the point. That is the whole reason this locus sits at priority DietGene.PRIORITY , ahead of every gene that paints. Nothing about the coat needs it there. What needs it is that a later gene saying “and this horse cannot be fed at all” wins by sorting after it, with no special case anywhere and nothing to register. Diet.NOTHING exists for exactly that and is not an allele of this locus. Nothing claims it today — the dhampir did, until it was split and its appetite became the Dbld allele here. Diet.fedByItems() is false for NOTHING and BLOOD , and it is the one test both halves of the game module ask: HorseDietHandler.canRegenerate (so the gated regen skips them) and DietFoods.accepts . The hunt itself is neoforge/server/BloodHuntGoal , wired onto every horse by BloodDietHandler and resolving the diet once, lazily. What the game module does with it HorseDietHandler intercepts PlayerInteractEvent.EntityInteract and does nothing at all unless the horse has a special diet: Not a feed attempt Left alone entirely — a saddle, a lead, an empty hand. A special diet must never stop a horse being ridden. A vanilla love item Left alone. The locus governs healing, not breeding ; a horse that could never be bred would be a dead end rather than a curiosity. This is the one deliberate leak: a golden carrot heals a little on the way past. Something it eats Heals, consumes the item (leaving a bucket or a bottle where there was one), awards double the ordinary feed bond, and cancels so vanilla does not also feed it. Anything else edible Refused — smoke, no healing, and a message naming what the horse actually wants. A diet nobody can see is a guessing game; this one is a puzzle with a solution. Natural or magical? An open call The locus is declared natural . It paints in neither phase, so the flag chooses nothing about the coat; it is declared that way because dietary preference is a real heritable thing, and because a magical gene would be thrown into BreedFounder ’s magic lottery — which would hand breeds the very alleles feralOnly() exists to keep off them. The lava and ingot ends of it are frank fantasy, so if the natural/magical split ever means more than which coat pass a gene paints in, this is the first gene to revisit.
