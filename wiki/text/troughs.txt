Troughs and salt licks
(Planned / not built)
A feeder a horse walks up to and eats from — assembled out of planks in whatever square or rectangular footprint the stall wants, in any mod's wood — and a salt lick beside it that a horse uses over time. None of it is built , and no block this mod adds feeds a horse at all today. This page exists so that the plan has a home of its own rather than a section on a shared list, and so that when the first piece of it ships there is already a page to document it on.
