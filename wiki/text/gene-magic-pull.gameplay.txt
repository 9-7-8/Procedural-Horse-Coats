Magic pull
(Magical gene / no coat effect)
How much the horse can shift. The fifth magical body-stat locus, beside size , speed , health and jump — and the one whose number is a score out of ten rather than a multiplier . A horse carrying nothing here pulls at 5 ; a Shire pulls at 10 and a Falabella at 1. Both copies count and they add, one allele positive and one negative.

Crossing two of them The scale Pull is written and read on the same 1–10 scale a breed sheet scores its stats with, and nothing converts it in between. Five is an ordinary horse. The draught breeds are the top of it — Shire, Percheron, Belgian Draft, Clydesdale and Suffolk Punch all score 10 — and the miniatures the bottom, with the Falabella and the American Miniature at 1. A breed’s founders are written to its score instead, so a wild-caught Shire is a 10 rather than a 6 — see below .
