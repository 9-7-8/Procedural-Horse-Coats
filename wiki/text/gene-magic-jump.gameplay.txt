Magic jump
(Magical gene / no coat effect)
The magical version of the natural jump locus, RYR2 . One of the five magical body-stat genes — size , speed , health , jump and pull — and like the other three multipliers, most wild horses carry a copy , worth about a tenth either way. Two copies of the same kind add , and no wild horse is ever born with two. (Pull is the odd one out: an additive score rather than a multiplier, and with no vampiric allele.)

The vampiric allele A third kind of copy, Vmp , and the Dhampir’s jump. Unlike Springy it is worth a fixed amount, the same on every horse: one copy jumps half again as high, two copies twice as high. Beside a Springy or Leaden copy, both count.
