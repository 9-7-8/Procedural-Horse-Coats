Base-aware phenotype naming

This is a surface, not a gene, and that is the whole point of it: nothing new is inherited, nothing new is painted from scratch. It reads a genotype that already exists — and the epigenetics riding on it — and works out what the horse should be called, so that a dilution can be named against the base it is sitting on rather than by its locus. None of it is built. It came off the roster audit on natural genes, which is where it was proposed as a gene and turned out not to be one.

Base-aware phenotype naming

It serves three separate gaps. The reference names gold / amber / sable / classic champagne and chestnut / bay / black / cream pearl as distinct phenotypes by base, and expects cream×champagne and cream×pearl to read differently again. Separately, it treats seal bay as its own base that sable champagne rides on — but seal has no gene here, it is the top of agouti's epigenetic distribution.

So the mod needs a way to resolve and name “this genotype is an amber champagne” or “this horse is seal” for the info panel, the paper dump and the gene dictionary — and champagne needs to read “is this horse seal” off the agouti epigenetics to paint sable rather than amber. One base-aware phenotype resolver serves all of it.
