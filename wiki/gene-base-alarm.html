Magical gene / no coat effect

Base alarm

It whinnies when something hostile comes near. Not near your base — near the horse, which is why where you stable one is the whole of how you use it.

Built, and not yet seen in game

The gene, its inheritance and its founder table exist and are covered by the test suite; the translator that turns it into behaviour was written against the 26.1.2 sources and is flagged unverified in its own comments. Nothing on this page has been watched happening to a horse. What to look at, and what is most likely to be wrong, is on the Verification tab.

There is no “base” in this mod, and inventing one was rejected

The original specification said “your base radius”. No such concept exists — there are stall signs, the barn and pens, and nothing that means home. Measuring from the horse was chosen over measuring from a stall sign, which would silently do nothing for any horse without one.

One whinny per detection, with a cooldown

Fire when something enters the radius, not once per beat per mob in it. Dominant inheritance makes this worse than it sounds — several alarm horses in one paddock all noticing the same skeleton is the normal case, not the edge case.

Interval scan with a cap, like every radius effect

Nothing new: the same max_targets and tick-offset discipline the format already requires of every radius effect.

Measured from the horse

It makes the gene work everywhere with no setup, no binding item and no concept to invent — and it turns stabling into the interface. Where you put the horse is the configuration.

Dominant on purpose

Most of the behaviour family is recessive and rare. An alarm is only useful if you happen to have one, so this is one of the few that should turn up without being hunted — a common, mildly useful gene that makes an ordinary stable slightly safer.

What needs verifying here

Base alarm has never been heard firing: its whinny is vanilla’s own entity.horse.angry, so only the census’s sound count can tell a working alarm from a silent one, and nobody has read that number yet.

Still open (NOT played)

The gene is one Sound ability gated on the hostile_near world flag, sampled every BaseAlarmGene.CHECK_TICKS with a COOLDOWN_TICKS under it. The yard’s BASE ALARM pen is one pen from the ward spawner precisely because that is the only place the condition can ever be true — and DebugWorldWatch.notePlayedSound tallies every gene sound by id into the census line, which is the evidence here. Do not try to judge this by ear: the sound it plays is the noise an ordinary annoyed horse makes anyway.

Nothing here needs looking at to judge — do these first

  • The alarm fires at all. Stand at the yard’s BASE ALARM pen until the neighbouring spawner is running. Pass: the census line “gene sounds since the last census” names minecraft:entity.horse.angry with a non-zero count. A zero there is the whole gene failing, and nothing else below is worth checking until it moves.
  • It is silent with nothing hostile about. Put an expressing horse in a lit, walled pen with no mobs and leave it for a census or two. Pass: that sound id is absent from the census entirely. This is the control that separates “the flag works” from “the flag is stuck true”, and the sampled world flags have been stuck-off for every gene in the mod once already.
  • The radius is measured from the horse. Two expressing horses, one within sixteen blocks of a spawner and one well outside it. Pass: the count rises while only the near one can see anything; move the far one in and the rate goes up.
  • One copy is enough. Spawn an Alm/n horse from the custom horse spawn egg. Pass: its gene row reads the Alarm expression and not a carrier, and it contributes to the count beside an Alm/Alm horse.
  • The cooldown holds in a herd. Four expressing horses around one spawner for ten minutes. Pass: the census count is bounded by roughly one whinny per horse per COOLDOWN_TICKS — about sixty each over that span. A count several times higher means the cooldown is not per horse, which is the failure dominance makes likely here.

In the game, with your hands

  • It is bearable. Stand in that paddock of four alarm horses while the spawner runs. Pass: you can work there. The cooldown was chosen by arithmetic and never heard, and “a dominant gene several horses in one stable have” is the normal case rather than the edge case.
