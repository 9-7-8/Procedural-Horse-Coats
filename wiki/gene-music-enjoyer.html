Magical gene / no coat effect

Music enjoyer

Play a record near this horse and it will stand there giving off hearts and slowly deciding it likes you. One of the few ways to build bond that does not involve feeding, riding or waiting.

Built, and not yet seen in game

The gene, its inheritance and its founder table exist and are covered by the test suite; the translator that turns it into behaviour was written against the 26.1.2 sources and is flagged unverified in its own comments. Nothing on this page has been watched happening to a horse. What to look at, and what is most likely to be wrong, is on the Verification tab.

It still respects the daily limit

Bond has a cap per day, and this counts against it like every other source. Leaving a jukebox running overnight will not hand you a tier-three horse by morning — it will hand you the same day’s worth of bond you could have earned another way, for less effort.

It must count against the daily bond cap

HorseCareAttachment carries bondToday precisely so no single source can run bond from nothing to the top tier overnight. A bond source that ignores it is an AFK exploit rather than a feature — and it devalues every other way of earning bond in one change.

near_jukebox is a block search, and block searches are the expensive shape

Every existing condition flag is a field read off the horse. This one has to look at the world, and a naive implementation scans blocks per horse per tick. Interval-sampled, small radius, cached between samples.

It is a bond source, which the backlog already wanted

§15 lists more bond sources as open work. The bond verb built here is the one food preference needs too, and the one a future grooming or sleeping source would reuse.

A small verb with more users than it looks

“Raise bond by an amount, on an interval, while a condition holds” is enough to express this gene, food preference, and every bond source the backlog has asked for. Building it here rather than hard-coding a jukebox check is what makes the next one free.

Verified

Bond rises beside a jukebox, stops at the daily cap, and only for a tamed expressing horse in range (test yard, pen MUSIC, the run of 2026-10-02 03:52 on a56afcbb, five clockwork PASS lines at 04:12:26). A tamed Msc/Msc mare 2–4 blocks from a jukebox with a record went 40 → 55, one point every 200 ticks from +200, until bondToday reached 15 at +3000; it never read above 15 and the bond did not move again for the rest of the care day. Over the same 118 readings an untamed Msc/Msc mare beside it, a tamed Msc/n carrier 6–7 blocks off, and a tamed Msc/Msc mare 13 blocks off each held bond 40, bondToday 0. No owner was near any of them and their hunger was held full, so the gene was the only thing that could move a number.

A finished record goes on paying (2026-10-08, test yard, pen MUSIC, the launch of 14:47). The flag reads the jukebox’s HAS_RECORD state and a disc that has played to its end is still in the box. With the disc left to finish, the tamed Msc/Msc mare began the next care day at bondToday 1 and over its first 6,000 ticks went 56 → 70, one point a beat up to the cap, while the jukebox read “HAS_RECORD true, not playing” at all thirty readings. So one disc in a jukebox is an unattended source of bond, bounded only by the daily cap of fifteen. Whether it should be is the owner’s call, and is #217. The two earlier readings of this (2026-10-02 and 2026-10-08 morning) said nothing: the mare was still at the day before’s cap, which was a defect of its own (#216). The pen was deleted on this reading.

Bond as something a gene can touch

Bond has so far been entirely about what the player does. This is the first locus where the horse has an opinion about how it would like to be won over, and it opens a category the mod has not used: genes that change the husbandry rather than the animal.

What needs verifying here

Music enjoyer’s bond, its cap and its controls are confirmed, and a finished record is known to go on paying; what is left is the hearts a player should see.

Still open (NOT played)

The gene is one Bond ability on an interval, gated on the near_jukebox world flag, and the yard has watched it earn bond and stop at the cap (§Verified). The flag reads the jukebox’s HAS_RECORD state, not whether it is playing.

In the game, with your hands

  • The hearts show. The yard read the bond number, not the particles. Stand a tamed Msc/Msc horse by a playing jukebox. Pass: hearts every MusicEnjoyerGene.INTERVAL_TICKS until the day’s cap, and none on an untamed or carrier horse beside it.
