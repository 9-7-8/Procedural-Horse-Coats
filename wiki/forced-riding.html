Forced riding — the kelpie

A wild horse that lets you mount, and then will not let you off. It carries the rider to deep water, and the rider’s only say in it is whatever warning they were given before they climbed on. It was asked for, and researched before it was deferred; the research went almost entirely into how to hold a rider, because the obvious way — cancelling the dismount key — is the way that does not port. None of it is built.

Forced riding — the kelpie

A wild horse that lets you mount and then will not let you off, carrying you to deep water. Researched as a temporary ride-lock state rather than an attempt to cancel the dismount key: the horse stays an ordinary rideable entity, and while the state is active the server re-mounts the rider the moment they dismount and drives the horse toward its destination. That is the version that survives a version port, because it leans on normal passenger mechanics plus server enforcement instead of intercepting a client keybind.

Held on the horse as a small state machine — idle, a warning beat, then the forced ride — rather than as scattered booleans. The warning beat is the design question: without it this is a mount that kills you with no tell, which is a different and worse thing than a monster you can recognise. It also has to agree with the two seats a strong horse already carries — a forced ride with a pillion aboard is a case the ride-lock has to answer rather than discover.
