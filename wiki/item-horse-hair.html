Item / material

Horse hair

Horse hair is the base material for almost everything the mod adds, and the only one — there is nothing you refine it into first. You get it by shearing a horse, and nearly every other item on the wiki takes it directly.

How you get it

Right-click an adult horse with shears. You get 1–3 horse hair, the shears lose a point of durability, and the horse likes you a little more (+5 bond — see horse care).

You can shear each horse once per Minecraft day. Foals cannot be sheared. Keeping a few horses around is the practical way to have hair coming in.

A sheared horse still looks the same

The shearing works, but the horse’s appearance does not change yet, so there is no way to tell by looking which of your horses you have already done today.

There is no chain — hair goes in raw

Hair used to be the bottom of a ladder: four hair made a hair bundle, three bundles a braided rope, four bundles a hair cloth, and the things you actually wanted took the rope or the cloth rather than the hair. All three are gone. Whatever a recipe wants hair for, it takes horse hair.

What that changed, in hair

The ladder was quietly expensive, because the cost compounded. A rope was twelve hairs and a cloth sixteen, so:

ItemWasNow
Double fence gate2 gates + 12 hair2 gates, no hair at all
Equestrian post32 hair + fences + goods2 hair + fences + goods
Supplier’s post56 hair + fences + carrot4 hair + fences + carrot
Cowboy hitch68 hair + fences5 hair + fences

Hair is sheared one to three at a time, once per horse per day, so sixty-eight hair for one hitching post was several real days of brushing. Owner’s call.

How recipes work in this mod
  • Nearly every recipe is shapeless — the shape is not the puzzle. The one exception is the blank transfer paper.
  • Every recipe uses at least one modded item and makes a modded item, so nothing here can clash with a vanilla recipe. The one exception is the double fence gates, which are two vanilla gates and nothing else.
  • Nothing you craft is a trap, because there is nothing to convert and so no conversion to regret.

What needs hair

  • Breeding carrots and splice carrots — one hair each.
  • Whistles, tickets, seed jars and transfer papers.
  • All five village posts — the cowboy’s hitch and the four posts the equestrians work at — each a couple of hair and a couple of fences, plus whatever that equestrian works with.

Shearing

Right-click an adult horse with shears (server/HorseShearHandler): 1–3 horse_hair drop, the shears take a point of durability, and the horse gains +5 bond through HorseCareHandler.awardBondFor — so shearing is the one interaction that is both an economy input and a relationship input (horse care).

It is gated to once per 24 000-tick Minecraft day, which is the mod-wide rule for every time-gated action. The gate is a stored game-time stamp under the key "shear" on data/HorseCooldownsAttachment — a plain Map<String, Long> on the horse, shared with the yield verb’s per-gene cooldowns ("yield:<geneKey>"). Because it is stored on the horse rather than in a static map, it survives a reload and cannot leak between worlds.

The sheared look is unbuilt

The mechanic is complete; the visual is not built. It is settled as a low-fidelity render-layer overlay — it only has to read as vaguely different, so no coat variant and no extra bake — driven off the same "shear" cooldown stamp. See the roadmap.

The bundle, the rope and the cloth were removed

Three items stood above hair: horse_hair_bundle (4 hair), braided_rope (3 bundles) and hair_cloth (4 bundles), each with a reverse recipe. Everything substantial took a rope or a cloth rather than hair, so the real prices compounded out of sight — a double fence gate at twelve hair, a cowboy hitch at sixty-eight. All three items, their six chain recipes, their lang keys, models and item definitions are deleted, along with horse_hair_bundle.png (the rope and the cloth never had art of their own; both borrowed it).

Substitution was flat, one for one — one rope or one cloth became one hair — which is what makes the new prices so much lower. A shapeless recipe holds nine items, so preserving the old cost was not on the table even in principle.

Three progress tasks went with them: BUNDLE_HAIR, CRAFT_ROPE and CRAFT_HAIR_CLOTH were about crafting items that no longer exist, and a task nothing can complete is exactly what check-progress-tasks.mjs exists to catch. KnownGeneSpliceRecipe also stopped accepting cloth as an alternative to hair, and the husbandry tutorial step lost three of its five icons.

Six villager trades dealt in the removed items and were folded into the hair ladder rather than deleted. Both professions already improved their buy rate by level, and in raw-hair terms the intermediate trades had all been worse than the supplier’s level-1 hair trade — 32 to 48 hair per emerald against 16. The supplier now buys hair at 16 / 14 / 12 / 10 / 8 per emerald across its five levels, the leatherworker at 14 (level 2) and 10 (level 4), and the supplier’s level-1 sale of a bundle is now four hair for an emerald. See villagers.
