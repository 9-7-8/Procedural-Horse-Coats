Licensing

The code is MIT. The art is CC BY‑SA 4.0. A few pieces that ship with the mod are somebody else’s and keep their own terms. This page says that twice — once in plain words, once in the formal language — and then lists the exceptions file by file. The author is not a lawyer and does not sell this software; the licences are here so people know they may share and build on it, not to be defended in court.

Common questions

  • Can I put it in my modpack? Yes. Credit the mod; nothing else is asked. A paid modpack is fine as far as our own parts go — but see the third-party rows before you repackage the files they own.
  • Can I fork it and sell the fork? Yes for the code (MIT). The art stays CC BY-SA, so a fork that keeps our art must keep it under CC BY-SA and credit it. The three Raven icons may not be redistributed by anyone, forks included.
  • Can I use the code in a closed-source mod? Yes. That is what MIT allows. The only price is carrying the copyright line and licence text along.
  • Can I use the art in a closed-source mod? You can ship it in one, but the art itself stays CC BY-SA: credit it, and if you change a texture, the changed texture is CC BY-SA too. Your code is not infected by that.
  • What about the horses? A coat is drawn by our code from a genome. The picture it makes belongs to whoever made that horse; we claim nothing over it. Screenshots, videos and streams of the game are yours to share.
  • It used to say CC BY-NC. It did. Every old release is now also available under MIT and CC BY-SA, so nobody needs to hold on to the old terms. Both are more permissive about money than the old licence was.

Which files are which

  • Art (CC BY-SA 4.0): the PNG textures under assets/horsegenetics/ that we drew or generated, the item, block and gene icons and previews under wiki/assets/, the models, and the prose of the wiki and README.
  • Code (MIT): everything else we wrote, including the data and JSON that configure the game and the generators that draw coats.
  • Neither: everything in the table below.

Changes of licence

The project began under CC BY-NC 4.0 and moved to MIT (code) and CC BY-SA 4.0 (art) with the commit that added this page. The change is retroactive. The copyright holder grants every earlier release, tag, commit and jar — back to the first — under the same MIT and CC BY-SA 4.0 terms as the current version, with the same third-party exceptions. A licence already granted is not taken back, so an old copy may still be used under CC BY-NC 4.0 if you prefer; nothing about the new terms asks you to.

What needs verifying here

The licence of the three borrowed stable buildings, and whether the saddle overrides are Mojang’s or ours, have not been established.

  • The stables. Planet Minecraft submissions carry no uniform licence. Each of the three has to be read, and permission asked where it is unclear, before the mod is distributed widely. Tracked with the stables themselves on the Stables page.
  • The saddle overrides. The three minecraft/textures/entity/equipment/*_saddle sets may be derived from Minecraft’s own saddle art. If they are, they are not covered by CC BY-SA and the art notice and the Mojang row above should say so. (The horse coat sheets are already settled: Mojang’s, see THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md.)
  • The art/code split. The art claim is written from the folder layout. Check that no image under wiki/assets/ or textures/ was copied from elsewhere before treating it as ours.
