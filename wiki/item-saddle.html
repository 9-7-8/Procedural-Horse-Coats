Horse gear / the saddle slot

Saddles

On a horse the vanilla saddle is the bridle, and it steers. The saddle is this mod's own item, worn in the Saddle slot beside it: it makes the ride. While a player is aboard, a saddle adds a share of the horse's own speed and jump, up to 10% at the top tier. It comes in every material a chestplate is made of (leather, copper, chainmail, iron, gold, diamond and netherite), and in every material another mod makes a chestplate of. Horse armour now follows the same rule, which adds chainmail horse armour.

What a saddle is

A horse used to need one item to be ridden, and that item did everything. Now there are two. The bridle (what vanilla calls a saddle) goes on the head and lets a rider steer. The saddle goes on the back and carries the rider better. A horse can wear either, both or neither:

  • Bridle and saddle: you steer, and the saddle adds its bonus.
  • Bridle only: you steer, at the horse's own speed and jump.
  • Saddle only: below bond tier 3 you are a passenger; the horse walks where it likes, and the saddle's bonus is still on while you sit there.

Donkeys, mules and llamas keep vanilla's saddle; this one fits horses only.

The tiers

Every saddle is leather first. A metal saddle is a leather saddle with that metal's fittings, so you cannot make a saddle without leather. Its fittings are the colour of the matching chestplate. The bonus is a share of the horse's own numbers, so a saddle is worth the same proportion on a slow horse as on a fast one. With the default settings:

Getting one

Craft it, or buy the leather, copper, iron, golden or diamond one from the equestrian leatherworker. Horses in a generated stable, and horses you buy from a dealer with transfer papers, come already wearing a bridle and a leather saddle. When the tack bench is built, it will make every saddle instead.

Putting it on

Right-click a tamed adult horse with the saddle, or put it in the Saddle slot of the horse's Gear tab. Shears take it off, as they do vanilla's. The horse's own screen shows its speed and jump without the saddle, because the bonus belongs to the ride, not to the horse.

Horse armour in the same materials

Horse armour and saddles come in the same materials: every material a chestplate is made of. Vanilla already makes horse armour in six of the seven; this mod adds chainmail horse armour (6 iron chains and a horse hair), which protects as much as chainmail does, between copper and iron. Another mod's metal gets a horse armour only if that mod also makes a chestplate of it. If a metal used to have a horse armour and no longer qualifies, an old copy in your world loads as iron horse armour.

How it is built

The rule: common/gear/Saddles

Saddles.Tier is the seven shipped materials, each with its item id, its fittings colour (what vanilla's chestplate icon of that material averages to, measured from the 26.1.2 jar with MaterialScan.averageColour; leather under vanilla's default dye) and its share of the top tier's bonus (leather 0.25, copper 0.40, chainmail 0.50, iron 0.60, gold 0.70, diamond 0.85, netherite 1.00). Saddles.Rates holds the server's switch and the top tier's speed and jump, capped at MAX_TOP_BONUS. Saddles.bonus(rates, tier, ridden) is the only place the bonus is worked out, and it is NONE unless a player rides. tierOf(gem) puts another mod's material on the diamond or the iron rung, as the shoes and the armour do. Unit test: SaddlesTest.

The items: item/HorseSaddleItem

One item per material, registered from Saddles.Tier in ModItems.HORSE_SADDLES and from compat/ModdedSaddles for other mods. HorseSaddleItem.properties gives each one an Equippable for EquipmentSlot.SADDLE built like vanilla's Equippable.saddle(), except that its allowed entities are minecraft:horse alone and its asset is horsegenetics:saddle. Because it is a real equippable for the vanilla slot, isSaddled(), equip on right-click, shearing and the Gear tab's HorseTackSlot.SADDLE all work with no new path. Reins already treats anything real in that slot as "a saddle" (#74).

Drawing it

The equipment asset assets/horsegenetics/equipment/saddle.json has two horse_saddle layers: vanilla's seat leather (minecraft:saddle) and the hardware that sits on the seat (horsegenetics:saddle_fittings, the stirrups and their buckles). The bridle and the bit are not in it; they are the bridle's, drawn from the Headstall. saddle_fittings.png is the complement of bridle_metal.png within vanilla's hardware: 16 of its 36 texels, baked by tools/tack/bake-saddle-textures.ps1. client/TackClientExtensions tints layer 1 with the item's own colour and layer 0 with a bench-dyed seat colour if there is one. The icon is item/saddle (leather, untinted) under item/saddle_fittings (greys, tinted by client/MetalTintSource), drawn in tools/bake-drawn-items.mjs as a placeholder.

What it does: server/SaddleEffects

Two transient ADD_MULTIPLIED_BASE modifiers, horsegenetics:saddle/speed and saddle/jump, held at Saddles.bonus's answer. Nothing ticks: it runs on LivingEquipmentChangeEvent for the saddle slot, on EntityMountEvent (lowest priority, counting the player in or out by hand because the event fires before the passenger list changes), and on join. SaddleEffects.withoutSaddle takes the share back out, and HorseInfoScreen shows that number.

Horses that come tacked up

HorseTackSlot.dressWithBasicTack puts a bridle in an empty Headstall and a leather saddle in an empty saddle slot, on adult horses only. StablePopulator.spawnHorse calls it, and so does TransferPaperHandler when the paper's horse had no previous owner (a dealer's stock), as decided while scoping #72.

The chestplate rule: compat/MaterialScan

MaterialScan also collects every material some jar ships a <material>_chestplate item for (assets/<ns>/items/ or the older models/item/), into Result.chestplates. It also averages each chestplate's own icon, and chestplateMetals is armourMetals cut to those materials and recoloured to their chestplates (owner, 2026-11-07; a chestplate with no readable art keeps the ingot's colour). The shoes keep the ingots' colours. ModdedArmour, GeneratedArmour and ModdedSaddles read it, and ModdedShoes still reads armourMetals, so every metal keeps its shoe. An armour whose material has no chestplate goes into droppedArmours, which aliases it to iron horse armour (and its would-be saddle to the iron saddle). The fingerprint's rules number went to 5 and lists the chestplates, so packs generated before this are rebuilt. Chainmail horse armour is ModItems.CHAINMAIL_HORSE_ARMOR: chainmail's ArmorMaterial with its own asset, the shipped plate tinted chain grey, because vanilla's chainmail asset has no horse layer.

The settings

  • saddles.enabled (server, default on): off, saddles are worn and drawn and add nothing.
  • saddles.top_speed, saddles.top_jump (server, default 0.1, range 0 to 0.25): what the netherite saddle adds. Every other tier is its fixed share of these. A changed number reaches a horse the next time a player gets on.

What is tested

  • SaddlesTest: the ceiling, nothing while unridden, the ladder climbs, the config caps, and the ids.
  • MaterialScanTest: only a material with a chestplate gets armour and a saddle, the shoes keep every metal, the older model file counts and our own does not, and a new chestplate moves the fingerprint.
  • Gametest a_saddle_adds_only_while_ridden: fits a horse and not a donkey, chainmail armour fits the body slot, no bonus unridden, the top share while ridden, the screen's number is the horse's own, gone after dismount, and dressWithBasicTack.

Design reasoning

The bonus is small and capped, so breeding stays what makes a fast horse (owner, 2026-10-09: 10% at the top tier, set in config). It is a share rather than a flat amount so a good saddle never matters more to a poor horse than to a good one. It applies only while a player rides, because the treatment says the saddle's stats belong to the ride and not to the horse, and the horse's screen leaves it out for the same reason.

The materials are the chestplate's (owner, 2026-11-07) because a saddle is armour-adjacent leatherwork, and armour follows the same rule so the two never disagree about which metals exist. Gold sits above iron as it does for the shoes: the gold-fitted saddle is the show saddle.

What needs verifying here

Saddles and chainmail horse armour were built on 2026-11-07 with their fit, their rider-only bonus and the chestplate scan covered by tests, and nobody has crafted, bought, worn or ridden with one in a game, or run the chestplate rule beside a real mod.

The look (2026-11-07, NOT played)

  • Seven saddles in the creative tab, a brown seat with fittings in each material's colour, none purple-and-black.
  • On a horse: the seat and stirrups, with no second bridle drawn over the Headstall's. A leather saddle's fittings are brown; netherite's are dark.
  • Chainmail horse armour shows as grey plate, edged like the rest.

The ride (2026-11-07, NOT played)

  • Ride a bridled horse, then put a netherite saddle on it: the horse should be noticeably but slightly faster (about a tenth) and jump a little higher, and its screen's Speed and Jump should not change.
  • Get off: the horse's wandering speed should be its own.
  • A donkey should refuse the saddle on right-click.

Getting them (2026-11-07, NOT played)

  • Each recipe crafts in a survival grid, and the Recipes tab shows a line for each.
  • A novice equestrian leatherworker shows four trades, one usually the leather saddle.
  • A horse in a freshly generated stable wears a bridle and a leather saddle; a horse bought from the cowboy and redeemed does too.

The chestplate rule beside a real mod (2026-11-07)

  • In a pack with a mod that makes both an ingot and a chestplate of it, that metal has a horse armour and a saddle; a metal with no chestplate has a shoe only. The compat: log line counts how many have a chestplate. The synthetic mod (procedures/compat-test.txt) does not yet ship a chestplate, so it would now show no modded armour.
