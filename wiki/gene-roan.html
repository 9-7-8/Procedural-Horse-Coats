Natural gene / phase 1

Roan

White hairs mixed evenly through the body coat while the head, mane, tail and lower legs stay solid. At 128 px that is a high-frequency dither — each body texel is either a white hair or a coloured one — from a BodyNoise field sampled in body space so the mix carries across part seams. The cheapest pattern gene in the mod.

The whole gene

common/genetics/genes/RoanGene.java
double region = regionWeight(skin, part, point, frontStart, frontEnd); // 1 rear .. 0 head
double n = PatchNoise.fbm2(seed, x * FREQ, y * FREQ, z * FREQ * 1.6);
// a texel is a white hair OR a coloured hair - the *fraction* white varies
// smoothly with region. Near-binary with a ~1px soft edge, so the flecks read
// crisp but not aliased, and never through the gradient's warm mid-tones.
double threshold = 1.0 - region * (WHITE_FRACTION + density);
double w = smoothstep(threshold - EDGE, threshold + EDGE, n);
// on the soft edge pull red down faster than black, so a partial texel greys
// out instead of going gold.
f.setRed(px, py,  red   * (1 - w) * (1 - 0.6 * w));
f.setBlack(px, py, black * (1 - w));
Why not a plain fade to white

Scaling both pigment channels toward (0, 0) walks the sample down the gradient's gold diagonal - a half-whitened black texel comes out orange, not grey. So roan keeps the decision per-texel binary (white hair or coloured hair) with only a ~1px soft edge, and biases that edge toward the neutral axis. A fully white texel is setRed(0) / setBlack(0) exactly - the same "resolve to transparent" path splash and dominant white use.

regionWeight is 1 over the hindquarters, tapering forward from mid-barrel and feathering out across the neck, so the roan never ends on a hard line against the solid face; on the legs it fades to nothing toward the hoof. The head, mane, tail and ears are excluded outright.

Result

Blue roan on a black base, red roan on a chestnut, bay roan on a bay — the body reads as a lighter, hazier version of the base colour with a solid-coloured head and points. Density varies horse to horse from the second epigenetic draw.
