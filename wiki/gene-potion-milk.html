Magical gene / no coat effect

Potion milk

A mare who can be milked into a glass bottle rather than a bucket, and hands back a potion. One allele per effect. It does not touch the ordinary milk locus — a horse can have both, and which you get depends on what you are holding.

Built, and not yet seen in game

The gene, its inheritance and its founder table exist and are covered by the test suite; the translator that turns it into behaviour was written against the 26.1.2 sources and is flagged unverified in its own comments. Nothing on this page has been watched happening to a horse. What to look at, and what is most likely to be wrong, is on the Verification tab.

What it does

Right-click a tamed, grown mare with an empty glass bottle. A horse expressing this locus hands back a potion of whatever its alleles say. A bucket still gets you milk, if it has the milk gene; a bottle on a horse without this gene does nothing.

Hold a bottle out to a stallion carrying it and he rears and kicks you, and tells you only a mare gives potion milk. A foal tells you it has nothing to give yet.

Two of the brews are not a gift

Every allele at this locus used to hand back something a player wants — swiftness, strength, regeneration, and so on down the list. On 2026-09-25 Wkn (Weakness) and Drk (Darkness) joined it, and they are the first effects here that nobody would drink on purpose. They were added for the Nightmare, whose whole idea is a mare whose milk comes back cursed. Nothing about the horse, and nothing about the bottle, says which sort you are holding: a cursed potion looks exactly like a good one until you drink it.

The Nightmare is also the first breed ever to pin this locus. Everything else that expresses potion milk does so because a wild carrier passed it on, and the pair is whatever breeding happened to produce; the Nightmare is Wkn/Drk on every founder. That is the compound heterozygote from the table above, so one bottle from a nightmare mare comes back carrying both Weakness and Darkness at the weak grade — the first compound anybody can brew where getting both is the punishment rather than the prize.

An ordinary wild mare can hand you one

The founder table spreads one fixed carrier share evenly across however many brews the gene lists, so adding two did not make wild potion mares any commoner — it made each particular brew rarer, and it put two effects nobody wants into the pool a wild mare draws from. A wild mare found in a meadow, with no connection to the Nether or to the Nightmare, can now milk out weak Weakness or weak Darkness. It is not a defect and it is not being fixed; it is written here because the horse gives no sign of it and the only way to find out is to drink.

How often

Governed by the same milk volume locus that governs everything else the horse can be milked for, without either gene knowing about the other.

Health

None to the horse. Like the plain milk gene, a mare has to be at her own full health: a hurt one refuses, and says she won’t give anything until she has healed (owner’s call, 2026-09-10 — before that, potion milk ignored health entirely).

Same-kind yields merge — built, and never bottled

A compound heterozygote carries two yield abilities, both triggered by a glass bottle, and a translator that fired only the first would hand back a single-effect potion while this page promises two. The merge is built: GeneYieldHandler collects every yield of one kind, de-duplicates by effect id, logs and skips an unrecognised one, and builds a single PotionContents. Nobody has bottled a two-effect mare to see it, which is the one outcome here that can be half delivered without looking wrong — it is the first check on the Verification tab.

Everything else already exists

yield already takes consumes, so minecraft:glass_bottle is expressible today. minDose already gates the stronger half on two copies — its javadoc names incomplete dominance as the reason it exists. And declaring kind: potion hands governance to milk volume for free.

The brew list is a divisor as well as a list

The founder table is built as double each = WILD_CARRIER_PERCENT / brews.size(), with that same share given to every brew, so the length of the list is the wild rate of everything already in it. Appending is safe in the way passification's offering table is not — nothing here is an index the epigenome stores, so no existing horse is rewritten — but it is not free. The two brews added on 2026-09-25 diluted every brew that was already there, without a line changing anywhere near them. Quote WILD_CARRIER_PERCENT and brews.size() if you need the number; do not write it down.

Nothing here knows a curse from a blessing

Wkn and Drk go through the same bottle(...) builder as every buff: the same WEAK_DURATION and STRONG_DURATION, the same minDose ladder, the same merge. So a matched Wkn/Wkn pair is a stronger, longer Weakness, and “the clearest reason to double a brew line” in the strong expression's own text is now written on two alleles where doubling is the opposite of a reward. That is the honest behaviour and it is deliberate — the gene has no opinion about which effects are nice — but it is the sort of sentence that reads as a bug when somebody meets it in play.

Unknown effect ids must be skipped, not produced

Effect ids are resolved against the live registry, following lycan's rule that an id this game version has never heard of simply does nothing. An unrecognised id must make the yield not fire at all — producing a potion with no contents is worse than producing nothing, because it consumes the bottle.

Why the compound gives both

The alternative was lycan's rule — a mismatched pair yields nothing — and it was considered and rejected here. Lycan needs mismatch to bite because a shifting horse is a whole animal and picking one of two shapes is incoherent. A potion is not a shape; two effects in one bottle is a thing that already exists in the game, so the honest reading of two half-doses is two half-doses.

Two milk loci, deliberately

This gene does not extend or override milk. They are different loci with different alleles and different containers, and a horse may express both. The container is the disambiguator, which is why the trigger carries the item rather than the gene carrying a mode.

Incomplete dominance without an expression language

This locus is the clearest demonstration of what minDose is for. A gene file has no way to write “if homozygous”, so the format gives every effect a minimum dose instead: the weak potion declares 1, the strong one declares 2, and the horse’s own genotype decides which is active. No expression language, no per-gene Java, and the same trick is available to anyone writing a gene file.

What needs verifying here

Potion milk’s compound-heterozygote merge is now written — GeneYieldHandler collects every yield of one kind into one bottle — and nobody has ever bottled a two-effect mare to see it, which is the one outcome this page promises that can silently be half delivered; and since 2026-09-25 the locus can also hand back a curse, which has been drunk by nobody at all — neither the Nightmare’s Wkn/Drk compound nor the wild single copy the diluted founder table now allows.

The first brews that hurt you (2026-09-25, NOT played)

Wkn and Drk were added for the Nightmare, which pins them as a compound heterozygote. Both halves of that — a negative brew at all, and two of them in one bottle — are new, and neither has been seen. This locus has no test class of its own, only the suite-wide checks every registered gene gets, and BreedFilesTest proves no more than that the Nightmare’s file names a pair the gene recognises. Nothing anywhere covers what arrives in the glass.

  • A nightmare mare’s bottle carries both curses, not one. Tame and grow a Wkn/Drk mare to full health and bottle her. Pass: one potion holding both Weakness and Darkness, each at the weak grade. Fail: a single-effect potion — which is the merge half-firing, and is the same failure the Spd/Str check exists for, except that here it is even easier to miss: nobody looks closely at a potion they did not want.
  • Darkness actually resolves. minecraft:darkness is the newest of the ids this gene names and the handler is written to log and skip an id the registry has never heard of. Pass: the effect lands and the screen dims. Fail: a potion holding only Weakness and a skipped-id line in the log, which would look exactly like the merge failing and is not.
  • A wild mare can roll one, and it is not a defect when she does. The founder share is divided across the brew list, so Weakness and Darkness sit in the wild pool beside the buffs. Pass: across a decent sample of wild expressing mares, a cursed bottle turns up at about the rate any other single brew does. Fail: never, which would mean the two new alleles are in the allele list but not in the founder table.
  • Doubling a curse makes it worse, as it is written to. Spawn a Wkn/Wkn mare. Pass: one Weakness at the stronger grade and roughly double the duration. This is the locus behaving correctly and reading like a bug; it is here so that it is recognised as the former.

Still open (NOT played)

The yard’s DAIRY + CLIP pen covers the three refusals, and its stone floor is what keeps the hurt mare hurt long enough to reach them. It does not cover the merge: its only compound horse is a stallion, who kicks instead of yielding, so the two-effect bottle needs a mare from the custom horse spawn egg. Note also that a single copy already expresses, so a wild carrier of this locus is a wild producing mare.

Nothing here needs looking at to judge — do these first

  • A two-effect mare gives one bottle with both in it. Spawn a tamed adult mare with two different brews — say Spd/Str — at full health, and bottle her. Pass: one potion carrying both Swiftness and Strength, each at the weak grade. A single-effect potion here is the failure the whole merge exists to prevent, and it looks like a perfectly good potion.
  • A matched pair gives one strong effect, not two weak ones. Bottle a Spd/Spd mare. Pass: a single Swiftness at the stronger grade and roughly double the duration — not two Swiftness entries stacked in one bottle. The handler de-duplicates by effect id and that de-duplication has never been exercised.
  • The three refusals each say why. The DAIRY + CLIP pen’s hurt mare, stallion and foal, with a bottle. Pass: a message every time — she will not give anything until she has healed, he rears and kicks, the foal has nothing yet. The message is the evidence. All three correct behaviours are silence plus no potion, which is indistinguishable from a locus that was never registered at all.
  • A bucket and a bottle do different things on one horse. Spawn a mare expressing both this locus and milk. Pass: the bucket returns milk, the bottle returns a potion, and neither suppresses the other. The container is the only disambiguator, and the two loci are not meant to know about each other.
  • Milk volume governs the cooldown. Two expressing mares at different milk volume genotypes. Pass: the wait between bottles differs. The yield declares kind: milk specifically so this is inherited for free, and “for free” is exactly the sort of claim that turns out never to have been wired.
  • A wild mare of this locus gives a weak potion. Incomplete dominance means a single copy expresses, so the founder table’s carriers are producers. Pass: about one wild mare in ten hands back a weak single-effect potion, and no wild mare ever gives the strong grade or a mixed bottle — those are bred.
