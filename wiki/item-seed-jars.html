Seed jars

A jar that stores a stallion’s genetics so you can breed a mare with him later — from anywhere, and without him being there.

How to use one

  1. Craft an empty seed jar: glass bottle + horse hair.
  2. Right-click an adult stallion you own, in breeding mode (fed a golden carrot). The jar fills and is stamped with his genetics, plus any breeding carrots he was carrying.
  3. Right-click an adult mare you own, in heat — the stallion in the jar does not have to be yours. If it takes she is pregnant, and the foal comes when the pregnancy ends. The jar is used up either way.

A mare who is not in heat refuses the jar, and you keep it. Her information screen says how long until she is. The jar’s tooltip names the stallion it holds and says if it carries carrot effects, so a shelf of jars reads as a list of sires. The foal is bred exactly as it would be if the two horses had met, so everything on breeding & pedigree applies.

What it costs

Filling the jar uses up the stallion’s breeding mode, and counts as one of the three covers he can make in a day before his odds drop. A jar that does not take is still used up.

What the jar stores

The jar stores a GenomeSample — a genotype and an epigenome frozen to code strings — in a stored_genome data component, and the allele draw happens at conception, exactly as for every other pregnancy. StallionSeedJarHandler checks ReproHandler.receptive and calls ReproHandler.breed with the mare’s live genome, the jar’s stored one, and the jar’s carrot_effects as the sire’s. There is no second breeding code path. Filling calls ReproHandler.recordCover and disarm on the stallion. Full flow on fertility & gestation.

Known gaps

  • The synthetic sire record still uses the stored UUID as its pedigree edge, and its generation is 0; see the roadmap.

  • Feed the stud a stabilizer carrot, then a golden carrot, and fill a jar. The tooltip says Carries 1 carrot effect.
  • Use the jar on OUT OF HEAT: refused, jar kept.
  • Use it on IN HEAT: is pregnant or didn't take.
  • Clock the other mare into heat and use a second jar.
  • When that foal is born, its Family tab shows the stud as sire (the pedigree edge), and its generation counts his.
  • Finally, geld him with the vet's kit — that step is on the vet's kit, and a new jar is refused afterwards.
