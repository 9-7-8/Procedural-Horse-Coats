Seed jars

A jar that stores a stallion’s genetics so you can breed a mare with him later — from anywhere, and without him being there.

How to use one

  1. Craft an empty seed jar: glass bottle + horse hair.
  2. Right-click an adult stallion you own, in breeding mode (fed a golden carrot). The jar fills and is stamped with his genetics, plus any breeding carrots he was carrying.
  3. Right-click an adult mare you own, in heat — the stallion in the jar does not have to be yours. If it takes she is pregnant, and the foal comes when the pregnancy ends. The jar is used up either way.

A mare who is not in heat refuses the jar, and you keep it. Her information screen says how long until she is. The jar’s tooltip names the stallion it holds and says if it carries carrot effects, so a shelf of jars reads as a list of sires. The foal is bred exactly as it would be if the two horses had met, so everything on breeding & pedigree applies.

What it costs

Filling the jar uses up the stallion’s breeding mode, and counts against the covers he makes in a day before his odds drop (three by default; the server sets it, fertility.free_covers_per_day). It never stops him: past them he goes on filling jars and covering mares, at half the chance. A jar that does not take is still used up.

What the jar stores

The jar stores a GenomeSample — a genotype and an epigenome frozen to code strings — in a stored_genome data component, and the allele draw happens at conception, exactly as for every other pregnancy. StallionSeedJarHandler checks ReproHandler.receptive and calls ReproHandler.breed with the mare’s live genome, the jar’s stored one, and the jar’s carrot_effects as the sire’s. There is no second breeding code path. Filling calls ReproHandler.recordCover and disarm on the stallion. Full flow on fertility & gestation.

Assisted reproduction: the rest of the seed jar, and the later tiers

Finishing the seed jar

The cut-down flow is built and written up on breeding. What is left:

  • A collectedFrom marker (player-made vs villager-sold) and the “no Known Gene Splice carrot on a jar” recipe restriction.
  • This is what the scientist is missing. His trade table shipped without the two rows that wanted a vial — selling a random rolled genotype, and buying a player-made one back — because both need collectedFrom and a vial worth rolling. His buy row takes a stallion seed jar at every tier instead. Adding the rows later is two JSON files and, for the buy-back, a components predicate on the wants block, which the trade format already supports.

The later tiers

Embryo extraction, gene screening, gene splicing, cloning, cross-species. Each is a block with an inventory and a screen, and each is endgame by intent. Three notes that change how they should be scoped:

  • The gene sequencer is not new work — it is the browser or the family-tree screen pointed at a vial or an embryo. Build it as a view, not a machine with its own UI.
  • Gene splicing is the first thing in the mod that writes an arbitrary genotype in survival. It needs the same validity predicate the creative spawner needs — is this allele pair legal, does this locus exist — and that predicate should be written once and shared.
  • Cross-species is much larger than its row. Donkeys and mules are separate vanilla entity types, mules are sterile by design, and neither carries any of this mod's genetics. Extending the genome to another species is a project, not a tier. Scope it separately or cut it.

  • Seed-jar breed labels. A jar foal from a pure mare and a pure sire of another breed reads as their cross, and one from a feral donor reads plain Mixed.
  • Seed jar collection refusals. Clicking an unfed stallion with a jar does not also mount him. An empty jar on a mare, or a filled jar on a stallion or foal, names the horse and does nothing. A filled jar keeps its sire across a reload and a chest.
