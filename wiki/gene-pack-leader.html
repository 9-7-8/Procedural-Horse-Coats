Magical gene / no coat effect

Leader of the pack

Something follows this horse. Which something depends on the allele — there is one for every non-hostile mob in the game — and they trail it within sixteen blocks wherever it goes. Two copies of the same allele or nothing.

Built, and not yet seen in game

The gene, its inheritance and its founder table exist and are covered by the test suite; the translator that turns it into behaviour was written against the 26.1.2 sources and is flagged unverified in its own comments. Nothing on this page has been watched happening to a horse. What to look at, and what is most likely to be wrong, is on the Verification tab.

One allele per mob

Every vanilla mob in a non-hostile spawn category, minus the horse family — the shared roster's peaceful half, whole. That is a mechanical rule rather than a taste, which is why it contains a pufferfish and a wandering trader without anyone having to defend either.

It is wider than lycan's, which is the same list with everything that swims or flies taken out. The difference is which way round the horse and the animal stand: a squid that follows a horse about is a fine thing to breed for, and a horse shut inside a squid drowns.

Chaos: an animal from another mod

One more allele, Chaos (Cha), stands for every peaceful animal another mod adds. Two Chaos copies lead one kind of modded animal — which kind is written on the horse’s copies, worked out from the mods your world has loaded, and it can change if the modpack does. Both copies must carry the same line: two different Chaos carriers crossed give a foal that leads nothing, exactly like two different vanilla alleles. Only peaceful animals are chosen (fliers and swimmers are fine, as above); a server can take a mod or one animal off the list in its config. With no modded animals installed it does nothing. See lycan, which has the same allele.

What you can catch

Carriers, and only carriers. No wild horse is ever homozygous here, so the first pack leader in a world is one somebody bred. Two carriers of different alleles produce nothing, which is most crosses.

Health

None — though a horse trailed by twelve chickens is a horse you can hear coming.

Continuous pathfinding for the followers is the real cost

Every follower recalculates a path on its own schedule, and pathfinding is the most expensive thing a mob does. A long interval and a low max_targets are not tuning, they are the design. Followers also drag chunks busy as they cross boundaries behind the horse.

Do not inject a goal into another mod’s mob

A goal added to an arbitrary mob has to be taken off again, and a horse that dies, despawns or stops expressing leaves it behind on every creature it ever met — the exact lifecycle failure mob aura already avoids by shoving rather than adding an avoidance goal. Nudge navigation per beat instead; it is stateless and it cannot leak.

The allele set is free

MobRoster already holds this list — token, mob id, label and habitat per entry, with the ids as plain strings resolved against the live registry. Read the roster rather than writing a second list that will drift from it. This locus takes peaceful() whole; lycan takes only ground().

The group filter must use entity type tags

Five genes share this filter. A hardcoded list makes every modded creature invisible to all five at once, and the failure is silent — the aura simply never fires on anything from another mod. Build it on tags in the change that introduces it.

A third mode, not a new verb

mob_aura already has repel and attract. Following is follow, on the same verb, with the same cap and the same beat. A summon verb was specified early in design and folded into this instead once it became clear that spawner was the only gene that genuinely needed one.

Why a matched pair

Same reasoning as lycan: a locus with dozens of alleles where any one showed would be a slot machine, and a locus where you must find the same allele twice is a search with a target.

Chaos (2026-10-01)

Cha sits after the last vanilla allele and before n, and the copy carries a chaos seed — the locus’s first epigenetic value. The shared base, AbstractMatchedPairGene, does the work for both mob loci on it: a matched Chaos pair whose two seeds agree hands its mob_aura the subject chaos:<seed> in the ordinary mob field, a disagreeing pair hands it nothing, and foundersShareSeeds makes a fresh horse one line. The game module swaps the subject for a real id at each beat through ChaosRoster’s PACK list — modded mobs that are not a MONSTER spawn category and not hostile on inspection. The design and the reasons it is one allele rather than one per mob are on lycan’s page; the seed field and the info-screen line are on its Roadmap tab. ChaosMobLociTest pins the common half.

Verified

The retinue costs nothing measurable (2026-09-13). A pen of four leaders and sixteen cows, twenty-four mobs re-pathing on a forty-tick beat, held a flat 50.0 ms per tick with 213 entities in the dimension, which is its baseline. A stable with several pack leaders in it is fine.

A retinue forms and follows, a mismatched pair leads none, and the cap holds (test yard, pen PACK LEADER, the run of 2026-10-02 03:52 on a56afcbb, three clockwork PASS lines). A Cow/Cow horse re-pathed cows to itself on 21 of 21 beats; the ten cows’ mean distance fell 9.3 → 4.8 while it shuffled, and the eight within 6 blocks then walked 13 blocks east after it, ending 2.9 from it against 12.3 had they stood still (03:53:55). No beat re-pathed more than 6 cows (MAX_TARGETS), on 21 beats with up to 10 in the radius (03:53:55). Before it, a Cow/Shp horse walked the same steps past the same cows and drew none to its block in 10 samples (03:53:07).

One allele list, two genes

Lycan turns the horse into the mob; this makes the mob follow the horse. The same list serving two opposite ideas is a good sign the list was derived from the right rule — and it means adding a mob to the game adds an allele to both loci without anybody deciding to.

What needs verifying here

Leader of the pack is confirmed cheap and working — a retinue forms, follows, stays capped, and a mismatched pair leads none — but its followers are not released at once when the horse dies, and the Chaos allele (2026-10-01) has never met a modded mob.

Chaos (2026-10-01, NOT played)

  • A modded retinue. In a pack with another mod’s peaceful animals, a Cha/Cha horse from the spawn egg gathers one kind of them, and the [chaos] ... pack leader count in the log is above zero. No modded monster is ever on that list.
  • Nothing installed. In a plain install a Cha/Cha horse leads nothing and logs no modded animal available for PACK once.

Still open (NOT played)

The gene is mob_aura in follow mode, and the translator issues the follower a path per beat rather than adding a goal to it. That matters twice: a pathfinder beats a velocity nudge, which is why the mirror-image repel aura had to be rewritten after three unattended hours proved a shove does nothing; and a path expires by itself, which is the whole argument that this cannot be left behind on another mod’s mob. The yard has seen the paths work and the cost stay flat (§Verified); the expiry is what is left.

Nothing here needs looking at to judge — do these first

  • They give up outside the radius. The yard saw cows walk after a Cow/Cow horse (§Verified) but never led it away from them. Ride it well past sixteen blocks. Pass: the followers stop and go back to grazing.
  • It does not create them. Take an expressing horse somewhere with none of its mob at all. Pass: nothing happens — and record it as the gene working. The allele description says this outright because it is the single most likely reason to file the locus as broken.
  • Nothing is left behind. Kill or remove the horse while a retinue is following it. Pass: the followers go back to wandering at once, and no mob keeps walking toward where the horse was. A leak here is the exact lifecycle failure the stateless design exists to prevent, and it would only ever be noticed as mobs behaving oddly long afterwards. Failed in the yard (2026-10-02 03:53:59): one and two beats after the leader was killed, two and then one cow were still walking out the last 2–3 blocks of their path to the block it died on — the path expires, but not at once.
  • No wild horse leads anything. The founder table for a matched-pair locus puts only carriers in the wild. Pass: you find carriers at roughly WILD_CARRIER_PERCENT and never a wild horse with a retinue.

In the game, with your hands

  • The gene row reads a mismatched pair. The yard proved a Cow/Shp horse leads no cow (§Verified), but had no sheep and did not read the screen. Open one’s info screen beside sheep. Pass: no sheep follow, and the gene row shows the mismatched expression — most crosses between two carriers land here.
  • It reads as a retinue rather than a tow rope. Ride one at speed with a following pack. Pass: they trail behind and drift, plainly untamed and plainly not commanded — the tone the page promises. A pack that moves in lockstep with the horse is the wrong feeling even when the mechanism is right.
