Magical gene / no coat effect

Singer

Occasionally, and without warning, the horse plays music. Which record depends on the allele; how long and how loud are written on the allele copy. It plays the opening and then stops — which is the part you would recognise anyway.

Built, and not yet seen in game

The gene, its inheritance and its founder table exist and are covered by the test suite; the translator that turns it into behaviour was written against the 26.1.2 sources and is flagged unverified in its own comments. Nothing on this page has been watched happening to a horse. What to look at, and what is most likely to be wrong, is on the Verification tab.

Why only the opening

A record in Minecraft is one long sound that can only be played from its beginning. There is no way to start in the middle, so “a section” can only ever mean the first few seconds. The duration is epigenetic, so a line can be bred toward a longer excerpt.

A herd of singers is an audio denial-of-service

Sound is cheap per firing and unbearable in aggregate. Several expressing horses in one pasture will overlap constantly, and there is no volume setting that fixes it. One at a time per area, with a cooldown — the arbitration is part of the gene, not a refinement.

Stopping a sound needs a per-listener packet

Playing is a one-shot; stopping partway is a stop-sound packet sent to everyone who can hear it. Cheap, but it is client state, so a listener who arrives mid-track and one who leaves must both end up correct.

Disc ids from other mods resolve live

Following lycan: a disc this game version has never heard of falls silent rather than erroring. An allele for a modded disc is legal and simply does nothing without that mod.

An engine limit written into the design

The original specification said “plays sections of a disc”, which is not possible. Rather than approximate it with note-block phrases — considered, and rejected because referencing music people already know is most of the charm — the gene takes the limit as its shape: the opening, for an epigenetic length. Recorded here so the impossible version is not attempted again.

Verified

The rate is exactly as specified (the night of 2026-09-12 to 13): two horses on a 1 200-tick interval made 4 × music_disc.cat per two-minute census, every census, with no drift.

Deliberately frivolous

It does nothing useful and it is not meant to. The magical side of the mod is allowed a few loci whose entire return is that they are funny to breed for, and a horse that will not stop playing Cat is one of them.

What needs verifying here

Singer’s firing rate is measured, so what is left is everything around it: only music_disc.cat has ever been heard out of fifteen disc alleles, the cut-off stop is a packet sent by sound id to every player in the level, and nothing in the code limits a herd of singers the way this page says it does.

Still open (NOT played)

The rate is settled — see the Verified note on the Coding tab — and every remaining question is about which horses sing, which discs reach the game, and what happens when more than one of them does it at once. The readout for all of that is the census’s sound tally, which counts firings per sound id since the last census and then resets, so a herd is arithmetic rather than a matter of taste.

Nothing here needs looking at to judge — do these first

  • All fifteen disc alleles actually resolve. An id this build does not register falls silent after one [genes] effect 'sound:...' is defined but not translated line, and only Cat has ever been seen to fire. Spawn one homozygous horse per allele and leave them a few censuses. Pass: no such log line for any of the fifteen, and the census tally names fifteen distinct sound ids. An allele that logs once and is never heard again is exactly the shape of a silent no-op.
  • Two different songs really do cancel. Spawn Cat/Blks alone in a pen. Pass: its gene row says the horse settles on neither song, and its pen contributes nothing to the census sound tally over several censuses.
  • What a herd actually costs, since the arbitration this page describes is not in the code. The only gate is a per-horse, per-gene cooldown of SingerGene.COOLDOWN_TICKS, which is shorter than SingerGene.INTERVAL_TICKS and so can never refuse a firing the interval already allowed; there is no per-area arbitration at all. Put five singers of different discs in one pen. Pass: the tally is what the arithmetic predicts — around five firings per census, scaling with the herd — and the verdict to record is whether that is bearable, because nothing is stopping it.
  • A stop by sound id does not cut the wrong horse off. The cut-off is a stop-sound packet naming the disc id, sent to every player in the level, so two horses singing the same disc share one switch. Put two Cat/Cat horses in one pen. Pass: each one’s excerpt runs its own length. An excerpt that ends the instant the other horse’s does is the shared-id stop, and it stays invisible until two horses happen to match.

In the game, with your hands

  • It is the opening, and it is cut off. Stand by a Cat/Cat horse and wait. Pass: the record starts from its beginning and stops partway, between three and eleven seconds in depending on the copy — not at the end of the track, and not left running.
  • Duration and volume differ between horses, and both are inherited. Spawn several singers of one disc, then breed a pair. Pass: the excerpts are audibly different lengths and loudnesses, and a foal is close to a parent rather than a fresh roll.
  • A loud singer is heard further away, not louder nearby. A volume above 1.0 extends a Minecraft sound’s reach rather than its loudness at the source. Pass: the loudest singer is the one you can still hear from the far fence.
  • A listener who arrives or leaves mid-track ends up correct. Walk in while a horse is singing, and out again. Pass: no track left playing in your ears after you leave the pen, and nothing stuck on when you come back.
