2026-09-14 — a wild-type copy carries no epigenetics

    Owner: “wildtype for most genes should not carry any epigene data”.
    It started as a log-size question: the spawn trace's epigenome was most of a 58 KB line
    per horse. Then the owner asked for the whole epigenome back in the log once it had shrunk.

    - Surveyed before building. For every gene that stores epigenetics, is
        the copy on its default allele ever the expressed copy in a non-wild outcome? None is. So
        “most” is “all” today, and the survey is kept as
        WildCopySurveyTest, with Gene.wildTypeCarriesEpigenetics() as
        the opt-out for a future gene.

    - One rule, in Genome's constructor
        (Epigenome.alignedTo): a wild-type copy is emptied
        (AlleleEpigenetics.NONE). A variant copy that arrives empty, from forced
        eyes or an editor changing an allele, gets deterministic numbers. Every horse is built
        through Genome, so saves, packets and codes all shrink. A code writes an
        empty copy as - and skips a gene whose copies are both wild.

    - Readers that took a copy by slot without checking the allele were found and
        fixed. These were the weather loci (read before the allele check), the
        info-screen readout (now "—"), and the breed founder's band stamping. The rest
        already checked the allele: stats, fighter, size, milk, meat, molten hooves, particles,
        sectoral heterochromia.

    - The survey missed one reader, and breeding threw on it.
        WhitePatternEyes.blueIf rolls the eye spread off a white locus’s copy
        whenever the whole horse passes the white threshold, even when that locus is wild. An
        empty copy now asks for nothing (owner: “eyes should default to brown if nothing
        else describes them”). No horse loses its blue: tobiano alone is under the
        threshold, so a horse past it always has a variant white locus to roll.
        WildCopySurveyTest.theEyeForceNeverReadsAnEmptyCopy runs every white
        combination through the eye force.

    - And it missed fertility, which crashed the owner’s world at the
        first natural cover (FertilityGene.fertility, 08:51). The wild outcome
        changes nothing visible, but an n/n mare’s number is her fertility,
        so the survey’s question could not catch it. Fertility now keeps its wild numbers
        (wildTypeCarriesEpigenetics). As a backstop, every read of an empty copy
        returns the gene’s schema midpoints (Epigenome.readable) instead of
        throwing, so any other unseen reader gets a default rather than a crash. The earlier
        claim that “most” meant “all” was wrong by one gene.

    - The whole genome is back on the [trace] horse-spawn line, as
        only what the horse has. The genotype is Genotype.presentCode(): it leaves
        out every gene at two wild-type copies that do nothing (owner: "that gene just doesn't
        exist"), keeps carriers and always keeps sex, and parses back to the same genotype. The
        epigenome carries numbers only on variant copies.

    - Getting started caught up with breeding and band life
        (TutorialPage). There are two new steps: heat, pregnancy and foals (natural covers, the
        crowding cap, nursing and weaning, the vet's kit, gelding, seed jars), and horses living
        in bands (the Social section, family and bachelor bands, dispersal, takeovers, and tamed
        sparring, grooming, displacement and a dam's defence). The existing steps now mention
        geldings, that horses breed on their own, and that a breeding carrot waits on a horse and
        makes a pregnancy. The shearing bond was corrected to two, from the five it said. Every
        number was checked against the code. The checklist's tasks were left alone: none was
        wrong, and a new one needs a hook.
