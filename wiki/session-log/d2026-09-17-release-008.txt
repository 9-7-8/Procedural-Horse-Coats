2026-09-17, late — A long run of small things, and 0.5.008 out the door

    Nine commits and a release, mostly of things that change what an ordinary horse
    or an ordinary village is like rather than adding anything new to find. The through-line
    was not planned: four separate items turned out to be a case of the mod contradicting
    something it had already written down about itself.

Breed spawn eggs get art, without becoming forty-five items

    Forty-five drawn eggs came in. The item cannot be one per breed — a breed dropped
    into .minecraft/phc/breeds/ after the jar was built has no registry entry
    and must still get an egg — so the art is chosen by a
    minecraft:select on the breed component's value, with the old
    vanilla-egg model as the fallback every other breed takes.
    tools/bake-breed-eggs.mjs derives the cases and the per-breed models from
    the textures on disk, and hard-fails on a filename that is not a real breed
    id — two arrived spelled “gypsey vanner” and
    “halfinger”, and a case on a value no horse carries never matches and never
    draws, which is the kind of failure that ships.

Brindle was contradicting its own explanation

    The owner's note said brindle was “backwards and inconsistent” and should
    need two copies. Making it so turned up why it read as inconsistent: the gene's
    stated cause is X-inactivation mosaicism — the pattern is a record
    of which X each patch of skin silenced, which is the whole reason the two sides of the
    horse never match — and a stallion has one X, nothing to silence, and cannot be a
    mosaic at all. Yet under the old one-copy rule he was the common case: 2% of
    wild stallions against 0.04% of mares.

    So expressionOf now counts to two instead of asking “is every real
    copy Brn”, and the difference is the gene. Segregation is untouched; only which
    genotypes paint moved, and the crosses invert accordingly — a carrier mare's sons
    go from 50% brindle to none, a Brn/Brn mare's from 100% to none, and the
    only road to a brindle filly now needs the allele from both sides. The frequency went
    from 2% to 10% per X because p of one sex is a far smaller number than the old
    one-copy rate: at 2% the only horses that could show it were one mare in twenty-five
    hundred. The pipeline golden moved exactly six rows, all of them
    brindle=Brn/Y, and the new hash is byte-identical to the carrier's
    — which is the proof that the stallion form now paints nothing.

The horseman needed no mixin, and that was the thing worth finding out

    Breed eggs on his counter were drawn from every breed in the game, so a Scandinavian
    village sold Marwaris. The fix wanted to know which horseman was offering, and
    the trades are fully data-driven — no ItemListing, no event to hook.
    Reading 26.1.2's own sources settled it: AbstractVillager builds the trade
    loot context with THIS_ENTITY set to the villager and
    LootContextParamSets.VILLAGER_TRADE declares it required, so the loot
    function can simply ask. It reuses the nearest-cowboy scan that already decides his
    surname, so nothing new is stored and a cowboy who moves in changes the counter.

    Where a region has no breed in a price band the trade is dropped rather
    than widened or filled from the world pool — owner's call, taken against a measured
    number: three of the ten regions have nothing in the commonest band. Both alternatives
    lie, one about the price and one about the place.

Twins, appetite, mutation

    - Identical twins. Every twin pregnancy was two independent draws, so
        every pair was fraternal. One pair in ten is now identical, and the owner's call was
        true clones — the second embryo is not drawn at all, it IS the first,
        epigenome included. Two things then fall out rather than being coded: an identical
        pair can never be a colt and a filly, and it shares its lethal draw.

    - Hunger follows the body. Linear in the scale, and the javadoc says
        why it is not the metabolic exponent: real intake goes as mass0.75 against
        a body that cubes with scale, which would put the smallest ponies past a week between
        meals. The honest reason is that the hunger pool is a fixed 100 for every horse, so
        what the number says is how fast a stomach-ful goes.

    - Mutation. One bred foal in a thousand takes a magical allele from
        nothing; one in a million takes two. Foals only, which keeps
        MagicalVariant's rule intact. The roll sits at the end of the foal draw
        so it shifts no other gene's inheritance, and before the eyes are forced so a mutated
        gene that asks for an eye colour is honoured. Detection for the checklist compares
        against both parents rather than storing a flag: it under-reports and never
        over-reports, because every other allele a foal owns came from a parent.

Blight, four markings, and an empty intake folder

    Blight is verdant's opposite and reuses its machinery
    entirely — the same spread verb, one new vocabulary word, and the
    judgement about which blocks may be eaten staying on the game side where it belongs. It
    will not touch a crop or a planted sapling, the same line melt draws around
    packed ice.

    Four markings intaken. Two had to move priority: they were written at 263 and 264, both
    taken, and 250–298 is solid, so they sit at 248/249 and paint about fifteen places
    earlier than intended. Every coat in the golden is byte-identical; the
    1,092 rewritten lines are genotype code strings that grew a locus, and not one hash
    moved. The design documents that were sitting in intake are roadmap 24.10–24.13,
    the two tool gaps filed with the markings joined the mask-parameter list, and
    Drag.md turned out to be already covered — better — by 24.4.

The parity check had been red since flying shipped

    Running it for the first time in a while found the creator's schema mirror missing
    flight_true, flight_glide and cloud_walk as well
    as the new cover. Nothing had caught it because nothing runs the check but a person.
    That is gap 269, and it is a different shape from
    the stale-fixture problem: the oracle was current and correct, and simply was not
    consulted.
