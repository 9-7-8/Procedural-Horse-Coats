  target changes and ReachCheck.approaching(). That form is strict within 12 blocks. Past
  12, a short path still counts while its end is 2 or more blocks from the horse, because
  createPath only searches about the follow range (16, vanilla's Mob default; not checked
  against a horse's own attribute table, and flagged in the class). Without that, a foal
  would stop following its dam across an open field. Every goal above asks the check when
  it picks a partner and again in canContinueToUse. HeatAttraction tries the 3 nearest
  candidates and takes the first one it can reach. WildHerdGoal resets the cache only when
