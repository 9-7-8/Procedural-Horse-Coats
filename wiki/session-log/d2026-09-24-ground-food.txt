2026-09-24, after the bond-reach fix — a dropped carrot is food, the same as a planted one

    One bullet, the next in the Bugs section of intake/To do.txt, in the owner's words, and
    written twice in a row because she queued it and then wrote it up:

        Queue: horses should eat food dropped on the ground near them, if it's in their diet.
        Horses should eat food dropped on the ground near them when the food is part of their
        diet.

    Dated against the code first, which is the step that decides these. The behaviour is not
    absent: HungerFoodGoal has had a DROPPED rung since 2026-09-14, and the 2026-09-15 yard
    run logged a horse eating a dropped chicken. But the bullet is from 2026-09-23, nine days
    after that, and it comes off the heavily modded server she actually plays on. Newer than
    the fix, so: open, and go and read the code.

The set was the hand-feeding set

    The rung asked GeneAbilityHandler.eats — "would this horse take this from a hand?" For a
    special diet that is DietFoods.accepts, which is right. For an ordinary horse it falls
    through to vanilla's horse.isFood, which in 26.1.2 is #minecraft:horse_food: wheat, sugar,
    hay_block, apple, carrot, golden_carrot, golden_apple, enchanted_golden_apple. Eight items,
    all vanilla. On a modded server, almost nothing anybody drops is in it.

    It is wrong without mods too, and that is the sharper way to see it. rungOf sends an
    ordinary horse across a pen to eat a *planted* potato, beetroot, melon or sugar cane —
    none of which are in that tag — and then the item rung had the same horse walk past the
    same potato lying at its feet. One food model, two answers, depending on whether the food
    was in the world or on the floor.

What changed

    DietFoods.acceptsFromGround is the ground question, beside accepts, which stays the hand
    question and is unchanged. For NORMAL and ANYTHING it is the item form of every rung the
    horse already walks to: c:crops, c:foods/fruit, c:foods/vegetable, c:foods/berry,
    c:mushrooms, #minecraft:small_flowers and cake, plus #minecraft:horse_food itself and any
    mod's bale through HayBales. Common tags rather than an item list, so another mod's grain
    or fruit is food without this mod having heard of it — that is the modded-server case, and
    it is also the only shape that does not need editing every time she adds a mod.

    A narrow diet is untouched and still answers accepts. A wheat-eater walking past the rest
    is the whole point of the diet locus, so the widening is fenced to the two diets that eat
    broadly anyway.

    Two things fell out of it:

    - HungerFoodGoal.search now asks with the HorseDiet it resolved once for that horse.
      GeneAbilityHandler.eats calls HorseDietHandler.dietOf itself, so the old line parsed the
      genotype and epigenome once *per dropped item examined* — inside the loop over every
      ItemEntity in a 10-block box. That is precisely the cost this class was rebuilt on
      2026-09-14 to avoid, reintroduced through a helper.
    - grazeInPlace takes a dropped mouthful within 2 blocks before it takes the grass. The ask
      was "near them", not "when starving": a horse below three quarters full standing on a
      dropped carrot should take it rather than wait to fall under Hunger.HUNGRY. It costs one
      entity query on the one-in-1,200 roll the grass mouthful already pays for.

    Every tag key was read out of the 26.1.2 sources before being written (ItemTags.HORSE_FOOD
    and SMALL_FLOWERS; Tags.Items.CROPS, FOODS_FRUIT, FOODS_VEGETABLE, FOODS_BERRY, MUSHROOMS).
    Their *contents* were not, and DietFoods says so in the comment.

Not played

    wiki/horse-care.html grew #ground-food on the Coding tab — the two questions, why they are
    different, and the tag list — and #ground-food-0924 on the Verification tab with six
    checks. The one that matters is on her own server: drop another mod's crop by a hungry
    horse and watch for the "ate <modid>:<item> (dropped)" line. The last check is a worry
    rather than a defect — a player emptying crops out of a chest beside a pen now loses them
    to the horses, and if that is a nuisance the answer is a rule about how long food has lain,
    not a narrower list.

    The Gameplay tab's food list gained the ground/hand distinction and the grazing line now
    mentions dropped food.
