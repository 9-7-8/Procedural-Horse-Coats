2026-09-24 — the Horse Stasis Bank: stage two, and almost all of it was the shelf again

    Stage two of the six-stage build order on wiki/horse-stasis.html, picked up straight from
    intake/START HERE.txt's one-bullet-one-session rule with the chambers' bullets already
    retired: the bank block, its block entity, its menu and its screen, with the Storage grid
    and nothing else. The Browse tab, passive healing, the Advanced drop buffer, Spacer in-bank
    breeding and the emergency chamber are all still planned and all still on that page's
    Roadmap tab.

    Fifty-four slots — the shelf's grid, a double chest's worth — that take a stasis chamber of
    any tier, full or empty, and refuse everything else. Crafted from five planks in a U, an
    ender pearl in the middle, two iron ingots in the top corners and a horse hair between
    them, which is the recipe the Roadmap tab had already specified. Any wood, by
    #minecraft:planks, so it is not a per-wood block family and none of the double-gate
    bake-twice machinery applies.

There was nothing new to invent, and that was the finding

    Stage one's lesson was that capture/release already existed as the lycanthropy swap. Stage
    two's is smaller and the same shape: a bank is EquineResearchShelfBlockEntity with one
    container instead of three and a different slot predicate. The setChanged forwarding, the
    SimpleContainer the menu wraps on the server against an empty copy the client's slot sync
    fills in, preRemoveSideEffects for the drop-on-break, ValueOutput/ValueInput with
    ContainerHelper.saveAllItems — every one of those is the shelf's, copied deliberately
    rather than re-derived. The screen needed no texture either: VanillaPanel draws the window,
    the bevel and every sunken slot procedurally, so the whole of the art for this block is one
    blockstate pointing at cube_column wearing vanilla's barrel textures.

    The one thing the bank does that the shelf does not is count what is in it. The title row
    reads "empty", then "n chambers" while only empties are filed, then "n horses of m". That
    is a loop over the slots rather than anything synced, and it is free precisely because of
    the decision stage one made for a different reason: a chamber's component carries the
    horse's whole entity tag to the client, so the client can already see which slots are
    occupied.

Three things left out on purpose

    No ticker. Nothing in the bank advances with nobody looking yet. Passive healing is stage
    four and is what will want one, in the research shelf's server-only createTickerHelper
    shape; adding the override now would have been a hook with an empty body.

    No ContainerData. There is no progress bar and no server-side selection to sync. The count
    is read, not sent.

    No tab bar. The page's Roadmap tab described the menu as opening with two tabs "the way the
    Equine Research Shelf's menu already does", and the honest reading of stage two — "the
    Storage tab only (a filtered chest)" — is that a single-tab tab bar is a control that does
    nothing and a second tab the owner can click into and find empty is worse than no tab at
    all. So the chrome waits for the thing it selects. What the Coding tab now carries instead
    is the list of the three pieces to copy when the Browse tab lands: ResearchShelfScreen's
    Tab enum and tabAt, VanillaPanel.tab, and the menu's setStoreTab/activeOnTab pair — the
    last being the load-bearing one, since it is why every slot stays live on the server and
    only the client hides the tab it is not drawing.

Two deliberate divergences from the chest it resembles

    It is not flammable. Vanilla's chest takes ignitedByLava() and this does not, which is one
    omitted call on the reasoning that mod blocks are absent from FireBlock's flammability
    table unless they register themselves. The justification is worth stating because it is a
    gameplay call and not a technical one: a chest that burns down loses its diamonds, and a
    bank that burns down would drop fifty-four pedigreed horses into the fire that did it. The
    reasoning is also unverified in game and now says so on the Verification tab — set fire to
    one, drop one in lava.

    It is not a Container. The block entity holds one rather than implementing one, exactly as
    the research shelf does, so hoppers and pipes cannot reach it. Automating the filing of a
    live animal is not something anybody asked for, and not exposing the inventory is the
    cheapest way to not have it.

    A third, smaller one: the slot predicate takes any tier, empty or occupied. A bank that
    refused a Basic chamber would have inverted the whole ladder, since the bottom rung is the
    one that has to be cheap enough to actually get used — and a rack of spare empties beside
    the full ones is the obvious way to use the block.

What was run, and what the port collision cost

    :neoforge-26.1.2:build green. runGameTest green, all 8 including every_recipe_encodes, so
    the new shaped recipe survives the whole-recipe-set login packet — the v0.5.014 shape that
    only a real join catches. check-recipes.mjs 132 files with no collision,
    check-block-models.mjs 250 model ids all present, bake-recipe-reference.mjs re-run so the
    Horse Browser's Recipes tab has the bank in it with a .desc line.

    runServer was worth a note. It loaded clean all the way through the gene registry freeze,
    1647 recipes (up exactly one) and 1729 advancements, then failed to bind port 25565 —
    because the owner's own live server, running out of C:\Users\Isabel\26.1.2-neoforge-server,
    already holds it. Nothing to fix, but it means the in-repo runServer cannot boot to Done(
    while she is playing, and the signal it would have given about world load came from the
    gametest server instead, which prepared a spawn area and started with no errors. Worth
    remembering before treating a bind failure as a mod problem.

    Nothing has been played. The bank has never been placed in a running game, so the slots,
    the count, the drop-on-break and the cost of opening a bank full of horses are all newly on
    wiki/horse-stasis.html's Verification tab, along with everything stage one left there.
