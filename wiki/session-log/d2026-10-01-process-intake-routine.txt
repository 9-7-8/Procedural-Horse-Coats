    Owner: "Can you help me scope a new procedure and create a markdown file for it, called

    then end the session. You should start with the smallest, highest impact things first, then

Done

    No code. .claude/skills/process-intake/SKILL.md, invoked as /process-intake. It is
    written from START HERE.txt and the intake sessions that built things from treatments
    (antlers, the breed climate rule, Chaos at the mob loci). What it adds:

    - The unit is a treatment's piece or slice, not the file, so a three-piece treatment
      offers three picks.
    - Every unit is scored on size (S/M/L, where L includes a foundation that other units
      wait on), impact (finishes something shipped, then an unblocker, then new content),
      whether it needs the owner, and what it depends on.
    - The tiers, in the order she gave: small with high impact, then anything that needs no
      owner call, then the big ones.
    - An UNVERIFIED line needs the source read, not the owner. An Open design line, a breed
      design or a licence call needs the owner.
    - Owner questions are asked once, as options, before any code. With no answer, the run
      drops to the best unit that needs none.
    - Retiring follows START HERE step 3. A draft folder retires with its treatment and
      never on its own. potential-assets/ is never a unit.
    - It ends with CLAUDE.md's routine and names the next unit by the same ranking.

    The routine deliberately names no current item. The queue is read fresh every run.
