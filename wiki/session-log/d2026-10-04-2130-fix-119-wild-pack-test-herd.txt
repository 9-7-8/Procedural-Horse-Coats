  clearing disabled the test was red ("removed DISCARDED ... chunk ticking true, herd <the
  intruder>"); with it, all 55 gametests passed. After the intruder was removed, the real
  suite ran 6 times: this test was green 6/6. Issue #119 is CLOSED, with the proof comment.
