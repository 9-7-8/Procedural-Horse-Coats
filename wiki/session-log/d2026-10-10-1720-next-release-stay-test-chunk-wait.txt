2. Fix #245, commit aba25435. Issue CLOSED, with the proof in a comment.
   What was wrong: ModGameTests.orderStayWalksBack forced the 3x3 chunks round its 1x1
   structure (aloneOnClearGround -> keepTicking) and, in the same tick, spawned its
   horse two blocks out and moved it three more. The harness waits only for the
   structure's own chunk, and the grid lands at a different place against the chunk
   borders every run, so the horse was often in a neighbour chunk whose forcing had not
   taken effect. Gametest ticks are unpaced (0.4 to 6 a millisecond), so now and then
   the whole 300-tick budget went by before that chunk ticked. The horse was still age
   1 and falling at the timeout, which is the state the issue's failure line prints.
   It is the mechanism of #70 and #150; this test had never been given their wait.
   The fix (test code only; nothing a player runs changed):
   - The test calls keepTicking, then spawns nothing until fieldTicks says all nine
     chunks tick, from inside its own succeedWhen, as order_hunt_picks_a_monster and
     order_guard_holds_its_reach do. aloneOnClearGround and the spawn follow the wait.
   - Its budget went from 300 to 1300 ticks, so the paced wait has room.
   - The "did not stay moved" premise check moved from runAfterDelay(2) into the wait
     (read once the horse's tickCount reaches 2; helper.fail, so it is not retried).
   - The failure message now prints chunkTicking for the horse's chunk.
   Proof (20 temporary copies of the test, each in its own batch by registerAlone, with
   temporary [temp245] log lines; all deleted before the commit):
   - Stock code, the red: it did NOT come back. 0 red in 190 cells (9 runs of
     ./gradlew :neoforge-26.1.2:runGameTest, 80 tests each).
   - Stock code, the precondition: in one logged run, the horse was spawned with a
     field chunk not ticking in 14 of 21 cells (its own chunk in 2 of them, the
     anchor's chunk in the other 12). Every horse thawed within 2 ticks that run.
   - With the wait: 168 of 168 cells spawned with both chunks ticking, and all 168
     passed (8 runs). Longest field wait logged: 7 ticks, 372 ms.
   - Copies removed: ./gradlew :neoforge-26.1.2:build green; check-parity OK (7163
     checks, 60 cases); two plain runGameTest runs, "All 60 required tests passed".
   The live server was checked before the runs (up, 0 players each time).
