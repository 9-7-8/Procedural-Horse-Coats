2026-09-24, after the Log tab — the last kinship rule in the mod is deleted

    The first bullet in the tuning section of intake/To do.txt, once the Bugs section had
    emptied:

        there should be no mechanic that inherently stops incest (aka line breeding) even
        in wild bands

    Retired from the file. The owner confirmed mid-session what the bullet already implied:
    this is to *remove* any kind of incest prohibition, not to tune one.

Dated first, as START HERE says

    A hit straight away, and the shape START HERE warns about. BandLife.closeKin refused a
    dispersing filly any bachelor suitor who shared her dam or her sire, or who was her
    sire — shipped 2026-09-15 as gap 234, at the owner's own request ("no suitor sharing a
    parent"), verified in the unattended run of 2026-09-18, and written up on horse-care as
    a Verified record.

    So the two dates decide it, and they are unambiguous: the rule is 2026-09-15, the
    bullet is 2026-09-22 (f4fc3e40). The bullet is newer than the fix, which makes it a
    reversal and not a stale report. The owner used the mod on her server for a week and
    changed her mind about the rule she asked for. Nine days of life.

    Nothing else gates on kinship. The conception path — ReproRules, NaturalCover,
    NaturalBreedingHandler — never reads motherId or fatherId at all, so player breeding was
    never touched and did not need a change. closeKin was the whole of it.

What went

    BandLife: the !closeKin(horse, h) term out of the dispersing filly's suitor predicate,
    and the method with it. A comment stands in its place saying what used to be there and
    why it is not, pointing at breeding.html#no-kin-gate — the next person to read that
    predicate and think "surely she shouldn't take her brother" needs to find the decision,
    not re-derive it.

    DebugYardHerd: the LEAVING HOME pen existed to ask this exact question, and the pen's
    sibling wiring existed only so that closeKin had records to read. Both gone, and the
    siblings() helper with them — its javadoc said outright that BandLife.closeKin was the
    only caller that cared. The pen stays, because the dispersal underneath it is still
    worth watching; its wall labels no longer say "she must NOT take her brother", because
    now she may, and a test yard that asserts the opposite of the code is worse than no pen.

    TutorialPage: the Grooming and company step told the player the rule in so many words.
    Rewritten — a filly leaves for another band or for a bachelor who founds one with her,
    her brother as readily as anyone. Two chapters along, "Line breeding, and what it costs"
    already said there is no inbreeding penalty and nothing refuses a stallion his own
    daughter; that was true of player breeding and is now true everywhere, so it needed no
    change, which is a small piece of evidence that the tutorial was written honestly.

    HorseRecord.withParents: its javadoc named closeKin as the reason it exists. Reworded to
    what the method is actually for.

What stayed, deliberately

    horse-care.html#verified-closekin is kept, retitled "withdrawn", because three other
    things link to that anchor and because the record is mostly not about kinship. It is
    about a test pen that answered the easy half of its question and read exactly like one
    that answered the whole thing: the colt was staggered three minutes behind his sister,
    so he had not left when she chose and was never a candidate, and the rule sat unexercised
    for three days looking verified. That lesson outlives the rule that taught it.

    The horse-care reality table's dispersal row now says "Partly, and deliberately" — the
    mod models female dispersal but not the inbreeding avoidance it exists for in the wild,
    and that is a design call rather than a gap. Anyone reading the BLM sources beside the
    code would otherwise file it as one, repeatedly.

Where the decision lives

    breeding.html#no-kin-gate, on the Known limitations list where "no inbreeding prevention"
    was already written as a fact about the world. It now says it is a decision, dated, with
    the withdrawn rule named — decisions.html is a pointer page these days and says on its
    face not to add decisions to it.

    The horse-care Verification tab lost its "A filly is not recruited by her brother"
    check outright: not resolved, moot. Its summary sentence ended on that same check and
    now ends on the thing that is actually still unseen — band life has never run outside
    the test yard, on real ground, at real distances.

Regenerated

    verification.html, roadmap.html, search-index.js, wiki/text/. check-links green across
    404 pages, check-roadmap-execution green. :neoforge-26.1.2:build green.

    common/ changed by one javadoc comment, so the designer wasm was deliberately not
    rebuilt — the build is not reproducible and a comment cannot move the compiled graph.

Not done, and worth knowing

    Nobody has watched a wild filly disperse outside the yard, before this change or after
    it. That is on the horse-care Verification tab as the one remaining band-life check.
