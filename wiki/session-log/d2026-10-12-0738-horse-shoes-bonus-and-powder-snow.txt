#247 Horse shoes - piece 2: the stat bonus and powder snow. Commit 05b69991. CLOSED.
  What was missing: after #164 a shoe could be bought, worn and seen and did nothing.
  What it does now:
  - Each worn shoe adds a flat amount to movement speed and jump strength from its own
    metal, summed over the four hooves. The rule is pure, in common/gear/HorseShoes:
    bonus(rates, fl, fr, hl, hr) -> Bonus(speed, jump); Rates carries the family's switch
    and every metal's two numbers, capped per shoe (MAX_SPEED, MAX_JUMP).
  - server/HorseShoeEffects reads the four hoof slots and holds the sum as two transient
    ADD_VALUE modifiers (horsegenetics:shoes/speed, shoes/jump). It runs when a hoof slot
    is written (HorseTackSlot.set, Zone.HOOVES) and on EntityJoinLevelEvent for a shod
    horse. It does not tick. Nothing new is saved, so hard rule 10 is untouched.
  - HorseShoeItem carries a tier (a HorseShoes.Metal): itself for a shipped shoe,
    HorseShoes.tierOf(gem) for a modded one (gem -> diamond, ingot -> iron).
  - mixin/PowderSnowShoesMixin: one shoe of any metal holds a horse up on powder snow.
  - ServerConfig: shoes.enabled, and shoes.<metal>_speed / _jump for copper, iron, gold,
    diamond, netherite; read together through ServerConfig.shoeRates().
  - The tooltip states the server's own numbers for one shoe, and the powder snow line.

DECIDED

All made without the owner. The issue said "names and numbers are the building
session's". Each is recorded on #247's closing comment and on the page's Coding tab.
- Scope: #247 is size L, so this run built the bonus, powder snow, config, tooltip and
  page, and split the enchantments off (process-intake tier 3; next-release step 5 keeps
  a remainder the stage cannot ship without on the same milestone).
- The default numbers, per shoe: copper 0.002 speed / 0.004 jump, iron 0.003 / 0.006,
  gold 0.004 / 0.008, diamond 0.005 / 0.010, netherite 0.006 / 0.012. Four netherite
  shoes are about +1.0 m/s and +0.3 m for an average horse: small, so that speed stays a
  matter of breeding.
- The per-shoe caps: 0.05 speed, 0.15 jump. No cap across shoes (owner, 2026-10-09).
- Event-driven, not per tick: the modifiers are refreshed on a gear change and on load,
  as HorsePackHandler.refresh does. The price is that a changed shoes.* number reaches a
  horse when it next loads or is next shod.
- shoes.enabled off turns off powder snow as well as the bonus. A metal set to 0 still
  holds a horse up.
- Config key names: shoes.enabled, shoes.<metal>_speed, shoes.<metal>_jump.
