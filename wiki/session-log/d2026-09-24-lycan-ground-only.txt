2026-09-24 — lycan keeps only the animals a horse can be found in again

    One intake bullet, the next one in the "Magical loci and modded content" group: "LYCAN's mob
    list currently includes aquatic mobs (water creature/water ambient/axolotl/underground water
    creature) and other categories that read as flying or hostile. Remove aquatic mobs, hostile
    mobs, and flying mobs from LycanGene's allele set and blacklist those categories/ids so they
    can't come back in."

    Step 1 of the intake routine first: the session log and the verification index were searched
    for the bullet's keywords, and the bullet is newer than everything they hold. The mob list was
    exactly as described — thirty-seven hand-written form(...) calls, alphabetical, with a
    pufferfish and a happy ghast on it.

Why the rule is different for this locus than for the other two

    Three genes have an allele per creature. Pack leader makes the mob follow the horse and the
    spawner makes the horse produce them — in both, the animal turns up beside the horse, and a
    squid or a bee is a perfectly good thing to breed for. Lycan replaces the horse. That is the
    whole difference, and it is why the same roster cannot serve all three unfiltered: a were-cod
    is a horse drowning in air on a hillside, and a were-bat is over the treeline two seconds
    after dusk with somebody's mare inside it. Both were observed, not imagined — the 2026-09-14
    log has two pufferfish forms drowning on land, and the 2026-09-15 investigation found three
    horses that fell out of the world as bats and a parrot.

    Fifteen shapes went: axolotl, cod, dolphin, glow squid, nautilus, pufferfish, salmon, squid,
    tadpole and tropical fish for swimming; allay, bat, bee, parrot and happy ghast for flying.
    Nothing hostile was ever on the locus. Frog, turtle and strider stayed — all three walk, and
    none of them suffocates on land.

The blacklist is a tag on the roster, not a list in the gene

    MobRoster.Entry gained a Habitat (GROUND / WATER / AIR) and LycanGene now derives its forms
    from MobRoster.ground(), which is the migration MobRoster's own javadoc had been putting off
    since the class was written: "Lycan has not been migrated ... its allele order is baked into
    the genotype code and into two checked-in goldens - moving it is a real change with a real
    diff, not a tidy-up." The allele set was moving anyway, so the real diff was already being
    paid for. The two copies of the list that were warned about can no longer drift, and a mob
    added to the roster as WATER or AIR is kept off the locus by existing rather than by anyone
    remembering the rule. The article ("a wolf", "an ocelot") is read off the label's first letter
    now instead of being a hand-written column, since the roster does not carry one.

    The second enforcement is LycanthropyHandler.isWearable, and it is the one that will still
    hold when the roster is derived from the live registry (the next bullet in the same group). At
    dusk the resolved entity type is asked whether it swims (the four water spawn categories),
    flies (AMBIENT, FlyingAnimal, a FlyingPathNavigation or a FlyingMoveControl) or is hostile
    (Enemy or MONSTER, through MobGroups.isHostile, the same broad test the aura genes use), and a
    body that fails refuses the shift and logs once. AMBIENT is in the flying list because
    vanilla's bat flies without a flying navigation or the interface to show for it — flagged in
    the comment as unverified in game, and the safe direction to be wrong in.

    That supersedes a settled call, which is worth saying out loud: on 2026-09-13 the owner
    overruled a guard that refused an aquatic shift on land — "It should absolutely still shift
    and then just die." The guard is back, and it is not a reversal of that reasoning: the fish
    alleles no longer exist, so what it now catches is only a body that reached the locus by
    mistake.

What it cost elsewhere

    Two goldens were the whole risk and only one moved: coat-bake-golden.txt lost exactly the
    fifteen rows, and the pipeline golden did not move at all, because the locus paints nothing
    and stays out of the texture key. Genotype codes are a different length at that locus, which
    is a save-breaking change the mod's own rules permit and which nothing was written to
    soften.

    The test yard lost LYCAN BAT. It had been put in the LYCAN ROUND TRIP pen on 2026-09-18 as the
    yard's only flying shifter, to exercise the dawn branch that has to put a horse back on the
    ground from the air — and that branch can no longer be reached by breeding at all. Open check
    243 closed with it: the set-down guard stays, because a walking animal still finds ledges,
    boats and a floor mined out from under it overnight, but the flying cause of it is gone. The
    record moved onto the gene's Coding tab, keeping the #gap-243 anchor the old known-gaps index
    still points at.

    Pack leader's page said its allele set "is exactly lycan's"; it is not any more, and now says
    which way it is wider and why. The tester script's were-cod step is gone. A new Verification
    entry says what has never been played: no shifter has been caught in the wild since the
    change, the runtime refusal has no way to fire in a vanilla game, and nobody has checked what
    a horse carrying one of the fifteen deleted alleles does when its world is loaded.

    Re-baked: the six gene tasks, bake-gene-timeline (no change), the designer wasm (common
    changed, so it is a real rebuild rather than a data-only one), the verification and roadmap
    indexes, the search index, agent text and check-links. :neoforge-26.1.2:build green,
    check-parity green, LycanGeneTest and both goldens green. sync-page-views found nothing.
