2. Fix #244, commit 86f10208. Issue CLOSED, with the proof in a comment.
   What was wrong: LycanthropyHandler.onWereAnimalDeath restores the horse and kills it
   with the blow that killed its animal form, then falls back to genericKill if the
   horse is still alive. A full-health horse has its last stand armed.
   HorseLastStandHandler saved it from the first blow (health 1). That saved blow still
   left vanilla's hurt cooldown (invulnerableTime 20, lastHurt Float.MAX_VALUE). The
   genericKill source is in bypasses_invulnerability, not bypasses_cooldown, so the
   fallback was refused. The horse stood at 1 health, shifted again within two seconds,
   and died only when the second form died (live server, 0.5.045, two horses).
   The fix:
   - LycanthropyHandler keeps the horse it is killing in a private static
     (dyingWithItsForm), set only for the two hurt calls, cleared in a finally.
   - HorseLastStandHandler.unsurvivable now also answers true for that horse
     (LycanthropyHandler.diesWithItsForm), on both the incoming and the killing-blow
     event. Its refusal line says "its animal form died".
   - The cooldown is cleared (horse.invulnerableTime = 0) before the fallback, so
     nothing that refuses the first blow can block it.
   The horse now dies of the blow that killed its form, so its death line names the
   real cause, not "was killed". The fallback is left for gene immunities.
   A test door was added: LycanthropyHandler.shiftInto(horse, level, type). shift() was
   split, and its second half is the new private wear(); shiftInto calls wear() with a
   chosen body, so a gametest gets a real shifted animal without a lycan genome or
   nightfall.
   Proof:
   - New gametest were_animal_death_kills_the_horse (ModGameTests): a founded,
     full-health horse is shut in a cow by shiftInto, the cow is killed with
     damageSources().generic(), and the horse must be dead of that same source.
   - Red with the two fix lines commented out: "the horse outlived its animal form at
     1.0 of 21.02916 health [invulnerableTime 20]". This run was both the reproduction
     and the break-on-purpose.
   - Green with the fix: ./gradlew :neoforge-26.1.2:runGameTest, "All 60 required tests
     passed", and the log reads "[phc-live] lycan | FORM DIED ... as minecraft:cow of
     generic ... | horse died of the same blow".
   - ./gradlew :neoforge-26.1.2:build green; check-parity OK (7163 checks, 60 cases);
     check-links OK.
