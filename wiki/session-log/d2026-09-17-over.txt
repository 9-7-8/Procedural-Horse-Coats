2026-09-17, last of all — A layer can say it paints over the ones above it

    Two pieces of work, and the first one is why the second was findable. The owner asked
    what was next on the roadmap, and the honest answer was that the top of the page was
    not answerable: its first item pointed at a verification entry that had been
    deleted because the thing was confirmed, and several others were checks
    rather than work. So the page was audited first, and then the real top item was built.

The roadmap was carrying nine things that were not work

    The roadmap is unbuilt work; to be verified is
    built-but-unseen. Nine entries had drifted across that line — balance
    questions and “go and look at it” checks about things already in the game.
    They were a trap rather than clutter: a session reading the top of the page for
    what do I build next found items it could not build, only look at.

    - Five moved to a new §0-EW,
        ranked below the behavioural checks by that page’s own rule, since each
        announces itself the moment anybody looks: the themed splices’ per-theme
        weighting, a scaled foal against Skin.BABY’s approximate
        projection, the animations at scale, whether the twelve eye wedges read as
        heterochromia or as a stray pixel, and leopard’s first-guess spot and blanket
        constants.

    - Four were deleted rather than moved, because the check already
        existed and a third copy is how three sources of truth drift apart: the
        browser’s untested tabs (gap 37), breed
        pool rates and world horse density (gap 31,
        verbatim), the unplayed health layer, and founder and wild-spawn frequency —
        which the owner parked on gap 214, so
        moving it would have quietly reopened a closed call.

    - Two stale things fell out of the audit. §21’s
        “split the corridor” had shipped that morning and should have gone with
        it, and the page’s own top item still pointed at the dialled genes, whose
        verification entry is gone because they were confirmed.

Then the thing at the top, which was a format change

    A magical gene’s layers each measured their delta against the colour the
    gene started from, and the deltas summed. Authors — human and model
    alike — write files as though each layer painted over the one above it, because
    that is what layers mean everywhere else, and the format had no way to say it. So they
    wrote the thing that looks like stacking and got summing: a solid black dorsal wing, a
    white contour, a starburst with no core, and eight of the twenty-four genes in the
    2026-09-10 intake, every one rewritten disjoint by hand. That is
    gap 169, and
    "over": true is its root cause fix.

    - The arithmetic falls out rather than being imposed.
        toward() returns a step from whatever view it is handed, so a step
        measured from the accumulated colour lands the layer on the colour it asked for,
        while a layer without the flag still measures from the gene’s start and sums
        exactly as before. The engine copies the idiom
        SpecPainter.restrict already used ten lines above — snapshot,
        apply the delta so far, hand that to the layer — and going through
        apply() rather than adding channels by hand means a FLAT
        layer underneath reads as the flat colour it set.

    - Opt-in is the whole design, and it was provable. No shipped gene
        sets the flag, so CoatBakeGoldenTest and DeadLayerTest
        pass with both golden files untouched. That answers the question the
        roadmap entry had parked — whether expected.json and the goldens
        move — without a migration: only the fixtures gained rows.

    - The five-file contract was the wrong template.
        SpecSchema holds mask and op parameters only — a layer’s
        allowed keys live in GeneSpecParser.expectKeys — and the designer
        inherits common/ through TeaVM. What it did need and the
        contract does not mention: the creator’s exporter whitelist, where an unknown
        key is silently dropped, and a ColorField.mutableCopy that only
        PigmentField had.

    - Parity coverage was demonstrated, not assumed. Prismatic gained an
        over wash across the barrel, all four BODY probes moved,
        and breaking the creator’s branch on purpose turned the check red on
        124 comparisons before it was restored — the step
        pointsMin nearly shipped
        without.

    - Two refusals, both load errors that name the reason:
        over on a natural gene, where restrict already re-reads
        between layers so the flag would change nothing, and on a FLAT layer,
        which replaces the texel anyway. A flag that quietly means nothing is how an author
        talks himself into believing a layer does something it does not.

    - The payoff is deliberately not taken. No real gene uses it yet.
        Dorsal wing, contour, starburst and the eight nested intake genes each want judging
        on a horse, so they belong after this is proven rather than inside the commit that
        built it.
