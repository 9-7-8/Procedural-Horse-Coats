DECIDED
-------
- "a" (everything) Aggression pairs unlock the combat orders (owner). Recorded on
  wiki/gene-aggression.html#ordered-pursuit and item-whistles.html#combat-code.
- Calls made without the owner (recorded on item-whistles.html#combat-code):
  - NOT_A_FIGHTER is asked before NOT_BONDED: the bond cannot change the genes.
  - Magic fighter counts when its Combat ability is above the 3.0 baseline: Fighter,
    Champion, or a Balanced horse whose gladiator copy outweighs its wimp copy. Weak and
    Harmless do not.
  - The gate reads only the three named genes, so a data-driven gene with a temper does
    not open it.
  - Between fights a hunter stands at its spot like a stayed horse; it does not roam.
  - Picked by nearest to the leash's centre, line of sight required (as the locus does).
  - Owned pets and tamed horses are never picked (a tamed zombie horse counted as hostile).
  - Vanilla's Mob home radius (setHomeTo / isWithinHome) exists in 26.1.2 but is not used:
    vanilla saves it and a lead sets it.
  - LEASH_SLACK_BLOCKS = 4, so an edge monster is not picked and dropped every second.
