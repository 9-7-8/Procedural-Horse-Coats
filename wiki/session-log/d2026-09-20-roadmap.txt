2026-09-20, later — The roadmap stops being four lists in a trench coat

    Wiki only, by instruction: no code was touched. The roadmap had
    grown to a little over three thousand lines and only a fraction of it was a roadmap. It
    carried defects in shipped code, questions waiting on the owner, a long record of settled
    calls under “do not reopen”, and — the reason it kept growing —
    shipped work that had been annotated rather than deleted. Whole sections
    opened with a green note saying the section was done. The owner’s instruction was
    blunt: nothing in there but planned future features.

    The settled calls became a page. They are the largest single thing that was
    in the wrong place and the one with the clearest claim to existing somewhere: roughly five
    hundred lines of “this was asked, this is the answer, here is why”, which is
    exactly the material a later session re-litigates if it cannot find it.
    Design decisions now holds them, regrouped by subject rather
    than by the date they happened to be made — what the mod deliberately is not doing,
    the authoring calls, the coat pipeline, the genetics model, health and aging, care and
    the gameplay layer — with the dated batches kept underneath in their original words.
    The open questions went with them, including four that were scattered through the backlog
    as if they were tasks: the champagne foal’s eye colour, the studbook taxonomy, what
    a breed’s country field is for, and the provisional calls made
    overnight that nobody has confirmed or reversed.

    Five defects moved to known gaps (292–296):
    the leopard complex not reading the coat it is handed, the one-tick default-black wild horse,
    the equipment that does not follow Attributes.SCALE, the hemizygous locus that
    prints as a pair, and the Java comments still pointing at roadmap anchors this change
    deleted. That last one is the price of a wiki-only session and is written down as such:
    four one-line comment edits nobody can make from here, and no check that would catch them,
    because check-links.mjs does not read Java.

    Two things were stale rather than merely misfiled. The roadmap still listed
    the saddle not fitting a resized horse; it was fixed and confirmed in a world on 09-16, and
    the items page has the write-up including the wrong
    diagnosis that preceded it. And the pipeline page still said
    EDNRB’s frame band and every cover knob were whole-horse fractions.
    Checked against EdnrbGene rather than against the prose: the band is measured
    on the barrel’s own bounds and paintFrame sorts the field and cuts at the
    quantile matching cover. Both claims were deleted. The roadmap had
    recorded both as done and nobody had propagated it, which is the failure mode the
    delete-don’t-annotate rule exists to prevent, caught in the act.

    Also written up rather than dropped: how to write a gene’s science tab
    is technique now, including the rule that none of
    it is ever written from the model’s memory. The roadmap keeps the list of which tabs
    are still thin, without the word counts — those were a derived number sitting in prose,
    which is the repeat offender.

    The result is 1,700-odd lines of planned features, sections named and not numbered, and four
    named destinations in a note at the top so the next thing that does not belong has somewhere
    to go. CLAUDE.md’s routing table gained the new page and the roadmap rule
    was rewritten to say “planned features only” rather than “unbuilt
    work”, which had been quietly wide enough to admit all four lists.
