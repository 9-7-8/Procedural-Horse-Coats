2026-09-18, after Cloudtouched — Carts, and a stat that finally does something

    A cart mod arrived in intake/ — UsefulCarts, MIT, four ports deep from
    MennoMax's AstikorCarts — with a brief saying to merge it in. The brief was about
    licences and entrypoints. The interesting half of the job was not in it:
    every horse in that mod pulls a cart at exactly the same speed, and this
    is a mod about horses that differ.

The rule, and the test that fixed it

    The model is one line: pull sets a ceiling, speed
    decides how close you get to it. Haul speed comes out as
    speed × capacity / (capacity + load × speed / BASE_SPEED), which
    approaches BASE_SPEED × capacity / load and never passes it. A weak
    horse is capped on a wagon however fast you breed it; a strong slow horse never reaches
    the ceiling its shoulders could carry; a balanced horse beats both
    specialists. That last property is the requirement — the owner's brief said
    outright that speed must not become a dump stat on draft breeds — and it is a test,
    not a claim.

    The test earned its keep on the first run. The model originally had a flat
    floor on retention: never below a quarter speed, so a hopeless horse still visibly moved.
    pullSetsACeilingSpeedCannotPass failed immediately, and the reason is worth
    keeping: past the speed at which that floor engages, haul becomes
    speed × 0.25 and climbs without limit. A fast enough
    weak horse out-pulls its own capacity, and pull is a dump stat again at exactly the margin
    the model exists to defend. The floor is now a clamp on the score instead, at
    MIN_PULL, which does the floor's actual job — never pin a horse —
    without touching the asymptote.

    That is the second time this week a test has caught a design error rather than a typo,
    and both times the error was a special case quietly destroying a general property.

Carts in every wood, by the gates' own contract

    Upstream keyed carts on vanilla's WoodType from a hand-written table of
    twelve, which is precisely why a modded wood could never have one. They are keyed on
    CartWood now: vanilla's twelve plus one per wood any loaded mod adds, from
    the same ModdedMaterials scan the double gates use. The asset split is the
    gates' split too — tools/bake-carts.mjs for vanilla at author time,
    compat/GeneratedCarts for everyone else at run time, and the same warning
    that the two can drift silently.

    The carts kept RegisterEvent rather than being converted to
    DeferredRegister, and for once that is the less awkward choice:
    DoubleGates and ModdedArmour both have to create their
    per-material entries during class load because a DeferredRegister will not
    take an entry afterwards, and RegisterEvent fires after mod construction, so
    the modded woods need no trickery at all.

Four API guesses, four corrections

    The port had never been compiled against the game jars, and this repo had never touched a
    crop, a block tint or a passenger limit. In order:

    - BannerFlagModel.flag is public in the decompiled sources jar and
        private in the compiled jar. The access-transformer line for it was removed
        on the strength of reading the sources, and cost two compile errors. The sources lie;
        the line is back with a comment saying so.

    - BannerModel.getChildPart does not exist — Model.root()
        plus getChild does.

    - ItemNameBlockItem does not exist in 26.1.2. Vanilla builds
        wheat_seeds as a plain BlockItem with
        useItemDescriptionPrefix().

    - RegisterColorHandlersEvent.Block does not exist either. The block form is
        BlockTintSources and takes a list of one-method
        BlockTintSource, indexed by the model's tint index.

    And one that only a running server could find. HorseTwoRidersMixin was an
    @Inject on canAddPassenger, and the server refused to boot:
    AbstractHorse does not declare that method. It is on
    Entity, and a mixin injector can only target a method the target class
    actually has — inheritance does not count. It is an override now, the same move
    CartHorseMixin already makes for maxUpStep, which avoids mixing
    into Entity for a one-expression change. Nothing but
    runServer would have found it: it compiled, and the checks were all
    green.

What else went in

    - Pull is on the horse screen, beside Jump where the other body stats
        are, with a word for the number (feeble … a true draft horse)
        and a Draught block saying what it buys — including
        how far this horse has hauled in its life, which is a fact about the
        animal and survives being sold or inherited.

    - Two riders on a horse at pull 6 or better. Carrying and hauling are
        the same claim about an animal, so it is the same stat rather than a second gene.

    - A Farming and haulage chapter in Getting Started, eleven
        sub-chapters, placed after Husbandry because everything in it needs a saddled horse
        and nothing in it needs genetics — pulling ability is taught there from scratch.
        One sub-chapter per vehicle, each ticked by actually driving it.

    - Golden carrot seeds, a crop that grows golden carrots and yields no
        gold at any point. Not craftable: the supplier's top rank sells them dearly, and
        dungeon chests have them. It wears vanilla's carrot sheets tinted gold, so no art
        ships for it.

    - The Horse Powered patch, half of it. Every horse carries
        horsegenetics:draft_power now, a work rate blended 60/40 from pull and
        speed. The half that reads it has to be written against their real API and is
        gap 281 — guessing their class names would
        have been a fabrication that fails silently the first time they rename anything.

Trimmed, on the owner's call

    The hand cart (player-pulled; the one vehicle a horse never touches), all eleven
    advancements and their seven criterion triggers, and the slow-speed toggle with its
    keybind, payload, config value and the whole client mixin that existed to add one line to
    the mount overlay. Per-cart pull_speed went too, and not as a trim: a config
    option that overrode the genetics would make the genetics decorative.

    None of it has been played. The build is green, the server boots, 106
    checklist tasks all have something that completes them — and
    §0-FO is fourteen checks long, starting
    with "the game starts" and "a strong horse is visibly faster than a weak one on the same
    cart", which is the whole feature in one line.
