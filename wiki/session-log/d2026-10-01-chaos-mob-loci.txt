    What shipped. Lycanthropy, Leader of the pack and Spawner each gained ONE allele,
    Cha, between their last vanilla allele and n, standing for every mob another mod

    the server builds out of the live entity registry the first time a Chaos horse

    excluded by the new chaos.exclude_mods / chaos.exclude_ids server config; then
    lycan keeps only what isWearable accepts, pack leader only the peaceful ones,
    spawner everything. The vanilla alleles are untouched.

    The one real design problem was founders. Two Chaos copies act only when their
    seeds agree (the matched-pair rule, one level down, the owner's call), but two
    copies rolled independently never agree, so every wild or spawned Chaos/Chaos
    horse would have been the mismatched outcome - and Lycan's founders are all
    homozygous. Answer: Gene.foundersShareSeeds(), a list of seed values a freshly
    made horse carries identically on both copies, applied wherever Epigenome rolls
    two copies together and never in breeding. Two lines crossed stay two lines;
    inbreeding within a line keeps its animal. A mutation test (removing the
    sharing) fails LycanGeneTest, so the test is real.
