    An intake session. One treatment written into intake/, no code touched,
    nothing built or played. Every design call in it was put to the owner with
    the question tool and is recorded there as Decided.

    Troughs and salt licks (intake/Troughs and salt licks - implementation
    treatment.txt). Three pieces, one per session: a plain-wood trough that
    holds food items and that a hungry horse eats from a mouthful at a time,
    as a new rung in HungerFoodGoal's search; connected segments that share
    one inventory in any rectangle, with the connectivity rule in common/;
    then the fill readout, a stall that feeds from a trough inside its own
    volume, and the salt lick (a finite-use placed block, refilled with salt,
    that speeds healing and never punishes its absence). Dye and paint are
    left out on purpose, waiting for the shared dye extraction.

    The find that shaped it: a hay bale is eaten whole and gone, so a pen has
    to be re-fed by hand, and the goal picks by a fixed rung order - a trough
    is a new rung, not a new goal. The other one: this mod appears to have no
    block entity and no item-holding block, so piece 1 is likely its first;
    the treatment says to confirm that before designing storage.
