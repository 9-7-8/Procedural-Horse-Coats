    The rule (common/breed/BreedClimate, held by BreedClimateRuleTest over every breed file
    and every strain that names the locus):
      - a breed listing any vanilla Nether biome pins magic_heat Htol/Htol at 100;
      - a breed listing cold biomes carries magic_cold at least at the tier its share asks
        for (>= 0.5 near-fixed, >= 0.25 partial, > 0 light), compared by expected Ctol
        copies; the rule only raises.

    Clydesdale (light -> near-fixed); Yili (light -> partial). Each file's notes say so.
