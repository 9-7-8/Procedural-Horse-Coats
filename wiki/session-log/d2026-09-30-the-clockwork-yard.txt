2026-09-30, the test yard overhauled so a session can test it without the owner

    Owner: "Can you overhaul the horse debug realm, removing everything in the debug yard
    which can't be tested by you without my intervention, and then add in new pens which
    you can test without me?"

What went

    Every pen that waited for a person: the tack room (B), the holding pen (C), the dairy
    and clip (D), the ocean-born pool (E), the starburst and F8 pens (F west), the ward's
    spawner room and ward post and the old base-alarm pen (A east - a vanilla spawner runs
    only with a player within 16 blocks), the guardian arena (J west), ender echo (K), the
    ten splice-carrot pairs (L-N), WEANING by lead (O east), the jar bench, GOLD ANY HEAT and
    SUBFERTILE GOLD (P), and the chests of leads and vet's kits on THE CAP and MATERNITY.
    DebugYardGameplay is deleted. Letters were not re-lettered (the yard runs A, J, O, Q...),
    because every sign and Verification tab names rows by letter. The yard went from
    z=4..617 to 4..518. The stat pens moved into A east.

What came in

    DebugYardClockwork, rows J west and AM-AQ: a FakePlayer that KEEPS what it is told (a
    stock one drops every chat line, and for a refusal the line is the evidence), driven
    through Player.interactOn so vanilla's half of a click runs too. Every check logs
    "CLOCKWORK PASS|FAIL|INCONCLUSIVE | check | reading", and a CLOCKWORK SUMMARY at 10 and 30
    minutes names every check that has not answered as PENDING. 45 checks: cleansing light
    (caged undead, a creeper control, XP, a carrier), base alarm (near, carrier, quiet - counted
    per horse by a new DebugWorldWatch.soundsFrom), the egg layer's four silent/other-allele
    cases, bird-boned drops, blight and its control, potion milk (three refusals, the merge,
    the curses), the diets, milk buckets and Mlk refill, the seed jar end to end plus gelding,
    same-sex pairs, and the healer's control.

    What a FakePlayer cannot do - ride, be found by an aura that scans for players, be found as
    an owner by entity - is the test kit's batch 5 now (guardian, ender echo ridden, ocean-born,
    healer eggs).

Three runs, launched by the session itself

    Run 1: 32 PASS, 4 FAIL. All four FAILs were one confounder: a horse hurt at build time was
    back at full health by its test. Healing has been hunger plus WATER since 2026-09-14 and every
    pen has had a cauldron since 09-16, so stone floors stopped mattering - which also means the
    infirmary's "patients must not heal" control could never have passed since then (two of three
    patients were full). Fix: DebugTestYard.dryPen for the pens whose horse must stay hurt, and
    hurt a second before the feed rather than at the build.
    Run 2: every one of those passes, plus all the new milk and gelding checks; the jar mare's
    conception failed on the dice and was logged INCONCLUSIVE, as designed. One new FAIL: the
    cleansing carrier's zombie stood 6.5 blocks from the NEIGHBOURING Cln/Cln horse, whose radius
    rolls 4-8 - run 1 rolled short. Cage moved; run 3 passed it.
    Run 3: "test yard built and walkable". The walkability grid had shifted with the shorter yard
    onto HUNGER ORDER's poppy and REACH WALL's stone; both are furniture now, and the warning lists
    every kind it hit rather than only the first.

    Runs 3-5: blight's "covers go to dirt" read +4, +8, +0 and +1 dirt at six minutes while the
    watch counted the gene landing conversions all along - grass and mycelium spread back over bare
    dirt in a lit pen, and the gene's two-block reach runs under the pen walls. The floor was the
    wrong instrument; the check now counts conversions per horse (DebugWorldWatch.spreadsPlacedBy)
    and keeps the floor for the crop half and the control. Run 5 passed it at 10 conversions.

Found along the way

    Every single-food diet refused with "It only eats lava only." (labels end in "only"); fixed
    in HorseDietHandler.refuse. HorseProgress.complete now ignores fake players, which would
    otherwise earn checklist tasks - another mod's deployer included.

Not done, and why

    No Verification-tab check was deleted on a clockwork PASS. The standing rule is that a check
    leaves only on the owner's in-game confirmation; each page now carries the clockwork result
    beside its checks, marked "not owner-confirmed", and whether a clockwork PASS may close a check
    is put to the owner.
