2026-09-13 — a long play session, and the day a convention turned out to be breaking features

    Hours of live testing with fixes going in between reports. The useful
    through-line is not any single bug; it is that three different
    things kept producing the same symptom, and in each case the code
    under suspicion was innocent.

One: the yard's own convention was breaking features

    The test yard stocks its horses tamed with no owner, and
    that is deliberate — evacuateTamedHorses walks tamed
    and owned horses back to the overworld when you leave, so an owned
    yard is a yard that empties itself into the portal on the way out.

    But every feature gated on “your horse” checks ownership,
    not tameness. Guardian fires on OnOwnerHurt and scans
    for horses whose owner is the hurt player: an ownerless horse matches nobody,
    so the gene never once fired. The bound ticket answered “That is
    not your horse” for the same reason. Two unrelated-looking reports,
    one cause, and the rule is now written where both live: in this yard,
    anything gated on ownership must arrive wild.

Two: the dhampir, five fixes deep, and the one that mattered

    Reported dying five separate times. Four fixes went in from reading code:
    the shade search named blocks a ground pathfinder cannot stand on; the
    shelter was built sixteen blocks from the spawn against a twelve-block search
    radius; vanilla's PanicGoal sits at priority 1 alongside the
    shade goal and took Flag.MOVE first, so a burning horse ran in
    circles instead of walking to a roof it had already found; and the nearest
    cover is always the roof's lip, where a 1.4-block-wide horse still
    stands half in the sun.

    All four were real. None of them was the thing killing it,
    and it took adding a trace to find that out:

stopped at -11,163 - in shade, health 51/66
stopped at  -6,163 - in shade, health 48/66
stopped at  -7,166 - in shade, health 33/66
stopped at  -8,166 - in shade, health  6/66

    It reached shade every time. The instant it did,
    canContinueToUse went false, the goal released the movement
    flag, and WaterAvoidingRandomStrollGoal walked it back into the
    daylight — burning out and back, losing ground each cycle. Four fixes
    aimed at getting it to cover; getting to cover was never the problem. It
    holds until nightfall now.

    The lesson is the one this project keeps re-learning and I keep
    having to be taught: four consecutive plausible causes, each
    confirmed by reading and each wrong about the outcome. One line of logging
    settled it on its first run.

Three: two things I told the owner that were wrong

    - Gladiator fights unridden. Its own description
        says so outright — “while nobody is riding it, the horse
        attacks hostile mobs… mount it and it stops immediately”
        — and its condition is has_rider negated. The pen's
        sign told her to ride it, the class doc said the same, and I pulled the
        zombies out of its arena reasoning the gene was off without a rider. The
        specification was one line away in the file I was already editing.

    - The egg-layer cap was not close to answerable.
        NEARBY_CAP counts items within six blocks of each laying
        horse, so eight layers spread over a nineteen-wide pen each have
        their own neighbourhood and a pen total above the cap is legal. What
        bounded that pen was the despawn timer, exactly as
        gap 207 predicted. Rebuilt as four
        layers of one allele packed into a corner.

And the gladiator died three times for three different reasons

    Worth recording because each looked like the previous fix failing. A
    spawner keeps six zombies alive and refills them as fast as
    they die. Then panic at priority 1 starved the melee goal,
    so it bolted from fights it had started. Then three placed
    zombies — nine damage a second against twenty-two health,
    which is arithmetic and not a gene. One opponent now, and armed the way its
    own page says to arm it.

Shipped alongside

    - The F8 outline is fixed
        (gap 205), in three parts: a
        per-player register of what was lit, a pending set cleared on entity load
        for horses in unloaded chunks whose effect timers are frozen, and a
        reaper for the ones stranded before the fix existed.

    - The ticket stalls were a mob farm. Eighteen of the
        yard's nineteen natural spawns landed inside them: stallBox
        clears the column to air, wiping the yard's own invisible lamp, then
        roofs it and puts nothing back.

    - The safe-spawn spiral could walk a horse off the edge of the
        world. It asked noCollision, which over the void
        answers yes. Added in the morning to stop horses suffocating; a horse
        died outOfWorld during the next build.

    - Echolocate was Condition.ALWAYS —
        every echolocating horse in the world, wild ones included, lighting
        everything within twenty blocks every six seconds for nobody. Gated on
        tamed and ridden, and confirmed in play the same session.

    - Grooming replaced shearing in word, sound and feedback;
        the dairy is floored in stone so gated regen cannot heal the hurt mare
        before anybody reaches her; ocean-born sinks on the sprint key; the
        splice carrots became ten breeding pairs, one per carrot, each a divided
        pen with a gate into both halves.
