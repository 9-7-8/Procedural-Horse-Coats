DECIDED
-------
All recorded in #250 (section 5 and the decision comment on it). All (owner) unless said.
- The mod READS the data repo as its source of truth; it is not a one-way mirror.
- The repo holds real science plus the mod's abstractions, each labelled, with a mod map.
  An abstraction is named "abstracted ...", not "magical ...".
- Natural-flagged inventions move only if they stand in for a real phenomenon not yet
  understood: flaxen, shade, sooty, markings, pangare, rabicano, manchado, cvm,
  megaesophagus, patn2, the eight eye loci, fertility, natural zebra (as a horse locus).
  Diet stays in the mod.
- From the magical side only the five body-stat genes move, as abstracted height, speed,
  jump, hardiness and draft power. Climate, temperament and yields stay magical.
- Names only: saved keys and tokens do not change; the mod map translates.
- Breed pools: cited where known, flagged estimated otherwise. Breed body: real
  measurements, and the 1-10 scores marked as abstracted estimates.
- Equids only. Repo name open-equine-genetics. GPL 3, a relicensing clause in
  CONTRIBUTING, and the data stays GPL 3 inside the jar.
- Delivery by a pinned git submodule.
- Release: rides with other releases. The mod-reads pieces are on v0.6.4, after the rename
  and the four-jar split, before the ports. (The repo-and-export pieces were first placed
  on a new v0.5.062; they then left this repo, and that milestone with them.)
- The audit is its own issue. Science tabs are not generated from the data for now.
- The issues about creating the data repo live in the data repo; the ones about adapting
  the mod back stay here.
- Scoping session's calls, marked Recommended in the issues and not asked: the exact
  five display strings; the stat genes keep isNatural() == false; the breed overlay split;
  the parity-snapshot tests. Exporting session's calls, not asked: the data format and
  every field name; the folder sitting beside this repo; adding a validator there.
