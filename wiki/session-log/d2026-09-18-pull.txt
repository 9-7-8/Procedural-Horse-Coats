2026-09-18, after the full suite — A fifth body stat, and the split that copy count cannot say

    Pulling ability, ahead of the cart mod sitting in
    intake/. A new locus, horsegenetics.magic_pull —
    Strong, Weak, wild type — codominant, with a signed
    number of points on each variant copy that add. See
    Magic pull; the numbers live in the gene.

    Two design calls, both the owner’s. The first: pull is a raw
    1–10 score, not a multiplier like the other four body stats
    — because it’s going to be used by a different part of the mod. That
    turned out to be the load-bearing decision. The other four multiply because they have
    to scale whatever the natural loci settled on, so a magically fast pony stays
    slower than a magically fast racehorse. Pull has no natural loci to compose with, so
    multiplying would have bought nothing and cost the one property worth having: that a
    copy worth +2.5 is worth two and a half points on every horse that
    inherits it, readable off the copy without a conversion. TraitBuilder
    gets addPull and deliberately no multiplyPullUnclamped.

    The second call was forced by an arithmetic fact worth writing down.
    The dhampir was to pull at 9 for the white strain and 6
    for the seal brown. The other three stat loci give the dhampir its strength through the
    Vmp allele, where one copy is worth half of two — so the obvious move
    was a fourth allele here too. It cannot work, and the reason is not a limitation of the
    code: 9 is four times as far above the baseline as 6 is, and two copies of
    anything are only ever worth twice one. No fixed per-copy amount can produce a
    4:1 split from a 2:1 mechanism. That is why pull has three alleles rather than four, and
    why a Strain now carries its own optional stats block, laid
    over the breed’s axis by axis, with the founder roll picking the strain
    before it reads any band. The dhampir is the only breed using it; any strain
    may. Format on the breed file format.

    Every breed on the roster is scored, from the owner’s list.
    Eleven breeds were not on it — the warmbloods, the trotters, Quarter Horse,
    Pony of the Americas, Walkaloosa — and were scored by analogy with ones that
    were, at the owner’s direction rather than by guess. Worth noting that a breed
    written "pull": 5 and a breed that omits pull produce the same horse,
    because the near-baseline rule drops a band that close to ordinary; the difference is
    only whether the sheet records that the 5 was a decision.

    Nothing reads Traits.pull(), on purpose. What a pull
    score is worth in cart loads belongs to whatever ends up hitching a horse to
    something, not to the genetics. That makes the gene unobservable in game —
    there is no attribute to watch and no screen showing it, so it got no debug-yard pen:
    the stat pens work by reading a named vanilla attribute’s range across a pen, and
    a pen that cannot answer its own question is worse than no pen. MagicPullGeneTest
    is what holds it down instead — the arithmetic, the invisibility, a breed’s
    score reaching its founders, and the dhampir’s two strains landing apart.

    The interesting part was the wreckage, and none of it was the gene.
    A new locus costs one extra draw per horse, which moves every seeded roll downstream of
    it — and that shook out two tests that had been passing on luck.
    ArcaneStockTest asserted that nothing a dealer’s horse
    shows repeats across the string. ArcaneStock never promised that:
    it promises not to force a token another horse already has, and the horse
    underneath is Feral Mixed, which may coincidentally roll one. Rather than reseed until
    it passed, the rate got measured — a throwaway probe over 400 seeds said the old
    assertion holds for 44% of them, and none of the
    failures involved a forced pair. So the assertion was the thing that was wrong, and it
    now checks the rule that exists. CowboyHandler.takenCombos had conceded
    exactly this in its own comment the whole time.

    And the body-stat key list turned out to exist five times.
    Four were findable by their own comments, which say they agree by test rather than by
    import; the fifth, in BreedFounderLog, said nothing and was the expensive one — left alone it would
    have logged every breed founder in the world as carrying stray magic, a false
    positive in the one diagnostic you would go to when hunting stray magic. It and
    BreedPlateTest now read BreedFounder.BODY_STAT_KEYS instead of
    keeping their own. Two copies remain, and both say in a comment that they agree by test
    rather than by import; those two have a reason (neither module should depend on the
    other), which the two that were changed did not.

    The re-bake also caught a stale artefact nobody was looking for.
    unverified-genes.txt — the horse dimension’s coat-check column
    — gained six lines, and only one of them was pull. Blight, smoke scroll,
    endless knot, paisley spill and stormvein all carry no Verified block and had
    simply never been baked in, so the dimension was hiding five pens it should have been
    showing. Exactly the failure mode the regenerate-what-you-invalidate table exists for,
    found by regenerating for an unrelated reason. Pull itself lands in that file and
    correctly produces no pen: CoatCheckPlan skips anything that paints
    nothing, so the list can name a gene the column has nothing to show for.

    Both coat goldens moved on the key only. A new locus lands in every
    genotype code, so both files rewrite wholesale — which is exactly the shape a real
    regression would also take. Checked before copying back rather than after: all
    1086 coats compose byte-identically once the new locus is stripped
    from the key, and no existing bake row’s hash changed, with the only new rows being
    one per allele.
