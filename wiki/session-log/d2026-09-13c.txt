2026-09-13 — the yard stopped protecting the horses, and grew the half of the mod it never had

    Two instructions, and the second one is the interesting half. “Add the yard
    able and entire item and gameplay layer as well as villagers. Add literally everything
    that CAN be tested in the yard. Remove the 'no horse damage' exception in the
    yard.”

    That rule was the fifth protection in this dimension to turn out to be the
    reason a gene “did nothing”. The others were the handler that
    deleted every non-horse mob (so the ward had no zombies and the pack leader had no
    cows), the spread verb returning early here (so no dryad ever planted
    anything), the missing timelines in the dimension type (so there was no
    night for eight temper alleles and five watcher alleles to happen in), and the same
    damage rule cancelling the dhampir's sunburn (so the animal never dropped below full
    health and never hunted). This one held back six genes at once:
    guardian and gladiator need a fight, healer needs a patient below full, cleansing light
    needs undead, ender echo is triggered by being hit, and both death loci need
    the horse to die.

    The pattern has now been written down five times and it is worth saying once more in
    plain words, because it is the single most expensive thing in this project's history:
    a gene that does nothing and a gene that is forbidden from doing anything are
    identical from inside the game. No log line, no sign, nothing on the horse
    distinguishes them. Every one of the five was found by adding an instrument, never by
    reasoning — and four of the five were found while looking for something
    else.

Rows J and K: what the removal unlocked

    Guardian and gladiator went into separate roofed arenas with a zombie
    spawner each, and the separation is the design decision worth recording. They are the
    same ability verb — Temper("aggressive") — with different
    triggers: guardian fires on OnOwnerHurt and only retaliates, gladiator
    fires Continuous while has_rider and starts the fight itself.
    In one pen with one batch of zombies, a dead zombie says nothing about which horse
    killed it, and “retaliates” against “starts it” is the entire
    distinction between the two genes. A shared pen would have been a cheaper build and a
    test that could not fail informatively.

    The healer's pen has three companions already at half health when the yard is
    built, which was impossible until this morning — a healer pen full of
    healthy horses measures nothing and would have read as the gene being dead. The
    deathbed holds six horses on a stone floor, because two of the six leave a fluid
    behind and a third detonates. Ender echo got a wide pen: a sixteen-block blink inside a
    nine-block stall either fails or puts the horse through a wall, and both look like a
    broken gene.

Rows B, C and D: the item layer, with the fetching taken out

    None of the item, block or villager layer had ever been in the yard. It lived in the
    test kit's hotbar — nine slots at a time, batches that overwrote each other —
    and the legends were full of building work: “hang on a pen wall”,
    “fill a stall solid”, “even L-shaped or narrower than the horse”.
    Those are instructions nobody follows twice. Batches 3 and 4 were taken and put away
    three days running.

    So the awkward cases are standing there: a stall barely wider than a
    horse, an L-shaped one (where “dead centre” is not the centre of the
    bounding box), one packed solid with hay so the ticket has to refuse without being
    consumed, and a roofed one so the bind's height message can be checked against a
    real lid. Beside them, a tack room of five labelled chests holding one of every item in
    the mod; a horseman at his table and a cowboy at his hitch with no village to find; all
    nine splice carrots with four horses whose genotypes nobody has read; and the three
    potion-milk refusals with the mare already hurt and the foal already a foal.

    Nothing here is cleverer than the kit was. It is the same list with
    the fetching removed, which is the only part that was ever expensive — and two of
    the six hotbar batches were retired the same day, because every prop their legends
    described is now standing in the yard with a chest beside it. The rule that fell out of
    it: if a batch can be replaced by a pen, it should be.

Lighting the yard found a bug that had been live all day

    A biome that spawns zombies plus horses that can be hurt is every unattended pen losing
    its subject overnight, so the open yard is lit now. Doing it turned up two things.

    The spawn-proofing in the dark rooms had never worked. It placed nine
    LightBlocks at level 1 on a four-block grid at gy+4 —
    and light falls off one per block, so a level-1 source lights its own position and
    nothing else. The floor read zero. The ward room had been filling with natural zombies
    all day and the log says so, dozens of times, with the reason column reading
    NATURAL rather than SPAWNER — which is only legible at
    all because that reason column was added yesterday for a different question. There is
    no spacing that works for a level-1 source, so it fills the floor layer instead. Level 1
    is still black to the eye and still dark to a gene, whose threshold is 7.

    And the first version of the yard's own grid would have broken the pen it was
    protecting. Level 15 on a six-block grid at gy+4 bottoms out
    around six at the floor, which is below DARK_LEVEL — so the
    new eyesight pen's lit control would have read as dark to the very gene it is
    the control for. A pen that agrees with its own experiment for the wrong reason is the
    worst kind of green, and it would have been indistinguishable from the gene working.
    Four apart and two up; the floor never drops below nine.

Smaller things, all of which were something being wrong quietly

    - The yard's depth was a literal, and it was seventy-five blocks short.
        YARD_DEPTH_Z read 110 while the rows chained past 150, so the back of
        the yard sat outside the plot box that tears a plot down and walks tamed horses
        home. It is derived from the last row now, which retires the whole class of bug.

    - A layout audit caught a stat pen one block past the wall. Three
        seven-wide pens plus their gaps overran EAST_MAX by one, into the strip
        that keeps the yard's own wall reachable. Found by mirroring the grid constants in a
        throwaway script and asserting no rectangle overlaps — not in game, which is
        the only way a one-block overrun ever gets caught.

    - /testkit night stopped crying wolf. It read the sky in
        the same tick it set the clock, and Level.updateSkyBrightness() runs
        during the level tick, so it returned the previous tick's value every time —
        including after the real bug was fixed. The owner had to tell the diagnostic it was
        wrong: “okay, it now visually changes to night even if the kit says an
        error”. It reads three ticks later now.

    - A chest's label sign would have popped off. A standing sign needs a
        sturdy block under it and a chest lid is not one, so every label in the new rows
        would have been an item on the floor by the time anybody walked in. They go beside
        the chest instead.

    What is not being worked on, by the owner's call: “we're not
    going to worry about older worlds for older versions of the mod until we get to 1.0.
    Nor am I super worried about wild spawn frequencies yet.” Old-world migration
    (gap 193) and founder-table frequency tuning are
    marked as deferred decisions rather than open gaps, which is a different thing
    and should not be counted as one.
