DECIDED
-------
- Without the owner: "herd-like carrying" means vanilla's stroll plus the PEACEFUL band
  goals (DamAndFoal, GroomAndRest). No sparring, guarding, displacing or courting with
  a rider aboard, and untamed horses are excluded. Recorded on horse-gear #passenger-only
  and in the #74 closing comment.
- Without the owner: the key is named behaviour.saddle_needs_bridle, sitting beside
  behaviour.owner_only_riding. The treatment fixed only "one server boolean, default ON".
- Without the owner: the client is told through a synced flag, not by recomputing Reins
  on the client, which knows neither the bond tier nor the bolting state.
- Without the owner: the bareback tier works through a saddle (the treatment: tier 3
  "steers freely with or without a bridle"). Steer bareback still fires only for a
  ride with no real saddle.
