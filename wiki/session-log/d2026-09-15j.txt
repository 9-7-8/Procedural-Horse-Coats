2026-09-15, last of the day — the intake queue empties: fourteen more breeds, one of them extinct

    The seventh and final intake batch. Fifteen drafts remained in
    intake/ — Spotted Saddle Horse through Zangersheide — and they
    became fourteen breeds. Nothing is left in that folder but
    breedbycountry.md, which is the country authority and stays, and
    breeds that might be missing.md, which is a candidate list rather than a
    write-up. Breeds.all().size() owns the count.

The batch

    Spotted Saddle Horse, Swedish Warmblood, Tarpan, Tersk, Trottatore Italiano, Ukrainian
    Riding Horse, Vyatka, Walkaloosa, Wielkopolski, Yakutian Horse, Yili, Yonaguni Horse,
    Yugoslavian Trotter, Zangersheide.

    - One breed arrived twice under two English names.
        Yakut.md and Yakutian.md are both the Sakha horse of
        Yakutia. Neither had shipped, so unlike the APHA re-supply there was no
        replace-or-not call to put to the owner — they were merged on that precedent.
        The merge is the better file than either draft: the base colours come from the one
        that cites a real study (45 purebreds, MC1R E 0.711 and
        ASIP A 0.400, one of the few measured base pools
        here), and the three regional strains come from the other. The same study's low
        gait-keeper frequency is why DMRT3 is left wild rather than written.

    - The mod now has an extinct breed, and extinction turned out to need no new
        machinery. The Tarpan is
        kind: natural like everything else — magical is for
        the Dhampir, not for “unusual” — and the whole of how it says it is
        gone is that its spawn omits wild. It is very rare, nearly
        fixed for dun, and carries a 5% ancient leopard strain, which is not folklore:
        ancient DNA finds leopard-spotted horses in prehistoric western Eurasia. Grey is
        forced wild for the reason the Konik and the Sorraia force it — “mouse
        grey” is dun, and STX17 would whiten the one thing it looks like.
        Pinned to Russia on the last captive animal (1909) under the
        nation-not-region rule, with the steppe range said in its notes.

    - A founder pool carries a disorder allele homozygously for the first time in
        this batch's reading of the files. A Vyatka population-genomic study puts
        GYS1 PSSM1 at an allele frequency of
        0.189 — about 31% heterozygous and 3.6% homozygous.
        PSSM1 is dominant, so those heterozygotes are affected rather than silent carriers and
        roughly a third of the breed is genetically positive. The number was written straight
        in rather than softened, because it is measured and the softening would have been the
        invention.

    - Two more warmbloods ship without WFFS, and one of them brings the best
        evidence in that roadmap section. A random cohort of 511 Swedish Warmbloods
        born in 2017 had 38 carriers, 7.4% — a random sample rather than
        a tested-because-suspected one, which is what makes it better than the warmblood-wide
        proxies. The Zangersheide ships clear too but adds
        nothing: its draft's rate was self-labelled as a proxy and was declined.
        The roadmap's health-layer entry (retired 2026-09-21) now names five shipped breeds
        missing their headline disorder. PLOD1 here is Friesian dwarfism, and both
        drafts wrote the WFFS one into it — the trap held.

    - patn1 and patn2 are two loci, not one pool.
        Found while writing the Walkaloosa and the Tarpan, and worth recording because a file
        written the other way does not fail: the parser warns and drops the pair, and the breed
        loads quietly missing its pattern modifiers. The Walkaloosa is the second breed here to
        fix LP in a strain, after the Pony of the Americas, and for the same kind
        of registry reason — it must be both spotted and gaited, inspected for each.

    - Three breeds wanted per-strain body sizes and cannot have them. The
        Yakutian's regional types, the Yili's production groups and the Spotted Saddle Horse's
        two registers all differ in size in life; a strain may override gene pools and nothing
        else, so each got one spanning band and a sentence in notes saying so.

    - Four sentences had to be un-written for ranking the new breeds against the
        catalogue — “the tallest band here”, “the highest jump
        in the mod”, “the smallest horse”, “the first breed to carry X
        homozygous”. CLAUDE.md's derived-number rule covers prose as much as
        figures, and one of the four was already false when written: the Yonaguni is nowhere
        near the smallest horse here, the Falabella is six hands.

    - This batch was committed unbaked and untested, deliberately. The owner
        was processing logs in a running client all session and a gradle run recompiles
        underneath one. Everything a JVM is not needed for was checked instead: all fourteen
        parse, use only legal top-level and strain keys, and every allele token was read off the
        new Allele(…) lines of the gene classes; index.json is
        sorted and matches the folder. The chain still owed is at the top of
        to be verified, and that block is the thing to delete
        when it goes green.
