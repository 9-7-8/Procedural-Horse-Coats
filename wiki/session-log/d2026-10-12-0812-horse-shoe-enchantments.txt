DECIDED
-------
All of these were made WITHOUT the owner; #248's Open line said the names, the numbers and
the conflict were the building session's. Recorded on wiki/decisions.html (pointing at
wiki/item-horse-shoes.html#enchantment-code) and in #248's closing comment.
- Names: Grip and Roadster. Three levels each.
- The conflict: one shoe carries one of the two (vanilla's exclusive_set); the four hooves
  may mix them. A stack given both by a command gets both.
- Numbers: one level on one shoe takes 8% of the slide out of ice, or adds 0.002 movement
  speed on a road. Four shoes at III: 96% of the slide, or about 1.04 m/s.
- Grip pulls a slick block's friction toward ordinary ground (0.6) and never past it.
- What a road is: the block tag above, a tag so a pack can add to it.
- How one is got: the enchanting table, a book on an anvil, and wherever ordinary
  enchanted books come from (minecraft:non_treasure).
- The caps are constants in HorseShoes, as the metals' are; the config has the per-level
  number only. The issue's "a number for each enchantment's effect per level and its cap"
  can also be read as a config key for the cap: not built.
- The shoe drawn on the hoof does not glint. The issue's check "an enchanted worn shoe
  shows it is enchanted" is met only by the item (inventory, Gear tab, tooltip). A glint
  pass in AttachedPartLayer is on the page's Roadmap tab, for #228.
