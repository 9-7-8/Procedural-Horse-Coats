  22 x 13 x 17 x 15 x 14 = 1,021,020. Inheritance picks a parent's index per layer, with
  a 1-in-10 random swap. The sizes are averaged and then multiplied by 0.75-1.25. Its

  flight for three of them, idle animations, and a Stay/Follow/Wander whistle. The copy

DECIDED (all owner, 2026-10-03; recorded in the treatment)
- It is a new item. You hold use on a horse to open a radial wheel and release to pick.
  Sneak-hold orders every owned horse within 16 blocks.
- The wheel's orders: Stay, Follow, Wander (roam and ignore me, which is distinct from
  Rejoin herd), Graze nearby, Guard here, Go home, Hunt monsters, Defend me, Rejoin herd.
- Bond: Stay and Follow need tier 1 (31+). The owner first said "a day's worth", which
  was offered as 15 and became tier 1. Everything else needs tier 2.
- The combat orders are gene-gated: Aggression with monster targets, Guardian, or Magic
  Fighter.
- Leashed pursuit. This REOPENS gene-aggression's settled no-chase call, for orders
  only. Hunt stays within 16 of the anchor; Defend stays within 8 of the owner.
- Needs (panic, hunger) break a Stay briefly and the horse returns. Riding pauses an
  order. A recall whistle clears it.
- Targets are hostiles, excluding creepers.
- Obtained by crafting AND from an equestrian supplier trade.
- Release: counts only when the LAST piece ships.
- Session calls, marked Recommended in the treatment, not asked: the recipe, the
  attachment shape, the break-off health, the tag-not-config creeper exclusion, a section
  on item-whistles rather than a new page.
