2026-10-01 - the mythic-breeds audit, and a biome for every one


    An intake session, a second one the same day. No code touched, nothing built or
    played. The owner pasted seven tables of mythical and folkloric horses (the
    Greek, Norse, Irish, Hindu, Chinese, Turkic and Islamic traditions, plus a few
    from books and games) and asked which genes the mod already has to make them,
    which are already scoped in intake/, and which still need scoping. Two rules
    arrived along the way: public domain only, and no talking horses.

    The audit (intake/Mythic breeds - gene audit.txt). Everything from Tolkien,
    Harry Potter, Dungeons and Dragons, Narnia, Zelda and The NeverEnding Story was
    removed under the first rule, and the speaking-horse gap under the second.
    What is left maps cleanly: about forty breeds can be built from existing genes
    with no engineering (the plain Norse and Greek named horses, the sun-chariot
    teams, Grani, Skinfaxi, Hrimfaxi, Gullfaxi, Svadilfari, the Irish chariot pair,
    Freyfaxi and more); seven wait only on an immortality design call; four are
    partial; nine are blocked on one real thing each. The biggest unblocker is
    wings, then forced riding (the kelpie), then tails. New gaps found: immortality,
    rider protection, fire breath, a skinless coat, rooster foreparts, extra legs
    and heads, hiding a bone, and a natural dapple gene (added to intake/To do.txt).

    The biomes (intake/Mythic breeds - biome assignments.txt). Every entry has a
    vanilla biome list drawn from the herd modifier's own list, a spawn time, a
    commonness tier, and a consequence check against the breed climate spec (eight
    breeds would pick up cold tolerance from their biomes). The coasts are the
    crowded part: a few water breeds were thinned to keep beaches and stony shores
    from becoming a fifth mythic.

    What the next session picks up first: the audit's own order (wings, forced
    riding, tails, one immortality-and-travel design call), plus the owner's open
    questions in the biome file (spawn kinds, whether the plain horses should be
    one breed, whether each sun team is one breed).
