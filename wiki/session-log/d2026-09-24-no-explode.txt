2026-09-24 — the volatile allele is deleted: a horse may flood the ground it dies on, not crater it

    One intake bullet, the first of the "Magical loci and modded content" group and the next one
    after the wings question: "Remove the 'explode' allele from Magic on death (MagicOnDeathGene,
    Xpl/Xpl) entirely - a doubled pair should resolve to wild type instead. Decide what happens to
    any horse or splice that currently carries Xpl."

    It is a tuning ask on something that was built and verified working. Xpl/Xpl had been
    confirmed in-game nine days earlier — on 2026-09-15 the yard's DEATH BOOM pen logged
    "[watch] EXPLOSION | 224 blocks, 1 entities", blew out part of its own pen, dropped 5 diamonds
    and no leather from the Dia/Dia locus on the same horse, and left a witness horse 6.2 blocks
    away on full health. It was removed because of where the mod is actually played, not because
    it failed: on a heavily modded server with other people's builds in blast range, a hazard
    whose carrier is invisible is a gamble the breeder's neighbour takes without being asked.

What went

    MagicOnDeathGene: the Xpl allele, the EXPLODE expression, WILD_EXPLODE_PERCENT and its founder
    row, and the explode branch of expressionOf/abilitiesFor. The locus is now Lav, Wat, n — n's
    order index moved 3 -> 2, which nothing reads (Allele#order is a slot order for AllelePair, and
    genotype codes are tokens). The founder table's n/n weight absorbs the freed 2%.

    The explode choice on the on_death verb went with it, which was a judgement call the bullet did
    not spell out: AbilityType.ON_DEATH's choice list, the creator's schema.js twin, and
    GeneDeathHandler's case "explode" plus EXPLOSION_RADIUS. Leaving the verb choice in place would
    have meant a data-driven gene could still author an exploding horse, which is the thing being
    removed. No gene in common/src/main/resources uses on_death at all, and the
    creator-effects-all-verbs fixture already used "water", so nothing authored needed changing.

    The yard's DEATH BOOM pen became DEATH DIAMONDS: a Dia/Dia horse on the same stone floor,
    keeping the 2-to-5-diamonds-and-no-leather PASS that nothing else in the yard watches, and
    dropping the blast, the witness horse and the rebuild-the-pen-afterwards step.

What happens to a horse or a splice that still carries it

    That was the half of the bullet that needed an answer rather than a deletion, and the answer is
    that the machinery for it already exists, in three different places, each already tested:

    - A saved horse: Genotype.readableStored drops a segment naming an allele this build does not
      have, logs it through GenomeCodeCodecs, and the locus comes back at n/n. This is the route
      Cleave's retired Clv took in 0.5.000, written after that allele would otherwise have thrown
      from inside an entity tick and taken the world down every time its chunk loaded. GenotypeTest
      pins it.
    - A splice carrot: the carrot stores "known:horsegenetics.magic_on_death:Xpl:Xpl" as text and
      resolves it at breeding time through alleleOrNull, never Gene#fromToken, specifically so a
      retired allele is inert instead of fatal. The carrot is spent and nothing happens;
      KnownGeneSpliceTest pins that too.
    - A gene-database entry: a collected "horsegenetics.magic_on_death|Xpl" id stops matching
      anything and the locus's "alleles collected" total drops by one. Nothing cleans the dead id
      out of that set — it is a string in NBT that no longer names anything. Harmless, and written
      down on the gene page rather than fixed.

    So there is no migration and none was written, per hard rule 6.
