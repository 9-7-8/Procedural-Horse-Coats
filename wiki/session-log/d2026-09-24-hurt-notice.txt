2026-09-24, after the sold-horse greying — an owner is told when their horse is hurt

    One bullet, the first in the Bugs section of intake/To do.txt, in the owner's words:

        When an owned horse takes damage, only its owner should receive a chat message
        with the horse name, damage source, and, when applicable, how to cure or prevent
        it. This should be configurable and on by default.

    Dated first, as START HERE says to. The bullet went into To do.txt on 2026-09-23
    (27cd45aa) and nothing in the repository sends a player any message about a horse
    being hurt — the only damage hook that speaks at all is DebugWorldWatch, which writes
    to the yard log. So: open, and genuinely absent rather than built and wrong.

    roadmap-execution.html's symptom map has the row ("Owned-horse damage/death message
    duplicates or reaches the wrong player") and its stage 5 already sets the terms:
    target damage messages only to the owner, deduplicate the hooks. The owning page is
    horse-care.html, which is where healing and hunger already live, and the notice's
    advice is mostly about healing and pens.

Where it lives, and why the words are in common/

    common/care/HurtNotice — the sentences, the quiet period, the hearts arithmetic.
    server/HorseHurtNoticeHandler — LivingDamageEvent.Post, the owner lookup, the map.
    ServerConfig.notices.owned_horse_damage, default true.

    The wording is the feature here, so it goes where a test can pin it down. That is
    ReproText's precedent, made for the same reason. HurtNoticeTest asserts the shape of
    the sentence, that an attacker's name outranks the cause's own phrase, that a lethal
    genotype is never offered a cure, that an unknown damage type is named but not
    explained, and that SUNLIGHT cannot be reached from a damage id at all.

Grouping damage types by the advice they earn

    Vanilla has dozens of damage types and this message has one job, so Cause is a
    grouping by what the owner should DO: inFire, onFire, campfire and hotFloor are four
    types and one sentence about keeping the pen clear of fire. Lava gets its own because
    the advice differs (nothing walks out of it). Cactus, sweet berries and thorns are one
    thing to clear out. Mobs, arrows, tridents and lightning are all "something is
    attacking it".

    Each Cause carries the prevention. The CURE does not vary by cause — it is water and a
    fed horse, every time (Hunger) — so it is appended once, and only when the horse is at
    or below a quarter of its own maximum health. That is the one judgement call in the
    file worth defending: a line on every scratch that ended "it heals beside water" is a
    line the owner learns to stop reading, and the half that changes is the half in front
    of it. GENETIC_DEFECT is excluded from it outright, because nothing heals a lethal
    genotype and saying otherwise would send somebody to fetch a bucket.

    The ids are matched, not verified (hard rule 8) — they are vanilla's own strings and an
    unrecognised one is simply "something". That fallback is also what makes another mod's
    damage safe: it is named, not explained, and nothing breaks.

Sunlight is fire on the wire

    SunSensitivityHandler burns a sun-sensitive horse with level.damageSources().onFire(),
    so the damage type cannot distinguish a dhampir caught in the open from a horse
    standing in a campfire — and those two want opposite advice (stable it by day, versus
    keep the pen clear). It never ignites the horse, though, so isOnFire() is false exactly
    in the sun case, and that is the discriminator.

    The cleaner fix is a horsegenetics:sun damage type of its own. It was not done: the
    burn is deliberately in vanilla's fire tags, where fire resistance and fire immunity
    reach it, and a new type would have to be re-added to every one of them to keep
    behaviour identical. A comment says so in both places. If the in-game check comes back
    saying "from fire", that is the fix, and the tags are what to get right.

Deciding who hears it

    getOwner() only answers for an owner loaded in the same level, and that turned out to
    be the right audience rather than a limitation to work around: a chat line for somebody
    who logged out an hour ago is not an alarm. An owner in another dimension, or offline,
    hears nothing, and that gap belongs to the browser's planned Log tab — written up as a
    deliberate scope line on horse-care.html rather than left to be discovered.

    Never when the source entity is the owner. A player hitting their own horse is looking
    at it, and in the test yard the line would be constant.

    AbstractHorse rather than Horse, so a donkey or a mule is covered; the name falls back
    from the tag, to this mod's record, to the species.

The quiet period, and what it is keyed on

    QUIET_TICKS is 100 — five seconds, per horse, whatever hurt it. Keyed on the horse and
    not on the cause deliberately: a burning horse takes damage every two seconds and a
    horse among zombies several times a second, and a key that included the cause would let
    two overlapping threats double the rate. Five seconds because the message is an alarm
    the owner is meant to be able to answer; not shorter because the second line about the
    same fire says nothing the first did not.

    The map is cleared on LivingDeathEvent and swept of five-minute-old entries above 512
    horses, so a busy server does not accumulate one entry per horse it has ever hurt.

    MIN_DAMAGE of 0.5 keeps a quarter-heart scratch from spending a horse's whole quiet
    period on the least interesting hit of the five seconds.

Not played

    Everything that is words or arithmetic has a test. Nothing about the routing does,
    because all of it is NeoForge: which player getOwner() returns, what getHealthDamage()
    is at Post, whether isOnFire() really is false for a sun-burnt horse, and whether the
    mod's own damage type reports msgId "genetic_defect". The checks are on
    horse-care.html#hurt-notice-0924; the two-player one is the one that matters most,
    since "only the owner" is the whole ask.
