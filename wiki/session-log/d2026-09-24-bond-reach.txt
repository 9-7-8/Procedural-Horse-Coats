2026-09-24, after the barn-name fix — a bonded horse stops staring through the fence, and
band life gets its movement back

    One bullet, the first in the Bugs section of intake/To do.txt, in the owner's words:

        A high bond horse should not attempt to follow or watch their bond if there is no
        direct path to them -- line of sight is not enough, they must be able to find a path
        to their bond. If they're in an enclosed area, they act like a normal horse. Right
        now, a high bond horse is ignoring breeding in preference of watching their bonded
        owner.

    Reported from the heavily modded server she actually plays on. Nothing in the session log
    or on any Verification tab had touched BondFollowGoal since it was written on 2026-09-02,
    so the bullet was live rather than stale.

Two causes, not one

    The reachability half is the half the bullet names, and it was simply absent: every tier
    gated on distance alone. A horse shut in a pen could see its owner through the rails at
    nine blocks, start the goal, and — at tier 3 — re-issue Navigation.moveTo at an
    unreachable target every ten ticks for as long as the owner stood there, which is both
    the reported behaviour and a quiet pathfinding cost per horse.

    The cause of the *symptom* she noticed is separate, and is about goal flags rather than
    pathing. Tier 1 of the bond ladder is head-turning: "turns its head to face the owner
    within 10 blocks; no movement". But the one goal carried EnumSet.of(MOVE, LOOK) for all
    three tiers and sat at priority 4, and vanilla's GoalSelector lets a running goal hold a
    flag against every goal with a higher priority *number* — so a horse doing nothing but
    looking at its owner locked MOVE away from all six of HerdGoals' band-life goals
    (Spar 3, DamAndFoal 4, StallionGuard 5, Displace 5, HeatAttraction 6, GroomAndRest 7).
    Bond 31 is enough. Any tamed horse within ten blocks of its owner had no band life at
    all, and HeatAttraction — the goal that walks a stallion to a mare in heat — was the
    most visible casualty. That is "ignoring breeding in preference of watching their
    bonded owner", exactly.

What changed

    server/BondFollowGoal now covers tiers 2-3 only and keeps MOVE|LOOK at priority 4.
    Tier 1 moved into BondFollowGoal.Watch, a nested goal claiming LOOK alone, registered by
    HorseCareHandler at priority 8 — deliberately below every band-life goal, so watching
    fills the gaps between doing instead of replacing it. Nothing about looking should
    out-rank anything about doing.

    Both goals gate on a shared ReachCheck: getNavigation().createPath(owner, 0), true only
    when the path exists and canReach(). A path that stops short at a fence counts as no
    path. It is a real pathfind, far too expensive for a canUse() that runs every tick for
    every horse, so the answer is cached for 40 ticks — which is also why opening a gate
    takes up to two seconds to register, and why that delay is correct rather than a bug.
    The cheap checks (tamed, tier, owner present, distance) run first, so the pathfind only
    happens for a horse that would otherwise have started.

    Tier 3's persistence is unchanged in intent but now means something: the route was
    proved walkable before the goal started, so re-issuing every cycle is persistence rather
    than spinning on an impossible request.

Not played

    wiki/horse-care.html grew a Verification section (#bond-reach-0924) with four checks: a
    bond-81 stallion still covering a mare while his owner stands in the pen (the regression
    that matters), a penned horse ignoring an unreachable owner outside the fence, the gate
    opening being noticed within a couple of seconds, and tier 1 still turning its head when
    nothing else is going on. /bond sets the number for testing. The bond table on the same
    page gained #bond-needs-a-route and the two-goal split; the class list names Watch and
    ReachCheck.

    Not done here, and worth knowing: the reachability predicate the intake advice asks for
    ("reuse the same reachability/boundary predicate for pasture crowding, crop eating, and
    food selection") is still three separate absences — this change only put one in
    BondFollowGoal. The pasture-edge pushing and eat-through-a-fence bullets are still open
    in intake/To do.txt and are the natural place for ReachCheck to become shared.
