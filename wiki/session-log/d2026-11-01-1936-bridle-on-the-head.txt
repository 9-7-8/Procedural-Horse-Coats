DECIDED
-------
Calls made without the owner (recorded on horse-gear's Coding tab, #bridle-code):
- The migration is a per-tick rule, not a flagged one-shot: a bridle in a horse's saddle
  slot is always wrong, and the same move covers dispensers, vanilla's horse inventory
  and other mods.
- A second bridle offered to a bridled horse mounts the player (the "full slot means you
  get on" rule) rather than being dropped.
- Bridling a tamed horse is owner-only, like every piece of this mod's gear (vanilla let
  anyone saddle a tamed horse).
- The bench and tooltip keep the Seat row on this item: on a donkey or mule it is still
  a saddle with a seat. #75 gives the seat to the new saddle.
- The treatment's "vanilla-saddled again if the mod is removed" does not hold: the bridle
  sits in the HORSE_GEAR attachment like all gear. Written on the Coding tab.
