2026-09-15, night — a country on every breed, and the first five out of intake/

    The ask was to start taking breeds out of intake/, five at a time and alphabetically, and to add a
    country to the breed files because it is going to drive a mechanic. Both done; the shipped count is 55.

    - intake/*.md is not drop-in material, and that shaped the whole session. Each
        file is a research write-up from another model carrying a “Breed JSON” block in a schema this
        mod has never had — coat_genes, sources, stat_scores,
        per-allele frequencies instead of weighted pairs, prices in the hundreds where this mod trades in tens, and
        invented gene keys (sabino_1, frame_overo). Every one says so in its own schema
        caveat. So all five were translated against BreedSpecParser rather than pasted.

    - The drafts' size bands contradicted their own research. Each states a hand range and then
        gives a scale band that does not match it. Calibrating against the Fjord's known
        [0.857, 0.921]  13.2–14.2 proved the convention — decimal hands
        = scale × 15.75, displayed in stable notation — and all five were re-derived to land on the
        heights their own sources give.

    - country is a lower-case token, not a display name
        (the format page), because it is meant to be keyed on rather than only
        printed; a capital letter is an error the way a bad id is. Blank is a real answer meaning
        nowhere on earth, and the Dhampir is the only breed with none. Plumbed through the record, parser, writer,
        breed designer and format page, and backfilled across all 49 earthly breeds.

    - The owner's own breedbycountry.md overrode two of my 49. It arrived mid-session
        in a push and is the authority: the Lipizzan is filed under Austria (I had Slovenia) and the
        Arabian under Saudi Arabia (I had a region token). The other 47 already agreed.

    - Three genes these breeds need do not exist, and went to
        the roadmap rather than known-gaps (owner's call): the
        curly coat (KRT25/SP6) — which is the whole of the
        American Bashkir Curly, so it ships with the colour pool and no curl — plus ACAN D5 and
        RYR1. What stayed a gap is a format limit: a strain can override gene pools but not stats, so the
        Chincoteague's sport type cannot be the taller of the two (gap 256).

    - main was already red before any of this.
        HerdManager used NeoRng without importing it — it sits in
        …neoforge.server, the class in …neoforge — from the previous
        session's commit. Fixed in its own commit so it is not buried in the breed work.

    - Two Windows traps worth knowing. A pushed intake filename containing double quotes cannot be
        created on NTFS, so git failed the checkout and it read like a merge conflict; the owner
        renamed it. And a bake dirties all 55 breed files under core.autocrlf with no
        .gitattributes — git diff --ignore-cr-at-eol showed no real change, and the
        committed bytes were identical, so it is noise rather than staleness.
