8f3eb625  Fix #246: the arcane dealer stocked lycan far too often.
  What was wrong: ArcaneStock.takeOne drew flat from a family's showing PAIRS, so a gene
  was weighted by how many showing pairs it has. A matched-pair locus has one per animal.
  In MAGIC_BEHAVIOUR (24 genes, even share about 4%) lycan was on about 12% of his
  horses, pack_leader 16% and spawner 38%; magic_sectoral_heterochromia took 13% of
  MAGIC_CORE (even share 2%). With up to ten horses in a string, a lycan was in nearly
  every string.
  The fix: draw the gene first, then one of its free pairs, the way MagicalVariant.pick
  already does for wild magical herds. The no-two-alike rule and the scarcest-first draw
  order are unchanged; both still count pairs.
  Proof: ArcaneStockTest.noGeneCrowdsOutItsFamily (300 fresh horses; each gene's share
  against its family's even share; bound 3x even + 2%, and 2x even for lycan). Green with
  the fix, red with ArcaneStock.java stashed, naming the four genes above. The other
  thirteen ArcaneStockTest tests stayed green. :neoforge-26.1.2:build, check-parity and
  check-designer-boots green.
  Pictures: no pictures (a frequency, nothing to see on one horse).
  Page: wiki/villagers.html#arcane-pools gained the paragraph on the draw.
  Issue: #246, closed, with the proof comment.

FOUND ON THE WAY
----------------
- Mistake: the first version of the test rolled 4000 horses and held Gradle for 13.5
  minutes, with four players on the live server. A roll is slow because ArcaneStock.pool
  is rebuilt for every family on every horse (about 0.25 s a horse). The test now rolls
  300. The pool rebuild itself was left alone: the registry can change under a data pack,
  and a restock rolls ten horses, not thousands.
- Mistake: one "still red after the fix" reading was a stale result file; the test source
  had a broken string escape and had not compiled. Read the head of the Gradle log, not
  only the XML.
- 300 rolls is noisy: an even 3% gene (rainbow_drip) came out at 8% on one seed and 2% on
  the next two. That is why the bound is wide. Not a bias; nothing filed.
