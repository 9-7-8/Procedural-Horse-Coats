- 371b9546 Fix #26: a horse could only reach a hay bale by standing on top of it.
  What was wrong:
  - Not the hay_bales tag. In the same yard run (2 Oct, 04:12 onwards) dozens of horses
    under open sky ate minecraft:hay_block.
  - Not the GRAZING pen's reachability either. A fresh vanilla PathFinder reaches the bale
    beside it with accuracy 1.
  - The cause: HungerFoodGoal.reachable/tick called createPath(BlockPos, 1) and
    moveTo(x, y, z, speed). Those go through GroundPathNavigation.findSurfacePosition,
    which lifts a solid target to the first non-solid block above it. A diagnostic trace
    printed the path target as the bale's y+1. So the goal was asking "can the horse stand
    ON the bale?" Under open sky it can, by jumping. Under the pen's glass lid (two blocks
    over the floor), or on a bale with another bale stacked on it, it cannot, so every
    search ignored the bale.
  The fix: HungerFoodGoal.pathTo(pos) calls createPath(Set.of(pos, pos.above()),
  REACH_ACCURACY). The Set overload is not overridden, so nothing is lifted, and pos.above()
  keeps "standing on it" valid for grass. The reach test and the pursuit's walk both use it.
  Crops are not solid, so they were never lifted, and the fence behaviour is unchanged.
  Proof:
  - New gametests hay_under_a_low_roof_is_eaten (the yard's W1 cell rebuilt block for
    block, vanilla-size wild-type horse, hunger 20) and stacked_hay_is_eaten (the same cell
    open to the sky, two bales stacked).
  - Both go red with HungerFoodGoal reverted (hunger 19, bale still there).
  - Both go green with the fix. All 53 gametests passed.
  - :neoforge-26.1.2:build green; check-parity OK.
  Issue #26 is CLOSED, with a proof comment.
- Docs:
  - compatibility.html: the Verification-tab check "a hungry horse still walks to a plain
    hay bale" is moved to the page body (#bales-reached).
  - horse-care.html#beside-not-on corrects the reach paragraph, which said a manhattan-1
    path reaches a bale the horse stands beside. It did not.
