DONE
- 8e477dbd "#35: a horse that grew could be left inside the blocks around it".
  What was wrong: almost every horse is placed at vanilla size and grown afterwards.
  Natural spawns, the cowboy (CowboyHandler.roomForAHorse) and the wild top-up
  (WildTopUp) check room for a scale-1 horse, and HorseFoundingTickHandler writes the
  breed's real SCALE 1-20 ticks later. A reloaded horse is rebuilt at scale 1 and grows
  back on its first tick, and a foal grows up where it stands. All of these end in
  vanilla's Entity.refreshDimensions, and its fudgePositionAfterSizeChange gives up
  silently when nothing within the size difference is clear. Its fallback also places
  for the new width at the OLD height and then keeps the taller box, and the whole nudge
  is skipped for a box over 4 blocks (magical giants). Under a two-block ceiling, a horse
  of scale 1.32 or more is left with its eyes in the stone.
  The fix: the new neoforge/server/HorseClearance notes each horse that grows
  (EntityEvent.Size; it only stores the id, since the event can fire mid-load). On its
  EntityTickEvent.Post, a horse whose box still collides is moved to the nearest clear
  spot within 3 blocks, or failing that, if its eyes are buried, lifted to the
  MOTION_BLOCKING_NO_LEAVES surface. The shell search is LycanthropyHandler's old
  clearOfBlocks, moved into HorseClearance; the lycanthropy restore now calls it. Every
  move logs "[clearance] <uuid> grew into blocks at x, y, z (scale s) - ..." through
  HorseGenetics.LOGGER, so it is logged with or without debug tools.
  Proof: new gametest grown_horse_leaves_the_ceiling (ModGameTests, registered at 200
  ticks) grows a horse from 1.0 to 1.75 under a two-block stone ceiling. With the
  handler disabled it FAILS ("still inside the blocks"). With it, all 48 required
  gametests pass. :neoforge-26.1.2:build green, check-parity OK, check-links OK.
  Wiki: horse-body.html#growing-room.
  Issue #35: OPEN, commented with the evidence. It waits on the live server running the
  next release: [clearance] lines and no [suffrescue] lines close it. A rescue with no
  clearance line before it for the same UUID means horses reach walls some other way.
