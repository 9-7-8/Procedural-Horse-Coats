What the shipped code already does for it

    Dyed leather horse armour is in the pool, tinted with the mod's own three-zone tack_tint,
    and it is the heaviest entry in that pool on purpose: weight 10 of 29, inside a 22% per-chest
    roll, over every table whose id matches [^:]+:chests/.+ — every chest in the game, vanilla or
    modded. About one chest in thirteen.

    "Loot tables that contain normal human leather armor" has no literal implementation: a global
    loot modifier is handed the rolled stacks, not the table's pools, so there is nothing to test
    a table's contents against. Every vanilla table that hands out a leather armour piece is a
    chests/ table, so the regex is a superset of the ask rather than an approximation of it —
    though nobody has enumerated vanilla's tables to prove that, and it is an assumption, not a
    check that was run.
