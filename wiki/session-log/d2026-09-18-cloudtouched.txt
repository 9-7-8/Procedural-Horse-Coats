2026-09-18, after the pull session — A flying breed that could not fly

    Cloudtouched, a second magical breed, from a design a friend of the
    owner wrote and handed over as JSON. The brief was: make it a base breed, make it
    super rare, make it a genetic example of a true flyer, and make sure it has
    bird-boned. See the breed book and the file
    itself, which carries the reasoning in its notes.

    The design pinned no flight gene at all. That is the whole entry,
    really. It pinned barred wing — which is a
    butterfly marking drawn across the ribs, with two paint layers per expression and zero
    effects blocks — and nothing else that leaves the ground. Because
    flying is registered MAGIC_BEHAVIOUR, and
    BreedFounder.roll forces every unnamed magical locus to wild
    type, every founder would have rolled n/n. The breed would have shipped
    as a fast jumper with a marking that looks like a wing, and nothing in the build would
    have said a word about it — no warning, no test, no log line. Worth knowing in
    general: for coat, magical and disorder loci, omitting a gene is not “leave it
    natural”, it is “homozygous wild type”. Only the natural performance
    loci fall through to the gene's own founder table.

    The flight is a reservoir, not a supply. The pool seeds
    El/n, El/Tf and Tf/n and never
    Tf/Tf, which honours the flying gene's own
    owner's call from the day before — true flight is bred for rather than found,
    and a glider may be carrying it without anyone knowing. Here that carrying actually
    happens at a findable rate: Tf sits at 28% allele frequency, so two
    Cloudtouched bred at random throw a true flier about one foal in thirteen and two
    known gliders one in four. n/n is absent from the pool, so there is no
    such thing as an ordinary one — the quiet two-thirds are Tf/n,
    which expresses nothing at all.

    Balanced against the other 122 breeds rather than in isolation, which
    is the part that needed the sheets open. Run through
    BreedStatCurve.factor, the original statline came out
    ×1.92 speed, ×1.50 health and ×2.94 jump — near the ceiling on
    all three at once, which no shipped breed is. The Thoroughbred pays for ×2.02
    speed with health 4; the Zangersheide pays for ×3.43 jump with speed 8. This one
    paid with nothing, and then also flew. It ships at speed 8, jump 9, health 4, pull 2:
    hollow-boned, fast, fragile, useless in harness. Speed 8 rather than the 9.5 asked for
    because airspeed is a pure function of MOVEMENT_SPEED —
    HorseFlightMixin cruises at 1.1× to 2.5× ground speed —
    and 9.5 would have put a horse past a rocket-boosted elytra at about 36 blocks a
    second.

    Bird-boned is in at 10% expressing, not the 50% the design asked for.
    Its own founder table is CARRIERS_ONLY: no wild horse anywhere expresses
    it, so this breed is the thing that makes it findable at all. But flight plus
    bird-boned is unconditional fall immunity that covers the rider too, at zero stat
    cost, and the two genes' comments say in four separate places that breeding them
    together is meant to be the point. Handing both to every founder would have retired
    the combination they were built to reward.

    Dropped twelve loci the design pinned at uniform weight. No other
    breed pins any of them, and the reason matters: naming a locus does not nudge its
    frequencies, it replaces the founder table outright and rescales to 100. So
    “every pair at weight 1” is a uniform distribution, which is neither the
    gene's natural rate nor Hardy-Weinberg. Egg layer
    was the worst of them — it is deliberately 0% expressing in the wild, because a
    horse that lays something worth having is meant to come out of the splice layer, and
    the uniform pool would have had about 18% of Cloudtouched dropping items on a
    3–12 minute timer. Kept the cosmetic identity (barred wing, both iris loci, mane
    and tail colour) and, on the owner's call, the weather affinity — leant towards
    storms, since a horse that comes alive in the weather that grounds everything else is
    the right flavour, with stacked pairs held to single figures because both copies emit
    their own multiply_total. Added grey at 76%, which is what makes them
    pale.

    Found on the way past: stats.jump and
    stats.health accept [lo, hi] syntactically and then silently
    discard hi, while speed, size and
    pull honour it and a strain's stats honour all five. The
    breed-format page says all four take a range. Nothing catches it, because the writer
    re-emits the collapsed point and the round-trip test passes.
    Gap 278.
