- bd2df7df "Fix #2: stables floated over valleys because the jigsaw sat them at their
  centre's height".
  Wrong: a minecraft:jigsaw structure gives a rigid building one height, the surface
  under its centre (project_start_to_heightmap), and never looks at the rest of the
  footprint. A stable started on a hilltop ran on over the valley, and beard_box filled
  only part of the drop, leaving the hollow slab in the report's screenshot.
  Change: the three stables (horse_stable, stables, tm_u_stable) are now
  "type": "horsegenetics:level_jigsaw" with "max_ground_spread": 10.
  - neoforge worldgen/LevelJigsawStructure wraps a JigsawStructure (vanilla's is final).
    Its codec is JigsawStructure.CODEC plus max_ground_spread. It builds the pieces in
    findGenerationPoint, samples first-free WORLD_SURFACE_WG height at the footprint's
    four corners and the start piece's centre, and either returns empty or moves the
    builder down to the lowest sample. Its own StructureSettings come from the same JSON,
    so structure modifiers and the beardifier see it.
  - worldgen/ModStructureTypes registers the type (wired in HorseGenetics).
  - common/stable/StableSite.fit is the rule (typed Fit: accepted, spread, drop; it
    never raises a stable).
  - worldgen/StableSiteCensus + the gametest stables_stand_on_level_ground generate every
    stable chunk near spawn twice on the same seeds: the wrapped vanilla jigsaw (control)
    and the real structure, re-measuring the ground under each kept stable.
  Proof:
  - Reproduced red first: with max_ground_spread at 64, the gametest failed with "a
    stable stands on ground spreading 62 blocks (limit 6)".
  - 12 seeds, 216 sites: control 95 over 10 blocks (worst 116); median spread
    horse_stable 7, stables 10, tm_u_stable 12. At limit 6: 73 kept (33.8%). At limit
    10: 121 kept (56.0%), worst kept spread 10.
  - :neoforge-26.1.2:runGameTest, all 27 required tests passed (twice, at 6 and at 10).
    :common:test --tests '*StableSiteTest' green. :neoforge-26.1.2:build green.
    check-parity OK.
