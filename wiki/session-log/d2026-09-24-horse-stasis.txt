The system, as specified

    Four tiers of stasis-chamber item, each the last plus one ingredient (Basic: bottle + wheat
    + a water container, matched by tag so any modded bucket-equivalent works, not consumed on
    craft + horse hair; Intermediate: + eye of ender; Advanced: + gold ingot; Spacer: + diamond),
    each tier unlocking one more thing the bank can do with a horse stored in it — nothing
    searchable, then everything searchable, then milk/drop collection into a 9-slot buffer, then
    in-bank breeding with the foal popping out. The Horse Stasis Bank itself is a block (5 wood
    in a U, an ender pearl centre, iron corners, a horse hair top-centre) with a two-tab menu —
    Storage and Browse — modelled explicitly on the Equine Research Shelf's own tabbed menu. The
    whole point is server cost: horses in stasis do not tick at all (no hunger, no growing up)
    except for a slow food/water-gated heal the bank can optionally provide. The Emergency Horse
    Stasis Chamber (a basic chamber ringed with ender pearls) auto-captures an owned horse the
    instant its health crosses 10%, since a horse cannot take damage while in any chamber.
