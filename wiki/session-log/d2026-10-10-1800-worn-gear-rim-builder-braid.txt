What was built:
- common/gear/RimOutline: the boundary between painted and unpainted texels on one face,
  merged into straight runs. It is also asked about the texel just beyond the face on
  each side, which is how a caller says what lies past the face's end.
- common/gear/RimMesh: one baked box (its faces as plain floats) plus a texture's painted
  texels -> quads. Each touched face is moved out by the lift and grown sideways by the
  same amount, so a box's lifted faces still meet at its corners. One wall per run goes
  from the lifted face down to the skin, textured along the middle of the texels it
  borders (so it takes their colour). No wall is built where the piece carries on round
  a corner of the box: the neighbouring face is found geometrically on the box, not by
  position on the sheet.
- common/gear/WornRule: FLOOR = 0.25 model units; lift() refuses anything under it.
- common/gear/WornCache: a capped least-recently-used cache.
- neoforge client/gear/WornMeshes: walks any baked ModelPart tree (ModelPart.visit),
  reads any pack texture's pixels, caches per (texture, model root by identity, lift),
  cap 64, and draws through submitCustomGeometry after walking the pose stack down the
  bone chain. Falls back to "flat" (the caller draws the old way) for an unreadable
  texture or a mesh over RimMesh.QUAD_LIMIT (4096), with one log line. Cleared in
  CoatAssetReload beside the coats.
- BraidLayer: draws the lifted mesh at WornRule.FLOOR when ClientConfig gear.edges is on,
  and its old flat submitModel when it is off or no mesh exists.
- ClientConfig: gear.edges, boolean, default true, safe accessor gearEdges().
- Photo shoot tool: DebugPhotoShoot.braids(maneRgb, tailRgb); DebugPhotoShootClient takes
  a shot whose name ends "-flat" with gear.edges off (in memory) and restores it.

DECIDED
-------
None by the owner this session. Calls made without her, all within "Recommended" or
"Open (building session)" lines of #239, recorded on wiki/model-parts.html#worn-geometry:
- The mesh is drawn with submitCustomGeometry, not by putting polygons into baked cubes.
  Reason: a cube with foreign polygons is something no other code expects. Cost: a lifted
  piece adds nothing to a glowing horse's outline.
- The mesh geometry (RimMesh) lives in common/, not only the outline, so a plain test pins
  it. The treatment put only the outline there.
- The cache (WornCache) is in common/gear and tested there, because the NeoForge module's
  test classpath carries no Minecraft. The treatment asked for a NeoForge unit test.
- Lifted faces are grown sideways by the lift, and walls are suppressed across a box
  corner the piece wraps.
- The face limit is 4096 quads per mesh. Measured: vanilla's seven horse armour sheets
  have 138 to 330 boundary runs each over the whole 64 px sheet.
- The braid's lift stays at the 0.25 floor. Raising it would only fatten the hair,
  because the masks have no edge. The owner's eye decides (Verification tab).
- The cache cap is the constant 64, not a config key.
- The config key is gear.edges, as recommended.
