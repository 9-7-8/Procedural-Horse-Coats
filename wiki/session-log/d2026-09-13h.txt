2026-09-13, late — fertility and gestation, designed with the owner and built

    The in-progress design page from the evening had seven open questions. This session put
    them to the owner one at a time, along with every implementation choice the breeding
    code raised, then built the whole first slice. The calls are under
    settled decisions, and the reference is
    fertility & gestation. Nothing has been played:
    §0-DV, test kit batch 1.

    - Where the line was drawn. Plain golden carrots stay instant; the
        owner's “we don't remove vanilla behavior” was the constraint that shaped
        every other answer. Pregnancy lives on the mod's own paths: the seed jar, a
        carrot-armed pair, the Spontaneous Breeding gene. The one thing fertility does to
        vanilla is the subfertile allele, which no unbred horse has.

    - common/repro: timing scaled from one
        gestation_days and floored at a day; a mare's state derived
        from a UUID phase and three ticks rather than stepped; and Conception,
        the single draw. The genome is drawn at conception and stored as embryos carrying the
        sire's whole sample, so birth needs no sire. 28 tests.

    - The fertility gene: a natural Java
        gene with n, sf and tw and a Gaussian number
        per copy. Both coat goldens moved only because the genotype code gained a locus: the
        whole-horse file was checked byte-identical with the token stripped before it was
        copied back.

    - The owner changed breeding carrots mid-build. They now wait on a horse
        until a conception uses them, where they had been a 30-second window that also started
        love. CarrotWindowAttachment became ArmedCarrotsAttachment, a
        jar carries a stallion's, and the yard's carrot pens were re-signed.

    - Checked, not assumed: vanilla's
        spawnChildFromBreeding resets love and sets both cooldowns when
        BabyEntitySpawnEvent is cancelled (the patched 26.1.2 sources), which is
        what lets a carrot pair become a pregnancy with no extra bookkeeping.

    - Found on the way: impossible KIT / MITF / PAX3 genotypes may be
        breedable (gap 225, a code read only).

    - Deliberately not built: wild band breeding (its own slice, on
        the roadmap), body condition, seasons, a stallion
        covers line on the info screen, and revealing twins before birth.
