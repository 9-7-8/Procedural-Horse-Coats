2026-09-15, late afternoon — the pen that was lying about the ward, and a fix that took two tries

    A short session, picking up where the 15:36 run stopped. Its one standing FAIL — the SPAWNER WARD pen reading
    0 of 16 and blaming the holy ward — was the pen, not the gene. That is now the fourth time this
    yard has accused a working feature, and the reason the reading is written down rather than the verdict.

    - The ward was never involved. Every meal logged [Spawner] made 2 of 2 Zombie, no
        [Ward] refusal line appeared all run, and both zombies joined the level — then vanished about
        40ms later. A summoned mob carries no persistence by design, and vanilla's Mob.checkDespawn
        takes a monster on its first mob tick when no player is near, which in an unattended yard is always. The pen
        counted five ticks after the feed, long after the bodies were gone. The sheep pens never hit this because a
        vanilla Animal does not despawn, which is why this was the only pen that failed.

    - The first fix was wrong, and was pushed before it was run (b9915ce5). It moved the
        count into the feed's own tick, on the assumption that the summon happens inside
        CommonHooks.onInteractEntity. It does not: GeneAbilityHandler.onFeed queues a
        PendingFeed and drainPendingFeeds summons on ServerTickEvent.Post, at the
        end of the tick. The 16:05 run showed it exactly — the count logged at 16:06:01.675, the zombies
        arrived at .683 and .689 — so it read 0 of 16 again, now by counting before they existed.

    - The fix that worked stops timing them altogether (751de924). The live window is
        about one tick wide, so every tick number is a race. While a meal is armed, an
        EntityJoinLevelEvent handler gives each arriving zombie setPersistenceRequired —
        what DebugYardHerd already does for the DAM DEFENCE zombie — and the original five-tick sweep
        counts and discards them unchanged. Nothing is cancelled, so made 2 of 2, which is the measurement
        itself, is untouched. Verified in the 16:11 run: eight meals at 2 zombie(s),
        16 made, 2 inside the ward, PASS
