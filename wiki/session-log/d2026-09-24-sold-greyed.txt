2026-09-24, after the pasture-edge fix — a horse you sold is greyed out, not gone

    One bullet, the first in the Bugs section of intake/To do.txt, in the owner's words:

        Horses that have been sold should remain in the family tree, but appear greyed out
        rather than being removed.

    Dated first, as START HERE says to. The bullet went into To do.txt on 2026-09-23
    (27cd45aa) and nothing since had touched selling or the tree, so it was open — but
    "open" turned out to mean something different from what the bullet says.

The premise is already true, and that is the finding

    Nothing removes a sold horse from a pedigree, and nothing ever did. A transfer paper
    sets the new owner, clears the brand and touches nothing else — TransferPaperHandler
    says so in its own class doc, "ownership is the only thing that moves". The cowboy sells
    a signed paper, not a horse. HorseOwnerTrackingHandler mirrors the new owner onto the
    record, which is a rewrite, never a delete. The only caller of
    HorseAncestryData.forget in the repository is the debug pen cleaner. A sold horse is in
    the tree, in its place, with its coat and its parents.

    So the defect was not the removal. It was that the chart said nothing: FamilyTreeScreen
    had exactly two box states, "record present" and "record absent", and a stable you had
    sold half of looked exactly like one you had sold none of. The owner read an absence of
    evidence as an absence of the horse, which is a fair reading of a screen that will not
    say. That is worth keeping: the ask named a removal that does not happen, and the fix it
    asked for was still the right fix.

What changed

    A third box state. A horse owned by somebody who is not the viewer draws dim — panel,
    border, name, attribution — and a scrim goes over the inside of the box. The scrim is
    there because the coat is drawn by an entity renderer that takes no tint from the
    screen; without it the horse sits bright inside a grey frame and reads as a rendering
    bug. The box keeps its place and stays clickable, since a sold horse is still a horse
    you want to re-root on.

    Two new predicates, both in common/ so the backport carries them:

        HorseRecord.ownedByAnother(viewer) — an owner is recorded and it is not you.
        Deliberately not !ownedBy(viewer). A founder, a wild ancestor and any horse whose
        owner was never mirrored are owned by nobody, and treating those as somebody
        else's would grey out most of a pedigree — the failure mode this predicate exists
        to avoid, and the one to look for if a tree ever comes up mostly grey.

        HorseRecord.soldOnBy(viewerName, viewer) — credited to you and owned by somebody
        else, which is the narrower claim the word "sold" makes. A stallion borrowed for
        one covering is another player's horse that was never yours to sell. It only
        decides the "sold - by" prefix on the second line.

    Ownership is a UUID and attribution (bredBy, else tamedBy) is a username, so the two
    comparisons are made on different things by necessity: two players can share a display
    name, and only one of them owns the horse.

Scope

    The family overview — the whole-world chart from the Everyone button — dims nothing.
    PopulationDataPayload carries a name, a sex, a generation and two parent ids and no
    owner, and on a chart of every horse a world has ever bred most boxes are somebody
    else's or nobody's, so the marking would be noise rather than information. Left alone
    deliberately; noted on breeding.html#sold-greyed rather than left as a surprise.

    HorseRoster still lists a horse you sold under My horses, because it keeps anything
    tamedBy you. That is the same "history is not current ownership" principle from the
    other side and was not touched.

Not played

    Two unit tests on the predicates (HorseRecordTest — anotherPlayersHorseIsNotTheSameAsAnUnownedOne,
    soldOnMeansItWasYoursAndIsNot); the drawing itself is a screen and has none. The checks
    are on breeding.html#sold-greyed-0924 and need two players to see at all, since a sold
    horse means somebody to sell it to. The one that catches an overshoot is "nothing else
    greys": founders, wild ancestors and horses you still own must look untouched.

    A sold horse whose owner was never mirrored — nobody has loaded it since
    HorseOwnerTrackingHandler existed — has no ownerId and will not dim. It fails to the
    old appearance rather than to a wrong one, which is the right way round.
