The inventory beside the doll

    The owner's words, 2026-09-25: "the player's inventory must be visible on the right side of
    the gear screen, so they can equip/unequip items". It had sat on horse-gear.html's Roadmap
    tab with a full treatment in intake/, gated on one decision: convert HorseInfoScreen into a
    container screen, or open a separate one. Asked as options; the owner took the separate
    window.

    Why that was the recommendation: HorseInfoScreen shows horses that are not loaded entities
    (the Offspring tab, the family tree), and is opened by the client from four places, while a
    container menu needs a live entity and can only be opened by the server. A mandatory menu
    under that screen could not be right.

    What was built - menu/HorseGearMenu, client/HorseGearScreen, network/OpenHorseGearPayload,
    ModNetworking.handleOpenHorseGear, a Dress button on the Gear tab. Written up on
    horse-gear.html's Coding tab (#gear-inventory, the anchor kept from the Roadmap entry).
    The part worth remembering: the server-side tack slots are a Container view over the horse
    whose reads and writes are HorseTackSlot.on/set, so the menu keeps no copy of the gear, and
    every rule the old packet checked is a slot rule delegated to HorseTackSlot. One rule the
    treatment did not list turned up on review: a double-click's pick-all would have pulled the
    worn saddle off the horse when a spare was double-clicked in the bag.

    Kept: the click picker. The treatment's step 8 asked whether it should go once dragging
    exists; both stay, since the picker works on the read-only tab and the window does not.
