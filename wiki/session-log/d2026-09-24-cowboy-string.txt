2026-09-24, after the mouth reach — a cowboy's string fights for him, and stops the moment he
falls

    One bullet, the top of the Bugs section of intake/To do.txt, in the owner's words:

        Attacking a cowboy makes his horses aggro and the cowboy flee, but killing him makes
        the horses wild and tamable. Add a way to obtain cowboy horses without buying them
        with emeralds.

    Dated first, per START HERE. The bullet went in on 2026-09-23; the kill-clears-the-brand
    half has been in CowboyHandler.onCowboyDied since 2026-09-07 and is written up on
    villagers.html. Bullet newer than the fix, so not stale — and reading the code turned the
    sentence inside out.

What was actually true

    Three claims in one sentence, and only two of them were code.

        the cowboy flees            built — Cowboy.java:199, a vanilla PanicGoal, which
                                    triggers on getLastHurtByMob
        his horses aggro            NOT BUILT. Nothing anywhere set a herd horse's target
                                    when the man was hurt.
        killing him frees the herd  built since 2026-09-07, and lazily re-checked in
                                    CowboyHandler.ownerOf for herd members that were not
                                    loaded at the time

    So the sentence was not a bug report. It was a description of the deterrent the owner
    expected to be there, written from the outside, and the emerald-free route it is attached
    to already existed — it was simply free. You walked up to a man with 200 HP who runs away,
    killed him, and six horses watched you do it and were then yours for the climbing on.

    A second, quieter gap fell out of the same read. HorseAggroHandler's herd-mate alert is
    gated on care.inWildHerd(), and a branded horse is not in a wild herd: CowboyHandler.settle
    puts the COWBOY's UUID in HorseCareAttachment.herd, and inWildHerd() wants a herdBreed that
    a cowboy's horse has never had. So sameHerd said no to two horses standing side by side in
    the same man's string, and hitting one of them rallied nobody either.

What changed

    HorseAggroHandler.onCowboyHurt — every branded horse within the herd-alert radius of the
    cowboy takes the attacker as its target. liveHerd covers loaded horses only, which is the
    right set: an unloaded one could not have seen it happen. Not restricted to players, though
    in practice only a player can get there, because CowboySafetyHandler already makes an Enemy
    incapable of targeting him.

    sameHerd asks the brand before it asks the care attachment, so a string counts as a herd and
    the mate alert works on it. That is the half the owner did not ask for and would have
    noticed the first time she hit one horse and the other five grazed on.

    CowboyHerdGoal yields while the horse has a target. This is the part that would have made
    the rest of it look broken. It and HorseMeleeGoal are both at priority 3 and both hold
    Flag.MOVE, and WrappedGoal.canBeReplacedBy needs a STRICTLY lower number — so whichever
    took movement first kept it, and a horse strung out behind the man when the fight started
    would have walked to heel with the melee goal starving behind it. Exactly the failure the
    panic goal had on 2026-09-13 ("the guardian makes a half-hearted attempt... runs off, then
    gets chased by the zombie and gets killed by it"), and behaviour-hierarchy.html had the
    collision listed at priority 3 the whole time.

The owner's correction, mid-session

        The horses should de-aggro after the cowboy is dead

    Right, and it is not a detail. The three-second line-of-sight forget in
    WildHorseForgetTargetGoal would have cleared it eventually, but "eventually" here means the
    player backing round a corner from the animals they had just freed — the emerald-free route
    turned into a fight you have to win twice, once against the man and once against the thing
    you came for. onCowboyDied now drops each horse's target and lastHurtByMob alongside the
    brand, in the same loop.

What this does not do

    The second half of the bullet — "a way to obtain cowboy horses without buying them with
    emeralds" — is still one way, and that way is murder. The owner chose "both, aggro first":
    ship the deterrent, write the honest route up as a plan. villagers.html#honest-route now
    carries four shapes for it (barter he wants, work done for him, a horse in part-exchange,
    gift on standing) with the cheapest named — Cowboy.updateTrades already builds one offer per
    horse, so a second ItemCost is nearly the whole change — and one constraint on whichever is
    picked: it must not be strictly cheaper than robbery, or the two routes collapse into one.

Not verified

    Nothing here is unit-testable; it is four goals arguing over Flag.MOVE in a live level, and
    the neoforge module has one test and it is about material scanning. villagers.html grew
