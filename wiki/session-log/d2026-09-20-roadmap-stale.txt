2026-09-20, after the roadmap cut — Three entries that described a mod from a fortnight ago

    The owner read the trimmed roadmap and said two of its entries were describing work that is
    in the game. They were right, and a third turned up on the sweep that followed.
    The cut had moved the page’s sections around faithfully and checked almost
    none of them against the code — which is the same failure the page exists to
    prevent, committed while tidying the page that exists to prevent it.

    - “Something has to read pulling ability” is done twice over.
        HorseDraft reads traitsOf(horse).pull(),
        AbstractDrawnEntity.applyDraught spends it, and
        CartDraft.retention turns it into a speed ceiling. The entry also said the
        number needed somewhere to be seen: the information screen prints a Pull row with a word
        for it, a Draught block giving empty and laden retention, whether the horse carries a
        second rider, and a lifetime hauled distance that survives being sold. And the “what
        happens above 10” question it parked is answered in CartDraft's own
        comment — the score is clamped at the floor and deliberately not at the
        top. Written up at carts §12 and §13.

    - The diet and vampire rows are the same mistake.
        Diet ships a wild type and nine narrow diets — meat,
        fish, wheat, cake, potions, lava, one metal, one gem and blood — with a carrot,
        feral-only inheritance and a documented override contract with
        food preference. And “vampire horses: heal
        only by damaging other living mobs, optionally take sun damage” is the
        blood diet composed with
        sun sensitivity, down to the detail that a
        sun-sensitive blood-drinker shelters first and eats after dark. Both rows went, and with
        them the whole behavioural-gene family section, which had nothing else left in it.

    - Two players on one horse, found on the sweep.
        HorseTwoRidersMixin gates a second seat on
        CartDraft.TWO_RIDER_PULL, places the pillion along the horse's body axis
        rotated by yaw, and squares it with the wagon's hidden postilion so a driver on the box
        and a rider on the horse coexist. The three questions the entry said to settle first are
        not open either: the rear mixin only touches isImmobile and ejects nobody,
        and the flight mixin works through getRiddenInput on the controlling
        passenger, so a pillion is carried by construction. The in-game check was already on
        the checklist.

    Gap 277 went with them — “pulling
    ability is a number nothing reads, shows, or can be checked against” was false in its
    headline and in most of its body. Its one surviving point, that the debug yard has no pull
    pen because the stat pens read a named vanilla attribute and pull is not one, was not
    re-filed: the information screen now answers the question the pen was for.

    The rest of the page was then swept rather than trusted, which is the part
    worth keeping. Every remaining entry in “asked for, and not built” was checked
    against the source: no chase gene, no grab gene, no storm-fed reservoir, no block-preference
    locus, no kelpie, no momentum sprint, no lasso or rope item, no horse chest, no horse shoes,
    no weather key in BreedSpecParser, nothing touching
    startSleeping or setRespawnPosition, and no checkbox rule anywhere
    in styles.css. The gameplay layer checked out too —
    RESEARCH_GENE is still a bare gene-key String, there is no
    collectedFrom, no bonding tab and no per-copy epigenetics editor. Those entries
    stand.

    And it happened again mid-session. A jump-styles commit landed on
    main while this was being written, shipping the oxer that the obstacles entry
    listed as missing. That entry now names the three styles that exist and drops the oxer from
    the wanted list — and it gained the thing the commit makes sharper rather than softer:
    all three styles collide identically on purpose, so a stack of three means the same
    thing whatever it is built from, and anything that made a style matter mechanically would be
    taking that back. The owner’s one-block-deep rule is recorded with it, since it bounds
    every obstacle still on the list.

    The lesson is cheap to state and was expensive here: moving an entry is not
    reading it. A backlog entry is a claim about the current code, and a session that
    reorganises one without opening the code is laundering its age — the entries came out
    of the cut looking freshly reviewed and three of them were describing a mod from a fortnight
    earlier. The grep that settles it is usually one line, and all three of these fell to one.
