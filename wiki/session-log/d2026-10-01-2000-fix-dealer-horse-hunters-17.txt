- d0e6d6ad - Fix #17: an arcane dealer's horses killed each other in his pen.
  Cause: ArcaneStock.REQUIRED_FAMILIES gives every dealer horse one showing
  MAGIC_BEHAVIOUR gene, and ArcaneStock.eligible let aggression in with all its pairs.
  The pairs that hunt "horses" or "all" therefore reached a string that stands within 5
  blocks of itself. On the live server five dealers lost between 6 and 12 unsold horses
  each (24 and 27 Sep).
  Fix (the owner's option 1): ArcaneStock.stockedPairs(gene) is the showing pairs minus
  those for which ArcaneStock.huntsHorses(gene, pair) is true. A pair hunts horses when
  its own abilities hold an aggressive GeneAbility.Temper with a Continuous trigger
  toward "horses" or "all". eligible, pool and showingTokens all go through
  stockedPairs. Guardian (also aggressive toward "all") is still stocked, because its
  trigger is OnOwnerHurt and unsold stock has no owner.
  To ask one gene cheaply, HorseAbilities gains of(gene, genotype, epigenome), the body
  of activeFor's loop lifted out; activeFor now loops over it, with no behaviour change.
  Proof: ArcaneStockTest.noStockedHorseHuntsHorses walks every pooled pair (the required
  families plus the optional one) and restates the rule against HorseAbilities.of. It
  also asserts that aggression is still on sale. Red on the old code
  ("aggression=Aaa/Aaa starts fights with all"), green with the fix, and red again with
  huntsHorses forced to return false. The other HorseAbilities users are green:
  ChaosMobLociTest, HornGenesTest, LycanGeneTest, MoltenHoovesGeneTest, ParticleGeneTest,
  RainbowDustGeneTest, SpawnerGeneTest and GeneAbilitySpecTest.
  :neoforge-26.1.2:build green, check-parity OK, :web:bakeDesignerAssets +
  check-designer-boots OK (web.wasm committed: reachable common/ code changed).
  Wiki: villagers.html#arcane-no-horse-hunters (new section), and one paragraph at the
  end of the gene-aggression.html gameplay tab. Search index, agent text and check-links
  rerun.
  Issue #17: CLOSED by the commit, with a proof comment. The comment notes that a running
  dealer who already holds a horse-hunter keeps it until it sells, rotates out or he
  leaves.
