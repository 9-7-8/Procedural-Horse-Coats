2026-09-17, later — Ordinary markings get a locus, and SW1 gives back a job

    “Roadmap natural genes”, taken one at a time per the
    owner’s instruction, starting with the biggest: the star, stripe, snip, sock and
    stocking that a horse can have without carrying any white pattern gene. The
    audit at the start of the day had established these did not exist, contrary to
    expectation — no markings locus was registered, and nothing outside the four
    allele-gated white loci drew a star.

An additive locus, not an allele-free roll

    Markings is a dosage locus in the
    sooty / shade shape:
    M2, M1 and m stacking to a score from a coronet
    up to a broad blaze and high stockings. The alternative considered and rejected was an
    allele-free per-horse score, which would have been cheaper and carried no genotype-code
    slot. The locus won because a breeder can select for it — the
    dosage inherits Mendelianly, shows in the code and prints on a paper — and because
    an allele-free marking could only be inherited through epigenetic drift.

    It runs at priority 65, before every white pattern locus (tobiano is
    72, and the rest run to 79), so KIT,
    MITF, PAX3 and
    EDNRB stack their white on top of it. A sabino
    horse with three markings copies is a horse with a blaze that its sabino then widens,
    which is the right relationship and not two genes fighting over one face.

    The face is drawn with WhitePattern.faceMarking — the same call the
    white loci use, so a markings blaze and a sabino blaze cannot drift apart; only the
    jag differs, small here because ordinary markings have a clean edge rather
    than a sabino’s roaned one. Legs use EpiValue.perLeg with a threshold,
    which is what leaves a three-socked horse one clean leg instead of dressing every horse
    in four matching socks. The gene consumes no nextFloat():
    every decision is stored epigenetics, so a horse marks identically every time it is drawn.

SW1 stops doing two jobs

    SW1 was at 55% of founders
    purely because it was standing in for this system — a majority of horses carried a
    splash allele so that the world would contain ordinary marked horses. It is now
    6%, a rate its own evidence supports: the study that identified it found
    no splash allele at all in 112 horses picked for minimal white. An ordinary star is now
    far more likely to be markings than a splash carrier, which is the real-world situation.

The golden covered every natural gene except the new one

    Worth recording as a method. CoatPipelineGoldenTest’s javadoc says its
    codes are “chosen to hit every natural gene”, and when the golden moved,
    decomposing the diff by column showed zero of 1056 coat hashes changed
    and all 1056 fixtures reading markings=m/m. The gene painted nothing in the
    entire regression suite: the file had moved only in its code-string column. Six fixtures
    were added — three dosages on a bay, one on a chestnut, one under sabino and one
    beside splash — taking the golden to 1092 rows, 36 of which now carry a markings
    allele. A golden that moves wholesale can still be testing nothing, and
    the column decomposition is what tells the difference.

A defect this session introduced, found the same way

    The four health gene pages written earlier in the day were missing
    gene-inheritance.js and gene-carrot.js, which every other gene
    page carries, so their inheritance and punnett widgets were inert on the live wiki. Found
    by reading sooty’s page as a template for the markings
    one and noticing two script tags that the PLOD1 page’s foot had not shown. Fixed on
    all four. The lesson is narrow and practical: copy a page template from its
    end as well as its beginning.
