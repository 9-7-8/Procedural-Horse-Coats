  (Bon/Bon). The counts weights were not changed, so the other four parts roll a
  little less often. Recorded at model-parts.html#bone-versions.
- (owner) A two-tone row is two-tone on each spine, base to tip, not along the row.
  Recorded at gene-dorsal-spine-colour.html#colours.

  not foresee that the pose-stack scale would stretch a row lengthwise.
  model-parts.html#row-scale.
- (without the owner) The saddle zone is the vanilla saddle box with a one-unit margin
  each side. That hides spines 1-8 of 11 and keeps one at the withers and two over the
  croup. model-parts.html#saddle-zone.
- (without the owner) The count shows from the withers back, the antler tine rule.
  Founders: Dsp/n at 0.3%. Count is wild 5..11, never under 1. The row is 15 units long.
  All of these are first values for the dev yard to judge.
- (treatment) Gate: a drawn saddle or ridden, so a phantom saddle with no rider shows
  the whole row. "Ridden" is EquineRenderState.isRidden (vanilla:
  entity.isVehicle()), read from the 26.1.2 sources.
