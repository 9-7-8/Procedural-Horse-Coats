DECIDED
-------
- Ancestry: prune at removal, only records nothing could ask for again; no sweep of
  old saves (owner). Recorded on breeding.html#ancestry-pruning.
- Dormancy: throttle and catch up, not freeze (owner); on by default at 128 blocks
  (owner). The stride of 10 and the aligned run ticks were this session's call, from
  the read-only survey of every tick gate. horse-care.html#dormancy, #dormancy-code.
- Embryos stop saving the sire's genome, accepting that an older jar cannot load a
  pregnancy a newer one saved (owner). fertility.html, Embryo javadoc.
- Realm backups: keep 3, count off the main thread (owner). horse-realm.html.
- Dormancy never applies with debug.tools on, so the yard and the debug corridor are
  unaffected (owner, at end-session; commit 3f687ebf). horse-care.html#dormancy-code.
  The yard therefore cannot check dormancy: horse-care.html#dormancy-1006 needs a world
  with debug tools off.
- Not asked, decided here: a stallion who has ever covered is never pruned (it is what
  makes dropping the sire copy safe); LAST_FIRED cooldowns are not evicted on unload (a
  horse on a chunk edge would get fresh cooldowns); presentCode storage, the meta
  payload, roster compaction and the stasis resend left out (#210); the request
  throttle drops only a repeat of the same question within a second.
