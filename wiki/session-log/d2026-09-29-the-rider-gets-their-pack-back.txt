    The survey, briefly. Three bullets in To do.txt and seven treatment files.
    Everything but the loot bullet is a whole system (troughs, horse shoes,
    the gear screen, the runtime-registry genes, attached model parts, undead

    marked Decided in its own treatment, which no other candidate did.

    The loot bullet was re-surveyed and the previous session's reading holds:
    AddHorseTackModifier already injects dyed leather horse armour into every
    chests/* table, but the bullet's second clause - "loot tables that CONTAIN
    normal human leather armor" - is genuinely unbuilt, because
    LootTableMatchesCondition matches a table's path and not its contents.
    Expressing it needs a datapack-load scan of table contents, and the first
    clause is a tuning number the owner deliberately set. That is a
    conversation, not a small build, and it stays parked.

    What shipped. client/RiderInventoryKeyBindings and
    client/RiderInventoryKeyHandler, in the two-class shape
    HorseBrowserKeyBindings/Handler already established, plus the
    key.horsegenetics.rider_inventory lang key. ALT+E opens your own
    inventory; E from the saddle still opens this mod's horse screen. No
    config switch, on the owner's call: an unbound key is already the switch.
    Works on foot too, also her call - a riding-only key makes "it didn't
    open" ambiguous between unbound and guarded-out.

    Two prescribed pieces of code were deleted after reading 26.1.2, and both
    are worth recording because in each case the plan was reasonable and the
    source said it was unnecessary.

    Creative mode needs no branch. The treatment said to pick the screen
    class by game mode. But InventoryScreen's init AND its containerTick both
    open by checking player.hasInfiniteMaterials() and swapping themselves out
    for a CreativeModeInventoryScreen - so constructing the survival screen is
    what vanilla itself does in both modes, and a hand-rolled game-mode test
    would have been a second copy of that rule, wrong the moment the first one
    moved. Verification carries the check that the swap is invisible rather
    than a one-frame flash.

    The chord does not need coordinating with the key it collides with. This
    was the session's real worry: HorseInfoKeyHandler drains vanilla's
    keyInventory in ClientTickEvent.Pre while you ride, so ALT+E firing BOTH
    mappings would put the horse screen on top and swallow the inventory - the
    feature failing precisely where it was wanted, and looking like the key
    not working. NeoForge already handles it. KeyMappingLookup.getAll collects
    the bindings registered under the held modifier first and only then, "if
    (releasing || matchingBindings.isEmpty())", adds the unmodified ones. A
    live ALT+E match is a non-empty list, so the bare-E fallback never runs
    and keyInventory.consumeClick() is never satisfied. Nothing in this mod
    had to change for that.

    Both of those are readings, not runs, so both are flagged UNVERIFIED in
    the source per the house rule and both are on rider-comfort.html's
    Verification tab. This is the mod's first KeyModifier - WhistleKeyBindings
    says in as many words that it avoided a chord to keep the modifier API out
    of it - so the tab also names the fallback the owner pre-agreed: a bare
    key that clashes with nothing here (H, apostrophe, F, F6, F7, F8 are
    taken), shipped and reported, rather than a chord left half-working.

    One small departure from the repo's own convention, deliberately. The
    category is KeyMapping.Category.INVENTORY where every other mapping here
    uses MISC. Still a built-in category with nothing to register or localise,
    so the stated reason for MISC is not violated - but it puts the row
    directly beneath vanilla's "Open/Close Inventory", which is the part of
    Controls a player is reading when they go looking for what happened to E.

    No test, and that is not an omission. The whole of it is a keybind
    registration and a setScreen on the client; the one claim worth checking
    is a fact about NeoForge's input layer that only a real client exercises.
    It is a Verification-tab item rather than a missing gametest, and the tab
    says so.
