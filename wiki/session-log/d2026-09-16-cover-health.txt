2026-09-16, evening — the mare who lost a pregnancy and was never bred again

    “Figure out why the mare who lost a pregnancy stopped getting covered.”
    She was half a heart short of full health, and a natural cover required full health
    exactly. Three deliberate designs, each defensible alone, multiplied into permanent
    sterility: a miscarriage costs the dam half a heart; NaturalCover demanded
    getHealth() >= getMaxHealth(); and healing only happens beside water, which the
    fertility pens did not have. She cycled forever, read In heat, and no stallion ever
    touched her.

Why it took three sessions to see

    Because it logged nothing. The refusal is NOT_A_BREEDING_MARE, and that is the
    verdict NaturalBreedingHandler's switch drops into default with the comment
    “nothing worth a line”. The only refusal the class has ever written is the crowding cap,
    which is why gap 258 was filed against the ratio pens
    — the pens were simply where a guaranteed miscarriage happened often enough to notice. That
    entry told the next session to check whether a stale pregnancy survived the loss. It did not; the
    repro state was correct throughout.

Settled from the logs before touching code

    The 2026-09-15 run was already on disk and answered it outright. Six mares miscarried and
    not one was ever covered again, against 30 of 48 whose cover merely
    did not take and 25 of 62 who foaled normally. Two of the six were their own
    controls: they had run COVER FOALED COVER FOALED COVER for hours and went silent at the
    exact moment of their loss. 21.0/22.0 is the last health line for every one of them
    — none ever healed. That is a within-subject result, not an inference from the code.

What changed

    - The rule is a fraction, not an equality.
        ReproRules.COVER_HEALTH (90%), with
        healthyEnoughToBreed in common/ so it is testable without a world, and
        NaturalCover.Party.fullHealth renamed healthyEnough so the field stops
        lying. ReproRulesTest pins it at exactly the health a miscarriage leaves her.

    - The silence is gone. The health refusal logs once a minute per mare, naming her
        health, throttled the way the crowding cap already was. Only the health case — by then she
        is known to be in heat with a stallion in reach, so ridden and leashed are the player's own doing
        and obvious.

    - Every pen has water, a cauldron sunk into the floor in
        DebugTestYard.fencedPlot, so it reaches all 21 fenced pens and every future one from
        one place. Sunk rather than raised, which is what keeps it clear of gap 247's escape route
        — the owner's existing call for the corridor pens. The splicePair breeding
        pens build their own walls and got one per half.

    The bug was wider than the mare. Exact-equality full health meant a wolf bite or a lost
    stallion fight sterilised any horse until it found water. Not played yet —
    three checks are waiting in §0-DV, and row W should
    finally produce a usable sample for gap 225's ratios.
