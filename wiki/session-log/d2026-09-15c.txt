2026-09-15, evening — what the three tools could not reach, and a breed that shapes its own herds

    The ask was whether the breed designer, the horse designer and the custom spawn egg were up to date. The
    artefacts were: re-running every bake moved nothing of substance — index.html,
    pages.js and the creator's textures.js came back as modified but with identical content,
    line-ending churn only, and were restored. So "up to date" turned out to mean two things the tools could not reach,
    one new feature, and a drifted twin.

    - Fertility was offered on no step at all. horsegenetics.fertility is natural and
        NATURAL_OTHER — and every breed-designer step filters by family or by a named key list, with
        no step covering that family. So a breed could never say anything about subfertility or twinning. It now has a
        Breeding step of its own (owner's choice of the three placements offered).

    - magical_variant was silently dropped on a round trip. The key went into the parser
        earlier the same day; bd-core.js neither read nor wrote it, so a breed file saying it has no magical
        herds lost that on load-and-save. Fixed, with a checkbox on Price and kind.

    - A breed can now say how its wild herds are founded (BreedHerd,
        the format page) — the share founded as bachelor bands, and how
        many stallions and mares each kind of band wants. The order of events is what shaped the design.
        The first cut was a single bachelor_chance number; the owner asked for the full makeup, at which
        point the honest constraint surfaced: vanilla spawns the pack before the breed is chosen
        (HerdManager elects a lead, derives the breed from its seed, then founds over the clump). So the
        sexes are ours to assign exactly, and the counts can only be a preference clamped by the clump — put to
        the owner as "assign within the clump" against "spawn extra horses to reach the minimum", and the first chosen,
        with the limit to be stated on the page. The assignment is made over the whole clump from the lead's seed, which
        is what lets every member agree on who is a stallion without talking to each other. Precedence when it cannot
        have everything: the stallion range, then the mare maximum, then the mare minimum.

    - Family relations were left out on purpose
        (roadmap §15). Doing them honestly means founding rolls a foal
        from the lead and a mare rather than independently from the sheet; stamping mother and father ids
        without the descent was offered and rejected, because the pedigree screen would then assert a relationship the
        genetics contradict.

    - The twin was audited against hard rule 5, and the rule itself was the least accurate document of
        the set: it named variantPair and randomizeGenes, neither of which has existed in
        either file since the identical rules moved to common/EditorRules. Four real drifts fixed —
        Clear rerolled the epigenome in game but not in the browser, paste skipped
        enforceSexLinkage, the browser drew an empty list where the screen says "No gene matches", and the
        dropdown panel had lost the screen's 0xF0 alpha. Both files also claimed Export / Import sit in
        Copy horse's slot; they sit one row higher, in Make egg's. Two findings were left as
        gap 254 and gap 255 rather than
        fixed in the same pass.

    - Hunger needed nothing. Worth writing down, since it was asked: there is no hunger gene.
        common/care/Hunger.java is pure constants driven by the diet gene and Food's order, and
        the designer's Diet step already offers diet and food_preference.

    - Green: :neoforge-26.1.2:build, creator parity (7169 checks across 60 cases),
        check-links, build-search-index, the designer boot check,
        check-ai.mjs (35 checks — which, usefully, already boots the breed designer), and the breed
        test classes including a new BreedHerdTest at 12 of 12. The 50 shipped breed files are deliberately
        unchanged: no breed sets herd, and the writer never writes a default.

    - A one-off headless probe of the breed designer (scratchpad, not checked in) pushed the new block
        through the game's own parser in wasm: 17 checks, all passing. It cost two false starts worth remembering —
        the page will not boot from file:// (it fetches marking-facts.json, so
        boot() never runs and bd.api stays null; check-ai.mjs stands up an HTTP
        server for exactly this reason), and checkBreedJson returns ok, not
        good — asserting the wrong field name reported four failures that were entirely the probe's.

    - Not played. Wild-herd founding changed for every breed, and none of it has been in a world:
        verification §0-ED, which is ranked first because a
        disagreement between clump members fails invisibly — as two herds where there should be one.
