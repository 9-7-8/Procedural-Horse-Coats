2026-09-29 - tack goes on with a click, and two thirds of the piece was already in vanilla


    Rider comfort piece 3, one bullet per intake/START HERE.txt. The piece was
    picked last session as the next in the treatment's own order, and it is
    the one that opened with a design question rather than a build.

    The question, answered by reading. The treatment marked it UNVERIFIED and
    said to read the decompiled AbstractHorse.mobInteract before writing
    anything. Doing so settled both halves of the piece against it:

      - The saddle is vanilla's. Items.SADDLE carries Equippable.saddle(),
        built with setEquipOnInteract(true), so ItemStack.interactLivingEntity
        routes through Equippable.equipOnTarget and the saddle is on before
        mobInteract reaches doPlayerRide. It already declines to swap, too:
        equipOnTarget requires !target.hasItemInSlot(this.slot).

      - The barding is vanilla's as well, which the treatment did not expect.
        mobInteract ends with "if (this.isEquippableInSlot(itemStack,
        EquipmentSlot.BODY) && !this.isWearingBodyArmor())" and equips it. That
        predicate is character-for-character what HorseTackSlot.accepts asks
        for the BARDING slot, so there was provably nothing to add there.

    So the pre-agreed fallback applied - keep the handler for what vanilla does
    not cover, do not take over the saddle, do not retire the piece - except
    that "armour" was in the part vanilla covers too. What shipped is the other
    half of that sentence: the mod's own tack slots.

    What shipped. server/TackEquipHandler, one LOWEST-priority listener on
    PlayerInteractEvent.EntityInteract, plus behaviour.rightclick_equips_tack,
    two lang keys and the right_click_equips_tack gametest. Hold a piece of
    gear, right-click your horse, and it goes into the first empty slot that
    takes it. It skips every isVanilla() slot deliberately - two code paths on
    one saddle would have meant one of them losing, and the loser would have
    been the one with the equip sound and the guaranteed drop.

    A stale comment worth catching. HorseTackSlot's class javadoc says "None of
    those tags has any item in it yet". Two of the seventeen do: gear/mane and
    gear/tail both hold the rescuing braid. So the feature has exactly one item
    to work with today, which is enough to be real and enough to test. It is
    not a special case in the code - the handler walks the roster asking each
    slot's tag, so every piece still to be made arrives wearable by hand the
    day it joins its tag.

    The design call that is not in the treatment. When every slot that would
    take the piece is already full, the treatment says "do nothing - never
    swap". Two readings: refuse the click with a line, or leave it uncancelled.
    Left uncancelled, which means vanilla handles it and you mount the horse.
    The other way you would be unable to get on your own horse while holding a
    spare braid, and nothing is ambiguous anyway - the braid is visibly already
    in the mane. Refusals that DO get claimed are not-your-horse and a foal,
    because both are rules this mod invented, and an invented rule that fails
    silently reads as a bug. An untamed horse is left alone too: vanilla rears
    it up at you, which says it better than a sentence would.

    That split - two refusals claimed, two not - is the thing the gametest
    exists for. right_click_equips_tack posts seven real EntityInteract events
    through NeoForge.EVENT_BUS, so a handler written and never registered fails
    it, and it asserts the claimed and unclaimed cases separately. A handler
    that cancelled all four would look right in game until somebody could not
    mount a horse. The test was mutated before being trusted: making the
    full-slot case cancel turned it red with "a third braid with nowhere left
    to put it: the click was claimed, and it should have been left to vanilla".
    Restored, all 21 gametests green, and no ERROR anywhere in the run's log.

    Two premise assertions in the test rather than in a comment. It fails
    loudly if the rescuing braid is not in gear/mane and gear/tail - fifteen of
    the seventeen tags are empty, so if the braid ever left its two the test
    would go on passing while exercising nothing - and it fails if the two mock
    players share a UUID, which would make the ownership case pass whatever the
    handler did.

    Ordering detail worth recording. The item is tested before the horse is:
    isModTack walks the roster asking each non-vanilla slot's tag, and only
    then does anything look at tameness, ownership or age. The other way round,
    a carrot would have earned a "not your horse" line.

    What the tests cannot reach, and it is the real risk. This put a new
    listener on the plain right-click, which is the busiest gesture in the mod
    - carrots, hay, name tags, transfer papers, whistles, shears, mounting. The
    gametest posts events into an empty world and cannot tell you any of those
    still work. The Verification tab leads with that rather than with "does it
    equip a braid", and the entry is mostly a list of things that must NOT have
    changed.

    Housekeeping. Piece 3's section is deleted from the treatment file per
    START HERE step 3, along with its fallback note and the two "what already
    exists" bullets that only described it. horse-gear.html's "Putting
    something on" grew a line for the new way, pointing at rider-comfort.html
    for the rules rather than repeating them.
