2026-09-17 — Aggression onto one locus, fear onto another, and a way in

    Four loci decided whether a horse was dangerous, and between them they could not
    answer the question. magic_night_temper and magic_day_temper
    each named a target but welded half the day into the verb;
    gladiator named a gate but only ever one target. A breeder had to know
    three of them to read one animal. They are gone, replaced by
    aggression — a grid of three times crossed
    with five targets, every allele recessive and recessive to the others — and by
    skittishness, the same grid for what a horse runs
    from. Passification is new beside them.

Splitting the mood out is the change that buys something

    The old loci carried four hunting alleles and four fleeing ones, so a horse could be a
    monster-hunter or shy of people and never both. Two loci say what one could
    not: “goes for monsters, runs from riders” is a single animal, a herd-guard
    nobody can catch, and it is worth breeding for. The owner's other three calls:
    gladiator folds in as an always-hostile allele while
    guardian stays its own locus (it is reactive, not a
    temperament); some aggression expresses in the wild, rarer the nastier the
    target, because passification needs a horse to rescue; and passification's alleles
    stack, so a compound heterozygote offers both ways in rather than the better one.

A saddle stopped being a gate, and only half of that works

    Gladiator stopped dead whenever anybody mounted it, on the argument that a horse
    fighting while you steer is a horse fighting your steering. The owner reopened that
    deliberately: a horse aggressive toward riders now bucks them off and
    comes for them, and one aggressive toward anything else ignores its
    rider. The buck shipped, at the moment a target is acquired rather than on one
    gene, so every route to targeting a rider gets it. The second half did not and could
    not from there — a ridden horse's movement belongs to its passenger, not to its
    navigation, so the melee goal cannot walk it anywhere. Written down as
    gap 268 rather than left to be discovered,
    because from outside it reads as the gene half-working.

The two time-of-day verbs were deleted, and the flee path nearly died with them

    night_temper and day_temper existed because a gate written
    onto every allele by hand would be wrong the first time somebody forgot it — an
    argument about gene files. These loci generate their alleles from a table in
    Java, so the gate is written once, and the general temper verb already
    carried a when. Both verbs went, from Java and from the creator's mirror;
    parity stayed green.

    The trap was underneath. GeneAbilityHandler.temper
    returned early on any mood that was not aggressive: fleeing was implemented
    over in the night handler, against the verb that was being deleted. Moving skittishness
    onto the shared verb without moving the flee path with it would have produced a locus
    that loads, registers, bakes, appears in the census and does nothing whatsoever.
    The same shape reappeared one layer down: passive excluded equines only in
    the night handler's private matcher, so routing flee through MobGroups
    would have re-opened gap 241 — the foal that fled its own band 1,578 times. The
    exclusion lives in the shared resolver now, where every caller gets it, and
    horses is a group in its own right because the aggression locus names its
    own kind.

Passification is a veto, and vetoes have to be asked at every door

    It grants no ability and is not an effect verb — it would have been one with
    exactly one user, the test lycanthropy already failed. The gene owns the alleles and
    the epigenetic offering (which item, how many, how long, how soon again, all written on
    the allele copy and inherited with it); the NeoForge side owns the feeding and the veto.
    “Will not target the player for any reason” only means something if each
    reason asks, so Passification.suppresses is consulted at all four: the
    temper verb, the wild aggro-and-alert path, the dam defending her foal, and guardian's
    retaliation.

What was rescued on the way out

    Deleting the three gene pages would have destroyed four verified records with them
    — the ten-tick swing rate, the re-target fix, the night-temper confirmation and
    the night-shy record. They were recovered from git and carried onto the new pages under
    the anchors the rest of the wiki already points at. Nothing about the new loci
