    #11. A generated stable drew the breed twice: StableSpawn.roll drew one and rolled the
    genome from it, and StablePopulator.spawnHorse drew another for the record, the
    founder log and withBreed. In a stable listing several breeds (TM U Stable lists
    thirteen), most horses were labelled with a breed their genes did not come from. Size,
    the stat curve and lineage followed the wrong label.
    Fix: roll returns StableSpawn.Rolled(breed, genome), and the populator uses that single
    draw. Commit 26d8b9cf.
    Proof: StableSpawnTest.theBreedReturnedIsTheBreedTheGenesCameFrom rolls 400 horses
    from a nine-breed stable and checks that no disorder gene is missing from the labelled
    breed's sheet, as BreedFounderLog does. It went red with the label drawn separately,
    then green. The issue is closed. Horses already spawned keep their label.
