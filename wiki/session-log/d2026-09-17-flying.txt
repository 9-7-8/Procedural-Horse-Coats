2026-09-17, night — Flying, and the effects bug that was hiding under it

    The owner asked for flight: one locus, a wild type, a creative-style allele and an
    elytra-style one. What shipped is flying — three
    alleles in a dominance series, El > Tf > n, gliding dominant and
    true flight recessive at two copies. It paints nothing, which makes it the first
    data-driven gene in GeneFamily’s override map: the bands assume a
    JSON gene always paints, and without an entry it would have been filed under
    “magical coat genes”.

Neither flight model is written here

    26.1.2 carries both already, on LivingEntity and neither of them
    player-gated: travelFlying is creative/ghast flight, and
    travelFallFlying is the real elytra maths. So the mixins decide
    when a horse is allowed each one and let vanilla do the physics. That is the
    determinism rule paying for itself — there are no invented constants to drift,
    and a 1.12.2 backport reimplements “which vanilla path” rather than a
    table of tuning numbers. True flight’s airspeed comes off the horse’s own
    MOVEMENT_SPEED, so the speed loci keep meaning something in the air.

Three sessions of fixing the wrong layer

    Gliding did nothing, three times running, and each fix was aimed downstream of a thing
    that did not exist. HorseAbilities gated every effect on
    SpecGene.dose(), which counts copies of the first-declared
    allele, against a minDose defaulting to 1. On a two-allele gene those
    agree; on a dominance series they do not, so El/El scored zero — it
    carries no Tf at all — and lost the effect its own expression
    declared. The ability was never created, so the flag, the sync and the mixins were all
    innocent. SpecGene.abilityDose counts non-wild-type copies instead;
    dose() is untouched, so perDose coat knobs and both goldens
    are unchanged. Any data-driven gene with three or more alleles was
    affected.

    The lesson worth keeping: the diagnostic that broke the deadlock printed what the
    server thought the horse had (traversal=[] against
    traversal=[flight_glide]), and it was deliberately placed outside the
    case it was meant to diagnose — a probe inside that case would have proved
    nothing, because the case was what was not running.

Two vanilla traps, both found by crashing

    A floating vehicle disconnects its rider after eighty ticks unless
    isFlyingVehicle() says otherwise. And updateFallFlying does
    not merely keep the elytra flag lit — every twentieth tick it picks a random
    glider item out of the entity’s equipment and damages it. Overriding
    canGlide() promised an item a horse does not have, so
    Util.getRandom was handed an empty list and took the server down
    mid-flight. Cancelling updateFallFlying outright is the honest version:
    a gene-winged horse has no glider to wear out. The cost is that sculk sensors cannot
    hear a gliding horse.

    Fall damage is cancelled at the damage event rather than reset afterwards, because the
    server accumulates its own fallDistance from the movement packet, outside
    the entity tick — a reset is always one call too late, which was learned twice.
    The gene still grants no fall protection: that is bird
    boned’s locus, and breeding the two together is the point.

    Parked deliberately: the cloud-treading allele. Clouds turn out to be
    reachable — 26.1.2 moved cloud height into the common-side
    EnvironmentAttributes and ships Entity.isInClouds() —
    but the allele was removed rather than left in place describing a thing it no longer
    did. The cloud_walk flag survives, unused.
