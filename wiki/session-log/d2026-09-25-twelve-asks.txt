2026-09-25 — twelve asks off one bug report, and four of them were not the bug


    It started as one sentence:

        I don't see either of the nether horse breeds in the nether

    and then eleven more arrived while that one was being fixed, queued one at a
    time. What follows is in the order they were asked, not the order they were
    done. The common thread, and the reason this file is worth keeping, is that
    FOUR of the twelve were not the thing the report described — the report was
    a symptom of something else, or the feature was already correct and only
    accidentally so. Those four are marked.


1. THE NETHER BREEDS COULD NEVER SPAWN. NOT RARELY — NEVER.

    Both breed files already said so in their own notes, and breeds.html said it
    too, so this was documented and simply never fixed.

    The mod's half was right. BreedHerdsBiomeModifier does add the horse spawn
    to all five Nether biomes, because Breeds.forBiome("minecraft:nether_wastes",
    WILD) returns both breeds and nothing on that path is dimension-aware.

    Vanilla's half rejected every position. EntityType.HORSE is registered with
    Animal::checkAnimalSpawnRules, which is:

        level.getBlockState(pos.below()).is(BlockTags.ANIMALS_SPAWNABLE_ON)
            && brightEnoughToSpawn

    and #minecraft:animals_spawnable_on in this SDK holds exactly ONE block:
    minecraft:grass_block. Generated Nether terrain has none. The light half
    (raw brightness > 8, block light only since there is no skylight) is
    sometimes satisfiable near lava, so THE TAG IS THE WALL, not the light.

    The fix is a new pair of breed-file fields rather than a special case:

        "spawn_ground": [ ...block ids... ]      extra floors it may stand on
        "spawn_in_dark": true                    waive the light over THOSE only

    common/breed/SpawnGround.java holds them; Breeds.wildGroundAllows is the
    per-position accessor; neoforge world/HorseSpawnGround.java hands it to
    EntityType.HORSE with RegisterSpawnPlacementsEvent.Operation.OR.

    OR and not REPLACE, deliberately: vanilla's rule keeps running underneath, so
    this can only ever ADD a legal position. Lit grass still works for every
    horse everywhere, a world with no breed naming a floor behaves exactly as
    before, and this class never becomes responsible for reimplementing the
    vanilla rule correctly.

    Both fields are additive and there is no migration and no off switch, because
    emptying the list IS the off switch.

    THE SECOND HALF, WHICH NOBODY ASKED FOR AND WHICH WOULD HAVE RUINED IT:
    the herd modifier had one weight for everything — weight 10, min 3, max 6,
    the density of a plains herd. The Nether has almost no other land CREATURE
    spawn entry to compete with, so unblocking the placement would have made two
    VERY RARE breeds the commonest animal down there, in herds of six. The
    modifier now carries a second, quieter rate (breed_weight 2, 1–3) used for
    any biome it reaches ONLY because a breed lives there, rather than from its
    own list. Listed biomes are unchanged, so no overworld herd moved.

    Owner chose the data-driven route over three alternatives (widen the vanilla
    tag globally; place herds at worldgen; accept it and strip the biomes), and
    the flat second weight over a per-breed one.


2. BLANK TRANSFER PAPERS COULD NOT BE SPAWNED IN.

    TransferPaperHandler.sign() refused any blank with no bearer, and
    onCrafted was the only thing that ever set one — so every blank from a
    creative tab, /give, a loot table or a test kit arrived dead, and the refusal
    said "not your paper" about a paper that was nobody's. The tooltip key
    transfer_paper.unbound already existed and said "remake it at a bench".

    An unbound blank now BINDS to whoever first signs with it. Not a hole: what
    keeps a paper safe is the ownership check on the HORSE, which is untouched.
    Someone else's crafted blank out of a chest is still refused.


3. EMPTY STASIS CHAMBERS NOW STACK — AND THIS ONE WAS HIDING A BUG.  [not the bug]

    The comment on ModItems said they were stacksTo(1) because "a chamber is
    about one specific horse", and that an empty one could not stack because "an
    item cannot change its own stack limit once a horse is in it". That second
    half stopped being true when a chamber with a horse in it became a DIFFERENT
    ITEM. An empty chamber can never come to hold a horse — filling one swaps it
    for the occupied twin, which keeps its own limit of 1.

    But making empties stack on its own would have DESTROYED ITEMS. capture()
    did:

        player.setItemInHand(hand, swallow(level, horse, stack, name));

    which replaces the WHOLE held stack with one occupied chamber. Holding five
    chambers and catching one horse would have silently binned four. It now takes
    one off the stack and adds the filled chamber separately.

    The emergency path was already safe, and it is worth knowing why: both
    HorseStasisBankBlockEntity's container and HorseStasisBankMenu's slot pin
    chamber slots to getMaxStackSize() == 1, so a stack of empties can never
    reach a bank slot — which is what makes EmergencyStasisHandler's
    write-back-into-the-slot safe. That was belt and braces before; it is now the
    only thing holding.


4. NEITHER PORTAL EXIT TAKES HORSES ANY MORE.

    HorseRealm.leave gathered every tamed horse you owned within 48 blocks
    (EVACUATE_RADIUS, now deleted) and dropped them at your overworld portal. The
    debug corridor did the same through DebugPenManager.evacuateTamedHorses.

    Both are gone. A horse comes home only by being led or ridden through the
    frame. The point of the realm is to be somewhere you LEAVE horses, and
    walking out with a herd in tow made it a stable you emptied every visit.

    The two exits are NOT symmetrical and the owner was told so before choosing
    "both": the realm has no teardown and keeps what you leave, but the debug
    corridor tears its plot down on exit (tearDown discards every entity and
    drops each horse from the ancestry database). So a horse left in a debug pen
    is DESTROYED. evacuateTamedHorses became countOwnedTamedHorses, and the exit
    now prints a red line naming how many you just lost. Owner accepted that.

    Four stale doc references to evacuateTamedHorses were repointed —
    DebugPenManager, DebugTestYard, DebugYardCombat, HorsePortalManager — plus
    HorseRealm's class header, which still promised the old behaviour.


5. A CLICK IN THE BANK'S BROWSE TAB TAKES THE HORSE OUT.

    The tab lists horses, so the obvious click should be "give me that one". It
    was the at-stud toggle instead and nothing withdrew at all. Left-click now
    withdraws, right-click keeps the stud toggle, and the hint line says both.

    clickMenuButton carries the second verb as an offset id (SLOTS + slot),
    checked rather than trusted. A chamber at stud is brought in first, so it
    does not resume breeding the moment it enters another bank.


6. RESEARCH BOOKS ALREADY WORKED ON ALL FOUR HORSE TYPES — BY ACCIDENT.  [not the bug]

    The ask was to make books work on a horse you own, one someone else owns, one
    the cowboy owns, and one donated to the horse dimension. Tracing all 29
    PlayerInteractEvent.EntityInteract listeners found that a book already reached
    GeneBookFromHorse for all four: it has no ownership test, and nothing at
    HIGHEST or HIGH cancels for a plain book.

    But it worked for the cowboy's string only because GeneBookFromHorse sorts
    EARLIER IN THE JAR than TransferPaperHandler, which cancels ANY held item on
    a branded horse (isBranded -> consume). Both were at default NORMAL priority,
    and within one priority NeoForge's bus dispatches in registration order,
    which is jar entry order. Nothing in the API promises that. And a listener
    with default receiveCanceled is SKIPPED once an event is cancelled — verified
    in bus 8.0.5's SubscribeEventListener.invoke — so the failure would have been
    the book doing nothing at all, one class rename away.

    Both listeners are now HIGHEST. Safe, because this claims strictly less than
    the two already there: both of those need an empty hand, this needs a Horse
    and a book. Also now subscribes to EntityInteractSpecific, so an aimed click
    cannot be lost upstream; and a horse with no founded record gets its own
    message instead of "entirely ordinary", which it was indistinguishable from.


7. UNTAMED AND SOLD HORSES ARE OFF THE H MENU.

    HorseRoster matched `username.equals(record.tamedBy())`. tamedBy is written
    once and NEVER rewritten — deliberately, it is a history field — so a horse
    you tamed and then signed away, sold, or released into the realm stayed on
    your My horses table for ever, as did every untamed horse you had touched.

    It now reads the record's ownerId, which HorseOwnerTrackingHandler re-mirrors
    off the live entity every ~2s and CLEARS when a horse is untamed, plus the
    live entity for the two-second gap before the mirror catches up.

    Checked before changing it: the allele collection is swept off this same
    list, and GeneDatabaseData.collect only ever add()s to a LinkedHashSet — so
    what you learned from a horse stays learned after you sell it. The comment
    there warning that dropping a horse would "un-collect her alleles" is
    over-cautious; it cannot.


8. THE PORTAL DWELL IS 5 SECONDS, NOT 10.

    PLAYER_DWELL_TICKS 200 -> 100. The horse timer stays at 60 on purpose: you
    push a led horse in and follow, so it has to be through before you are. Three
    places that said "ten seconds" were corrected — the class doc, TutorialPage,
    and horse-realm.html.


9. WAYSTONES: A RIDDEN HORSE COMES WITH YOU — AND NO SETTING COULD HAVE DONE IT.  [not the bug]

    Read entirely out of waystones-neoforge-26.1.2-26.1.2.15.jar with javap. The
    live server at ~/26.1.2-neoforge-server has the same jar and was NOT touched;
    the copy read was the ATM11 CurseForge instance.

    Waystones has two rules for bringing animals along:

        transportLeashed   scans Mob            -> a LEASHED horse already travels
        transportPets      scans TamableAnimal  -> can NEVER move a horse

    A vanilla horse is not a TamableAnimal. It is
    AbstractHorse extends Animal implements PlayerRideableJumping,
    HasCustomInventoryScreen, OwnableEntity. Wolves, cats and parrots are
    TamableAnimal; equines are not. So setting transportPets = ENABLED does
    nothing at all for a stable. The setting exists, reads as though it covers
    this, and cannot — which is why this is filed as a finding and not a config
    tip.

    The real gap is the RIDDEN horse: Waystones builds its batch by walking DOWN
    from the traveller with getPassengers() and never up to getVehicle(), so the
    animal you are sitting on is left at the departure stone.

    compat/WaystonesCompat hooks WaystoneTeleportEvent.Prepare and calls
    context.addAdditionalEntity(vehicle). Waystones' own EntityTeleportBatch
    already restores riding with getVehicle/startRiding, so you arrive mounted.
    Only the direct vehicle — gathering loose horses is transportPets' job and
    turning that on by proxy is not this mod's call.

    THE BUILD SIDE COST MORE THAN THE CODE. Three compileOnly coordinates, and a
    fourth that is not where you would look:

      - Modrinth version strings carry the GAME version too:
        "26.1.2.15+neoforge-26.1.2", not Jade's bare "+neoforge". A wrong one
        fails resolution rather than falling back.
      - SHOGI SHIPS ITS API AS A JARJAR-NESTED JAR
        (META-INF/jarjar/net.blay09.mods.shogi-api-*.jar) and Gradle never
        unpacks those onto a compile classpath. The Modrinth artifact resolves,
        looks correct, and is missing the one type the Waystones API is built out
        of — WaystoneTeleportContext extends shogi.context.ShogiContext, and
        javac must walk that to type-check any call on the context. It surfaces
        as "cannot access ShogiContext", not as a missing dependency. Fixed with
        net.blay09.mods:shogi-api from maven.twelveiterations.com, which is the
        second repository block in the module's build.gradle and exists for that
        one artifact.

    Second time the no-reaching-in rule bends, on the same terms as Jade: a
    published, versioned API is not a guess. Compile-only, nothing shipped
    (checked: 0 net.blay09 classes in the jar), and the API usage sits in a
    nested Hook class behind ModList.isLoaded so nothing in those packages is
    loaded when Waystones is absent.

    NOT RUN. Not one line has executed — the dev instance has no Waystones.


10. LAST STAND ON A HIGH FALL: NOT REPRODUCED, MADE DIAGNOSABLE INSTEAD.  [not the bug]

    Report: a horse that fell from a high place died instantly.

    Everything checked out and nothing explained it:

      - minecraft:fall is NOT in #minecraft:bypasses_invulnerability (read the
        tag, did not assume), so unsurvivable() does not exempt it.
      - AbstractHorse.causeFallDamage makes exactly one hurt() call.
      - Both handlers ARE subscribed at runtime — confirmed in the owner's own
        debug.log.
      - behaviour.last_stand = true in run/phc/server.toml.
      - HorseCooldownsAttachment.last() returns Long.MIN_VALUE for an absent key,
        so armed() is true for a horse that has never been saved.
      - FlightFallGuard, the only other fall-damage handler, only ever cancels.

    The owner's log covered a startup only, so there was no evidence of the
    event. The actual defect found was in OBSERVABILITY: the save wrote an
    ActionTrace line on SUCCESS and said NOTHING on any refusal, so a failure and
    a feature that was never reached looked identical. Every refusal path now
    logs, and only when the blow would have been fatal — so the log stays quiet
    until a horse actually dies and then says which of the four reasons it was.
    The likeliest innocent explanation, and the one the new line will name, is a
    save already spent and not re-armed because the horse never healed to full.

    Still open. Next fall answers it.


11. A PERMANENT CALM NOW SWITCHES THE TEMPERAMENT OFF ENTIRELY.

    Passification.suppresses was per-player and only ever true of a Player. For
    the PERMANENT kinds (Prm, FPr, APr) it is now true of ANYTHING — player, mob,
    another horse. A temporary calm stays per player, because that is a bargain;
    a permanent one is a change of character.

    DISABLED, NOT REMOVED, which was the explicit ask. A permanently calmed
    Netherhorse still carries Aaa/Aaa, still shows it on the Alleles tab, still
    writes it onto a research paper, and still passes it to every foal — which
    then has to be calmed itself.

    forget() had to widen too: it dropped only the paying player's target and
    flee leg, which would have left a permanently calmed horse still mid-charge
    at the mob it had picked, with nothing ever re-selecting it — the stale leg
    running to its end looking exactly like the veto not working. New
    GeneAbilityHandler.stopFleeing(horse) is the blanket version beside
    stopFleeingFrom.


12. FLIGHT IS NOW FULL FALL IMMUNITY.

    Report: pressing F to land a flying horse could still deal damage. That is
    inherent to what the guard tested. FlightFallGuard gated on
    HorseFlight.active — "is it flying this instant" — so F clears the toggle and
    the horse is no longer flying the MOMENT IT STARTS DROPPING. The guard
    switched off at the top of the fall.

    Gliding had the same hole from the other side: GLIDE is only active while
    getControllingPassenger() != null, so dismounting mid-air or stalling fell
    unprotected.

    Now tests HorseFlight.of(horse).flies() — does it carry flight at all. A
    horse with the flying gene takes no fall damage ever, and neither does anyone
    riding it. No toggle state, no airborne test, no ordering to get right. This
    does overlap bird_boned for a flier, deliberately.
