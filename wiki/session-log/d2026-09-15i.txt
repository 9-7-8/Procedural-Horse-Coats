2026-09-15, later still — fourteen breeds, a re-supply merged, and three headline disorders with no locus

    The sixth intake batch: the queue from the Newfoundland Pony to the Sorraia, alphabetically.
    Fifteen drafts went in and fourteen breeds came out, because one of the fifteen
    was a breed the mod already had. Breeds.all().size() owns the count.

The batch

    American Saddlebred, Newfoundland Pony, Nokota, Nonius, Novooleksandrivsky Heavy Draught,
    Oldenburg, Orlov Trotter, Pindos, Pony of the Americas, Riwoche Horse, Rocky Mountain Horse,
    Sanhe, Selle Franais, Sorraia.

    - The fifteenth draft was a re-supply, and the owner's call was to merge it rather than
        ship it twice. Paint Horse (APHA) arrived with a filename asserting it was
        “distinct from the mod's American Paint” and a request for a second
        apha_paint_horse id. It is not distinct: same registry, same 1965 merger, same
        horse. The instruction was to take its numbers only where they are cited to a real
        study, which turned out to be exactly two. HYPP moved to the 0.025 allele
        frequency reported for the control American Paint group of an inherited-disease survey —
        several times the generic 1.2% carrier rate it had been carrying, and the single biggest
        change in the batch to a breed that already existed. Frame did not move, and that is
        worth recording precisely because nothing had to be edited: the same study's
        0.107 gives about 19% carriers under Hardy-Weinberg and the pool already sat at
        20, so the measurement corroborated what shipped. The draft's GBED, PSSM1 and HERDA figures
        were declined — it calls all three Quarter Horse proxies itself.

    - Three breeds ship without the disorder they are actually known for. This is
        the first batch where that happened more than once. WFFS has no locus, so the
        Oldenburg and the
        Selle Franais both ship clear of the thing their
        drafts were built around; JEB has no locus, so the
        American Saddlebred does too.
        The evidence arrived with the gaps, and in two cases it is better than anything else
        in that roadmap section — a 2022 study measured 6% WFFS carriers in Selle
        Franais specifically rather than across warmbloods generally, and the Saddlebred's JEB
        has 9 carriers in 175 foals from a random 2007 foal crop, a 0.026 allele frequency. Both went
        onto the roadmap with their numbers (PLOD1 and
        LAMA3 have since shipped them). The Saddlebred
        also settles half of an open question there: its form is a LAMA3 deletion, separate
        from the LAMC2 variant, so JEB wants two alleles or two loci.

    - PLOD1 is Friesian dwarfism and it nearly ate this batch. Both warmblood drafts
        name their WFFS locus PLOD1, which is the correct real-world gene symbol and is
        also the key of a gene this mod already ships for something else entirely. A file that had
        pasted the draft's block would have loaded, parsed, and quietly seeded Friesian dwarfism into
        two sport horses at 3–5%. The roadmap had already flagged exactly this trap for the
        Gelderlander; it is the first time it was actually walked into and stepped over.

    - The Nokota could not have the strains it wants, and it is the third breed in
        two days to lose the same thing. The Conservancy's two types are named along a size axis
        — Traditional about 14 to 15 hands, Ranch to 17 — and a strain may override gene
        pools and nothing else, so the strains carry colour alone and one band spans both. Chincoteague
        and Budyonny each lost a shade of meaning to this; this one loses three hands.
        Gap 256 now names all three.

    - Silver turns out to owe a disease. Found taking in the
        Rocky Mountain Horse, whose chocolate coat is its entire
        identity and carries the highest silver rate of any breed here. The same PMEL variant
        causes multiple congenital ocular anomalies, badly in the homozygote. This is a different shape
        from every other row in the health section: not a missing gene, but a shipped gene
        missing its consequence, so it needs no allele and no locus, only a contribution that scales
        with copy count. On the roadmap.

    - Every draft's size scale was wrong. All fifteen, six batches running. All were
        re-derived from the cited heights through BreedStatCurve. The Pony of the Americas
        is the tidiest check in the batch, because its registry states the band in inches rather than
        hands: 46 to 56 inches, which lands on 0.730–0.889, and a pony
        outside it cannot hold permanent registration in life or be generated here.

    - Two breeds are visually fixed and one is nearly so. The
        Sorraia and the
        Riwoche Horse both fix dun in every founder, joining the
        Henson and the Konik. Both also force grey wild for the same reason the Konik does: their
        colour is called “mouse grey” in the old language, and seeding STX17
        would whiten the one thing either breed looks like. The Pony
        of the Americas is the inverse — the first breed in the mod where LP is
        fixed and no founder is ever lp/lp.

    - An empty file, and a file named for the wrong horse. Nonius .md
        with a trailing space was zero bytes beside a real Nonius.md; it was removed with
        the batch and nothing was lost. Novoalexandrovsk Trotter.md contains no trotter at
        all — it is the Novooleksandrivsky heavy draught, a breed with no trotting history —
        which is the third time a draft's filename has disagreed with its contents.

    Green on the way out: BreedFilesTest and BreedSpecParserTest (the two that
    matter are everyShippedFileLoadsWithNoErrorsAndNoWarnings, which proves no allele token
    was warned and dropped, and everyShippedFileIsExactlyWhatABakeWouldWrite),
    :neoforge-26.1.2:build, the designer boot check, and every internal link across the
    wiki. Nothing in the batch has been seen in game — see
    the checklist.
