2026-09-21, later — Simply Equine, walked from the jar: three sections harvested, five ideas refused

    No code. A survey session, and the first one to work a row off
    the survey backlog. The owner picked
    Simply Equine —
    the only row asked for exhaustively rather than for highlights — and asked for every
    choice to be posed as a choice rather than decided on her behalf.

    The store page and the jar describe different mods, and the jar is the one to read

        CurseForge advertises a new horse model, 18 coat textures, animations, jumps, level
        progression, armour, tack, fencing and stable decor. The jar is 197 items, 3
        blocks, 19 classes, zero recipes and no horse entity. Every item is
        creative-tab-only; the headline “grooming system” is
        util/GroomingTools, which checks whether your main hand holds the
        black rubber curry comb — one item out of 197 — and spawns
        particles on any entity, with no state and no effect. The layered shavings block is a
        Forge tutorial with the textures swapped: its config key is still
        enablePowderLayers and its class is TutorialConfig. The mod is
        abandoned at Minecraft 1.15.2 and its author joined
        SWEM, which we had already read.

        So the brief was reported back before any of it was walked: the row asked for an
        add/adapt/skip pass over a codebase that is mostly a statement of intent, and the honest
        harvest is ideas. The row is deleted rather than promoted to a
        compatibility entry — a 1.15.2 mod cannot run beside this one. The
        remaining survey rows should each be read the same way.

Taken — three sections, each attributed where it landed

    A grooming tool that replaces shears
    outright. Grooming already ships as minecraft:shears:
    server/HorseShearHandler gives an adult horse 1–3
    horse hair and +2 bond once per Minecraft day, and the
    dressing was renamed to grooming on 2026-09-13. What was missing was the item —
    the verb reads as care and the thing in your hand is a pair of blades. One verb tiered by
    material, where a better tool buys bond and cooldown and never hair, so grooming cannot
    become a farming action. The top tier grooms the whole herd, which is the
    only rung doing something the others cannot and which finally gives the herd computation a
    consumer — a gap listed two paragraphs below it on the same page. Note the contract is
    wider than the handler: ProgressTask.SHEAR_HORSE and
    husbandry/shear_horse.json are named for the old item.

    Ground work — halter, vanilla
    lead, lunge line. Bond you build without riding, for the foals and
    disorder-carrying horses that today have hand-feeding and nothing else. The halter joined
    the accessories slot roster, taking it from
    fourteen slots to fifteen and HorseTackSlot’s growth from twelve to
    thirteen. The lunge line is the one new item and the circling movement is the real work:
    it has to yield to riding, fleeing and the herd goals, so
    the behaviour hierarchy and its bake are in the
    contract.

    Stall bars, a third per-material
    block family after the double gates and the jumps. Both its utilities fall out of the same
    physical fact, which is the sign they are the right ones: bars are see-through, so a stalled
    horse keeps its herd where a solid wall would isolate it; and bars are reachable, so you can
    groom and feed through them. It is also the point at which
    JumpModel.Unbaked.bake’s per-variant loop stops being wasteful and starts
    being the cost.

Refused — and the reasoning recorded, so the same source cannot re-propose them

    - The six-tool grooming roster (dandy brush, body brush, rubber and metal
        curry combs, hoof pick, sponge) and the stable-mucking loop (muck rake,
        layered shavings, thatch). Both want a per-horse condition that decays, and a horse that
        suffers for going unbrushed or unmucked is the punishing husbandry
        philosophy rules out.

    - Riding crop, lunge whip, driving whip. Implements for striking a horse
        to make it go, against the premise the care layer is built on.

The rosettes forced a new kind of section

    Twelve show ribbons, Champion and Reserve Champion among them, and the owner’s first
    answer was to take them as cosmetic decor. That collided with a rule she had settled
    earlier the same day —
    nothing this mod adds may be purely
    aesthetic except breeding, the reversal that gave tack its stats. Re-asked rather than
    written through, and the answer went further than the question: racing and
    competitions are a separate add-on, attaching to a horse’s
    pedigree rather than living in the base mod, because a
    competition ladder is progression for the player and breeding is this mod’s
    progression.

    The roadmap is planned-features-only and had no way to say “considered, and placed
    outside”, so Deliberately out of scope was
    added as a single labelled exception with the rosettes as its first entry. It exists so the
    next person with the idea finds the verdict rather than the request.
