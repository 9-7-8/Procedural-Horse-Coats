What was built:
- common/gear/WornPieces: the table. A row (WornPieces.Piece) is a name, a kind (LIFTED or
  BOXES), a lift, and for a lifted piece a Region of the horse's mesh and the file stem of
  its mask. The constructor sends the lift through WornRule.lift, so a row under 0.25
  cannot be written; table() refuses two rows with one name or one mask. Regions:
  whole(part), and band(part, from, to), a sleeve round one box that leaves its top and
  bottom faces out. Rows today: BRAID_MANE, BRAID_TAIL, and two debug rows TEST_BOOT_FORE
  and TEST_BOOT_HIND (LEFT_FRONT_LEG and LEFT_HIND_LEG, 0.14 to 0.55 of the leg's height).
  No BOXES row exists yet; the worn side of AttachedPartLayer (#140) will draw those.
- common/coat/skin/HairMaskTool (:common:bakeHairMasks) now walks WornPieces.lifted() and
  writes one mask per row. braid_mane.png and braid_tail.png came out byte-identical (git
  showed no change); test_boot_fore.png and test_boot_hind.png are new, 288 texels each.
- neoforge client/gear/WornLayer replaces client/BraidLayer (deleted). For each lifted row
  it reads WornLayer.WORN, one line per row giving the colour a horse wears it in or 0,
  fades it with the horse, and draws the row's mask through WornMeshes at the row's lift,
  or flat through the parent model when gear.edges is off or no mesh can be built. Debug
  rows are left out of the layer unless the client was started with
  -Dhorsegenetics.photoShoot=true; a lifted row with no WORN line is logged as an error
  when the renderer is made.
- The photo shoot: DebugPhotoShootClient.testBoot (the boot's colour while the shot on
  stage has "boot" in its name), a held stride for shots with "-walk" in the name,
  DebugPhotoShoot.rearing(), and Framing.LEGS.
