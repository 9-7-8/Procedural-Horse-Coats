2026-10-01 - scoping exits, the stasis bank, a climate rule, and the model parts


    An intake session, a continuation of the one that scoped rider comfort and the
    Chaos allele. Nine treatments or specs written into intake/, no code touched,
    nothing built or played. Every design call in them was put to the owner with the
    question tool and is recorded there as Decided; each file also lists what is
    still UNVERIFIED for the session that builds it.

    Horse exits and free send home. An Exits tab on the H menu backed by its own
    permanent per-owner ledger (the Log keeps only 200 rows), filtered by died, sold
    or given to a player, sold to a dealer, and sent to the realm (a new event: the
    portal and the freedom stick were never logged). Send home becomes free by
    default, including out of the stasis bank, with an optional configured item
    consumed per trip and a 5-second per-player cooldown.

    Realm breeding pause. A realm mare's breeding timers (pregnancy, heat, cooldowns,
    foal growth) advance only while she is loaded, default on, with a "paused" sign
    on the pregnancy readout. Pregnancy ticks are absolute game ticks today, so an
    unloaded mare gives birth the moment she loads; the recommended mechanism shifts
    her timers forward by her unloaded time.

    Stasis bank. The saved-searches arrow shows on the Chambers tab because the
    screen hides the filter box but never the button; Basic chambers become
    searchable by plain name only; one press ejects every empty chamber.

    Breed climate tolerance (a spec). Nether breeds pin heat tolerance at 100%; a
    breed's cold tolerance is raised to a share-of-cold-biomes ladder. Six breeds
    change. One documentation contradiction found: Wielkopolski.

    Model parts, in four treatments: head parts (dragon horns, ear fins, cheek
    spikes, brow ridge, goat beard, ruff), body parts (back sail, dorsal spines,
    plates, crystal growths, with the saddle zone hidden under a saddle or rider),
    the tusks gene (boar tusks, sabre fangs and a narwhal horn as one dominant locus
    whose forms combine) and feathering (coloured from the leg beneath it).

    What the next session picks up first: the shared colour-gene base class and the
    hair sheet region are needed by several of these treatments, so settle which
