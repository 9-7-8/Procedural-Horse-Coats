2026-09-16, later — Getting started becomes a book, and every item gets a box

    “Overhaul the getting started page on the H menu in line with the new plans
    in the road map. Also add dying each piece of tack as a sub chapter. Also make
    subchapters on the table of contents have a yellow background when you've finished the
    checklist for that sub chapter.” The plan was already written down —
    roadmap 24.5, from 2026-09-15 — asking for chapters and sub-chapters with every
    sub-header ending in at least one checklist item.

The yellow is the reason for the restructure, not a decoration on it

    A sub-chapter can only be finished if it owns checklist items, so the two halves
    of the request turned out to be one change. The checklist stopped being a section of its
    own and its items moved up under the sub-chapters that teach them. What that arrangement
    loses is the overview — “how much is left?” — so a Checklist
    section at the end keeps the total and lists only what is outstanding, and therefore
    shrinks as the player works through it.

Every item has a task, which cost twenty-six of them

    Asked mid-session. Auditing the registered items against the then 36 tasks left eleven
    items nothing could ever tick a box for: the five themed splice carrots, braided rope,
    hair cloth and hair bundles, two of the three ticket tiers, and breed spawn eggs. A
    twelfth, the blank ticket, looked like a mere crafting base until the recipe folder
    showed the horseman sells it. Each new task was wired to a real hook rather than declared
    and left dangling, because ProgressTask's own rule is that an unreachable
    task reads as a bug the player cannot diagnose.

    Two of them had no player to credit. Healing at water and forming a herd
    happen on the horse's shared slow tick, with nobody interacting, so they are credited to
    horse.getOwner() when that owner is loaded. That is the most likely thing
    here to be subtly wrong in either direction, and it is why it leads
    §0-EO rather than sitting in
    the list with the rest.

Written from the code, because the wiki was wrong in four places

    Checking every claim against the source rather than the wiki turned up four stale ones,
    all fixed in the same change: grooming is +2 and not +5; riding is a point per forty
    seconds, because a rider always also earns the proximity gain; a stall sign refuses an
    unenclosed room rather than falling back to “the ground in front of the sign”,
    a fallback that was removed some time ago; and breeding still
    said heat and pregnancy were unbuilt and that there were no sex-linked loci, both long
    since false.

    The one that changed the writing most was inbreeding. Roadmap 24.5 asks
    the genetics chapter to cover “line breeding, inbreeding and the risks of both”,
    and there is no inbreeding penalty in the mod at all — no coefficient, no
    warning, no weaker foal; the only kinship rule anywhere is a wild dispersing filly
    refusing a close-kin suitor. So that sub-chapter says so outright and describes line
    breeding as a practice with a real cost that the game does not enforce, rather than
    implying a mechanic that does not exist.
