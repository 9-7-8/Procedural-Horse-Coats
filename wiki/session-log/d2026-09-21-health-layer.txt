    No code. The owner read the roadmap's health-layer section and said, in
    four lines, that most of it had already happened: the expected-foal display exists in the
    H menu, so does carrier display, per-part scaling is not worth the effort, and the

Two were shipped, and the roadmap said the opposite of the code

    The bullet asking for “a punnett square answered about two specific animals,
    in game” describes the H menu's Breeding preview tab exactly: two
    pickers over your own tamed horses, then one row per locus reading
    chance | foal pair | expression name, phrased as 1 in 4 /
    3 in 4 / always, grouped stat axis  disorders  coat
     abilities, with settled loci hidden behind an “All loci” toggle. It also
    re-derives which parent is the dam from the sex locus rather than trusting the picker.
    BreedingPreview's own class comment argues the owner's framing back at the
    roadmap — “it does not draw a foal, because a predicted coat is a promise
    the draw does not make”, and the screen says “deliberately not ‘what
    will the foal look like’”.

    The carrier bullet claimed “every disorder declares a carrier sentence
    and nothing shows it”. RecessiveDisorderGene generates that sentence, and
    it renders under the row on the information screen's Coat and Other genes
    tabs, and per gene in the H menu's Gene database tab. What is genuinely absent is
    narrower and was not what the bullet asked for: no surface says “this horse of
    yours is a carrier” in prose — the Genes tab tints the row, and the paper
    dump carries it only inside the short genetic code.

    The reopen trigger on the first is written down deliberately: the mod has its own horse
    model, never “someone has time”. The second is simpler — the affected
    foal dies within seconds of birth, so a bare-skin template buys a few seconds of a horse the
    player is about to lose. B4GALT7,
    ST14 and the body page now
    say both are the shipped shape by design; gap 28
    kept only what is still really missing, with no “closed” note left behind.

Gait had no roadmap entry at all, while the game promised it to the player

    Deleting the four left the section empty — and surfaced that
    DMRT3 is registered, inherited, carried by the breeds that
    really have it, and moves no horse, with no roadmap entry anywhere while its
    three expression strings tell the player “the gait is not animated yet”.
    The owner's call was to keep it planned but not under health, so it is now
    its own section. It is explicitly not bundled with the
    per-part cut: that one wanted the mod to own the horse model, this one wants new motion on the
    model it already borrows.

The find: five breed rows advertised three genes as unbuilt, and all three ship

    “JEB is not built” — linked to a roadmap section that had not mentioned JEB in a long time

        Chasing what still linked to the doomed anchor turned up five rows in
        the breed table claiming JEB,
        FIS and WFFS were missing features. All three ship
        (Lama3Gene, Slc5a3Gene, Plod1Gene), and four of the
        five breeds carry exactly the rates their own row quotes as absent: Fell Pony
        38% fis/N, Selle Franais 6% ffs/N, Swedish Warmblood
        7.4%, American Saddlebred 5.14% jeb/N. Zangersheide is the one real
        “no” and for a different reason — its draft's rate was a self-labelled
        proxy and was declined — so it now says that instead. The body page's
        “No CSNB — the leopard complex is not built” was stale the same way.

        The shape to remember: a stale claim survives because its link still
        resolves. Every one of these pointed at a live anchor whose section had long
        since stopped saying what the sentence promised, so nothing ever failed.
        check-links.mjs cannot see this, and did not.
