DECIDED
-------
Calls made without the owner (all recorded on wiki/model-parts.html#worn-side):
- A worn part names its own anchor (on WornPart), and a kind's anchor() stays the grown
  part's place. One shoe mesh goes on four feet; four kinds per piece was the alternative.
- A worn piece is still a PartKind and an AttachedPart, so it shares the mesh cache and
  the draw code, as the treatment's P1 says (two sources, one renderer).
- Worn parts ignore parts.enabled (the grown parts' switch) and gear.edges (a box has
  depth already).
- The block reaches the worn floor under the sole as well as round the walls.
- There is no foal offset for a hoof anchor: nothing reaches it for a foal.
Owner's earlier decisions this built on (2026-10-09, on #140): its own piece, before
shoes, after #239; hard pieces are box-built.
