2026-09-20, later still — Four base-colour loci get their science, and three of them disagree with us

    Four source files landed in intake/ over the afternoon — MC1R,
    ASIP, EDNRB and KIT — which is four of the loci the roadmap
    listed as carrying a paragraph where they needed a page. All four science tabs are written
    from them and the files are consumed. Not one word came from memory, which
    is the only reason these can state variants, coordinates and frequencies;
    the method note written this morning got its
    first use the same day.

    The tabs.
    Extension was the shortest in the mod and now
    carries the three-allele series with its two missense variants, the epistasis argument, the
    ea testing trap, and why ED is a myth that
    keeps coming back.
    Agouti keeps both of its existing sections and
    gains the 11-bp exon-2 deletion, what the commercial test actually detects, and a full
    treatment of why A+ and At failed.
    EDNRB was the highest-priority one — four
    other tabs cite it as the pigment gene with a health consequence and it could not
    support the citation — and now has the neural-crest mechanism that makes white hair and
    a fatal gut disease one gene.
    KIT keeps its locus table and gains the melanoblast
    mechanism, the booster group, multi-variant haplotypes, and a careful reading of what
    “homozygous lethal” is actually claiming.

    Three departures, filed as
    gaps 297–299. Two are the familiar shape
    — a deliberate design limit whose consequence was undocumented, which is what
    gap 134 exists to catch. KIT cannot express haplotype
    phase, so the mod can breed a W22 horse with no W20 where reported
    W22 horses always have one; and frame white is capped below the spine as a hard
    rule where the literature says “usually”.

    The third is different and is the find of the session.
    The settled call not to add
    ea to Extension is recorded on the grounds that “the two-allele
    model is accurate to the science as it stands”. The source the owner supplied says the
    established, routinely tested series is E, e and
    ea. So the decision’s premise is false —
    and the gene page had been saying so all along, describing ea as real
    and merely unmodelled, which means page and decision had quietly disagreed since the call was
    made. The conclusion may still be right for a different reason: nothing would look different,
    because ea is phenotypically red like e, so the value is
    entirely in the genotype code and the carrier display. The decision is not reopened
    here — it is the owner’s to confirm on honest grounds or reverse.

    The pleasant surprise is how much of this the mod already had right, and not
    by luck. Both sources close with advice for a genetics simulator, and both describe what is
    already built: treat Extension as a pigment-permission gate and let Agouti operate only when
    eumelanin is enabled; model ASIP with two alleles and push wild bay, dark bay and
    seal-brown-like expression into probabilistic modifiers with breed-linked frequencies rather
    than Mendelian alleles. That is this gene plus shade, down to
    the skewed pools in the breed files. The KIT review’s advice — treat white extent
    as a probability distribution conditional on genotype rather than a fixed sprite — is
    the combination table plus a strength roll. The A+/At call made on
    2026-09-15 is confirmed outright by a source that arrived five days later.

    Also intaken: To do.txt, whose own last line says to empty it
    once its contents reach the roadmap. Its three asks are there now — more flavour genes
    for breed designers, a gene that reads what the horse is wearing, and a possible aggression
    overhaul. The tack-aware one is the interesting entry: the when vocabulary has
    no flag that can see an equipment slot, so it is a condition-contract job before it is a
    gene, and BarebackSteeringHandler’s phantom saddle means a naive
    “is saddled” check is true for horses the player thinks are bare. The aggression
    note is recorded as unspecified on purpose: it says only that they may need an
    overhaul, and nothing should be rebuilt off that sentence.
