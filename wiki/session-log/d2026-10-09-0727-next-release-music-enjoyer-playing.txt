Fix #217 - commit d4c12b91. Issue CLOSED, on milestone v0.5.046.
  What was wrong: the `near_jukebox` world flag in
  neoforge-26.1.2/.../server/GeneAbilityHandler.java (sampleWorld) read the jukebox
  block's HAS_RECORD state. A disc that has played to its end stays in the box, so one
  disc inserted once paid every tamed Msc/Msc horse within eight blocks the whole daily
  bond cap, every day, with nobody there and no music (measured in the yard's MUSIC pen on
  2026-10-08: 56 to 70 over 6,000 ticks beside a finished disc).
  The fix: the search moved into a public static
  GeneAbilityHandler.jukeboxPlayingNear(Level, BlockPos). It still reads HAS_RECORD first
  (a block-state read) and only then fetches the block entity and asks
  JukeboxBlockEntity.getSongPlayer().isPlaying(). In the 26.1.2 sources isPlaying is "a
  song is set", and JukeboxSongPlayer.tick calls stop() when the song has finished; the
  jukebox ticks only while HAS_RECORD is true (JukeboxBlock.getTicker). The 20-tick
  sampling and the reach are unchanged. The flag's name is unchanged, so no gene file,
  schema or fixture moved, and nothing saved changed (hard rule 10 not involved).
  Proof: new gametest `jukebox_pays_only_while_playing` in ModGameTests (registered in
  TEST_FUNCTIONS and in onRegisterGameTests). One jukebox, four states, through the
  handler's own search: empty (false), disc in and playing (true), song stopped with the
  disc left in (false), a fresh disc (true). With the isPlaying condition removed on
  purpose, `./gradlew :neoforge-26.1.2:runGameTest` failed with exactly "a finished record
  still in the jukebox (HAS_RECORD true, not playing) counted as music" (1 required test
  failed); with the fix, "All 59 required tests passed". Then
  `./gradlew :neoforge-26.1.2:build` green and check-parity OK.
  Docs in the same commit: wiki/gene-music-enjoyer.html - the Coding tab gained
  #pays-only-while-playing and #verified-finished-record now reads as history; the
  Verification tab gained one in-game look. Bakes run: sync-page-views (nothing),
  bake-verification-index, bake-roadmap-index, check-roadmap-execution,
  build-search-index, check-links, bake-agent-text.
