- c3400b5a  #36: the command whistle's plain hold opened nothing on any horse.
  Cause: CommandWhistleClient.aimedOwnHorse asked horse.getOwnerReference(), which is
  always null on a client. Vanilla syncs AbstractHorse's flags byte (isTamed) and never
  its owner. So every aimed horse read as someone else's and the click went on to
  vanilla. Only the sneak-hold, which checks no owner, opened the wheel. This matches the
  owner's report on 0.5.043. It was not another handler (HorseInfoInteraction) taking the
  click: that one needs an empty hand.
  Fix: the decision moved to common/ as HorseOrders.whistleUse(sneaking, record, player)
  -> WhistleUse {EVERY_HORSE, AIMED_HORSE, NOTHING}. It reads HorseRecord.ownedBy, the
  owner HorseOwnerTrackingHandler mirrors onto the synced record every 40 ticks. The
  client now ray-casts for any horse and asks the rule. The wheel's title is now always
  the record's display name. A horse with no record yet opens nothing.
  Proof:
  - :common:test --tests '*HorseOrdersTest' green, with three new tests:
    thePlainHoldOpensTheWheelOnYourOwnHorse, thePlainHoldLeavesEveryOtherHorseAlone and
    theSneakHoldIsEveryHorseWhateverIsAimed. With the rule's AIMED branch cut out, the
    first goes red.
  - New gametest whistle_sees_the_owner (ModGameTests, registered at 300 ticks; 48 -> 49
    registered). On a founded, tamed horse the record names the tamer, follows
    setOwner(buyer), and empties on setTamed(false), and whistleUse answers each state.
    :neoforge-26.1.2:runGameTest: "All 49 required tests passed". With
    HorseOwnerTrackingHandler's setOwner line cut out it fails: "the taming player's own
    horse opens no wheel: the record's owner is Optional.empty".
  - :neoforge-26.1.2:build green; check-parity OK; check-designer-boots OK.
