2026-09-14 — the yard packed, three pens retired, and the first quarter hour read off the log

    The owner played the new rows for a quarter of an hour, then asked for the testing area to be
    condensed. Her words were “there's so much empty space in the pens, there's no way it can all stay
    loaded”.

    - Pens isolated, yard only. The breeding and herd rows were spread over
        three hundred blocks because every breeding and band check counts horses by distance
        through walls. Tall walls would not have helped, and I said so before building anything.
        The owner chose isolation over line of sight and over merely trimming the gaps.
        YardPens registers each pen, and inside the horse dimension those checks see
        only the horses in their own pen (or pen group). Rows O–X became O–T, about 240
        blocks shallower.

    - What isolation does not reach is vanilla's golden-carrot breeding, which
        pairs two horses in love within three blocks through a wall. So breeding pens keep a
        three-block gap, and the jar stud got his own pen (owner: “he'll just breed them as
        normal”).

    - Retired on the owner's word: the deathbed (“we've confirmed on death
        works”), the food-preference pen, and the ticket mare. The holding pen stays for a
        later test.

    - Confirmed off the log with nobody touching the pens: a band staying one
        band after its stallion dies; dispersal of both sexes; herding firing; sparring without
        damage; grooming partners; tick time with about 270 horses; maternity to the minute; natural
        covers; the subfertile pair's single cover per heat; the gelding control; the cap holding;
        cowboy stock. The details are on §0-DW and
        §0-DV.

    - Then the confirmed pens went. NATURAL PAIR, GELDING CONTROL, SUBFERTILE
        PAIR, COWBOY STOCK, SPARRING and HERDING were deleted, and what was left packed into four
        rows, O–R. Pens with an open half stayed.

    - Horses kick monsters twice as hard. The owner was hit first in the
        guardian test, so the gene fired; the horse lost the trade, because an ordinary horse hit
        for 3, exactly a zombie's hit. Her call was that a horse should out-hit a zombie against
        hostile mobs only, not players or passive animals.
        HorseAggroHandler.onHorseKicksMonster doubles a horse's melee hit on any
        Enemy, so an ordinary horse kicks for 6 and a gladiator keeps its lead.

    - A For Testers page (for-testers.html, Coding
        view): the open overworld tests rewritten for volunteers, one action per step, with what a
        pass and a fail look like. It leaves out what needs dev tools (/testkit, the
        clock on a horse, stick-taming) and what a release jar cannot show (a wild horse's info
        screen). Each test has a report button: with the AI helper set up it opens the chat
        already reporting that test (HG.aiChat.report), otherwise it opens GitHub's
        new-issue form filled in.

    - Three test setups were wrong, and are fixed. DAM DEFENCE put a full-health
        zombie against a plain horse, which killed the dam. HURT MARE's half health was undone when
        her traits applied as she joined the level. Both geldings lost their labels to the record
        rewrite.
