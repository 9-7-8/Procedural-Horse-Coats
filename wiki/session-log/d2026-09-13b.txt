2026-09-13 — the ward proved itself unattended, and the project took its first mixin

    The holy ward works. Thirty-nine spawn attempts inside the warded
    chamber and none of them arrived; fifty-three attempts in the control and all
    fifty-three did. Ten minutes, nobody watching, the player parked on a block of teal
    wool thirty blocks away. This is a gene whose headline claim had never once been
    tested, and two separate structural blockers had to be cleared first — the
    dimension generated the_void, which spawns nothing, and every previous
    attempt stood next to the horse, inside the 24-block radius where vanilla was never
    going to spawn anything anyway.

    The evidence came out stronger than the test was designed to produce, by
    luck. Two hooks happen to straddle the cancellation:
    FinalizeSpawnEvent fires before the ward can cancel, so it counts
    attempts; EntityJoinLevelEvent only fires if nothing cancelled,
    so it counts arrivals. The gap between those columns is the gene.
    The design had aimed at "the warded room is empty", which is consistent with a dozen
    boring explanations; 39-and-0 beside 53-and-53 is consistent with one.

Both night loci, and both weather loci, closed in a day

    Thirteen variants that nobody had ever watched. All eight night-temper
    forms and all five night-watch forms are confirmed, and every one of those stalls is
    gone. §0-AT had carried the family as unwatchable since 2026-09-09.

    Not one of them was broken. They were unwatchable for three separate
    reasons, and each looked exactly like "the gene does nothing": the dimension had no
    night, because a dimension_type needs timelines as well as a
    default_clock; the monster-targeted forms had nothing to be about,
    because the biome was the_void; and the fleeing forms were in stalls too
    small for the navigator to accept their path. Three different silences, one symptom.

    The weather loci went the same way and are the sharper lesson. The
    owner reported them as not working — and looking at the horses would have said
    exactly that. The census says otherwise: speed 0.188 flat when dry against
    0.194–0.252 in rain, jump 0.500 flat against 0.584–0.591. Both move, and
    the shape is right too: identical when dry because there is no modifier at
    all, spreading into a range under rain because the magnitude is drawn per allele copy.
    It is a fact rather than an impression only because the watch prints a named
    attribute's range, and a conditional modifier is a question about numbers.

    Rain being invisible was a fault of mine and a small one: the new biome carried
    has_precipitation: false, copied from the_void. The level
    rained and the biome dropped nothing.

    The yard is ten pens, from thirty-five.

The hunters are done; the shy ones needed a bigger box

    Every hunting form of night temper is confirmed and their stalls are
    gone. The shy forms were reported as doing nothing, then as "working now, but hard
    to tell in their tiny pens" - and the pen was the whole of it. A shy horse paths
    FLEE_DISTANCE, twelve blocks, directly away; in a
    4×7 stall the navigator refuses that before it starts.

    The asymmetry is the lesson. Hunt and flee are the same locus with the
    sign flipped, they were built into identical stalls, and the stall confirmed one half
    and hid the other — because a hunter closes on you and a wall helps it,
    while a flee-er needs room, which is the one thing a stall cannot give. Four paddocks
    at 19×30 now.

    And a second pass on the stalker: "watch behind still walks through the
    player." The first fix diverted only when the horse was on the far side of the
    player from its goal, which catches the head-on case and nothing else. The real reason
    no choice of destination can fix it alone is that
    Minecraft's ground pathfinder does not treat entities as obstacles at
    all - it will walk a horse through a player as though they were not there. So
    the player is made an obstacle by hand, and the test is now the perpendicular distance
    from them to the segment the horse is about to walk.

Night temper, confirmed - every hunting form but one

    base alarm next to the spawner. A test needs something to happen to.

    One tuning item out of it (gap 213): the horse
    stands still and then the damage arrives. Half of that is vanilla's 20-tick swing
    interval, and the half worth knowing is that a horse has no attack animation
    at all — it was never a mob vanilla intended to melee. Making it hit
    faster would leave it looking just as odd.

The first mixin, and why it is allowed to exist

    LivingEntity.travelInLava is moveRelative(0.02F, input) with
    hardcoded drag and reads no attribute — in 26.1.2 and in 1.12.2 alike. Three
    cheaper repairs were tried on paper and all three fail on the case that matters, a
    horse with somebody riding it: a velocity nudge is overwritten by the rider's
    client, there is no existing attribute, and NeoForge's FluidType.move
    belongs to the fluid type.

    What makes it defensible is that it does nothing unless asked: a
    ModifyConstant on one float in one private method, returning vanilla's own
    value unless the entity carries this mod's lava_movement attribute, which
    is horses only. A mixin on LivingEntity sits in a hot path shared with
    every mod in the pack, and "no-op by default" is not a nicety there; it is the licence.
    And it costs the backport nothing that matters — the epigenetic speed, the
    founder floor and the breeding all live in common/, and the mixin is the
    translation, which is what the platform module is for.

Four more settled, and a yard that keeps shrinking

    Intimidating holds its radius now (min 7.9, median 8.1, against a pre-fix median of
    4.2) — the path-based repel was the right mechanism and the shove never was. The
    retinue costs nothing measurable: a flat 50.0ms/tick with twenty-four
    continuously re-pathing mobs, which answers §0-BT's one open performance worry by
    saying there was nothing to worry about. Lycanthropy works, found by accident in a log
    line — the horse became an allay, which is also why its stall could
    never have held it. And the new biome spawns: forty-two hostiles in two minutes.

    All of those pens are gone. What went in on the freed ground is aimed at what the
    biome and a new attribute readout just made possible: the two monster-targeted night
    stalls stopped saying CANNOT FIRE HERE, and the weather loci
    got a pen at last. §0-BT called them "the one locus family you cannot check on
    demand", which is true of looking at a horse and false of reading its numbers —
    a conditional modifier is either on the horse or it is not. The watch prints a named
    attribute's range across a pen now, so the test is: read the census,
    /weather rain, read it again.
