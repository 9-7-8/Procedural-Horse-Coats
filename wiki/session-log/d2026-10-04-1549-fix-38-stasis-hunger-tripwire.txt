  What was wrong:

    to exactly Hunger.FULL. HorseCareHandler's slow tick, phased by entity id, had
    sometimes already drained about 0.06 by then (99.94 seen), so the test failed on
    alternate runs.

    a renamed hunger attachment, which is what this tripwire exists to catch.
  The fix: the test writes STASIS_TRIPWIRE_HUNGER (80.0) with setData before snapshotting,
  and expects 80 back. 80 is well above Hunger.STARVING, so the upkeep half still heals.
  Proof:
  - With the fix, stasis_tag_is_readable passed in 4 of 4 runGameTest runs (it failed in
    4 of 8 before). Two runs were 54/54 green. The other two were red only on #37's
    order-goal tests.
  - Mutation: StasisCare.hunger pointed at the key "hungerMUTANT" makes the test fail
    with "expected 80.0 and read 100.0". The old check would have passed that. Reverted.
  - :neoforge-26.1.2:build green; check-parity OK (7163 checks).
  Issue #38 is CLOSED, with a proof comment.
