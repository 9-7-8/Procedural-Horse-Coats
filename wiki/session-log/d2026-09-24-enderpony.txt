Two things the proposal assumed and the code did not have

    The proposal said to give the breed ender-pearl laying by "using the existing
    horsegenetics.egg_layer gene with the item subject set to minecraft:ender_pearl", and said
    the repo already supported that. It did not: the locus ships a fixed list of eight mundane
    items and its own class note says so as a design position — "nothing valuable is rollable in
    the wild; a horse that lays something worth having is one somebody engineered through the
    splice layer". Worse, the base class spreads one wild-carrier percentage evenly over the
    variants, so a ninth allele is automatically carried by ordinary wild horses and two
    unrelated carriers can throw a pearl layer with no Enderpony anywhere in the story.

    Put to the owner as three options (breed-only allele with an exclusion from the wild table /
    plain ninth allele / ship the breed without it). Her call: the PLAIN NINTH ALLELE. So Endp →
    minecraft:ender_pearl is in the ordinary carrier spread, the breed is the reliable source
    rather than the only one, and the gene's class note and the "high-value alleles must never be
    rollable in the wild" box on gene-egg-layer.html were rewritten to say what is now true
    rather than left contradicting the code. The stance is not dead — it is now "what the wild
    may roll is a decision every time an allele is added", and the next valuable item is that
    decision again rather than a precedent already set.

    The second was the purple nebula. The proposal asked for Nebula Light "with its palette or
    breed band tuned to the purple/violet end". nebula_light.json has no hue knob at all: its
    colour is a nine-entry PALETTE op running pink through violet to blue, a property of the gene
    and shared by every horse carrying it, so there is nothing a breed band can reach and tuning
    it purple would have repainted every nebula horse in the game. Options were ship-as-is /
    add a hueShift knob / drop it. Her call: DROP IT. The purple identity is carried by the
    marking strain instead, and the reason is written in the breed's notes so the next person
    does not re-open it.

The bake caught a sheet saying two contradictory things

    First bakeBreedFiles printed:

        enderpony.json: "stats.speed" is ignored wherever a pool names horsegenetics.magic_speed
        enderpony.json: "stats.jump" is ignored wherever a pool names horsegenetics.magic_jump

    The proposal asked for both a low speed score AND Magic Speed Sluggish/Sluggish, and those
    are the same locus: magic_speed and magic_jump are two of the five body-stat genes, so a
    pool naming one takes that axis away from the stats block. Dropped both pools and kept the
    scores, because BreedFounder.bodyStatPair already resolves a below-baseline score to the
    "down" allele homozygous — speed 2 IS Sluggish/Sluggish, with the magnitude drawn from the
    score instead of fixed. Same horse, tunable number. Worth knowing generally: naming
    magic_speed, magic_jump, magic_health, magic_pull or body_size in a pool silently overrides
    the stats block for that axis, and the bake is the only thing that says so.

The breed, in one paragraph

    Magical, very rare, every overworld biome, any hour, and no country — the second breed with
    that field blank after the Dhampir, because it is not from anywhere on earth. Fixed black
    (E/E a/a) under a dark-leaning shade pool. Both irises Cha/Cha. Endp/Endp at the egg layer,
    End/End ender echo, Prtl/Prtl portal motes. Rv/Rv on both weather loci, so rain is a double
    penalty on a pony that is already slow and flat — the proposal's own asked-for check is that
    a wet one is still recoverable and breedable, and nothing in the file touches health, pull or
    the laying interval. Both watcher loci in the pool with all five alleles and no restriction,
    night commoner than day. Speed 2, jump 2, health 3, pull 2, size 0.78–0.92.

    Five marking strains — stars, wormholes, crescents, duskwing bands, polymoon — one
    homozygous coloured pair each, never a plain allele and never a heterozygote. Strains rather
    than five weighted pools because a strain is the only shape that can promise exactly one
    marking: a gene named only by another strain rolls as unnamed, and an unnamed coat gene is
    forced wild.

It is the first breed file to band a hue, or an eye colour, at all

    Every marking gene declares a hue knob over the whole 0–360° circle; the breed bands all
    five to 265–290°, so a horse runs amethyst to violet and no further. Both iris loci band
    eye_chaos_r/_g/_b into a purple box — chaos has no rgb of its own and reads one off the
    allele copy. That is the part with no precedent: no shipped breed had banded a hue, an eye
    colour, or a category (the particle site band, 1–2, is spine or hooves). It parses, the bake
    warns about nothing, and BreedFilesTest is green, which proves the value NAMES resolve —
    it does not prove a purple horse. That is the first thing to look at in game, and it is on
    wiki/breeds.html's Verification tab with what a failure would look like (a rainbow of
    ponies means the band is not reaching the knob).
