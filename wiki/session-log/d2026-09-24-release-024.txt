2026-09-24 — 0.5.024, cut over a day that had already shipped one release

    Released as 0.5.024: horse stasis whole, the Log tab, bond decay, the
    configurable crowding cap, the Enderpony, and the three deletions. Thirty-one
    commits, all of them from the same day — 0.5.023 went out at 11:34 and this
    one at 20:21, which is the widest range a release here has covered in nine
    hours.

    The audit is the work. The rule is that a release note covers the whole tag
    range rather than the tagging session's own changes, and this session wrote no
    code at all: everything in the note came out of reading thirty-one commit
    bodies. That is worth saying plainly because the temptation at tag time is to
    write up whatever is freshest in the conversation, and the freshest thing here
    was the Cloudtouched commonness row — a two-line change sitting at the top of a
    log whose actual headline was an entire storage feature six stages deep.

    Nothing was trusted from a commit message. Each commit in the range claims its
    own tests were green, and several claim the gametests specifically. Those claims
    were re-run rather than quoted: :neoforge-26.1.2:build, all nine gametests
    (1,648 recipes encoding for the join packet), a dedicated server to Done, and
    check-parity at 7,163 checks across 60 cases. The jar's own
    META-INF/neoforge.mods.toml was read back as 0.5.024 rather than inferred from
    the filename, and the jar was opened to confirm enderpony.json is actually
    inside it — the breed shipped hours earlier and a stale :common jar would have
    been invisible otherwise.

    The server-log grep matters more than the boot. Done() is not a clean boot;
    the log was grepped for ERROR and FATAL and came back with none, and the only
    horsegenetics WARN is the dev-environment refmap line. The port was moved to
    25566 for the run because the owner's own server holds 25565, and moved back
    afterwards — which means the next runServer on this box will fail to bind again
    while that server is up. Left as found deliberately rather than quietly
    re-pointing the owner's dev environment.

    The release body carries the two removals in a box of their own. The
    exploding allele and the fifteen lycan shapes are the only things in the range
    that take something away from a world already running, so they are called out
    rather than left as bullets among the additions.
