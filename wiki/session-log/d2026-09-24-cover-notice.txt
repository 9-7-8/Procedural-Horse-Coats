2026-09-24, after the chest loot — a mare's owner is told why she was not covered

    One bullet, the first in the Bugs section of intake/To do.txt, in the owner's words:

        If a breeding attempt does not go through for any reason, print the failure to
        the horse's owner in chat.

    And, higher up the same file in the tuning section, the narrow half of it:

        If a cover fails because there's too many horses, print it to the chat for that
        player.

    Those are one bullet, which is what the previous session's handoff asked to check
    before starting: the second is the crowding cap specifically, the first is every
    reason including it. Both are retired; the rest of that tuning bullet — a stallion
    attempting every eligible mare rather than stopping at one — is untouched and stays.

    Dated first, as START HERE says. The phrase went into To do.txt on 2026-09-23
    (de4cde82). Nothing in the repository sends an owner any message about a cover: the
    only breeding chat lines are the same-sex joke and the carrot cooldown's refusal,
    both aimed at whoever fed the carrot. Open, and genuinely absent.

    The symptom map's row ("Natural cover is missing, repeated, or reports the wrong
    reason") names the four classes, and all four were the right ones.

What was already there, and what was missing

    Every reason already existed as a typed value. NaturalCover.Verdict has COVER,
    NOT_A_BREEDING_MARE, NOT_NOW, NO_STALLION and CROWDED; Conception.Outcome has
    CONCEIVED, DID_NOT_TAKE and NOT_RECEPTIVE. Both were already being switched on in
    NaturalBreedingHandler, with the mare's owner resolvable two lines away. What was
    missing was only the translation to chat: all four failure branches ended in
    ActionTrace.log, the debug record, or in nothing at all.

    So this is not new logic. Implementation advice.txt's rule — return typed results
    from the common breeding logic instead of leaking chat into the model layer — was
    already satisfied, and the work sat entirely in the NeoForge translation plus a new
    file of sentences.

Where it lives

    common/repro/CoverNotice — the sentences, the Reason enum, the quiet period.
    server/NaturalBreedingHandler.tell — verdict to reason, owner lookup, the map.
    ServerConfig.notices.owned_horse_breeding, default true, beside the damage and
    death notices it follows.

    The wording is the feature, so it goes where a test can pin it down: ReproText's
    precedent, and HurtNotice's, both made for this reason. CoverNoticeTest asserts that
    every reason names the mare, that the crowding line carries both numbers, that only
    CONCEIVED is good news, that the same reason waits out the quiet period, that a
    changed reason does not, that a clock which went backwards does not silence her
    forever, and that the two empty rolls read differently.

The judgement call: two refusals say nothing at all

    NOT_NOW (she is between heats) and NO_STALLION (nobody eligible in reach) have no
    Reason and produce no line. The scan runs every 40 ticks on every adult mare in a
    loaded chunk, and those two are the answer for nearly every mare nearly all of the
    time — a line each would be a wall of text that trains the owner to stop reading the
    one that matters. That is the same argument HurtNotice makes for not appending the
    cure sentence to every scratch.

    What is left is the set an owner can act on: the cap (with the count and the limit,
    which is what makes it actionable rather than mysterious), a hurt mare, one being
    ridden, one on a lead, and a cowboy's branded stock. Note that the ActionTrace log
    beside it logs only the health case, on the grounds that ridden and leashed are the
    player's own doing and obvious — that reasoning holds for a debug log read after the
    fact and not for chat, where a mare left saddled in a pen overnight is exactly the
    "why is she the only one not in foal" the notice exists to answer.

Two smaller calls

    The successful cover is announced too, though the bullet asked only for failures. An
    owner who is told why a cover did not happen and then hears nothing when one does
    reads the silence as another failure. A cover is once per heat, so it is not noise.

    The throttle is five minutes and per reason, not per mare. Five because this is not
    an alarm the way a burning horse is — a full paddock is still full a minute later and
    there is nothing new to act on — and per reason because the owner who has just moved
    six horses out needs to hear what stopped it this time rather than silence.

What is not done

    A cover arranged by hand — a golden carrot, or the seed jar — still reports only on
    the action bar, as it always has. The player just acted and is looking at the horse,
    which is what the action bar is for. If that flash turns out to be too easy to miss,
    the same CoverNotice line should go to chat there too; that call is written on
    fertility.html#cover-notice-0924 for the owner to make.

    The owner asked mid-session that failed breedings also go into the horse log. The
    Log tab is not built — horse-browser.html carries the plan and nothing else — so the
    note went onto that plan instead: its rows should be CoverNotice.Reason constants
    rather than a second list of strings that can drift from them. That page's Roadmap
    tab already required a failed cover to keep its refusal reason; now it names which
    reasons those are.
