The consolidation, which was the actual brief

    The accessories system is the
    example the owner gave and the largest of the nine. Enchanted barding, enchantable shoes in
    every metal and gem, bridles, saddle pads, boots × 4, fly masks, martingales, two
    chest slots and the enchantments on all of them arrived as separate asks over months and
    kept being scoped separately — and each time the same four questions came back: where
    does a piece live when the horse has two equipment slots, how is it made, how is it
    decorated, how is it drawn. They are now one section answering those four
    once, and the two roadmap entries that already belonged to it —
    #native-tack and #horse-shoes — were moved inside
    it rather than left to drift alongside. Curios is written up the way it was
    asked for: an optional view onto our own attachment, never a dependency, with the two things
    to verify before promising it.

Each written from the code, so the next session does not pay twice

    Two audits ran before a word was written, and they changed the text
    repeatedly — which is the argument for doing it that way. What they turned up, all of
    it now on the roadmap rather than here:

    - Nothing in the mod is enchantable, and the near-miss matters:
        compat/ModdedArmour gives every generated armour iron’s
        enchantmentValue(), but with no enchantable component
        that value is inert. The first enchantable piece pays for the whole path.

    - Netherite is absent on purpose.
        MaterialScan.collectTagged skips every minecraft: tag entry,
        because vanilla’s own armours are left to BHAR. So a netherite horse armour means
        crossing that line knowingly — a call, not a task.

    - A cart’s wood is in its registry id (oak_wagon), and
        CartWood says that id is permanent once a world uses it. Per-piece wood is
        therefore the jumps migration done a second time, not a
        screen — and jumps are already dyeable per half, which the ask did not
        assume.

    - The cowboy is plains-villages-only —
        BarnPoolInjector touches one terminator pool — but
        “he doesn’t need a house” is already built:
        CowboyHitchHandler converts an unemployed villager standing near a hitch.

    - The horse menu’s Where column exists and is inert, and
        data/HorseWhereabouts already stores last-seen position for the unloaded
        horses the column cannot show. Wiring those together improves the column whether or not
        a teleport button is ever built.

    - The rejected undead design had a price tag: teaching the mod about two
        more entity types means 125 instanceof Horse checks, done
        in handlers rather than in a retargetable mixin. Making undeath a gene avoids all of it.

The opening paragraph was philosophy, not roadmap

    It stated eight things about what the mod is for, and six were absent from
    the philosophy page outright — a page with eight
    good rules about the genetics and no answer to “does this belong in the mod at
    all”, which is the first question a design call asks. The owner’s instruction was
    to rewrite the page to agree with it, so there is now an unnumbered
    frame above the eight: one major system with a
    two-to-three-level budget for everything else, horses-only, vanilla stays intact,
    carrot-not-stick as a mod-wide rule rather
    than a care-layer one, utility not
    decoration with breeding as the sole exception, and
    the SWEM gap stated as design rather than as
    compatibility.

    The numbering was deliberately left alone. Seven wiki pages and four Java
    files cite Philosophy §N in running prose —
    GameteBias, ArcaneStock and HorsePrices among them
    — where a renumber breaks silently and no checker can see it. Every heading gained an
    id instead, so the page can be deep-linked at all; roadmap.html
    had been writing “Philosophy §8” in prose because there was no anchor to
    point at. Recorded as
    a trap between pages.

Two contradictions, handled differently on purpose

    - “Tack gets no stats” is
        reversed (owner). The system the batch describes is tack with real effects,
        and the old call was argued from “no outcome is better”. That argument
        was not wrong, it was misapplied: no-gene-is-better is a rule about the genetic
        model, where a value judgement tells a player what to want, and a saddle that helps you
        is not a judgement about a horse. The entry now records the reversal and the replacement
        reasoning rather than being edited to look like it always said this.

    - The undead genes contradict
        “a concept is a breed, not a
        gene” — which used zombie as its worked example. The
        planned zombie locus bundles double health, a speed penalty, a carnivorous diet,
        aggression and its own taming rule: the “indivisible lump” that rule exists
        to prevent. This one was not resolved, because the undead case has two good
        answers — undeath is arguably one trait with many consequences, the way a lethal
        is, and the design wants an undead horse of any breed, which a breed cannot
        express. The example changed to war horse; the question is
        an open call, flagged from both the
        philosophy note and the roadmap section, and it decides the shape of both genes.

    Nothing was built, so nothing is newly unverified and no gap opened or closed. The intake
    queue is empty again, down to its one permanent line.
