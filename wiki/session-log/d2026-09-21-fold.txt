    dismantles moved the content; what they left behind was eighty-eight pages still
    saying “Checklist on To be verified” and
    linking a page that is now an index with no checklist on it. That is precisely the failure
    the dismantle existed to remove — a link that resolves and tells the reader nothing
    — so it had survived the fix for it.

    Forty-nine of those pages already had a Verification tab and simply pointed
    at the wrong place; they now link #verification on themselves, which
    wiki/tabs.js opens as a tab switch, and their See also rows read
    “What to check in-game” like every other gene page's. Thirty-three gene
    pages promised a checklist that lived nowhere at all, and each got a real one,
    written from what the page itself claims is unproven and ordered by the owner's rule: what
    can be wrong invisibly first, “does it look right” last, every item with
    a pass condition somebody can judge without asking what was meant. The index is
    155 pages and 913 checks, from 122 and 713.

The bake had been eating this work, and today it ate twenty-four tabs

    :common:bakeGeneWikiPages rewrites a data-driven gene's page whole, so
    anything a person adds has to be carried across by name. verifiedBlock carries
    the Verified records — written on 2026-09-15 after a bake emptied
    thirteen pages' — but it shipped with no test, so a routine re-bake
    this session silently deleted the Verification tabs of twenty-four gene pages and one
    Roadmap tab, all written the day before. Nothing went red. The pages are still valid
    pages, the build was still green, and the only evidence was the verification index dropping
    from 155 rows to 131 and one broken #fragment in the roadmap index.

    The 24 were restored from HEAD, and GeneWikiTool.keptPanels now
    carries any panel whose data-tab is not one of the three views
    the tool writes, rather than naming verification and roadmap — so the next list broken
    up onto its subject's page survives by having done that, with no edit to that method. A
    re-bake reproduces all 24 byte-identically.
    GeneWikiCarryTest is the guard the first fix lacked, and it
    asserts the thing that actually broke rather than the extractor: every page the two generated
    indexes name still carries the tab they name it for, and it fails in the other direction too
    when a tab is added and its index is not re-baked. It was demonstrated to fail by stripping
    one page's tab, because a test nobody has seen fail is a test nobody has tested. The contract
    is written up on making a gene.

Writing thirty-three checklists audited the code, as it always does

    Gap 304 is the real one, and it is confirmed in the
    source rather than suspected: a gene's two allele copies cannot both move one
    attribute. AbstractWeatherGene deliberately emits one conditioned
    AttributeMod per copy instead of summing them, because each carries its own
    weather condition — but GeneAbilityHandler names a modifier
    gene/<key>/<attribute>, one slot, and
    addOrUpdateTransientModifier overwrites. Both weather pages promise that two
    copies add. A thunderstorm reaches it with a single horse, since vanilla raises
    isRaining() and isThundering() — which also falsifies
    those pages' argument that the two conditions can never hold at once.
    Gap 303: singer promises
    a per-area arbitration that does not exist, and its cooldown (900 ticks) is shorter than its
    interval (1200), so it can never refuse a firing the interval allowed.
