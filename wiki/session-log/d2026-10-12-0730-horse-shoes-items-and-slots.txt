What was built.
- common/gear/HorseShoes: the shipped metals (HorseShoes.Metal: copper, iron, gold,
  diamond, netherite, with their ids and colours); ships(material), which stops another
  mod's copper or gold getting a second shoe; and worn(frontLeft, frontRight, hindLeft,
  hindRight), which turns four hoof tints (0 = bare) into one WornPart per shod hoof on
  that hoof's PartAnchor. An unshod horse allocates nothing.
- item/HorseShoeItem: one class, an item per metal, stacking to 1. The colour is a
  constant of the item (tint()), read by both the icon and the worn shoe.
  ModItems.HORSE_SHOES registers the five.
- compat/ModdedShoes: a shoe per material out of ModdedMaterials.armourMetals(), the list
  generated horse armour uses. A shoe whose mod was removed is aliased to the iron shoe.
- compat/GeneratedShoes: each modded shoe's item definition, name, entry in the four
  gear/shoe_* tags and metalsmith trade. No texture, no model, no recipe.
- compat/MetalsmithTrades: the trade-set writing moved out of GeneratedArmour so that
  armour and shoes add to each level's file through one writer.
- MaterialScan.fingerprint: "rules|3" became "rules|4", so a generated pack from an older
  jar is rebuilt and gains the shoe files.
- Resources: five items/*.json on one model (models/item/horse_shoe.json) and one
  greyscale sprite (textures/item/horse_shoe.png, from tools/bake-drawn-items.mjs),
  tinted by client/MetalTintSource; five lang names; the four gear/shoe_* tags; five
  trades, one a level, and each trade_set's "amount" raised from 3 to 4.
