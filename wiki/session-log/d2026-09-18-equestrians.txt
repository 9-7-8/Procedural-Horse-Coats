2026-09-18, after the herd test — The horseman is four people now

    One villager profession sold tack, carrots, armour, papers, eggs and tickets across five
    tiers. That is a shop rather than a tradesman, and it left nothing for any of it to
    become as he rose: his fifth tier was the same counter with dearer things on it. He is
    deleted outright — no alias, no transitional profession, hard rule
    6 — and four take his place: an
    equestrian leatherworker, scientist, supplier and
    metalsmith, each with a post, a POI, a hat colour and five tiers of its own.

    Four blocks and not one, and that is forced rather than chosen. A POI
    hands out exactly one profession — vanilla reads the job off the job site claimed,
    not the other way round — so four jobs need four job sites. They are the same block
    four times over, sharing one model and one texture set with the
    hitch, and making each look like its trade is
    open work. The recipes are not shared: every post takes two hair cloth and two fences, and
    the remaining three slots are what tells them apart, which is what keeps
    check-recipes.mjs able to hold the whole set distinct.

    Two loot functions did the work the tiers needed.
    set_tack_tint writes the mod’s own three-zone tack_tint
    from a named palette, so it reaches the saddle and the metal fittings that vanilla’s
    dyed_color cannot — which is what makes the leatherworker’s five
    tiers a ladder you can see, bone meal and flowers at Novice through lapis on diamond
    fittings at Master. That also answers the
    open question the roadmap had been holding since the dyed-saddle trade was withdrawn for
    selling an invisible upgrade: the objection has expired, and the master tier sells a
    fitting material, which makes the trade a source rather than a convenience.
    set_random_gene grew a homozygous flag, which names both alleles
    the same so the foal is certain to get the gene rather than getting it on a coin flip, and
    skips genes whose homozygote canOccur rejects rather than selling an inert
    carrot.

    The homestead was rebuilt around them, and the interesting part is what the bake
    stopped counting by hand. The house is deepened three rows as well as widened a
    column, for five beds — one per sleeper, because a villager who claims a job site
    and has no bed never sleeps and never restocks, so the bed count is the other half of the
    post count. The row that is duplicated is the only cross-section a hip roof tiles, and it
    is also the door’s own row, so the door is lifted out first and put back or the west
    wall grows one door per copy. Interior extents, the run of wall a post may stand in front
    of, and every post position are now read off the finished grid and asserted
    rather than counted up from the duplication constants — so retuning the depth moves
    the frontage with it instead of silently posting a block over the doorstep.

    The cowboy got his own hat file. He used to read the horseman’s
    profession PNG directly, which was the cheapest possible guarantee the two could not
    drift. That stopped being possible the moment the horseman became four files in four
    colours, so tools/villagers/bake-profession-hats.ps1 now writes all five from
    one piece of source art. The trap it documents is that the hat is two rectangles
    on the sheet and a long way apart — the crown, and the brim, which unwraps into the
    far corner — and recolouring only the crown gives a villager a teal hat with a brown
    underside, which reads as a texture bug rather than as a mistake in a bake. The texel
    counts are asserted so a redrawn source fails loudly. None of the four is purple: that is
    the arcane dealer’s, and he has to stay
    pickable out of a crowd.

    Fixed on the way past: cowboy_hitch had shipped with no loot
    table at all, so breaking one dropped nothing. It has one now and so do the four new posts.

    What this bought and what it owes. It bought four shops that can each get
    better at one thing, and a checklist item that names which POI is broken rather than
    reporting that nothing happened. It owes a
    confirmed homestead: the piece grew on the axis the
    jigsaw bounding-box check is tightest on, the pool weight behind it is arithmetic against
    the old footprint, and nothing downstream — four jobs, one surname across
    five, five beds slept in — can be tested until a homestead is found in a world. The
    whole batch is §0-FJ.
