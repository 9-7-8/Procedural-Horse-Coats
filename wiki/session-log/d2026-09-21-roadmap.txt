2026-09-21, after the verification work — the roadmap is dismantled the same way, and a plan with no page now means a missing page

    Same instruction, same day, the other list. The roadmap was
    2498 lines and about fifty sections; it is now 363,
    of which everything below the markers is generated. The rule is hard rule 9 in
    CLAUDE.md, which now covers both indexes in one paragraph because they are
    one mechanism.

    The half that was new. Verification had it easy: every checklist was
    about something that exists, so every section had a page to go to. A roadmap does
    not — most of what is planned has no page precisely because it is not built. The
    owner's call closed that: if an item is partly implemented its plans go on the
    Roadmap tab of the page for the existing version, and if it is not implemented at all it
    gets a new page carrying a Roadmap tab and nothing else. So “this plan has
    nowhere to live” stops being a reason to keep a shared backlog and becomes a missing
    page, and the page is already there to document the thing on when the first piece ships.

Where it went

    Twenty-four existing pages grew a Roadmap tab — the plans for a
    subject now sit behind the same tab bar as what it does and how it is built, after
    horse gear, which had been the only one.
    Twenty new pages were written, each a single subject with a single tab:
    undead horses, horsemanship,
    the horse browser, the cart and
    jump bench, attached model parts,
    virtual stables, troughs,
    stall bars, config,
    rider comfort,
    the magical gene backlog,
    curly coat,
    base-aware phenotype naming, and the seven researched-then-deferred
    ideas — chase, magic grab,
    storm fed, block preference,
    forced riding,
    momentum sprint and the lasso.
    They are registered in wiki/pages.js under a Planned section
    of their own rather than among the built pages, and the landing page carries them as a
    section that starts collapsed — a reader looking under Items for a block that does
    not exist is being lied to. When a piece of one ships, its page grows the other tabs and
    its entry moves to the section it then belongs in.

    One section deliberately did not get a Roadmap tab.
    Deliberately out of scope — racing, competitions and the rosettes — was
    the old page's single labelled exception, and it was never a plan: each entry was proposed,
    considered and placed outside the base mod. It went to
    design decisions, which is where an answer lives.
    With it gone the page has no hand-written body at all, which is the whole point.

The machinery, and the two things it had to be told

    wiki/tools/bake-roadmap-index.mjs is the twin of the verification baker and is
    deliberately the same shape: it harvests one note roadmap-summary sentence per
    Roadmap tab, counts the panel's own headings, and hard-fails rather than write an
    index that omits a page. It caught horse gear
    immediately — that tab predated the contract and had no summary box.
    The count is floored at one, because two pages dissolved their only heading
    into the <h1> (repeating the page title under it read badly) and
    “0 entries” beside a row that exists is simply wrong.

    Two ids had to be renamed, and only two. #sex-linked and
    #bond already existed on the genetics model
    and horse care, so the moved sections became
    #sex-linked-plans and #bond-roadmap. Everything else moved with
    its id intact, which is what let 86 inbound links across 21 pages be
    rewritten by one script rather than by hand. Historical mentions inside <code>
    on this page were left alone: they record what a path was.

What the move exposed, which is the part worth keeping

    Putting a plan next to its subject made two things visible that were invisible while they
    sat apart, and both are now recorded rather than quietly fixed.
    Gap 301: seven pages already described the
    same plan in their own words, and the two copies disagree — carrots on
    whether the Epigenetic Stabilizer has a reason to exist yet, items on whether three or four
    whistles share a texture, jumps on whether crossrails and the half-height rungs shipped.
    The older half is usually the stale one, and the fix is a merge each.
    Gap 302 is not a move artefact at all:
    research papers’ Gameplay and Coding tabs give
    opposite answers to what right-clicking a paper does, and it was found only because
    a third tab moved in beside them.

    The move was verbatim on purpose, which left one predictable class of damage: prose saying
    “the table above”, “the chase gene below” or “a position this
    page held twice” when the referent was the roadmap. Those were repaired in a separate
    pass after the move rather than during it, so that the moving could be checked against the
    source byte for byte first. check-links.mjs, check-gene-tabs.mjs,
    sync-page-views.mjs, creator parity and both index bakes are green, and
    headless Chrome confirmed the tab bars, the collapsed Planned section and its
    Coding-view-only visibility. Nobody has looked at any of it, which is
    three new items on coding notes —
    ranked last, deliberately, because none of it can be wrong invisibly.
