- 98671d04 "Fix #37: order gametests flaked on a random genome and on their
  neighbours". Test-only (neoforge-26.1.2/.../gametest/ModGameTests.java). Two causes:
  1. order_combat_gate's "plain" horse was whatever its founding rolled. MagicFighterGene
     gives six founders in a hundred a gladiator copy, and HorseOrders.fighter then
     rightly lets the horse fight. With the failure message printing the genome, both
     failures were magic_fighter=Gld/n. New makePlain() writes Genotype.wildType(), the
     way makeGuardian() writes Grd/Grd (both now go through refound()).
  2. order_hunt_picks_a_monster, order_guard_holds_its_reach and order_stay_walks_back
     reach past their 1x1 cell. StructureGridSpawner puts tests 6 blocks apart in x and
     7 in z, and padding does not move them. The hunt test held a husk that was not its
     own (twice). The guard test's husk was 4 blocks off, alive, with a healthy fighting
     horse still under its order, but hasLineOfSight was false (a neighbour's build).
     New registerAlone() gives each of the three an environment of its own. The
     environment is the batch key, and GameTestRunner.runBatch runs the batches one
     after another, so each runs alone. New aloneOnClearGround() discards the entities
     round it (hunt radius + slack) and clears the blocks with no block entity in
     x -4..6, y -2..2, z -8..6 before it spawns anything. y -2 is feet level, because
     each test stands on a pedestal two blocks above the ground.
  Proof:
  - Diagnostic loop before the fix: 4 of 6 runGameTest runs had an order test red.
  - After: 7 of 7 runs passed all 55. An eighth run had no order failure either: it
    used the first cut of the clearing (y 0..3, which missed the eye line).
  - Mutation: makePlain forced to Gld/n turns order_combat_gate red.
  - :neoforge-26.1.2:build and check-parity green.
  #37 closed by the commit, with a proof comment.
