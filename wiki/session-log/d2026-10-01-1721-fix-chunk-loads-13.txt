    f9d604b1  #13: horse AI and the realm forced synchronous chunk loads
      What was wrong. The live server runs Cupboard, which logs a stack trace for every
      synchronous chunk load. Our frames were in about 250 of 911 traces (17 Sep to
      1 Oct). One trace per site, pulled from the raw logs including the .log.gz files,
      showed two causes:
      - WRITES. HorseRealmLift.lift (101 traces) and HorseRealmTerrain.pool wrote with
        setBlock(..., 2). Without flag 16 (UPDATE_KNOWN_SHAPE), vanilla re-shapes every
        neighbour: setBlock -> markAndNotifyBlock -> updateNeighbourShapes. An edge
        block's neighbour is in the next chunk, so each write loaded that chunk. Both
        now write with 2 | 16. The lift moves whole columns, states and all, and water
        re-shapes nothing, so neither needs neighbour shape updates.
      - READS ON TICK PATHS. HungerFoodGoal.rungOf (reached from canContinueToUse, so
        every tick), PortalEventHandler.portalBlockUnder (every horse and player, every
        tick), and the "nowhere to put it" branch of GeneAbilityHandler.reconcileGlow
        now skip a position whose chunk is not loaded. That branch keeps its GLOW_LIGHT
        entry, so the old light is still cleared later.
      - INTENDED LOADS, now commented as such: HorseRealmTerrain.decorateNow (arrival),
        HorseRealm.safeReturn, TicketHandler.landingSpot (already commented),
        StallDetector.forSign (sign placement and ticket time), and
        GeneAbilityHandler.clearLight.
      Proof. New gametest realm_lift_loads_no_neighbour (ModGameTests). It generates
      chunk [-3907, 4111], marks it as old-floor, rings its edge with stone, runs the
      real HorseRealmLift.liftNow, and asserts all four neighbours are still unloaded.
      With the old flag 2 it went red: "loaded its neighbour [-3908, 4111]". With
      2 | 16 it is green. All 24 gametests pass on the final tree.
