#240, Worn gear is 3D - piece 2: edged horse armour. Commit 33933253. Closed.
- What was missing: horse armour on our horses was vanilla's shell, the body mesh
  inflated 0.1 with nothing at its edge.
- What changed: client/gear/BardingLayer replaces the SimpleEquipmentLayer that
  GeneticHorseRenderer registered for HORSE_BODY. It reads the item's equipment asset
  layers, ClientHooks.getArmorTexture and IClientItemExtensions.getArmorLayerTintColor
  exactly as EquipmentLayerRenderer.renderLayers does (read in the 26.1.2 sources), and
  draws each layer as a WornMeshes mesh. The mesh is cut from the body mesh baked with NO
  inflation (createBodyMesh(CubeDeformation.NONE), 64x64, MeshTransformer.scaling(1.1F),
  as LayerDefinitions builds HORSE_ARMOR) at WornRule.FLOOR, so the rim reaches the coat
  and the lift passes the 0.25 rule; lifting vanilla's inflated model would have needed
  0.15. The layer owns that model and calls setupAnim on it before walking its bones.
  The glint is the same quads submitted through RenderTypes.armorEntityGlint (the GLINT
  pipeline draws at depth EQUAL). A fading horse fades the armour here (entityTranslucent
  plus RiderFade.fade), as HarnessLayer does.
- client/gear/BardingRule is the pure fallback decision: gear.edges off, a per-item model
  swap (getGenericArmorModel), a trim, no drawn layer, or any layer whose mesh is flat
  sends the WHOLE item to the vanilla layer kept inside BardingLayer.
- Proof: :neoforge-26.1.2:test --tests '*BardingRuleTest' 5 green; broken on purpose
  (flatLayers > 99) and "one layer with no mesh sends the whole item to vanilla" went
  red; restored. :neoforge-26.1.2:build green; check-parity OK.
- Pictures (photo shoot, 12 shots, shown to the owner): iron, gold, diamond, blue-dyed
  leather each beside a -flat twin; diamond with a glint; iron with saddle and storage
  harness; gold over dorsal spines; iron from behind. Every suit sits on the body, neck,
  head and legs; leather's three colours are right; saddle and harness are clear of the
  armour; spines poke through; no "drawn flat" line was logged, so no vanilla sheet trips
  RimMesh.QUAD_LIMIT. A 2x zoom of the diamond pair shows the edged suit thicker with
  walls at the chest and ear bands. At whole-horse distance edged and flat are hard to
  tell apart. The glint cannot be judged from a still. Nothing was seen moving.
- Docs: wiki/horse-gear.html#barding-edged (Coding), #verify-barding (Verification),
  wiki/compatibility.html#armour, wiki/decisions.html, wiki/model-parts.html.

DECIDED
-------
Calls made without the owner (all recorded on wiki/horse-gear.html#barding-edged):
- The mesh is cut from an uninflated body mesh baked by the layer, not from vanilla's
  HORSE_ARMOR layer definition. Cost: a pack or mod that replaces that layer definition
  is not followed.
- An item with an armour trim, or whose item swaps the armour model, is drawn by
  vanilla's path (the treatment's Recommended fallback named only unreadable textures
  and the face limit).
- The fallback is per item, never per layer.
- Edged armour adds nothing to a glowing horse's outline (custom geometry carries no
  outline colour). Left as a Verification question for the owner.
- The treatment's Open on the glint: it is drawn on the rim too, by resubmitting the mesh.
The owner's standing decisions (#223) were followed as written: 0.25, horses only, all
armour, one switch.
