2026-09-29 — the 0.5.034 cut


    Short. Seven sessions above shipped since the last tag; this is the
    release. Second cut of the same day, which is why the range audit matters
    more than usual - 0.5.033 went out at 12:21 and this one at 22:00.

    RANGE AUDIT. v0.5.033..HEAD is 59 commits, and 59 is not the size of the
    release. One commit carries over a thousand files on its own (the CC0
    texture pack under intake/potential-assets/), and a large share of the rest
    are intake treatments, scoping notes and session-log entries with no code
    in them. What actually ships is:

        rider comfort, all five pieces - mounted mining, the ALT+E inventory
        key, right-click to equip tack, the snapped lead, and the fade
        whistles, all four pieces - the bond gate on binding, near horses
        walking in instead of teleporting, one key for every whistle, and a
        glint on a bound one
        the art session - five posts drawn apart, three blocks that had worn
        vanilla furniture, the gold crop, three stall signs, three licensed
        Raven icons
        one real bug, found sideways: the rescuing braid had never been drawn

    TWO ROADMAPS EMPTIED. Both rider-comfort.html and item-whistles.html had
    their Roadmap tabs deleted in this range, so both left roadmap.html by
    themselves. That is the clean version of "what shipped" for this note: not
    a list of commits, but two plans that are finished.

    HOW THE NOTE IS LED. Unlike 0.5.033 there is no rule change here - nothing
    in this release takes a capability away from a player, so there was no
    warning of that shape to lead with. The two things that do want saying
    first are both about risk rather than about rules:

      - The fade is on by default and NOBODY HAS SEEN IT. It is the only
        deliverable in the release whose correctness is a matter of looking,
        and the standing instruction on it is to report rather than patch
        round. So the note asks for that in as many words, names the switch,
        and names the two fallbacks already agreed.
      - LeashDropMixin sits on Entity.spawnAtLocation, which every dropped
        item in the game passes through. The guard is one identity comparison,
        but the symptom of it being wrong is items vanishing nowhere near a
        horse - which nobody would think to report as a horse bug. The note
        says that plainly and names behaviour.leads_return as the first thing
        to try.

    That is a different shape of first block from last time and deliberately
    so: last time the reader needed to know a rule had changed under them,
    this time they need to know which two things to distrust.

    THE BRAID GOT ITS OWN SECTION. A bug fix usually does not, but this one is
    the only item in the release that is retroactive in the other direction:
    anybody who has ever crafted a rescuing braid has been wearing an invisible
    one. It is also the sharpest example of why the Verification tabs exist, so
    it is written as "check the thing you believe you already saw".

    PRE-TAG CHECKS. Version bumped in gradle.properties only. Jar verified
    rather than assumed - it declares 0.5.034 and carries every new class
    (RiderFade, all three new mixins, TackEquipHandler, MountedMiningHandler,
    WhistleCalls, WhistleCallGoal, both keybinding pairs, WhistleSelection out
    of common/). Build green, creator parity green (7163 checks across 60
    cases). All 22 gametests pass in 32.98 s with no ERROR line in the run.
    runServer on the release build to Done (0.349s), zero ERROR lines, and
    debug.log confirms LeashDropMixin into Entity and LeashPlacerMixin into Mob
    with no injection failure - which with defaultRequire at 1 is the
    guarantee. FadingGearMixin is in the config's "client" block and therefore
    correctly never loads on a dedicated server; the fade session had already
    confirmed it applies in a real client boot.

    Ran runServer at all because this release adds five config options and
    three mixins, which is exactly the shape of change that crashes at mod load
    and nowhere else.

    THE LIVE SERVER WAS RUNNING THROUGHOUT AND WAS NOT TOUCHED. The java
    process on this box at cut time was ~/26.1.2-neoforge-server, not ours -
    identified by its world/session.lock and its nogui command line before
    anything was built, so that the dev server's JVM could be killed at the end
    by pid without going near it. Worth writing down because "kill every java"
    at the end of a session would have dropped somebody's players.

    gh release create with the jar attached. A pushed tag is not a release.

    STILL UNPLAYED. All of it. Two keybinds, a new listener on the busiest
    right-click in the mod, three mixins and a visual feature - none of which a
    headless test can see. The fade is first in the queue for eyes.
