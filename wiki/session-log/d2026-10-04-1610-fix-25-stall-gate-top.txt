- a1a2bea9 "Fix #25: the top of a closed gate counted as stall floor".
  What was wrong: StallDetector.standable read the air over a closed gate as floor one
  step up. A gate's collision is 1.5 tall, so blocksMotion() is true. The floor walk
  climbed the gate into the open yard, ran past MAX_COLUMNS, and only the wall-ring
  search at the floor's own level (withinLowestRing) brought it back.
  - A slab floor stands a level above that ring, so the ring search started above the
    gate, and every level up to sign+3 was open over the gap: refused.
  - A raised east column cuts the ring at gy+1 down to the low column: 2 of 4 tiles.
  Fix: StallDetector.tallerThanABlock. A block whose collision shape rises above 1.0
  (a fence, a wall or a closed gate) is never floor, so the walk stops at a gate the way
  it already stopped at a fence top.
  Proof: new gametest stall_shapes_bind_as_one_room (ModGameTests). It builds SLAB, STEP,
  DROP, PLAIN-gate and FENCES stalls on a 25x25 platform at origin+40, reads each with
  StallDetector.forSign, and requires exactly the floor tiles (DROP: not all of them).
  - Without the fix: "SLAB refused, expected exactly its 2 floor tiles; STEP bound 2
    tiles, expected exactly its 4 floor tiles", the yard's exact reading, with the
