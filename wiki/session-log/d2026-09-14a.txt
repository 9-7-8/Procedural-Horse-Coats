2026-09-14 — the last two breeding gaps, the vanilla way

    The owner settled the two gaps left open overnight and ended the session. The morning's
    audit is unchanged: §0-DV.

    - Gap 230, covers through a fence: done as vanilla does it. The patched
        sources show vanilla's BreedGoal ignores line of sight on purpose
        (ignoreLineOfSight()) and instead requires sixty ticks of approach, strictly
        inside three blocks. A natural cover now requires exactly that (a
        NaturalCover.Courtship), instead of covering on the first scan. So a fence
        still does not stop a stallion; it does not stop a cow either.

    - Gap 231, server-authoritative: the seed jar and debug clock cancel
        unconditionally on the client and decide on the server. That is safe because the
        interact packet is sent before the event fires, and in single-player the integrated server
        runs the same handler.

    - Both behaviours are recorded in the API notes, since each is a vanilla rule that looks like
        the opposite from the outside.
