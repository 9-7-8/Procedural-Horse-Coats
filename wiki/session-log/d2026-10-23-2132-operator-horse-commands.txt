DECIDED (all without the owner; recorded in the class javadocs and the page sections above)
-------
- send lives under /horserealm; the sweep and breeding switch are their own top-level
  commands, matching /horseundead and /horsebounty.
- Owned horses sent by command keep their owner (as if led through a portal) rather than
  being released; the realm's existing feral rule then applies.
- Natural breeding switch is one global key, not split wild/owned.
- Sweep never takes ridden/leashed/sold horses; an unloaded dealer counts as "sold" (safe side).
- RealmSend.dealerOf reads the brand directly instead of CowboyHandler.ownerOf, which clears
  the brand of a horse whose dealer is merely unloaded.

FOUND ON THE WAY
----------------
- Bought cowboy stock is indistinguishable from sale stock without asking the dealer
  (Cowboy.hasSold); a naive sweep would have taken paid-for horses. Handled, not a bug.
- CowboyHandler.ownerOf clears a brand when the dealer is not loaded, not only when dead.
  Possibly intended (its comment says "they are gone"); left alone, not filed - worth a look.
