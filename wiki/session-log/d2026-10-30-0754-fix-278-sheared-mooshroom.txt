- a5677485 "Fix #278: a sheared were-mooshroom lost the horse inside it".
  What was wrong: MushroomCow.shear converts the mooshroom into a cow and fires
  LivingConversionEvent.Post. NeoForge's AttachmentInternals.onLivingConvert then copies
  attachments with isDeath = true, so only copyOnDeath ones cross over. LYCAN_SHIFT is
  not copyOnDeath on purpose, so the cow came out plain and the horse tag was discarded
  with the mooshroom. Both call sites were read in the 26.1.2 sources.
  Live evidence: the live server's console.log on 2026-10-29 has three mooshroom SHIFT
  lines at 18:43 (9b4a940b, 9c84f266, ccf71b78) and no REVERT for any of them, while
  every other form reverted at 18:53. The player reported it at 18:55.
  The fix: LycanthropyHandler.onFormConverted moves the shift (and LAST_GROUND) onto the
  outcome mob and makes it persistent. rewireOnJoin then registers the new body. An
  outcome that is not a Mob reverts the horse at once. It logs
  "lycan | CONVERTED <from> into <to>". Two test doors were added: isShifted and dawnFor.
  Proof: gametest sheared_were_mooshroom_keeps_the_horse (ModGameTests). With the
  listener's @SubscribeEvent removed it failed with "the cow out of the sheared mooshroom
  carries no horse - the horse is lost". With it, all 72 required gametests passed, and
  the log read "CONVERTED minecraft:mooshroom into minecraft:cow ... the horse goes with
  it". No pictures (nothing visible changed).
  #278 is closed, with a proof comment. The three horses lost on 2026-10-29 cannot be
  recovered.
