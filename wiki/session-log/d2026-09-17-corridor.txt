2026-09-17, last — The horse dimension stops being a random gallery

    The owner’s brief: the aisles should be every breed down one side and
    every unchecked marking down the other, generated as you walk, with more
    signs saying what each thing should look like — and the visual checks taken out of
    the test yard and put first in the tester page’s visual section.

The corridor is two lists now, and one of them ends

    It was PEN_COUNT = 2,000 random showcase genotypes on both sides: a corridor
    that never ran out and so never told you anything was left. Now the right-hand
    (+Z) column is CoatCheckPlan — one pen per
    allele combination of every gene whose wiki page carries no Verified block,
    newest-gene-first by the timeline, because a painter written last week is where a drawing
    bug actually is. The left (-Z) column is every breed alphabetically, a mare and
    a stallion each, looping for as long as the right column runs.

    The length is derived and it shrinks as the mod is tested.
    COAT_CHECK_PENS is CoatCheckPlan.size(): confirm a gene, write its
    Verified block, re-bake, and its pens leave the corridor. That is the opposite
    direction from the old catalogue corridor, which grew with every gene added until it wanted
    seven million blocks.

    - Four horses to a pen, one combination on a black, a bay, a chestnut and
        a white base, named on their nameplates — chosen over four consecutive pens because
        “does this work on every base?” is answered by looking at them together, and
        four pens would have made it a memory test and quadrupled the walk.

    - The white horse is the masking check, on KIT
        W2/N — one copy of an allWhite allele, which
        removes every pigment. Anything still drawn on it is a gene ignoring a mask. Not
        W22, which is sabino-like alone and all-white only beside a booster.

    - Up to four sign boards per pen, replacing the single genotype-code sign:
        the pen number and gene, the outcome’s name, then the gene file’s own
        description wrapped across the rest — so a tester compares the horse to what the
        gene claims, not to a guess.

    - A breed pen’s horses really are that breed: a new
        HorseRecords.newFounder(horse, rng, breed, sex), because the existing pair of
        overloads could give you the lineage token or a forced sex and never both. A pen
        labelled with a breed whose horse reads as feral is a label, not a breed.

Where the list comes from

    :common:bakeUnverifiedGenes reads the wiki’s own
    Verified blocks — the record hard rule 9 already requires — and writes
    horsegenetics/unverified-genes.txt, because the wiki is not in the jar and the
    game cannot ask a web page. The alternative was a second hand-kept list of what is untested,
    and a drifted list of that kind is worse than none: it says “done” about something
    nobody looked at.

    Two filters stay at runtime rather than bake time, so the resource is a faithful copy
    of the wiki and the build decides what it means: a gene absent from genes/index.json
    is skipped (an absence there is a killswitch, not an oversight), and a gene that paints nothing
    is skipped — several of the newest genes are health and behaviour loci whose pen would have
    been four identical horses. Extension and agouti are excluded as the base coat rather than a
    marking on one, the same exclusion ShowcaseGenotypes makes.

    The first scan was wrong in the mod’s favour and had to be fixed: agouti and
    champagne carry the older marker (Owner-verified in-game, a
    note good div with no id="verified" anchor), so they were being counted
    as unconfirmed and would have filled the corridor with pens for work done on 2026-09-01.

The yard gave up its looking pens

    - Row E’s gilded-crackle display stall is gone — it asked only
        “does this look right?” for one gene, which the corridor now does for every
        unconfirmed marking. The row itself stays: the same west block holds the pool, six
        blocks along. Deleting the row outright broke the pool’s z and failed the
        build, which is the one mistake the chained-row layout cannot absorb for you.

    - Row D’s egg-layer pen is gone — its last open question was the
        cap, and the overnight run of 2026-09-14/15 answered it on that very pen: the despawn timer
        bounds the floor and nearby_cap never fires.

    - The magic stat pens now say what to do (“RIDE both horses”)
        rather than what the census prints, which is not something a tester does.

    - Everything else stayed. Ender echo’s ridden half, holy ward’s
        spawner farm, starburst’s pale structure, lantern’s bloom and the stat loci are all
        still open on verification.html, so their pens are still earning their place. The
        rule used was the pen’s own question being answered in writing — not the gene
        being flagged verified, which would have deleted the egg-layer pen while its cap question was
        still live.
