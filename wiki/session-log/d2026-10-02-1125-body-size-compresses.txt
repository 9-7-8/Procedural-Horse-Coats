- f8bb8745 "body.size off compresses scale into 0.85-1.15 instead of flattening it".
  Before: HorseRecords.applyTraitsToEntity wrote a flat 1.0 to Attributes.SCALE when
  body.size was false, so a Shire and a Falabella stood the same height. Now it writes
  HorseTraits.compressScale(traits.scale()) = 1 + 0.15*tanh((s-1)/0.15). That always
  stays inside 0.85-1.15, keeps every horse in the same order, and leaves a near-1.0
  horse almost unmoved. HorseTraits.COMPRESSED_SCALE_BAND = 0.15 was measured from the
  26.1.2 sources rather than chosen:
    - EntityType.HORSE is sized 1.3964844 x 1.6 with eyeHeight 1.52;
    - Entity.isInWall tests the eye position;
    - a stall is a floor with two blocks of air (StablePopulator).
  So the hitbox meets the roof at 1.25x and the eye suffocates past 1.316x. At 1.15x
  the top is at 1.84 and the eye at 1.75.
  The ServerConfig key comment and class javadoc were rewritten to match.
  CustomHorseSpawnScreen.drawSizeReadout adds "(n.nnx in this world)" when the setting
  is off. ServerConfig is ModConfig.Type.SERVER, which is synced to clients, the same
  thing MountedMiningHandler relies on. The designer (wiki/horse-designer/js/gui.js)
  cannot know the setting, so its readout says "(genotype; servers may differ)", and
  shrinks to fit when the panel is narrow. That is a deliberate divergence, commented in
  both files (hard rule 5). The effects-verb scale modifiers are still switched off
  outright when body.size is false; a modifier is not compressed.
  Proof:
