2026-09-21, after the gear work — the verification page is dismantled, and becomes a generated index

    To be verified had reached 3734 lines and
    sixty <h2> sections. The owner's instruction was to move all of
    it onto the page for the thing each section tested, and to rebuild the page itself as
    nothing but a collector. Both halves shipped, and the rule behind them is now
    settled and is hard rule 9 in
    CLAUDE.md.

The rule, in the owner's words

    Verification data goes on the item's page. Nothing is ever hand-written onto the
    verification page. That page exists to do one thing: collect the pages that have
    an open Verification tab. And a verified item is not ticked, it moves
    — off the Verification tab and onto the Coding tab beside it, as
    what was seen, when, and on what evidence. A page whose last check is confirmed loses the
    tab and drops off the index by itself, which is the property that makes the list
    genuinely only shrink.

    Why it had to change. A checklist kept beside the thing it tests is read
    by whoever opens that page and deleted by whoever confirms it. Gathered into one document
    it is read by nobody and deleted by nobody, and its entries outlive the memory of what
    they were about. The measurable form of that rot: sixty-seven of the page's
    anchors were empty <span id> stubs, left behind by sections
    deleted months earlier, and eighty-nine links elsewhere in the wiki still pointed at
    them. Every one of those links resolved. Every one of them told the reader nothing. This
    is the third document to fail this way after CLAUDE.md and the roadmap.

What was actually moved

    Thirty-six sections had a single owner and moved whole. The other twenty-four were
    grab-bags — “still open after 2026-09-13”, “what the overnight run
    left unplayed”, “tuning and balance, moved off the roadmap” — and
    the owner's call was to split them bullet by bullet rather than park each
    on a nearest page, on the grounds that a cross-cutting list on a page it is only half
    about is exactly the wall this change exists to remove. The largest,
    Still open from before 2026-09-13, was 273 bullets across four ordered
    groups, and fanned out to 92 pages; it was done by script with a
    reconciliation that proved every <li> appears byte-for-byte exactly
    once in the output.

    The end state: 122 pages carry a Verification tab, 83 of them gene pages
    and 15 item pages. The original section ids travelled with the bulk of each section, so
    verification.html#jumps-0920 became
    item-jumps.html#jumps-0920 and 81 inbound links were re-pointed
    mechanically by looking up where each id had actually landed rather than from a
    typed table.

The page is now generated, and refuses to be wrong

    wiki/tools/bake-verification-index.mjs scans wiki/ for a
    verification panel, harvests the one sentence each opens
    with in a note verify-summary box, and writes an alphabetical index between
    generated markers. A page joins the list by growing the tab, the same way a gene
    joins the census by existing. The per-row check count is the panel's own
    <li> tally computed at bake time, so it is never a stale number
    somebody typed.

    It hard-fails rather than write a wrong index — a panel with no
    summary box, two of either, or a summary box loose on a page each fail it by name. That
    is deliberate: an index that quietly omits a page is worse than no index, because it is
    trusted. The one thing it does tolerate is a page that only mentions the
    attribute in prose, which is why known gaps writes it
    escaped.

What the writing pass found, as it always does

    One real defect: on making a gene the new panel had been
    inserted inside the “In Java” tab rather than beside it, because that
    page carries a page-foot div within a panel and the insertion point looked
    for the first one. A validator now confirms every verification panel is a direct child of
    article.doc and that every page's <section> tags balance.
    The 122 summary sentences also turned up several checks whose bullets were about a
    different gene entirely — those were split as they were written.

    Checked in a browser, not just in the markup (headless Chrome, over
    file://, through wiki/tools/chrome.mjs): the index renders 122
    rows each with its sentence, the first row's href resolves to a real panel, the
    Verification button appears in the tab bar on a gene page, an untabbed page such as
    villagers builds a tab bar rather than hiding its new
    content, and the repaired making a gene panel is now the
    last top-level panel on the page.
