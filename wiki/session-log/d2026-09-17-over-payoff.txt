2026-09-17, after the flag — Eleven genes stop faking it with subtraction

    The entry below built "over": true and deliberately left the payoff untaken:
    no real gene used it, and the eleven candidates each wanted judging on a horse rather than
    folding into the commit that built the mechanism. This session took it. Dorsal wing,
    contour, starburst and the eight nested genes of the 2026-09-10 intake now carry the flag,
    and the inverted copies that stood in for it are gone — 165 splices, overwhelmingly
    deletions.

The conversion was derived, not hand-written — and the derived rule was wrong

    These files are big and repetitive: the same 9KB SVG d string appears in
    several layers, differing only by an "invert": true. Hand-editing that is how
    you get a gene that is subtly wrong in one expression. So the edit was stated as a rule and
    applied by script: drop a carving mask exactly when it duplicates a non-carving
    mask of a later layer, then mark that later layer over, splicing byte
    ranges so untouched number literals and line endings survived.

    - The rule is not sufficient, and finding out why was the session. It
        flagged dorsal wing's margin band for conversion, because the margin's carve
        matched the squiggles layer — whose region is the inner shape. But the
        squiggles only paint where their SPOTS land, so they do not repaint all of
        it. Dropping that mask floods the whole butterfly teal over the dark ground. The real
        condition is that the later layers must jointly cover the carved shape, and
        two layers keep their subtraction because what they must not cover is painted
        BEFORE them: that margin, and contour's drawn line, which would otherwise wash
        across the summit bands.

    - Static reasoning about it was unreliable — the renderer settled it.
        Contour's case was talked into and back out of twice on paper. What resolved it was
        CoatBakeGoldenTest, whose own javadoc says a diff there is “a prompt
        to go and look at the icons, never a verdict”, followed by a per-pixel diff of the
        icons themselves.

What the measurement said

    Corolla, taper flame, barred wing, uraniid, trillium and ooze drip move by at most
    one channel bit — the same picture out of fewer masks — and foxglove's
    coloured form holds its mean saturation to three decimals. The genes that move are the ones
    painting at strength below 100: stacking makes a layer measure from the
    accumulated colour rather than the gene's start, so instead of landing on its stated colour
    it compounds. Contour is seven bands at 93 and shifts about 38% of the horse, visibly
    greener. The owner took that deliberately rather than revert it, on the
    argument that stacking is what the layers always meant; whether the hypsometric ramp still
    reads as a contour map is the open question at
    §0-EY.

Two traps worth keeping

    - An icon diff is not necessarily like-for-like. GeneIconTool
        photographs whichever allele repaints the most, so a change that moves those scores
        changes the subject. Foxglove's icon switched from its coloured form to its pale
        one and read as a total loss of hue — identical pixel counts, saturation 93% to 0%
        — and was very nearly written up as a regression. It was nothing of the kind. Only
        contour and dorsal wing declare a preview expression; the other nine are
        auto-picked, and for those the honest check is a per-allele render through
        CoatSampleTool.

    - Three icons were stale in git. flaxen, roan and
        silver changed in this bake without their genes moving in the coat golden.
        Baking twice proved the tool deterministic — 230 of 230 byte-identical — so
        they were simply committed before some earlier change and never re-baked. Fixed here by
        accident rather than design.

    DeadLayerTest passed untouched, which is a real result: nothing died and nothing
    on the 29-line ratchet came alive. CoatPipelineGoldenTest did not move either,
    correctly — no gene was added or removed, so no epigenome slot shifted, and none of the
    eleven is among its 39 genotypes. Only coat-bake-golden.txt needed regenerating.
