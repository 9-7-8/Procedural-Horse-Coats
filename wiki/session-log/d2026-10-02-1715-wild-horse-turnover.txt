DECIDED
-------
Owner calls are the treatment's (3 days, touched -> realm, 32-block watch, 8x8 cells,
minimum 5 as a trigger, herd keyed to the lead, five config keys). Calls made without the
owner, recorded at breeds.html#turnover-calls:
- the biome is read at each spot tried, not the cell's centre column (treatment UNVERIFIED
  6 allowed this);
- "out of sight" = vanilla's 24 blocks plus a ~60-degree view cone with a block raycast,
  out to 128 blocks (vanilla itself checks distance only);
- a SPAWNER's horse is not stamped;
- naming is dropped from "touched": an untamed horse cannot be renamed in this mod
  (HorseInteractionHandler opens the window only for the owner);
- a new herd never lands within HerdManager.HERD_RADIUS of another horse, so it is a new
  herd rather than more of the old one;
- the ledger keeps only today's stamps (an older one means what a missing one means);
- old horses: only untamed, never-persistent, wild-herd members get a fresh stamp, so
  released / cowboy / stall / egg horses and lone old Feral Mixed keep their old life.
