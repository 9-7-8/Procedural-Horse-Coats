2026-09-24, after the cover notice — a stallion's day stops being a hard stop

    Two bullets from the tuning section of intake/To do.txt, in the owner's words:

        when a stallion is in a pen with mares, he should always attempt to cover them
        if the conditions are right (ie theyre in heat).

        stallions should ALWAYS cover every day as much as they can, if they don't have
        any kind of negative fertility gene.

    They are one mechanic, so they were worked as one bullet — the same judgement the
    cover-notice session made about its own pair. Both are retired from the file.

Dated first, as START HERE says

    The phrase went into To do.txt on 2026-09-22 (29a1b009). Nothing has closed it since:
    d2026-09-24-cover-notice.txt, which worked the neighbouring bullet in the same section,
    says in as many words that "a stallion attempting every eligible mare rather than
    stopping at one — is untouched and stays". decisions.html has no entry on a stallion at
    all, so the number was never a settled call to reopen. Open, and genuinely absent.

What was actually stopping him

    NaturalCover.decide filtered out any candidate whose coversToday was at or over
    ReproRules.FREE_COVERS_PER_DAY. Not a penalty — a filter. Past three covers a stallion
    was invisible to every other mare in the paddock, and what those mares got back was
    Verdict.NO_STALLION.

    That is the thing worth writing down. NO_STALLION is one of the two verdicts CoverNotice
    deliberately says nothing about, because it is the honest answer for nearly every mare
    nearly all of the time and a line each would be a wall of text. So a paddock whose
    stallion had topped out looked, from the owner's chair, exactly like a paddock where
    nobody was in heat: no foals, no chat, no log line. The cover notice shipped that
    morning could not have surfaced this one, which is why it read as "nothing is
    happening" rather than as a rule.

    The other two breeding paths never had the stop. The carrot and the jar both go through
    Conception.attempt, which multiplies by ReproRules.stallionFactor — a halving, not a
    refusal. ReproRules' own comment admitted the asymmetry in passing: "A natural cover is
    a hard stop at the same number instead." The natural path was the odd one out, and the
    fix was to delete the filter rather than to add anything.

The owner's call, and the one she amended mid-session

    Three options went to her: drop the hard stop only (the taper stays, unifying the three
    paths), drop the taper too for any stallion who is not sf/sf, or keep the shape and just
    raise the number. She took the first.

    Then, unprompted, she named the tie-break: "mares should always prefer stallions who
    have 3 covers or less, and a stallion with 3+ covers should only cover mares if there's
    no other, more virile, stallion around". That is NaturalCover.better — rested beats
    tired at any distance inside NATURAL_REACH, and distance decides only within a group.

    Her two halves collide at exactly three ("3 or less" preferred, "3+" deprioritised), and
    the split was put at >= FREE_COVERS_PER_DAY, where stallionFactor actually halves. A
    stallion who has made his third cover is already on half odds for his fourth, so
    counting him fresh would have had her prefer the halved one — the opposite of what the
    preference is for. Flagged to her in the summary rather than decided quietly.

    Worth noting what is NOT done: the second bullet's "if they don't have any kind of
    negative fertility gene" clause. Under the option she chose the halving applies to every
    stallion, sound or subfertile, and the subfertile allele keeps only its existing 0.25x.
    That is written on fertility.html#settled-no-daily-cap so it is her call to reopen, not
    a thing that silently got lost between two bullets.

The wording the change broke

    ReproText.vetMale read "N of 3 covers made today". With no cap that sentence would have
    reported "5 of 3". It is now the count on its own, plus a second line when he is past
    three saying what it costs him — which is more use to a breeder than a fraction was.
    ReproTextTest asserts the string never contains " of " again, because that is the shape
    the bug would come back in.

    The same number was prose in four more places: the tutorial's breeding and seed-jar
    steps and the vet's-kit step (TutorialPage), item-vet-kit.html and item-seed-jars.html.
    All four said he was "done until tomorrow" or counted him against a limit.
    releases.html was left alone — it records what shipped then, and it was true then.
