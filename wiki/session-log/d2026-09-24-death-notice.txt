Vanilla is not saying it either, which settles the deduplication question

    The bullet was handed over with "a different deduplication problem", the worry
    being that vanilla already writes this sentence and the mod would double it.
    Read the decompiled 26.1.2 sources rather than guess: the block that tells an
    owner their pet died is in TamableAnimal.die, and AbstractHorse extends Animal
    and merely implements OwnableEntity. It never runs for a horse. Vanilla says
    nothing at all when a horse dies, not even to the owner, so there is no line to
    duplicate or suppress.

    The real dedup problem is smaller and the other way round: NeoForge's hook is at
    the very top of LivingEntity.die, before vanilla's own `!this.dead` guard, so a
    second die() in the same tick would say it twice. ANNOUNCED is a map of horse to
    tick with the hurt notice's sweep, and that is all it guards against.

Why the sentence is not CombatTracker.getDeathMessage()

    It was the obvious thing to broadcast and it is wrong here, because it names the
    entity. A horse of this mod carries its name in its record, not on a name tag, so
    vanilla's sentence would announce "Horse was slain by Zombie" about a named mare
    with a pedigree and an owner — and the name, the owner and the cause are the
    entire content of this message. common/care/DeathNotice writes it instead, beside
    HurtNotice and for the same reason: the wording is the feature, so a test pins it
    down.

    The Cause table is reused, with the advice deliberately dropped: one table of
    vanilla damage ids for both messages, so the line about a burning horse and the
    line about the horse that burned to death cannot drift apart, but a dead horse is
    not the moment to be told to fence the drops. The one cause that changes the verb
    is GENETIC_DEFECT — "did not survive a genetic defect", matching what the mod's
    own damage type already says in lang, because a lethal genotype is not something
    that killed the foal.

    Extracting server/HorseNotices (name, causeOf) out of HorseHurtNoticeHandler is
    what makes that shared rather than copied.

Grey for everyone, red for the owner

    Server-wide is the ask, and it is right: a stable hand who finds the body should
    not have to work out whose mare it was. But on a server keeping hundreds of
    horses the one line that matters to a player is their own, so the owner's copy is
    red. That costs a loop over getPlayerList().getPlayers() comparing UUIDs instead
    of broadcastSystemMessage — nothing in the repository used that method anyway.

    The owner is matched by getOwnerReference(), not getOwner(): unlike the hurt
    notice, this message does not need the owner to be standing there. Their name
    comes from HorseOwnership.ownerName and so from the server's name caches, which
    means a mare that dies while her owner sleeps still says whose she was. An owner
    neither cache has seen degrades to "somebody's horse" rather than losing the
    clause. ownerName is the one method in HorseOwnership now taking AbstractHorse
    rather than Horse — the rest of that class guards items bound to a horse and
    nothing binds to a donkey, but a mule that dies still has an owner.

    The showDeathMessages game rule is checked, in vanilla's own order of business. A
    server that turned death messages off has said what it wants of its chat, and a
    horse is not an exception to it. notices.owned_horse_death is the mod's own
    switch, default true, next to notices.owned_horse_damage.
