2026-09-18, after the wizard — The red test was asserting the bug it was written before

    BreedHerdTest had been red on a clean tree since the previous afternoon, found
    by accident and parked as gap 269 without anyone deciding
    which half was wrong. It was the test.

    aDefaultFamilyBandIsOneStallionAndTheRestMares asserted one stallion for a band
    of four and of six. The day before, the tester
    fixes had capped the default harem at BreedHerd.MAX_HAREM_MARES —
    because a traditional band took exactly one stallion however many horses turned up, so every
    adult past the first was a mare and the wild came out overwhelmingly female. With six adults
    one stallion now leaves five mares, one over the cap, so the surplus turns stallion and the
    answer is two. The test was pinning the ratio bug that the cap was written to
    remove, and it was the only thing in the repo still claiming the old behaviour was
    correct.

    Worth naming because the failure had the shape of a regression and was not one. The commit
    that made it red was a deliberate behaviour change with its reasoning written down in three
    places — the constant's javadoc, the breed
    format page, and its own commit message — and a test asserting the opposite outlived
    all three because nothing ran it. The replacement reads MAX_HAREM_MARES rather
    than the number four, so the next move of the cap moves the test with it, and a second case
    walks four sizes past the cap to check each surplus adult turns stallion rather than just the
    first.

    Gap 269 is rewritten, not deleted. The specific failure is closed, but the
    reason it sat unseen for a day is the part that was always the real entry: no CI, no periodic
    full-suite run, and a standing rule against running :common:test unasked because
    it takes ten minutes. “The suite is green” means “someone ran it once”.
    The gap now says that and names what would close it — a nightly run, or a pre-tag gate
    beside the runServer boot check.
