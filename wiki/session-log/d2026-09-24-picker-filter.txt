    One bullet, the next in the Bugs section of intake/To do.txt, in the owner's words:

Dated first

    The phrase went into To do.txt on 2026-09-23 (de4cde82), in the same batch of sixteen
    lines the last several sessions have been working top-down. One day old. The previous
    session (0c753f96, this morning) explicitly did NOT touch it — its commit message says
    so — because "the breeding overview" is the Breeding preview tab and the bullet it did
    retire was about the My horses box. Two bullets, two tabs.

The state it was in

    HorseBrowserScreen's Tab.BREEDING_PREVIEW draws two pickers stacked down the left
    column — mares above, stallions below — and the Punnett squares down the right. The
    lists had no control of any kind: drawPicker() called ClientHorseRoster.of(sex) inline
    and drew it in arrival order. layoutGeneButtons() gates horseFilterBox, genePickButton
    and allelePickButton to the My horses tab, so nothing on this tab could narrow
    anything.

    So "which of my mares and which of my stallions carry SB1" — the question this tab
    exists to answer — could not be asked of a stable big enough to need asking.

What the owner chose

    Two decisions, put as options with previews rather than guessed:

    One filter box for both lists, not one per picker. A mating has two sides and the
    question is asked of both at once; two boxes would also have cost each list about
    two-thirds of a row out of a column that is 150px wide at its narrowest and needed the
    picker geometry reworked. The left column already left sixteen bare pixels between the
    Refresh row (contentTop()-1, 18 high) and the Mares heading (listTop() =
    contentTop()+34), which is exactly an EditBox, so the box cost nothing.

    Sorting on the headings rather than a button. The heading line carried "Mares  (12)"
    and nothing else across the full column width, so the order label fits right-aligned
    beside it and costs no space at all. A "Sort: Name v" button would have eaten ~70px of
    that 150.

The change — all of it in HorseBrowserScreen

    breedingFilterBox, a second EditBox, static-backed by breedingFilter the way
    horseFilterBox is by horseFilter (so it survives a window resize rebuilding the
    screen). Its own field, not the My horses box moved: one box serving two tabs that ask
    about different things reads as a bug the first time it appears to clear itself — the
    same reasoning already written on searchBox at drawChrome.

    pickerList(Sex) is the new single source. It returns
    HorseQuery.apply(ClientHorseRoster.of(sex), breedingFilter, pickerSort,
    pickerDescending), cached against the roster version, the query and the sort. Three
    call sites moved onto it: drawPicker(), horseAt() (the click hit-test) and the wheel
    branch of mouseScrolled(). That is the part that mattered — ClientHorseRoster.of()'s
    own javadoc says the gelding cut is made there "because the click lookup and the drawn
    list must index the same list", and a filter is exactly what makes two such lists able
    to differ by a row. A click landing on the mare below the one pointed at is not a bug
    a player can diagnose.

    The cache is not an optimisation for its own sake: a gene: term walks every locus of
    every horse, and pickerList is asked on every draw AND every wheel notch.
    rebuildPickerLists() also clamps mareScroll and stallionScroll, since a filter that
    shrinks a list can otherwise leave it scrolled past its end.

    pickerHeadingClicked() cycles PICKER_SORTS — nine of HorseQuery.Sort's twelve. Where
    and Bond are left out because a picker row draws a portrait, a name and "g<n>  <coat>"
    and nothing else, and a list that reorders for no visible reason is worse than no sort.
    A plain click takes the next order at defaultDescending()'s direction for it, the same
    call the My horses columns make; shift-click turns the current one round.

    The heading now reads "(4 of 19)" while a filter is on, so a short list is never read
    as a stable that has lost horses, and the empty note says "no mare matches that filter"
    instead of "no tamed mares on record".

    typingInABox() gained the new box, or the inventory key would close the screen on an
    "e" — the vanilla trap the class already documents at length.

An API note

    Screen.hasShiftDown() does not exist in 26.1.2; it is Minecraft.hasShiftDown(). The
    compile caught it. CartsClient already works around the same move, so it is a known
    one rather than a new find.

What was not done, and why

    No wasm re-bake: nothing in common/ or web/ changed. HorseQuery was left exactly as the
    previous session left it — this bullet was a consumer of it, not a change to it.

    No new unit test. The language is already pinned by HorseQueryTest and nothing was
    added to it; what is new is a screen, and there is no screen harness here.
