For Testers

Thank you for helping. This page is a list of things that still need someone to try in a normal Minecraft world. Each test is a short list of steps, one action per step. Do the steps in order. At the end, each test says what it looks like when it works and when something is wrong. If something is wrong, press the Report a problem with this test button under that test.

You do not need to do all of them

Pick any test. The ones near the top matter most, because they can break a save or crash the game without anyone noticing. The ones at the bottom are about how things look. Even one test done carefully is a real help.

Before you start

What you need

  • Minecraft with NeoForge 26.1.2, and the Horse Genetics mod jar in your mods folder.
  • A new, throwaway world for most tests. Some tests kill horses or break things on purpose.
  • Cheats turned on, so you can switch to creative mode and use commands like /time set night.
  • For a few tests, a second player. Those tests say so.

Things the tests assume you know

  • The horse browser: press H. It lists your horses, the gene database, recipes and a "Getting started" page.
  • A horse's information screen: ride or tame the horse, open its inventory, then click the small i button. Only tamed horses have an inventory.
  • Taming: ride a wild horse until hearts appear, the normal Minecraft way. Some horses also come to you if you crouch while holding food.
  • The vet's kit tells you about a horse. Use it on a horse without crouching: it says whether a mare is in heat, whether she is pregnant (and with twins), or how many times a stallion has bred today. Crouch-using it on your own stallion turns him into a gelding, so be careful.
  • Heat and pregnancy: a mare is in heat for one Minecraft day (20 real minutes), then out of heat for one day. A pregnancy lasts one Minecraft day. Plain golden carrots still make an instant foal, like normal Minecraft.
  • Making a horse with a particular gene: in creative mode, open the Horse Genetics creative tab and take the Custom Horse Spawn Egg. Right-click the ground to open its editor. Type the gene's name into the search box, add the gene, and set both of its buttons to the variant the test names. Then press Spawn. A test that says "a Holy ward horse" means a horse made this way.

How to report a problem

Every test has a Report a problem with this test button. What it does depends on whether you have set up the wiki's AI helper (how):

  • With the AI helper: a chat opens and helps you write the report, asking only for what is missing. You check it and send it.
  • Without it: GitHub opens a new issue with the test's name and a short form already filled in. You need a free GitHub account to press Submit.

Either way, it helps a lot to say which step went wrong, what you saw, the mod version, and whether you were in single player or on a server. If the game crashed, attach the newest file from the crash-reports folder.

1. Crashes and save safety (most important)

Joining a world near horses does not kick you

You need: a new world. A server and a second player if you can.

  1. Create a new world.
  2. Travel until you can see wild horses.
  3. Stay near them for one minute.
  4. Switch to creative mode.
  5. Take a Custom Horse Spawn Egg from the Horse Genetics tab.
  6. Open it and press Make egg.
  7. Keep the egg it gives you in your hotbar.
  8. Press H and open My horses.
  9. If you are on a server, ask a second player to join while horses are in view.

It works if: nobody is disconnected.

Something is wrong if: anyone is kicked with a message like "Failed to encode packet".

The mod works in a plain install

You need: a fresh NeoForge 26.1.2 profile with only this mod.

  1. Launch the game.
  2. Create a world with cheats on.
  3. Tame a wild horse.
  4. Open its information screen.
  5. In creative, take any filled breed spawn egg from the Horse Genetics tab.
  6. Use it on the ground.
  7. Type /locate structure horsegenetics:horse_stable in chat.
  8. Press H.
  9. Open the Gene database tab.
  10. Stand near a wild herd and watch it for one minute.
  11. Open a Custom Horse Spawn Egg, press Make egg, and hold the egg it gives you.
  12. Fill a seed jar from your own stallion (see the seed jar test) and hold it.

It works if: the world loads, the tamed horse has a generated name, wild horses have markings, the egg makes a horse of its breed, the command finds a stable, the gene list is filled, and holding the eggs and the jar near horses never disconnects you.

Something is wrong if: the game crashes, you are disconnected, the horse has no name, the breed egg makes a "Feral Mixed" horse, or the gene list is empty.

The F8 horse highlight does not crash the game

You need: a wild herd nearby.

  1. Press F8.
  2. Press F8 again.
  3. Press F8 once more.
  4. Stay near the herd for 4 minutes.
  5. Look at the name of the herd's leader.

It works if: the second press says "Horse highlight OFF" and the outlines go away; after 4 minutes it says "Horse highlight timed out"; the leader's name turns back to white; the game keeps running.

Something is wrong if: the game crashes, or chat says "Horse highlight hit an error and switched itself off". Report that message even though nothing crashed.

Flying through new land does not crash

You need: creative mode and a new world. A server too, if you can.

  1. Switch to creative mode.
  2. Fly quickly in one direction through land nobody has explored.
  3. Keep flying for at least 10 minutes, through as many biomes as you can.
  4. If you can, do the same on a server.

It works if: nothing crashes.

Something is wrong if: the game or server crashes. Attach the newest file from crash-reports.

Horses left alone do not breed without end

You need: a fenced paddock about 30 by 30 with hay bales and water inside, one stallion and 6 to 8 mares at full health. Horses only breed while you are nearby, so you will stay close by (being away from the keyboard is fine, but do not pause).

  1. Put all the horses in the paddock.
  2. Count them and write the number down.
  3. Stay near the paddock, with the game not paused, for several Minecraft days.
  4. Count the horses once every Minecraft day.

It works if: foals appear at first, then the number stops growing and stays about the same.

Something is wrong if: the number keeps going up day after day.

Horses are the same after saving and reloading

You need: three tamed horses that are easy to tell apart (spotted or grey, a very big one, and one that is hurt).

  1. Take a screenshot of each horse.
  2. Open each horse's information screen.
  3. Write down its speed, health, jump, size, sex and bond.
  4. Press Save and Quit to Title.
  5. Open the world again.
  6. Compare each horse with your notes and screenshots.

It works if: the markings, numbers and sex are all the same, and the hurt horse is still hurt.

Something is wrong if: a marking moved, a number changed, a horse's sex changed, or the hurt horse is suddenly healed.

  2. Check that it says she is pregnant.
  3. Press Save and Quit to Title.
  4. Open the world again.
  5. Open her information screen and read the Breeding line.
  6. Wait up to one Minecraft day (20 real minutes) nearby.

It works if: the Breeding line still says Pregnant, and she gives birth.

Something is wrong if: the pregnancy is gone, or she never gives birth.

Something is wrong if: the spawner slows down or stops, or monsters still do not spawn after the horse is dead.

A Hot blooded horse does not make holes or floods

You need: creative mode, two Hot blooded horses, a frozen lake, and a snowy slope with a small house below it.

  1. Put one horse on the frozen lake.
  2. Wait 10 minutes.
  3. Put the other horse on the snow above the house.
  4. Wait 10 minutes.
  5. Look at the lake and the house.

It works if: the ice under the first horse turns to water and it swims, and melted snow does not flood the house.

Something is wrong if: a horse falls into a hole, or water floods the house.

A Verdant horse does not change what you built

  4. Look at the ground around both.

It works if: the dirt slowly grows some grass, and the cobblestone and planks are untouched.

Something is wrong if: blocks you placed turn into something else.

You cannot get the invisible saddle out of a horse

You need: a horse with a bond of 81 or more and no saddle (see the bond test), a hopper, a dispenser, and a chest. A second player if you can.

  1. Ride the horse with no saddle.
  2. Steer it around and jump.
  3. Look at its back.
  4. While riding, open its inventory and try to take the saddle out of the saddle slot.
  5. Close the inventory and count the saddles in your own inventory.
  6. Get off, and look at its back again.
  7. Place a hopper under where the horse stands, with a chest under the hopper, and ride the horse onto it.
  8. Place a dispenser facing the horse while you ride it, and power it.
  9. If you have a second player, ask them to open the horse's inventory while you ride it.
  10. Ride the horse, then type /kill to die while riding. Look at what you drop.
  11. Put a normal saddle on a different horse, ride it, get off, and check the saddle is still there.

It works if: the horse steers and jumps like a saddled horse; no saddle shows on its back; you never end up with a saddle from it (not in your inventory, the chest, on the ground, or in the other player's hands); it is bare when you get off; the horse you saddled yourself keeps its saddle.

Something is wrong if: you get a saddle from the bond-81 horse in any way. Say which way, even if the saddle looks invisible.

2. Breeding

Plain golden carrots still make an instant foal

You need: a tamed mare, a tamed stallion and golden carrots.

  1. Feed the mare a golden carrot.
  2. Feed the stallion a golden carrot.

It works if: a foal appears right away.

Something is wrong if: no foal appears.

A stallion and a mare in heat breed on their own

You need: a tamed mare and stallion at full health, a vet's kit, and a small fenced area.

  1. Use the vet's kit on the mare, without crouching.
  2. If she is not in heat, wait a few minutes and use it again.
  3. When she is in heat, put the stallion in with her.
  4. Watch them.
  5. When hearts appear, use the vet's kit on her again.
  6. Wait one Minecraft day (20 real minutes) nearby.

It works if: they walk to each other and stand close for a few seconds before hearts appear; the kit then says she is pregnant (or that it did not take); and a foal is born about a day later.

Something is wrong if: it never happens, or it happens while she is not in heat.

A hurt mare is not bred until she heals

You need: a tamed mare in heat, a stallion, a vet's kit.

  1. Hurt the mare a little, for example with a small fall.
  2. Put her in a fenced area with the stallion, away from hay and water.
  3. Watch them for 5 minutes.
  4. Let her heal by standing near water and a hay bale.
  5. Watch again while she is still in heat.

It works if: no hearts appear while she is hurt, and they breed after she heals.

Something is wrong if: the stallion breeds her while she is hurt.

It works if: a yellow message says no, and you do not start climbing on her or see her eat.

Seed jars

You need: two players, empty seed jars, golden carrots, a vet's kit, a chest, and a stallion and mare of your own. Do it once in single player and once on a server if you can: clicking a horse with a jar should never start the climbing-on animation in either.

  1. Use an empty jar on your stallion without feeding him first.
  2. Feed your stallion a golden carrot.
  3. Use an empty jar on him again.
  4. Hover over the filled jar to read it.
  5. Try to fill a jar from the other player's stallion.
  6. Use your filled jar on your mare while she is not in heat.
  7. Use it on your mare when the vet's kit says she is in heat.
  8. Ask the other player to fill a jar from their own stallion and give it to you.
  9. Use their jar on your mare the next time she is in heat.
  10. Put a filled jar in a chest.
  11. Save and quit, open the world again, and hover over that jar.
  12. When a foal is born, open its Family tab.

It works if: the unfed stallion says he must be fed first; the fed one fills the jar and its tooltip names him; the other player's stallion refuses; the out-of-heat mare refuses and you keep the jar; the in-heat mare says pregnant or "didn't take"; their jar works on your mare; the jar still names the stallion after reloading; the foal's family tab shows the stallion; and a jar never makes you start riding a horse.

Something is wrong if: a refusal is missing, a jar is used up on a mare that was not in heat, you start mounting a horse, or the jar forgets its stallion.

Something is wrong if: any of those does not happen.

3. Looking after horses

Healing needs water, and a horse that is not starving

You need: a tamed horse, water, wheat, and a fenced spot on stone or sand with nothing a horse eats (no grass, hay or flowers).

  1. Hurt the horse a little with a small fall.
  2. Stand it next to water on grass.
  3. Watch its health for 20 seconds.
  4. Lead it away from the water.
  5. Watch its health for 20 seconds.
  6. Feed it wheat by hand.
  7. Now put it in the fenced spot beside water, with nothing to eat, and leave it for two Minecraft days (40 real minutes). Stay nearby.
  8. Hurt it a little again and watch its health for a minute.

It works if: beside water it heals quickly, with hearts; away from water it stops healing; wheat still heals it; after two days with nothing to eat it no longer heals at all, even beside water; and it never loses health from being hungry.

Something is wrong if: it heals far from water, never heals, keeps healing after two days without food, or takes damage from hunger.

Hungry horses go and eat, in order

You need: a tamed horse that has been away from food for a Minecraft day (20 real minutes), a fenced area, a cake, a hay bale, a poppy and some grass.

  1. In the fenced area, put the poppy close to the horse, the hay a little further, and the cake furthest away.
  2. Watch the horse for a few minutes.
  3. Watch the grass under the horses in your pasture over an hour of play.

It works if: the horse walks past the poppy to the cake and eats it whole, then eats the hay bale whole; grass it eats turns to dirt, the way sheep leave it.

Something is wrong if: it eats the poppy first, the food is not used up, or grass never turns to dirt.

Bond goes up, but only so much a day

You need: a tamed horse and plenty of wheat or apples.

  1. Open its information screen and write down its bond.
  2. Ride it for a few minutes.
  3. Open the information screen again and write down its bond.
  4. Feed it by hand, again and again.
  5. Watch for hearts each time you feed it.

It works if: bond goes up, you see hearts while it is going up, and the hearts stop after the bond has gone up by 15 in one day.

Something is wrong if: bond never stops going up, or never goes up.

A horse with a strong bond follows you

You need: one horse you look after for several Minecraft days (bond can only rise 15 a day), a lead.

  1. Raise the horse's bond every day until it reaches 31.
  2. Get off within 10 blocks of it and walk around it.
  3. Keep raising the bond until it reaches 61.
  4. Walk around it again.
  5. Keep raising the bond until it reaches 81.
  6. Walk around a fence and see what it does.
  7. Put a lead on it and walk around it again.

It works if: at 31 it turns to face you; at 61 it walks towards you; at 81 it follows you around the fence and stops about 3 blocks away; on a lead it ignores you.

Something is wrong if: it does these at the wrong bond, or follows you while on a lead.

Feeding tells you when it did nothing

You need: a hurt horse, a horse at full health with full bond, and food.
