Extension

Locus Symbol E
Dominance Dominant (E > e)
Alleles E, e
Category Base Pigment
Controls the production of black (eumelanin) vs. red (pheomelanin) pigment across the body. The dominant allele E enables black pigment production, while the recessive e allele restricts pigment to red (chestnut).
