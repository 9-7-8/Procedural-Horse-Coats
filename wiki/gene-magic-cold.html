Magic cold tolerance

Better or worse in a cold biome. A tolerant allele, a sensitive allele and a neutral wild type; how much each copy is worth, and whether it goes to speed, jump or both, is written on the copy. Its twin is magic heat tolerance.

Built, and not yet seen in game

The gene and its breed pools are tested. What it does to a horse in the snow has not been watched yet; see To be verified.

What it does

CombinationIn a cold biome
two tolerant copiesmarkedly faster, or jumps markedly higher, or some of each
one tolerant copysomewhat better
one tolerant, one sensitiveclose to normal — the two pull against each other
one sensitive copysomewhat worse
two sensitive copiesmarkedly slower, or jumps markedly lower, or some of each
anything with no variant copyno difference, anywhere

Cold means anywhere snow can fall where the horse stands: every snowy biome, and the high ground of a temperate one once it is above the snow line. It does not have to be snowing.

Who carries it

Northern and mountain breeds tend to be tolerant: the Icelandic, Fjord, Shetland, Exmoor, Canadian Horse and Przewalski's horse nearly all are, and the Dales, Dartmoor, Haflinger, Trakehner and Mustang often are. The Thoroughbred is the one breed that leans sensitive. The full list is on the breeds page. A Feral Mixed horse carries one copy now and then.

Health

None. A sensitive horse is slower in the cold, never hurt by it.

How it reaches the game

The same shape as heat tolerance, gated on cold_biome instead. That flag is Biome.coldEnoughToSnow at the horse's own position, the game's own "snow falls here" test, which is why a snowy peak in a temperate biome counts. The existing snowing flag is the same test with isRaining added. The API use was type-checked against the running client, not run.

What it stands for

Breeds made in hard winters show it. Icelandic horses, Fjords, Shetlands and Exmoors grow a dense double coat, and the Exmoor adds a hooded brow and a fan of hair at the root of the tail that turns snow and rain. They are compact, which keeps heat in, and they are easy keepers that hold condition on poor winter forage. Przewalski's horse lives through Mongolian winters far below freezing. The Thoroughbred, fine-coated and thin-skinned, is the usual example of a horse that needs a rug.

As with heat, real cold tolerance is coat, body shape and metabolism, not one gene. The mod makes it one magical locus so it can be bred for, with breed shares that follow each breed's history (the breeds page). The magnitude and the speed/jump split are game choices.
