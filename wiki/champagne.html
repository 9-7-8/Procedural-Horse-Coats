Champagne

Gene Key horsegenetics.champagne
Dominance Dominant (Simple)
Alleles Ch (Champagne), c (Wild-type)
Wild Allele Odds 1 in 40
Red Kept 0.55
Black Kept 0.42
Black Tint Boost 0.30
A non-dose-dependent dilution gene that severely cuts eumelanin (black) while leaving most pheomelanin (red) intact.
  • Gold Champagne: Dilutes chestnut coats to a rich gold.
  • Classic Champagne: Dilutes black coats to a muted taupe.
  • Amber Champagne: Dilutes bay coats to a gold body while retaining rich chocolate points via black-to-red tint feedback.
