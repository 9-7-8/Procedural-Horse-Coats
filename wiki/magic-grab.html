Magic grab

A horse that takes hold of something in its teeth and hauls it away, with the allele naming the largest thing it can lift — nothing, something smaller than itself, something its own size, or something larger. It was asked for, and researched before it was deferred; the research is mostly about what the mod already has, because the dosage arithmetic exists and the verb does not. None of it is built.

Magic Grab — a horse that drags what it bites

Alleles n, smaller, same size, larger, naming the largest thing the horse can pick up. Epigenetics carry drag speed and the damage the horse will absorb before dropping its prey short of wherever it was taking it. Simulates a horse grabbing someone by the teeth and hauling them off — uncommon in life, common enough in fiction to deserve a locus.

  • The dosage pattern already exists and should be copied rather than reinvented: AbstractMagicFactorGene sums a signed per-copy epigenetic value so that n/weak is weakest and doubled-strong is strongest, with six outcomes falling out of one comparison. MagicFighterGene is the two-value variant of the same shape.
  • There is no existing verb for carrying a mob. The effects vocabulary has temper, mob_aura, summon and teleport, none of which pick anything up, so this is a new AbilityType — and that contract is five files: the record on GeneAbility, the registration in AbilityType, the translator case, the creator's schema.js mirror (which check-parity.mjs compares against the real Java), and the Effects tab.
  • Mob groups must resolve through entity type tags, never a hardcoded vanilla list — a hardcoded list makes every modded creature invisible to the gene and fails silently.
