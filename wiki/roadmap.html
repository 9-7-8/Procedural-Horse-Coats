Roadmap

Every page of this wiki that has something planned and not built, alphabetically, in the one sentence each of those pages says about itself. The plans themselves are not here — each lives on a Roadmap tab of the page for the thing it is about, which is where it is read, where it grows, and where it is deleted from when the thing ships.

Nothing on this page is written by hand

node wiki/tools/bake-roadmap-index.mjs scans every page in wiki/ for a Roadmap tab and harvests the one-sentence summary box each of them opens with. A subject joins this list by growing that tab, and leaves it by losing it — the same way a gene is on the census by existing. The counts beside each row are the panel's own heading tally, computed at bake time, so a list that shrinks as things ship says so without anybody editing this page.

So a plan is written where the thing is documented. Add a panel to the page, re-run the bake, and the row appears:

wiki/<the thing>.html, before the Verification panel
<section id="roadmap" class="tab-panel" data-tab="roadmap"
         data-tab-label="Roadmap"
         data-tab-hint="Planned, not built">

<div class="note roadmap-summary">
    <span class="label">What is still planned here</span>
    <p>One sentence saying what is planned and unbuilt.</p>
</div>
...
</section>

The bake refuses to write rather than produce a wrong index: a roadmap tab with no summary box, two of either, or a summary box loose on a page all fail it by name. An index that quietly omits a subject is worse than no index, and is exactly how the old version of this page rotted.

Nothing is ever hand-written onto this page. Not a plan, not an exception, not “just this one, it has no page yet”. If a plan has no page to live on, that is a missing page, not a reason to write it here — make the page, give it a Roadmap tab and nothing else, and it becomes the home the thing is documented on when the first piece of it ships. Everything between the markers below is harvested; everything above them is this page's own standing rules.

Four kinds of writing keep trying to settle here, and each has an owner that is not this page
  • Something that shipped → delete it from the Roadmap tab and write it up on that page's Gameplay and Coding tabs. “Marked shipped” is not “moved”, and only moving it keeps one source of truth. A page whose last plan ships loses the tab and drops off this index by itself.
  • A decision, open or settled → Design decisions. Including a decision not to build something: that is an answer, not a backlog item, and the racing and competitions call is the worked example of one that used to sit here.
  • A defect, a gap or an unchecked assumption → Known gaps. A built thing behaving wrongly is not a planned feature.
  • Something to go and look at in-game → To be verified — built, not yet seen, which is a different and much longer list than this one, and is kept by the same machinery on a Verification tab of the same page.
Why this page was dismantled

It was two and a half thousand lines, and it had swallowed all four of the above at least once. The cost was not its length: it was that a reader who wanted to know about one thing learned two thirds of it on that thing's own page and had to come here and search for the rest, and that a plan written far from the code it describes goes stale without anybody noticing. Now a page carries what its subject does, how it is built, what is still planned for it and what needs looking at, in that order, and this page only counts them. Horse gear was the first page moved this way and is still the worked example of all three tabs.

What is planned, page by page

47 pages carry plans that are not built, 116 entries between them.

  • Aggression 1 entry An overhaul is recorded as asked rather than specified: the note says only that these genes may need one, with no statement of what is wrong, so the first piece of work is saying what the complaint is — nothing should be rebuilt off that sentence.
  • Architecture & the build 1 entry A compact binary encoding for the sync payloads comes first — the genotype code and the fatter epigenome code both grow a segment per locus — then grouping and scrolling in the info panel, and a :common:test that fails when a wiki page’s number disagrees with the accessor that computes it.
  • Attached model parts 4 entries Blockbench meshes parented to the horse model — unicorn horns, dorsal spines and crystalline growths first, wings and hoof feathering later — the mod’s first non-texture visual; the wings now have a settled route (a plain body-parented layer over a copy of the elytra mesh, so any elytra retexture pack is the art), and that route is the argument against taking a GeckoLib dependency for the rest.
  • Base-aware phenotype naming 1 entry One base-aware phenotype resolver, so the info panel, the paper dump and the gene dictionary can say “amber champagne” or “this horse is seal” — and so champagne can read seal off agouti’s epigenetics and paint sable rather than amber.
  • Block preference 1 entry A recessive locus pulling a horse toward Water, Lava, Underwater or Tree, as hard as the allele’s author chooses and up to taking damage when it cannot get there — an AI goal with a priority in the behaviour hierarchy, not an effects state applied per tick.
  • The breed book 2 entries The catalogue’s backlog is a shopping list, not a commitment: a continental draft cluster from the Ardennais to the Schleswig Coldblood, the sport-horse books gated on the open-studbook call, and the reindeer mod’s breeds, whose wings and horns mean learning to 3D model.
  • Breeding carrots & the gene database 1 entry Every built-in gene falls back to the minimal recipe and a uniform splice draw, so the work left is content, not machinery: a hand-tuned carrot block per gene, the Epigenetic Stabilizer carrot, carrot effects on a seed jar, and a database check inside the recipe.
  • Breeds 6 entries The unbuilt work here is spawning and the render layer: an opt-in weather field that would be the breed parser’s first cross-field validation, horses that may never spawn in chunks already explored, flowing manes and lower-leg feathering, and a herd flood-fill that can still split a pack streaming in over several ticks.
  • The cart and jump bench 3 entries A bench that builds a cart or a jump piece by piece — a wood and a paint per wheel, bed and shaft — which forces cart materials off the registry id onto stack components, and retires the baked per-wood recipes that compat/GeneratedCarts mirrors at run time.
  • Carts: wagons, plows and what a horse is worth in harness 3 entries Two or four horses to one wagon on a whippletree — a change to the shape of the cart code rather than an addition beside it, since applyDraught takes a single puller — and a vardo you sleep in, whose respawn point drives away.
  • The chase gene 1 entry Six alleles — timed, distance, sight, never, infinite and wild-type — generalising WildHorseForgetTargetGoal into a Java gene whose chase duration is inherited and drifts, with vanilla’s panic goal to out-argue for Flag.MOVE first.
  • Coding notes 5 entries The thin science tabs are blocked on owner-supplied sources rather than on anyone getting to them — silver’s absent MCOA section, MITF’s SW1, a fifth of the natural tabs naming no breed at all — and the wiki has no checklist idiom and no item art.
  • Compatibility 4 entries Two small patches — a load-time warning when Realistic Horse Genetics is installed, and check-recipes.mjs pointed at BHAR’s recipe ids — plus a survey backlog that is reading work rather than build work: mods nobody has opened yet.
  • Config 1 entry One sweep over a config surface that grew option by option, into a convention for server versus client and generated blocks wherever the roster is data — plus a refusal: per-gene config must mean expression or founder frequency, never registration, since a genotype code is positional.
  • Curly coat 1 entry A coat-structure axis the pipeline does not have yet, so that KRT25 and SP6 can curl and thin hair rather than recolour it — without one the American Bashkir Curly ships with the right colour pool and no curl at all.
  • DMRT3 - gait 1 entry What is left is animation on the vanilla horse model: a four-beat amble and a lateral pace chosen by copy count, with the two-copy horse losing the diagonal gaits it trades away — the promise the gene’s own expression text already makes to the player.
  • Flying 1 entry The third flying ability, cloud-treading — an allele that glides and can come to rest on a real cloud — killswitched so the elytra glide could ship first; the engine research is already done, so what is left is design rather than discovery.
  • Forced riding - the kelpie 1 entry A wild horse that lets you mount and will not let you off, carrying you to deep water — a server-enforced ride-lock rather than an intercepted dismount key, waiting on the warning beat that separates a recognisable monster from a mount with no tell.
  • Generated stables 1 entry More stables are a content backlog rather than a build one — an NBT and a JSON each — with three questions open first: nothing decides which stable generates where, a stable has no owner, story or loot, and nothing stops a player taking the horses.
  • The genetics model 2 entries Nothing is Y_LINKED yet, and no punnett square anywhere draws a sex-linked gene — brindle’s is drawn wrong rather than refused, pairing a stallion’s reserved Y as an ordinary allele — while the naturalgeneref.md roster audit stays a standing task.
  • The horse browser 10 entries A server-side owned-horse registry is still owed — My horses ships on HorseRoster walking the whole ancestry table instead — and beside it the vial, herd, bonding and crafting-tree tabs, a Where column that teleports rather than merely printing, a roster that refreshes on birth and death, and the rest of the tab-strip work: nothing scrolls the selected tab into view and the tabs take no keyboard focus.
  • Horse care: gated healing, bond & herds 6 entries A grooming tool that retires shears on a horse and at its top tier grooms the whole herd, ground work on a lunge line for foals and horses you never saddle, bond tiers that stop the bucking, and founding bands whose foals are nobody’s foals.
  • Horse gear: the nineteen slots 12 entries Nineteen slots ship with almost nothing to put in them, so the whole accessories system is the plan: derived models rather than drawn ones, a tack bench, dye and pattern, enchanted tack, hoof equipment, native tack, horse chests and the saddle pad — with the armour materials the one piece that is only data; and beside all of that the tab needs the player's own inventory drawn next to the doll, which means putting a real container menu under a screen that has never had one.
  • Horse shoes 1 entry Horse shoes are planned as four independent metal item stacks: each shoe contributes a flat speed and jump bonus from its metal tier, every shoe grants powered-snow walking, and enchantments add special abilities without becoming shared horse state.
  • Horsemanship 4 entries A rider-skill curve that moves speed and jump only — never health — as an AttributeModifier applied on mount and removed on dismount, with an uncapped, sharply diminishing shape, and an optional Pufferfish’s Skills tree shipped as pure data.
  • Items & recipes 2 entries The roster wants an audit of item/ModItems against the recipe folder — an item with no recipe and no page is invisible to anyone not reading the source, and a 1.0 blocker — plus real textures for the tickets, whistles and stall signs that currently share one.
  • Jumps 3 entries Standards of any solid block are scoped and deferred — ModelBaker.materials() makes the model files avoidable — while the rest of the vocabulary (logs, brush, ditches, fillers, water) and anything that notices a round are unbuilt.
  • The lasso 1 entry A thrown or held rope that stops a wild horse bolting so a band can be stalked rather than chased — the first item in the mod to target an entity, with the source mod’s two-day taming timer cut as a chore with a progress bar.
  • LUT 1 entry More palettes beyond Blupnk and Grnpnk — a new palette is a new allele here, never a new gene, each one a lutResources() entry, an expression row and a founder weight, so what the plan actually waits on is a batch of hand-authored gradient PNGs.
  • Magic grab 1 entry Four alleles naming the largest mob a horse can pick up and drag, with drag speed and damage tolerance in the epigenetics — needing a new AbilityType, whose contract is five files, and mob groups resolved through entity type tags rather than a vanilla list.
  • Magical genes still to build 4 entries A batch of magical marking and “stamp” genes before 1.0, more flavour loci for a breed designer to name in JSON, and a tack-aware locus that needs a new when condition flag able to see an equipment slot before it can exist at all.
  • Making a gene 4 entries Retire the creator’s hand-written JS port behind a @JSExport that bakes an unsaved spec, so its preview can show a glow; then an unspecified usability pass, and four mask additions the intakes asked for — RAMP angle, a per-cell FAN, a wrapping SVG plane, a FRACTAL grain.
  • Momentum sprint 1 entry Speed that builds while a rider holds a line and drains on braking, hard turns, collisions, leaving the ground or stopping — a loop the rider can play badly rather than a permanent Speed modifier, and wanting the same per-tick attachment storm fed wants.
  • Rescuing braid 1 entry Dyeing a braid at the Tack Dyeing Bench rather than only in a crafting grid, and a crafting recipe that takes its colour from the wool that went into it.
  • Rider comfort 1 entry Four small borrowings from other mods are left, each shippable alone and each wanting a config switch — no mining-speed debuff while mounted, an inventory keybind, a right-click that equips tack, and returning the leads vanilla drops, which has no event to hang on — plus fading the horse out as you look down, which asks the coat pipeline for a transparency it has never had.
  • Saddle pads 4 entries A fitted saddle pad with a banner-to-saddlepad editor that controls coverage, zoom, centring and tiling, then saves reusable designs to apply across a player's horses.
  • Seed jars 3 entries A collectedFrom marker is what the scientist’s two missing trade rows wait on — selling a rolled genotype, buying a player-made one back — and behind it sit embryo extraction, screening, splicing and cloning, with cross-species a project rather than a tier.
  • The spawn eggs 1 entry A per-copy epigenetics editor on the custom spawn egg: every allele copy’s priority and seed shown individually with a type-it-in field, so that which copy is expressing can be asked without reading a log — eighty fields that want a per-gene expander rather than a flat list.
  • Stall bars 1 entry A connecting, waterloggable bars block for stall fronts, which has to earn the utility rule: bars are see-through so a stalled horse still counts its herd, and reachable so you can groom, feed and lead through them.
  • Stall signs 1 entry Auto-return — a horse that takes itself home rather than one sent by a ticket — is the largest thing missing, alongside holding pens distinct from stalls, a real stall-sign block instead of a repurposed oak_wall_sign, and a persistent wireframe in place of the F7 flash.
  • Storm fed 1 entry A recessive locus filling an internal reservoir from storms, rain, snow, lightning, water or lava and spending none of it — deliberately half a gene, waiting on the mod’s first per-horse attachment and on a second gene written to consume what it stores.
  • The trait & effect architecture 3 entries Two or more genes that only together trigger an outcome, as a separately registered TraitRule modelled as a pseudo-gene so it reuses the coat pipeline’s phase and priority; behind it the unbuilt rest — selectors, auras, resource pools, goals, and conditions as 0–1 scalars rather than booleans.
  • Troughs and salt licks 1 entry A trough a horse actually eats from — the first block in the mod that feeds one — buildable in any rectangle out of any mod's wood, plus salt licks that must grant something rather than punish their absence.
  • Undead horses 3 entries A recessive skeleton gene that heals without eating and is pacified forever by one bone, a zombie gene with double hearts at a heavy speed cost, and the spawning overhaul that suppresses vanilla’s two undead entities so that any breed can carry either.
  • Villagers & transfer papers 2 entries The cowboy spawns in plains villages and nowhere else — injecting horsegenetics:cowboy_barn into the other four terminator pools is a handful of lines, but a barn per village material palette is the real project, and a plains barn in a desert is why neither is done; and the only way to get a horse off him without emeralds is still to kill him.
  • Virtual stables 2 entries An object that holds a horse you own — not a genome sample, a spawn-egg recipe or a ticket that moves a live animal, but the horse itself — with the open call being whether that is an item or a stall in the horse dimension.
  • Whistles 1 entry Six pieces of the callable-horse design are unbuilt: a whistle that walks a near horse in rather than teleporting it, a bond tier before binding is allowed, an optional keybind, a texture that tells a bound whistle from an unbound one, and binding for donkeys, mules and llamas.
