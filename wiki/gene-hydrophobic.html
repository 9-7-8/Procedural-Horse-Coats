Hydrophobic

It will not be ridden into water. Take one in past its knees and it throws you and makes for the nearest shore — which is what every horse in vanilla Minecraft does, and which this mod otherwise quietly fixed for you.

Half seen: it throws you, and it heads for land

Both halves were confirmed in the yard on 2026-09-13 — it does more than this page originally claimed. What has not been watched is everything it must not do: a carrier must still carry you across a river, a ford must not dismount anybody, and the deep-water ejection must not become a drowning. Those are on the Verification tab.

Why this is the odd one out

In this mod a tamed horse gets a swimming assist and will carry you across a river. That is a deliberate improvement on vanilla. This gene takes it away again for the horses that have it — so it is a defect to breed out rather than a trait to breed for.

Health

None to the horse. Being thrown into deep water is a hazard to you.

It finishes the other flag that currently does nothing

water_averse is declared in the gene format and falls into warnUntranslated at GeneAbilityHandler.java:438, described there as “an AI-goal weight” and never wired. Like fireproof's lava flag, this gene turns a dead branch into a feature.

Recessive, not dominant — and that was the whole decision

The original specification made it dominant. Dominant would mean most horses revert to vanilla water behaviour, silently changing how every horse already bred behaves, and quietly retiring HorseWaterRidingHandler. Recessive keeps the swimming assist universal and makes this a rare defect. Nothing existing changes behaviour.

Do not dismount the rider into a drowning loop

Bucking someone off in deep water and then swimming away from them is a death. The dismount needs to leave the player somewhere they can act — low cost to get right, very high annoyance to get wrong.

Both halves are built, and the second was a surprise

The water_averse flag ejects the rider in water deep enough to swim in, and the horse then makes for an edge — owner-confirmed on 2026-09-13, which is also how the fleeing half was discovered, since this box used to say it was not implemented. What still matters is where the rider is left: bucked off in the middle of a lake they are swimming, and that is the open question on the Verification tab.

A gene that removes a feature

Unusually, the wild type here is the improved behaviour and the allele restores the vanilla one. That is worth stating plainly on the page, because a reader who knows Minecraft will assume the opposite and conclude the gene does nothing.

Verified

Confirmed in the yard (2026-09-13), and it does more than this page claimed: it throws the rider and heads for an edge.

Works, and the horse heads for an edge afterwards (owner, 2026-09-13: “hydrophobic works AND it seems like the horse moves for an edge after too?”). The checklist had said the head-for-shore half was not implemented, from reading the translator, which only ejects. Heading out of deep water is vanilla’s own horse behaviour, and the gene inherits it for nothing — reading the code tells you what the code does, not what the player sees.

A defect in the magical family

The natural side of the mod has disorders; the magical side has almost nothing a breeder actively wants to remove. This is one, and it is a useful shape to have: a locus whose entire breeding project is getting rid of it.

Still open (NOT played)

The gene is the water_averse traversal flag, and the translator ejects passengers only while the horse is in water deeper than its fluid-jump threshold. The point of the locus is that it is a rare defect: the interesting failures are all on the horses that do not have it, which is the half nothing announces. What has been seen is on the Coding tab — do not re-open it.

Nothing here needs looking at to judge — do these first

  • A carrier keeps the swimming assist. Ride a Hyd/n horse from the custom horse spawn egg across a river. Pass: it carries you the whole way and never ejects you. This is the claim the recessive decision rests on — nothing existing changes behaviour — and a flag that fired on carriers would quietly retire the mod’s swimming assist for a fifth of all horses.
  • A ford does not throw you. Ride an expressing horse through water about a block deep. Pass: you stay aboard. The threshold is deliberately set at swimming depth rather than “wet”, so an over-eager check would make the gene unridable on any shoreline.
  • It does not re-eject in a loop. Take an expressing horse into the middle of a lake. Pass: you are thrown once and can then swim out under your own control — not held under, and not remounted and thrown again on the next tick.
  • It is findable at about its stated rate. The founder table puts the expressing pair in the wild. Pass: over a lot of wild horses you occasionally meet one that will not be ridden into water, rather than never. A locus whose whole breeding project is removing it has to be encounterable or the project does not exist.
