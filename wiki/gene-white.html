What it does

common/genetics/genes/WhiteGene.java
@Override
public PigmentField restrict(AllelePair pair, CoatBuildContext ctx, PigmentView coat) {
    if (!isWhite(pair)) {
        return null;
    }
    PigmentField f = coat.mutableCopy();
    CoatRegions.restrictAll(ctx.skin(), f, (field, px, py, p) -> {
        field.setRed(px, py, 0f);
        field.setBlack(px, py, 0f);
    });
    return f;
}

Both pigments land exactly on zero, which is what the composer’s TRANSPARENT_EPS = 0.001 cutoff is looking for. Only dominant white and a splash marking ever reach it — a dilution floor never does, even grey stacked on a double-dilute cream.

Complete dominance, and what it buys

COMPLETE_DOMINANT means dominant and epistatic: while W shows, no other gene is visible, so every white horse looks alike whatever else it carries. Two consequences:

  • The catalogue keeps exactly one white entry — the variant homozygote on an otherwise wild-type background. Without that reduction, white would claim a quarter of the gallery corridor with indistinguishable horses.
  • A white horse’s genotype is still fully there. It is carrying whatever it is carrying, and its foals will show it. The masking is visual only — which is exactly the kind of thing a carrier display should surface.

A magical gene can still paint a white horse

White is natural; magical genes run after every natural gene

So magic zebra’s stripes and pink hair’s mane do show on a dominant-white horse — both raise addOpacity as well as adding colour. A magical gene that adds colour without opacity paints nothing there, because the texel has no paint to tint.

Test reaches it a different way: it uses ColorField.set, flat opaque paint that replaces the accumulator entirely.

If a chestnut renders as a white horse, it is not this gene

The texture-id collision, fixed 2026-09-01

An older sanitize() lower-cased the texture key on its way to an Identifier, folding all 19,683 genotypes onto 27 ids — E/e ≡ e/e, W/w ≡ w/w, and so on. Case is the dominance encoding.

TextureManager#register is a silent Map.put that closes the loser, so every deterministic coat in a bucket rendered whichever one baked last — a plain white horse whenever that was a W_ one. The victim’s own bake was correct, so a “flat white” debug line never caught it. CoatTextureId is now injective and KEY_BY_ID throws if two keys ever share an id again.

Owner-verified in-game, 2026-09-01

Wild spawns show a wide variety of genotypes and every one renders its correct coat — no more flat-white horses.

Not modelled

Real KIT dominant white is a series of 36 alleles of graded extent, most of them not fully masking. Expanding it is the single largest combinatorial change on the backlog and is deliberately deferred — or would be capped at a handful of representative alleles. KIT also carries tobiano and sabino 1 in reality.
