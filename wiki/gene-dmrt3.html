DMRT3 — gait

The “gait keeper”: the gene that lets a horse amble and pace. The locus, its inheritance and every breed’s share are in; the gait itself is not animated yet, so for now it is a fact about a horse’s breeding rather than something you can ride.

It changes nothing about movement yet

A gaited horse walks, trots and canters like any other until gait animation is built. What it does now is show on the information screen and pass on properly, so a gaited line bred today is gaited when the gaits arrive.

CombinationWhat it means

Who carries it

Every Paso Fino (both breeds) and Standardbred is gaited, the Tennessee Walking Horse nearly always, and most Icelandic horses. A few Morgans carry it, a very few Quarter Horses and Arabians, and Thoroughbreds and Przewalski’s horses never do. A Feral Mixed horse carries a copy now and then.

A stop codon in the spinal cord

DMRT3 is expressed in a set of spinal-cord neurons that coordinate the left and right limbs. The gait variant is a nonsense mutation, Ser301STOP, found by Andersson et al. (2012) in Icelandic horses. It is permissive rather than simply dominant or recessive: it makes lateral gaits possible, and other genes and training decide which one a horse uses.

  • Pace needs two copies. Almost every five-gaited Icelandic is A/A, but only about 70% of A/A Icelandics actually pace.
  • One copy improves the tölt and still scores well at walk, trot, canter and gallop.
  • Two copies give the best tölt and pace and a poorer trot and canter.
  • In Standardbreds, trotters and pacers are both A/A, so other loci decide trot versus pace, and A/A horses race better than C/A ones.

The mutation arose within the last ten thousand years. The earliest ancient DNA carrying it is from England around 850–900 AD, and it reached Iceland soon after. Breeds selected for smooth riding gaits have fixed it; breeds selected for the gallop have not.

What the mod does not model yet: which lateral gait a gaited horse prefers, which is the modifier loci plus training, and the gait itself. Both wait for movement and animation work.

Gait — the gene ships, the animation does not

DMRT3 is registered, inherited and carried by the breeds that really have it, and it changes nothing about how a horse moves. The locus contributes no trait and every outcome is a wild type, because there is no ambling or pacing animation to switch on. The gene's own expression text says so to the player in three places — “the gait is not animated yet” — so this is a promise already made in game, not a loose idea.

What it needs is animation work on the vanilla horse model: a four-beat amble and a lateral pace, chosen by copy count, with the two-copy horse losing the diagonal gaits it trades away. That is the whole entry. It is deliberately not bundled with the per-part scaling call, which was cut: that one wanted the mod to own the horse model, and this one only wants new motion on the model it already borrows.
