Magical gene / no coat effect

Skittishness

What a horse runs from, and when. The mirror of aggression, allele for allele — and being ridden makes no difference to it at all.

Built, and not yet seen in game

The locus was split out of the two temper genes on 2026-09-17. The record below was earned by the allele it replaces, on the same flee machinery. What to look at is on the Verification tab.

The grid

The same three times crossed with the same five targets as aggression, on F-prefixed tokens so the two loci read alike: Fd by day, Fn after dark, Fa always.

by dayFdcFdhFdpFdeFda
after darkFncFnhFnpFneFna
alwaysFacFahFapFaeFaa

Two copies of the same allele, exactly as on the aggression locus. Two different shy alleles show neither.

It does not stop when you get on

Aggression pauses nothing for a rider and neither does this, but the consequence is different and worse to own: a mount that bolts when a creeper comes round the corner is a horse you cannot take anywhere. That is the version of this gene a rider actually meets, and it is deliberately not gated away.

The one that is genuinely hard to keep

Fnp — flees riders after dark — is the difficult one. By day it is a perfectly ordinary horse that can be caught, tamed and ridden. After dark it will not let you near it. Nothing is wrong with the animal; you simply own a horse that is only yours for half of each day.

If it runs from you, you may not be able to keep it either

A wild horse that flees every player it sees cannot be approached, so it cannot be fed, so it cannot be tamed. That is the same dead end aggression reaches, walked from the other side: the hunting horse will not let you near it because it is coming for you, and this one will not let you near it because it is going the other way, and neither can be caught. The way in is the same. Passification is a separate locus that says what you can offer the animal, and since 2026-09-25 its veto covers fleeing as well as fighting — pay a shy horse what it asks and you stop being a reason to run, while everyone and everything else still is. A horse with an Fap or Faa pair and no passification allele is, in practice, a horse nobody will ever touch.

That combination has only just become possible to meet. The Nightmare, added on 2026-09-25, is the first breed ever to pin this locus — Faa/Faa, flees everything, always — and it carries Prm/Prm beside it, so it is also the first horse in the mod that is frightened of you and buyable at the same time. Until then every shy horse was a wild roll, and nobody had bred one that had a way in; which is exactly why the flee half of the veto was missing for eight days and nothing noticed.

Getting close enough to hand it the offering in the first place is the tempt goal's job: an item that is a live passification route counts as a favourite whatever the horse eats, which means the wider notice radius and a gallop rather than a drift. The arithmetic is meant to beat the flee — the lure is noticed further off than a shy horse's flee radius and re-issues its path every tick, where a flee only re-picks on its beat — and it has never been watched doing so.

Health

Running is the safest thing on either temperament locus, and a shy horse generally outlives a brave one. The cost is where it ends up: a fleeing horse paths twelve blocks away from whatever frightened it and does not consider what is in that direction.

Fleeing was once implemented somewhere else entirely

Until 2026-09-17 the temper verb returned early on any mood that was not aggressive, and fleeing was implemented in NightBehaviourHandler against the night verb instead. When this locus moved onto the shared verb the flee path had to move with it, into GeneAbilityHandler.flee. Had it not, this gene would have loaded, registered, baked, appeared in the census and done nothing whatsoever — which is the failure mode this codebase keeps paying for. If you add a mood, follow it all the way into the translator.

“Herds” must not include the horse's own band

passive excludes every equine in MobGroups. It is the whole of gap 241 below, and the exclusion moved from the night handler's private matcher into the shared group resolver when this locus was built — where every caller gets it rather than one.

There is no vanilla avoid-goal to borrow

Fleeing is a navigation call rather than a goal: the horse paths to a point FLEE_DISTANCE directly away from the nearest match and drops any target it had. Log whether the path was accepted. From outside, a flee that never fired and a flee that fired into a fence four blocks away look exactly alike, and Navigation.moveTo's return is the only thing that tells them apart.

The candidate predicate is not only MobGroups.matches. Since 2026-09-25 it also asks !Passification.suppresses(horse, c), so a player who has paid this horse is filtered out before the nearest match is picked and cannot be the thing it runs from. Passification counts its chokepoints, and flee is the fifth — it was missing while that page promised there were four, which is worth knowing before adding a sixth.

Dropping the reason is not enough here, because the leg is already issued

Every other chokepoint guards a target, which is re-read each tick, so forgetting it is the whole fix. This one issues a moveTo FLEE_DISTANCE blocks away and only re-picks on the temper beat, so a horse calmed mid-flight would run the leg out and turn round at the far end — which looks, from across a paddock, almost exactly like it working. Passification.forget therefore calls GeneAbilityHandler.stopFleeingFrom, which stops the navigation only if the FLEEING entry it removes was keyed to that player: a horse running from two people is still running from the other one and re-picks it on the next beat.

flee's own no-candidate branch stops the navigation on the same condition rather than unconditionally, and the condition is the point. Stopping every beat that finds nothing to run from would cut the legs off whichever goal has the navigation instead — FoodTemptGoal walking a calmed horse back over, most of all, which is the exact behaviour this change exists to allow.

Verified

A night-shy horse leaves its own herd alone (2026-09-15, formerly gap 241). The bug: a Flc/Flc foal born into a wild band logged 1,578 night flee | ... from minecraft:horse lines in eight hours, because NightBehaviourHandler.matches never left equines out of “passive” despite a comment saying it must. The fix excludes equines and has the flee trace compare UUIDs with equals. Evidence: the yard's NIGHT SHY pen (row X) put a Flc/Flc mare in with a stallion, two mares and two cows for the 06:49 run's whole first night: 96 night flee | ... "NIGHT SHY" from minecraft:cow - path accepted lines and none from anything else. The allele is Fnc now, and the exclusion it tests lives in MobGroups.

Fear is not negative aggression

It would have been cheaper to keep one locus with a mood axis, and it would have been wrong. Temperament is not a line with hunting at one end and bolting at the other: a great many real animals are aggressive toward one thing and frightened of another, and the same animal is often both at once. Two loci say that. One could not.

Shy is commoner than dangerous

A frightened horse costs its owner a fence and an afternoon; an aggressive one costs them the horse. So the expressing combinations here are several times commoner in the wild than aggression's, and a wild herd that scatters when you approach is meant to be an ordinary thing to meet rather than a find.

What needs verifying here

Gap 241 re-asked, after the equine exclusion moved into MobGroups and fleeing moved onto the shared temper verb: a Fnc/Fnc horse must flee minecraft:cow and never its own band — and a silent no-op is the failure to watch for; and since 2026-09-25 a horse that has been paid must stop running from that player and nobody else, dropping the leg it is already on rather than walking it out.

A shy horse can now be bought off (2026-09-25, NOT played)

The Nightmare is the first breed to pin this locus, and the first horse that is frightened of you and calmable at once — which is what turned up the missing veto. The checks that belong to the veto itself are on passification's tab and are not repeated here; these are the ones about the shy half.

  • The filter narrows the flee and does not empty it. Pass: a calmed Faa/Faa horse stands its ground for you and still bolts from a cow, a zombie and a second, unpaid player. Fail: it stops fleeing everything, which would mean Passification.suppresses is answering true for something that is not the player who paid — and an Faa/Faa horse that has stopped running is indistinguishable from one whose gene never loaded.
  • The trace still fires on a change of quarry only. A horse calmed and then set off again by something else should write one new flee | ... line, not a line a beat. Fail: a wall of lines, meaning the FLEEING entry is being removed and re-put every beat rather than held.
  • An untamed shy horse can be reached at all. Hold its offering and stand still. Pass: it comes at a gallop from further off than it flees. Fail: it oscillates, or drifts slowly away — the tempt path and the flee path fighting, which is the one outcome the radii are supposed to prevent and nothing has measured.
