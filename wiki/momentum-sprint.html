Momentum sprint

Speed you have to earn by riding well, and can lose in one corner. A horse that gathers pace for as long as the rider holds a straight line, and sheds it on braking, turning hard, hitting something, leaving the ground or stopping. It was asked for, and researched before it was deferred — and the research is almost entirely the argument for why this is not simply a Speed modifier. None of it is built.

Momentum sprint

For a Chollima-, Arion- or Sleipnir-style ground mount: speed that builds while you hold a line and drains when you brake, turn hard, collide, leave the ground or stop, with a short cooldown after an impact.

Why it is not just a Speed modifier, which is the whole of the research: a permanent modifier makes a horse that is simply faster, and this makes a loop the rider can feel and play badly — run straight, watch the top speed climb, lose it on a corner, build it again. It wants the same per-tick mutable state storm fed wants, and for the same reason, so whichever is built first should put that attachment somewhere the other can use.
