Gameplay / non-genetic

Storage harness

The strap over a horse's rump and the metal frame on each flank that a chest hangs in. A horse wears one before it can carry a chest at all, and it takes a little of the load off. It comes with copper, iron, golden or netherite fittings — the better the metal, the more it helps — and its leather takes dye. Built on 2026-10-08 and not yet played. It ships without enchantments (owner, 2026-10-09); which ones it gets is to be decided later.

What it is

A piece of gear with a slot of its own, Harness, beside the two Pack slots on the Gear tab. Hold one and right-click your horse and it goes on; or put it in its slot on the Gear tab or in the Dress window. An adult horse of your own, like every other piece.

The harness comes first, and leaves last

  • No harness, no chest. The Pack slots refuse everything on a horse that is not wearing one, and a right-click holding a chest says so rather than putting you in the saddle.
  • It does not come off while a chest hangs from it. Take both chests off, then the harness.
  • A server can drop the rule with packs.harness_required; a harness is then only its weight bonus.

The four metals

What a horse carries slows it down, by how many items there are against how strong it is. A harness makes the load count for a little less.

HarnessFittingsThe load weighs, by default
Copper2 copper ingots2.5% less
Iron2 iron ingots5% less
Golden2 gold ingots7.5% less
Netherite1 netherite ingot10% less

Every one is crafted the same way otherwise: two leather and a horse hair with the metal, in any arrangement. There is no upgrading one into the next.

Small on purpose

How much a horse can carry is its pulling ability, and pulling ability is something you breed for. A tenth off, at best, is a harness that helps a good horse and cannot stand in for one. A server that would rather gear mattered more than bloodlines raises one number, packs.harness_best_reduction on the config page, and all four tiers move with it: the netherite harness takes that much off, and the other three are even steps below. The item's tooltip shows the figure your server uses.

Dyeing it

The leather dyes like leather armour — in the crafting grid with any dye, or several mixed. The fittings keep their metal's colour. On the horse the strap is the colour you dyed it.

What it looks like on the horse

A strap over the rump, a second running back along the spine to the tail, and on each flank a frame with a shelf under it. A chest sits in its frame; with no chest the frame is empty.

One number, divided evenly

A tier's reduction is share() * best, where share() is its place in HarnessTier's order over the number of tiers, and best is packs.harness_best_reduction. So the server has one dial and adding a tier re-divides it; nothing saved refers to a tier by number. The load the curve is asked about is PackLoad.lightened(weighed, reduction) — fractional on purpose, because a tenth off sixty-four items is not a whole number of items and rounding it would make a harness worth nothing on a small load. It is applied before the curve, so it moves a horse's maximum by the same share. MAX_HARNESS_BEST stops a config from making a load weigh nothing.

An item per tier, not a component

The house rule from bake-recipe-reference.mjs: a recipe cannot tell “with a component” from “without”, so anything a recipe must distinguish is its own item. The ids use vanilla's own spelling for the metals (golden_storage_harness) and are permanent. The tooltip is written from ServerConfig.packHarnessBestReduction() rather than from a lang string with a figure in it.

Both rules live on the slot

HorseTackSlot.accepts refuses a storage slot on a horse that is not harnessed, which every path asks: the Dress window's mayPlace, the picker's packet, and the right-click. mayTakeOff refuses the harness while either pack slot is full. TackEquipHandler adds only the sentence — a refusal that is claimed and said, the same way a foal's is, because a rule this mod invented that fails silently reads as a bug. isStorage() is the two pack slots by name, not the whole Storage zone the harness also sits in.

The art

Drawn in tools/bake-drawn-items.mjs as a two-layer item: storage_harness_leather, near white so that minecraft:dye tints it true and shared by all four, under a storage_harness_fittings_<metal> sprite each. All four are in minecraft:dyeable.

The layer

HarnessLayer is the first piece of gear here drawn as geometry rather than as a mask over the horse's own mesh, because a frame stands off the flank and a mask cannot. Two model parts built once from boxes in the body bone's units, baked straight from a LayerDefinition, submitted on the body bone through one flat white texture with the colour as the tint. The frames are placed from PackBox, the same numbers the chest is drawn and clicked at, so a chest sits in its frame by construction. Photographed on 2026-10-08, bare and with chests, undyed and dyed.

What needs verifying here

The storage harness was built on 2026-10-08 with its rules covered by a gametest and its look by a photo shoot, and no player has crafted, dyed, worn or ridden with one.

The storage harness (2026-10-08, NOT played)

  • Craft all four from the recipes on the Recipes tab; each should be its own item with its own fittings.
  • Try to hang a chest on a bare horse in the Dress window: refused. (The right-click and its line were seen in the yard on 2026-10-09.)
  • Harness on, then two chests, then try to take the harness off: it stays, and the picker's Take off line says the chests hang from it.
  • The weight. (A netherite harness taking its tenth off on the live speed attribute was seen in the yard.) Fill both chests on a horse in a copper harness, read the Gear tab's Carrying line, swap to netherite (chests off, harness off, new harness, chests on) and read it again: the speed kept should rise a little. Then set packs.harness_best_reduction to 0.5 and relog: the tooltip and the line should both follow.
  • Dye one in the crafting grid and put it on: the strap on the horse is that colour, the fittings are not.
  • Then look at it: the strap and frames on a galloping and a rearing horse, on a very small and a very large one, under a saddle and a barding (does the rump strap fight the barding's own geometry?), and whether an empty frame reads as a frame.
What is still planned here

The harness was asked for with enchantments and has none, and other mods' metals do not make harnesses yet.

Enchantments

The owner asked for a harness that “has tiers and enchantments”. The tiers are built; the enchantments are not, because the gear plan says the rule for what is an enchantment and what is a gene should be written down before the first enchantable piece is made — nothing in this mod is enchantable yet, and the first one pays for the whole path. What needs deciding: which enchantments a harness takes (a further weight reduction, and if so whether it stacks past the best tier's share; anything else), whether they come from the enchanting table or only from books, and what each costs. Tracked on #220.

Other mods' metals

Four vanilla metals are written by hand. The generated-materials layer already makes a barding per modded ingot; a harness per modded metal would be the same scan, and needs a rule for where a metal nobody ranked sits among the tiers.
