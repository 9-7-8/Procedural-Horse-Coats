RYR1 (malignant hyperthermia)

The only disorder in this mod that costs an affected horse nothing. One copy is enough to have it, and a horse that has it is a completely ordinary horse until something triggers an episode — and nothing in this game can.

Crossing two of them

A disorder you can see, and mostly ignore

Dominant, so there is no silent carrier: every horse with a copy shows it on its info panel and on its papers. Unlike HYPP and PSSM1 — the other two dominants — one copy takes no hearts, no speed and no jump. The horse is sound.

That is not the mod being generous. Malignant hyperthermia is episodic: the fault only shows under general anaesthesia or extreme stress, and between episodes the animal is genuinely normal. There is no anaesthetic here and no stress model to hang a trigger on, so giving it a permanent heart penalty would invent a symptom the real condition does not have.

Two copies is different. A horse with no normal channel left is genuinely compromised, and that combination does cost hearts, speed and jump. As with every dominant here, no wild horse is ever born with two — the worst outcome is always something a player bred.

Why a wild horse can have this and a wild horse can never have PLOD1

A recessive disorder can be absent from its founder table entirely, because a wild-caught horse is an adult that survived. A dominant has nowhere to hide: if founders could never be affected, the allele could never enter the world and the locus would be dead code. So founders really can be born with one copy. See HYPP, which was the first locus in this mod to break that rule.

The first informational disorder that is not a side effect

Two other conditions in the mod cost nothing — splash deafness and silver’s ocular cysts — but both ride along with a coat gene the player wanted for other reasons. This is the first locus whose entire purpose is to be known rather than felt. Its value is the line on the papers, which is exactly what the American Azteca registry screens breeding stock for.

What breaks

RYR1 encodes the channel that releases calcium from the sarcoplasmic reticulum into the muscle fibre — the step that actually starts a contraction. The variant channel leaks: it opens too readily and closes too reluctantly.

Under ordinary conditions that is compensated for and invisible. Under a trigger it is not. Calcium floods the fibre and will not be pumped back, the muscle contracts continuously, and the metabolic cost of that runaway contraction produces the picture the condition is named for: a very high body temperature, rigid muscles, acidosis, a racing heart and muscle breakdown. Untreated it is frequently fatal, and it develops over minutes rather than hours.

Why “affected” and “unwell” are different words here

This is the part the mod had to take a position on. An MH horse is not a sick horse that occasionally gets sicker. It is a normal horse with a conditional fault — correct temperature, correct muscle function, correct performance, right up until a trigger it may never encounter. Many affected horses live an entire working life without an episode.

So the honest model is a condition that is reported and not priced, which is what Condition.informational exists for. The alternative — docking hearts to represent a risk that is currently zero — would say something false about the horse standing in front of the player.

Breeds

The variant is concentrated in Quarter Horse-derived lines rather than spread through the species, which is why the global founder rate here is low and the breed pool carries the real weight. The American Azteca registry screens breeding stock for it alongside HYPP, PSSM1, GBED and HERDA — the four the Azteca already carried in this mod, and the reason this locus was the conspicuous absence on that panel.

MH and PSSM1 are different things that look alike

Both cause exertional muscle damage and both turn up on the same Quarter Horse test panels, so they are easy to conflate. PSSM1 is a glycogen-storage defect and its horse ties up recurrently under ordinary work; MH is a calcium-channel defect whose horse is fine until a specific trigger. A horse can carry both, and in this mod they are separate loci that stack.

What needs verifying here

Designed to look like a bug: an MH/N horse names malignant hyperthermia yet must keep full hearts, speed and jump, since one missing heart breaks the informational path and nothing else would reveal it — while MH/MH costs six health and no founder may carry it.
