Magical gene / no coat effect

Gladiator

It picks fights. Any hostile mob that comes close enough gets attacked, with no prompting and no target from you — unless you are riding it, at which point it is a horse again.

Built, and not yet seen in game

The gene, its inheritance and its founder table exist and are covered by the test suite; the translator that turns it into behaviour was written against the 26.1.2 sources and is flagged unverified in its own comments. Nothing on this page has been watched happening to a horse. What to look at, and what is most likely to be wrong, is on To be verified.

It stands its ground

It does not chase. Anything that walks into range is a problem it will solve; anything that stays away is not its business. That is what makes it safe to leave in a paddock, and it is the difference between a guard horse and a horse you find dead in a ravine.

It needs teeth

A vanilla horse has no attack at all. This locus makes it willing; how hard it hits is magic fighter, and a gladiator without one is a horse that bravely inconveniences a zombie.

Health

It picks fights, so it takes damage. Pair with magic health or expect losses.

It holds a radius. It must never chase

A horse that pursues is a horse that dies forty blocks from its owner, and the owner blames the gene — correctly. Attack what comes within reach and return. Full pursuit and owner-leashed pursuit were both specified and both rejected in design; if a later session wants either, it is reopening a settled call, not filling a gap.

Re-check the condition every beat

has_rider already exists as a condition flag, but a temper that is evaluated once when the fight starts leaves a horse still swinging after someone mounts it. The check belongs on every beat, not on engagement.

Classify hostiles the way night temper already does

A hardcoded list makes every modded hostile invisible to the gene. Night temper already answers this question; reuse its answer rather than writing a second one that will drift.

The verb this gene exists to justify

night_temper is the right machine with the wrong switch. Its own javadoc defends welding night into the verb — and that argument holds for that locus and stops holding the moment a second gene wants the same behaviour on a different gate. temper is night_temper with the gate lifted out into an ordinary when, which makes this gene and guardian data rather than code.

Verified

A horse swings every ten ticks once a rear no longer freezes it (2026-09-15, formerly gap 222). HorseMeleeGoal keeps its own ten-tick cooldown, because vanilla's reset hard-codes twenty (API notes). The yard's KICK GLADIATOR pen puts a Gld/Gld horse in with two husks. Its first run (07:10) swung at ticks 84 and 143. The cause was the rear after each blow: a standing horse is immobile, and an immobile mob runs no goals (API notes). With HorseRearFightMixin (the owner's call: rear, and keep fighting), the 07:33 run swung at ticks 56, 70 and 80, killing the first husk in four blows. KICK PLAIN's horse, which has no temper, never swung at its husks. What that run also showed, the gladiator not turning on the second husk, is the next record.

A gladiator that kills one attacker turns on the next (2026-09-15, formerly gap 248). In the 07:33 run it killed the first husk at tick 130 and then never struck again. The second husk hit it four times in 3.6 seconds and killed it. GeneAbilityHandler.temper took the first matching entity in its 12-block box each beat: dead or alive, near or far, seen or not, while the neighbouring pen's husks sat inside that radius behind a wall. The fix keeps a live target it can see. Otherwise it takes whatever last hurt the horse, if that is alive, hostile, in range and in sight, and failing that the nearest live, visible match. A dead target is dropped. Guardian is untouched, because its owner-hurt trigger fires from GeneReactionHandler. Evidence, 07:44 run: the first husk died at tick 135. The next horse hurt line read no target, and the gladiator struck the second husk at tick 162, 27 ticks later. It killed that husk at tick 213 and lived, on 1 of 22 health. Every horse hurt line named a target it could see. Whether the old code had aimed through the wall was never logged, so that stays a likely cause rather than a proven one.

Why it stops when you get on

A war horse that fights while you are steering it is a horse fighting your steering. The gate is what makes the gene usable: it is a guard when you are away and a mount when you are there, and those are the two states a horse is ever actually in.
