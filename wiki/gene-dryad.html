Magical gene / no coat effect

Dryad

Once a day, roughly, a sapling appears where the horse has been. Left in one place for long enough it will grow a small wood around itself, one tree at a time and entirely without hurrying.

Built, and not yet seen in game

The gene, its inheritance and its founder table exist and are covered by the test suite; the translator that turns it into behaviour was written against the 26.1.2 sources and is flagged unverified in its own comments. Nothing on this page has been watched happening to a horse. What to look at, and what is most likely to be wrong, is on To be verified.

The rate is epigenetic

Once a day is the default; the interval is written on the allele copy and drifts with breeding, so a line can be pushed faster. Not much faster — the cap is deliberate.

It plants; it does not fertilise

An earlier specification had it bone-mealing the surrounding area. That would make it a farm, which is a far bigger thing than this gene is asking to be. It plants saplings and nothing else.

The daily cap does the safety work

One block change a day is nothing. Of the two spread genes in this batch this is by far the cheaper, and the epigenetic interval is bounded so breeding cannot turn it into a per-tick effect.

Bone meal was rejected, and the reason should survive

“Occasionally activates a bone-meal effect on the surrounding area” makes a horse that auto-farms every crop you own, which is an economy lever nobody asked for and very hard to walk back once players have it. Saplings only. Recorded here so it is not quietly “improved” later.

Do not plant on anything somebody built

Same rule as hot blooded: the vocabulary word keeps “what may be converted” in the translator. A sapling through a stable floor is the failure to avoid.

The cheapest world-editing gene

One vocabulary word on an existing verb, one bounded interval, one block a day. If spread's new words are being built in one pass, this is the one to do first — it exercises the whole path at a rate where nothing can go wrong quickly.

A gene you notice a week later

Almost everything in the mod expresses immediately or not at all. This one expresses across sessions: you stable a horse, and later there are trees there. That is a different relationship to a gene than any other locus offers, and it is worth having one.
