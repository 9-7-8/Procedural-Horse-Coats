Bay Coat

Gene / Locus Agouti (A)
Body Black Level 0.32
Min Hoof Fraction 0.12
Leg Range 0.15 - 0.95
Leg Jitter ±0.14
Paints a red-brown body with black points (mane, tail, ears, and hooves). The height of black points climbing up the legs and face is determined continuously from the Agouti allele value. Higher rolls result in a "Seal Brown" phenotype rather than using a separate gene.
