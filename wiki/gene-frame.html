Frame overo

The inverse constraint of tobiano: ragged horizontal white patches on the sides of the neck and barrel that do not cross the topline, plus the broad white face frame is the classic gene for. The legs stay coloured. White comes from a low-frequency BodyNoise field multiplied by a weight that goes to zero at the spine, pinning the patches to the flanks.

Pinning white to the flank band

common/genetics/genes/FrameGene.java
// flank band in ABSOLUTE body-space Y - no dependence on which face a texel is on
double side = smoothstep(bandLo - feather, bandLo, y) * (1 - smoothstep(bandHi, bandHi + feather, y));
if (side <= 0) return;
double v   = PatchNoise.field(seed, x, y, z, SCALE);
double jag = JAG * (PatchNoise.fbm2(seed ^ k, x*JAG_FREQ, y*JAG_FREQ, z*JAG_FREQ*1.4) - 0.5);
// outside the band the threshold climbs steeply, so white never reaches the topline
if (v + jag > (1 - cover) + (1 - side) * 0.6) { f.setRed(px,py,0f); f.setBlack(px,py,0f); }

The band is measured in absolute body-space Y against the whole-body bounds rather than a per-part fraction, so it lands the same whether a texel is on a side, top or bottom face - the earlier per-part version left the barrel almost untouched. White is bold inside the band (coverage 0.52–0.74) and its threshold climbs hard as side falls, so the spine and belly stay solidly coloured - the "frame". The jag term wobbles the margin at a fine scale, which is the ragged edge frame is known for. The face is whitened along the centreline out to faceHalf - a floor guarantees at least a blaze, a high roll gives a bald face.

Health — out of scope for now
