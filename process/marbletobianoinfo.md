# The Marble Tobiano — Full Text & Marking Analysis

## Text Content

**The Marble Tobiano**

The Marble Tobiano or Marmo is a heritage remnant of old baroque breeds in Obertaurer and has stayed hidden in plain sight, obscured by the presence of the more widespread Tobiano. A bunch of wild Obertaurers are found to be carriers but aside from a few individuals, always in combination with regular Tobiano, which dominates over Marmo, or as nT* which does not show the full extent of the characteristics possible with Marmo.

This coloration's gencode is marked as T* (nT* or T*T*) and co-exists with Tobiano as it is merely a variant of that gene. Marble Tobiano only expresses it's striking features when inherited homozygously. In combination with regular Tobiano it is not expressed and remains barely noticeable with one copy of the gene. Now with careful testing it is easier to find parents that carry the gene to elevate chances of a dominant expression: to become Marble Tobiano. The color was first mentioned by Italian ancestry thus "Marmo" is also used to describe the marking.

Since the discovery of the distinct gene is so recent, there could be unknown carriers out there in Obertaurers originating from wild herds that express very angular or "streaky" Tobiano markings.

## Detailed Marking Descriptions by Horse

### MINIMAL MARBLE TOBIANO (Left)

**Head & Neck:**
- **Face marking**: Thin, delicate white stripe running down the center of the face, **narrow and linear**
- **Neck**: Sparse, fragmented white patches with **high transparency** (semi-opaque); **feathered/broken edges** suggesting overlay effect rather than solid blocking
- **Texture**: Fine, almost ethereal—resembles dry brush or watercolor wash rather than defined patches

**Shoulder & Body:**
- **Chest**: Minimal white presence; narrow, **crisp vertical lines** in white
- **Ribcage & Flank**: Scattered **angular white streaks** with **medium opacity**, oriented diagonally or vertically; **sharp-edged** with **thin weight** (1–3 inches wide each)
- **Texture**: Streaky, almost veined—lines follow musculature rather than pooling into shapes

**Legs:**
- **Front legs**: Deep solid black from knee to hoof
- **Hind legs**: Black with minimal white on inner surface
- **Hooves**: Black

**Tail & Mane:**
- Dark/black

**Overall Opacity & Texture**: Low opacity throughout; **feathered, delicate, sketchy quality**—appears as if watercolor has been lightly applied over a dark base.

### TYPICAL MARBLE TOBIANO (Center)

**Head & Neck:**
- **Face blaze**: Broader than minimal version; white stripe covers most of the face width from forehead down through muzzle, **medium opacity**, with **slightly diffused edges**
- **Neck**: Larger connected patches of white; **moderately opaque** with **soft feathered boundaries**; patches extend across the full width and down past the shoulder

**Shoulder & Body:**
- **Chest**: Larger triangular or block-like white patch, **solid opacity**
- **Shoulder**: Generous white coverage extending from chest into the shoulder; **edges soften toward the flank**
- **Ribcage & Flank**: Larger irregular patches with **medium-high opacity**; **curved, organic boundaries** rather than straight lines; patches appear to flow around the body rather than pierce through it
- **Hindquarter & Croup**: Substantial white coverage with **high opacity**; large patch extends onto hip and upper hind leg
- **Texture**: Balanced between defined patches and slightly blurred edges; **less ethereal than minimal**, more solid

**Legs:**
- **Front legs**: Primarily dark with possible white socks or partial white on inner sides
- **Hind legs**: Mixed white and dark; substantial white on the leg carrying the flank patch
- **Hooves**: Mixed dark and light

**Tail & Mane:**
- Tail shows warm reddish/chestnut tones; mane dark
- **Hair texture at seams**: Where white meets dark, strands transition with **soft blending**

**Overall Opacity & Texture**: **High opacity** in core patches; **soft feathering at edges**—appears intentional and balanced, less random than minimal version.

### EXTENSIVE MARBLE TOBIANO (Right)

**Head & Neck:**
- **Face**: Extensive white blaze covering most of face, with dark stripe down center; **very high opacity**
- **Neck & Poll**: Massive white coverage; dramatic cape-like white patch extends from withers all the way up and forward to the poll; **near-opaque white**
- **Texture**: Bold, **sharply defined edges** separating dark and white zones; crisp boundary lines

**Shoulder & Body:**
- **Chest & Shoulder**: Enormous solid white patch, **completely opaque**; extends from chest through entire shoulder region
- **Ribcage & Flank**: Large white patch with **high opacity** and **defined edges**; patch is more geometric/blocky than organic
- **Hindquarter**: Substantial white hindquarter patch, **solid and opaque**
- **Croup to Tail Base**: Extensive white coverage transitioning the hindquarter pattern
- **Texture**: Bold, **angular edges**; minimal soft feathering; appears as **large geometric blocks** rather than flowing shapes

**Legs:**
- **Front legs**: Significant white stockings or boots; **high opacity**; possible white from hoof well up the cannon
- **Hind legs**: Extensive white with dark only on outer surfaces in places; one hind leg nearly entirely white
- **Hooves**: Mixed dark and light, following white leg patches

**Tail & Mane:**
- Mane and tail predominantly light/white; strong contrast with dark neck zones

**Overall Opacity & Texture**: **Near-complete opacity** in all patches; **sharp, defined boundaries** with minimal feathering; appears as **bold geometric blocking** of white onto dark base. Most dramatic and "sculptural" of the three expressions.
