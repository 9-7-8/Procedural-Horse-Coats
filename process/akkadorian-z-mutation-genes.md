# Akkadorian Z — Mutation Gene Specifications

Visual specification only. All geometry is given for a reference animal of 160 cm withers height, measured on the lateral silhouette of one side. Coverage percentages are fractions of one-side lateral body area, excluding mane and tail unless stated.

---

## 0. Shared conventions

### 0.1 Base classes

| Class | Body value | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Moonlight base | High-value white/silver coat | Markings render dark on this base |
| Colored base | Standard coat color (black in all reference examples; any real base color permitted) | Markings render light on this base |

**Moon pattern** = the light-value marking layer generated by a mutation. **Colored field** = the region retaining base coat value.

### 0.2 Base compatibility field

- `interchangeable` — expresses on either base class; polarity flips with the base
- `moonlight-only`
- `colored-only`

### 0.3 Frequency tiers

`common` / `uncommon` / `rare` / `divine`. Used for allele frequency in generation only.

### 0.4 Edge morphology classes

| Class | Transition width | Description |
|---|---|---|
| E0 | < 0.3 cm | Hard, smooth |
| E1 | 0.3–1 cm | Crisp, irregular/scalloped margin |
| E2 | 1–4 cm | Soft |
| E3 | 4–20 cm | Diffuse gradient, monotonic falloff |
| E4 | 5–25 cm | Stippled dissolve — discrete particles thinning to zero, no continuous boundary |

### 0.5 Axes and regions

Cranio-caudal (nose→tail), dorso-ventral (topline→ventral midline), proximo-distal (limbs). Named regions: muzzle, face, cheek, poll, crest, throatlatch, lateral neck, withers, scapula/shoulder, humeral, barrel, ventral midline, flank, back, loin, croup, hip, thigh, gaskin, forearm, carpus, tarsus/hock, cannon, pastern, hoof.

Bilateral patterning is generated independently per side. Left/right correlation coefficient 0.2–0.5 for placement, 0.6–0.9 for coverage magnitude.

### 0.6 Inverting rule

Genes flagged `inverting` reverse local polarity: where the inverting field overlaps a moon-pattern region, that region renders in the colored value; where it overlaps a colored field, that region renders in the moon-pattern value. Multiple inverting genes stack, each inversion applied in sequence (two overlapping inversions return the original polarity).

`Inverting` genes: Bottom Accretion, Top Accretion, Part Quarter, Double Quarter, Black Holes, Collar, Polymoon.

## 2. Punctate and stipple genes

### G-01 — STARS

Discrete punctate marks.

- **Element geometry:** circular to four-point stellate. Circular elements 0.3–1.2 cm diameter. Stellate elements 1.0–2.5 cm across, formed of two crossing rays at 90° ±15°, ray width 0.1–0.25 cm, ray length asymmetric (long axis 1.3–2.0× short axis).
- **Count:** 40–200 per side.
- **Size distribution:** right-skewed; ~70% of elements < 1.0 cm, ~5% stellate.
- **Density field:** maximum 0.8–2.5 elements/100 cm² over the dorsal third of the barrel, the croup, and the dorsal half of the lateral neck; falling to 0.1–0.3 at the ventral midline; 0 distal to carpus/tarsus; 0 on the muzzle and cheek.
- **Clustering:** mild clumping over Poisson — 3–8 element groups within 6–12 cm radii, 4–10 groups per side.
- **Edge:** E0–E1.

### G-02 — STELLAR

Angular multi-stroke glyphs, larger and sparser than G-01.

- **Element geometry:** 2–4 straight or shallow-arc strokes (arc radius 8–20 cm) intersecting at 20–90°, stroke width 0.2–0.5 cm, stroke length 1.5–6 cm. Each glyph 2–6 cm across its bounding box. 1–3 satellite dots 0.2–0.6 cm within 3 cm of the glyph.
- **Count:** 8–30 glyphs per side.
- **Placement:** scapula, cranial barrel, dorsal neck, withers. Density falls to 0 caudal to the flank and ventral to the elbow line.
- **Orientation:** stroke sets randomly rotated; no preferred axis.
- **Edge:** E0.

### G-03 — DUST

Fine stipple with no discrete boundary anywhere.

- **Particle diameter:** 0.05–0.5 cm.
- **Peak density:** 20–200 particles/100 cm² at field cores, decaying to 0 across 10–25 cm (E4 throughout).
- **Field locations:**
  - Distal limbs — proximal core at pastern/fetlock, dissolving upward, terminating between mid-cannon and 10 cm below carpus/tarsus.
  - Ventral midline band — 6–14 cm dorso-ventral height, running from the elbow to the flank.
  - Muzzle cap — from the upper lip caudally 8–14 cm, dissolving at the nasal bone.
  - Caudal crest margin — 2–6 cm band adjacent to the mane, length 20–50 cm.
- **Total coverage:** 6–14%.

## 3. Linear genes

### G-04 — STRIPED MANE/TAIL

No body coat expression.

- **Mane:** 3–9 contrasting strand bundles, bundle width at the crest 0.5–3 cm, spaced 4–15 cm along a 60–90 cm crest. Each bundle runs full strand length; ~30% taper to a feathered termination at 40–80% of strand length.
- **Forelock:** included in ~50% of expressions, 1–3 bundles.
- **Tail:** 2–6 bundles, 1–4 cm wide at the dock, spanning full or partial length.
- **Value:** contrasting to the mane/tail base value (light strands on dark hair, dark strands on light hair).

### G-05 — SCRATCHES

Parallel spindle-profile streaks.

- **Element geometry:** length 15–45 cm, maximum width 0.8–3 cm located at 40–60% of length, tapering to points at both ends.
- **Sets:** 3–5 near-parallel streaks, inter-streak spacing 3–8 cm, divergence between streaks ≤ 10°.
- **Orientation:** long axis 30–60° to the cranio-caudal axis, running dorso-cranial to ventro-caudal.
- **Set count:** 1–4 per side. Primary sites: hip and thigh, caudal barrel, lateral neck.
- **Limb variant:** short transverse streaks 4–10 cm long, 0.5–1.5 cm wide, 4–9 per limb segment, oriented 75–90° to the limb axis across the cannon and forearm, reading as banding.
- **Edge:** E0–E1.

### G-06 — ZEBRA'S COAT

Transverse narrow striping restricted to the cranial body.

- **Element geometry:** width 0.6–2 cm, length 6–30 cm, straight to shallow-arc (radius 30–90 cm), tapered terminations.
- **Count:** 10–30 stripes per side.
- **Spacing:** 2–5 cm between stripe centers.
- **Orientation:** 70–90° to the cranio-caudal axis over the lateral neck; rotating to 45–70° and curving caudally as the field crosses the withers and shoulder.
- **Field limit:** poll to the caudal scapular border. No expression caudal to the scapula, none on limbs.
- **Bifurcation:** ~20% of stripes split into 2 branches over the distal 20–40% of their length, branch angle 10–25°.
- **Edge:** E0–E1.

### G-07 — INTERNAL FLOW

Thin contour lines tracking underlying musculature.

- **Element geometry:** width 0.4–1.5 cm, length 20–60 cm, curvature radius 25–80 cm, constant width along length.
- **Sets:** nested groups of 2–5 lines, inter-line spacing 2–6 cm, following a common curve family.
- **Anatomical tracks:** caudal margin of the scapula/triceps; the arc of the ribcage (concave cranially); the cranial margin of the hindquarter sweeping from the point of hip to the stifle.
- **Continuity:** 60% continuous; 40% broken into 2–4 dashes with 1–3 cm gaps, dash ends tapered.
- **Set count:** 2–4 per side.
- **Edge:** E0.

## 4. Ring, disk and blotch genes

### G-08 — CRESCENTS

Annulus sectors.

- **Element geometry:** outer radius 8–25 cm, arc band width 2.5–9 cm (band width 25–45% of outer radius), angular extent 90–240°. Band width tapers to 30–60% of maximum at both arc terminations.
- **Count:** 1–4 per side; largest element centered on the mid-barrel, subordinates on the scapula, hip, and caudal neck.
- **Orientation:** concavity direction unconstrained; cranial or dorsal facing in the reference.
- **Interior:** retains base field value.
- **Edge:** E2 on the convex margin, E2–E3 on the concave margin.

### G-09 — WORMHOLES

Annular marks with contrasting centers.

- **Element geometry:** outer diameter 3–12 cm, ring wall thickness 0.8–3 cm (20–30% of outer diameter), interior retains base field value.
- **Count:** 10–40 per side.
- **Distribution:** 2–4 clusters over scapula, barrel, croup/hip, lateral neck; cluster radius 15–35 cm, 4–12 elements each. Isolated elements at 15–25% of total.
- **Variants within an expression:**
  - Complete rings — 65%
  - Incomplete arcs (200–330°) — 25%
  - Concentric doubles — 10%; second ring gap 1–3 cm, wall thickness 0.5–2 cm
- **Edge:** E1.

### G-10 — BLACK HOLES  `inverting`

Solid hard-edged patches. Placement is unrestricted — any body region including face, limbs, ventral midline, and over the mane base.

- **Element geometry:** long axis 6–25 cm, aspect ratio 1:1–2:1, long axis oriented within ±35° of cranio-caudal.
- **Count:** 3–12 per side.
- **Margin:** E0–E1 with scalloping amplitude 1–4 cm, wavelength 4–12 cm.
- **Satellites:** ~20% of patches carry 1–3 dots 1–3 cm diameter within 5 cm of the parent margin.
- **Fill value:** either (a) the animal's main coat color, or (b) pure black (L* ≤ 15) independent of the main coat color. Fill choice is per-expression, not per-patch.
- **Inversion:** over a moon-pattern region the patch renders in the colored/black value; over a colored field it renders in the moon-pattern value.

### G-11 — POLYMOON  `inverting`

Chain of circular disks.

- **Element geometry:** diameter 2–7 cm, circular, edge E1.
- **Chain path:** curved, total path length 40–110 cm, running from the throatlatch or cranial shoulder along the caudal scapular margin and onto the barrel. Secondary chains of 3–6 disks on the hip or caudal flank in ~40% of expressions.
- **Spacing:** center-to-center 1.2–2.5× local disk diameter; 0–30% overlap permitted between adjacent disks.
- **Count:** 8–25 disks in the primary chain.
- **Size gradient:** diameter varies smoothly along the chain, maximum at 40–60% of path length, minima at both terminations (30–50% of maximum).

### G-12 — LASERTAE

Large ellipses composed of a high-value halo enclosing a dark core.

- **Outer geometry:** long axis 15–40 cm, aspect ratio 1.3:1–2:1, long axis 0–30° from cranio-caudal.
- **Halo band:** width 3–10 cm, outer edge E3 (6–15 cm), inner edge E2.
- **Core:** occupies 30–60% of outer ellipse area, concentric or offset up to 20% of the long axis, value equal to or 5–15% darker than the surrounding colored field, edge E2.
- **Count:** 2–5 per side, centered on the scapula, mid-barrel, and hip.

## 5. Field and region genes

### G-13 — QUAZARS

Elongated high-value beams with no hard boundary.

- **Element geometry:** length 30–80 cm, maximum width 8–25 cm, spindle profile with maximum width at 45–60% of length.
- **Edges:** E3 throughout, 8–20 cm gradient; peak value at the geometric center with approximately linear falloff to the margins.
- **Orientation:** long axis within ±25° of cranio-caudal.
- **Count:** 2–4 per side, sited on the caudal scapula/cranial barrel, the hip, and the caudal thigh.
- **Topline crossing:** permitted; a beam crossing the topline continues onto the contralateral side at 40–80% of its original width.

### G-14 — WAVES

Continuous caudal field with a sinusoidal cranial boundary.

- **Coverage:** 20–40%, occupying hip, thigh, caudal flank, and gaskin.
- **Cranial boundary:** runs dorso-ventrally, modulated as a sinusoid — wavelength 8–20 cm, amplitude 4–12 cm, 3–6 lobes between topline and ventral limit.
- **Dorsal limit:** at or 0–8 cm below the topline. **Ventral limit:** gaskin to hock.
- **Edge:** E1 at lobe crests, E2 in troughs.
- **Satellites:** 0–4 detached lobes 4–12 cm across, positioned 4–12 cm cranial to the main boundary.

### G-15 — SHARK

Single falcate dorsal field.

- **Geometry:** base length 25–50 cm along the topline centered on the withers; maximum ventral height 10–25 cm; apex displaced 5–15 cm cranial to the base midpoint; caudal margin concave with a chord-to-arc depth of 3–10 cm; cranial margin straight to slightly convex.
- **Crest extension:** tapering band 2–6 cm wide running cranially along the crest from the field apex to the poll, present in ~70% of expressions.
- **Edge:** E1 dorsally and along the caudal margin, E2 ventrally.

### G-16 — COLLAR  `inverting`

Transverse encircling band at the neck base, dividing the body into two value fields.

- **Band geometry:** width 12–35 cm along the cranio-caudal axis; caudal margin located at the cranial scapular border ±8 cm; full circumferential wrap.
- **Field split:** the region caudal to the band expresses the moon-pattern value (coverage 45–65% of the body); the head, cranial neck, and crest retain the colored field. Distal limbs retain the colored field from carpus/tarsus down, proximal boundary E2–E3.
- **Margins:** cranial E2, caudal E2–E3.
- **Mane and tail:** mane retains the colored value; tail follows the caudal field in ~50% of expressions.

### G-17 — ACCRETION locus  `inverting`

A single continuous boundary line divides the body into a dorsal and a ventral field; the allele determines which field carries the moon pattern.

**Bottom Accretion** (common)

- **Coverage:** 25–45%, ventral.
- **Boundary:** runs cranio-caudally at 30–55% of local dorso-ventral body depth measured up from the ventral midline; rises 5–15 cm over the shoulder and 5–20 cm over the flank; undulation amplitude ≤ 6 cm, wavelength 25–60 cm.
- **Edge:** E3, 6–18 cm gradient.
- **Includes:** ventral barrel, elbow and stifle regions, all four limbs to the hoof (limb coverage complete, no separate boundary). Muzzle included in ~60% of expressions as a cap 10–18 cm from the upper lip.

**Top Accretion** (uncommon)

- **Coverage:** 20–40%, dorsal.
- **Boundary:** runs cranio-caudally at 45–70% of local body depth measured down from the topline; undulation amplitude ≤ 8 cm, wavelength 20–50 cm.
- **Edge:** E3, 6–20 cm gradient.
- **Includes:** crest, dorsal neck, withers, back, loin, croup, dorsal hip. Excludes limbs. Mane included in ~50% of expressions.

### G-18 — QUARTER locus  `inverting`

The trunk is partitioned by two transverse planes — at the caudal scapular margin and at the cranial iliac margin — and by the dorso-ventral midline of the trunk, producing quadrant fields.

**Part Quarter** (common)

- One contiguous quadrant expresses the moon pattern.
- **Coverage:** 20–35%.
- **Boundary orientation:** the transverse boundary sits 60–90° to the cranio-caudal axis; the longitudinal boundary follows the trunk midline ±10 cm.
- **Edge:** E3, 10–25 cm gradient on both boundaries. Boundaries do not meet at a visible corner — the intersection is rounded with a radius of 15–30 cm.
- **Partial fill:** the expressing quadrant may be filled 60–100%, the shortfall taken from the corner furthest from the quadrant center.

**Double Quarter** (common)

- Two quadrants express: the two cranial, the two caudal, the two dorsal, the two ventral, or diagonal opposites.
- **Coverage:** 35–60%.
- **Adjacent pairs:** no seam at the shared boundary; the pair reads as a single field.
- **Diagonal pairs:** the two fields meet at the trunk center over a 10–20 cm bridge, or remain separated by a 5–15 cm colored isthmus.
- **Edge:** E3, 10–25 cm.

### G-19 — SKY SHADOW

Non-patterned whole-body value modifier. No discrete boundary at any location.

- **Global effect:** base coat value shifted toward mid-grey by 25–45%, reducing coat-to-marking contrast proportionally.
- **Gradient:** an additional 15–40% lightening over the ventral barrel, flank, and the caudal half of the scapula, falling off E3 over 15–40 cm.
- **Retained value:** mane, tail, distal limbs from carpus/tarsus down, and the muzzle retain full base value with no shift; transition to the shifted field is E3, 10–25 cm.

## 6. Distribution genes

### G-20 — INTEGRATION

Spot field propagating outward from existing colored regions. Reads as an appaloosa-type blanket at medium spot scale.

- **Source regions:** any existing colored field — mane base and crest, dorsal stripe, distal limbs, the margins of other expressed markings.
- **Element geometry:** 2–9 cm across, circular to lobate (1–3 lobes), long axis aligned to the local direction of spread, aspect ratio 1:1–2:1.
- **Density vs. distance from source:** 4–10 elements/100 cm² within 10 cm; 1.5–4 at 20 cm; < 1 at 40 cm; 0 beyond 55–70 cm.
- **Confluence:** within 8 cm of a source, elements merge into continuous patches; merged margin scalloping amplitude 2–5 cm.
- **Total coverage:** 10–35%.
- **Edge:** E1 on individual elements; E4 at the leading edge of the field as elements thin out.

### G-21 — PAINT MOON

Real-world pinto and roan genetics applied to the moon-pattern layer. Variants may co-occur; combinations follow standard real-world stacking (e.g. tovero = tobiano + overo-type).

**Tobiano**

- White fields oriented dorso-ventrally, crossing the topline at 1–4 locations.
- Boundary E0–E1, smooth-curved, low scalloping (amplitude ≤ 2 cm).
- Head retains base color apart from ordinary facial markings (star, stripe, snip, blaze 3–8 cm wide).
- All four limbs white distal to carpus/tarsus, proximal margin roughly horizontal.
- Coverage 30–70%.

**Frame overo**

- White fields oriented cranio-caudally in horizontal bands, centered on the mid-body depth; never crossing the topline.
- Boundary E1, jagged, scalloping amplitude 2–6 cm, wavelength 5–15 cm.
- Limbs retain base color in most expressions.
- Extensive facial white — bald face or apron, 12–25 cm wide.
- Coverage 15–50%.

**Splashed white**

- White ascending from the ventral midline; boundary E0, smooth and continuous, as a horizontal dip line.
- Broad blaze to full apron face; white extends past the eyes in high expression.
- Limbs white from the hoof upward with smooth horizontal margins.
- Coverage 20–60%.

**Sabino**

- Roaned, interdigitating boundary — E4, 3–15 cm of intermixed white and colored hairs.
- High limb white with jagged proximal margins reaching to the forearm/gaskin.
- Belly spotting: 2–8 irregular patches 5–25 cm.
- Extensive face white, often with a lower-lip or chin patch 4–12 cm.
- Coverage 10–90%.

**Rabicano**

- White ticking restricted to the flank, tail dock, and caudal barrel.
- Tail dock: 2–6 transverse white bands, band width 2–6 cm, spanning the first 15–30 cm of the dock.
- Barrel: vertical rib-following white streaks 10–30 cm long, 0.5–2 cm wide, 8–25 per side, density decreasing cranially.
- No white crossing the topline, no face or limb white attributable to this variant.
- Coverage 3–12%.

**Modifiers**

- Classic roan — uniform white hair intermixture 30–60% over the trunk, head and limbs retaining full base value.
- Grey — progressive whole-body depigmentation, advancing 5–15% per year from the face and flanks; overrides all other coat-value patterning at completion; mane and tail may retain residual dark hairs.

## 7. Chromatic genes

### G-22 — GAMMA  `divine`

Chromatic expression modifier. Marks render in a saturated hue rather than a value contrast.

- **Hue set (one per mark, multiple marks may differ):** yellow ≈ 55°, orange ≈ 30°, red ≈ 0°, violet ≈ 285°, blue ≈ 230°, green ≈ 120°.
- **Color parameters:** saturation 60–100%, luminance 45–75% at the mark core.
- **Glow:** outer bloom 2–8 cm beyond the mark margin at 20–40% of core saturation, falling to 0 (E3).
- **Base form (Gamma):** diffuse chromatic field 20–50 cm across, aspect ratio 1:1–2.5:1, sited on the barrel and caudal flank, edges E3 throughout, 2–4 fields per side.
- **Alternate forms** — geometry taken wholesale from the named gene, rendered in the hue set with the glow parameters above:
  - Gamma Stellar (per G-02)
  - Gamma Dust (per G-03)
  - Gamma Stars (per G-01)
  - Gamma Internal Flow (per G-07)
  - Gamma Scratches (per G-05)
  - Gamma Striped Mane/Tail (per G-04)

### G-23 — NEBULA LIGHT  `divine` · colored base only

Multi-hue diffuse field with embedded point sources.

- **Coverage:** 25–50%, centered on the caudal barrel, flank, croup, and hip, extending cranially onto the scapula in ~50% of expressions.
- **Hue composition:** 2–4 overlapping zones drawn from magenta ≈ 320°, violet ≈ 275°, blue ≈ 215°, teal ≈ 185°. Zone diameter 15–45 cm, blended across 10–30 cm transitions. Saturation 40–90%, luminance 30–65%.
- **Point population:** 0.2–1.0 cm points at 5–30/100 cm² within the field; plus 3–10 larger points 1.5–3 cm with radial halos 2–5 cm at 30–60% core luminance.
- **Edges:** E3–E4, no hard boundary at any margin.
- **Alternate forms** — geometry per the named gene, fill replaced by the nebula hue field and point population:
  - Nebula Crescents (per G-08)
  - Nebula Wormholes (per G-09)
  - Nebula Dust (per G-03)
  - Nebula Quazars (per G-13)
  - Nebula Waves (per G-14)

### G-24 — SPECTRUM LIGHT  `divine` · colored base only

Continuous monotonic hue ramp.

- **Field geometry:** single field 40–90 cm long, 15–35 cm tall, sited over the caudal barrel, flank, and hip; long axis 0–20° from cranio-caudal.
- **Ramp:** hue rotates continuously along the long axis through spectral order — red 0° → orange 30° → yellow 55° → green 120° → cyan 185° → blue 225° → violet 280°. No banding, no repeated segments; hue rate 3–7° per cm.
- **Saturation:** 50–90% along the field centerline, falling to 0 at the margins.
- **Edges:** E3, 6–20 cm.
- **Mane:** adjacent mane segments pick up the local hue at 40–70% saturation across a 10–25 cm crest length in ~60% of expressions.

## 8. Burn locus

### G-25 — BURN

Renders pure black (L* ≤ 15) on both moonlight and colored bases, independent of main coat color. Alleles below; co-expression is **Full Burn** and counts as one mutation.

**Burnt Moon** (common)

- **Limb fields:** black from the hoof upward on all four limbs; proximal boundary between mid-cannon and 10 cm below the carpus/tarsus, the boundary rising 3–10 cm on the cranial aspect relative to the caudal. Edge E2–E3, 3–12 cm.
- **Muzzle cap:** black from the upper lip caudally 8–15 cm, enclosing the nostrils; edge E3.
- **Ventral shading:** soft black gradient along the ventral midline and beneath the jaw, 5–15 cm dorso-ventral height, edge E3 throughout, present in ~60% of expressions.
- **Coverage:** 8–18%.

**Burnt Mane/Tail** (uncommon)

- Mane, forelock, and tail render black.
- **Retained strands:** 0–40% of strands may retain the base coat color or white, distributed as bundles 0.5–4 cm wide; retention frequency highest at the caudal third of the crest and the distal third of the tail.
- No body coat expression.

**Full Burn**

- Burnt Moon + Burnt Mane/Tail expressed together. Counts as a single mutation for mutation-count purposes.

## 9. Notes on unresolved points

1. **Burnt Moon field extent.** The reference illustrates this allele on a moonlight base only, where the visible black regions are the distal limbs, muzzle, and a soft ventral gradient. Its appearance on a colored base is not illustrated; the rule "always black on both bases" is preserved above, but on a black colored base the field is not separately distinguishable. If Burnt Moon is instead intended to blacken the moon-pattern layer generated by other genes rather than to occupy fixed anatomical fields, the fields listed in G-25 should be replaced by a per-marking value override. This is the one entry where the reference image supports both readings.
2. **Part Quarter vs. Double Quarter in the reference.** Both reference animals show a light body with a retained dark topline, neck, and limbs, which does not by itself distinguish one from two quadrants. The quadrant model above is inferred from the allele names and the coverage difference between the two thumbnails.
3. **Excluded by instruction:** lunar phase is not used as a determiner anywhere. The circular icons in the source legend have been read strictly as base-class compatibility (moonlight / colored / interchangeable).
4. **Frequency tiers** are retained only as generation weights for allele frequency. Remove the column if the sim assigns frequency elsewhere.
