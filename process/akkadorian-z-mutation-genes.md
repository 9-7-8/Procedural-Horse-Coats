Every locus in this file is built except the one below, which is
refused as a duplicate: Paint Moon is real-world pinto and roan
genetics, and this mod already has tobiano, frame overo, splash,

### G-21 — PAINT MOON

Real-world pinto and roan genetics applied to the moon-pattern layer. Variants may co-occur; combinations follow standard real-world stacking (e.g. tovero = tobiano + overo-type).

**Tobiano**

- White fields oriented dorso-ventrally, crossing the topline at 1–4 locations.
- Boundary E0–E1, smooth-curved, low scalloping (amplitude ≤ 2 cm).
- Head retains base color apart from ordinary facial markings (star, stripe, snip, blaze 3–8 cm wide).
- All four limbs white distal to carpus/tarsus, proximal margin roughly horizontal.
- Coverage 30–70%.

**Frame overo**

- White fields oriented cranio-caudally in horizontal bands, centered on the mid-body depth; never crossing the topline.
- Boundary E1, jagged, scalloping amplitude 2–6 cm, wavelength 5–15 cm.
- Limbs retain base color in most expressions.
- Extensive facial white — bald face or apron, 12–25 cm wide.
- Coverage 15–50%.

**Splashed white**

- White ascending from the ventral midline; boundary E0, smooth and continuous, as a horizontal dip line.
- Broad blaze to full apron face; white extends past the eyes in high expression.
- Limbs white from the hoof upward with smooth horizontal margins.
- Coverage 20–60%.

**Sabino**

- Roaned, interdigitating boundary — E4, 3–15 cm of intermixed white and colored hairs.
- High limb white with jagged proximal margins reaching to the forearm/gaskin.
- Belly spotting: 2–8 irregular patches 5–25 cm.
- Extensive face white, often with a lower-lip or chin patch 4–12 cm.
- Coverage 10–90%.

**Rabicano**

- White ticking restricted to the flank, tail dock, and caudal barrel.
- Tail dock: 2–6 transverse white bands, band width 2–6 cm, spanning the first 15–30 cm of the dock.
- Barrel: vertical rib-following white streaks 10–30 cm long, 0.5–2 cm wide, 8–25 per side, density decreasing cranially.
- No white crossing the topline, no face or limb white attributable to this variant.
- Coverage 3–12%.

**Modifiers**

- Classic roan — uniform white hair intermixture 30–60% over the trunk, head and limbs retaining full base value.
- Grey — progressive whole-body depigmentation, advancing 5–15% per year from the face and flanks; overrides all other coat-value patterning at completion; mane and tail may retain residual dark hairs.
