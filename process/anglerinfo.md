ANGLER

Occurs As: ___ __ nAng // ___ __ AngAng
ABOUT ANGLER:

Angler is a caused by the Ang gene and adds colourful oval or circular markings across the body of the horse, similar to markings found on luminescent fish. These markings can appear anywhere on the body, but should travel horizontally across the horse, save for the face and legs, where the pattern of lights may deviate.

The orbs have a faint glow and can be any color. They can either be one solid color, or a gradient of two. See below for more details on how Angler displays.

Please refer to the Mutation Dominance Order on page 5 to determine interactions with other mutations.
HORSES WITH THIS GENE CAN:

    Have eyes that match the color of the lights seen on the body.

    Have lights that are either a single color, or two colors as a gradient.

        Gradients must be within each light–lights may not be multiple colors.

    Show lights in a horizontal-only manner. Vertical lights allowed on the face and legs only.

    Have small to medium sized lights, no larger than the example to the right.

    Show lights arranged in one long string, or several shorter ones. Strings of lights should be made up of 2 lights as a minimum.

    Have natural curves and bends to the strings of lights.

HORSES WITH THIS GENE CANNOT:

    Have singular lights on their own that are not part of a string.

    Display multiple colors or light designs on one horse.

    Have altered mane, tail, or hooves even if the lights touch.

    Have any other shape of light, they may only be circular or oval.

    Have a gradient across the string of lights as a whole instead of per individual light.

    Have hard-edged areas of different colors, or shapes contained within the lights.
