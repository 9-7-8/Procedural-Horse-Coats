Tail gene - implementation treatment

Scoped with the owner. Every "Decided" line below is her call; "Recommended" lines are the scoping
session's, not asked; "Open" lines are still to be settled, by whom is said on the line; "UNVERIFIED"
lines are facts this file has not proved and the building session must check first. Source of truth for
what exists is wiki/model-parts.html (the grown-parts machinery), the antler form and ram horn form gene
pages (the dominance-series precedent) and wiki/gene-tail-color.html. This file is retired (deleted, per
/process-intake step 8) as each piece ships and moves onto those pages' Gameplay and Coding tabs.
Release: patch per piece (each piece is something a player notices); saves keep loading.

1. The request, as given
========================
"on another one of the tails, but I want to overturn a decision in lion tail: we need to create a single
tail gene, which wildtype means a horse tail, and every new tail gene we add comes with its own priority,
and each is strictly dominant over the lower prio ones, and it only ever shows one tail"

Reading: ONE locus for every tail the mod will ever grow. Its wild type is the ordinary horse tail. Each
non-wild tail (lion now; fish, blade, club, dragon later) is an allele with its own rank in a dominance
list; the highest-ranked allele a horse carries is the only tail it shows; the vanilla tail is hidden
whenever that winner is not wild. This OVERTURNS the lion tail file's probe call "separate genes, ALL
DRAW" (Decided by her on 2026-10-02, now reversed by her): there are no per-tail genes and no OR-ed hide
rule, and no horse ever shows two tails.
Confirmed with her this session: a NEW file builds the locus and the lion allele; the lion tail file is
trimmed to the lion-specific parts; a new tail may take any rank, by explicit rank; only the dominant copy's
numbers are used; every other lion tail call stands.

2. In scope, and what is not
============================
In scope:
- One new magical gene "tail" (key horsegenetics.tail): alleles Lt (lion) and n (wild = horse tail), the
  dominance list Lt > n, the hide rule "the shown allele is not wild", the TAIL_BASE anchor and the adult
  lion tail part (the lion-specific shape lives in "Tufted lion tail - implementation treatment.txt").
- The conventions every later tail follows (section 4, "Adding a tail"), written once on the gene's page.
- Both editors (rule 5), the info screen, the gene's wiki page, the founder entry.
Not in scope (all re-askable):
- Any tail other than the lion (fish, blade, club, dragon): each is its own later treatment that appends
  an allele and names its rank. The fish tail also needs "hide the hind half", a separate capability
  (intake/Mythic breeds - gene audit.txt gap 7); not built here.
- A tail colour change: TailColorGene stays a separate locus and keeps painting the hair.
- Two tails on one horse, by any route (Decided: never).
- Breeds that carry a tail (design-a-breed, a separate task), and the whipping chain (lion treatment piece 3).

3. What already exists, so the building session does not redo it
================================================================
Read from the code on 2026-10-02 (common/src/main/java/com/example/horsegenetics/common/):
- Closest precedent, the dominance series: genetics/genes/AntlerFormGene (alleles Frk Brw Pal Crn n;
  "Dominance is not the declaration order; it is a separate list": a private List<Allele> dominance, walked
  in rank order) and RamHornFormGene (Crl Crk Fhn Scr). Allele storage order is append-only; the rank list
  is what lets a new tail sit anywhere. The wiki pages: wiki/gene-antler-form.html, gene-ram-horn-form.html.
- The part machinery, the tail on the vanilla models, TailColorGene, BraidLayer and EmissiveCoatLayer:
  all described in "Tufted lion tail - implementation treatment.txt" section 3 (same classes; not repeated).
- Priority slots: the grown-part loci run 230-240 (AntlersGene 230 .. DragonHornColourGene 240). The
  next free slot is UNVERIFIED (241 is the proposal; see section 6).
- LOOKS built but is not: no tail gene, tail PartKind, TAIL_BASE anchor or hide flag exists. The lion
  treatment's earlier "one boolean per tail gene, OR-ed" rule was never built, so nothing has to be undone
  in code; only that treatment's text changes.

4. The pieces
=============
Piece 1 - A rare lion-tailed horse (the locus, the hide, the adult lion tail)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
What the player sees: unchanged from the lion tail treatment: a horse with a Lt allele has a thin tail
ending in a brush of hair instead of its own tail. New: the horse info screen and the gene page say
"tail: horse (wild) or lion", and nothing about a second tail can ever appear on one horse.
- Decided (owner): ONE locus, key horsegenetics.tail, wild type n = the horse's own tail.
- Decided (owner): each tail allele has its own priority in a dominance list; strictly dominant over every
  lower one; the winner is the only tail shown. Dominance is plain, never codominant.
- Decided (owner): a new tail takes any rank, by explicit rank in the dominance list (declaration order is
  append-only and separate). The wild n is always the bottom of the list, below every tail.
- Decided (owner): only the dominant allele's copy supplies the epigenome numbers; the other copy's are
  carried, inherited and drifted like any recessive's, but never drawn.
- Decided (owner): the lion allele is Lt (token proposal; building session greps every allele token),
  wild n; Lt > n.
- Decided (owner): magical gene; rare and hand-seeded, wild n dominates the founders; each new tail adds
  its own small founder share, tuned in the yard; no invisible carriers in the wild.
- Decided (owner): every other lion tail call stands: hide by hiding the model part (set for every horse
  every frame), rigid thin tail + tuft, hair-patch colour, parts-off or beyond the detail distance shows the
  vanilla tail, rider fade, adult and foal, braid and tail glow invisible on a tailed horse.
- Recommended: the hide rule is one method in common/ (for example GrownParts.hidesVanillaTail(genotype)),
  true when the dominant allele is not n, so HdHorseModel needs no change when a tail allele is appended.
- Recommended: the part comes from the dominant allele: TailGene.tailFor(genotype, epigenome, ...) returns
  Optional<List<AttachedPart>> like AntlersGene.racksFor, switching on the winning allele; each tail
  allele owns one PartKind (the lion treatment's LION_TAIL).
- Recommended: dominance is one private List<Allele> walked in rank order, exactly as AntlerFormGene does;
  a test pins the whole list so a later tail cannot reorder an existing one by accident.
Adding a tail (the convention, written on the gene's Coding tab):
 1. Append the allele to the declared order (never reorder, never remove).
 2. Insert it into the dominance list at its explicit rank, and say in the commit and the page whether it
    outranks an existing tail. Placing a new tail BELOW an existing one changes what a heterozygous carrier of
    the two looks like, so a rank change for an existing pair is a Decided (owner) line in that tail's treatment.
 3. Add its PartKind, generator, shape cases, anchor offsets (adult and foal), founder share, and a gene
    carrier entry if the carrot rules list every magical gene.
 4. Extend the dominance-list test and the "exactly one tail" test.
Where the code lives:
- common/: TailGene (alleles, dominance list, expressionOf, isNatural() false, label, founder entry), the
  hide rule, the PartKind LION_TAIL and its switch cases and generator (per the lion treatment), GrownParts.of
  and shapes, and a test of the winner for every genotype.
- NeoForge: the TAIL_BASE case in AttachedPartLayer.anchor, the setupAnim hide on HdHorseModel and
  HdBabyHorseModel (reading the render state's hide flag), the hair-patch tint, the sheet bake: all as in
  the lion treatment piece 1.
Data and saves: one new locus appended to the genotype code; existing worlds read n/n everywhere. Allele
declaration order is permanent once a world has used it; ranks live in code and may change only with a
Decided line and a note on what changes for existing mixed pairs. Nothing is saved but the allele pair and the
epigenome numbers on its copies. Parked genes follow genes/index.json (hard rule 6).
Sync: none new; the part is computed on the client from the already-synced genotype.
Config: none new; the parts switches and detail distance gate the part and the hide together.
Tests (common/): TailGeneTest: allele table, the full dominance list, winner for every genotype pair
(Lt/Lt, Lt/n, n/n, and with a second allele once one exists), "at most one tail part per horse",
"hides the vanilla tail iff the winner is not n", shape cache key, genotype-code round trip with the locus
appended; the parity snapshot and CommonPortabilityTest rerun; a web/ designer test that the locus lists.
Play checks: a Lt/n horse and a Lt/Lt horse show exactly one lion tail; an n/n horse shows its normal tail;
a pen of mixed horses never shows two tails or none (the shared model's visible flag is set every frame);
parts switched off shows the vanilla tail on every horse. When a second tail exists: a pair of different
tails shows only the higher-ranked one.
Docs: a new gene page wiki/gene-tail.html (add-a-gene; Gameplay, Coding, Science tabs), wiki/model-parts.html,
wiki/horse-designer.html, a one-line note on wiki/gene-tail-color.html.

5. Decisions
============
A. The ask: a single tail locus, wild = horse tail, ranked alleles, one tail shown. Decided (owner).
B. Fit: unchanged from the lion treatment (horses-only, vanilla intact, a look gene, any breed can carry
   it). Bends the lion treatment's own "separate genes, all draw" call, which she overturned; no settled
   wiki decision is bent. A multi-allele dominance series is the existing pattern (antler form).
C. How met: spawning and breeding; survival-reachable; no new item; explained by the gene page, the info
   screen and the designer.
D. Genetics: priority slot UNVERIFIED (proposal 241; read before choosing); family Grown parts; alleles Lt, n
   appended; epigenome per tail allele, ranges set in its generator; not sex-linked, no lethal, no health effect.
   Both editors: the locus must list in the designer and CustomHorseSpawnScreen (rule 5, UNVERIFIED how
   each picks genes; web/HorseEditor.java and common/EditorRules).
E. Breeds: none changed.
F. Items and blocks: none. A gene carrot / splice entry only if the carrot rules list every magical gene.
G. Config: none new.
H. Saves: one appended locus; old worlds read n/n. Never a compat bump.
I. Multiplayer: client render of synced data; dedicated-server safe if new client classes stay under client/.
J. Compatibility: as in the lion treatment (a mod drawing its own tail through the hidden part shows nothing).
K. Performance: per frame, at most one tail part per horse in view (the single locus bounds it), same detail
   distance as horns.
L. Testing: as listed in piece 1; hide and offsets by eye with photograph-horses.
M. Release (owner, carried over): each piece counts toward the next release; no compat bump. The release
   procedure still confirms it.

Three likeliest failures for a real player:
1. Two tails or none on a mixed pen: the shared HD model's visible flag left from the previous horse
   (set it for every horse every frame; play check in a mixed pen).
2. A later tail added at the wrong rank quietly changes how existing carriers look (the test pins the full
   rank list; a rank change for an existing pair needs a Decided line).
3. A hidden Lt carrier looks like a horse with a normal tail once a higher tail exists (accepted; it is how
   dominance works, and the info screen can show the carried alleles).
