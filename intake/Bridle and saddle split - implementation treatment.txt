Bridle and saddle split - implementation treatment

Scoped with the owner in a cloud session (intake only: no code, wiki or asset changed). Every
"Decided" line is her call; "Recommended" lines are mine, not asked; "Open" lines still need an
answer, by whom is on the line; "UNVERIFIED" lines are facts this file has not proved. Source of
truth for what exists: wiki/horse-gear.html (Gameplay and Coding tabs, read as wiki/text/
horse-gear.*.txt) and wiki/philosophy.html ("utility, not decoration"). This file is retired
(deleted, per /process-intake step 8) when it ships and moves onto horse-gear's Gameplay and
Coding tabs. It was written AFTER the answers were given, from the owner's choices; the question
text itself is not preserved, so where an answer left a gap it is marked Open.
Release: not asked in this session. Recommended: patch, and because the split changes what an
existing saved item MEANS, say so in the release note. Open (owner): confirm with
/probe-a-scope or release step 1.

1. The ask, in one paragraph
============================
Split what the vanilla saddle does today (it both lets you sit on a horse and lets you steer it)
into two pieces of tack. A BRIDLE steers. A SADDLE is a new item of this mod's own that carries
stats. Below the top bond tier a horse that wears a saddle and no bridle can be ridden as a
passenger but not steered.

2. What already exists (read from wiki/text/horse-gear.*.txt on 2026-10-02)
==========================================================================
- Nineteen gear slots; two are real vanilla EquipmentSlots (SADDLE and BODY) and seventeen are a
  data attachment (HorseGear). A foal wears nothing.
- The slot roster is entity/HorseTackSlot. The Head zone has one slot, Headstall, documented as
  "A bridle or a halter - one or the other, never both"; the Body zone has Pad, Saddle, Barding,
  Blanket. Slot tags (horsegenetics:gear/<slot>) decide what a slot accepts; fifteen are empty.
- Today the Saddle slot is the vanilla one and takes the vanilla saddle. Hold a piece of gear and
  right-click the horse and it goes into the first empty slot that takes it (rider comfort).
- Gear must do something: "Cosmetic-only equipment is the thing this mod does not add", and tack
  carries real effects (philosophy, utility not decoration; that call reversed an earlier "tack
  gets no stats").
- A Tack Dyeing Bench already dyes tack; the saddle has a dye zone called "Bridle" today
  (UNVERIFIED which zones: read the Tack Dyeing Bench page and the saddle item).
- Bond tiers live on wiki/horse-care.html; the bond tier that counts as "tier 3" is read from
  there, not from this file (UNVERIFIED which constant names it).

3. Decided (owner)
==================
- A horse below bond tier 3 wearing a saddle and NO bridle: you may mount it, as a PASSENGER ONLY.
  You cannot steer it. (Same as vanilla bareback riding is today.)
- The saddle's job is STATS: it carries real stats the way the planned tack stats do (speed,
  jump, stamina and the like). It is not just a gate for the pad and saddlebags.
- Existing saves: EVERY VANILLA SADDLE BECOMES A BRIDLE, renamed and retextured. THEN this mod
  adds its own saddles as new items. So a world's existing saddles and saddled horses carry on
  steering (they are now bridles).
- The bridle is crafted from leather plus iron or string, and is dyed at the Tack Bench. The
  saddle's current "Bridle" dye zone moves to the bridle item, so the bench dyes bridle and saddle
  separately.

4. What the answers leave unsaid (Open, each needs the owner unless marked)
===========================================================================
These are the gaps a building session would otherwise guess at. None is decided.
A. Which slot does the bridle go in? Today a bridle belongs in Headstall, the vanilla saddle item
   goes in the vanilla SADDLE slot, and vanilla's own steering reads that SADDLE slot. If the
   vanilla saddle ITEM becomes the bridle, it still needs to live in a slot vanilla treats as
   "saddled" for steering to keep working, but the owner's gear model puts bridles in Headstall.
   Open (owner): is the bridle worn in Headstall (and the SADDLE slot is repurposed for our new
   saddle), or does the renamed item stay in the SADDLE slot until the saddle ships?
B. "Renamed and retextured" a vanilla item: does the item KEEP the id minecraft:saddle (only its
   name and texture change, by a resource override and a lang change) or become a new item of our
   own with a one-time conversion of every saved stack and worn piece? The first is invisible in
   saves; the second needs a migration (CLAUDE.md hard rule 10). Recommended: keep the id and
   relabel, because it needs no migration and the owner's wording ("renamed and retextured") says
   so. UNVERIFIED: that a client resource pack and our lang can rename and retexture an item
   that other mods, loot tables, villagers and the cowboy also produce. Cost to note: every
   vanilla-source saddle (loot, trades, other mods) is now a bridle; a pack with no recipe for
   "our saddle" has no saddle until the new item exists.
C. The craft recipe. Her answer: leather plus iron or string. Open (owner, small): exact counts
   and shape, and whether the recipe produces the old minecraft:saddle id (a bridle by B) so the
   old item is craftable for the first time. Recommended: yes, same id; shaped, leather x? with a
   string or iron nugget as the buckle. The building session picks counts, tuned by feel.
D. Which saddle stats, and how many. "Stats on the saddle" is the call; the numbers are not.
   Open (owner): which of speed, jump, stamina, comfort, carrying capacity; whether stats vary by
   a saddle's material tier; whether a saddle's stats stack with a pad's. This overlaps the
   tack-stats call on horse-gear's Roadmap tab; read it first and answer both together.
E. Where does our saddle come from? Crafting, loot, a cowboy trade? Not asked. Open (owner).
F. Tier 3 and above: is a saddle or a bridle needed to STEER at all, or does a fully bonded horse
   steer unbridled? The answer covers only "below tier 3". Recommended: a bonded horse (tier 3
   and up) steers with or without a bridle, as the wording "below tier 3" implies; a bridle then
   adds a smaller bonus (Open: what). Confirm with the owner.
G. A horse with a bridle and NO saddle: can you ride it, and does it steer? Not asked. Open
   (owner). Recommended: you cannot mount at all without a saddle (nothing to sit on), the
   same as today.
H. Passenger-only riding, mechanically. Vanilla steers a saddled horse with the rider's input;
   a passenger-only ride needs the horse to ignore rider input (like a vanilla unsaddled-but-
   tamed horse does not, which is not the same: UNVERIFIED how vanilla lets you sit on a tamed
   unsaddled horse in 26.1.2 and what controls steering). Read the vanilla horse sources before
   building; do not assume.
I. What a passenger-only horse does while ridden: stands still, wanders, follows the herd,
   panics? Not asked. Open (owner).
J. Carts, second seats and other mods. A cart or a second rider on the horse may need the
   bridle rule too. UNVERIFIED which of our carts mount through the saddle slot.
K. Donkeys and mules and other equids share the vanilla saddle path (AbstractMountInventoryMenu
   is shared). A rename of the vanilla item reaches them too. Open (owner): do the same rules
   apply to them, or horses only?
L. The bridle-and-halter rule: "a bridle or a halter, never both". The halter is still the other
   Headstall piece. If the bridle is the vanilla saddle item, the halter is unaffected. No
   change, said here so nobody assumes one.

5. Where it touches (implications, one line each)
=================================================
- Genetics, coat, breeds, pedigree: untouched.
- Gear: touches - HorseTackSlot accepts tags, slot meaning, the Gear tab hint text, the tag files.
- Items and crafting: touches - one new craft recipe (bridle), a new saddle item and its recipe
  or loot, lang keys, textures, the Tack Dyeing Bench zones.
- Riding, steering, bond: touches - the mount and steering rules, bond tier 3 as a threshold.
- Saves: touches - the rename is not a data change if the id is kept (B); if it is a new id, a
  migration is required. A saddled horse in an old world must still be ridable.
- Multiplayer: server decides steering; the client only draws the new texture.
- Config: Recommended one server switch to turn the passenger-only rule off, for servers that
  want the old behaviour. Open (owner).
- Other mods: touches - any mod that adds or reads saddles (see B).
- Wiki: wiki/horse-gear.html (both tabs), the Tack Dyeing Bench page, rider-comfort, the item
  page for each new item; a new saddle item gets its own page (add-an-item).

6. Order and size
=================
Do NOT build in one go. Recommended pieces:
  1. Rename and retexture the vanilla saddle as the bridle, and the dye zone move (small, no new
     logic, needs A and B answered).
  2. The passenger-only rule below tier 3, with the bridle as the steering item (the riding
     change; needs F, G, H, I answered).
  3. The new saddle item with stats (needs D and E answered, and the tack-stats call).
Size: piece 1 S, piece 2 M, piece 3 M-L. Each shippable alone, in that order.
