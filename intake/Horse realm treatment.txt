Horse Realm - Treatment
=======================

Purpose
=======
A safe, shared release field where players can leave surplus horses without
fear of harming them, and other players can find and tame them. This is a
player-facing feature and must be separate from the existing F6 debug realm.

Dimension separation
====================
- Create a new, technically distinct horse-realm dimension for the public
  experience. The existing debug realm remains a different dimension.
- The ordinary horse portal sends players to the new horse realm.
- F6 continues to send operators and players with command permission to the
  debug realm, including on production builds. Do not apply the public realm's
  terrain, spawning, time, ticking, breeding, or horse-protection rules to it.
- Scope every special rule to the new horse-realm dimension, never globally.

World layout and travel
=======================
- The realm is a bounded square of 1,000 by 1,000 chunks. The earlier
  description of an infinite field is superseded by this finite size.
- Use a flat plane at Y=0: bedrock at Y=-1, and one dirt layer at Y=0.
  Replace the dirt with water for each 2-by-2 pool in the 5-chunk grid.
- Place horse portals on a grid spaced 100 chunks apart inside the realm.
  These provide exits throughout the field so a player is not stranded far
  from an exit. A returning player goes back to the Overworld portal they used
  to enter.
- Enforce the perimeter with an invisible barrier; players and horses cannot
  leave the bounded field by crossing its edge.
- Keep it eternally day.
- The realm has no natural mob spawns. More strictly, no non-horse entity may
  spawn in this dimension by any spawn path. Horses are the only allowed
  spawns. The debug realm remains unrestricted.

Released horses
===============
- Add a freedom stick crafted from a feather, a stick, and one piece of horse
  hair. The exact recipe layout and horse-hair acquisition method remain
  implementation details to settle against the existing item system.
- Right-clicking a horse with the freedom stick while in the horse realm
  removes its ownership and makes it wild. It must be tamed again by the
  ordinary Minecraft horse process of riding and/or feeding it.
- Releasing a horse immediately restores it to full health.
- Preserve the horse's identity, genetics, and pedigree when unclaiming it.
  When a player tames it again, restore its mod record and assign ownership to
  the new player.
- All horses in the horse realm, including player-owned horses brought in,
  cannot take damage, die, or despawn while in that dimension. These guarantees
  are dimension-scoped; ordinary behavior resumes outside it.
- Horses can form herds. No other mob type spawns in the realm.

Breeding and simulation
=======================
- Breeding is enabled in the horse realm. This supersedes the earlier request
  that released horses not breed.
- Add a server-side realm breeding-rate config expressed as a whole percent,
  bounded from 0 to 100, with a default of 25. A value of 25 runs every
  reproduction timer at 0.25 times its normal rate (four times the duration).
  Apply the modifier to heat, conception retry/cooldown, and gestation timers;
  do not treat it as a 25% conception-success chance.
- A value of 0 pauses all reproduction, including progress of existing
  pregnancies. At nonzero values, pregnancies progress and mares give birth
  while the relevant horse and chunk are ticking.
- Horses residing in the realm may breed. Foals are born wild/unclaimed and
  receive the realm's protection while there.
- No horse ticks while the realm has no player in it. When at least one player
  is present, use normal loaded/ticking-chunk behavior; do not force-tick
  distant or unloaded horse chunks solely because the dimension is occupied.
  Thus an unclaimed pregnant mare still gives birth if she is ticking, but an
  inactive realm does not advance her pregnancy.

Implementation choices and checks
=================================
- Number realm chunks from 0 through 999. Anchor both grids at chunk (0, 0);
  place each portal or pool at its grid interval origin.
- If an entry portal has been removed, recreate it at its remembered location
  when safe; otherwise place it at the nearest safe location.
- Craft one freedom stick shapelessly from one feather, one stick, and one
  horse hair. Use the existing horse-hair source. The stick loses durability
  by one point on a successful release; failed attempts do not consume
  durability.
- Apply breeding-rate config changes immediately on the server.
- Test that spawn denial, time control, horse protection, and dormant ticking
  affect only the public horse realm, while F6 still enters the unrestricted
  debug dimension for authorized players.
