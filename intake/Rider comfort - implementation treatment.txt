4. Return the leads vanilla drops (deferred - a decision first, not a build)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
Do not build this in the same session as anything above. What ships when it
does: a snapped or hand-cut lead goes to the player's pack, not the ground.
- The page's fact: NeoForge 26.1.2 publishes no leash event and dropLeash spawns
  the item through Entity.spawnAtLocation, so there is nothing to hook. That is
  from the page, not re-proved here.
- Decided: a mixin at the drop (the repo already carries mixins, e.g.
  HorseAlwaysParentMixin). Not an EntityJoinLevelEvent filter on ItemEntity: it
  cannot tell a snapped lead from one a player threw, and would silently eat
  the thrown one. The building session still has to find the right seam - read
  Leashable.dropLeash / Entity.spawnAtLocation first, since dropLeash is an
  interface default and mixing into it is the unverified part.
- Decided: the lead goes to the player who PLACED it, even if they are far
  away or in another dimension. Not "the holder within reach". A lead on a
  fence knot has no player holder, so the placer must be RECORDED when the lead
  is attached (a small per-horse value: the attaching player's UUID, cleared
  when the lead comes off). Nothing records it today, so this is new state.
- Decided: if the placer is offline, in an unloaded place, or was never
  recorded (a horse leashed before this shipped), fall back to vanilla's drop
  on the ground. Never lose the lead, never hold it for later.
- Ship it with behaviour.leads_return covering it (the option already exists and
  its comment says the vanilla cases are excluded - update the comment).

Settled with the owner: what to do when an UNVERIFIED assumption is wrong
=========================================================================
Each of these answers a check the treatment above already marks UNVERIFIED. The
building session tries the assumption first; only if it fails does the fallback
below apply.
- Piece 1 (mounted mining): if the slowdown is not vanilla's "not on the ground"
  penalty, STOP AND REPORT. Do not ship a guess. Write what was found on the
  Roadmap tab and ask the owner before choosing another fix.
- Piece 1 config sync: the treatment assumes the server config syncs to clients
  (mining needs both sides). If it does not, SEND THE SWITCH IN A PAYLOAD on
  join, the way other synced data in this mod travels; do not make it a client
  config as well.

5. Fade the horse as you look down
-----------------------------------
What the player feels: the mount stops blocking the view of the ground.
- Decided: the horse NEVER goes fully invisible. Default floor 25% opacity.
- Decided: only the horse you are RIDING fades. Other horses stay solid.
- Decided: settings live in ClientConfig (visual, per player): enabled, minimum
  opacity (default 0.25), the pitch it starts fading at, the pitch at which it
  reaches the floor. Fade is a smooth ramp between the two pitches. No server
  config, no payload.
- Real unknown, unchanged: nothing here has ever drawn the generated coat at
  partial alpha. So the FIRST step is a spike, before any config is written:
  force a fixed alpha on the ridden horse's render state in
  GeneticHorseRenderer, and look at how the generated coat, the cutie-mark
  layer, the emissive layer and any worn gear draw under it (do they fade
  together, or leave a floating saddle or a solid glow?). Translucency also
  changes sort order and can show the horse's insides through itself; write the
  finding on the Roadmap tab.
- If the coat cannot be drawn cleanly at partial alpha: STOP AND REPORT. Do not
  ship a fade that looks broken; ask the owner (e.g. fade only the base coat, or
  drop the piece).
- Build last: it is the only piece with a rendering risk, and 1-4 do not depend
  on it.
