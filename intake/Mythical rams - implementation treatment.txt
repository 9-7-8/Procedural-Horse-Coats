Mythical rams - implementation treatment

Scoped with the owner. Every "Decided" line below is her call; "Recommended" lines are the scoping
session's, not asked; "Open" lines are still to be settled, by whom is said on the line; "UNVERIFIED" lines
are facts this file has not proved and the building session must check first. Source of truth for what
exists is wiki/gene-ram-horns.html, wiki/gene-ram-horn-form.html, wiki/gene-flying.html, wiki/model-parts.html,
wiki/breeds.html, wiki/breed-format.html and wiki/curly-coat.html. This file is retired (deleted, per START HERE
step 3) as each piece ships and moves onto those pages' Gameplay and Coding tabs.
Release: rides the next release (Decided). Applies when each piece ships; none cuts its own.
It sits beside "Mythical goats - implementation treatment.txt" (which appends the scimitar shape to the same gene
and builds goat-build.html), "Mythical equids - implementation treatment.txt" and "Mythical antlered beasts -
implementation treatment.txt". Rules carried from the audit: public domain only, no talking.


1. The request, as given
========================
"Now create a set of sheep/ram:" and a table of five rows. Reading: five horse breeds that are rams, in the way the
goats were built, plus a sheep build plan page (asked and confirmed).
  Chrysomallos          Greek     golden fleece; powerful ram; flies, carries Phrixus and Helle away; speed 10+; wings in accounts
                                  that call him winged; ram horns. Aries and the Golden Fleece ram are this one entry.
  Vegetable Lamb        medieval  white wool in one version; lamb-sized; grows on a stem at its navel, grazes only as far as the
                                  stalk reaches. An animal-plant hybrid; some accounts show several lambs as fruit.
  Criosphinx            Egyptian  no fixed colour (takes its material's); lion mane may be present, lion tail, lion paws not hooves;
                                  heavy, lion-bodied; ram head and curved horns on a lion's body; no human parts.
  Banebdjedet (ram form) Egyptian not specified; fleece; ram-bodied; four ram heads; only the animal-bodied form (the ram-headed man
                                  is excluded). Divine form of several ba-souls.
  Khnum (ram form)      Egyptian  not specified; fleece; ram; horizontally projecting, twisting horns in early depictions; only
                                  depictions as a complete ram (the ram-headed man is excluded).


2. In scope, and what is not
============================
In scope
 - Piece 1: a "horizontal twist" shape appended to Ram horn form (Khnum's horns). Decided.
 - Piece 2: five breeds, "all five, noting what each lacks". Decided. Banebdjedet, the Criosphinx and the Vegetable
   Lamb ship as their sheep or lamb half only.
 - Piece 3: sheep-build.html, a Roadmap-only plan page, separate from the deer and goat build pages. Decided.
Not in scope (all re-askable)

   and the page says flight and wings are one later pass (audit gap 1, wings).
 - A woolly look. Decided: gold and white coats now; the fleece is a plan (the Curly coat page), not built here.
 - Extra heads (Banebdjedet's four), the lion body (Criosphinx), the plant stem (Vegetable Lamb). Decided: noted as
   not drawn. Banebdjedet pins curled horns (Decided), and keeps four-horned for Jacob-type sheep.
 - The Vegetable Lamb's tether and stem. Decided: lore only, no AI goal and no part.
 - Any new ability. Recommended: none (the deer's rule); not asked.
 - Speech, none. Divine powers (Khnum's creation, Banebdjedet's souls): lore only.


3. What already exists, so the building session does not redo it
=================================================================
3a. Horns: RamHornsGene (Rh any sex, Rhm stallion only), RamHornFormGene "horsegenetics.ram_horn_form", PRIORITY 237,
    allele order Crl, Crk, Fhn, Scr (permanent, append only), dominance Fhn > Crl > Crk > Scr; RamHornGenerator shape
    constants CURL 0, CORKSCREW 1, FOUR 2, SCURS 3 with FORMS 4, CURLS 4, MAX_NODES 24; PartKind's baked count for RAM_HORN_RIGHT
    and RAM_HORN_LEFT is FORMS * CURLS (re-bake neoforge-26.1.2/tools/bake-part-sheet.mjs). Corkscrew is "long, straight and twisted,
    rising up and out" - up and out, not horizontal, so it is NOT Khnum's horn.
    The goats treatment appends a scimitar shape to this same list first. THE ORDER OF APPENDING MATTERS: allele order is permanent
    the moment a world uses it. Whichever of the two ships first takes the next slot; the other appends after it. Dominance is
    independent of order, so the two sessions do not conflict, but they must not run in one session.
3b. Flight: the data gene "flying" (wiki/gene-flying.html): glide dominant, true flight recessive (two copies). Paints nothing.
    Cloudtouched pins it. Wings are unbuilt (audit gap 1). Chrysomallos does NOT pin it (Decided).
3c. Breeds: breeds/not_a_sheep.json is the nearest precedent (magical, "pair" weights, ram horns pinned with Rhm, Rh, n). Not A Sheep
    exists and is NOT one of these rams: it is a very small ordinary-looking sheep, uncommon, sold. Do not touch its pool.
3d. Wool: the coat pipeline has no hair axis (wiki/curly-coat.html). Gold is the champagne, palomino and dunalino looks; white is the
    dominant whites and Grey.
Looks built but is not
  - Wings, extra heads, a lion body, a plant stem, a horizontal twist horn, a woolly coat, a "sheep build": none exists.
  - Four-horned (Fhn) is four horns, not four heads; it is a Jacob sheep's, not Banebdjedet's.
  - HornGenerator mentions a scimitar curve for the unicorn horn; irrelevant here.


4. The pieces
=============
Piece 1 - Khnum's horns: the horizontal twist
---------------------------------------------
What the player sees: a ram's horns that project sideways, flat and wide, twisting - Khnum's old horns - on either sex
 (Rh) or a stallion only (Rhm).
 Decided: append one shape to Ram horn form, not a new gene. Recommended name "Horizontal twist", token "Hzt" (a PROPOSAL; grep
   every existing allele token).
 Recommended: dominance below Crk and above Scr (Fhn > Crl > Crk > Hzt > Scr, adjusted if the goats' scimitar already sits in the
   series: Open, building session). Founder frequency about 0.04, taken from Crl's remainder.
 Recommended: shape is two roots leaving the skull sideways in a shallow twist, with the CURLS knob as the twist count.
   UNVERIFIED that MAX_NODES 24 allows it at every size.
 Where: common/ - RamHornFormGene (allele, expression text, founder table), RamHornGenerator (the shape, FORMS +1); NeoForge -
   part sheet re-bake.
 Data and saves: allele order permanent; append only; see 3a for the ordering with the goat scimitar.
 Sync: none new. Config: none.
 Tests: RamHornFormGene (the allele in the series and a pair), RamHornGenerator (nodes inside the budget at every curl and size),
   PartGeneratorTest, EditorRules parity; the two goldens move by KEY ONLY (horns paint nothing).
 Play checks: the horns on both sexes, a foal, both sizes, a halter and a saddle on, against the other shapes; the horns clear the
   ears; the horns are not hidden inside a halter.
 Docs: gene-ram-horn-form.html (Gameplay shapes table, Coding allele order, Science), gene-ram-horns.html, model-parts.html if
   the sheet changes.

Piece 2 - The five breeds
-------------------------
What the player sees: five sheep-like horses in the wild and as spawn eggs, each with a breed-book entry that tells the myth in
 fresh words; three say plainly what is not drawn.
 Decided: all five are breeds, kind magical, "wild" and "spawn_egg" only (as the deer and goats). Commonness very_rare for
   Chrysomallos, the Vegetable Lamb, Khnum and Banebdjedet; rare for the Criosphinx (a type). Rides the next release.
 Every row is a PROPOSAL for review; biome lists, stats and sizes were NOT asked. Every founder carries the expressing allele;
 every pool pins ram_horns and ram_horn_form. Weights come from the breed designer's output, not by hand.
 Chrysomallos (very_rare)
   biomes: minecraft:plains, minecraft:sunflower_plains, minecraft:meadow, minecraft:savanna
   stats: speed 10, jump 6, health 7; size standard. Coat: gold (champagne, palomino, dunalino, full golds), no spots. Horns: Rh,
   Crl (a powerful ram's, ordinary shape), gold-leaning tint. No flight, no wings (Decided). UNVERIFIED that speed 10 is
   reachable in BreedStatCurve. Page: Aries and the Golden Fleece ram are one animal; the wings and flight are not built.
 Vegetable Lamb (very_rare)
   biomes: minecraft:plains, minecraft:meadow, minecraft:sunflower_plains, minecraft:windswept_hills
   stats: speed 2, jump 2, health 3; size very small (the low end of the shipped range, below Not A Sheep). Coat: white (the dominant
   whites), some creamy. Horns: none (a lamb); no ram_horns pinned beyond the polled pair. Page: lore only for the stem and the
   tether; not rooted, not tethered, no stem drawn (Decided).
 Criosphinx (rare; SHEEP HEAD OF A SPHINX, NOT DRAWN)
   biomes: minecraft:desert, minecraft:badlands, minecraft:savanna
   stats: speed 6, jump 4, health 9; size large. Coat: sandstone tones (the whole pool, with tans, golds and dun weighted). Horns: Rh,
   Crl. Notes: the lion body, lion tail and paws are not drawn (audit gaps 2, 7); it reads as a heavy ram-horned horse until they
   are. Open (building session): a mane (a lion's) once the mane-and-tail shape gene ships.
 Banebdjedet (very_rare; ONE HEAD ONLY)
   biomes: minecraft:river, minecraft:swamp, minecraft:beach
   stats: speed 4, jump 3, health 7; size standard. Coat: not specified; Recommended whole pool with whites and browns weighted.
   Horns: Rh, Crl pinned (Decided: curled; four-horned is for Jacob-type sheep). The four heads are not drawn (audit gap 6).
 Khnum (very_rare)
   biomes: minecraft:desert, minecraft:river, minecraft:savanna, minecraft:badlands
   stats: speed 4, jump 3, health 7; size standard. Coat: not specified; Recommended whole pool. Horns: Rh with the Hzt allele pinned
   (Piece 1) - the horn design is the reason the row qualifies. Waits on Piece 1.
 Common checks: cold share of each biome list against the climate spec (none of these is cold, so none gains cold tolerance);
   every biome on data/horsegenetics/neoforge/biome_modifier/add_horse_herds.json (UNVERIFIED for swamp, river and beach: all
   are listed in the biome-assignments file but confirm).
 Where: breed JSON under common/src/main/resources/horsegenetics/breeds/; breeds.html and breed-book.html (a Rams section).
 Data and saves: breed ids are permanent once a world has horses of them.
 Tests: *BreedsTest per breed; BreedFilesTest; BreedBandsReachTest for a band on a knob no pinned pair reads.
 Play checks: each breed in a yard pen; a cross of two (a cross label per breeds.html); a wild herd in each biome; the three
   half-drawn breeds read as sheep and not as unfinished horses.
 Docs: breeds.html, breed-book.html, the breed census; check-breed-heights.mjs for hand-typed heights.

5. Decisions (anything not inside a piece)
===========================================
A. The ask: five breeds and a plan page. Smallest worthwhile version: Chrysomallos and the Vegetable Lamb (no new gene).
B. Fit: horses-only, vanilla intact, carrot not stick; horns are grown parts that do nothing. A concept is a breed; the
   horizontal shape is an allele because any horned horse may wear it. Does not bend a settled decision.
C. Meeting it: wild herds and spawn egg. Tutorial text none; refusal text none.
D. Genetics: one appended allele (Piece 1). No new epigenome ranges. No sex linkage change. Not a lethal, no health effect. Both
   editors (rule 5): rebake the wasm and run check-designer-boots.mjs; check the editor's shape list.
E. Breeds: five new; Not A Sheep is untouched.
F. Items: none. Carrot: Open (building session) whether the new allele needs a gene-carrot row (read making-a-gene.html#contracts).
G. Config: none. H. Saves: tokens and allele order permanent. I. Multiplayer: nothing to authorise.
J. Compatibility: the biome analogues carry these herds to modded biomes automatically.
K. Performance: one shape in a baked sheet; no tick work.
L. Tests: see the pieces. M. Release: rides the next release (Decided).

Three likeliest ways it goes wrong for a real player
  1. A half-drawn breed reads as an unfinished horse. Handled by the page and the play check.
  2. The two appended horn shapes (this and the goats') land in a different order than a world expects. Handled by the ordering rule
     in 3a: the second to ship appends, never reorders.
  3. Chrysomallos is expected to fly. Decided; the page and the Verification tab say wings and flight are one later pass.
