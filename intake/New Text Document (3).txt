```
ELEMENT   The sprays of fire in the boulder-opal reference (image_1_.svg) —
          a scatter of fans at unrelated origins across the stone.

SHIPPED   Two FAN layers at hand-placed origins (body u 0.60/0.47, v ~0.34),
          sharing spacing, duty and twist so they read as one kind of fire.
          What a viewer sees that is wrong: the sprays land in the same two
          spots on every horse carrying the gene, and there are two of them
          rather than a field. The reference's density — a dozen sprays,
          unevenly clustered, some clipped by the edge of the stone — is not
          there. Adding more layers does not fix it; by the fourth they
          overlap into a wash and the shared-origin reading is lost.

WHY NOT   FAN measures the angle around ONE pivot, given as originU/originV.
          Every field mask in the vocabulary that does have a lattice
          measures a distance — SPOTS and DAPPLES to a cell centre, CRACKLE
          to a wall or (with measure=centroid) out from a centroid. None of
          them measures an ANGLE around the cell's own centre, so there is no
          arrangement of them that gives per-cell bars sharing a per-cell
          origin. Multiplying FAN by SPOTS does not reach it either: that
          clips one global fan into cell-shaped pieces, so every piece keeps
          the global pivot and the bars point the wrong way everywhere except
          at the pivot. This is the same shape of gap that CRACKLE
          measure=centroid closed for radial coordinates, one axis over.

PROPOSAL  A new PARAMETER on FAN rather than a new mask.

          FAN gains:
            origin   "fixed" | "cell"   default "fixed"
            scale    float              default 5.0   (cell spacing, body units)
            seed     seed               (lattice seed)
            vary     float 0..1         default 0.4   (per-cell rotation of the
                                                       fan's zero, fraction of
                                                       one full turn)

          At "fixed" it is exactly today's mask and nothing changes. At
          "cell" the mask walks the same lattice SPOTS uses, finds the centre
          of the cell the texel falls in, and measures the angle and the
          radius from THAT point instead of from originU/originV; spacing,
          duty, twist, inner, outer and softness all keep their present
          meanings, now per cell. Coverage 1 is on a bar, coverage 0 is in the
          gap between bars or outside inner/outer.

          Closest existing mask: FAN itself. The one thing it does that FAN
          cannot: give every cell of a lattice its own pivot, so a field of
          independent sprays is one layer rather than one layer per spray.
          Sharing seed and scale with a SPOTS or CRACKLE mask on the same
          numbers would make the sprays concentric with that field, which is
          rule 14's existing idiom rather than a new one.
```
