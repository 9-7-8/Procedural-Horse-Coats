1. The request, as given
========================
"A useful sheep breeding simulator should have two layers: a small set of loci with fairly well-established,
sometimes unusual inheritance, and a quantitative layer for traits such as height, weight, horn dimensions, and
body proportions." Plus the research's horns-and-body table: RXFP2 (horned/scurred/polled, sex-influenced), HOXD1
(polyceraty), NCAPG-LCORL (size, quantitative), MSTN (muscle, variant-specific), the callipyge region (polar
overdominance, parent of origin), PDGFD and BMP2 (fat tail), TBXT and HOXB13 (tail length), FGF5 and IRF2BP2
(coat architecture). "For ordinary body shape - leg length, chest depth, rump width, face profile, adult height,
and growth rate - start with polygenic scores plus age, sex, nutrition, and random variation."
Reading, confirmed: ALL THREE layers (coat, horns/body, disease) go into the plan; only the coat layer is built
first (Decided). This file is therefore mostly a plan with two small buildable pieces and a flagged unverified one.


2. In scope, and what is not
============================
In scope
 - Piece 1: reconcile the existing horn genes with the research (RXFP2 sex-influence, scurs, polyceraty) - what
   already matches, what is a gap. No new horn gene unless the audit finds one.
 - Piece 2: quantitative sheep body-frame effects as natural-sheep genes that nudge the existing resolved stats and
   size, with no new stat and no new part.
 - Piece 3: the callipyge/imprinting plan - parent of origin - as a Roadmap-only plan plus the unverified check.
Not in scope (all re-askable)
 - A sheep build gene, a woolly model, a fat tail or a short tail drawn on the model (parts are a separate system;
   the sheep-build.html plan page owns that).
 - Fleece length and hairy-versus-woolly (FGF5, IRF2BP2): needs the fleece axis (curly-coat.html).
 - Disease alleles: the disorders treatment.
 - Sheep breeds, a sheep mob.


3. What already exists, so the building session does not redo it
=================================================================
3a. Horns: RamHornsGene (Rh for any sex, Rhm stallion only) and RamHornFormGene "horsegenetics.ram_horn_form",
    PRIORITY 237, allele order Crl, Crk, Fhn, Scr (permanent, append only), dominance Fhn > Crl > Crk > Scr;
    RamHornTipGene; RamHornGenerator shapes CURL, CORKSCREW, FOUR, SCURS with FORMS 4, CURLS 4, MAX_NODES 24
    (from the rams treatment; re-read the code). Both horn genes are MAGICAL (isNatural() false) today.
    The wiki says Rh/Rhm are "that difference as two alleles" - the sheep sex-difference - and "does not borrow its
    name" (gene-ram-horns.html#real-sheep, science tab).
    NOTE: the Mythical goats and rams treatments both append a shape to this same list; whichever ships first
    takes the next slot (see them).
3b. Four-horned (Fhn) and scurred (Scr) already exist as horn forms. They are not HOXD1 polyceraty and not RXFP2
    scurs - they are magical shape alleles. Polyceraty, horned-at-all and scurred must be "separate variables"
    (research); today Rh/Rhm decides horned-at-all and the form gene decides shape.
3c. Body stats: speed, health, jump, size resolve from genes; the "stat curve" and resolved-not-rolled stats are
    wiki/horse-body.html and wiki/breeding.html. Natural performance genes live in the NATURAL_HEALTH family and
    "paint nothing" (GeneFamily; Genes.influencesCoat). Magical body-stat genes are AbstractMagicStatGene,
    MagicSizeGene, MagicSpeedGene, MagicHealthGene - far wider ranges, no real-world claim.
3d. The epigenome: literal named numbers on EACH allele copy, inherited with the allele and slightly drifted each
    breeding (CLAUDE.md; genetics-model.html). Horn size, curl, thickness and shade are already epigenome numbers on
    the horn alleles. A quantitative "horn dimension" is therefore not a new mechanism.
3e. Inheritance: breeding stores one AllelePair per gene; whether it remembers which parent gave which copy is
    UNVERIFIED (read the pair class and the breeding code). Callipyge needs it.
3f. Sex handling: the sex gene (horsegenetics.sex) and EditorRules.enforceSexLinkage; Rhm is the existing
    "stallion only" precedent for a sex-influenced horn.
3g. The three build plans: sheep-build.html is created by "Mythical rams" Piece 3, deer-build.html by "Mythical
    deer" Piece 5, goat-build.html by the goats. All are Roadmap-only pages and none exists yet.
Looks built but is not
  - Any body-proportion system (leg length, chest depth, rump width, face profile). Body space exists
    (HorseSkinGeometry, the vanilla model tables) but there is no build gene.
  - Any fat-tail, short-tail, tail-skeleton or muscle visual; any polygenic score; parent-of-origin; sheep sex-
    influenced scurs beyond what Rhm gives.
  - "Natural" horn genes: both horn genes are magical.


4. The pieces
=============
Piece 1 - The horn audit and the sheep sex-influence
-----------------------------------------------------
What the player sees: possibly nothing; the sheep horn rules the owner wrote are matched against what ships, and any
 real gap is closed with the smallest change.
 Decided: honest hybrid; RXFP2 is sex-influenced and breed-dependent, NOT "horns dominant". The research's Soay model
   (Ho+/Ho+ females horned, Ho+/HoP females scurred, HoP/HoP females polled; male heterozygotes horned, HoP/HoP
   males horned or scurred) is a MODEL, named as one.
 Recommended: do not touch RamHornsGene or RamHornFormGene (allele order is permanent). Write the audit as a table
   on the horn pages' Science tab: Rh = horned either sex, Rhm = stallion-only, Scr = scurs, Fhn = four horns,
   against the research. A real gap (for example "heterozygous females scurred") becomes an Open owner question,
   NOT an edit.
 Open (owner): does the owner want a NATURAL-SHEEP RXFP2 locus beside the magical Rh/Rhm (a sex-influenced
   three-allele gene, Ho+/HoP/Hos, with a stochastic horn-length knob)? Recommended: no for now - the magical pair
   already is the sheep difference; a second locus would give two horn genes disagreeing. Re-askable.
 Open (owner): HOXD1 polyceraty. Recommended: model as a dominant natural-sheep allele that selects Fhn (four-
   horned) form when horns are present, with horn NUMBER separate from horned-at-all; the eyelid defect is the
   disorders treatment's, as a risk not a given. UNVERIFIED that the form gene can be driven by another gene
   without a pair of its own (read the dependsOn mechanism in Gene).
 Where: wiki prose (Science tabs), and common/ only if the owner says yes to either Open above.
 Tests: none for the audit; if a gene is added, as in the coat treatment (golden key only: horns paint nothing).
 Play checks: a ram, a ewe and a wether carrying each horn allele - horns present or absent as the table says.
 Docs: gene-ram-horns.html and gene-ram-horn-form.html Science tabs, model-parts.html if anything is added.

Piece 2 - The quantitative layer: frame, muscle, size
------------------------------------------------------
What the player sees: sheep-lineage horses are measurably a little taller or heavier, more muscular, or shorter-
 legged, by amounts that vary with the whole genome rather than one gene - with no new stat on the screen.
 Decided: start with polygenic scores plus age, sex, nutrition and random variation (the research's own advice);
   named regions are small contributions, "no universal dominant/recessive rule", never "has the large allele means a
   large adult".
 Recommended: reuse the existing resolved-not-rolled stats and the size gene's range; each sheep body locus is a
   NATURAL_HEALTH-family natural-sheep gene whose alleles add to the existing stat curve inputs with a small effect
   size. No new stat, no new part. The effect is on stats, not a drawn proportion (that is the build gene's
   plan page).
 Loci (PROPOSALS, evidence level on the page): an NCAPG-LCORL-style frame/size region (quantitative, additive);
   MSTN (variant-specific, a Texel-style muscle gain with reduced fat; never a congenital illness - the research
   says so); PDGFD and BMP2 fat-tail (a separate "tail fat" number, not tail skeleton); TBXT and HOXB13 tail length
   (effect sizes, not "one recessive short-tail"). Of these only the first two have an obvious existing hook
   (size, and a muscle/health-adjacent stat); fat tail and tail length have NO existing visual hook (3 above), so
   they are PLAN entries unless the owner wants a stat-only version.
 Open (owner): which of the four loci to build now. Recommended: the frame/size region and MSTN muscle only.
 Open (owner): does muscle map onto speed, health, or a new "muscle" stat? Recommended: none new; muscle shifts
   health a little and speed slightly downward, since no new stat is wanted.
 Open (building session): the cap. The existing horse-body wiki owns what a stat can be; keep the effect inside it.
 Where: common/ - natural-sheep genes (Java, as stat genes are, since data genes "paint"); breed stat bands
   (BreedStatCurve, UNVERIFIED how a gene's effect enters it); wiki.
 Data and saves: keys and allele order permanent; effect sizes are tuning and may change without breaking a save.
 Config: none. Sync: stats already sync; UNVERIFIED that nothing new does.
 Tests: a stat test that the effect is within its band for every genotype; that two sheep-frame alleles are additive;
   that a horse with no sheep genes has identical stats; BreedBandsReachTest if a breed band reads the new knob.
 Play checks: stats of a no-gene horse vs the same horse with each allele; a foal and an adult.
 Docs: horse-body.html (Coding), gene pages (three tabs), gene-census.html.

Piece 3 - Parent of origin: the callipyge plan
----------------------------------------------
What the player sees: nothing; a plan on the page.
 Decided: plan only, flagged UNVERIFIED. The classic callipyge phenotype needs the mutant copy to come from the SIRE
   with the dam supplying the normal copy - polar overdominance - so "an unordered pair of alleles" is not enough.
 Open (building session): read the pair class and the breeding code. Does an offspring AllelePair keep which parent
   gave which copy? If yes, callipyge is a gene whose expression tests the origin. If no, it needs a save-format
   change (a large, owner-approved piece, never silently).
 Recommended: put the plan on a Roadmap tab (data-tab="roadmap", one sentence in a note roadmap-summary box) of the
   page that owns heredity (genetics-model.html), per rule 9, with the unverified check on its Verification tab.
   NOT a new page unless the subject has none.
 Tests/Play checks: none until built. Test when built: the sire's mutant copy gives the phenotype, the dam's does
   not, and a reciprocal cross differs.
 Docs: genetics-model.html Roadmap and Verification tabs, then bake-roadmap-index.mjs and bake-verification-index.mjs.

B. Fit: bends horses-only (owner, kind treatment). Horns and stats are existing systems; no new system. Carrot not

D. Genetics: natural-sheep kind. Magical vs natural: the existing horn genes stay magical; do not duplicate them.
   Epigenome: horn numbers exist; frame/muscle effect sizes may ride the allele copy. Sex linkage: the horn

E. Breeds: none (Decided). F. Items: none. G. Config: none. H. Saves: permanence as above; the callipyge piece may

J. Compatibility: none. K. Performance: stats resolve at breed/birth, not per tick.
L. Tests: see pieces. M. Release: section 8.

Three likeliest ways it goes wrong for a real player
  1. Sheep stat genes quietly make every sheep-lineage horse faster or bigger (power creep). Handled: small, bounded
     effect sizes; the "no-gene horse identical" test; muscle trades speed down.
  2. A second horn locus disagrees with Rh/Rhm. Handled: Piece 1 recommends no second locus.
  3. Polyceraty or an eyelid defect is applied to every four-horned horse. Handled: polyceraty is a separate allele;
     the eyelid risk is the disorders treatment and conditional.
