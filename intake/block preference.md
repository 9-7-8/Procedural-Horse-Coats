This gene creates a preference towards certain blocks or areas in a horse. The designer of the allele can define how strong this preference is, up to and including taking damage if they cannot reach it. 
By default, this gene ships with these alleles, which are recessive:
Water: horse prefers to always be in/near water. They will walk towards water and stand in it if they can, but won't push against fences and won't jump. 
Lava: same as water, but with lava. 
Underwater: the horse will attempt to submerge itself. This could lead to drowning if the horse does not also have the water breathing gene. This is intended. This allele is only meant to be used in aquatic breeds. 
Tree: horse will attempt to get on top of the nearest tree. If the horse cannot fly, this will probably not do much. 
n: wildtype, no effect.
