Troughs and salt licks - implementation treatment

OWNER'S ANSWERS, 2026-10-02 (these override any "Open" line below; also on wiki/troughs.html#plan-owner-answers)
- Shape: ANY connected set is one trough (L, ring, mixed woods); joins by adjacency only.
- Rung: just above HAY, below FAVOURITE and CAKE. A hay bale in a trough counts as HAY (80).
- Empty in-stall trough: the horse stays put until its hunger hits ZERO, then forages (may leave).
- Hoppers: allowed.
- Salt: no new salt item or ore. The lick is CRAFTED FROM SAND (sand stands in for salt), ALSO a
  cowboy trade row and a loot find, AND any other mod's salt is accepted via a tag (support, not add).
- Lick wear: per lick tick-window, so a herd wears it down faster.
- Still open: the lick's heal amount/duration and capacity per segment (building session's numbers).

Scoped with the owner. Every "Decided" line below is her call; "Open" lines are
still to be settled by the building session. One piece per session per START
HERE. Source of truth for what exists is wiki/troughs.html (Roadmap tab only
today); this file is the handoff for the work that tab lists, and it is
retired (deleted, per /process-intake step 8) as each piece ships and moves onto
that page's new Gameplay and Coding tabs.

What already exists, so the building session does not redo it
==============================================================
- HungerFoodGoal (server/) already walks a hungry horse to the best food
  within 10 blocks, in Hunger.Food's declared order (common/care/Hunger):
  FAVOURITE, DROPPED, CAKE, HAY, CROP, GRASS, MOSS, MUSHROOM, FLOWER. Each
  rung is one of: a dropped ItemEntity, or a block found by search() and
  eaten by eatBlock(). A trough is a NEW RUNG, not a new goal.
- It already answers "can the horse get its mouth to it" (canReach plus
  exposed(), the fence check). A trough must go through the same two checks,
  not bypass them.
- HayBales (compat/) is the precedent for "any mod's version works": a
  block+item tag, horsegenetics:hay_bales, and no Java per mod. Do the same
  for troughs (tag below).
- DietFoods.accepts(diet, stack) and acceptsFromGround(diet, stack) already
  decide what a given diet may eat from a hand and from the ground. The
  trough takes from stacks through acceptsFromGround, so a wheat-eater, a
  carrot-eater and a cake-eater each take what they would take from the
  floor, and a horse that eats nothing never looks.
- StallDetector (server/) plus StallFill (common/stable/) already work out a
  stall's volume from its sign, and refuse rather than guess. That is how a
  trough is found "inside a stall".
- compat/MaterialScan and ModdedMaterials.woods() serve double gates, jumps
  and carts from one roster: "any wood, vanilla or modded" has three
  precedents. Follow bake-jumps.mjs / compat/GeneratedJumps, and remember the
  CLAUDE.md warning: the per-wood asset shape is written TWICE (author-time
  bake for vanilla woods, run-time compat/Generated* for other mods'), and a
  drift shows as a modded purple cube and logs nothing.
- UNVERIFIED, check first: this mod appears to have no real BlockEntity type
  and no block with a container of its own (the BlockEntity hits in server/
  are debug tooling, and EquestrianBenchMenu is a menu, not a block entity).
  Confirm that before designing storage; if it is true, piece 1 introduces
  the mod's first block entity and the first item-holding block, and the
  save/reload check below is not optional.
- No salt item exists (grep for "salt" in java, data and lang finds nothing
  relevant). Piece 3 adds one.

Three pieces. Smallest blast radius first, each shippable alone.

1. One trough block, plain wood, that a horse eats from
-------------------------------------------------------
What the player sees: a wooden trough, in every vanilla wood and any mod's
wood, that they fill by hand with food, and a hungry horse walks up to it and
eats a mouthful at a time.
- Decided: FILLED WITH FOOD ITEMS, EATEN PER MOUTHFUL. Not an infinite feeder
  and not a hay-bale counter. Right-click with food to add it; one fill feeds
  many horses over days. The diet-specific horses must get something from it,
  which is why it holds items and not a bale count.
- Decided: it holds only what some diet can eat from the ground
  (DietFoods.acceptsFromGround true for at least one HorseDiet), so it cannot
  become a general chest. Anything else is refused on insert, with the
  ordinary "that will not go in" feedback.
- Decided: a horse takes a stack it may eat, one mouthful, and the mouthful
  restores what the same item does as DROPPED food, so a trough is the same
  feed on a different shelf and adds no new balance number. Open: whether a
  hay bale in a trough restores the HAY rung's 80 or is broken into wheat's
  worth; read Hunger.Food and DietFoods.WHEAT_ITEMS first and pick whichever
  makes the fewest special cases.
- Decided: it is a NEW RUNG in Hunger.Food, sought like the others. Open, and
  it is the one number to settle: where it sits. The natural reading is above
  HAY (a stocked trough is better than a lone bale, which a pen "does not
  keep") but below FAVOURITE and CAKE only if the horse's favourite is in it.
  Decide by playing a pen with all three, not by argument.
- Decided: it joins horsegenetics:horse_food (block tag), the way the bale tag
  does, so gated healing beside food and the herd's pasture search read a
  stocked trough as pasture. An EMPTY trough must not count: the tag says
  "block is a feeder", so the check in code is "and it holds a mouthful for
  this horse", not the tag alone. Get this wrong and herds camp at an empty
  trough forever.
- Decided: other mods' trough-alikes join by tag, block and item, named
  horsegenetics:troughs, with no Java per mod, exactly as hay_bales did. The
  catch is the same as the bale's: a foreign block has no inventory this mod
  can read, so a tag member is treated as a permanent "bale-like" source only
  if the building session finds a safe generic reading (a Container / item
  handler capability). Open: if there is none, the tag ships for OUR
  troughs' wood variants only and the compat half is a Roadmap line, not a
  promise.
- Decided: PLAIN WOOD ONLY. No dye, no paint. The Roadmap tab's "extract the
  shared dye-reading code" is due, but it is its own piece with the cart and
  jump bench, not part of this one.
- Eating uses up what it takes (as everything else in HungerFoodGoal does).
  The horse takes from the trough on the server tick only; gate on
  EventHooks.canEntityGrief? NO - taking from a container is not griefing,
  and a horse must still eat with mob griefing off. Say so in a comment.
- Cost. HungerFoodGoal already carries a warning from the day it slowed the
  server by a third. search() runs per hungry horse per SEARCH_INTERVAL. Do
  NOT scan every block entity in range: keep a per-level index of loaded
  troughs (updated on load/unload/place/break) and test only those within
  SEARCH_RADIUS. Add the timing check before the slice is called done.
- Hook: search() gets a rung that asks the index for the nearest reachable
  stocked trough for THIS diet, runs canReach, and eatBlock gains a branch
  that pulls one mouthful from the trough instead of removing a block.
  tick() must drop the pursuit if the trough empties or is broken en route.
- Ships with: block + item for every wood, blockstate/model/item/recipe/loot
  from a bake tool written on the jumps pattern, lang keys, a recipe that
  check-recipes.mjs accepts, an advancement only if a ProgressTask is added
  (do not add one for this).
- Test at the boundary: common/ gets the rule that decides "does this stack
  feed this diet, and how much" (it should already be DietFoods; if it is
  not in common, do not move it in this slice). Gametest: fill a trough, a
  hungry horse eats from it, the count goes down by one, an empty trough is
  not a target, a horse with a diet that refuses the contents ignores it, a
  trough behind a fence is not a target until a gate opens.
- In-game matrix (record on the page's Verification tab, unplayed): stocked
  vs empty; two horses at one trough; a horse that is not hungry and passes
  it; a modded wood; save and reload with a half-full trough; break it with
  contents (drops them - never voids items).

2. Rectangles: connected segments share one inventory
-----------------------------------------------------
- Decided: EACH BLOCK IS A SEGMENT; ADJACENT SEGMENTS JOIN INTO ONE TROUGH.
  Not a placed frame plus a marker block, and not a fixed-size multiblock.
  Any square or rectangular footprint the player lays down, in any wood.
- Decided: breaking one segment splits the trough. The contents are shared
  out to what remains by capacity, and anything that no longer fits is
  DROPPED at the broken block. Items are never voided or duplicated.
- The hard part is the union: "what counts as one trough" is a connectivity
  question over placed blocks and belongs in common/ (a pure function over a
  set of positions: components, and which shapes are valid), where it gets
  tests, on the StallFill pattern - StallDetector translates, StallFill
  decides. Read StallDetector's "It used to never refuse" section first: its
  bug was a fallback that turned every failure into a plausible success.
  Open: is an L-shape or a ring one trough, or refused? A rectangle is the
  ask; recommend accept any connected set, since refusing a shape after the
  player has built it is the worse surprise. Settle it before writing the
  function, not after.
- Mixed woods in one trough: Open. Recommend joins are by connectivity only
  (wood is cosmetic), which also spares a "which wood wins" rule.
- Capacity scales with segment count; the number is the building session's to
  pick and belongs in ServerConfig only if it is worth tuning, per Config
  page's rule that a config option is expression or frequency, never
  registration.
- Rendering: the fill level shows on the block (a model state, not a block
  entity renderer, if it can be done that way).

3. Owner-facing polish, the stall link, and the salt lick
----------------------------------------------------------
3a. Fill readout (owner-facing)
- Decided: the owner can see how full a trough is. Smallest form: a message
  on right-click with an empty hand, or a comparator-style state; not a new
  screen.

3b. A trough in a stall is where a stalled horse eats first
- Decided: a trough INSIDE a stall's volume (as StallDetector/StallFill
  computes it from the stall sign) is the first thing a horse standing in
  that stall tries to eat from, ahead of every other rung, and it does not
  path outside the stall to eat. Not "near the sign".
- Open: what a stalled horse does when the in-stall trough is empty. The
  default already in the goal (go and look elsewhere) walks a stalled horse
  out through a gate, which is the exact opposite of what the stall is for.
  Recommend: an empty in-stall trough means the horse stays put and hungry,
  and the OWNER gets one throttled notice (per the "report a refusal once per
  meaningful attempt, not once per tick" rule in
  /process-intake step 6). Hunger never hurts a horse in this mod, so staying hungry costs
  healing, not health.
- Gate: the stall must be resolved once per horse per search, not per tick,
  and never by scanning the world. Reuse the cached stall record if there
  is one before adding another.

3c. Salt lick
- Decided: A PLACED BLOCK WITH FINITE USES, REFILLED WITH SALT. Not
  permanent. It needs a salt item (new) and a recipe; the Roadmap's philosophy
  line governs the rest: it grants something, and its absence punishes
  nothing. A horse that has never seen a lick is exactly as before.
- Decided: THE REWARD IS FASTER HEALING while the horse has licked recently.
  Open: how much and for how long; keep it in the same units as
  Hunger.HEAL_PER_SECOND (a multiplier on the heal rate for a window of
  ticks, plus a small refund of hunger so it is not free healing off
  nothing). A hurt-and-hungry horse must still be unable to heal from a lick
  alone: "a starving horse cannot heal at all" stands.
- The lick is a GOAL, not a rung: it is not food. A horse below full health,
  or that has not licked recently, walks to a reachable lick with a low
  priority, licks, and resumes. It is a separate small goal beside
  HungerFoodGoal, and it needs an entry in the behaviour hierarchy: after
  adding it, run node wiki/tools/bake-behaviour-hierarchy.mjs.
- It arbitrates with the rest, and the hardening matrix applies: a failed
  route relinquishes movement (see roadmap-execution "AI arbitration"), it
  loses to panic, low-health escape, bond following and breeding, and it
  never keeps a horse standing still because a path was refused.
- Open: does the lick's finite use count per mouthful licked or per horse
  visit? Recommend per lick tick-window, so a herd wears it down faster than
  one horse, which is the natural husbandry loop and the reason to refill.
- Open: where the salt comes from. Options for the building session to bring
  to the owner as ONE question: a craftable from an existing item, a
  cowboy trade row, a loot-table find. Do not decide it in code.

Gate before piece 1
====================
roadmap-execution.html says troughs come "after feeding rules are fixed;
build the trough as the first world feeding block". The feeding fixes it means
(dropped diet food, crops through barriers, pasture-edge pushing, low-health
escape) are the "breeding, food and boundary corrections" pass. Check the
Verification tab of horse-care.html and wiki/verification.html for that pass
FIRST. If the fence/reach behaviour is still unproven in-game, the trough
inherits it: a horse that leans on a fence beside a trough looks exactly like
a broken trough. Record the result and stop if the gate is open; that is a
finding, not a failure.

Out of scope, deliberately
==========================
Dye and paint (needs the shared extraction). Automatic feeders and hoppers as a
feature (only the Open decision above). Watering troughs and a water source
(horse_water already exists; a drinking trough is a different piece).
Animania-style husbandry chores of any kind: hunger never hurts a horse here
and a lick that is absent punishes nothing.
