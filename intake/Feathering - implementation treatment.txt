Feathering - implementation treatment

Scoped with the owner. Every "Decided" line below is her call; "Recommended" lines are mine, not
asked; "UNVERIFIED" lines are facts this file has not proved and the building session must check
first. Source of truth for what exists is wiki/model-parts.html (hoof feathering is on its Roadmap
tab) and the unicorn horn and antler gene pages. This file is the handoff for ONE gene that grows
feathering - long hair hanging from each fetlock, as on a Clydesdale or a Friesian. It sits beside
"Head parts", "Body parts" and "Tusks gene" and follows the same pattern. It is retired (deleted, per
/process-intake step 7) when it ships and moves onto the gene page's Gameplay and Coding tabs.

Scope
=====
The ask was "all the feet parts". Decided, from her choices: FEATHERING ONLY. Not in this file, left for later and
re-askable: fetlock claws, cloven/horn hoof caps, fetlock spurs. Nothing here blocks them; the four per-leg anchors
below are where they would hang.

What already exists, so the building session does not redo it
==============================================================
Everything the head-parts treatment says about the existing machinery applies and is not repeated:
AttachedPartLayer, PartKind / PartAnchor / PartShape / PartGenerators / PartNode / PartSheet, GrownParts.of as the one
door, the "adding a part" recipe on model-parts.html's Coding tab, the cost-sorted variation rule (topology baked,
proportions free, counts shown out of a baked maximum), and the deferred-submit trap in PartModel. Read that file's
first section. What is specific to feet:
- The legs are four separate model parts, children of the ROOT (siblings of body, not children of it), in HdHorseModel:
  left_hind_leg, right_hind_leg, left_front_leg, right_front_leg. Each is one box (about 4 wide, 11 long, 4 deep, hung
  from a pivot at about y=14) with its own non-mirrored texture patch. A part parented to a leg bone swings with the
  leg for nothing, which is the same "inherits an already-animated bone" claim the horn proves for the head. Read the
  exact numbers from HdHorseModel and HdBabyHorseModel; do not copy them from here.
- The foal's legs share bone NAMES with the adult's and nothing else, so each leg anchor needs an adult offset and a
  foal offset, like every other anchor in AttachedPartLayer.anchor.
- Per-leg epigenome values already exist: EpiValue.overLegs() carries one number per leg, in CoatRegions.LEGS order
  (left front, right front, left hind, right hind). Not used here (see "one setting" below) but available.
- The coat's lower legs are the thing the feathering's colour comes from, and the coat knows exactly where they are:
  common/coat/pattern/CoatRegions.paintLowerLeg and HorseSkinGeometry.bounds(skin, leg) / forEachTexel(skin, leg, ...)
  already map "the lower part of leg X" onto texels of the coat sheet, game-free. Socks, stockings and every pattern
  that reaches a leg are painted there.
- The coat is generated on the CLIENT into a DynamicTexture by GeneticCoatTextureFactory (budgeted, LRU-cached by
  CoatData.textureKey(); it builds a NativeImage). So the pixels are on the client, behind a texture that may not
  exist yet on the first frames of a horse.
- Grown parts already fade with the horse as a rider looks down (RiderFade), and a foal wears soft parts (feathering
  is one) scaled with the foal. Nothing to build for either.
- Horse shoes are planned to draw through this same layer at per-hoof anchors (Horse shoes.txt). Feathering anchors at
  the FETLOCK, above the hoof, with its own anchors, so the two do not share an offset.

Decided
=======
- ONE gene, horsegenetics.feathering, granting feathering on all four legs. Four legs, ONE setting: one set of numbers
  on the allele copy drives all four legs, with a front-to-hind ratio so hind feathering can be shorter. Not separate
  front and hind genes, and not a per-leg number.
- The feathering's COLOUR is the colour of the LEG UNDERNEATH THE ANCHOR POINT. Not the mane colour, and no colour
  gene. A horse with one white sock has feathering that is white on that leg and the coat colour on the others. This
  is a different mechanism from the head and body parts (which use a horn-style colour gene), so it is written out
  below rather than inherited.
- Foals wear it (soft part), scaled with the foal.
- The part does NOTHING. No stat, ability or hitbox. Say it plainly on the Gameplay tab.
- No config key beyond the existing client parts.* switches.
- Everything can stack with every other grown part (decided for the head parts).
Not asked, my recommendation (change it if she says otherwise):
- DOMINANT, one copy shows, no sex limit. She had no preference. Reasoning: breeds want it near-fixed and pin the gene in
  their breed files (a later task), and a dominant allele means a draught cross shows it, which matches how the look
  behaves in real draught crosses. Recessive is the alternative and changes only expressionOf and the founder table.

How the feathering is built
===========================
Four new PartKinds, one per leg (not one kind drawn four times: a PartKind carries exactly one anchor):
    FEATHER_FRONT_LEFT, FEATHER_FRONT_RIGHT, FEATHER_HIND_LEFT, FEATHER_HIND_RIGHT
with four new anchors (FETLOCK_FRONT_LEFT and so on), each a case in AttachedPartLayer.anchor that walks the root and
then the leg bone, and yields an adult and a foal offset. The root is walked first for every anchor (the HD adult
carries a 1.1 scale on the root; skipping it makes a part 10% small and 2.5 units high). Never add a child to a leg
in createHdBodyMesh for a part: that moves the mesh the HD coat UVs are baked against.
- Shape (a starting proposal, tuned by eye in the dev yard): a fringe of thin hanging boxes around the lower leg,
  starting at the fetlock and falling over the hoof. Styles (topology, baked): fringe (a back-and-sides fringe only),
  skirt (a full ring of boxes around the leg) and heavy (a two-tier ring, longer). Each is a numbered set of boxes, so a
  count can show k of N and the last grow in, like tines.
- Epigenome on the allele copy, one setting for all four legs: length, density (boxes shown), droop (how far the hair
  falls outward), and a front-to-hind ratio. A category for style.
- Sheet: a hair grain, the HAIR region the head parts' beard and ruff also need. The sheet has room (five of sixteen
  regions are painted today). If the head parts have shipped HAIR, reuse it; if not, this adds it, and which session
  owns the region must be settled before either starts, or two regions appear.
- It is a part with NO colour gene, no glow and no translucency: one opaque pass per leg.
- Rigid in v1. Decided by the "no animation" brief and her earlier note, not asked: no counter-swing. The old plan
  suggested a pure formula (feather.xRot = -0.3 * leg.xRot) so the hair trails the hoof instead of swinging like a rigid
  sleeve; that is the same "polish, not v1" call the wings got. Leave it as a follow-up.

Colour: how a leg's tint is found
=================================
This is the one genuinely new mechanism, and the one with a real unknown in it.
- Where it lives: the sampler's TEXEL LIST is game-free and belongs in common/ (a small class, say LegPatches, beside
  CoatRegions): for a Skin (adult or baby) and a leg, return the coat-sheet texels of a small band of that leg
  directly under the feathering's anchor, using the same HorseSkinGeometry.bounds and forEachTexel the sock-painters
  use, so it can be unit-tested with no game and cannot drift from the coat's own leg geometry. The AVERAGING of those
  texels' colours happens on the client, which has the pixels.
- The client reads the generated coat's pixels, averages the patch's opaque texels per leg, and passes that colour as the
  feathering's base tint (the same arguments a horn's tint travels in). Cache the four averages keyed by the coat's
  texture key, so it costs once per coat and not per frame; evict with the coat.
- "Under the anchor point" is decided to mean the band of the leg just BELOW the fetlock where the hair hangs, not the
  whole leg. Recommended, not asked: ONE tint per leg (the average of that band), no tip tint. Two-tone feathering
  (the colour at the anchor fading to the colour at the hoof) is a possible later refinement and is not in v1.
- UNVERIFIED, and the first thing to check: that the CPU-side NativeImage of a generated coat is still readable on the
  client after upload. DynamicTexture normally holds its pixels, but GeneticCoatTextureFactory caches and may drop or
  close the image. Read it. If the pixels are not kept, the fallback is to compute the leg's colour in common/ from the
  coat pipeline (heavier) or to keep a tiny per-leg colour recorded at bake time next to the texture (a four-int
  record in the same cache entry). Decide with the owner only if neither is cheap.
- UNVERIFIED and second: the coat may not exist yet. A horse's coat is baked on a budget, so the first frames of a horse
  can draw with no texture. Recommended: do NOT draw the feathering until the coat's four leg colours exist, rather
  than draw it in a wrong colour and flip. Check what the horse itself draws meanwhile; if it draws a placeholder,
  the feathering is simply absent for those frames.
- Dilute, grey and patterned coats: the band's average is the colour a player sees on that leg, which is what was
  asked for. A leg whose band straddles a sock's edge averages to a blend; that is acceptable and a Verification-tab note.
- Emissive/glow coats: the average is of the base coat, not the glow. No feathering glow in this treatment.

The gene
========
horsegenetics.feathering, alleles Fth (feathered) and n (wild type); one granting method (copy UnicornHornGene.hornFor
and AntlersGene.racksFor) that returns the four AttachedParts for a genotype and epigenome, and one line in GrownParts.of.
- A dominant expression (Fth/Fth and Fth/n both feathered) and a wild one. Founder frequency is a number to tune in a dev
  yard, not here; start at a small share near the antlers'.
- Copy the horn's/antlers' isNatural() and rarity. Priority: a magical-band code-order slot (the gene paints nothing),
  UNVERIFIED which are free; read the Genes registry. UNVERIFIED which GeneFamily; reuse the horn's.
- It is NOT natural to a breed until a breed pins it. Breeds that want it (the draught breeds: Clydesdale, Shire, Percheron,
  Friesian, Gypsy Vanner, Dales, Fell and so on) pin it through the ordinary genes block of their breed file. That is a
  SEPARATE task with its own spec (it moves breed files, bakeBreedFiles and the census); it is deliberately not in this
  treatment, but the owner should expect to ask for it next.

Cost, from the old plan
=======================
Four legs times about ten boxes is about forty extra boxes per feathered horse, and four draws because each leg has its own
tint. The meshes are shared and bounded (kinds times styles times size buckets, so no eviction is needed). A draught herd
is the case worth measuring; parts.enabled already switches every part off for a client that pays for it.

Tests and checks (CLAUDE.md: no full suite; run by name)
=========================================================
- PartGeneratorTest: build every mesh of every feathering kind, style and size bucket, parents before children.
- A LegPatches test (common/, pure): for the adult and the baby Skin and all four legs, the texel list is non-empty,
  lies inside that leg's bounds, and is the band just under the anchor, not the hoof and not the thigh. Mirror check: left
  and right legs return the mirrored texels the coat's own medial-face rule would.
- A FeatheringGene test (copy UnicornHornGeneTest / AntlersGeneTest): dominant expression, founders, the epigenome defaults.
- The coat goldens must NOT move (the gene paints nothing); the coat-bake golden gains a row, so regenerate it and check
  only the NEW row appeared.
- Play checks (the gene page's Verification tab, never seen in game): a horse with one white sock and feathering (that leg's
  hair is white, the others the coat colour); a dun, a grey and a patterned horse; a tiny and a huge horse (the size gene
  scales parts through the pose scale already on the stack, which the page records as a claim from reading); a foal; the
  horse walking, galloping and rearing; two horses in one frame with different coats (the deferred-submit and tint-cache
  check); a herd of draught horses for cost; and the first frames of a horse whose coat has not baked yet.
