ENDERPONY BREED PROPOSAL
========================

Name: Enderpony
Kind: magical
Commonness: very rare
Spawn: all biomes, with no biome restriction

Concept
-------
A small, slow, poor-jumping black pony that carries a purple End-themed coat
and is primarily worth finding because it lays ender pearls. It should feel
like a bad horse with one unusually valuable magical purpose, not like a
powerful general-purpose mount.

Founder identity
----------------
- Black coat: Extension E/E and Agouti a/a for every founder. Do not allow
  other base-colour or agouti pairs into the breed pool.
- Purple nebula: Nebula Light Neb/Neb, with its palette or breed band tuned to
  the purple/violet end rather than pink or blue.
- Purple eyes: Eye Colour right Cha/Cha and left Cha/Cha, using the chaos
  allele as requested. Constrain the chaos colour to purple shades.
- Ender pearl layers: use the existing `horsegenetics.egg_layer` gene with the
  item subject set to `minecraft:ender_pearl`. The breed should be built around
  a homozygous laying pair that produces ender pearls as its primary output,
  not a bespoke new gene.
- Injury teleport: Ender Echo End/End. The existing gene is the hurt-triggered
  teleport and carries the rider with the horse.
- Enderman particles: use the existing `horsegenetics.particle` locus and pick
  the enderman-family / portal-family emitter from that gene so the horse sheds
  the same kind of particles as an Enderman, without inventing a second custom
  particle gene.

Marking variants
----------------
A founder may carry one of these five marking strains, but the selected
marking gene is always homozygous for its coloured allele and every colour
value on that gene is controlled by epigenetics and restricted to purple
shades:

- Stars: homozygous Stc/Stc
- Wormholes: homozygous Wmc/Wmc
- Coloured Crescents: homozygous Crc/Crc
- Duskwing Bands: one homozygous pair, Dwb/Dwb, Dwk/Dwk, or Dwc/Dwc
- Polymoon: homozygous Pmc/Pmc

The breed may either make these five alternatives separate weighted strains
or choose one at founder creation. It must not produce the plain allele,
uncoloured allele, or a heterozygote for the selected marking. The exact
epigenetic hue range for every marking gene is purple-only: narrow enough that
every Enderpony reads as purple while still allowing individual horses to vary
from amethyst through violet.

Body and weather
----------------
- Slightly smaller than an ordinary horse: use a modest pony-sized body-size
  range, not dwarfism.
- Slow: low baseline speed stat, with Magic Speed Sluggish/Sluggish if the
  breed needs an explicit magical penalty.
- Poor jumper: low baseline jump stat, with Magic Jump Leaden/Leaden if the
  breed needs an explicit magical penalty.
- Weather sensitive: Weather Speed Rv/Rv and Weather Jump Rv/Rv. Rain should
  make an already slow, flat-jumping Enderpony dramatically worse. The rain
  penalty is the intended weather identity; do not add a compensating positive
  weather pair.

Watcher suite
-------------
- Add the existing `horsegenetics.magic_night_watch` and
  `horsegenetics.magic_day_watch` genes to the Enderpony breed pool.
- Any of the existing watcher alleles may show up: `Wst`, `Wnr`, `Wsi`, `Wun`,
  and `Wbh` on either gene, in any combination the breed generation allows.
- The breed should not be restricted to a single watcher allele or a single
  watcher pair; the watch behavior is already in the repo and this breed should
  simply inherit whichever watcher variant(s) roll from those existing loci.
- In other words, an Enderpony may spawn with any combination of the existing
  night/day watcher alleles, and those genes already exist in the codebase
  rather than needing a bespoke Enderpony-only watcher gene.

Spawn and value
---------------
- Spawn in every biome, but at very rare breed weight.
- The pearl-laying ability is the primary reason to keep and breed one.
- Keep health, speed, jump, and pull below useful riding or working breeds;
  the Ender Echo and particle display are flavour and survival tools, not a
  reason to make the breed broadly strong.
- The final breed file should document the pearl production rate and cooldown
  separately from the ordinary stat line, so the valuable output can be tuned
  without accidentally improving the mount.

Implementation questions to resolve when routed
-----------------------------------------------
1. Whether the Enderpony breed should encode ender-pearl laying as a named
   `egg_layer` allele setup against `minecraft:ender_pearl` or as a dedicated
   founder-only strain. The repo already supports the former and it is the
   cheaper, cleaner fit.
2. Which particle variant on `horsegenetics.particle` gives the closest match to
   Enderman dust while staying within the existing emitter family; the breed
   should reuse that gene rather than adding a bespoke enderman-particle locus.
3. Whether the five markings are represented as five strains within one
   Enderpony breed or as weighted alternatives in the breed sheet. In either
   case, each selected coloured marking remains homozygous and purple.
4. Verify that the breed's low stats and doubled weather penalties do not make
   the pearl layer impossible to use in rain; the bad weather performance is a
   feature, but the horse must remain recoverable and breedable.
