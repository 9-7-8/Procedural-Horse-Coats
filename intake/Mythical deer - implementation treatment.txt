Mythical deer - implementation treatment

Scoped with the owner. Every "Decided" line below is her call; "Recommended" lines are the scoping
session's, not asked; "Open" lines are still to be settled, by whom is said on the line; "UNVERIFIED" lines
are facts this file has not proved and the building session must check first. Source of truth for what
exists is wiki/model-parts.html, wiki/gene-antlers.html, wiki/gene-mane-color.html, wiki/gene-prismtail.html,
wiki/gene-lycan.html, wiki/breeds.html and wiki/breed-format.html. This file is retired (deleted, per START
HERE step 3) as each piece ships and moves onto those pages' Gameplay and Coding tabs.
Release: rides the next release (Decided). Applies when each piece ships; none cuts its own.
It sits beside "Mythic breeds - gene audit.txt" and "Mythic breeds - biome assignments.txt" and follows their
two rules: public domain only, no talking.


1. The request, as given
========================
"In the same way you made the mythic breeds from the previous session, help me create treatments for each of
these mythical deer. We're going to implement these deer as horses. Let me know if we need to add new marking
genes for any of these, or any other new kinds of genes we need to create to support them."
Then, mid-session: "add this to the deer pool as well" - Artemis's chariot deer.

Reading: eight table rows, so eight entries (one of them a group). Each is a horse breed that wears antlers.
The answer to "what new genes" is below in section 3a: four new things, one stub.

The eight, with the table's facts kept and nothing invented:
  Ceryneian Hind        Greek      coat not fixed; no mane; bronze/brass hooves; golden antlers; speed 10+; large for a deer
  Artemis's chariot deer Greek     coat not specified; no mane; bronze hooves; golden antlers on hinds; speed 10+;
                                   four enormous hinds "larger than bulls"; draw a goddess's chariot. One group entry.
  White stag            European   white; no mane, white tail; ordinary stag antlers; a recurring type
  Saint Eustace's stag  Christian  white; no mane; a radiant cross between the antlers
  Ruru                  Buddhist   golden; no mane; graceful deer. (Speaks in the tale: lore only, no-talking rule.)
  Nine-Colored Deer     Buddhist   nine-coloured multicoloured coat; no mane; slender; antlers. (Speaks: lore only.)
  Marica                Ramayana   gold with silver spots and jewel colours; no mane; rainbow-variegated tail;
                                   gem-like hooves; gem-like antler tips; slender. Shapeshifter.
  Takemikazuchi's deer  Japanese   white; no mane; large enough to carry a rider; antlers unspecified


2. In scope, and what is not
============================
In scope
 - Piece 1: a mane-and-tail shape gene (full / cropped / none; flag tail). Decided.
 - Piece 2: a hoof colour gene. Decided.
 - Piece 3: a nine-colour coat gene, and a crown part for Eustace's cross (with the gene that grants it). Decided.
 - Piece 4: the breeds.
 - A Roadmap-only page for a deer BUILD gene (see Piece 5). Decided: pin it now, build nothing.
Not in scope
 - A deer body shape (thin legs, long neck, smaller head). Decided: breed stats and the existing size genes only;
   the build gene is a plan page, "currently does nothing" (the Curly coat page is the precedent). Re-askable.
 - Speech, in any form (Ruru, Nine-Colored Deer). Decided: lore only.
 - Marica's disguise as a deer. Decided: she carries Lycan (see Piece 4) - the nearest shipped thing; it turns a
   horse into a peaceful land animal at night, which is NOT the poem's shapeshift. Say so on her page.
 - A new ability per deer (dazzle, vision, rescue). Decided: none.
 - Cloven hooves, tails of other shapes, wings, extra heads or legs: untouched, see the audit.


3. What already exists, so the building session does not redo it
=================================================================
3a. The answer to "what new genes": FOUR new things plus one stub
  1. MANE AND TAIL SHAPE (new, natural, any breed can carry it). The model always draws a mane and a tail:
     HdHorseModel has the named children mane and tail, and nothing in common/ or the client can hide one.
     This is gap 7 of the audit ("nothing HIDES a vanilla bone") in its smallest form.
  2. HOOF COLOUR (new, magical, codominant like HornColourGene). Hooves come only from the dark template
     multiplied by the coat (wiki/pipeline: "the template's dark detail - hooves, nostrils - survives on every
     coat"), so there is no hoof colour control at all. The audit said "nothing needs one"; the Hind, the chariot
     deer and Marica now do.
  3. NINE-COLOUR COAT (new, a data gene under horsegenetics/genes/, see wiki/making-a-gene.html). Nearest
     shipped looks: stained_glass, harlequin_shield, iridescent_jewel, kintsugi. None lays out nine distinct hues.
  4. CROWN CROSS (new part kind plus its gene). Eustace's cross, glowing, between the antlers.
  5. DEER BUILD (stub only, Piece 5).
  NOT new, used as they are: Antlers (Ant, both sexes, so hinds have them), antler_glow, antler_crystal (gem
  tips for Marica), Prismtail (rainbow mane-and-tail banding; Marica's tail), mane_color and tail_color, Lycan,
  the white and gold coats (Grey, Champagne, Matp cream, Silver, Rosette and Leopard for silver spots),
  Waterborn is NOT used.
3b. The files (read before building)
  - Parts: common/src/main/java/.../common/parts/ (PartKind, PartAnchor, PartNode, PartShape, PartGenerators,
    HornGenerator, AntlerGenerator, PartSheet, AttachedPart); genetics/GrownParts.of is the one door;
    neoforge-26.1.2/.../client/AttachedPartLayer.java places a part by anchor with one offset per horse model;
    neoforge-26.1.2/tools/bake-part-sheet.mjs paints the sheet. The "adding a part" list is on
    wiki/model-parts.html's Coding tab; the Head parts treatment restates it. A new anchor (CROWN_CENTRE) goes
    on PartAnchor and in AttachedPartLayer.anchor with a foal offset.
  - Light/hoof mask: LightGene already knows a per-texel hoof mask ("four hooves and two eyes - neither is a part,
    the bake already knows exactly" - wiki/pipeline). The hoof colour gene reads that mask; it does not invent one.
  - Colour-gene precedent: genes/HornColourGene.java (codominant, a hue and tone number on each allele copy, a chaos
    allele). Hoof colour copies the shape, with a metal-and-gem palette, not the rainbow one.
  - Breed precedent: breeds/rimehart.json (antlered, magical, "pair" weights, biomes, stats, size, pull).
    Biome vocabulary and herd list: "Mythic breeds - biome assignments.txt".
  - Rule 5: any gene here goes in BOTH editors (CustomHorseSpawnScreen and wiki/horse-designer, EditorRules).
Looks built but is not
  - Mane colour is not mane SHAPE (wiki/gene-mane-color). A maneless horse is not reachable by colour.
  - There is no hoof gene; "Molten Hooves" is a glowing hoofprint trail, nothing to do with hoof colour.
  - Curly coat is a plan page, not a gene. The deer build page copies that, not a shipped gene.
  - Antlers' tint is on the allele copy, so a gold rack is a tint value; UNVERIFIED that the range reaches a
    convincing metallic gold. Check against gene-antlers.coding first.


4. The pieces
=============
Piece 1 - A horse with no mane and a short tail
------------------------------------------------
What the player sees: a mane-less horse, or a cropped mane, and a short flag tail - on any breed.
 Decided: new natural gene. Recommended key horsegenetics.mane_tail_shape; priority and family are the building
   session's call (a natural gene that paints nothing files under NATURAL_HEALTH per the antlers' note - check, it
   may be magical grown-parts-adjacent instead).
 Recommended: alleles Fu (full, wild), Cr (cropped), Ns (none), Fl (flag tail). Tokens are PROPOSALS; grep every
   existing token. Dominance: Ns > Cr > Fu for the mane; the tail axis is its own locus if one gene cannot hold both
   (Open, building session; two loci is fine and probably cleaner).
 Recommended: the effect is a render-state flag (mane scale 0..1, tail scale 0..1), not a mesh; the client hides or
   scales the existing mane and tail bones. Same shape as CutieMarkContribution: a record in common/, folded over
   the expressing genes, copied through GeneticHorseRenderState; its javadoc says record and layer change together.
 Where: common/ the gene, the record, the founder tables; neoforge client the model change.
 Data and saves: allele order is permanent once a world uses it; append only.
 Sync: through the existing render-state extract; no new payload.
 Config: none (it follows the existing parts switches if any; UNVERIFIED whether parts.enabled should gate it).
 Tests: a gene test per wild/expressing combination, EditorRules parity, the two goldens (it paints nothing, so
   expect key-only moves, like the antlers).
 Play checks: a horse with Ns/Ns has no mane in the yard; a foal; the saddle and braids do not float over a hole
   (BraidLayer reads the mane: UNVERIFIED how it behaves with no mane).
 Docs: a new gene page with three tabs, genetics-model priorities, breeds.html/breed-format.html mention.

Piece 2 - Metal and gem hooves
------------------------------
What the player sees: bronze, brass, silver, gold or gem-coloured hooves, with a gleam.
 Decided: new hoof colour gene, codominant like horn colour.
 Recommended: alleles Dk (dark, wild), Brz, Brs, Sil, Gld, plus a gem allele (hue number on the copy) and Cha
   (chaos hue). Two different copies: front legs one, hind legs the other (Open, building session - horn colour
   splits base and tip; hooves have no tip).
 Recommended: a gleam on the metal ones through the existing emissive coat layer path; no glow unless a gene says so.
 Where: common/ the gene and palette; the painter reads LightGene's hoof mask.
 Tests: a coat-bake golden row per allele; the pipeline golden stays at the baseline for a Dk horse.
 Play checks: every allele on all four hooves, white socks versus the hoof colour (a leopard draws striped hooves -
   wiki/pipeline - check they coexist).
 Docs: a new gene page; pipeline.html.

Piece 3 - The nine colours, and the cross between the antlers
--------------------------------------------------------------
What the player sees: a multicoloured Nine-Colored Deer; a stag with a glowing cross hanging between its antlers.
 Decided: nine-colour coat is a data gene (a file under horsegenetics/genes/); the cross is a part.
 Recommended: nine hue bands in patches, one allele pair, any breed; the order of the hues is the gene's
   (the table says "not standardized", so a palette is the breed's, not canon).
 Recommended: the cross is its own part kind with its own anchor CROWN_CENTRE, glowing (PartKind.glowRegions),
   foal: no (like antlers). The gene that grants it is separate from Antlers and does not require them.
   Open (building session): its colour (white gold) is a fixed tint or an allele value.
 Tests: PartGeneratorTest walks the new mesh; AntlersGeneTest as a model; goldens move by key only for the part,
   by rows for the coat gene.
 Play checks: the cross clears a massive rack at both rack sizes; it does not clip into the forehead.
 Docs: model-parts.html, the new gene pages; making-a-gene.html if a contract list changes.

Piece 4 - The breeds
--------------------
All kind magical, spawn wild and spawn_egg only (Decided, as the Nether breeds): not the cowboy, not stables.
Commonness very_rare for named individuals; rare for types (Decided; matches the earlier audit).
Rows are PROPOSALS for the owner's review - the biome lists were NOT asked.
Every founder carries the expressing allele (no invisible carriers). Every pool pins the maneless and flag-tail
alleles. Antlers: Ant (any sex) for hinds and stags alike unless noted.

 Ceryneian Hind (very_rare, unique-flavour)
   biomes: minecraft:forest, minecraft:flower_forest, minecraft:birch_forest, minecraft:meadow
   stats: speed 10, jump 6, health 6; size: large (the top of the deer range). Coat: pale gold ("not securely
   fixed" -> a deliberately weighted coat of fawn and palomino, so it stays unfixed). Hooves: Brz/Brs.
   Antlers: Ant, gold tint, standard to large rack, both sexes. This breed is the only one that is female-leaning
   in lore; no sex rule (Open: the owner may want a breed that only females carry - not supported today).
 Artemis's chariot deer (rare, a type, ONE group entry)
   biomes: minecraft:forest, minecraft:taiga, minecraft:meadow, minecraft:savanna
   stats: speed 10, jump 4, health 8, pull 8; size: the largest of the deer (larger than a bull). Coat: not specified
   -> the whole pool is allowed; Recommended a mild spread of golds, tans and reds. Hooves: bronze (Brz). Antlers:
   gold, both sexes (Ant). Recommended: a separate breed from the Hind, since the table says only that she is the
   fifth deer of the herd, and a breed can carry a pull stat ("draw a chariot") the single Hind need not. Re-askable.
 White Stag (rare)
   biomes: minecraft:snowy_plains, minecraft:snowy_taiga, minecraft:taiga, minecraft:grove
   stats: speed 6, jump 5, health 7; size standard. Coat: white (the dominant white genes, plus Grey for the aged
   look). Mane: none; tail: flag, white. Antlers: Ant/Antm, ordinary stag rack, Open (the building session): stallion
   only via Antm, or both sexes.
   - Takemikazuchi's deer folds into this breed (Decided: one White Stag breed). Its larger rider-carrying frame is a
     size band, lore on the page; it is not a separate breed.
   - Saint Eustace's cross: a rare allele of the cross gene in this breed's pool (Decided: the cross is a gene a
     White Stag line can carry, so players can breed toward it). The breed description says so.
 Ruru (very_rare)
   biomes: minecraft:savanna, minecraft:savanna_plateau, minecraft:meadow, minecraft:sunflower_plains
   stats: speed 6, jump 5, health 7; size standard. Coat: golden (palomino and champagne, full golds, no spots). Mane:
   none. Antlers: Ant, stag rack ("not firmly specified" - modest). Description: lore only for the speech.
 Nine-Colored Deer (very_rare)
   biomes: minecraft:badlands, minecraft:eroded_badlands, minecraft:desert, minecraft:savanna
   (the Dunhuang cave tradition: dry country). stats: speed 6, jump 5, health 7; size small. Coat: the nine-colour
   gene, pinned. Mane: none. Antlers: Ant. Lore only for speech and the rescue.
 Marica (very_rare)
   biomes: minecraft:jungle, minecraft:sparse_jungle, minecraft:bamboo_jungle, minecraft:swamp
   stats: speed 6, jump 8, health 5 ("modest and equal" except a leaning toward jump, as discussed); size small. Coat:
   gold with silver spots (palomino or champagne plus Rosette/Leopard-silver-white; UNVERIFIED which spots gene paints
   them silver on gold - check by baking a sample). Mane: none; tail: Prismtail pinned (the rainbow tail).
   Hooves: the gem allele. Antlers: Ant, antler_crystal pinned (gem-like tips). Carries Lycan (Decided).
   Biomes and the cold-tolerance rule: none of the above are cold, so the climate rule changes nothing for Marica.
 Common checks: cold share of each biome list against the climate spec (White Stag picks up cold tolerance: that is
   right for it; Hind, Artemis's deer, Ruru, Nine-Colored Deer, Marica should not). Breed colour weights go through
   the breed designer's output, not by hand, per wiki/breed-format.html.
Tests: *BreedsTest per breed; BreedFilesTest; BreedBandsReachTest for any band on a knob no pinned pair reads.
Play checks: each breed in a yard pen; a cross of two (a cross label per breeds.html); a wild herd in each biome.
Docs: breeds.html, breed-book.html (a new section of Deer, in lore written fresh from the myth), breed census.

5. Decisions (anything not inside a piece)
===========================================
A. The ask: confirmed - eight rows, four new things. Smallest worthwhile version: Piece 1 plus the breeds that need no
   new genes (White Stag without the cross, Ruru, Hind with dark hooves). Recommended order below.
B. Fit: horses-only, vanilla intact; carrot not stick (no penalty anywhere); the antlers and cross are grown parts, which
   do nothing (Decided for the head parts, applied here). A concept is a breed, not a gene - that is why the White Stag
   is a breed; the cross is a gene because it can sit on any breed. No settled decision bent.
C. Meeting it: wild herds, spawn egg, stable-and-cowboy not used. Tutorial text: none, a note in the H menu's breed list.
   Refusal text: none (no new action).
F. Items and blocks: none. Carrots: the hoof colour, mane-and-tail and cross genes each get a gene carrot recipe (four
   or five files each; read wiki/making-a-gene.html#contracts) - Recommended.
G. Config: none.
H. Saves: tokens and allele order permanent; adding an allele later is append-only.
I. Multiplayer: nothing to authorise; the client reads the render state only.
J. Compatibility: none beyond the breed biome analogues (a modded biome standing in for forest or jungle reaches these
   herds automatically, as the audit says).
K. Performance: one extra part and a hoof-mask paint; no tick work.
L. Tests: see the pieces.
M. Release: rides the next release (Decided).

Three likeliest ways it goes wrong for a player
