Mythical antlered beasts - implementation treatment

Scoped with the owner. Every "Decided" line below is her call; "Recommended" lines are the scoping
session's, not asked; "Open" lines are still to be settled, by whom is said on the line; "UNVERIFIED" lines
are facts this file has not proved and the building session must check first. Source of truth for what
exists is wiki/gene-antlers.html, wiki/gene-unicorn-horn.html, wiki/gene-ram-horns.html, wiki/model-parts.html,
wiki/breeds.html and wiki/breed-format.html. This file is retired (deleted, per START HERE step 3) as each piece ships
and moves onto those pages' Gameplay and Coding tabs.
Release: rides the next release (Decided). Applies when each piece ships; none cuts its own.
It sits beside "Mythical deer" (issue #106), "Mythical goats", "Mythical rams" and "Mythical equids". Rules: public domain only, no talking.
Named "antlered beasts" because most of them wear a horn or rack; the zebra is in the equids file, and the Allocamelus is out.


1. The request, as given
========================
A table queued mid-session; "zebra" went to the equids file, the other seven rows are here, and the owner chose "Three files" and
"All as breeds, noting what each lacks; merge the heraldic antelope into the calopus", and "All lore only, no abilities".
  Shadawar / Aras        Arabic bestiary: antelope-like, one-horned, light; the wind through its hollow-branched horn (42 in a well-
                         known account) makes music that draws animals. The branching, musical horn is the whole point.
  Yale / Eale            ancient and heraldic: goat- or antelope-like, large, powerful; two independently swivelling horns, boar-
                         like tusks, sometimes a tail tuft; no fixed colour.
  Calopus (with the heraldic antelope)
                         bestiary: roe-deer-like, light, swift (speed 10); two large saw-edged horns that cut trees but snag in
                         bushes. Heraldic antelope (merged): deer-antelope composite, serrated horns, a small nose tusk, a tufted
                         neck and a lion-like tail.
  Tarandrus / Parandrus  reindeer- or moose-like, about ox-sized, deer outline; bear-coloured, changing colour with surroundings;
                         long shaggy hair; branching antlers; cloven hooves.
  Leucrocotta            Pliny: donkey-sized; stag hindquarters, lion neck and chest, a badger-like head (a horse or camel head in
                         some texts), mouth reaching to its ears; lion tail; cloven; extremely fast (speed 10+); imitates human
                         voices (lore only, no talking rule).
Reading: five breeds (the heraldic antelope folds into the Calopus), each a horse breed with the nearest shipped horn.

3. What already exists, so the building session does not redo it
=================================================================
3a. Antlers: AntlerGenerator (common/.../parts/AntlerGenerator.java), FORMS 5, MAX_NODES 44; the gene pair Ant (either sex) and
    Antm (stallion only); size, tines, reach, thickness and bone tone are on the allele copy. UNVERIFIED which of the five forms
    reads as a branching rack, and which reads saw-like.
3b. Horns: the unicorn horn (a single horn, colour, glow, dust), and ram horns (Rh, Rhm; curl, corkscrew, four-horned, scurs; the
    goats and rams treatments append more shapes). The Yale pins ram horns (two horns); the Shadawar the unicorn horn.
3c. What these need that does not exist (written on each page as "not drawn"; each a separate, unscoped piece of work):
    - A branching hollow horn with many branches (Shadawar). New part kind.
    - A saw-edged horn (Calopus). New part kind or a serrated antler form.
    - Swivelling horns (Yale): animation; two horns that move on their own. UNVERIFIED that the part layer can animate a part.
    - Boar tusks and a nose tusk (Yale, heraldic antelope): the Tusks treatment (intake/Tusks gene - implementation treatment.txt).
    - A lion neck and chest, a stag's hindquarters, a mouth to the ears (Leucrocotta): hiding a vanilla bone (audit gap 7) plus
      parts; and a lion or tufted tail (audit gap 2).
    - Cloven hooves (audit gap 8), a shaggy coat, a colour that changes with the surroundings (Tarandrus: no such gene).
    - A mane SHAPE: the deer treatment's mane-and-tail gene.
Looks built but is not
  - "Molten Hooves" is not hoof colour; hoof colour is in the deer treatment.
  - Antlers are tines and a rack; none is a branching hollow horn or a saw edge.
  - Goat beard (Head parts) is scoped, not built.


4. The pieces
=============
Piece 1 - The five breeds
-------------------------
What the player sees: five horse breeds that wear horns or antlers, each with a breed-book entry that says what is not drawn.
 Decided: all as breeds, kind magical, "wild" and "spawn_egg" only; very_rare for named individuals (Shadawar, Tarandrus,
   Leucrocotta), rare for types (Yale, Calopus). Rides the next release. Open (owner): none; the rarity ladder is the deer's.
 Rows are PROPOSALS; biomes, stats and sizes not asked. Weights from the breed designer's output.
 Shadawar (very_rare)
   biomes: minecraft:desert, minecraft:badlands, minecraft:savanna
   stats: speed 6, jump 4, health 5; size small. Coat: dun and sandy tones, pale underbelly (the whole pool with those weighted).
   Horn: unicorn_horn pinned, horn colour pale (Wht), glow and dust off. NOT DRAWN: the branches and the music.
 Yale (rare)
   biomes: minecraft:jungle, minecraft:sparse_jungle, minecraft:savanna, minecraft:plains
   stats: speed 5, jump 4, health 8; size large. Coat: the whole pool (no fixed colour). Horns: ram_horns Rh, Crl pinned (two
   horns). NOT DRAWN: the swivel and the tusks.
 Calopus (rare; the heraldic antelope folded in)
   biomes: minecraft:forest, minecraft:birch_forest, minecraft:flower_forest, minecraft:taiga
   stats: speed 10, jump 5, health 5; size small. Coat: roe-deer tones (bays, browns, red dun). Horns: Antlers, the nearest
   serrated-looking form (UNVERIFIED which AntlerGenerator form), large. NOT DRAWN: the saw edge, the nose tusk, the tufted tail.
   The page tells both the bestiary and the heraldic design.
 Tarandrus (very_rare; closest to ready)
   biomes: minecraft:taiga, minecraft:snowy_taiga, minecraft:snowy_plains, minecraft:grove
   (cold: the climate rule gives it near-fixed cold tolerance, right for a northern beast)
   stats: speed 6, jump 4, health 8; size large (ox-sized). Coat: bear-brown (bays, browns, dark browns). Antlers: Ant, large, branching.
   NOT DRAWN: the colour change, the long shaggy hair (Curly coat plan), cloven hooves.
 Leucrocotta (very_rare)
   biomes: minecraft:savanna, minecraft:desert, minecraft:badlands, minecraft:plains
   stats: speed 10, jump 5, health 6; size small (donkey-sized). Coat: the whole pool, tawny weighted. No horns. NOT DRAWN: the lion
   chest, the stag hindquarters, the wide mouth, the cloven feet, the lion tail. Voice: lore only.
 Common checks: cold share against the climate spec (Tarandrus near-fixed, right; no other breed is cold); every biome on the herd
   list in add_horse_herds.json (UNVERIFIED for savanna, jungle, sparse_jungle and flower_forest: confirm).
 Where: breed JSON under common/src/main/resources/horsegenetics/breeds/; breeds.html and breed-book.html.
 Data and saves: breed ids are permanent once a world has horses of them.
 Tests: *BreedsTest per breed; BreedFilesTest; BreedBandsReachTest.
 Play checks: each breed in a yard pen; the Calopus's rack against the Tarandrus's; each half-drawn breed reads as itself;
   a cross of two with a cross label; a wild herd in each biome.
 Docs: breeds.html, breed-book.html (an Antlered and horned beasts section), the breed census; check-breed-heights.mjs.
