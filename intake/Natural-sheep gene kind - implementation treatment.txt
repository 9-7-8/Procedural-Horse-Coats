1. The request, as given
========================
"We're going to add a bunch of sheep genes into this, to widen the marking pool. This will fundamentally change
the philosophy of this mod from being strictly about horses, to more generally about ungulates (with an idea to
eventually add all kinds of future mammal coat genes). Note that this is the lowest possible priority to add.
This builds off the planned, currently non-functional, deer-build and sheep-build genes."
Mid-session: "we'll also need a new distinction beyond "natural" and "magical" -- "natural-sheep""
Reading, confirmed in two question rounds: a THIRD kind of gene, "natural-sheep": sheep-derived, real-biology
loci that paint (and later grow parts) on a horse, opt-in, in the natural paint phase, with their own family in
both editors and their own random button. It is not a second species and not a sheep mob (Decided, see 4).
The wider ungulate / mammal aim is the reason the kind is built as a general slot rather than a one-off.

============================

3. What already exists, so the building session does not redo it
=================================================================
3a. The split today is a boolean. Gene.isNatural() (genetics/Gene.java, default method near line 378) is true
    for natural, false for magical. Hand-written genes override it: AbstractNaturalGene and AbstractMagicalGene
    make it final; many genes (RamHornsGene, AntlersGene...) return false directly; AbstractEyeGene returns a
    field. Data-driven genes get it from SpecGene.isNatural() -> GeneSpec.natural(), which GeneSpecParser reads
    from the file's "phase" ("natural" or "magical", anything else is an IllegalArgumentException, line ~116).
    The parser also rejects a layer/op whose own natural-ness disagrees with the gene's phase (line ~949).
3b. The boolean is really "which paint phase", and the code says so: GeneFamily's javadoc: "the pipeline splits on
    Gene#isNatural() and orders on Gene#priority()". Genes.MAGICAL_BAND_START = 100; Genes.java logs a warning when
    a natural gene sits at priority >= 100 or a magical one below it (lines ~963-969).
3c. Families are a VIEW, never a mechanic (GeneFamily javadoc). A gene declares no family; it lands in one by
    isNatural(), by priority band, by Genes.influencesCoat (NATURAL_HEALTH) and by the NATURAL_OVERRIDES table.
    Current naturals: NATURAL_COAT, NATURAL_DILUTION, NATURAL_WHITE, NATURAL_HEALTH, NATURAL_OTHER. Each carries a
    title and a lede that the sidebar, the landing page and both editors share.
3d. Random buttons: RandomizeMode (genetics/RandomizeMode.java) - Scope.MAGICAL, "Random magical", the +1/+2/+3
    magical modes, and a Scope test at line ~148 (MAGICAL -> !gene.isNatural()). EditorRules has its own MAGICAL
    group (line ~35). The designer's twin is web/HorseEditor.java via web/DesignerApi.java (rule 5).
3e. Every other reader of "natural": genetics/spec/GeneCensusTool (line ~289, "Natural"/"Magical" column),
    spec/GeneWikiTool (lines ~296, ~735), spec/SpecFixtureTool (~122), coat/pattern/MarkingFactsTool (line ~166,
    writes a JSON "natural" boolean the breed designer reads), progress/ProgressTask, genetics/SpliceCategory,
    trait/HorseTraits and TraitBuilder, breed/BreedLineage, breed/spec/BreedSpecParser and BreedSpecWriter,
    genetics/ShowcaseGenotypes, genetics/EyeColor, genetics/Genes, and wiki/tools/bake-gene-timeline.mjs. The
    gene creator's legacy JS (wiki/gene-creator/js/schema.js, spec-model.js, ui.js, parity.js, preview.js) and the
    breed designer's (wiki/breed-designer/js/bd-core.js, bd-steps.js, bd-ai.js) also mention it. That list is a
    grep for "isMagical|MAGICAL|magical" files, NOT an audit: the building session reads each hit.
3f. Natural priorities in the Java genes use 1, 3, 5, 31, 36, 37, 60, 65, 68, 69, 71, 77 and 80-99. Data-driven
    genes carry their priority in horsegenetics/genes/*.json, so the free slots are not known from this list.
3g. A horse's founder roll keeps "no invisible carriers in the wild": every founder genotype shows. Magical genes
    are absent from ordinary founder rolls and reach a horse through breeds, editors and gene carrots.
3h. Precedent: the ram-horn pages already say "Analogous, not measured ... does not borrow its name"
    (gene-ram-horns.html#real-sheep). The honest-hybrid wording for natural-sheep genes continues that.
Looks built but is not
  - sheep-build.html and deer-build.html: planned in the rams and deer treatments, NOT in wiki/ yet. There is no
    "build gene" either; they are Roadmap-only plan pages. This treatment depends on neither.
  - Any sheep gene, any ungulate model, any non-horse species. None exists. Not A Sheep (breeds/not_a_sheep.json)
    is a magical horse breed, not a sheep.
  - "Natural-sheep" as a word: it appears nowhere in code or wiki yet.


4. The pieces
=============
Piece 1 - The kind exists
-------------------------
What the player sees: nothing yet; a place for sheep-derived genes to live.
 Decided: a third kind, "natural-sheep", beside natural and magical. It paints in the NATURAL phase, in its own
   priority sub-band, so it can restrict red/black pigment like a natural gene.
 Decided: it is opt-in on horses: absent from ordinary founder tables and from the ordinary "Random natural" rolls.
 Recommended: do NOT widen Gene.isNatural() into a tri-state that the pipeline reads. Keep isNatural() meaning
   "paints in the natural phase" (true for natural-sheep), so the three-phase pipeline, the op validation in
   GeneSpecParser and the two goldens' structure are untouched. Add a second, orthogonal fact - a
   GeneKind-style enum {NATURAL, NATURAL_SHEEP, MAGICAL} or a default method such as Gene.isSheep() - that only
   the family, roll, census and wiki code read. Every isNatural() caller in 3e is then audited once and put in
   one of two piles: "means phase" (leave) or "means kind" (change). Open (building session): the exact name.
 Recommended: data files say it with one new key beside "phase", for example "kind": "natural-sheep", defaulting
   to the old behaviour, so all existing gene files stay valid unedited. Open (building session): the key name.
 Open (building session, UNVERIFIED): the sub-band. Natural slots 80-99 are crowded; a block in 40-59 or 61-79 is
   the Recommended hunt, after reading every data-gene priority. It must paint AFTER the horse's own base coat
   genes (Decided in the coat treatment) and respect Genes.MAGICAL_BAND_START.
 Where: common/ only - Gene (or a new enum), the hand-written abstract base classes if a Java gene needs it,
   GeneSpec/GeneSpecParser/SpecGene for data genes. Nothing in neoforge-26.1.2 should need to change except what
   reads the web/Java family list.
 Data and saves: gene keys and allele order are permanent once a world uses them; this piece adds none. A world
   saved before this piece loads identically (no gene carries the new kind).
 Sync: none. Config: none.
 Tests: GeneSpecParserTest (kind defaults, a natural-sheep gene file parses, a bad value fails with a message that
   names the allowed values), GeneFilesTest (every shipped file still parses), a Genes registration test that a
   natural-sheep gene in the magical band warns like any other, CommonPortabilityTest.
 Play checks: none (nothing visible yet); a horse loads from an old save and looks identical.
 Docs: genetics-model.html (the kind, in Coding), making-a-gene.html#contracts (the new file key - add it to the
   contracts list so it is not missed), pipeline.html (one line: the kind does not move a phase).

Three likeliest ways it goes wrong for a real player
  1. A natural-sheep gene leaks into "Random natural" or wild founders and every new horse starts carrying one.
     Handled: Piece 2 tests that no roll but its own produces it.
  2. A caller meaning "phase" is changed to mean "kind" (or the reverse) and a coat shifts. Handled: the
     two-pile audit in Piece 1 and the goldens not moving.
  3. The family list, the wiki and the two editors disagree on the group. Handled: one GeneFamily table feeds all
     (its own javadoc says that is the point) and the play check compares both editors.
