Scoped with the owner. Every "Decided" line below is her call; "Recommended" lines are the scoping
session's, not asked; "Open" lines are still to be settled, by whom is said on the line; "UNVERIFIED" lines
are facts this file has not proved and the building session must check first. Source of truth for what
exists is wiki/gene-ram-horns.html, wiki/gene-ram-horn-form.html, wiki/model-parts.html, wiki/breeds.html,
wiki/breed-format.html and wiki/curly-coat.html (the plan-page precedent). This file is retired (deleted, per

Release: rides the next release (Decided). Applies when each piece ships; none cuts its own.
It sits beside issue #106 (Mythical deer) and shares two of its pieces (the mane-and-tail
shape gene and the hoof colour gene); it follows the audit's two rules: public domain only, no talking.


1. The request, as given
========================
"Similar to the last sessions, you're going to add new breed plans for some goats. While we're shipping them as
horses, we're in a magical fantasy world where goats and horses are cross fertile. Create a 'goat build' gene in
the same way you made the deer build gene."
Then a table of six rows. Reading: six entries, each a horse breed that is a goat in a horse's body, plus a goat
build plan page. "In the same way as the deer build" means a Roadmap-only plan page that currently does nothing
(Piece 3), not a built gene. Confirmed by the owner's answers below.

The six, with the table's facts kept and nothing invented:
  Amalthea              Greek      colour not specified; ordinary she-goat build; two horns (one broken off in the
                                   cornucopia account). Lore: the cornucopia, a horn of abundance. Some traditions make
                                   her a nymph; "the goat" is version-specific.
  Zlatorog / Goldhorn   Slovene    white; no mane, tail not specified; compact agile mountain ungulate; golden horns;
                                   speed 10, jump 10. A chamois, or an ibex in some tellings. Flowers grow from his blood
                                   and restore his strength (lore only, see Decided).
  Sea-goat / Capricorn  Greek      goat half and fish half, colours vary; goat hair in front, a fish tail behind; powerful
                                   forequarters; goat horns and a scaled tail; aquatic movement.
  Dahu                  Alpine     variable, mountain-goat or chamois colouring; small to medium mountain-goat build; legs
                                   shorter on one side; ordinary horns in some tellings. A humorous tall tale.
  Chimera               Greek      no canonical colour; lion mane, serpent tail; heavy and monstrous; lion forepart, goat head
                                   or middle, serpent-headed tail; breathes fire. A goat-containing hybrid.
  Yule Goat / Julbock   Scandinavian straw-gold; woven straw mane and tail, straw feet not hooves; straw horns, often red
                                   ribbons; no inherent power; a folkloric effigy.

3. What already exists, so the building session does not redo it
=================================================================
3a. Horns, the one piece of machinery this touches
  - RamHornsGene (common/.../genetics/genes/RamHornsGene.java): dominant, sex-limited allele pair Rh (either
    sex) and Rhm (stallion only). Grows geometry, paints nothing. Not a Sheep (breeds/not_a_sheep.json) pins it
    with "pair" weights - the precedent for a horned breed's pool.
  - RamHornFormGene (same folder): "horsegenetics.ram_horn_form", PRIORITY 237, alleles in generator order
    Crl, Crk, Fhn, Scr (permanent, append only), dominance Fhn > Crl > Crk > Scr, one shape on both sides.
    Founder frequencies Crk 0.12, Fhn 0.08, Scr 0.10, Crl the remainder. Each allele is an Allele whose second
    argument is RamHornGenerator's shape index.
  - RamHornGenerator (common/.../parts/RamHornGenerator.java): shape constants CURL 0, CORKSCREW 1, FOUR 2,
    SCURS 3; FORMS = 4, CURLS = 4, MAX_NODES = 24. PartKind uses FORMS * CURLS as the baked variation count for
    RAM_HORN_RIGHT and RAM_HORN_LEFT, so a fifth shape raises that count and means a re-bake of the part sheet
    (neoforge-26.1.2/tools/bake-part-sheet.mjs). RamHornSize and RamHornTipGene carry size and tip on the copy.
  - Horn colour: HornColourGene is codominant with hue and tone on each allele copy; the ram horns already read a
    tint. A golden horn (Zlatorog) is a tint value. UNVERIFIED that the range reaches a convincing metallic gold
    on a ram horn: bake a sample before pinning (the deer treatment asks the same of antlers).
  - HornGenerator's own comment mentions a "gentle scimitar curve" on the unicorn horn; that is a curve on one
    shape, not a shape of ram horn. Do not read it as the feature being built.
3b. Breeds and the pages
  - Closest breed precedent: breeds/not_a_sheep.json (magical, uncommon, "pair" weights, biomes, size
    [0.508, 0.667], pull 2, ram horns pinned). Other precedents for mythic breeds: breeds/rimehart.json.
    Biome vocabulary, spawn rules, the cold-share rule and the herd list: "Mythic breeds - biome assignments.txt".
  - A breed is a JSON file (wiki/breed-format.html); the breed designer writes the weights, never by hand.
  - Lycan (a matched pair, a peaceful land animal at night) already includes a goat among its shapes. These
    breeds are goats, not shapeshifters; Recommended: they do not pin Lycan. Not asked.
  - The plan-page precedent: wiki/curly-coat.html ("Planned / not built", Roadmap tab only). The deer treatment's
    Piece 5 builds deer-build.html the same way; goat-build.html is its twin.
3c. What the six need that does not exist (the owner's "noting" request)
  Zlatorog and the Yule Goat need pieces from the DEER treatment (Decided: they wait for them):
   - Mane and tail shape gene (deer Piece 1): Zlatorog's "no mane"; the Yule Goat's straw-like cropped mane is
     the nearest. UNVERIFIED whether that gene's cropped mane reads as straw; it will not read as WOVEN.
   - Hoof colour gene (deer Piece 2): the Yule Goat's "straw feet, not hooves" is the nearest, a pale gold hoof.
     UNVERIFIED that the deer's metal-and-gem palette reaches a straw tone; if not, a straw allele is appended
     to that gene, not built here.
  Sea-goat and Chimera need new genes and models, none of which is scoped (audit gaps; numbers are the audit's):
   - A fish tail (audit gap 2) and HIDING the horse's hind half (gap 7). The machinery can ADD parts and nothing
     HIDES a vanilla bone. Together, an L piece of work, the Hippocampus's own blocker.
   - Chimera: a lion forepart and mane, a serpent-headed tail, a goat head on the middle: hiding (gap 7), a tail
     part with a head (gap 2), and extra heads (gap 6). Each an L piece. Nothing here scopes them.
   - Chimera's fire breath (gap 19): nothing sets targets alight; GeneAbility's effects verbs have no ignite
     verb. A cosmetic flame from the head (the Particle locus's flames) works today.
   - Sea-goat's aquatic movement: shipped. Ocean Born (cannot drown), Magic Swim Speed, Magic Water Breathing
     and Waterborn all exist (wiki/gene-*.html); the water coat patterns (fish_scales, tidemark) give scales.
  Dahu: uneven legs. Nothing makes one side's legs shorter; Decided lore only.
  Amalthea: one horn missing. The pair is symmetric by default; the head parts treatment mentions an
   asymmetry knob that is not built. Decided: a Roadmap line only.
Looks built but is not
  - Goat is a Lycan shape, not a build. A goat build gene does not exist; "currently does nothing" is its state.
  - There is no goat horn. Ram horns are sheep horns (curl, corkscrew, four-horned, scurs); the corkscrew is the
    nearest goat look (markhor), and no shape sweeps back like an ibex or a goat's.
  - There is no goat beard yet. Head parts' goat beard (BEARD, a single centred part) is scoped, not built.
  - "Molten Hooves" is a glowing trail, not hoof colour; hoof colour is the deer treatment's unbuilt Piece 2.
  - HornGenerator has a scimitar curve for the unicorn horn; the ram horn has no scimitar form.
  - Curly coat is a plan page, not a gene.


4. The pieces
=============
Piece 1 - Goat horns: the scimitar shape
------------------------------------------
What the player sees: a goat or ibex's long horns sweeping back from the head in a gentle blade, on either sex
 (Rh) or a stallion only (Rhm). Gold is the existing horn tint.
 Decided: append one new shape to Ram horn form, not a new gene. Name "Scimitar".
 Recommended: token "Sci" (a PROPOSAL; grep every existing allele token first). Allele list order becomes
   Crl, Crk, Fhn, Scr, Sci (append only, permanent). Dominance independent of order: Fhn > Crl > Sci > Crk > Scr,
   so a scimitar goat crossed with a corkscrew goat throws scimitar and a curled horse still beats it. Open
   (building session): the dominance slot.
 Recommended: founder frequency modest (about 0.06 for Sci, taken from Crl's remainder) so the wild pool
   carries it rarely; the goat breeds pin it. The value is a PROPOSAL.
 Recommended: shape is a long, slightly backswept blade with faint ridges; one root, no corkscrew. The curl knob
   (CURLS) is the sweep amount. UNVERIFIED whether the current node budget (MAX_NODES 24) allows a ridged blade.
 Where: common/ - RamHornFormGene (the allele, expression text and founder table), RamHornGenerator (the shape,
   FORMS from 4 to 5), PartKind's baked count follows FORMS * CURLS; NeoForge - re-bake the part sheet.
 Data and saves: allele order is permanent once a world uses it; append only. A world without the allele is
   unchanged; a world saved with it and then loaded without it is not a case (the allele ships with the mod).
 Sync: none new; the part rides the render state it already rides.
 Config: none (the existing client parts switches apply).
 Tests: RamHornFormGene test for the new allele in the dominance series and in a pair; RamHornGenerator test
   that the new shape produces nodes inside the budget at every curl value and at every size class;
   PartGeneratorTest; EditorRules parity.
   The two goldens move by KEY ONLY (the horns paint nothing); run *CoatBakeGoldenTest and
   *CoatPipelineGoldenTest and check which rows moved, as for the antlers.
 Play checks: the blade on a mare and a stallion in the yard, foal (ram horns do not show on foals: confirm),
   both sizes, gold and plain tint, with a halter and a saddle on, against the existing four shapes at the same
   size; clipping with the ears and a neighbouring antler or unicorn horn (accepted per the head parts decision).
 Docs: gene-ram-horn-form.html (Gameplay: the shapes table and the inheritance paragraph go from four to five;
   Coding: the allele order; Science: the real-goat remark), gene-ram-horns.html, model-parts.html if the part
   sheet changes, genetics-model.html only if priorities move (they do not).
 Re-bake after (CLAUDE.md table): any file in genes/ is NOT touched (this gene is Java); the long gene row's
   bakes still apply because the gene's allele list changed - run them; the web/designer rebake and boot check;
   the part sheet.

   (the head parts decision applied). A concept is a breed, not a gene: these are breeds; the scimitar is an
   allele because it can sit on any horned horse. The magical setting (goats and horses cross-fertile) is the
   owner's, stated in the request; it needs no mechanism, it is why they are horse breeds. No settled decision bent.

   tip, and the horn tint on the copy; no new ranges. No sex linkage change (Rh and Rhm as before). It is not a
   lethal and not a health effect (horns do nothing to the horse). Both editors (rule 5): the horse designer and
   CustomHorseSpawnScreen show the allele by reading the gene's list; rebake the wasm and run
   check-designer-boots.mjs, and check the editor's horn shape list has five entries.
E. Breeds: see Piece 2. No existing breed's pool needs to move. Open (building session): Not a Sheep's pool
   keeps Crl, Crk, Fhn, Scr and gains no Sci; check its test does not enumerate shapes by count.

J. Compatibility: none beyond the biome analogues (a modded biome that stands in for a mountain biome reaches
