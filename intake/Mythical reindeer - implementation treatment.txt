Mythical reindeer - implementation treatment

Scoped with the owner. Every "Decided" line below is her call; "Recommended" lines are the scoping
session's, not asked; "Open" lines are still to be settled, by whom is said on the line; "UNVERIFIED" lines
are facts this file has not proved and the building session must check first. Source of truth for what
exists is wiki/gene-antlers.html, wiki/gene-flying.html, wiki/model-parts.html, wiki/breeds.html and
wiki/breed-format.html. This file is retired (deleted, per START HERE step 3) as each piece ships and moves onto
those pages' Gameplay and Coding tabs.
Release: rides the next release (Recommended: the owner chose the same rule for every other set; not asked for this one).
Applies when each piece ships; none cuts its own.
It sits beside "Mythical deer" (which builds the mane-and-tail shape gene these need), "Mythical equids" (which builds the
head-colour gene Meandash needs) and the other mythic files. Rules: public domain only, no talking. Meandash's human-form tales are
excluded, as the table says.


1. The request, as given
========================
"add these as well" and a table of three rows; the owner chose a new file, "Mythical reindeer".
  Meandash, the sky reindeer  Kola Sami: giant reindeer; white body, black head; no mane, tail not specified; golden antlers; speed 10+;
                              tied to world-shaking events, or crossing the sky as a sun-reindeer. Only the fully animal-shaped form.
  White reindeer              Sami motif: reindeer; white, no mane, tail white if wholly white; natural reindeer build; reindeer antlers,
                              no special shape; regarded as significant or fortunate; no universal power. A motif, not one animal;
                              do NOT give it Meandash's golden antlers.
  Saint Nicholas's eight reindeer  A Visit from St. Nicholas: "tiny"; no mane; tail and coat unspecified; flight, carrying a sleigh and
                              rider; speed 10+; no wings specified; antlers not described. One group entry, not eight designs.
Reading: three horse breeds that wear antlers, in the deer treatment's pattern.

3. What already exists, so the building session does not redo it
=================================================================
 - Antlers: Ant (any sex, so cows have them: true of reindeer) and Antm; size, tines, reach, thickness and bone tone on the allele
   copy (AntlerGenerator, FORMS 5, MAX_NODES 44). Golden antlers are a tint value. UNVERIFIED that the range reaches a convincing
   metallic gold (the deer treatment asks the same).
 - Flight: the data gene "horsegenetics.flying" (common/src/main/resources/horsegenetics/genes/flying.json); glide dominant, true
   flight recessive and needs two copies (wiki/gene-flying.html). Cloudtouched's pool uses the alleles El and Tf (UNVERIFIED which is
   which; read the gene file). Flight paints nothing and needs no wings.
 - No mane: the deer treatment's Piece 1 (mane-and-tail shape gene, unbuilt). It decides the tail too (a flag tail).
 - Head colour: the equids treatment's Piece 1 (a head-colour coat gene with Red and Black alleles; unbuilt). Black is Meandash's.
 - Size: breeds carry a size range in the breed file ("size": [min, max]); Not A Sheep is [0.508, 0.667], the Unicorn [0.92, 1.05].
   UNVERIFIED how large "giant" can be and how small "tiny" can be inside the shipped bounds.
Looks built but is not
  - A maneless horse (deer Piece 1), a head colour (equids Piece 1), a deer or reindeer build (a plan only), a sleigh: none exists.
  - Flight is a shipped gene; wings are not, and none is needed here.


4. The pieces
=============
Piece 1 - The three breeds
--------------------------
What the player sees: a giant white sky stag with a black head and golden antlers; a plain white reindeer; and a herd of tiny flying
 reindeer that need no wings.
 Decided: three breeds, kind magical. "wild" and "spawn_egg" only, Recommended (the deer, goat and ram rule, not separately asked).
   Commonness Recommended very_rare for Meandash, rare for the types (White reindeer, the eight).
 Decided: true flight pinned on Meandash and the eight (two copies of the flying gene's true-flight allele); the sleigh is lore.
 Rows are PROPOSALS; biomes, stats and sizes not asked. Weights from the breed designer's output.
 Meandash (very_rare; waits on deer Piece 1 and equids Piece 1)
   biomes: minecraft:snowy_taiga, minecraft:taiga, minecraft:snowy_plains, minecraft:frozen_peaks, minecraft:grove
   (cold: the climate rule gives it near-fixed cold tolerance, right for a Kola reindeer; frozen_peaks is UNVERIFIED on the herd list)
   stats: speed 10, jump 6, health 8; size giant (the top of the size range; Open: how far above Artemis's deer). UNVERIFIED that
   speed 10 is reachable. Coat: white body (dominant whites), Head colour Black pinned. Mane: none (deer Piece 1). Antlers: Ant,
   large, gold tint pinned, both sexes. Flight: true flight pinned. Lore only for the world-shaking pursuit and the sun crossing.
 White reindeer (rare; waits on deer Piece 1)
   biomes: minecraft:snowy_taiga, minecraft:taiga, minecraft:snowy_plains, minecraft:grove
   stats: speed 6, jump 5, health 7; size standard (natural reindeer build). Coat: white (the dominant whites, with Grey for age).
   Mane: none; tail: flag, white. Antlers: Ant, ordinary reindeer rack, NOT gold (Decided by the table's note). No flight; no
   ability; the luck is lore.
 Saint Nicholas's eight reindeer (rare, ONE group entry; waits on deer Piece 1)
   biomes: minecraft:snowy_plains, minecraft:snowy_taiga, minecraft:taiga, minecraft:grove
   stats: speed 10, jump 8, health 4; size tiny (very small, like Not A Sheep or below). Coat: not specified, so the whole pool is
   allowed with a mild spread (Recommended); no distinct coat, nose or antler design is invented. Mane: none. Antlers: Ant, plain.
   Flight: true flight pinned. The poem names eight; the page lists the names as lore and says nobody is distinguished. Open
   (owner): none.
 Common checks: cold share (all three pick up cold tolerance, right for reindeer); every biome on the herd list in
   add_horse_herds.json (UNVERIFIED for frozen_peaks).
 Where: breed JSON under common/src/main/resources/horsegenetics/breeds/; breeds.html and breed-book.html (a Reindeer section).
 Data and saves: breed ids are permanent once a world has horses of them.
 Tests: *BreedsTest per breed; BreedFilesTest; BreedBandsReachTest for a band on a knob no pinned pair reads (the gold tint).
 Play checks: each breed in a yard pen; Meandash's black head on a white body with gold antlers; an eight-herd that flies; a cross with
   a deer breed (a cross label per breeds.html); a wild herd in each biome.
 Docs: breeds.html, breed-book.html, the breed census; check-breed-heights.mjs for hand-typed heights.
