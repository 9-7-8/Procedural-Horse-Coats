Tufted lion tail - implementation treatment

Scoped with the owner. Every "Decided" line below is her call; "Recommended" lines are the scoping
session's, not asked; "Open" lines are still to be settled, by whom is said on the line; "UNVERIFIED"
lines are facts this file has not proved and the building session must check first. Source of truth for
what exists is wiki/model-parts.html (the grown-parts machinery and its "Adding another part" recipe)
and the antler / unicorn-horn gene pages. This file is retired (deleted, per /process-intake step 8) as
each piece ships and moves onto those pages' Gameplay and Coding tabs.
Release: patch per piece (each piece is something a player notices); saves keep loading.

1. The request, as given
========================
"Help me scope out a tufted lion tail attachment. Making the pixels on the horse skin where the tail is
transparent should hide their original tail"

Reading: a new grown part, a thin tail ending in a tuft, that replaces the horse's own tail on horses
that carry the gene. Her first idea for hiding the vanilla tail was alpha-0 pixels on the coat's tail
region. When asked, she chose instead to HIDE THE MODEL PART in the layer (Decided, section 4 piece 1),
so the alpha-cutout trick is NOT in this file; see "In scope, and what is not".

2. In scope, and what is not
============================
In scope:
- One new magical gene (dominant tuft allele + wild type) granting one grown part, a rigid thin tail with
  a tuft of boxes at its end, parented to the tail bone so it swishes with the vanilla animation.
- The vanilla tail hidden on horses that have the part, adult AND foal (Decided).
- The part's colour sampled from the horse's own tail/mane hair patch (Decided).
- Both editors (designer and spawn screen, hard rule 5), the info screen, the gene's wiki page.
- A founder frequency and a spawn story that leaves no invisible carriers in the wild.
Not in scope (all re-askable):
- Alpha-0 coat pixels as the hiding mechanism (she chose hiding the part). Do not build a second path.
- A segmented, whip-curving chain (Recommended as a later optional piece, not asked beyond "rigid").
- Blade and club tails (the body-parts treatment lists "tails (tufted, blade, club)" as later), and a
  multi-allele form gene: she chose the two-allele dominant shape.
- A tuft colour gene of its own (she chose hair sampling).
- Drawing the rescuing braid, tail stripes or tail glow on the lion tail (they stay on the hidden
  vanilla tail and are therefore not drawn - Decided, section 5).
- Any stat, ability or hitbox change (a part never has one; say so on the Gameplay tab).
- Breeds that carry it (a breed designed with the owner via design-a-breed; a separate task).

3. What already exists, so the building session does not redo it
================================================================
Read from the code on 2026-10-02 (paths under common/src/main/java/com/example/horsegenetics/common/ or
neoforge-26.1.2/src/main/java/com/example/horsegenetics/neoforge/):
- The machinery: parts/PartKind (one value per part, carries ONE anchor and a sheet region; switch methods
  styles(), sizeBuckets(), showsOnFoal(), glowRegions(), label()), PartAnchor (FOREHEAD, CROWN_RIGHT/LEFT,
  NAPE_RIGHT/LEFT today), PartShape (the mesh cache key), PartNode, PartGenerators.build (a switch on
  kind), AttachedPart (shape + stretch, girth, tilt, base and tip tint), PartSheet (64x64 greyscale, 16px
  regions; PartSheet.HAIR exists and no part uses it yet), genetics/GrownParts (of(Genotype, Epigenome) is
  the ONE door from genes to parts; shapes(Gene) needs the new allele added to its baseline).
- Client: client/PartMeshes (bakes a shape), PartModel, AttachedPartLayer (the single layer; its anchor()
  switch walks the ROOT first, then head_parts, then head, calling translateAndRotate, then applies
  an adult/foal offset), GeneticHorseRenderer (calls GrownParts.of in extractRenderState into
  GeneticHorseRenderState.parts, gated by ClientConfig.parts() and the parts detail distance).
- The recipe: wiki/text/model-parts.coding.txt, "Adding another part": PartKind value + its switch cases,
  a generator, cases in PartGenerators.build and PartShape.nominalLength/stretchTo, a PartAnchor and a case
  in AttachedPartLayer.anchor with one offset per model (adult, foal), a PartSheet region plus a bake in
  neoforge-26.1.2/tools/bake-part-sheet.mjs, a gene, a line in GrownParts.of and an entry in shapes.
- Closest precedents: genes/UnicornHornGene.hornFor and AntlersGene.racksFor (a gene returns its
  AttachedParts); DragonHornsGene (paired kinds, one anchor each). Closest layout precedent for "no
  animation, a child of an animated bone": the head parts, which inherit the head animation.
- The tail today: client/HdHorseModel builds "tail" as a child of "body" (texOffs(42,36), addBox 3x14x4,
  offset 0,-5,2, rotation PI/6); client/HdBabyHorseModel builds the foal tail separately (texOffs(24,34),
  3x3x8 box, offset 0,-1,7, xRot -0.7418). common/coat/skin/HorseSkinGeometry.Part.TAIL holds both rows.
  coat/pattern/CoatRegions.fillTail, HairPattern (mane + tail = HAIR in genetics/spec/PartGroups).
- Tail colour already exists: genes/TailColorGene (key horsegenetics.tail_color, twin of ManeColorGene).
  There is NO tail length or shape gene, and no tail-related grown part. Only intake mentions one.
- Things that draw ON the vanilla tail and will therefore vanish with it: BraidLayer (rescuing braid; a
  submitModel of the WHOLE model through braid_tail.png, a mask baked by HairMaskTool), EmissiveCoatLayer
  (glow masks), the coat's own tail pixels.
- Precedent for hiding a part: CowboyTradeFilterHooks sets ModelPart.visible = false (an unrelated model;
  it proves the field exists, not that it works on this horse).
- LOOKS built but is not: PartAnchor's comment names SPINE, WITHERS_PAIR, HOOF_x4 and the draft names
  TAIL_BASE; none exist. PartSheet.HAIR is baked but unused. The "lion tail" in intake/Attached model
  parts.txt (about lines 429-443) is a plan only. AttachedPartLayer's offsets are documented as "not
  seen in a running game".
- Related treatments: intake/Body parts - implementation treatment.txt (builds the first non-head
  anchor, SPINE; this file adds a second, TAIL; whichever ships first builds the "walk root to body bone"
  branch in AttachedPartLayer.anchor), intake/Feathering - implementation treatment.txt (the first user of
  PartSheet.HAIR and of the "sample the hair patch" colour mechanism; this file reuses it, see UNVERIFIED 1).

4. The pieces
=============
Piece 1 - A lion-tailed horse (the adult, rigid)
------------------------------------------------
What the player sees: a horse with the tuft allele has no ordinary tail; it has a thin tail ending in a
brush of hair, the same colour as its own tail hair, swishing as the horse moves.
- Decided: magical gene (owner). A new locus, appended after the last, so the genotype code only
  lengthens.
- Decided: dominant tuft allele and a wild type (owner): one copy shows. Tuft size comes from an
  epigenome number on the allele copy: length and tuft fullness only, no thickness (owner; a size category if the
  existing PartKind.sizeBuckets pattern fits).
- Decided: hide the vanilla tail by HIDING THE MODEL PART in the layer, not by alpha-0 pixels (owner).
- Decided: rigid thin tail + tuft, boxes parented to the tail bone (owner). No chain in this piece.
- Decided: colour sampled from the tail/mane hair patch (owner), so TailColorGene, stripes and prismtail
  show through it.
- Recommended, not asked: the hide is driven from the render state in HdHorseModel.setupAnim (the
  model is shared across horses and layers, so the visible flag must be SET FOR EVERY horse, true or
  false, never left from the previous one).
- Recommended: a new anchor TAIL_BASE; PartKind LION_TAIL (one kind: a PartKind carries one anchor; it is
  not paired).
- Open (building session): the exact thickness and length in model units; tune in the dev yard.
Where the code lives:
- common/: the gene (LionTailGene, alleles, expressionOf, isNatural() false, founder entry, label), the
  PartKind value and its switch cases, the generator (LionTailGenerator + a size class beside
  HornSize/AntlerSize), PartGenerators/PartShape cases, GrownParts.of and shapes. A pure rule
  "this genotype hides the vanilla tail" belongs here too (a method on the gene or GrownParts) so the
  designer and the unit tests read the same answer.
- NeoForge: the PartAnchor case in AttachedPartLayer.anchor (walks root, body, tail bone, translateAndRotate,
  adult offset), the tail.visible handling in HdHorseModel, the hair-patch tint, the sheet bake.
Data and saves: a new locus lengthens the genotype code; existing worlds load with every horse reading
"wild type" at the new locus (the migration the other appended genes used; the building session names it
from the last appended gene). Nothing else is stored: the part is recomputed client-side from the
genotype and epigenome. If the gene is parked later, the genes/index.json killswitch rule applies (hard rule
6: never regenerate the index from the folder).
Sync: none new; the part list is computed on the client from CoatData (CoatSyncPayload,
HorseCoatBatchPayload, SpawnCustomHorsePayload). UNVERIFIED that no payload filters unknown genes.
Config: none new. The existing client parts.* switches and the parts detail distance gate it; a horse with
the gene whose parts are switched off MUST show its vanilla tail (the hide is gated by the same switch, or
a player turning parts off would see a horse with no tail at all). Decided (owner): a lion-tailed horse shows its
normal vanilla tail when parts are off or beyond the detail distance (a pop at the distance is accepted).
Tests: a common/ test pinning the allele table, dominance, GrownParts.of (one part for Tuft/n and
Tuft/Tuft, none for n/n), the shape cache key for each size, and the "hides vanilla tail" rule; the
existing parity snapshot and CommonPortabilityTest rerun; a genotype-code round-trip test with the new
locus appended; a designer test (web/) that the part is listed.
Play checks (become the page's Verification tab): the tail is fully hidden and never doubled at rest,
walking, trotting, galloping, rearing and while grazing; the tuft is the horse's tail colour on a bay, a
grey, a dilute and a pied horse; it swishes; a horse with parts switched off shows a normal tail.
Docs: wiki/model-parts.html (Gameplay, Coding); a new gene page for the lion tail (add-a-gene,
three tabs); wiki/horse-designer.html; wiki/gene-tail-color.html gets a one-line note that the lion tail
hides it; wiki/genetics-model.html's priorities if a slot is claimed.

Piece 2 - The foal's lion tail
-------------------------------
What the player sees: a foal of a lion-tailed parent also has the thin tuft tail, smaller, and it is
there from birth.
- Decided: adults AND foals (owner).
- Decided: part must be shown on the foal, so PartKind.showsOnFoal() is true for LION_TAIL (it is false
  for the body kinds in the body-parts treatment; this one is the exception, the owner's call).
- Recommended: scale by AttachedPartLayer's existing FOAL_SCALE and give the foal its own TAIL_BASE offset
  (the foal tail is a different box in a different pose: 3x3x8, xRot -0.7418, offset 0,-1,7).
- Open (building session): whether the foal tuft needs a smaller shape rather than a scaled adult one;
  decide from a yard screenshot (photograph-horses).
Where the code lives: NeoForge only (the foal offset in AttachedPartLayer.anchor, the foal hide in
HdBabyHorseModel); common/ gets the showsOnFoal() value and its test. A foal that grows up changes from
the foal to the adult anchor in the same frame: UNVERIFIED (check the layer's baby flag changes with growth).
Data and saves: none.
Sync: none.
Config: none.
Tests: the showsOnFoal() case in the PartKind test.
Play checks: a newborn foal shows the tuft in place, tiny, no vanilla tail; it follows the growth to adult
with no pop.
Docs: wiki/model-parts.html; the lion tail gene page.

Piece 3 (optional, not asked, Recommended to offer later) - A whipping tail
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
What the player sees: the tail curves along its length as it swishes, instead of moving as one stiff rod.
Recommended: 2-3 segments, each a child of the last, rotating a fixed fraction of the segment above (a pure
formula off the parent's posed rotation; no animation asset, no state), per the existing plan (intake/Attached
model parts.txt F3). Decided (owner): stays an optional, unscheduled piece, re-askable. UNVERIFIED that the posed rotation is still
the posed value at RenderLayer.submit time in 26.1.2 (the existing plan's stage 0 notes the same).
Everything else (data, sync, config, tests, docs) follows piece 1; size M if built. Not to be built unless she
says yes.

5. Decisions
============
A. The ask: a tufted lion tail that replaces the horse's own, hidden by hiding the model part. Decided (owner).
   For breeders and for players who want a look; smallest worthwhile version is piece 1 alone.
B. Fit: horses-only, vanilla intact (a client look on the same entity; nothing replaced). It is NOT a carrot
   or a utility; it is a look. Philosophy says cosmetic-only equipment is not wanted but a coat/look gene is
   breeding, the one thing allowed to be purely aesthetic; so it is a gene, not an item, and it is not
   "better" than the wild tail (no stat). Gene rather than breed: any breed can carry it. Settled
   decisions touched: "Grown parts: two sources and one renderer, boxes built in code, meshes baked at
   run time, no animation asset for any part" (obeyed); "Antler loci: magical, not natural" (same call here);
   "A concept is a breed, not a gene" (a lion-tailed breed would pin this gene; not built here).
C. How met: by spawning (founder frequency) and breeding; survival-reachable; no new item. Explained by the
   gene page, the horse info screen, and the designer. No ProgressTask. Wording of its info line lives in
   common/. Recommended: no feedback messages.
D. Genetics: magical (owner), dominant tuft + wild (owner); alleles appended, never reordered; token must
   not clash with any existing token (check every allele token in genes/ before choosing; UNVERIFIED which
   token is free; Recommended "Lt" / "n" if free). Epigenome numbers on the copy: length and tuft fullness
   (ranges set in the generator; Recommended). Not sex-linked, not lethal, no health effect. Shown on foals
   (owner). Rarity: rare and hand-seeded like antlers (owner), not in every breed's pool; starts near the antlers' share, tuned in the yard, expressing combinations
   in the founder table so there are no invisible carriers in the wild; tune in the yard. Both editors
   (rule 5): the browser designer lists a part it cannot draw (settled 2026-10-02) so it will list this in
   words; CustomHorseSpawnScreen must offer the same gene (UNVERIFIED how it picks genes; check
   web/HorseEditor.java and EditorRules). The info screen lists it by PartKind.label().
E. Breeds: none changed. A lion-tailed breed is designed with the owner (design-a-breed), later.
F. Items and blocks: none. Art: the tuft draws from PartSheet.HAIR (a greyscale region, no external art).
G. Config: none new; the existing parts.* switches must also restore the vanilla tail (piece 1).
H. Saves: see piece 1. The locus lengthens the code; old horses read wild type. Release: no compat bump.
I. Multiplayer: a client-side render of data already synced; nothing for ownership; dedicated-server safe if
   every new client class stays under client/ and common/ stays game-free.
J. Compatibility: other mods that replace the horse model or tail (UNVERIFIED which are on her pack; read
   wiki/compatibility.html). A mod that draws its own tail on the vanilla horse model would draw it through the
   hidden part and show nothing; accepted, mention on the compat page.
K. Performance: per frame, a few extra boxes per lion-tailed horse in view, gated by the parts detail
   distance; the mesh is baked once per PartShape key and cached (existing). Worst case: a thousand
   lion-tailed horses in view, bounded by the same detail distance as horns.
L. Testing: the common/ tests above; a gametest is not needed; the hide and the offsets need the yard and
   photograph-horses (by eye).
M. Release: each piece counts toward the next release; saves keep loading (owner). Never a compat bump.
Braid and tail painting (owner): the rescuing braid stays on the vanilla TAIL tack slot and is drawn nowhere
on a lion-tailed horse (Decided). Its rescue function still works. Consequence she should know, written on
the Gameplay tab: a worn tail braid and a tail glow/stripe are invisible on a lion-tailed horse. Because
BraidLayer and EmissiveCoatLayer submit the WHOLE shared model through masks, hiding the tail part hides their
tail pixels too (UNVERIFIED, see 6).

The three most likely ways it goes wrong for a real player:
1. Two tails: the hide flag is left from another horse because the HD models are shared (Recommended handling:
   set the flag for every horse, every frame, and a play check in a pen of mixed horses).
2. A horse with parts switched off or beyond the detail distance has NO tail (Decided (owner): the hide is
   gated by the same switches and distance as the part).
3. The tuft is the wrong colour or flips colour while the coat is still baking (see UNVERIFIED 1, 3; Decided (owner):
   if the coat has no pixels yet, draw nothing for the part rather than a wrong colour).

6. UNVERIFIED (riskiest first)
==============================
1. The hair-patch colour. The CPU-side pixels of a generated coat may not be readable after upload
   (GeneticCoatTextureFactory.registerCoat builds a DynamicTexture from a NativeImage; it may drop or close
   the image). Check first; if not, compute the tint in common/ from the coat or keep a small colour record at
   bake time beside the texture. Feathering has the same unknown, so settle it once there or here, whichever
   builds first, and do NOT build a second sampler.
2. That setting ModelPart.visible = false on "tail" in HdHorseModel / HdBabyHorseModel hides it in every layer
   that submits the whole model (the coat, BraidLayer, EmissiveCoatLayer) and does not hide the part's
   children, and that the flag is not reset by vanilla's own setupAnim. Check by reading HorseModel and the
   26.1.2 ModelPart.visible/skipDraw semantics, then a one-horse spike.
3. That the coat exists when the part first draws (a coat is baked on a budget; a horse can draw with no
   texture for its first frames).
4. That a TAIL anchor can walk root -> body -> tail and apply the tail's own PI/6 pose without the
   offsets being wrong on the HD adult (the layer comment: walk the root first; the HD adult root carries a
   1.1 scale and a -2.4 y offset). Offsets are "not seen in a running game".
5. Whether ModelPart has xScale/yScale/zScale in 26.1.2 (the plan's "make the tail thin" idea). This file
   builds a thin tail as boxes rather than scaling, so it is only a fallback.
6. That no payload drops an unknown new locus, and the spawn screen and the breed designer read the same
   gene list as the registry.
7. Foal-to-adult anchor switch at growth.

7. Order and size
=================
Order: piece 1 (the part, adult), then piece 2 (the foal), then optionally piece 3. Piece 1 proves the
hide and the anchor; piece 2 is only an offset and a flag. Sizes: piece 1 M (M-L if it also has to build the
shared "walk root to body bone" branch and the hair sampler); piece 2 S; piece 3 M (not scheduled).
Waits on: nothing hard. Shares machinery with Body parts (the first non-head anchor) and Feathering (the
hair-patch colour); whichever of the three builds first builds the shared piece, and the other two reuse it.
Unblocks: a lion-tailed breed, blade/club tails, the chain.
Do NOT do in the same session: piece 1 and a new breed; piece 1 and piece 3; any change to the coat pipeline
(this feature touches no pixels, and a coat change forces a golden re-bake).
