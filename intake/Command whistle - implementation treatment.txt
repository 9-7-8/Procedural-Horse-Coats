Command whistle - implementation treatment

Scoped with the owner, 2026-10-03. Every "Decided" line below is her call; "Recommended"
lines are the scoping session's, not asked; "Open" lines are still to be settled, by whom
is said on the line; "UNVERIFIED" lines are facts this file has not proved and the building
session must check first. Source of truth for what exists is wiki/item-whistles.html
(the whistles), wiki/horse-care.html (bond tiers, BondFollowGoal) and
wiki/gene-aggression.html (horse combat). This file is retired (deleted, per
/process-intake step 8) as each piece ships and moves onto those pages' Gameplay and
Coding tabs.
Release: counts toward a release only when the LAST piece ships (owner). Saves keep loading
(a new attachment whose default is "no order").

Inspired by the Bedrock addon "1,000,000 Horses!" (Cynosia; scripts by InnerTri), surveyed
from its .mcaddon on 2026-10-03: its Horse Whistle cycles Stay / Follow / Wander on
right-click and sneak-applies to all nearby tamed horses. Per the attribution rule
(wiki/compatibility.html#mod-survey) it is credited on item-whistles' Coding tab when
Piece 1 ships. NOTHING is copied from it: the copy surveyed was a re-upload of a paid
Marketplace pack, so no code, art or text from it may enter this repo.


1. The request, as given
========================
"let's add a "command whistle" which controls the mode of any horse it's pointing at when
you blow it, and maybe open up a wheel where you just select what you want? So point at a
horse with the whistle, hold right click, then hover over the option you want from the
wheel, and then release to pick it. The options are stay, wander, follow, hunt monsters
(seeks out hostile mobs), defend me (follows the user and attacks any hostile mobs which
come near), and any other behaviors that would make sense."

Reading: a NEW item (not a mode on the existing area/ender whistles, which keep their one
job of calling horses in). Aim at an owned horse, hold use, a radial wheel opens; move the
cursor over a slice and release to give that horse a standing ORDER. The order persists
until changed. The owner then added Guard here, Go home, Graze nearby and Rejoin herd to
the wheel, and sneak-hold to order every nearby horse at once.

Also said, and NOT part of this file: "I'd love to eventually add idle animations, and loop
them into our social system, but that's the lowest possible priority." See section 2.


2. In scope, and what is not
============================
In scope
- The command whistle item: recipe, equestrian-supplier trade, tooltip, lang, art,
  advancement / ProgressTask row.
- A per-horse ORDER, saved: Stay, Follow, Wander, Graze nearby, Guard here, Go home,
  Hunt monsters, Defend me, Rejoin herd (= no order).
- The radial wheel screen, opened by holding use while aimed at an owned horse, or while
  sneaking (anywhere) for all owned horses within 16 blocks.
- Gating: bond for every order, plus a fighting gene for the three combat orders.
- Showing a horse's current order: the whistle's hover on the aimed horse, the H browser
  roster, the horse info screen.

Not in scope (re-askable)
- Idle animations (sleep, roll, rear, buck) and tying them to the social system. Owner:
  "lowest possible priority". Needs its own treatment; nothing here builds toward it.
- Changing the area or ender whistles beyond "a recall clears an order" (Piece 1).
- Orders to horses you do not own, or to untamed horses.
- Pursuit for gene-driven aggression. The no-chase rule on wiki/gene-aggression.html
  stands for the GENE; only an ORDER may pursue, and only inside a leash (section 5).
- A keybind that opens the wheel without the item (offered, not chosen).
- Ordering donkeys, mules, camels, llamas: horses only, the same as every goal in
  HorseCareHandler, which adds goals to Horse, not AbstractHorse.


3. What already exists, so the building session does not redo it
================================================================
All paths: NF = neoforge-26.1.2/src/main/java/com/example/horsegenetics/neoforge,
COMMON = common/src/main/java/com/example/horsegenetics/common.

Whistles

  COOLDOWN_TICKS = 60; private ownedBy() duplicates HorseOwnership.isOwner. No sneak
  branch, no useOn, no interactLivingEntity, no hold behaviour.
- NF/server/WhistleCalls.java: walk-vs-teleport (tryWalk, WALK_PATH_BLOCKS = 16), calls
  ended in onServerTick; ACTIVE map, never saved. NF/server/WhistleCallGoal.java at
  priority 1 (MOVE+LOOK).
- NF/item/EnderWhistleItem.java, NF/server/EnderWhistleCalls.java (BIND_BOND_TIER = 2),
  NF/data/BoundHorse.java, ModDataComponents.BOUND_HORSE.
- NF/client/WhistleKeyBindings.java + WhistleKeyHandler.java -> BlowWhistlesPayload ->
  NF/server/WhistleBlowing.blowAll(); COMMON/care/WhistleSelection.pick()
  (WhistleSelectionTest).
- Data: recipe/{basic,golden,echo,ender}_whistle.json; advancement/husbandry/whistle_*.json;
  villager_trade/equestrian_supplier/level_2..5/sell_*_whistle.json.
- COMMON/progress/ProgressTask.java: WHISTLE_BASIC, WHISTLE_GOLDEN, WHISTLE_ECHO,
  WHISTLE_ENDER (Group.HUSBANDRY); NF/advancement/ProgressTaskTrigger.java.
- Whistle tooltips are Component.literal, not lang keys. The command whistle's wording
  goes in common/ (hard rule 1, "the wording lives in common/").

Goals, and how they collide
- No registerGoals mixin. Every custom goal is added on EntityJoinLevelEvent, to Horse
  only, skipping a horse that already has it. HorseCareHandler.onHorseJoin:
  HorseEscapeGoal 0, InspectHoldGoal 0, WhistleCallGoal 1, CrouchFeedGoal / FoodTemptGoal
  / HungerFoodGoal 3, BondFollowGoal 4, WildHerdGoal 6, BondFollowGoal.Watch 8.
- Others: HorseAggroHandler (HorseMeleeGoal 3, target WildHorseForgetTargetGoal 1,
  HorsePanicGoal replaces PanicGoal); HerdGoals (Spar 3, DamAndFoal 4, StallionGuard 5,
  Displace 5, HeatAttraction 6, GroomAndRest 7); SunShadeGoal 1; LycanTemperGoal 2;
  BloodHuntGoal 3; NightWatchGoal 4; CowboyHerdGoal 3; cart goals via
  carts/util/CartGoalAdder.
- Vanilla: FloatGoal 0, RunAroundLikeCrazyGoal 1, BreedGoal 2, TemptGoal 3,
  FollowParentGoal 4, WaterAvoidingRandomStrollGoal 6, LookAtPlayerGoal 7.
- Equal priority never interrupts; ties go to the goal that started first (WhistleCallGoal
  javadoc). MOVE is contested at 1, 3 and 4.
- wiki/tools/bake-behaviour-hierarchy.mjs -> wiki/behaviour-hierarchy.html.

Per-horse state and sync
- NF/data/ModAttachments.java: AttachmentType.builder(default).serialize(...), optional
  .copyOnDeath() and .sync(StreamCodec). Built-in sync precedent: CART_METRES, PREGNANT,
  COWBOY_BRAND, HORSE_GEAR.
- HorseCareSyncPayload(entityId, bond, inHerd) -> client/ClientHorseCareCache, so the
  client already knows bond for horses it tracks.
- NF/data/HorseCareAttachment.behaviourTier(): 0 = 0-30, 1 = 31-60, 2 = 61-80, 3 = 81-100.
- Payloads registered in NF/network/ModNetworking.java.

Combat
- Horses CAN fight already: HorseAggroHandler.addAttackDamage gives EntityType.HORSE
  ATTACK_DAMAGE = MagicFighterGene.BASELINE_DAMAGE (3.0); HorseMeleeGoal (extends
  MeleeAttackGoal, 10-tick swing, rears); mixin/HorseRearFightMixin keeps a rearing horse's
  AI running. Any horse with a target fights; nothing yet GIVES a tame horse a target on
  command.
- COMMON/genetics/genes/AggressionGene.java (RADIUS 16, INTERVAL_TICKS 20, MAX_TARGETS 8,
  TIMES 3 x TARGETS 5); GuardianGene.java (RADIUS 16, INTERVAL_TICKS 10, MAX_TARGETS 4);
  MagicFighterGene.java (BASELINE_DAMAGE 3.0, MIN_DAMAGE 1.0).
- NF/server/GeneReactionHandler.onOwnerHurt: Guardian targets mobs (never players) that
  hurt the owner within 16, and holds ground. Defend me is the ORDERED, gene-gated cousin
  of this; read it first, including its comment on why the first version scanned every
  hit in the world.
- NF/server/MobGroups.java: matches(group, candidate), isHostile(candidate). "Monsters"
  for every combat order is MobGroups.isHostile minus the never-target tag (section 5).
- NF/server/Passification.java: Passification.suppresses vetoes targets. An order never
  overrides it.

Ownership
- NF/server/HorseOwnership.java: isOwner(Horse, UUID), ownerId, bindRefusal, ownerName.
  Use this, not another private copy like WhistleItem.ownedBy.

Screens opened from item use
- client/CustomHorseSpawnEggClient handles RightClickBlock / RightClickItem /
  EntityInteractSpecific / EntityInteract, cancels them and calls setScreen;
  client/HorseInfoInteraction does the same at HIGHEST priority. That is the precedent for
  stopping vanilla from mounting the horse or opening its inventory when the whistle is used
  on it.

Home and tethering
- Stalls: item/StallSignItem, server/StallSignHandler, StallDetector, data/StallData
  (SavedData). Send home: server/StallRecall, COMMON/care/SendHome.java, used by the H
  browser's HorseRecallPayload. Go home is a CALL into this path, not a second one.
- Leads: server/HorseLeads (untieFor). Hitching: server/CowboyHitchHandler (not read).

Looks built, is not
- No stay, sit, wander radius or home position exists anywhere: no restrictTo or
  setHomeTo in the code base. Stay and Graze nearby are new.
- No radial, pie or hold-to-open UI exists: no releaseUsing, getUseDuration, onUseTick or
  ItemUseAnimation anywhere. The wheel is new.
- BondFollowGoal is not a "Follow" order: it is bond-driven, tier 3 only, and gives up. A
  Follow ORDER is new behaviour that must suppress or take over from it, not edit it.
- Aggression's "hunts monsters" alleles already hold a 16-block radius. A horse with those
  alleles needs no order to fight, and an order does not switch that off.


4. The pieces
=============

Piece 1 - SHIPPED 2026-10-03: the item, the order attachment, the wheel, the payload, and
Stay / Follow / Wander / Rejoin herd. What it is and how it is built: wiki/item-whistles.html
#command and #command-code. Pieces 2 and 3 plug into its HorseOrder enum (append-only, saved
by name), HorseOrders (gate + wording), HORSE_ORDER (anchor already stored) and the wheel
(HorseOrders.wheel(): append the new slices; the wheel sizes itself). Its order goals sit at
priority 4, not 1, so needs pre-empt them (wiki, #command-code) - a Guard / Hunt goal that
must fight should sit with them, below hunger.

Piece 2 - Graze nearby, Guard here, Go home
-------------------------------------------
What the player sees: three more slices. Graze nearby lets a horse roam but never far from
where you left it. Go home sends it to its stall. Guard here keeps it on the spot, kicking
any monster that comes close.

Decided
- These three are on the wheel, at bond tier 2.
- Guard here is a combat order: it needs a fighting gene (Piece 3's gate, shipped).
- Targets for every combat order: hostile mobs EXCEPT creepers (Piece 3 built this).

Recommended
- Graze nearby: a tethered stroll, radius 12 around the anchor; outside it the horse walks
  back. Its own goal, not vanilla's home radius: 26.1.2 has Mob.setHomeTo / isWithinHome,
  but vanilla saves it and a lead sets it (PathfinderMob), so it would fight leads
  (answered by Piece 3; wiki/item-whistles.html#combat-code).
- Go home: calls the existing send-home path (server/StallRecall + common/care/SendHome);
  a horse with no stall refuses with "has no stall". It is a one-shot: the order becomes
  Rejoin herd when the horse arrives. Not a second recall implementation.
- Guard here: Stay, plus targets hostile mobs within 6 blocks of the anchor; never chases
  beyond 8; returns to the anchor when the target dies or leaves.
- Creeper exclusion: the tag horsegenetics:command_never_target already exists (Piece 3).

Where the code lives: radii and the "return to anchor" rule in common/ (HorseOrders); the
goals in NeoForge.
Data and saves: anchor already stored by Piece 1. Nothing new saved.
Config (server): graze_radius (default 12, 4..32), guard_radius (6, 3..16). Off state:
none; a bad value is clamped, never crashes a load.
Tests: HorseOrdersTest additions (radius maths, return rule); gametest: a guarding horse
with a fighting gene kills a zombie inside 6 and ignores a creeper; a grazing horse
leaves a 12 radius and returns.
Play checks: a grazing horse in an open field stays near; Go home with and without a stall.
Docs: item-whistles, horse-care, stables (Go home), behaviour-hierarchy rebake.

Piece 3 - SHIPPED 2026-10-03: the fighting-gene gate, Hunt monsters and Defend me. What it is
and how it is built: wiki/item-whistles.html#fighters and #combat-code. Piece 2 plugs into it:
HorseOrders.fighter (the gate - Guard here is a third combat() order, so the refusal and the
wheel's greying come free), HorseOrders.Leash and ServerConfig.orderLeash (add guard_radius
there), OrderCombatGoal (its centre() and its leash are per order; Guard here is Hunt monsters
with a smaller radius), and the never-target tag, which already exists.


5. Decisions
============
Covered in the pieces, plus:
- B. Fit: horses only, adds without altering vanilla (vanilla horse behaviour is untouched
  when no order is given: Rejoin herd is the default). Utility, and it serves the genetics
  "once removed", like the whistles already do; the combat gate makes fighting genes worth
  breeding for. Bends one settled call (Aggression's no-chase), asked and decided above.
- C. Explained by: the item tooltip ("Hold use on a horse to give it an order; sneak for all
  nearby"), a new ProgressTask row "Give a horse an order" (Recommended: Group.HUSBANDRY,
  beside the WHISTLE_* rows), the wheel's hover reasons.
- D. Genetics: no new gene. Reads Aggression, Guardian, Magic Fighter. Rule 5 (designer /
  spawn screen) untouched: neither edits orders.
- E. Breeds: none.
- F. Items: one item, its art (Recommended: recolour of the basic whistle; an art pack under
  intake/potential-assets may be used, logged per use-an-art-pack). Stack size 1. A section
  on wiki/item-whistles.html, not a page of its own (Recommended: it is a whistle and the
  page already covers four). No per-wood variants.
- G. Config: Piece 2 and 3's radii and break-off, server side, clamped. Nothing client side
  (Recommended).
- I. Multiplayer: owner only; the server re-checks everything. Dedicated-server safe: the
  wheel and the interact cancel are client classes, registered on the client only.
- J. Compatibility: modded hostiles count via MobGroups.isHostile; packs extend the never-
  target tag. Other mods that also hook right-click on a horse may fight the interact
  cancel; same priority as HorseInfoInteraction (HIGHEST).
- K. Performance: every order goal's canUse reads one attachment; combat scans only for
  ordered horses, on an interval, bounded. A thousand unordered horses cost one attachment
  read each per goal tick. Orders never force chunk loads; a horse in an unloaded chunk
  simply does not tick.
- L. Testing: rules and geometry in common/ unit tests; behaviours as gametests / yard pens;
  the wheel's feel only by eye.
- M. Release: only the last piece counts toward a release (owner). Saves keep loading.

Implications sweep
- Genetics model / registry: untouched, read only (fighting-gene gate).
- Coat pipeline, eye colour, body space, grown parts: untouched, no visuals on the horse.
- Breeding / pedigree / stats: untouched. A foal is born with no order.
- Breeds, spawning: untouched; wild horses cannot be ordered.
- Horse body (speed/health/size): touched only by break-off health.
- Care: touched. Bond gates orders; Wander suppresses bond-follow and herd-following;
  hunger may break a Stay; herds: an ordered horse still counts as a herd member for
  healing (Recommended), it just does not follow the herd.
- AI goals / behaviour hierarchy: touched heavily; rebake it.
- Items / carrots: one new item. Carrots untouched.
- Tack and gear: untouched.
- Villagers: equestrian supplier gains a trade row.
- Realm / portals / dimension / stables: dimension change clears an order; Go home uses
  stalls; the public realm's protections are not bypassed (an order cannot be given there
  to a horse you do not own, which ownership already prevents).
- H menu: Recommended to show each horse's order in the roster (read-only); its existing
  Send home stays.
- Stasis / bank: storing a horse clears its order.
- Designer / spawn screen: untouched.
- Gene creator / breed designer / web: untouched.
- Config: Pieces 2-3 add server keys (procedures/add-a-config-option.txt).
- Other mods: modded hostiles counted; interact-hook collisions possible.
- Advancements / checklist: one new ProgressTask row + advancement.
- 1.12.2 backport: rules, wording and wheel geometry in common/; the rest is thin.
- Wiki: item-whistles owns it; horse-care, gene-aggression, guardian, magic-fighter,
  stables, items and behaviour-hierarchy change.

The three most likely ways this goes wrong for a real player
1. Holding use on a horse mounts it or opens its inventory instead of the wheel: the
   interact must be cancelled on the client at HIGHEST before vanilla sees it (Piece 1
   play check; UNVERIFIED 1).
2. A stayed horse drifts: some other priority-0/1 goal (HorseEscapeGoal, SunShadeGoal,
   RunAroundLikeCrazyGoal, WhistleCallGoal) moves it and nothing walks it back. Decided
   handling: those may move it; OrderStayGoal returns it to the anchor. Gametest it.
3. A hunter dies far from home or a defender drags itself into a creeper: the leash, the
   creeper tag and break-off health are the handling; yard pens prove them.


6. UNVERIFIED
=============
Riskiest first.
1. Hold-to-open on an ENTITY. Vanilla fires the entity interaction once on press; there is
   no "use duration" while aimed at a mob. The likely shape is: cancel the interact on the
   client, open the wheel, and read the use key's isDown() each frame; release = pick.
   Check in 26.1.2 sources how Minecraft.options.keyUse behaves while a Screen is open
   (a screen normally swallows key-up), and whether the wheel must be an overlay
   rendered over the world rather than a Screen. Spike this before anything else.
3. That a ridden horse's order goals do not run (rider control drives movement; see
   gene-aggression's "a ridden horse will not pursue"). Confirm "riding pauses" needs no
   extra code beyond canUse checking for a passenger.
4. Built-in attachment .sync firing when the order changes on the server (precedent:
   HORSE_GEAR). Confirm the client sees an order changed from the H browser too.
(2 and 5 were answered by Piece 3: wiki/item-whistles.html#combat-code.)
