AA gene which is primarily designed to be consumed by other genes and used polygenetically. 
While it's known as stormfed, that's only because that was the specific allele that was discovered first. 
In reality, this gene just builds up an interior resoivor of power while a certain condition is met. 
All alleles are homozygous recessive. 
The epigenetics in each allele control the rate at which power accrues, the rate at which it decay
Known alleles, other than wild type:
Storm: stores energy during storms
Rain, Snow
Lightning: gets a huge burst of energy after being struck by lightining (also, does not take damage from lightning)
Snow: slowly gains energy while standing on snow
Water, Lava: see snow

To note again: this gene does not DO anything with the energy. It only controls storing up the magical energy, and other genes will need to be made to consume it.
