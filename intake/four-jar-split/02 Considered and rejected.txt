FOUR-JAR SPLIT - 02 CONSIDERED AND REJECTED

Everything offered during scoping and not taken, plus the shapes the plan went through. Kept so a
later session does not re-propose a dead option without knowing why it died. The owner chose the
option marked "TAKEN" in each; the others are the trash.

1. THE FIRST DRAFT (superseded twice)
- FIVE jars: Core, Coats, Equipment, Quality of Life, All-in-One. Core was the small required jar
  with a slim record and the framework; QOL was an optional add-on. Replaced by FOUR jars when the
  owner merged Core and QOL into Base and renamed Coats to Breeding. Anything in old notes that says
  "Core", "Coats" or "QOL jar" is dead.
- Piece order in the first draft: 1 Core extraction, 2 Equipment, 3 QOL, 4 Coats + pipeline. Now
  three pieces (Base replaces 1 and 3).

2. WHO OWNS THE GENETICS (first round)
- TAKEN: the Coats/Breeding jar owns all of genetics, breeding, stats, breeds, designer, carrots, papers.
- Rejected: Coats = looks only, with breeding/pedigree/stats/carrots moved to QOL (a large rewrite:
  stats and pedigree are woven into the genome today).
- Rejected: a fourth shared core jar holding genome, record, pedigree and stats, with the add-on
  only drawing the look.

3. QOL SCOPE (first round)
- TAKEN (owner wrote her own answer): stasis and changes to breeding (vet kit) go to Breeding; care,
  bonding, whistles and signs go to QOL.
- Rejected: realm, stables and villagers in QOL (they would then need a no-breeds fallback).
- Rejected: a separate fourth "world" jar for realm, stables and villagers.

4. EQUIPMENT SCOPE
- TAKEN: gear slots, tack, shoes, pads, carts are Equipment; jumps, gates, posts and bench go to QOL
  (now Base).
- Rejected: everything on that list in Equipment.
- Rejected: carts as their own jar (72 files, derived from three other mods; a separate jar would
  isolate their licences, but it adds a jar nobody asked for).

5. HOW THE JARS SHARE CODE
- TAKEN: a required base jar the player downloads, add-ons on top, or the all-in-one instead.
- Rejected: a shared library embedded in each jar with Jar-in-Jar (users would see only the jars they
  named; NeoForge loads the newest copy once). Not taken because the owner wanted an explicit base.
- Rejected: each jar fully standalone with the shared code copied in (duplicate classes when two jars
  are installed, so subsets break).
- Rejected: no shared jar at all, Equipment and QOL requiring Coats (fails "any subset").

6. THE HORSE RECORD
- TAKEN: a genome-agnostic record in the base (Breeding attaches the genome).
- Rejected: the whole record including the genome in the base (the base would be most of common/,
  about 250 genetics files; "small" would no longer hold).
- Rejected: no record without Breeding; Equipment and QOL then need Breeding.
- Rejected, for the no-Breeding horse: "record only, no generated names" and "no record at all". TAKEN:
  a plain record, a generated name, reduced screens.

7. EXTENSION POINTS
- TAKEN: info-screen tabs, H-browser tabs, ProgressTask per jar, per-jar registries, public API.
- All four options in that question were taken (it was a multi-select).
- Rejected: leaving the screens hard-coded (they cannot compile without every jar).

8. BORDERLINE PIECES
- Bench: the first recommendation (bench to Equipment, braid to Breeding) was rejected. TAKEN: bench to
  QOL/Base, braid to Equipment, overlay only with Breeding. Rejected: bench in QOL with the dye part moved
  to Equipment; bench dyes only when Equipment present; bench dye recipes quietly vanishing.
- Rider comfort: rejected "all of it to QOL except armour generators" and "all of it to Equipment".
  TAKEN: comfort, leads, leash mixins, rider fade -> QOL; right-click-equips and armour generators ->
  Equipment.
- Hunger gating in Breeding with a plain fallback: first answer (hunger Breeding-only, absent without
  it). REPLACED by D15: hunger and healing move to Base with a toggle; only diet stays in Breeding.
- Bareback steering and bolting: rejected Equipment and Breeding; TAKEN Base.

9. IDS, NAMES AND ORDER
- Rejected: independent per-jar versions; lockstep numbers publishing only changed jars.
- Rejected: each jar its own namespace (breaks every saved key; a compat bump with a heavy converter).
- Rejected: split first and rename later; both in one compat release.
- Rejected jar names: "Genetics" (ixoras_horses_genetics) and keeping the Coats id with a Breeding
  display name. Rejected: leaving the name to the building session.
- Rejected base names: ixoras_horses_qol (misleading for a required framework) and ixoras_horses_core.
- Rejected: piece order "Coats before Equipment" and "one big piece". Rejected for the merged shape:
  "framework only first, then QOL moved in" (a four-piece plan).
- Rejected size-gate options: report only; fixed target now (could not be measured); drop the goal.

10. SAVES AND REDUNDANT COMBOS
- TAKEN: a world that used an add-on refuses to load without it.
- Rejected: preserved untouched and round-tripped when absent (would have kept every world loadable);
  dropped on save (silently strips genomes).
- Rejected "used" definitions: "the part was loaded when the world was last saved" (one launch locks the
  world); "a hand-kept signature list per part" (goes stale).
- Rejected escape-hatch options: no override; ask at load on singleplayer only. TAKEN: a server.toml
  switch, default off.
- Redundant combos: TAKEN incompatible declarations. Rejected: let NeoForge fail however it fails;
  make parts defer to the all-in-one.
- Rejected API options: stable within one 0.X series; internal only. TAKEN: documented, unstable until
  1.0.0.

11. SEX, GELDING AND BREEDING WITHOUT THE ADD-ON
- Rejected: sex in Breeding too (a Base horse would have no sex and every nameplate/herd-role line would
  handle "unknown"); sex and gelded both staying in Base.
- Rejected: vanilla hand-breeding disabled without Breeding; heat and pregnancy as part of Base.
- Rejected: dropping the gelded flag when Breeding is absent (it would un-geld every horse on the next
  save).
- Sex-off options rejected: sex off also turns off autonomous covers; sex off treats every horse as a mare.
- Rejected for hunger: hunger moves but healing gate stays in Breeding (hunger would do nothing alone);
  two separate toggles.
- Rejected for wild herds: herd behaviour only with all turnover in Breeding; everything except breeds
  including the realm hand-off (would pull the realm into Base).

12. WHERE THE TRADES LIVE
- Rejected: Breeding owns the professions and Equipment adds trades through a hook. TAKEN: the leatherworker
  and metalsmith go to Equipment whole.

13. DOWNLOAD PAGE
- Rejected: deciding the page layout inside the treatment. TAKEN: handled by GitHub releases first, then
  host sites; each jar its own download with its own "requires / incompatible" text.

14. THINGS NOBODY ASKED FOR AND THIS PLAN DOES NOT DO
- A Core jar as a separate download (dead).
- Per-feature jars (carts, realm, jumps).
- Making any add-on work on top of the all-in-one.
- Changing any gameplay beyond the listed toggles and rules.
