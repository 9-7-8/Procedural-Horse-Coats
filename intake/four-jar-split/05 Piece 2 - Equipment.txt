FOUR-JAR SPLIT - 05 PIECE 2: THE EQUIPMENT ADD-ON (size L)

Goal: make Equipment a separate unit that needs only Base. After this piece, a build of Base +
Equipment (no Breeding code) compiles, boots and passes its own gametests, while the shipped
artifact is still the one all-in-one jar. Needs piece 1 shipped (the hooks and registries).
Read 00, 01, 03 and 04 first.

WHAT THE PLAYER SEES
Nothing changes in the all-in-one. A Base + Equipment run (dev only until piece 3) gives: the 19-slot Gear
tab, tack slots and right-click-equips, carts, the rescuing braid, the leatherworker and metalsmith
villagers and their trades. The braid draws its overlay only when Breeding is present.

WHAT EQUIPMENT OWNS (D14, D17, D19, D20)
- The gear slots: HorseTackSlot (N/entity), the HORSE_GEAR attachment and HorseGear (N/data), HorseGearMenu,
  HorseGearScreen, TackEquipHandler (right-click-equips; config behaviour.rightclick_equips_tack), the payloads
  TackSlotPayload and OpenHorseGearPayload, tack looks and tooltips (TackClientExtensions, TackTooltip), loot
  SetTackTintFunction and AddHorseTackModifier (they use Base's tint component), the 17 gear item tags.
- The rescuing braid: RescuingBraidItem, RescuingBraidHandler, BraidLayer, and its recipe.
- All carts: the whole N/carts tree (about 72 files: entity, item, container, network, client, util, CartsConfig,
  CartWood, HorseCarts), the cart payloads (RequestCartUpdate, ActionKey, CoachmanMove, OpenSupplyCart,
  UpdateDrawn), CART_METRES, the cart mixins, :common-equipment (CartDraft, CartKind), cart config files,
  cart advancements, cart tutorial pages, the cart creative tab, GeneratedCarts and HorsePoweredCompat, tools/
  bake-carts.mjs.
- Armour and materials: GeneratedArmour, ModdedArmour, ModdedMaterials, MaterialScan, GeneratedPack.
- Villagers: equestrian_leatherworker and equestrian_metalsmith (professions, POI blocks, recipes, five trade levels
  each).
NOT Equipment: the Equestrian Bench and its dyeing (Base, D22); rider fade and leads (Base, D19); the
HorseTwoRidersMixin gene mixin (Breeding; carts reach it through a hook).

STEPS, IN ORDER

STEP 1 - INVENTORY AND BASELINE
- Re-list every file by grep (names above come from a survey; do not trust them). Record the baseline: cart
  gametests, MaterialScanTest, CartDraftTest, the gear gametests in ModGameTests (around the TackEquipHandler and
  HorseTackSlot tests), a server boot.
CHECKPOINT: the list is written in the session log; baseline recorded.

STEP 2 - THE EQUIPMENT MODULES
- Move :common-equipment's classes (CartDraft, CartKind, their tests) as in file 04 step 2; create the NeoForge
  side as a set of packages/source set that depends on Base only. Decide the physical layout (a second Gradle
  module :neoforge-equipment versus a source set inside :neoforge-26.1.2) at build time: Recommended a separate
  module, so an accidental Breeding import fails to compile. The all-in-one task (piece 3) merges them.
- Each unit gets its own mixins json (CartHorseMixin, CartEntityInvoker; client: CartClientLevelMixin,
  CartLocalPlayerMixin, CartModelManagerMixin, CartModelPartMixin). Move their entries out of
  RES/horsegenetics.mixins.json. `required: true` and defaultRequire 1 mean a miss fails at load: verify by boot.
CHECKPOINT: build green; Base + Equipment compile without any Breeding class on the classpath.

STEP 3 - THE BRIDGE: HorseDraft AND THE STAT PROVIDER
- HorseDraft is the carts' only link to horse stats (pullOf, speedOf, retention, workRate, carriesTwoRiders). Rewrite it
  to ask Base's StatProvider (file 04 step 4). Default: vanilla movement-speed attribute and a plain pull value.
  Breeding supplies the genetic values and carriesTwoRiders (from HorseTwoRidersMixin's gene).
- AbstractDrawnEntity, HorsePoweredCompat, HorseTwoRidersMixin and ModAttributes call HorseDraft today: update them.
- CartItem calls HorseProgress.complete(PLACE_CART): that goes through the ProgressTask registry (file 04 step 7).
CHECKPOINT: CartDraftTest green; a gametest that a vanilla horse (no genome) pulls a cart at a sensible speed; in the
all-in-one the same cart with a genetic horse behaves as before (compare against the baseline).

STEP 4 - THE GEAR TAB AS A PLUG-IN
- Remove the Gear tab, the paper doll (HorseInfoScreen ~:190, :2315-2339), the gear picker (~:779-976 using
  TackSlotPayload and HorseTackSlot), the Dress button (~:687-698) and drawDraught (~:1570-1610, which uses CartDraft,
  CartKind and CART_METRES) out of HorseInfoScreen and register them as Equipment's tab(s) (file 04 step 7). Line
  numbers are from a survey: re-find them.
- Overview used to carry the tack slots and now says how many pieces the horse wears and links to Gear
  (HorseInfoScreen ~:1452 comment): that line is drawn only when Equipment is present.
- HorseTackSlot.verify() is called from HorseGenetics.java at startup: call it from Equipment's constructor.
CHECKPOINT: Gear tab identical in the all-in-one; absent in a Base-only run, with no error and no empty tab.

STEP 5 - THE BRAID AND THE OVERLAY HOOK
- GeneticHorseRenderer :120-124 and :194 read HorseTackSlot.MANE and TAIL for BraidLayer. Replace with the
  OverlaySource hook (file 04 step 4): Equipment contributes the braid layer, and it is drawn only where a renderer that
  supports overlays exists (Breeding's GeneticHorseRenderer). Without Breeding the item, slot and handler still work
  and nothing is drawn (D20).
- RescuingBraidHandler's "sends home" half uses SendHome and stall logic (stall signs are Base; the realm is Breeding):
  degrade quietly when Breeding is absent (no realm destination) and write the event to Base's log (the Rescues and
  Homings kinds exist in HorseEventLog).
CHECKPOINT: a Base-only horse with a braid in the tail slot: no crash, no overlay; with Breeding: overlay drawn.

STEP 6 - REGISTRIES, PAYLOADS, CONFIG, TASKS
- Move Equipment's entries out of ModItems, ModBlocks, ModMenus, ModAttachments, ModDataComponents, ModLootFunctions,

- Config: carts keep horsegenetics-carts.toml and the -client file (their names change with the rename);

- Tasks: PLACE_CART, HITCH_CART, six DRIVE_* tasks (ProgressTask.java about :220-247) move to Equipment's
  contribution; so do the cart advancement JSONs (drive_* x6, hitch_cart, place_cart) and the cart tutorial pages.
  DYE_TACK (:212) goes to Base with the bench.
CHECKPOINT: with Equipment installed everything is registered once and in a stable order (compare registry dumps
before and after); without it nothing from Equipment is referenced by Base.

STEP 7 - VILLAGERS
- Move the equestrian_leatherworker and equestrian_metalsmith professions, their POI blocks (the "post" blocks and

CHECKPOINT: a villager trade works in a Base + Equipment run; Breeding's villagers still work in the all-in-one.

STEP 9 - A BASE + EQUIPMENT RUN AND TESTS
- Add run configs for Base + Equipment (client, server, gametest). Add a Base + Equipment gametest suite: slots
