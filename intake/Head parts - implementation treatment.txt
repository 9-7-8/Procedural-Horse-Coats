Head parts - implementation treatment

BONE VERSIONS (owner, 2026-10-02): every grown part that realistically has bone in it gets a BONE VERSION: a set of
alleles and/or bands that draws it as bare bone on a skeleton horse - bone-coloured,
unlit, nothing soft on it (no keratin sheen, membrane, feathers, leaves, glow or dust).
Wings, for instance, become the bones alone with no membrane.
Every part here with bone in it needs one, and the skeleton breeds may carry it;
see wiki/model-parts.html#bone-versions and procedures/add-a-gene.txt (dd, 10b).

Scoped with the owner. Every "Decided" line below is her call; "Recommended" lines are
mine, not asked; "UNVERIFIED" lines are facts this file has not proved and the building
session must check first. Source of truth for what exists is wiki/model-parts.html (the
attached-parts layer) plus the unicorn and antler gene pages. This file is the handoff for
the head and neck parts; the dragon horns (Slice A) shipped 2026-10-02 and are written up on
wiki/gene-dragon-horns.html and model-parts.html, which is the worked example for the rest. It is retired (deleted, per /process-intake
step 7) as each slice ships and moves onto those pages' Gameplay and Coding tabs.

The parts, and nothing more
===========================
Ear fins/frills, cheek spikes, brow ridge, goat beard, ruff. Decided: only
parts stuck to the head (the ruff rides the neck, which is the same bone as the head's
parent - see its section). NOT in this file: dorsal spines, crystalline growths, wings,
tails, hoof feathering, tusks (all on model-parts.html's Roadmap tab, unchanged).

What already exists, so the building session does not redo it
==============================================================
- The renderer for grown parts is built and proven (seen working in the dev yard):
  AttachedPartLayer draws a mesh hung on an already-animated bone, so a part needs NO
  animation of its own. One horn and an antler rack (two kinds, one per side) ship.
- common/parts/: PartKind (a value per kind of part; HORN, ANTLER_RIGHT, ANTLER_LEFT today,
  each carrying its anchor and default sheet region), PartAnchor (an enum of PLACES, never a
  bone name; FOREHEAD, CROWN_RIGHT, CROWN_LEFT today), PartNode (one box), PartShape (the cache
  key: kind, style, size bucket), PartGenerators (the one door from a shape to a box list),
  HornGenerator and AntlerGenerator (the two worked examples), PartSheet (the 64x64 greyscale
  sheet in 16x16 regions; eight of sixteen painted, HAIR among them), AttachedPart (a shape plus the numbers
  applied at draw time, with a base and a tip colour).
- genetics/GrownParts.of is the ONE door the client asks; the render layer never learns which
  loci grant parts. A new part is a line there, not a change on the Minecraft side.
- wiki/model-parts.html's Coding tab lists exactly what adding a part takes. That list is the
  spine of the plan below: a value on PartKind (anchor, sheet region) and cases in styles(),
  sizeBuckets(), showsOnFoal() and glowRegions(); a generator that emits PartNodes with every
  parent before its children (PartGeneratorTest walks every mesh the mod can build); a case in
  PartGenerators.build and PartShape.nominalLength/stretchTo; if it needs a new anchor, a value
  on PartAnchor and a case in AttachedPartLayer.anchor with ONE OFFSET PER HORSE MODEL (adult and
  foal share bone names and nothing else); a region in PartSheet plus a paint() call in
  neoforge-26.1.2/tools/bake-part-sheet.mjs; a gene that returns the part and a line in GrownParts.of.
  NOT on that list, and not needed: a new render layer, a new render-state field, a new config key,
  a texture per colour. The antlers proved that. Do not add one.
- Variation is sorted by what it costs: topology (segment counts, branching, twist) goes in the
  PartShape and bakes a mesh, quantised, so the set of meshes is finite and bounded; proportion and
  pose (length, girth, lean) is a free pose-stack transform; counts (an antler's tines) bake the
  maximum and show the first k with the next scaled by the fraction (PartModel.Slice.shown and
  PartNode.group); colour and glow are arguments to the draw. One greyscale sheet serves every colour.
- The deferred-submit trap (read model-parts.html before touching PartModel): never vary a shared
  mesh by mutating it outside PartModel's setupAnim; two horses in one frame would swap parts.
- The unicorn horn's colour is a separate codominant gene (HornColourGene: Wht, Red Org Yel Grn Blu Ind
  Vio, Pnk Blk Gry, and Cha - a chaos colour written on the allele copy; two different copies make a
  two-tone part, the first-listed colour at the base, the other at the tip; each copy carries HUE and TONE
  epigenome numbers). Glow and dust are two more genes. The antlers instead keep a TINT on the granting allele.
- The part's config is only the client's parts.* switches (parts.enabled and friends); every part is
  gated by them and nothing else. Do not add any.

Decisions that apply to all of them
===================================
- Decided: everything CAN STACK. A horse may wear a unicorn horn, antlers and dragon horns together,
  each at its own anchor and offsets. There is no exclusion rule and nothing is suppressed. The offsets
  below are chosen so the DEFAULT sizes do not intersect, but extremes of the epigenetic ranges can clip
  through each other; that is accepted, and is a Verification-tab check, not a bug to engineer away.
- Decided: COLOUR IS ITS OWN GENE PER PART, the way the unicorn horn's is: one colour gene per part, each a
  codominant locus with the horn's allele set (white wild type, the rainbow, pink, black, grey, and chaos),
  two different copies giving a two-tone part, each copy carrying hue and tone numbers. A colour gene does
  nothing to a horse without its part. Each is a subclass of AbstractPartColourGene (shipped 2026-10-02);
  DragonHornColourGene is the three-line example.
- Decided: foals wear the SOFT parts only: ear fins/frills, goat beard and ruff. The hard parts - cheek
  spikes, brow ridge - do not show on a foal, like antlers. This is PartKind.showsOnFoal().
  Soft parts on a foal are scaled with the foal like the horn is (the anchor already applies FOAL_SCALE).
- Decided: inheritance is per part (below). No part is sex-limited (unlike the antlers' Antm); say so on
  each page so nobody assumes it.
- Decided: THE PART DOES NOTHING. Like every grown part, no stat, no ability, no hitbox change. Say it plainly
  on each Gameplay tab.
- Mirrored pairs are TWO KINDS, one per side, not one mesh with a negative scale (a negative scale turns a
  culled box inside out; the antlers settled this). Ear fins and cheek spikes are pairs; brow
  ridge, goat beard and ruff are single, centred kinds. A pair is symmetric by default; an asymmetry knob is
  the antlers' ASYMMETRY value if wanted later. Recommended: start symmetric and omit the knob.

The parts
=========
Geometry below is a starting proposal in simple tapered boxes, not a drawing; the owner's eyes in the dev
yard decide the final numbers, and every proportion is a pose-stack transform so tuning is cheap. All use
boxes only. All are static, parented to a bone that already animates.

1. Ear fins / frills (EAR_FIN_RIGHT, EAR_FIN_LEFT)
   - Inheritance: decided RARE RECESSIVE.
   - New anchors EAR_RIGHT, EAR_LEFT: on the HEAD bone at the outer base of each ear, NOT on the ear bone.
     UNVERIFIED: whether the vanilla ears animate on their own (a flick or a lay-back). If they do, anchoring
     to the head keeps the fins still while the ear moves, which is the safe choice; if they never move,
     either bone works. Read HorseModel setupAnim first and say which was used.
   - Shape: thin slabs. Styles: blade (one slab), fan (three slabs of rising length) and frill (five slabs
     with rays). Show the first k of the baked maximum, like tines (PartNode.group and Slice.shown), so a
     line bred for more rays grows them in continuously.
   - Epigenome: size, spread (the fan angle), ray count. Sheet: HORN. Colour: its own colour gene.
   - On foals: yes (soft part).
   - Out of scope for now: a see-through MEMBRANE between the rays. The crystal antlers already draw some
     regions translucent by region through the same layer, so it is possible later; do not build it now.

2. Cheek spikes (CHEEK_SPIKE_RIGHT, CHEEK_SPIKE_LEFT)
   - Inheritance: decided DOMINANT, one copy shows.
   - New anchors CHEEK_RIGHT, CHEEK_LEFT: the side of the head about midway between the eye and the mouth.
   - Shape: two to four short cones pointing outward and a little back. One style, count shown out of
     the most baked (like tines).
   - Epigenome: length, girth, count. Sheet: BONE. Colour: its own colour gene. Not on foals.

3. Brow ridge (BROW, single, centred)
   - Inheritance: decided DOMINANT.
   - New anchor BROW: across the front of the skull above and in front of the eyes, LOWER than the unicorn
     horn's FOREHEAD anchor so a horn can still stand on top of it.
   - Shape: one low wide box (the ridge). Styles: plain, notched (three bumps) and spined (a row of three
     short cones). Epigenome: width, height, bump size.
   - Sheet: BONE. Colour: its own colour gene. Not on foals.

4. Goat beard (BEARD, single, centred)
   - Inheritance: decided DOMINANT.
   - New anchor CHIN: under the tip of the lower jaw.
   - Shape: three to five boxes hanging down and slightly forward, tapering, like a goatee. Static: it does
     not swing (nothing here animates). Epigenome: length, width, count.
   - UNVERIFIED which bone: the vanilla horse's lower jaw may animate (mouth open when grazing). Anchoring
     to the head bone keeps the beard on the skull while the jaw moves; anchoring to the jaw would move
     it with the mouth. Recommended: the head bone. Read HorseModel and say which was used.
   - Sheet: the HAIR region (PartSheet.HAIR). Colour: its own
     colour gene. On foals: yes (soft part).

5. Ruff (RUFF, single, a ring)
   - Inheritance: decided RARE RECESSIVE.
   - New anchor NECK: the ring sits around the base of the neck. This is the one part not on the head
     bone: it hangs off head_parts, the bone that carries the neck angle (AttachedPartLayer's own comment:
     "head_parts carries the neck angle; head is its child"), so it still needs no body anchor and none of
     the armour-layer collision worry a back anchor has. Decided as "stuck to the head" for that reason.
   - Shape: a ring of 8 to 12 short flat plumes radiating around the neck, in one or two tiers, like a lion's
     or a bird's ruff. Epigenome: size, plume count, droop.
   - UNVERIFIED: the vanilla horse's mane is a model part along the neck and the barding layer covers the
     neck, so a ruff can clip through both. Check in the dev yard with a maned horse and a barded one.
   - Sheet: HAIR (shared with the beard). Colour: its own colour gene. On foals: yes (soft part).

Genes this adds (ten more)
==========================
Five granting genes and five colour genes.
  granting: ear_fins (recessive), cheek_spikes (dominant),
            brow_ridge (dominant), goat_beard (dominant), ruff (recessive)
  colour:   one per granting gene, horn-style, as above
- Keys follow the house form horsegenetics.<name>. Each granting gene exposes a method that returns its
  AttachedPart(s) for a genotype and epigenome (copy UnicornHornGene.hornFor and AntlersGene.racksFor), and
  each is one line in GrownParts.of, dressed by its colour gene the way dressHorn does.
- Priorities: magical-band code-order slots only (a part gene paints nothing, so a priority has no coat
  effect, exactly as UnicornHornGene says). The horn's genes occupy 227-229 and the antlers' from 230.
  241-247 were free on 2026-10-02 (re-check: read the Genes registry for the highest magical priority and take
  the next free run), keeping each colour gene beside its granting gene as the horn's are.
- Founder frequency: numbers to tune when built, in the same spirit as the horn and antlers. Recommended
  starting points: the recessive three at an allele frequency near the unicorn's (about 0.05, which is a
  quarter of a percent of wild horses showing it); the dominant three at a share near the antlers'
  (a small fraction of a percent of horses). The owner tunes them in a dev yard, not here.
- Copy the horn's isNatural() and rarity settings (UnicornHornGene is the template; check them, they
  decide whether the gene needs a Science tab per check-gene-tabs.mjs). GeneFamily: MAGIC_PARTS by a MAGICAL_OVERRIDES line each, as the dragon horns did; formerly unverified which family the six belong to
  (it groups them in both gene editors and on the wiki landing page); reuse the horn's family unless the
  owner says otherwise.
- No breed is changed by this. Breeds can pin any of these genes later through the ordinary "genes" block;
  that is a separate task.

Slices and order
================
One bullet per session is the rule, so three slices, each shippable alone:
  The worked example for B and C is the dragon horns: wiki/gene-dragon-horns.html, DragonHornsGeneTest,
           priorities 239-240 (241-247 are the next free run; JSON genes start at 248).
  Slice B: the hard-edged remaining parts that need no new sheet region: ear fins, cheek spikes, brow ridge
           (new anchors EAR_*, CHEEK_*, BROW; the count-shown machinery is already there).
  Slice C: the soft parts that need HAIR and the neck anchor: goat beard and ruff.
C uses the HAIR region (shipped) and introduces the only non-head-bone anchor.

Tests and checks (CLAUDE.md rules apply: no full suite; run by name)
=====================================================================
- PartGeneratorTest: extend it to build every mesh of every new kind and style at every size bucket, with
  parents before children. This is what stops a bad generator reaching a player as a crash report.
- A gene test per granting gene (copy UnicornHornGeneTest and HornGenesTest): a carrier and a bearer, the
  recessive/dominant rules above, founders at the stated frequencies, the epigenome defaults.
- A colour gene test: two different copies make a two-tone part, a chaos copy ignores hue and tone.
- The coat goldens: these genes paint nothing, so the coat-golden rows should NOT move; the coat-bake
  golden is per gene and gains rows, so regenerate it and check only NEW rows appeared.
- Play checks (each page's Verification tab, never seen in game): each part in the dev yard on an adult and
  a foal, with a tiny and a huge horse (the size gene scales parts by the pose stack, which the page notes
  is a claim from reading); the parts stacked together; a horse with a maned neck and barding for the ruff;
  the beard and ear fins while the horse grazes and while its head moves.
