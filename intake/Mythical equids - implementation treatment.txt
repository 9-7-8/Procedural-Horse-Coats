session's, not asked; "Open" lines are still to be settled, by whom is said on the line; "UNVERIFIED" lines

1. The request, as given
========================
Queued during the rams session: a table with the "One-horned Indian wild ass" and the "Allocamelus / ass-camel" (the
owner then said "skip anything with a camel body", so the Allocamelus is OUT, and re-asked about the ass's head), and,
in a later table, "Zebra of the waterhole story".
  One-horned Indian wild ass  Greek account: white body, dark-red head, dark-blue eyes; swift, hard to capture; large,
                              horse-sized or larger; speed 10; one forehead horn white at the base, black in the middle,
                              crimson at the tip. Not a standard unicorn.
  Zebra of the waterhole      San folktale: white at first, later white with black scorch-stripes; powerful kick; light to
                              medium equine; speed 9, jump 8. An origin-story zebra, not a separate species.
Reading: two breeds, one of which needs two new gene-level things (a head-colour coat gene, a three-band horn colour).

3. What already exists, so the building session does not redo it
=================================================================
 - Horn colour (HornColourGene): codominant, a hue and tone on every allele copy; two different copies make a two-tone horn,
   the earlier of the pair the base, the tip the later, "fading about halfway up". Alleles: Wht, Red, Org, Yel, Grn, Blu, Ind, Vio,
   Pnk, Blk, Gry, Cha (chaos). A third band does not exist; the base/tip split is the whole model.
 - Unicorn horn (RamHorn is not involved): "horsegenetics.unicorn_horn"; breeds/unicorn.json pins unicorn_horn, horn_colour,
   horn_glow, horn_dust, light, prismtail (stats 7/7, size [0.92, 1.05], rare).
 - Zebra striping: natural, codominant (wiki/gene-natural-zebra.html); the dark bands are the horse's own colour and the gaps lose
   pigment. Also zebras_coat (magical, neck and shoulder stripes), striped_mane_tail, opaline_zebra under horsegenetics/genes/.
 - Eye colour: the thirteen loci on wiki/eye-colour.html; a blue eye exists. UNVERIFIED which locus and allele reads as dark blue.
 - Coat masks: LightGene knows hoof and eye masks (wiki/pipeline.html). UNVERIFIED whether any head mask is exposed to a data
   coat gene; check wiki/making-a-gene.html (masks and ops) before choosing how the head is painted.
 - A coat gene is a data file under horsegenetics/genes/ (making-a-gene.html), plus its gene page with three tabs.
Looks built but is not
  - No coat gene colours only the head; Masked and War Mask are face markings, not a head colour.
  - A three-band horn does not exist; two-tone is base and tip only.


4. The pieces
=============
Piece 1 - A coloured head
-------------------------
What the player sees: a horse whose whole head is a deep red or black, whatever the body is.
 Decided: a new data coat gene. Recommended name "Head colour"; key horsegenetics.head_colour; priority and family are the building
   session's call; natural or magical is Open (building session; the colours are fixed, so Recommended magical).
 Recommended: a wild type plus two alleles, Red and Black (Recommended tokens "Hrd" and "Hbk", PROPOSALS; grep every token); the colours are fixed dark tones, the head region is a mask. Dominance between Red and Black is Open (building session; Recommended codominant is not possible for a flat colour, so Black over Red). Appending a later colour is append-only. UNVERIFIED that a head
   mask is available (3). If not, the building session's first job is to add the mask (a contract: making-a-gene.html#contracts
   lists the files; a mask touches four or five).
 Where: the gene file under common/.../horsegenetics/genes/, its gene page; the founder frequency rare (the gene must be absent
   from the wild or very rare; the wild ass pins Red and Meandash pins Black). Both editors (rule 5).
 Data and saves: allele order permanent; the key is permanent.
 Tests: *CoatBakeGoldenTest (a row for the gene), *CoatPipelineGoldenTest unmoved for a horse without it, *DeadLayerTest.
 Play checks: each head colour on a white, black and grey body; a foal; a face marking over it; eyes and nostrils survive.
 Docs: a new gene page (three tabs), pipeline.html if a mask is added, making-a-gene.html if a contract list changes.
 Re-bake after: the long gene row, the wasm rebake and boot check, bakeGeneWikiPages and the generated spans.

Piece 2 - The three-band horn
-----------------------------
What the player sees: a unicorn horn banded white at the base, black in the middle, crimson at the tip.
 Decided: append one allele to Horn colour, a "tricolour" allele (Recommended token "Tri", a PROPOSAL; grep tokens). It stands for
   the white-black-crimson horn exactly; it is a codominant allele like the rest, so a pair of Tri gives the three bands and a
   Tri with another colour gives a two-tone whose base or tip is Recommended to read as the other allele's colour (Open, building
   session).
 Recommended: the bands are fixed, the hue and tone on the copy vary slightly.
 Where: common/ HornColourGene and HornGenerator's colouring; NeoForge re-bake of the part sheet if the horn colour is baked into it.
 UNVERIFIED how the unicorn horn is coloured (a fade between base and tip, in the generator or the layer).
 Data and saves: allele order permanent, append only.
 Tests: HornColourGene test for the allele and the pair; the two goldens move by KEY ONLY; PartGeneratorTest.
 Play checks: the three bands on the horn at every length, glow and dust on, a foal (the unicorn horn on foals per its page).
 Docs: gene-horn-colour.html (Gameplay table of colours, Coding allele order).

Piece 3 - The two breeds
------------------------
All kind magical, "wild" and "spawn_egg" only (as the deer, goats and rams). Rows are PROPOSALS; biomes, stats and sizes not asked.
 One-horned Indian wild ass (very_rare; waits on Pieces 1 and 2)
   biomes: minecraft:desert, minecraft:savanna, minecraft:badlands, minecraft:plains
   stats: speed 10, jump 5, health 6; size large (larger than the unicorn's). UNVERIFIED that speed 10 and the size cap allow it.
   Coat: white body (dominant whites), the Head colour Red allele pinned. Eyes: the blue eye allele pinned (UNVERIFIED which). Horn:
   unicorn_horn with the Tri allele pinned, glow and dust off. "Not a standard unicorn": a donkey-like horse; Recommended no light
   or prismtail (those are the Unicorn's). Lore: swift and hard to catch; no ability (Recommended).
 Zebra of the waterhole (rare: a type; ships on shipped genes only)
   biomes: minecraft:savanna, minecraft:savanna_plateau, minecraft:plains
   stats: speed 9, jump 8, health 6; size standard (light to medium). Coat: black with the natural zebra striping, the dark bands
   black and the gaps white; zebras_coat as the alternative (UNVERIFIED which reads better; bake and look). The "white at first,
   then scorch-stripes" tale is lore (a foal grey is not built). Kick: lore only. Commonness Decided (owner, 2026-10-02): rare.
 Where: the breed JSON; breeds.html and breed-book.html (an Equids section).
 Tests: *BreedsTest per breed; BreedFilesTest; BreedBandsReachTest.
 Play checks: each breed in a yard pen; the wild ass's head, eyes and horn together; a wild herd in each biome.
 Docs: breeds.html, breed-book.html, the breed census.

K. Performance: no tick work. L. Tests: see the pieces. M. Release: rides the next release (Decided).

Three likeliest ways it goes wrong for a real player
  1. The head mask does not exist and the head colour spills onto the body. Handled by UNVERIFIED 1 and a play check.
  2. The three-band horn reads as two-tone when the tip is Tri-and-X. Open, building session; a play check.
  3. The wild ass reads as a plain white horse with a unicorn horn. A play check; the page names the head colour and the bands.
