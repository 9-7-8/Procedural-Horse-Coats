TThis gene needs to be extremely high priority, so that its Passification effects can overwrite any other aggression effects in the mod. 
A horse that is aggressive to players with no Passification gene is functionally just an enemy, and cannot be tamed. 
This gene does not itself add any aggression esion mechanics 
Rather, the goal of this gene is to add a way that any wild horse which is ordinarily aggressive to players can be tamed.
It is strongly recommended to make all alleles incomplete dominant, so there's many ways to passify a horse.
This gene applies at all times, to tamed and untamed horses, to adults and to foals. 
Alleles it ships with:
n: wildtype. No additional passification measures. 
Permanent: feeding an item to this horse permanently passifies it. The item needing to be fed, and the amount, are stored in the epigenetics. 
Temporary: feeding an item to this horse temporarily passifies it. The item, the amount, and the duration of the passification, and the cooldown between passifications, are all stored epigenetically. 
FoalPermanent: same as permanent, but only applies while the horse is a foal. May mean the adult version is permanently aggressive if the taming window as a foal is missed. 
AdultPermnent: the complement to foal permanent. 
FoalTemporary, AdultTemporary. 
This is a behavior gene. 

Passification means the horse will not select the player as a target for attacks for any reason. Harmful auras may still apply.
