Realm breeding pause - implementation treatment

Scoped with the owner. Every "Decided" line below is her call; "UNVERIFIED" lines
are facts this file has not proved and the building session must check first.
Source of truth for what exists is wiki/horse-realm.html (the realm) and the breeding
pages (wiki/breeding.html, wiki/fertility.html). This file is retired (deleted,
per /process-intake step 7) when it ships, and moves onto those pages' Gameplay and
Coding tabs.

The ask, in one paragraph
==========================
In the horse realm, breeding should advance at normal speed, but only while the mare
is actually loaded, which is to say while a player is near enough to have her in a
loaded chunk. A mare nobody is near does not progress, and does not catch up when
somebody arrives.

What already exists, so the building session does not redo it
==============================================================
- common/repro/Pregnancy: "Every time is an absolute game tick, so a mare whose
  chunk was unloaded for a week is exactly as far on as one that was watched the
  whole time - nothing counts down." conceivedTick, dueTick, lossTick; due(now) is
  now >= dueTick. That is the property this ask reverses for the realm only.
- So today an unloaded realm mare is not frozen: her due tick passes while no one is
  there, and she gives birth the moment her chunk loads. The realm is 1,600 blocks in
  radius and a player loads only a dozen chunks of it (wiki/horse-realm.html), so most
  of the field is unloaded most of the time, and most pregnancies would currently
  finish off-screen.
- server/ReproHandler is the thin game-side wrapper (breed, set, withPregnancy, the
  late-pregnancy slowdown, weaning). The realm is a dimension (server/HorseRealm,
  HorseRealm.REALM_LEVEL) with rules keyed to it in server/HorseRealmRules.
- "Shared slow tick" in wiki/horse-care.html: one periodic tick that visits loaded
  horses; the natural place to notice "this realm mare was last ticked a while ago".
- UNVERIFIED: the realm applies no time change of its own today. "Normal speed" is
  read as one loaded tick = one tick of progress, the same as the overworld. Confirm
  nothing in HorseRealmRules scales or skips time before relying on that.

Design
======
- Decided: "while a player is in there" means WHILE THE MARE IS LOADED, per mare,
  not a realm-wide clock that runs whenever anybody is anywhere in the realm. A
  mare in a chunk nobody has loaded does not progress even if a player is on the
  other side of the realm.
- Decided: the scope is THE WHOLE BREEDING CYCLE, not only gestation: pregnancy
  (due and early-loss timers), heat, breeding cooldowns and foal growth/weaning all
  follow loaded time for a horse in the realm.
- Decided: it is CONFIGURABLE, a server option, DEFAULT ON. Off restores today's
  plain game time exactly.
- Decided: a paused pregnancy SAYS SO. A mare in the realm that is paused shows it
  where the pregnancy is read (the info screen's pregnancy line), so it does not
  look stuck: for example "paused while nobody is near".

Mechanism (recommended; the owner has not chosen a mechanism)
------------------------------------------------------------
Do not change the stored absolute ticks into counters. Keep every timer an absolute
game tick, and move them FORWARD by the time the horse spent unloaded:
- Record when the horse was last seen ticking (the shared slow tick already visits
  loaded horses; an attachment holding "last seen at game tick T").
- When a realm horse loads or is next ticked, work out gap = now - lastSeen, and add
  gap to every absolute-tick timer she carries (dueTick, lossTick, the heat and
  cooldown stamps), then set lastSeen = now. Net effect: the unloaded time did not
  happen to her.
- Only do this for a horse that is IN the realm (HorseRealm.isRealm(level)). A
  horse that leaves the realm pregnant keeps her shifted timers and from then on
  runs on plain game time; a horse that enters pregnant starts being tracked on
  arrival, with lastSeen = the moment she arrives so she does not get a free gap.
- Why this rather than a counter: it leaves Pregnancy and every reader of dueTick
  untouched (due(now) stays now >= dueTick), it works the same for a mare saved
  mid-pregnancy under the old rules, and it needs no new clock.
- Pregnancy is an immutable record (withPregnancy). Shifting means building a new
  Pregnancy with moved ticks, in common/repro where a test can pin it down
  (a pure function: shift(pregnancy, gap)), and the game module only stores it.
  Keep the rule in common/, hard rule 1.

UNVERIFIED - the building session checks first (each needs a call from the owner if false)
===========================================================================================
1. Which timers are absolute game ticks and which already only advance while the
   horse ticks. Vanilla entity age and in-love counters only advance while loaded and
   would already behave as wanted. The mod's own heat, cooldown and weaning stamps
   (BreedingCooldownHandler, NaturalBreedingHandler, ReproHandler) are the ones that
   need shifting. Inventory them all before writing the shift; one missed timer is a
   mare that is paused for pregnancy but not for heat.
2. Whether a horse can be loaded and ticked in a way that does not reach the shared
   slow tick (a chunk loaded for a moment, a forced chunk, a ticket from the ender
   whistle's call). A momentary load would otherwise count as "loaded time" for the
   whole gap. Decide what counts as loaded: ticked for at least one slow-tick
   interval is the recommendation.
3. A horse that dies, is claimed, or leaves the realm while unloaded cannot be
   shifted; the first load after the change does it, which is fine.
4. Whether the info screen's pregnancy line can see "this mare is in the realm" on
   the client. If not, add one flag to the synced record rather than a payload.

Config
======
One server option under behaviour.*, default true, with the safe accessor beside it
(copy leadsReturn()). Its comment must say plainly that off is today's behaviour and
that on changes when realm foals are born.

Tests and checks
================
- common/: a pure test for shift(pregnancy, gap): due and loss both move by the gap,
  a gap of 0 is the identity, and the invariant dueTick > conceivedTick still holds.
- A gametest: a pregnant realm mare, unload her, advance game time, load her, and
  assert she is NOT due; the same with the option off IS due.
- Play checks (Verification tab): put a pregnant mare through a portal, leave the
  realm for a long while, come back and see she has barely progressed; stand near her
  and watch her progress at normal speed; read the paused line while away.
