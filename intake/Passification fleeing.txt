PASSIFICATION SHOULD STOP FLEEING
=================================

Request
-------
The passification gene should also passify horses that flee from the player who
has calmed them. If a horse is fleeing because of a passifiable player, the
active flight should stop once that player's passification is granted, and the
horse should stop selecting that player as the reason to flee while the calm is
valid.

Meaning of "passify" here
-------------------------
This extends the existing per-player passification bargain. A calm granted to
Alice applies to the horse's aggression and flee decisions about Alice; it does
not make the horse generally fearless, tame, or immune to panic. The same horse
may still flee from Bob, a mob, fire, freezing, or any other non-player danger.
A temporary route stops working when its calm expires, and a permanent route
continues to suppress Alice indefinitely.

Required behaviour
------------------
- The fleeing path must use the passification gene's existing per-player veto
  when considering a player as the nearest flee source.
- If the horse is currently fleeing from a player and that player becomes
  passified, cancel that flee leg immediately rather than waiting for the next
  path refresh. Clear the flee bookkeeping and let ordinary goals resume.
- If another valid threat remains nearby, the horse may begin fleeing from that
  threat on the next evaluation. Do not clear all fleeing just because one
  player was passified.
- Keep passification per player. Passifying one player must not suppress flee
  reactions to every player or creature.
- Keep environmental and injury panic separate. This change only affects the
  player-threat route that is represented by the passification gene; it must not
  suppress fire, freezing, escape-from-death, or other non-target panic.
- Do not make the horse stop fleeing merely because it has no aggression target.
  Fleeing and attacking are different temper outcomes and must remain so.

Likely implementation surface
-----------------------------
The NeoForge flee implementation is in
`GeneAbilityHandler.flee(...)`. Its candidate predicate currently checks the
mood's group only, while the existing aggression path calls
`Passification.suppresses(...)`. Reuse that same veto at the flee candidate
chokepoint and add the smallest state/path cancellation needed for a newly
passified current quarry. Keep the gene and per-player route semantics in
`PassificationGene` and `Passification`; do not add a second calm state.

Be careful about the distinction between a player and an environmental source:
the existing `Passification.suppresses(...)` contract returns true only for a
player, so it is the appropriate filter for this extension. The active fleeing
map is keyed by horse and records the current quarry; it must not retain a
passified player's UUID after the flee is cancelled or the player disappears.

Validation
----------
- A horse with a flee temperament flees from an unpassified player.
- Feeding the required passification offering to that player makes the horse
  stop fleeing and prevents a new flee decision against that player while the
  calm lasts.
- A second, unpassified player still causes fleeing after the first player is
  passified.
- A temporary calm expires and the original player can again be a flee source.
- A permanent calm remains effective after reload if the existing attachment
  persistence already covers the aggression veto.
- Fire, freezing, injury escape, and fleeing from non-player entities continue
  to work on a passified horse.
- The behaviour remains per player: Alice's calm does not protect Bob.

Open runtime check
------------------
Verify in-game that granting passification while the horse is already moving
away from the player actually releases the navigation goal promptly, rather
than only preventing the next flee path from being chosen. Record the observed
result on the owning passification or temperament page when this proposal is
routed out of intake.
