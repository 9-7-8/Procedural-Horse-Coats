Mythic breeds - biome assignments

A PROPOSAL, NOT A SPEC. Nothing in the repo has changed. It assigns vanilla Minecraft biomes to every breed in "Mythic breeds - gene audit.txt", following the
biome structure the shipped breeds already use. Read from the breed files, wiki/breed-format.html, wiki/breeds.html and common/breed/BiomeAnalogues. UNVERIFIED
lines are things I could not confirm. The owner's two rules still apply: public domain only and no talking.

How the existing biome structure works
======================================
- A breed names the biomes it lives in, in VANILLA's vocabulary ("biomes": ["minecraft:plains", ...]). A modded biome that stands in for a vanilla one gets that
  vanilla biome's whole pool automatically (common/breed/BiomeAnalogues: terralith:steppe is plains and windswept hills, regions_unexplored:bayou is swamp, oceans map to
  beach). So nothing here names a modded biome; every assignment below reaches modded packs by analogue. Dimensions a mod adds never have horses.
- Herds only form in the biomes on the biome modifier's list (data/horsegenetics/neoforge/biome_modifier/add_horse_herds.json): the plains, savannas, deserts, badlands,
  forests, taigas, snow biomes, peaks, rivers, beaches, stony shore, swamps, jungles, cherry grove, mushroom fields and the windswept hills. Every biome below is on that list
  (checked by a script). UNVERIFIED observation: several shipped breeds also name biomes that are NOT on it (stony_peaks, dark_forest, old_growth_spruce_taiga,
  snowy_beach, and the End biomes), for example Cloudtouched's stony_peaks. Whether a breed's herds can form there is worth confirming; I avoided off-list biomes.
- The Nether and the End work differently: a breed must name its floor blocks in "spawn_ground", and the herd then counts only in those dimensions. None of the breeds
  below is placed there. Fire-themed breeds (Aithon, Konabos, Phobos) went to hot overworld biomes instead, so the existing Nether trio (Netherhorse, Nightmare, Mirrorgazer)
  is not crowded and the heat-tolerance rule for Nether breeds (the climate spec) is not triggered.
- "commonness" is a weight ladder: extremely_common 48, very_common 24, common 12, moderate 6, uncommon 3, rare 1.5, very_rare 0.75. Every shipped magical breed is rare or
  very_rare except Not A Sheep (uncommon). I used very_rare for named individuals and rare for types, and uncommon only for the plain horses that have no powers (the eight
  Aesir horses, Freyfaxi and the Irish trio Attach, Gaoth and Sidhe).
- "spawn_time" is day, night or any. A day or night breed is the "light" rule the Unicorn (day) and Dhampir (night) already use.
- "spawn" (wild, cowboy, spawn_egg, stable): not assigned here. Shipped Nether breeds use ["wild","spawn_egg"] and keep out of the cowboy and the stable; recommended the same
  for these mythic ones, but it is the owner's call (see the open questions).

The climate rule from the breed climate spec applies
=====================================================
The spec ("Breed climate tolerance - spec.txt") raises a breed's cold tolerance by the share of its biomes that are cold (snowy plains, ice spikes, snowy taiga, snowy slopes,
grove, frozen and jagged peaks, frozen river and snowy beach). Applied to the assignments below, it gives: Pegasus, Sleipnir, Hrimfaxi and Silfrintoppr near-fixed cold tolerance;
Gullfaxi, Svadilfari, Longma and Tulpar partial. This is a consequence to review, not an extra decision: if any of these should NOT carry cold tolerance, drop its cold biome
(or one of them) rather than the rule. Pegasus is the surprising one: it picks up near-fixed cold tolerance only because grove and jagged_peaks are two of its four biomes.

Crowding check
==============
Meadow, plains, forest and windswept hills already carry a lot of breeds (the shipped files list meadow 94 times, plains 82, forest 66, windswept_hills 62). The mythic entries
are rare or very rare (weights 0.75 to 3 against commons of 12 to 48), so by weight they stay a modest fraction of the big biomes: windswept_hills about 12%, sunflower_plains about 9%, plains about 8%, forest about 7%, meadow about 4%. The biomes that tip are the coasts
and a few small ones, where few breeds live. After thinning the sea breeds (Hippocampus, Each-uisge, Nuckelavee and the Poseidon team each lost a shore biome, and two moved to
very_rare) the most affected biomes by weight are:
  beach: existing weight 15.0, added 4.5, mythic share 23%
  stony_shore: existing weight 15.8, added 3.8, mythic share 19%
  windswept_forest: existing weight 33.0, added 6.0, mythic share 15%
  bamboo_jungle: existing weight 14.2, added 2.2, mythic share 14%
  eroded_badlands: existing weight 9.8, added 1.5, mythic share 13%
  mangrove_swamp: existing weight 15.8, added 2.2, mythic share 12%
That is still a lot for a coast or a small biome. The easy dials are to drop a biome from a breed, leave the blocked ones (Nuckelavee, the Hippocampus and the rest) unspawned until
they are built, or move the water breeds to spawn_egg only.

Breeds that are blocked still get biomes
========================================
Several entries cannot ship yet (Sleipnir, Kelpie, Each-uisge, Hippocampus, Hippalektryon, Uchchaihshravas, Nuckelavee, Pegasus and Longma wait on parts or abilities). Their biomes are
decided now so the file is ready when they are built; until then they should not spawn (a breed file with no genes beyond what exists would spawn a plain horse with a myth's name).

Open questions for the owner
============================
- Spawn kinds: wild, spawn_egg, cowboy and stable. Recommended wild + spawn_egg only, like the Nether trio.
- Whether the plain horses (the eight Aesir horses, Freyfaxi, the Irish trio) should be separate breeds at all. They have no coat, power or look to tell them apart; they could be ONE
  breed or two with many name options, which would avoid a dozen near-identical entries in a spawn pool.
- Whether the sun-team horses (Horses of Helios, Arvakr and Alsvidr, Lampos and Phaethon) are one breed with several names or separate ones. The table gives names, not differences.
- Named lineages (Hofvarpnir's parents, the sire of Askhetos and Kygnos) have no entries here; add them if wanted.
