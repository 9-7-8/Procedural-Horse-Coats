Body parts - implementation treatment

Scoped with the owner. Every "Decided" line below is her call; "Recommended" lines are mine, not
asked; "UNVERIFIED" lines are facts this file has not proved and the building session must check
first. Source of truth for what exists is wiki/model-parts.html and the antler pages. This file is the
handoff for four new parts that grow on the horse's BODY: a back sail, a dorsal spine row,
shoulder/hip plates and crystal growths. It sits beside "Head parts - implementation treatment.txt"
(the head parts, which have their own slices) and follows the same pattern. It is retired (deleted, per
/process-intake step 8) as each slice ships and moves onto those pages' Gameplay and Coding tabs.

In scope, and what is not
=========================
Decided: back sail, dorsal spine row, shoulder/hip plates, crystal growths.
NOT in this file, left for later: wings, tails (tufted, blade, club), hoof feathering, fetlock claws,
tusks. Wings are body-parented too but need their own poses and a flap story; they have a plan in
"Attached model parts.txt" and are deliberately not started here.

What already exists, so the building session does not redo it
==============================================================
Everything the head-parts treatment says about the existing machinery applies and is not repeated:
AttachedPartLayer, PartKind / PartAnchor / PartShape / PartGenerators / PartNode / PartSheet,
GrownParts.of as the one door, the "adding a part" recipe on model-parts.html's Coding tab, the
cost-sorted variation rule (topology in the shape, proportions on the pose stack, counts shown out
of a baked maximum), and the deferred-submit trap in PartModel. Read that file's first section.
What is specific to the body:
- Anchors today are FOREHEAD, CROWN_RIGHT, CROWN_LEFT, all on the head. A body part needs the FIRST
  anchor off the head, so AttachedPartLayer.anchor gets its first case that walks the root to the body
  bone instead of head_parts then head. The layer's own comment says to walk from the ROOT first (the
  HD adult carries a 1.1 scale and a -2.4 y offset on the root; skipping it is how a part ends up 10%
  small and 2.5 units high), and never to add a child to body in createHdBodyMesh (that moves the mesh the
  HD coat UVs are baked against). Parts are separate geometry from a separate bake, always.
- The layer is added BEFORE the vanilla armour and saddle layers (GeneticHorseRenderer): armour and
  saddle are vanilla's bakes of the whole horse and are drawn after the parts. That order was noted as
  "cheap to revisit when a saddle pad or a set of dorsal spines makes it matter". This is that
  moment: a back part is exactly the thing the saddle layer then draws over.
- The saddle is on the render state as state.saddle, and a "phantom" saddle (ModDataComponents.PHANTOM_SADDLE,
  how a top-bond horse is steered bare) is a saddle that is present and NOT drawn: the saddle layer turns it
  into ItemStack.EMPTY. A rider can therefore sit on a horse whose saddle is not drawn at all.
- Grown parts already fade with the horse as a rider looks down (RiderFade; the layer passes the horse's
  fadeAlpha through every part colour). Body parts get this for free; nothing to build, only to check.
- Translucency already exists for ONE case: the crystal antlers draw their shafts see-through and their
  points solid, as two passes selected by sheet REGION. In AttachedPartLayer the see-through region is
  hard-coded as PartSheet.BONE and the strength is AttachedPart.CRYSTAL_ALPHA. A sail membrane and crystal
  growths both need a translucent region of their own, so that selection has to become data on the kind
  (a PartKind.translucentRegions() beside glowRegions()), not a second hard-coded constant.
- The size gene scales a part through the horse's pose scale already on the stack; the page records that as
  a claim from reading, not from watching. Body parts are large, so it becomes the first thing worth watching.

Decisions that apply to all four
================================
Carried over from the head parts, NOT re-asked (Recommended, change if she says otherwise):
- Everything can stack. A horse can carry a sail, spines, plates and crystals, and the head parts too.
  Default sizes are offset to avoid each other; extremes can clip, which is a Verification-tab check.
- Colour is its own gene per part, horn-style (codominant, rainbow plus chaos, two-tone from two different
  copies, hue and tone on each copy): four new colour genes. One shared abstract base with the head parts'
  colour genes (AbstractPartColourGene) rather than copies. How a two-tone pair maps onto a body part is below.
- No part moves a stat, grants an ability or changes a hitbox. Say so plainly on every Gameplay tab.
- No config key beyond the existing client parts.* switches.
- None of these show on a FOAL (PartKind.showsOnFoal() false for every body kind). They are hard parts; the
  foal's back is also tiny. Because AttachedPartLayer.anchor is a switch EXPRESSION with an adult and a foal
  offset per anchor, each new body anchor still needs a foal value to compile: give it the adult one and note
  it is unreachable, rather than a throw.
- Mirrored pairs are TWO KINDS, one per side (plates), not a negative scale. Centred parts (sail, spines,
  crystals) are one kind.
- A horse is symmetric by default.

Decided: the saddle zone
========================
Back parts run through where the saddle and the rider sit. Decided: UNSADDLED, a back sail or spine row runs
its full length; with a saddle on, ONLY the segments inside the saddle zone are hidden and the rest remain.
- Mechanism (recommended): a back mesh is built as numbered segments along the spine, front to back (the
  same way an antler is baked with its most tines and shows the first k via PartNode.group and
  PartModel.Slice.shown). Add a second argument to Slice: a MASK of segment groups to skip. It must be set
  every submit inside setupAnim, for every box, every time (the shared mesh may have been another horse's
  last): the deferred-submit trap in PartModel is the reason, and two horses in one frame, one saddled and one
  not, is the check that shows it.
- Decided by extension, not asked: the gate is "a saddle is DRAWN or the horse is RIDDEN", not "a saddle item
  is present". A phantom saddle is not drawn but its rider still sits there, so keying on state.saddle alone
  would put a rider inside the sail. UNVERIFIED: where the render state says "ridden" in 26.1.2; read the
  state's fields before writing it (a rider passenger check is the fallback).
- Where the zone is: a z-range on the body bone sized from the saddle model (EquineSaddleModel and
  the vanilla body box). UNVERIFIED: the numbers. Do NOT copy numbers from this file; read the vanilla horse
  body box and the saddle model in the 26.1.2 sources and the geometry tables on wiki/body-space.html, and
  keep the range as named constants in the client with a comment saying where they came from. The saddle
  pad (HorseGear) draws in the same zone; check it with the zone, do not assume it is smaller.
- The unsaddled, unridden horse always shows the full length. The foal shows no body part at all.
- Parts that do not run along the spine (plates on the flanks) are outside the zone by construction;
  check them against the saddle's flaps in the dev yard instead.

Anchors (new)
=============
Each is a value on PartAnchor and a case in AttachedPartLayer.anchor:
- SPINE: the midline of the top of the body, running from the withers back to the croup. The body bone's own
  animation (rearing, the body tilt) carries it, as the head carries the horn. Used by the sail, the spine row
  and the crystals. Because the three share it and can stack, they use DIFFERENT offsets and shapes so their
  defaults do not fill the same box; the crystals sit slightly to either side of the midline and fan outward.
- BODY_RIGHT, BODY_LEFT: the upper flank of each side, used by the plates; the mesh carries both the shoulder
  cluster and the hip cluster as two node groups offset along the body, so one kind per side holds both.
  Recommended: one mesh with two groups, not four kinds.
UNVERIFIED: the exact bone name and offsets. The layer already references head_parts; read the HdHorseModel
and HdBabyHorseModel to find the body bone's name and the root's transform, and write the offsets for the
ADULT only (the foal wears none).

The parts
=========
Geometry is a starting proposal in tapered and flat boxes; the owner's eyes in the dev yard decide the numbers,
and every proportion is a pose-stack transform so tuning is cheap. All static, parented to the body bone.

1. Back sail (PartKind SAIL, one kind, anchor SPINE)
   - Inheritance: decided RARE RECESSIVE, two copies, the one big dramatic part of the four.
   - Shape: a row of spines along the spine with a membrane panel between each pair of neighbours, so the
     sail is spines plus panels, built as numbered segments (so the saddle-zone mask and a shown-count both
     work). Styles (topology, baked): tall (a Spinosaurus: tall in the middle, curved), low (a ridge fin the full
     length) and scalloped (arched panels between spines). Size buckets as the horn has.
   - Decided: the membrane is TRANSLUCENT and the spines opaque, drawn through the translucent-by-region
     path the crystal antlers already use. That needs two things the antlers did not (below): a translucent
     region that is data on the kind, and a membrane region on the sheet. Spines and panels are separate boxes
     in separate regions of one mesh, so the expensive blended pass draws only the membrane.
   - Colour (recommended, not asked): the colour gene's two-tone maps BASE to the membrane and TIP to the
     spines. That is how the crystal antlers already use base for their see-through shafts and tip for their
     solid points, so it follows the existing mapping; two different colour alleles then make a sail whose
     spines and membrane differ, a one-colour horse gets a sail all one colour.
   - Epigenome on the granting allele copy: height, coverage (how much of the back it spans), spine count
     shown out of the most baked, curvature, and membrane opacity inside a bounded range. A category for style.
   - Translucency risk, from the page's own warning: UNVERIFIED which entity render types 26.1.2 offers a
     layer for a blended pass, and how a translucent sail sorts against the translucent coat passes, the
     armour and the horse fading. The antlers prove the region-selected two-pass draw works for a small part;
     a big flat membrane is the case that shows sorting and see-through-the-horse problems. If it looks wrong,
     fall back to the opaque "solid fin" look as a style and STOP AND REPORT to the owner rather than ship a
     broken membrane.

2. Dorsal spine row (SPINES, one kind, anchor SPINE)
   - Inheritance: decided DOMINANT, one copy shows.
   - Shape: a row of N tapered cones along the spine. Styles: uniform, graduated (largest at the withers,
     falling toward the croup) and alternating (large and small). Numbered segments so the saddle-zone mask
     and a shown-count work.
   - Epigenome: length, girth, count, taper. Sheet: BONE (or HORN) for the cones; no translucency.
   - Colour: its own colour gene, one colour or two-tone base-to-tip along the row.
   - This is the simplest of the four and the right first slice (below): it needs the SPINE anchor and the
     saddle-zone mask and nothing else new.

3. Shoulder / hip plates (PLATES_RIGHT, PLATES_LEFT, anchors BODY_RIGHT / BODY_LEFT)
   - Inheritance: decided DOMINANT.
   - Shape: overlapping slabs, three to five per cluster, stepped like armour scales, a shoulder cluster and a
     hip cluster as two node groups of one mesh per side. Styles: smooth slabs, ridged slabs and spiked slabs.
   - Epigenome: size, count per cluster, overlap, spikiness. Sheet: HORN or BONE.
   - UNVERIFIED: how the plates sit against the saddle flaps and the barding; the horse's body armour is a
     whole-body bake drawn after the parts, so plates beneath it are covered by it. Decide with the owner
     what the armoured case should look like once seen; do not engineer it first.
   - Colour: its own colour gene (two-tone: base plate colour, tip the edge colour).

4. Crystal growths (CRYSTALS, one kind, anchor SPINE)
   - Inheritance: decided DOMINANT.
   - Shape: clusters of three to seven crystals scattered over patches along the spine, each crystal two or
     three long boxes at slightly different yaws plus a shorter offset one (reads as an eight-sided prism),
     fanning from the midline. Cluster scatter is seeded: a SEED epigenome value picks the arrangement, so a
     horse's growths are its own (a seed is never nudged by drift, only rarely re-rolled whole). Count shown out
     of the most baked, along the row, so the saddle-zone mask applies.
   - Rendering: translucent shafts and solid tips through the same region-selected path as the antlers
     (a CRYSTAL region on the sheet, marked translucent on the kind), the same crossed prisms the existing
     crystal antlers use for a head.
   - Glow: NOT included. There is no glow gene for these in this treatment; the existing emissive path
     (AttachedPart.emissive and PartKind.glowRegions) is there to add one later. Say so on the page.
   - Colour: its own colour gene, as above; a gem colour is exactly what the colour gene gives.
   - Epigenome: cluster count, crystal size, lean spread, seed.
   - Not on a foal. Last slice, since it needs the cluster generator and the translucent path proven by the sail.

Genes this adds (eight)
=======================
Four granting genes and four colour genes.
  granting: back_sail (recessive), dorsal_spines (dominant), body_plates (dominant), back_crystals (dominant)
  colour:   one per granting gene, horn-style, as above
Same rules as the head parts: a method on each granting gene that returns the AttachedPart(s), one line in
GrownParts.of per gene dressed by its colour gene (dressHorn is the template), priorities in the magical-band
code-order slots after the head parts' (UNVERIFIED which are free: read the Genes registry), copy the horn's
isNatural() and rarity settings, and founder frequencies as numbers to tune in a dev yard: the recessive sail
near the unicorn's allele frequency, the dominant three near the antlers' small share. UNVERIFIED which
GeneFamily; reuse the horn's.
No breed is changed. Breeds can pin these through the ordinary genes block later.

Slices and order (one bullet per session, each shippable alone)
================================================================
  Slice A: the SPINE anchor, the first body case in AttachedPartLayer.anchor, the saddle-zone MASK on
           PartModel.Slice and the drawn-saddle-or-ridden gate, and ONE part through it: the dorsal spine row
           (opaque, no new render path). This settles the body anchor, the zone and the gate once.
  Slice B: the back sail. Adds the translucent-region-as-data change (PartKind.translucentRegions) and the
           membrane region on PartSheet (+ a paint() call in bake-part-sheet.mjs). The sail is also the first
           large translucent part, so the sorting question is answered here, on the part that can show it.
  Slice C: the shoulder/hip plates (BODY_RIGHT / BODY_LEFT anchors, no new render path).
  Slice D: the crystal growths (cluster generator, seed, the CRYSTAL region), reusing Slice B's path.
Do not start B before A (it needs the mask) or D before B (it needs the translucent path generalised).
If the head parts' slices have shipped first, reuse AbstractPartColourGene instead of writing it again; if
not, this treatment's Slice A writes it and the head parts reuse it. Either order works, but settle which
session owns it before either starts, or two copies appear.

Tests and checks (CLAUDE.md: no full suite; run by name)
=========================================================
- PartGeneratorTest: build every mesh of every new kind and style and size bucket, with parents before children,
  and assert the segment numbering the mask and shown-count rely on is contiguous.
- A pure test for the saddle-zone mask: which segment groups a given zone hides, none when unsaddled and unridden,
  and the foal drawing nothing.
- A gene test per granting gene (copy UnicornHornGeneTest / HornGenesTest): the recessive and dominant rules above,
  founders, epigenome defaults. A colour gene test: two different copies make a two-tone part, a chaos copy
  ignores hue and tone.
- The coat goldens must NOT move (these genes paint nothing); the coat-bake golden gains rows, so regenerate it and
  check only NEW rows appeared.
- Play checks (each page's Verification tab, never seen in game): each part on a tiny and a huge horse (the size
  gene's pose-scale claim is the big one now); saddled, unsaddled, ridden with a phantom saddle, and a saddle pad;
  two horses in one frame, one saddled and one not (the deferred-submit check); the horse rearing and the body
  animating; body armour over each part; the sail's translucency against the coat passes, the armour and the fade
  as a rider looks down; the parts stacked together; and the horse in the realm and in the browser portrait if
  parts draw there.
