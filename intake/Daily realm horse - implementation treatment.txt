Daily realm horse - implementation treatment

OWNER'S ANSWERS, 2026-10-02 (these override any "Open" or "Recommend" line below)
- Breed: EQUAL chance of literally any breed in the game, rarity ignored, with Feral Mixed as one
  more entry in that uniform list (option (a), not the recommended (b)).
- Non-WILD breeds: INCLUDED. Every breed may be the daily horse, cowboy-only, undead and Nether ones too.
- Clock: CATCH UP ON RETURN. Mornings are counted by the day count; while nobody is in the realm
  nothing spawns, and the missed mornings spawn when someone next enters, up to 7 at once.
- Placement: 8-24 blocks from a random untamed realm horse (joins its herd); with none, a random
  in-bounds point; never on a portal cell or a stable.
- Soft cap: yes, N untamed realm horses derived from the field's area (not a literal); the catch-up
  respects it.
- Still the building session's: the realm_born NBT flag (recommended), config names.

The request, as given
======================
Every "morning" in Minecraft, one new, fully random horse spawns in the horse
realm, to add genetic diversity. It follows normal spawn rules, except that it
may be Feral Mixed or ANY breed, regardless of biome.

What already exists, so the building session does not redo it
==============================================================
- The realm is the level horsegenetics:horse_realm (HorseRealm.REALM_LEVEL,
  HorseRealm.isRealm(level)). Its biome (data/horsegenetics/worldgen/biome/
  horse_realm.json) has EVERY spawner list empty, so nothing spawns there
  naturally today; horses only arrive by portal. The new spawn is therefore a
  server-driven spawn, not a spawner entry and not a biome modifier.
- HorseRealmRules (server/) refuses any mob that is not a horse, and marks every
  arriving horse persistent. A spawned horse is a horse, so it passes; make sure
  the spawn path sets persistence itself rather than relying on the join hook,
  and check which EntitySpawnReason the rules accept (UNVERIFIED: read the
  FinalizeSpawnEvent / EntityJoinLevelEvent handlers in that class before
  choosing a reason; NATURAL and CHUNK_GENERATION also trip BreedSpawnHandler,
  see below).
- The realm is a circle that GROWS: HorseRealmSize / HorseRealmCensus count
  every horse standing in it (size.note on each horse's scan) and
  growToFit() enlarges the field to keep density. A daily horse therefore adds
  to the census and, over a long world, grows the field. That is intended and
  free, but say so on the page, and the in-game matrix below includes it.
- Realm horses are not tamed. HorseRealmHerds already gathers untamed realm
  horses into bands on a slow scan, and HorseRealmFeral turns a tamed horse
  whose owner has left the realm wild. A fresh horse needs no new herd code:
  spawn it, tag it, let the herd scan find it. Check whether a LONE horse is
  acceptable to HorseRealmHerds (it elects leaders from "clumps"); UNVERIFIED.
- BreedSpawnHandler (FinalizeSpawnEvent, reasons NATURAL / CHUNK_GENERATION /
  SPAWNER) tags a horse with WILD_SPAWN_KEY and CANCELS it when
  Breeds.anythingMaySpawn(biome, dark) is false. The realm biome has no breed in
  it, so a NATURAL-reason spawn there would be cancelled or demoted to a lone
  Feral Mixed. This is the trap: do NOT spawn with a reason that routes through
  that gate and hope. Use a reason the handler ignores (EntitySpawnReason.
  COMMAND or MOB_SUMMONED - UNVERIFIED which of the two the SDK exposes under
  that name) and apply the breed explicitly.
- HerdManager.pickWildBreed(biome, rng, dark) picks from
  Breeds.wildCandidates(biomeId, dark): the breeds whose biomes() cover the
  biome AND whose spawn_time allows this hour. "Regardless of biome" means
  bypassing exactly this filter. Breeds.all() is the whole registry (shipped
  breeds, owner breeds from .minecraft/phc/breeds, Feral Mixed).
- A Feral Mixed horse rolls every gene unconstrained ("the pre-breeds
  behaviour" - wiki/breeds.html). A breed horse is rolled through that breed's
  constrained pool. There is already a code path that makes a horse "of breed
  X" for the breed spawn egg and for /summon-style creation
  (HorseEggSpawner, ModBreedSpecs); reuse it, do not re-roll genes by hand.
- Day detection exists in two spellings and ONE of them is a trap:
  Level.isBrightOutside() / isDarkOutside() are what every gene condition and
  handler here uses. DebugWorldWatch and DebugTestWorldHandler both record that
  26.1.2 REPLACED getDayTime() with a ServerClockManager of WorldClock holders,
  so do not write `dayTime % 24000`. The realm's dimension type declares
  default_clock minecraft:overworld, so its sky follows the overworld clock;
  UNVERIFIED that isBrightOutside() on the REALM level flips at the same moment
  as on the overworld (DebugTestWorldHandler.setNight records an owner report
  that "night isn't working in the horse dimension" - read that comment before
  trusting the realm's own sky).

The design
==========
One slow server tick, one transition, one spawn.

1. Detect the morning as a TRANSITION, not a state. Keep a per-world SavedData
   (or a field on HorseRealmSize's data, which is already per-world and
   already persisted) holding the last observed bright/dark reading and the
   last day a horse was spawned. On the server tick (ServerTickEvent.Post, the
   same shape as HorseRealmCensus.onServerTick, gated to run once every N
   ticks, N around 40) read the realm level's isBrightOutside(); when it goes
   dark -> bright, it is morning. Persisting "last spawned" is what stops a
   server that restarts at dawn, or a player who sleeps the night through and
   triggers /time skips, from spawning twice or never.
   - Decided: once per morning, not per player and not per loaded chunk.
   - Open: SLEEP and /time set. A skipped night should still count as one
     morning; a /time set day issued twice should not give two. Keying the
     "already spawned" guard on the clock's day COUNT (not the time of day)
     answers both. Find the day count under the new clock API (api-notes).
   - Open: should the realm tick at all with no players online, or only
     while someone is in the dimension? Natural horse spawns do not happen in
     unloaded levels, and the realm is huge (HorseRealm.radius). The simple
     rule - spawn only when the realm level is loaded (it is, at spawn
     chunks, but the field's far chunks are not) - needs a decision on WHERE
     (next heading) that works whether or not a player is nearby.
2. Pick a breed, with no biome filter. Candidate set = Breeds.all() plus Feral
   Mixed. Decided: ANY breed, or Feral Mixed. Open, and it is the one number
   to settle: the weighting. Three honest options, in rising cost:
   (a) uniform over the list - every breed equally likely, Feral Mixed one
       entry among ~dozens, so almost every daily horse is a breed horse;
   (b) Feral Mixed as one outcome with its own share (suggest 1 in 4), then
       uniform over breeds - keeps the realm from filling with breed founders
       only, which is the opposite of "more genetic diversity" (a breed is a
       CONSTRAINED slice of the pool; Feral Mixed is the wide one);
   (c) weight each breed by its own wild frequency. Rejected for this: those
       weights encode biome rarity, and the request says regardless of biome.
   Recommend (b). Whichever is chosen, skip a breed whose BreedSource does not
   allow WILD (the cowboy-only breeds, the undead and magical ones the
   Nightmare/Netherhorse style entries are) UNLESS the owner wants them in the
   realm; that is a second open question, because the realm is persistent and
   protected from damage, so a hostile-by-design breed there is a permanent
   resident. Open: ask the owner. Default: honour BreedSource.WILD.
   Also skip anything Breeds.anythingMaySpawn would refuse under the owner's
   spawn settings? NO - the settings are per-biome and the request is
   explicit that biome is irrelevant. Say that on the page.
3. Pick a place. Decided: normal spawn rules, which for a land animal means a
   legal standing spot, not inside a wall, on a solid floor, with headroom.
   The realm is a flat plane (GROUND_Y 64, STAND_Y 65) so "legal" is nearly
   always true; the real constraints are the perimeter (HorseRealm.inBounds /
   isWall - a horse must start well inside it) and the portal cells
   (isPortalChunk - do not drop one on a portal frame or on top of someone's
   stable). Open: random point anywhere in the circle, or near existing horses
   so it joins a herd instead of standing alone. Suggest: pick a random
   existing untamed realm horse, spawn within 8-24 blocks of it; if the realm
   has none, spawn at a random in-bounds point. That makes the newcomer a
   herd member by the existing scan with no new code.
   Chunk loading: the spawn point's chunk must be loaded. Use
   level.isLoaded / getChunkSource().getChunkNow and, if absent, pick the next
   candidate. Do NOT force-load a chunk (the force-load re-entrancy crash is
   documented at length in HorseFoundingTickHandler and CowboyHandler; spawning
   a horse writes Attributes.SCALE, which scans collisions). Spawn from the
   tick, after chunk work, never from a task queued mid-chunk.
4. Make the horse. Create the entity, call the existing breed-application
   path (so genes, coat, name, pedigree, body stats and epigenome all resolve
   exactly as a spawn egg's would), set persistent, and tag it so it can be
   found and counted. Open: tag with a small NBT flag (a new
   "horsegenetics:realm_born" boolean beside WILD_SPAWN_KEY) so a later session
   can show "born in the realm" on its record. Do not reuse WILD_SPAWN_KEY:
   HerdManager reads it to decide whether to found a herd from a clump, and
   sharing it would pull this horse through that path and its biome gate.
5. Log through ActionTrace.log("realm", ...) with the breed and the position,
   so the owner can read "a horse was born in the realm" from latest.log
   without being there. CLAUDE.md already says the owner's session is on disk.

What this must not do
======================
- No pre-generated list of coats (CLAUDE.md hard rule 4): the horse is rolled
  at the moment of the spawn, from the real gene pool, never picked from a
  baked set.
- No logic in neoforge/ that could live in common/. The weighting rule in step
  2 (given the breed list, the RNG and the settings, return a breed or Feral
  Mixed) is pure and belongs in common/breed/ beside Breeds.wildCandidates, with
  a unit test; the neoforge side is only the transition detector, the placement
  and the entity creation. Touching common/ means :web:bakeDesignerAssets and
  check-designer-boots.mjs per procedures/rebake.txt.
- No effect on the debug corridor (DebugPenManager.DEBUG_LEVEL): HorseRealm.
  isRealm(level) is false for it, which is exactly the check to use.
- No server-config surprise: add a ServerConfig toggle (default ON, since it
  is the owner's design; a server owner who finds the realm too full needs an
  off switch) and an optional per-morning count (default 1). Check how
  ServerConfig names its other realm options before naming these.
- No cap-free growth. The realm already grows to keep density, so population
  is self-limiting on the field's side, but a server that runs for a year gets
  hundreds of daily horses. Open: a soft cap (stop spawning above N untamed
  horses in the realm, N derived from field area, not a literal) so the realm
  does not become a lag source; the census already counts them.

Tests and checks
=================
- common/: the weighting rule, deterministic from a seeded Rng - every breed
  reachable, Feral Mixed at its share over many draws, a WILD-forbidden breed
  never picked, an empty breed list yields Feral Mixed. Run with
  --tests '*DailyRealmBreedTest' (name it), never the full suite.
- Gametest in neoforge gametest/ModGameTests: set the realm clock to just
  before morning, advance, assert exactly one new horse in the realm, it has a
  record, it is persistent, it is not in the debug level, and a second
  transition the same day adds none. Setting the clock: DebugTestWorldHandler
  already drives it through `time set` and `gamerule doDaylightCycle`, and says
  why (the clock API is several lookups deep); do the same.
- In-game matrix (record on the realm page's Verification tab, unplayed):
  (1) a normal overnight wait gives one new horse at dawn; (2) sleeping through
  the night gives one, not zero; (3) `/time set day` twice gives one; (4)
  restart the server at dawn: no duplicate, no miss; (5) the new horse is
  protected like every other realm horse (damage cancelled); (6) it joins a
  band within a few minutes; (7) its record opens in the H menu with a name,
  pedigree and coat; (8) across thirty mornings the breed mix is what the
  weighting says and Feral Mixed appears; (9) the field size line in the log
  moves when the census grows it; (10) nobody spawns in the wall or on a
  portal; (11) with the realm unloaded and nobody online, nothing spawns and
  nothing is owed (decide whether missed mornings are made up - suggest NOT).
