Mythic breeds - gene audit

An AUDIT, NOT A SPEC. Nothing here has been changed in the repo and no decision has been made for the owner. It
maps six tables of mythical and folkloric horses from the owner onto what the mod already has (implemented genes,
parts and breeds), what is already scoped in intake/ but not built, and what is still unscoped. Read from the
shipped gene classes and gene files, the wiki gene pages, the shipped breed files and every treatment in intake/.
UNVERIFIED lines are things I could not confirm.

Scope rules (the owner's)
=========================
- PUBLIC DOMAIN ONLY. Anything drawn from a still-protected work is out: the Lord of the Rings (Shadowfax, the Mearas),
  Harry Potter (the thestral), Dungeons & Dragons (the D&D nightmare), The Legend of Zelda (Epona) and The NeverEnding Story
  (Artax), Narnia (Fledge; C. S. Lewis) and Tolkien's legendarium (Nahar, Felaroef, Asfaloth, Snowmane, Rochallor) and further Narnia horses (Bree, Hwin, Jewel) were in the tables and are now REMOVED from this audit. What stays is myth and folklore with no living rightsholder:
  Greek, Norse, Hindu, Chinese, East Asian, Irish, Scottish, Orcadian, Turkic and Islamic traditions.
- Corollary for the building sessions: a breed's name and lore must be written fresh from the myth, not copied. The tables cite
  Wikipedia and other sites; their wording is not public domain (Wikipedia text is CC BY-SA). Name the creature, write our own
  description.
- NO TALKING. A horse that speaks or prophesies is a huge feature and is out of scope for now. It is dropped as a gap everywhere
  below (Arion, Xanthus; the Narnian Talking Horses, Bree, Hwin, Jewel and Fledge, went out with their stories). It stays as breed-description lore only, or not at all.
- The mod's own shipped breeds are not affected. The existing breed called Nightmare (a tiny will-o'-the-wisp) is the owner's own
  invention, so the earlier naming clash with the D&D steed is moot now that the D&D steed is removed.
- A natural DAPPLE gene is now on the owner's to-do list (intake/To do.txt). Balius is the breed that wants it.

Legend
======
 HAVE     implemented and shipped today
 INTAKE   scoped in intake/ but not built; named by file
 ROADMAP  named on a page's Roadmap tab or inside a big intake note, but with no treatment of its own
 GAP      nothing anywhere: still needs scoping

How the tables' columns map onto the mod
========================================
- Body colour, mane and tail colour: HAVE. White, grey, silver, black, chestnut, palomino, buckskin, yellow, reddish-brown and
  "variable" are all reachable with the base-coat genes (Extension, Agouti, Shade, Matp cream/pearl, Dun, Champagne, Flaxen, Grey,
  Silver) and the independent mane_color and tail_color genes. Spotted looks: Leopard, the Appaloosa modifier and Rosette.
  DAPPLE: no explicit gene (Shade is the bay-shade locus). The nearest are Grey, Sooty and the data gene Scarab Dapple. A natural
  dapple gene is on the to-do list; until then Balius ships as spotted or with the nearest of those.
- Hoof colour: nothing in either table needs one. There is no hoof-COLOUR gene (hooves come from the coat pipeline's region rules).
  Molten Hooves is a glowing hoofprint trail. Not a gap.
- Speed / jump: HAVE. Breed stats run to 10 (the shipped Nightmare is jump 10), so "10+" means the cap. UNVERIFIED that speed 10 is
  reachable in the stat curve; check BreedStatCurve before pinning a breed to it.
- Pulling strength (Svadilfari): HAVE. "pull" is a first-class stat out of ten (a Shire pulls 10) with a Magic Pull gene beside it.

What the mod already covers (HAVE)
==================================
- Flight: the data gene "flying". Gliding is dominant (an elytra-like glide) and TRUE flight is recessive, two copies, "a horse that
  goes wherever it is pointed". It paints nothing. Cloudtouched already pins it. Cloud-treading, a third mode, is unbuilt (Roadmap).
- Fire: Fireproof, Emberveins, Molten Hooves, the 40-allele Particle locus (flames, souls, snow, hearts, water wake, rain and more;
  recessive), the eye-colour and eye-glow loci. Mane and tail can be fire-coloured through mane_color and tail_color.
- Water: Waterborn (walks on water), Ocean Born (cannot drown), Magic Water Breathing, Magic Swim Speed, Hydrophobic. Coat patterns
  that read as water: dripwork, ooze_drip, rainbow_drip, foamed, tidefoam_sash, tidemark, tidewave, coral_bloom, gill_bloom, verdant.
- Healing and purity: Healer (mends nearby people), Cleansing Light (burns the undead), Holy Ward, Potion Milk. None purifies water.
- Scales and dragon coats (coat patterns, visual only): diamond_scutes, scuted, beadscale, fish_scales, dorsal_shield, serpentine.
- Shapeshifting: Lycan, matched pair, at night, into a peaceful LAND animal (wolf, cat, fox, goat, rabbit and the rest).
- A horn: Unicorn Horn with horn colour, glow and dust; Ram Horns; Antlers.
- Blight: kills grass and growth under the horse.
- Breeds that already exist and overlap: Unicorn (rare, 7/7), Nightmare (the owner's tiny flying wisp), Cloudtouched (a glide horse),
  Netherhorse, Enderpony, Mirrorgazer, Rimehart, Greenwood, Dhampir, Not A Sheep.

Already scoped in intake/ but not built (INTAKE)
================================================
- Head parts (intake/Head parts - implementation treatment.txt): dragon horns, ear fins/frills, cheek spikes, brow ridge, goat beard,
  ruff.
- Body parts (intake/Body parts - implementation treatment.txt): back sail, dorsal spine row, shoulder and hip plates, crystal growths.
- Tusks gene (intake/Tusks gene - implementation treatment.txt): boar tusks, sabre fangs, narwhal horn.
- Feathering (intake/Feathering - implementation treatment.txt): fetlock feathering coloured from the leg.

What is still unscoped
======================
ROADMAP only, no treatment (wiki/model-parts.html's Roadmap tab, and Stage 6 of "intake/Attached model parts.txt"):
 1. WINGS. Feathered pegasus wings and dragon wings. The settled route is a copy of the elytra mesh textured from Minecraft's own
    wings/elytra.png (so any elytra retexture pack is the wing art, no licence issue), two static poses, a flap as polish. Never broken
    into a treatment; the Roadmap tab lists pegasus wings as "lower" priority than the horn, antlers, spines and crystals. The biggest
    blocker (Pegasus, Uchchaihshravas, and part of Longma), and optional for Tulpar and Buraq.
 2. TAILS. A lion or tufted tail (the Unicorn's "tufted or lion-like tail") and a fish tail (Hippocampus), plus a dragon or blade tail.
    On the Roadmap tab; not scoped.
 3. FORCED RIDING, "the kelpie" (wiki/forced-riding.html Roadmap tab): a wild horse that lets you mount and will not let you off, carrying
    you to deep water. Deferred, waiting on ride-lock and warning feedback. The Kelpie's and the Each-uisge's defining behaviour. Related,
    also Roadmap only: Block Preference (a locus pulling a horse toward water).
 4. CLOUD-TREADING flight (gene-flying Roadmap tab), only if a Pegasus should stand on clouds.

GAP, nothing anywhere:
 5. EXTRA LEGS (Sleipnir's eight). The part layer hangs boxes on existing bones; extra legs would be copies of leg bones that must swing
    with the leg they copy. No part kind, no anchor, no plan.
 6. EXTRA HEADS (Uchchaihshravas's seven). A head is a big multi-box assembly; seven is a different order of work from one horn. Could be a
    reduced count. No plan.
 7. HINDQUARTER REPLACEMENT (Hippocampus's fish rear, webbed front feet instead of hooves). The machinery can ADD parts but nothing HIDES a
    vanilla bone. Needs a "hide this part of the horse" capability plus the fish tail above.
 8. CLOVEN HOOVES / HOOF CAPS. Dropped from the feet scope when feathering was chosen alone. The Unicorn's "sometimes cloven hooves".
 9. A DRAGON-LIKE MANE (Longma): mane COLOUR is there; mane SHAPE (flame-like, frill, crest) is not. A neck frill or ruff from the head and body
    treatments gets part of the way.
10. "JOURNEYS ASSOCIATED WITH OTHER REALMS" and a supernatural NIGHT JOURNEY (Sleipnir, Buraq): no ability. Flight and speed are present; realm travel needs a design call (a portal-making
    ability, a dimension hop, or just lore).
11. PURIFY WATER (Unicorn): no gene. Healer and Cleansing Light are nearest. Optional per the table ("in some traditions"); a design call.
12. KELPIE SHAPESHIFTING into something other than a land animal (a person, a fish): Lycan cannot, by design.
13. IMMORTALITY (Arion, Balius, Xanthus, Podarkes, and the Dioscuri's horses Kyllaros, Phlogeus and Harpagos): GAP. No gene makes a horse unkillable or brings it back. Nearest shipped: immunities (Fireproof, Ocean
    Born, Bird Boned), Magic Health, and /horseresurrect, an OPERATOR command and not a gene. A design call before an engineering one: (a) takes no
    damage, (b) cannot die from ordinary causes but still dies to the void or a command, (c) dies and stands back up after a delay (a totem). It
    touches death handling, breeding economics, the Exits ledger and any server that kills horses on purpose. All three breeds are otherwise fast
    ordinary horses, so they ship without it.
14. A SKINLESS, EXPOSED-FLESH COAT (Nuckelavee): GAP, but cheap. A coat gene is a data file under horsegenetics/genes/ (wiki/making-a-gene.html);
    Bloodstained, Cleave and Burn are the nearest looks and none is a flayed-muscle coat.
15. A HUMAN TORSO ON THE HORSE'S BACK and FIN-LIKE LEG APPENDAGES (Nuckelavee): GAP. The first is a very large attachment (torso, arms, head hung on
    the body bone). The leg fins resemble feathering (a part on each leg bone) and could reuse the Feathering treatment's per-leg anchors.
16. POISONOUS BREATH / DISEASE (Nuckelavee): partial. Blight kills growth; no gene poisons or sickens creatures near the horse. A small effects-block
    aura gene would cover it (the effects verbs are a contract, see making-a-gene.html), but it is not scoped.
17. WILD JOURNEYS (Puca): optional. The same family as forced riding (3), as a gentler variant; until then it is flavour text.
19. FIRE BREATH, "breathes destructive flame" (Ares's team: Aithon, Konabos, Phobos): GAP. Nothing in the mod sets targets alight. Fireproof makes the HORSE immune, Molten
    Hooves is explicitly "light and particles only - nothing it runs over catches", and the Particle flames are cosmetic. The gene effect verbs (GeneAbility: Emitter, Combat,
    OnHurt, Summon and others) have no ignite or projectile-fire verb. UNVERIFIED whether Combat or an Emitter could carry one; a new verb costs four or five files (the
    contract is on wiki/making-a-gene.html#contracts). A cosmetic version (a flame-breath particle from the head, the Particle locus's head site) would work today and would
    not hurt anything.
20. ROOSTER FOREPARTS (Hippalektryon): GAP, the same family as the Hippocampus. A rooster front on a horse's hind half needs a way to HIDE the horse's head, neck and forelegs (gap 7)
    and then to ADD a beaked head, a comb and wattles, a feathered chest and bird feet. The ruff (INTAKE) and the bird coat patterns (corvid, peafowl) give a feathered neck and
    chest look; beak, comb and talons are unscoped parts (the talons would be the unscoped foot claws, gap 8). Out of reach without 7.
18. A NATURAL DAPPLE GENE: now on the owner's to-do list (intake/To do.txt). Wanted by Balius.

 Hippocampus .... BLOCKED on 7 (hide the rear) and 2 (fish tail). Swimming and water breathing are in.
 Uchchaihshravas  BLOCKED on 6 (seven heads), with 1 (wings) optional. Snow-white and flight are in.

 Xanthus ........ READY minus 13. A yellow, blond or reddish-brown coat, speed 10.
 Gullfaxi ....... READY. A golden mane (mane_color plus Flaxen), speed 10, a powerful build. Nothing blocks it.
 Svadilfari ..... READY. Heavy and powerful, pull 10, speed 10. The only claim to check is the stat: pull and speed both at 10 on one breed.
 Puca ........... READY on genes: black, and a Lycan shape pool (a breed pins the alleles it wants, e.g. goat, rabbit, dog-like wolf, hare). The "wild

 Grani .......... READY. Grey (Grey), a powerful build, speed 10. "Exceptional strength and courage" are the pull and health stats plus lore. Sleipnir's descendant:

 Hrimfaxi ....... READY, with one UNVERIFIED. A frost mane is Frostfall (mane, forelock and tail fading to white); Rime and Orchid Frost cover frost on the coat. "Dew falling

 Tulpar ......... PARTIAL. Flight (the flying gene), varied colours and a light fast build all exist. "Wings in many depictions" is gap 1, optional: it ships wingless like

Removed from this audit
=======================
Not public domain: Bree, Hwin, Jewel and Fledge (The Chronicles of Narnia, C. S. Lewis), Asfaloth, Snowmane, Rochallor, Nahar and Felaroef (Tolkien's legendarium), Shadowfax and Mearas (The Lord of the Rings), the Thestral (Harry Potter), the Nightmare (Dungeons & Dragons), Epona (The Legend of
Zelda) and Artax (The NeverEnding Story). Dropped with them: the "seen only by those who have witnessed death" rule, longevity and endurance as gene gaps (they
were only the Thestral's and Mearas's), and the Nightmare naming clash. If any of these returns it must be as an original creature with no borrowed name,
art or text.
Out of scope for now: a talking or prophesying horse (Arion, Xanthus; the Narnian Talking Horses were removed for the first rule anyway). A huge feature; not worth it yet.

What this means for scoping
===========================
Ready now, no engineering (build breed files): Gullfaxi, Svadilfari, Puca, Grani, Skinfaxi, Hrimfaxi, Arvakr, Alsvidr, Mares of Diomedes, Horses of Helios (with Bronte, Sterope, Aithops), Chollima (wingless), Hofvarpnir, Sinir, Skeidbrimir, Gisl, Falhofnir, Bloudughofi, Skylla, Sthenios, Askhetos, Kygnos, Pedasus, Gulltoppr, Silfrintoppr, Gladr, Gyllir, Glenr, Lettfeti, Devadatta, Lampos, Phaethon (and Kyllaros, Phlogeus, Harpagos without immortality). Ready without immortality: Arion, Balius (better with a dapple gene), Xanthus. Partial
(wings optional): Tulpar, Buraq, Qilin (a horse-bodied version). Mostly covered once the parts are built (INTAKE): Longma, Unicorn.
One real blocker each: Pegasus (wings), Uchchaihshravas (seven heads), Sleipnir (eight legs), Hippocampus (hide the rear plus a fish tail), Kelpie and Each-uisge
(forced riding), Nuckelavee (several).
In priority order, by how many breeds each unblocks:
  A. WINGS (Pegasus, Uchchaihshravas, Longma; optional for Tulpar, Buraq, Chollima and Helios's horses). A real treatment is needed; the elytra route is already researched.
  B. FORCED RIDING (Kelpie, Each-uisge, and Puca's journeys). Already researched and deferred; needs its dependencies decided, then a treatment.
  C. TAILS (Unicorn, Hippocampus, Qilin, a dragon blade). Small: a tail is a body-bone-adjacent part, like a spine row.
  D. IMMORTALITY (Arion, Balius, Xanthus): a design call first.
  E. A NATURAL DAPPLE GENE (Balius): on the to-do list.
  F. FIRE BREATH (Aithon, Konabos, Phobos). A new ability; a cosmetic version needs nothing.
  G. THE BODY-STRUCTURE PROBLEMS (extra legs, extra heads, hide-a-bone, a human torso). Each unblocks exactly one breed and each is a different engineering
     problem; scope separately and only if the owner wants those breeds.

Design questions this raises that are not gene gaps
===================================================
- Named individuals versus breeds. Pegasus, Sleipnir, Uchchaihshravas, Arion, Balius, Xanthus, Gullfaxi, Svadilfari, Grani, Skinfaxi, Hrimfaxi, Arvakr, Alsvidr, Buraq and nearly every row of the fifth table (Hofvarpnir, Skylla, Sthenios, Podarkes, Askhetos, Kygnos and the rest) are single beings (Tulpar, Chollima, the Mares of Diomedes, the Horses of Helios and the Qilin are types or groups); the mod's unit is a
  breed (a population). Options: a breed called after the creature, a unique spawn that exists once per world, or a breed plus a named-founder mechanism.
  Nothing in the repo decides this.
- Which of these should be wild spawns, creative/spawn-egg only, or reachable only by breeding. The breed file's spawn list already supports all three; the
  tables do not say.
- Whether "10+" speed means the stat cap or a stat past it. UNVERIFIED.
- Naming: the fifth table has several beings who share a name with another (Aithon of Ares and Aithon of Helios, Podarkes and Podargos, Kyllaros and the centaur Cyllarus, Phaethon the horse and Phaethon the charioteer, Lampos and Lampon). Breed ids must be unique and readable.
- Whether to scope Momentum Sprint (wiki/momentum-sprint.html, Roadmap tab only, no intake treatment). Chollima's whole identity depends on it, and the page also names Arion and Sleipnir.
- A team of two or four to one chariot (Arvakr and Alsvidr, the Horses of Helios): a carts Roadmap item, not scoped.

What I could not verify
=======================
- Whether the breed stat curve can express speed 10 (or pull 10 together with it) for a whole breed.
- The exact allele tokens of the flying gene's true-flight form; the Nightmare and Cloudtouched breed files already pin the gene, so copy their pairs.
- Whether a dragon-like mane can be done with the existing mane colour genes plus the head and body parts; a visual judgement.
