Mythic breeds - gene audit

An AUDIT, NOT A SPEC. Nothing here has been changed in the repo and no decision has been made for the
owner. It maps the eleven breeds in her table (Pegasus, Unicorn, Sleipnir, Kelpie, Hippocampus,
Uchchaihshravas, Longma, Shadowfax, Mearas, Thestral, D&D Nightmare) onto what the mod already has
(implemented genes, parts and breeds), what is already scoped in intake/ but not built, and what is
still unscoped. Read from the shipped gene classes and gene files, the wiki gene pages, the shipped
breed files and every treatment in intake/. UNVERIFIED lines are things I could not confirm.

Legend
======
 HAVE     implemented and shipped today
 INTAKE   scoped in intake/ but not built; named by file
 ROADMAP  named on a page's Roadmap tab or inside a big intake note, but with no treatment of its own
 GAP      nothing anywhere: still needs scoping

How the table's columns map onto the mod
========================================
- Body colour, mane and tail colour: HAVE. White, grey, silver, black and "variable" are all reachable with the
  base-coat genes (Extension, Agouti, Grey, Silver, extreme white and the rest) and the independent mane_color and
  tail_color genes. Breeds already pin them.
- Hoof colour: nothing in the table asks for one except the D&D Nightmare's flaming hooves, which is Molten Hooves
  (HAVE). There is no hoof-COLOUR gene; hooves are painted by the coat pipeline's own region rules. Not a gap for any
  breed in this table.
- Speed / jump: HAVE. Breed stats run to 10 (the shipped Nightmare is jump 10), so "10+" means the cap. UNVERIFIED that
  speed 10 is reachable in the stat curve; check BreedStatCurve before pinning a breed to it.
- Special abilities: see the table below.
- Attachments: the whole gap lives here.

What the mod already covers (HAVE)
==================================
- Flight: the data gene "flying". Gliding is dominant (an elytra-like glide) and TRUE flight is recessive, two copies, "a
  horse that goes wherever it is pointed". Painting nothing: a flying horse looks like any other until it leaves the ground.
  Shipped breeds Cloudtouched and the small Nightmare already pin it. The third mode, cloud-treading, is unbuilt (Roadmap).
- Fire: Fireproof, Emberveins (cracked glowing veins), Molten Hooves (glowing hoofprints, any colour), the 40-allele Particle
  locus (flames, souls, snow, hearts, water wake, rain and more; recessive, two copies), the eye-colour and eye-glow loci.
  Mane and tail colour can be fire-coloured through mane_color and tail_color.
- Water: Waterborn (walks on water, leaves sparks), Ocean Born (cannot drown), Magic Water Breathing, Magic Swim Speed,
  Hydrophobic (will not be ridden into water). Coat patterns that read as water on the coat: dripwork, ooze_drip,
  rainbow_drip, foamed, tidefoam_sash, tidemark, tidewave, coral_bloom, gill_bloom, verdant. Visual only.
- Healing and purity: Healer (mends nearby people), Cleansing Light (burns the undead), Holy Ward (no hostile spawns near),
  Potion Milk. None "purifies water".
- Scales and dragon coats (coat patterns, visual only): diamond_scutes ("a dragon's wing-scutes"), scuted (snake belly),
  beadscale, fish_scales, dorsal_shield, serpentine, dragon_shroud (plumage, not scales).
- Skeletal / light build: Riblines (rib and bone strokes on the coat), Bird Boned (light bones, no fall damage),
  Shadowcreature (the front third goes black with tentacles).
- Shapeshifting: Lycan, but only a matched pair, only at night, only into a peaceful LAND animal.
- A horn: Unicorn Horn with horn colour, glow and dust; Ram Horns; Antlers.
- Breeds that already exist and overlap the table: Unicorn (rare, 7/7), Nightmare (very rare; a tiny fast will-o'-the-wisp
  that flies and flees, NOT the D&D steed), Cloudtouched (a glide horse), Netherhorse, Enderpony, Mirrorgazer, Rimehart,
  Greenwood, Dhampir, Not A Sheep.

Already scoped in intake/ but not built (INTAKE)
================================================
- Head parts (intake/Head parts - implementation treatment.txt): dragon horns, ear fins/frills, cheek spikes, brow ridge,
  goat beard, ruff.
- Body parts (intake/Body parts - implementation treatment.txt): back sail, dorsal spine row, shoulder and hip plates,
  crystal growths.
- Tusks gene (intake/Tusks gene - implementation treatment.txt): boar tusks, sabre fangs, narwhal horn.
- Feathering (intake/Feathering - implementation treatment.txt): fetlock feathering coloured from the leg.
- Other intake items that touch this table only indirectly: the Daily realm horse, Villagers in every village, Horse
  shoes, Realm breeding pause. None grants a part or ability the table asks for.

What is still unscoped
======================
ROADMAP only, no treatment (wiki/model-parts.html Roadmap tab, and Stage 6 of "intake/Attached model parts.txt"):
 1. WINGS. Feathered pegasus wings, leathery bat wings, dragon wings. The settled route is a copy of the elytra mesh textured
    from Minecraft's own wings/elytra.png (so any elytra retexture pack is the wing art, no licence issue), two static poses,
    and a flap as polish. It has never been broken into a treatment; the Roadmap tab lists pegasus wings as "lower" priority than the horn, antlers, spines and crystals. This is the
    single biggest blocker in the table (Pegasus, Thestral, Uchchaihshravas, Longma).
 2. TAILS. A lion or tufted tail (the Unicorn's "tufted or lion-like tail") and a fish tail (Hippocampus), plus a dragon or
    blade tail. Listed on the Roadmap tab; not scoped.
 3. FORCED RIDING, "the kelpie" (wiki/forced-riding.html Roadmap tab): a wild horse that lets you mount and will not let you off,
    carrying you to deep water. Deferred, waiting on ride-lock and warning feedback. It is the Kelpie's defining behaviour.
    Related and also Roadmap only: Block Preference (a locus pulling a horse toward water).
 4. CLOUD-TREADING flight (the gene-flying Roadmap tab), only if a Pegasus should stand on clouds.

GAP, nothing anywhere:
 5. EXTRA LEGS (Sleipnir's eight). The part layer hangs boxes on existing bones; extra legs would be copies of leg bones that
    must swing with the leg they copy. No part kind, no anchor, no plan.
 6. EXTRA HEADS (Uchchaihshravas's seven). A head is a big multi-box assembly on the neck; seven is a different order of work
    from one horn. Could be a reduced count. No plan.
 7. HINDQUARTER REPLACEMENT (Hippocampus's fish rear, and webbed front feet instead of hooves). The machinery can ADD parts but
    nothing HIDES a vanilla bone. Needs a "hide this part of the horse" capability, plus the fish tail above.
 8. CLOVEN HOOVES / HOOF CAPS. Dropped from the feet scope when feathering was chosen alone (claws, hoof caps and spurs are the
    ones left). The Unicorn's "sometimes cloven hooves" lives here.
 9. VISIBLE ONLY TO THOSE WHO HAVE SEEN DEATH (Thestral): a per-player render rule. Nothing like it exists; it would need a
    per-viewer visibility flag and a definition of "witnessed death" for a player.
10. A DRAGON-LIKE FACE (Thestral) beyond head parts: the brow ridge, cheek spikes and dragon horns in the Head parts treatment
    cover the look; a reshaped skull is a model change and would be its own plan.
11. A DRAGON-LIKE MANE (Longma): mane COLOUR is there; mane SHAPE (flame-like, frill, crest) is not. A neck frill or ruff from the
    head and body treatments gets part of the way.
12. "JOURNEYS ASSOCIATED WITH OTHER REALMS" (Sleipnir): no ability for it. Flight and speed are present; realm travel needs a
    design call (a portal-making ability, a dimension hop, or just lore).
13. PURIFY WATER (Unicorn): no gene does it. Healer and Cleansing Light are the nearest. Optional per the table ("in some
    traditions"); a design call, not a blocker.
14. KELPIE SHAPESHIFTING into something other than a land animal (a person, a fish): Lycan cannot, by design.
15. LONGEVITY (Mearas) and ENDURANCE (Shadowfax): UNVERIFIED that no lifespan system exists; I found none. Endurance maps loosely to
    Momentum Sprint (Roadmap) and horse health, nothing exact. Both are lore the stats can carry; confirm with the owner.
16. UNDERSTANDING OF SPEECH (Shadowfax): no gene. Pure lore; recommend leaving it as breed description text.

==============

Numbers refer to the lists above.

 Pegasus ........ BLOCKED on 1 (wings). Everything else exists: white body and mane, true flight (two copies of the flying gene),
                  a light fast build. Without wings it is close to the existing Cloudtouched (a glide horse), which has no wings either.
 Unicorn ........ PARTIAL, and it already exists. Horn, glow, dust, white coat and rainbow mane are in. Missing from the table:
                  goat beard (INTAKE), tufted/lion tail (2), cloven hooves (8), purify water (13); stats 7/7 against the table's 8/8.
 Sleipnir ....... BLOCKED on 5 (eight legs), and 12 (realm travel) is a design call. Grey and 10/10 stats are in.
 Kelpie ......... BLOCKED on 3 (forced riding), its whole point. In already: black body, water walking, no-drowning, swimming, water
                  patterns and water-wake particles. 14 (non-animal shapes) is optional.
 Hippocampus .... BLOCKED on 7 (hide the rear) and 2 (fish tail). Swimming and water breathing are in.
 Uchchaihshravas  BLOCKED on 6 (seven heads), with 1 (wings) optional. Snow-white and flight are in.
 Longma ......... MOSTLY COVERED by INTAKE: scale patterns exist now, dragon horns, ear fins, cheek spikes, brow ridge, ruff, a sail,
                  spine row and plates are all scoped. Open: 1 (wings, "sometimes") and 11 (dragon mane). Shippable as a wingless
                  dragon-horse once the head and body parts are built.
 Shadowfax ...... READY as a breed (silver-grey via Grey and Silver, speed 10, the stats). 15 and 16 are lore. It is one named

 Mearas ......... READY as a breed, the lineage Shadowfax belongs to. 15 (longevity) is lore or a design call.
 Thestral ....... BLOCKED on 1 (wings) and 9 (seen only by those who have seen death). In already: black, flight, a light skeletal

 Nightmare (D&D)  READY on genes: black coat, flaming mane and tail (mane_color plus a flame particle), flaming hooves (Molten Hooves),
                  true flight with no wings, Fireproof, heavy and fast. NAME CLASH: a breed called Nightmare already exists (the tiny

What this means for scoping
===========================
In priority order, by how many breeds each unblocks:
  A. WINGS (Pegasus, Thestral, Uchchaihshravas, Longma). The biggest. A real treatment is needed; the elytra route is already researched.
  B. TAILS (Unicorn, Hippocampus, a dragon blade). Small: a tail is a body-bone-adjacent part, like a spine row.
  C. FORCED RIDING (Kelpie). Already researched and deferred; needs its dependencies decided, then a treatment.
  D. THE THREE BODY-STRUCTURE PROBLEMS (extra legs, extra heads, hide-a-bone). Each unblocks exactly one breed (Sleipnir, Uchchaihshravas,
     Hippocampus) and each is a different engineering problem. They should be scoped separately and only if the owner wants those breeds.
  E. THE DESIGN CALLS with no engineering (purify water, realm travel, longevity, speech, "seen death" visibility, named individuals).

Design questions this table raises that are not gene gaps
=========================================================
- Named individuals versus breeds. Pegasus, Sleipnir, Shadowfax and Uchchaihshravas are single beings; the mod's unit is a breed (a
  population). Options: a breed called after the creature (the table's own "Mearas" row is the lineage Shadowfax would belong to; no Mearas breed exists in the repo), a unique spawn that can only exist
  once per world, or a breed plus a named-founder mechanism. Nothing in the repo decides this.
- The existing Nightmare breed. See above.
- Which of these should be wild spawns, which creative/spawn-egg only, and which reachable only by breeding. The breed file's spawn
  list already supports that; the table does not say.
- Whether "10+" speed means a stat past the cap (a new magic-speed stack) or the cap. UNVERIFIED.

What I could not verify
=======================
- Whether the breed stat curve can express speed 10 for a whole breed.
- That no lifespan or age-death system exists.
- The exact allele tokens of the flying gene's true-flight form; the Nightmare and Cloudtouched breed files already pin the gene, so
  copy their pairs.
- Whether a dragon-like mane can be done with the existing mane colour genes plus the head and body parts; it is a visual judgement.
