What the page says is in it
===========================
Two downloads:
  Pixelated Patterns 32x32.zip   98 kB
  Pixelated Patterns 64x64.zip   183 kB
Pattern textures at 32x32 and 64x64 - close to the pixel scale of the mod's own
horse and mesh textures (vanilla horse sheets are 64x64), which is why this pack
may suit the greyscale base textures for the add-on meshes better than the 256x256
tiles in ../pixel-art-textures/. Contents beyond that (how many, which patterns,
whether coloured or greyscale, tileable or not) are UNKNOWN until the zips are
opened. Whoever opens them should record the list here.
