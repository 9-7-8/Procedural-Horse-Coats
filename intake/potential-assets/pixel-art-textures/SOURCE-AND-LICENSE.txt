Pixel Art Texture Pack (256x256) - source, licence, credit

What it is
==========
Over a thousand hand-drawn pixel-art tiles, every one 256x256, as PNGs under
PNGs/: Bricks, Concrete, Debris, Decorations, Dirt, Doors, Fabric, Foliage,
Glass, Grass, Gravel, Metal, Paper, Patterns, Pebbles, Rockface, Roofing, Sand,
Snow, Stones, Tiles, Wall, Water, Windows, Wood. This is the free "PNG - Pixel
Art Textures.zip" (26 MB on the page). The PSD sources are separate downloads
and are not held here. Files as received, unmodified.

Licence
=======
The store page states: "CC0 1.0 Universal. Feel free to use these assets in any
personal or commercial projects." The author repeats in the comments that the
textures may be edited and used in personal or commercial products.
CC0 dedicates the work to the public domain: no credit, no restriction on
modification or redistribution.

DISCREPANCY TO KNOW ABOUT
=========================
The zip's own License.txt (dated 2024-08-19, an earlier release; a second copy
sits in PNGs/) says something stricter: free for commercial and non-commercial
use, modification allowed, no credit required, "Do not redistribute,
repackage or sell this asset, modified or unmodified, except if they are
content files in your commercial or non-commercial project." That is NOT CC0.
It is kept verbatim in License.txt.
The decision recorded here: the store page is the author's current, stated
licence (CC0, updated Dec 2025, later than the file) and CC0 is a deliberate
public-domain dedication, so this pack is treated as CC0 and IS committed to
the repo's intake/. If the author ever says the stricter file governs, delete
the PNGs from git and follow the Raven pack's handling (see ../raven-fantasy-hd-free/).
Individual tiles copied into a module are unproblematic under either text.
