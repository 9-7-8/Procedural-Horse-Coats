What it is, in the uploader's words
===================================
  "The Internet Pattern Book is an ongoing effort to develop a workable archive
  and sortable resource to showcase tilable backgrounds used in early web
  design. To date, it contains 317 patterns that you can browse by style or
  colour. These are taken as background assets from various defunct sites on
  Geocities circa the 2000s."
  "During the HTML era of web design, repeating background assets known as tiles
  were used frequently to minimise bandwidth."
And, in the download's install instructions:
  "This download is for the patterns only without the interactive web frontend.
  Patterns are not owned by the uploader and provided as-is."

Licence - THE UPLOADER DOES NOT HOLD THE RIGHTS, AND SAYS SO
============================================================
This pack is not licensed to us by anyone. It is an archive of tiles scraped
from defunct Geocities pages circa 2000; the uploader states plainly that the
patterns are not theirs and are provided as-is, so there is no grant to rely on,
no named author to credit, and no terms to comply with. The individual tiles
were made by hundreds of unknown people twenty-five years ago.

THE OWNER'S CALL, 2026-09-29, recorded here so no later session reopens it:
  Take them anyway, on the same footing as the other packs. The reasoning she
  gave: this is a free and open-source Minecraft mod, not paid software; we are
  not redistributing the archive as it is; and any use would be a few tiles
  worked into something else. She is aware the licence is murkier than the other
  three and is accepting that risk knowingly.

What that does and does not permit
  - The images stay OUT of git, like the Raven icons and the pixelated patterns.
    The .gitignore rule for this folder keeps them out.
  - The archive is never republished whole - not in a release, not on the wiki,
    not in the gene-creator or horse-designer asset bundles.
  - Anything used is worked into a new texture rather than shipped as the tile,
    and is logged in USAGE.txt with what it became.
  - THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md gets an entry the first time anything from here is
    used, saying where the tile came from and that its original author is
    unknown. There is no credit line to give beyond the archive itself.
  - If an original author is ever identified and objects, that file comes out.
    Keep USAGE.txt accurate enough to make that a small job.

THREE TRAPS, all of them the archive's own, none of them ours
=============================================================
1. THE FILE EXTENSIONS LIE. 116 of the 331 files are not the format they are
   named: 99 files called .jpg are really GIF, 8 called .jpg are really PNG, and
    8 called .gif are really JPEG. By magic bytes the real split is 192 JPEG,
   131 GIF, 8 PNG. Anything that reads this folder must SNIFF THE HEADER, never
   the extension - ImageIO will cope, a hand-rolled reader chosen by suffix will
   not, and a bake script that trusts the name will fail on a third of the pack.
2. One file has no extension at all: img/fir_plant_green. It is a JPEG.
3. Eight of the GIFs are animated (autumn_plant_yellow, blink_space_black,
   riches_other_yellow, swirly_other_black.jpg, swirly_other_brown.jpg,
   twinkle_space_black, whichway_other_red, wunderbar_tile_red) - note that two
   of those are the ones misnamed .jpg. Reading frame 0 is fine; just know they
   are not stills.

Sizes, and the fit with this mod
================================
156 distinct sizes, and they are web tiles, not game art. The commonest are
96x96 (30), 128x128 (27), 100x100 (21), 160x160 (13), 256x256 (12), 200x200
(12), 80x80 (12), 150x150 (11), 64x64 (10). 57 files are larger than 256 in some
dimension, up to 512x512.
So only ten are already at the horse sheet's own scale, and JPEG means the rest
carry compression artefacts that will show as colour noise when scaled down onto
a 64x64 or 128x128 sheet. Treat these as DESIGN REFERENCE and as raw material to
redraw from, rather than files to resample and ship. The pixelated-patterns pack
is the one that is already at the mod's pixel scale.
