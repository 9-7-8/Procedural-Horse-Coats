Raven Fantasy HD Ultimate (FREE files) - source, licence, credit

*** THE IMAGES IN THIS FOLDER ARE NOT COMMITTED TO GIT. ***
The licence forbids distributing the pack as a separate product. The owner keeps
the files directly on the machine that hosts this repo. .gitignore makes git
skip them; the notes in this folder (this file, MANIFEST.txt, USAGE.txt and the
licence PDF) ARE committed. MANIFEST.txt says what the machine's copy should
look like and how to check it.

What it is
==========
"RAVEN FANTASY HD ULTIMATE FREE": 32x32 HD pixel-art RPG icons. Full
Spritesheet.png (512x1792) plus the cut-up singles in Separated Files/
(FHD (1).png ...). The FREE sampler from the store page's own download list
("RFHD Free Files.zip", 1.1 MB); the paid "Premium Files" zips are a different
product and this folder does not hold them.

What this means for the mod
===========================
Using an icon in the mod is allowed IF ALL of these hold:
  1. The mod stays free. It does today. (The repo's CC BY-NC licence permits
     donations on a free derivative; whether a donation link counts as
     "microtransactions" under the page's wording is not addressed by either
     document. Ask the author before relying on it.)
  2. THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md (which ships in the jar) states, next to the credit,
     that these icons may NOT be used or redistributed by the mod's users.
  3. The mod's CC BY-NC grant is explicitly carved back for these files. The
     repo's licence lets forks reuse and redistribute; this pack's licence does
     not extend that to them. Anything copied from this pack into a module needs
     that carve-out written where it lives (the notices file, and the folder
     that holds the file), or a fork inherits rights that are not ours to give.
  4. The pack itself is never published: not committed to git, not in a release
     zip, not on the wiki or in the gene-creator / horse-designer assets. Only
     the individual icons actually used, inside the mod.
  5. The name "Clockwork Raven" appears only as credit.
  6. Nothing from it is used to train or prompt-tune a model.
