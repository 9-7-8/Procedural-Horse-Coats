1. The request, as given
========================
The owner's research, congenital section: "Keep a distinct disease record for each particular variant: gene,
allele, genotype, penetrance, age_of_onset, severity, and any parent-of-origin requirement." Four rows: spider lamb
syndrome (FGFR3, recessive, severe bone growth, carriers with a small additive long-bone effect); the EDNRB
deletion hypopigmentation syndrome (recessive, ~110-kb, homozygous lambs white, blue-eyed, dying soon after birth;
carriers unaffected); polyceraty-associated eyelid defects (sometimes with HOXD1 multiple horns, not obligatory);
other inherited dwarfism/chondrodysplasia ("add only when you can identify a defined breed, syndrome, or variant").
"Do not make ordinary ASIP-white or ordinary spotted sheep sick by association." "do not invent a general rule that
white spotting causes deafness or that an MSTN muscular sheep has a congenital illness."
Reading, confirmed: reuse the horse disorder machinery (Decided); EDNRB is its own locus, never tied to any other
white.


2. In scope, and what is not
============================
In scope
 - Piece 1: the EDNRB white (recessive, lethal in the homozygote).
 - Piece 2: spider lamb (FGFR3 analogue: recessive severe, carrier frame effect).
 - Piece 3: the polyceraty eyelid risk and the dwarfism rule, as a plan or a conditional.
Not in scope (all re-askable)
 - A deafness rule for white sheep. Decided by the research and owner: none.
 - A congenital illness for muscular (MSTN) sheep. None.
 - A parent-of-origin disease. Callipyge is in the horns and body treatment and is not a disease.
 - Sheep breeds that carry these (later, with the owner). No invisible carriers in the wild: a founder table only
   contains genotypes that show.
 - A new health system; a death that is not one of the two existing lethal paths.


3. What already exists, so the building session does not redo it
=================================================================
3a. Disorder genes: RecessiveDisorderGene and DominantDisorderGene (common/genetics/genes), SkittishGene, MagicHealthGene.
    NATURAL_HEALTH family lede (GeneFamily): "Almost every one is recessive and absent from its own founder table
    as a homozygote - a wild horse can carry a disorder but never have one."
3b. Lethals: wiki/horse-body.html owns "disorders, the two lethal paths". Read it for what a lethal does to a foal
    and how the two paths differ before choosing one. UNVERIFIED here which of the two fits "dies soon after birth".
3c. Eye colour: wiki/eye-colour.html owns thirteen loci and "the genes that request one"; a blue-eyed result is
    requested through that system, not painted by the disorder. UNVERIFIED how a gene requests an eye colour -
    read it before wiring the blue eyes.
3d. THE HORSE ALREADY HAS AN EDNRB GENE. EdnrbGene.java (wiki/gene-ednrb.html) is frame overo, "the model's first
    genuine lethal": two carriers give an all-white, blue-eyed foal that does not survive its first days, and it
    claims blue eyes through the shared eye-colour channel. It was renamed to its locus alongside KIT, MITF and
    PAX3. The sheep EDNRB deletion is the SAME real locus with the SAME outcome (white, blue-eyed, dies soon
    after birth), so Piece 1 must not simply add a second gene that does the same thing. Read EdnrbGene and the
    page first.
Looks built but is not
  - Any sheep disease allele; "spider lamb", "EDNRB" and "polyceraty" are in no code or wiki page.
  - Penetrance, age_of_onset, or severity as stored fields. The research wants them; the mod's disorders may not
    have them (UNVERIFIED). The mod's convention may be "a sentence on Gameplay" (CLAUDE.md: health goes as a
    sentence), so the record may be a table on the Science tab rather than fields in code.


4. The pieces
=============
Piece 1 - The EDNRB white
-------------------------
 Open (owner, ASK FIRST - decides the whole piece): the horse's EdnrbGene already is a recessive-lethal white with
   blue eyes. Options: (a) a sheep EDNRB allele APPENDED to the existing gene (one locus, no duplicate, but a
   natural gene would gain a natural-sheep allele, which the kind treatment does not allow - a gene is one kind);
   (b) a separate natural-sheep gene with its own key that gives the same outcome through the same eye-colour
   channel (two EDNRB loci, a duplicate but clean kinds); (c) no new gene - document that frame overo's lethal
   white already is the model's EDNRB white and do nothing. Recommended: (b), named for its variant, with the page
   linking to gene-ednrb.html. Never reuse the horse key.
What the player sees: a very rare sheep-lineage white horse with blue eyes whose foal does not survive; carrier
 parents look ordinary. Breeding two carriers is the only way to get one.
 Decided: a recessive natural-sheep disorder gene reusing RecessiveDisorderGene's pattern: homozygous = white coat,
   blue eyes, dies shortly after birth. Heterozygotes are unaffected carriers. It is its OWN locus; ordinary
   ASIP white and ordinary spotted sheep carry no risk.
 Decided: absent from founder tables as a homozygote; a carrier may appear only where a breed pins it.
 Open (building session): the coat the homozygote paints (a white overlay that wins over sheep ASIP/MC1R/TYRP1) and
   its priority inside the natural-sheep sub-band; Recommended: after the pigment genes, so white wins.
 Open (owner): how severe in the mod - the research says "died soon after birth", with intestinal-obstruction signs
   in some lambs but the investigators "did not establish" lethal-white-horse aganglionosis. Recommended: the
   gentlest existing lethal path, with page text saying exactly that and not claiming the horse syndrome.
 Where: common/ - one gene, a gene page with a Science tab carrying the evidence level and the research's caveat.
 Data and saves: key and allele order permanent. A world that has a carrier keeps it.
 Sync/config: none. Tests: GeneFilesTest/RecessiveDisorder test (homozygote shows, carrier does not); a founder-table
   test that no ordinary founder is homozygous; the lethal path test the horse disorders use; both goldens by KEY;
   a test that no other white gene triggers this death.
 Play checks: two carriers bred in a yard; the foal's coat, eyes and fate; an ASIP-white sheep stays healthy.
 Docs: the gene page, horse-body.html (Coding: a new disorder), gene-census.html, eye-colour.html if the eyes are
   requested through a locus there.

Piece 2 - Spider lamb (FGFR3 analogue)
--------------------------------------
What the player sees: a recessive condition that gives a foal long, bent legs and a poor outcome, with carriers a
 touch leggier. Carrier x carrier gives 25% affected, before any survival modelling.
 Decided: recessive severe disease; carriers get a small additive frame effect (the research: "an additive effect on
   long-bone length in carriers", with "genetic background" modifying manifestation).
 Recommended: do not model the background modification as a gene; leave it as the existing epigenome/size spread.
 Open (owner): what "severe" costs in the mod: stats only (health and jump down), or a lethal path? Recommended:
   stats only, a disorder not a lethal, because the research describes malformation, not certain death.
 Open (building session): whether the carrier frame effect is a size nudge through the size gene's range or the
   frame effect of the horns-and-body treatment; Recommended: ONE mechanism, not two.
 Where/data/tests/play/docs as Piece 1. Extra test: carrier x carrier distribution over a fixed seed set is near
   25% affected; carrier stats within the small band.

Piece 3 - Conditional risks: polyceraty eyelids and the dwarfism rule
---------------------------------------------------------------------
What the player sees: nothing certain. Four-horned (polycerate) sheep-lineage horses may rarely have a defect; there
 is no generic "small sheep is diseased" rule.
 Decided: incompletely penetrant, conditional, NOT "every four-horned lamb has defective eyelids". Dwarfism and
   chondrodysplasia are added only for a defined breed, syndrome or variant.
 Recommended: a plan only (Roadmap tab on the disorder/horn page) until polyceraty exists as a gene (horns and body
   treatment, Piece 1, Open). The mod's horses already carry a four-horned shape (Fhn) which must NOT gain this
   risk - that is a magical shape, not HOXD1.
 Open (owner): whether penetrance (a percentage that a polycerate horse is affected) is stored on the allele
   epigenome or fixed in code. Recommended: a fixed, small number per genotype.
 Where: plan page text only until a gene exists. No tests until built.
 Docs: a Roadmap tab on the page that will own the disorder (a plan with no page means writing the page - rule 9).

Three likeliest ways it goes wrong for a real player
  0. Two EDNRB loci with the same name confuse players and the census. Handled: the Open above; a distinct name.
  1. A player breeds a "white sheep" and loses foals, thinking ordinary white is the cause. Handled: the lethal is
     a separate locus; the pages and the H-menu text say the carriers look normal and name the locus.
  2. A founder table gives a homozygote in the wild. Handled: the founder-table test.
  3. A four-horned (Fhn) horse is read as sick. Handled: no coupling to Fhn at all.
