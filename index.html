Natural genes

Genes that exist in real life. They restrict pigment in phase 1, and only ever take colour away.

Sex

Mares are X/X, stallions X/Y. First gene in the order, and the only one that paints nothing — so a foal’s sex is inherited, not rolled.

X / Y · 2 outcomes · 50/50

Extension

Can this horse make black pigment at all? ee gives chestnut.

E / e · dominant · 50/50

Agouti

Bay: black restricted to the points. Seal brown is the top of its distribution, not a separate gene.

A / a · dominant · 50/50

MATP — cream and pearl

One locus, three alleles, six combinations: palomino and buckskin, classic pearl, and the cremello a cream and a pearl together produce.

Cr / prl / N · 4 outcomes · 1 in 30, 1 in 22

Champagne

Gold, classic and amber champagne from a single rule that reads the current pigment.

Ch / c · dominant · 1 in 40

Grey

Dapple grey on adults only, at a per-horse stage from dark steel to nearly white.

G / g · dominant · 1 in 16

Dominant white

Both pigments gone everywhere. The only coat that should look like the bare template.

W / w · complete · 1 in 50

Splash white

Random socks and a centreline blaze — dipped in white from below.

Spl / spl · incomplete · 1 in 20

Magical genes

Invented for this mod. They add signed RGB in phase 3, after the pigment has resolved to colour.

Pink hair

Mane and tail walked toward hot pink, keeping their shading. The mod’s only recessive.

Pihr / n · recessive · 1 in 12

Magic zebra

Black stripes hung from the topline at −200% per channel — black over any coat at all.

Mzeb / n · dominant · 1 in 100

Test

The diagnostic gradient, painted flat on any base. Exercises the projection engine end to end.

T / t · complete · 1 in 4
